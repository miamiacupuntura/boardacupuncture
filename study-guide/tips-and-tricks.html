Universal method

Do not confuse

Ma Huang Tang* vs. Gui Zhi Tang*

Board

No sweat points to Ma Huang Tang*; spontaneous sweating points to Gui Zhi Tang*.

Both release Wind-Cold, but the state of the pores and the nutritive/protective relationship separate them.

Release the Exterior

Decisive clue

Bai Hu Tang*

Board

Think intense Qi-level Heat—not simply “fever.”

High fever, great thirst, profuse sweating and a forceful pulse create the classic four-greats pattern.

Clear Heat

Pattern separator

Deficiency insomnia vs. Phlegm-Fire

Night sweating and irritability suggest deficient Blood/Yin; explosive behavior with a greasy yellow coat suggests Phlegm-Fire.

Do not select a Shen formula from the word “insomnia” alone. Tongue coat, behavior and root pattern decide.

Memory house

The Exterior house

Board

Ma Huang* opens the closed door; Gui Zhi* warms the branches; Zi Su Ye* returns to the kitchen.

Use the house to remember pore opening, channels/extremities and the herb that also harmonizes the Stomach.

Individual herbs

Duì Yào

Dang Gui* + Huang Qi*

Board

The classic 1:5 relationship says: “Qi generates Blood.”

Do not flatten the pair into equal doses; the proportion explains the exam logic and the direction of the combination.

Safety first

Recognition does not equal recommendation

Historic toxic or endangered substances may appear on exams but must not be normalized for clinical use.

Zhu Sha is obsolete internally because of mercury toxicity; Xi Jiao and Ling Yang Jiao require legal and ethical substitution awareness.
