Formula name pair

Zuo Gui Wan vs. You Gui Wan

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueZuo Gui WanYou Gui Wan
Shared clueBoth are rich Kidney-tonifying formulas for deficiency of Essence, low-back weakness, dizziness, tinnitus and reproductive weakness.
Fast distinction“Restore the Left”: Kidney Yin and Essence deficiency with dryness or deficiency Heat.“Restore the Right”: Kidney Yang and gate-of-vitality Fire deficiency with Cold signs.

Comparative answer key

CompareZuo Gui WanYou Gui Wan
Primary directionEnriches Kidney Yin, Essence and marrow.Warms and tonifies Kidney Yang while replenishing Essence and Blood.
Decisive signsNight sweats, dry mouth, heat sensations, seminal emission, red scant-coated tongue.Cold limbs, aversion to cold, impotence, infertility, edema or clear copious urine.
Formula clueUses rich Yin/Essence tonics without Fu Zi or Rou Gui.Includes Fu Zi and Rou Gui to restore Ming Men Fire.
Exam shortcutLeft = Yin and Essence.Right = Yang and Fire.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersThe pair shares deep Kidney supplementation, so temperature is decisive: dryness/empty Heat points left; Cold and failed transformation point right.
Memory cueZuo/Left stores Yin; You/Right restores Yang.
SafetyYou Gui Wan contains Fu Zi and requires processed material, reliable sourcing and qualified professional supervision. Neither formula suits Dampness or digestive weakness without modification.
SourceSource bank: FWB 8.2 series and individual formula files; corroborated with Chinese Herbal Medicine: Formulas & Strategies, 2nd ed.
