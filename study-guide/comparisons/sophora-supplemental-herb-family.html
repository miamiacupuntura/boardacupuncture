Supplemental family 2 of 6

Sophora Cluster — Bud, Root and Toxic Throat Root

The pea family contributes a bleeding bud, a Damp-Heat root and a more toxic throat root.

Memory rule

AnchorHuái bud stops Blood; Kǔ root dries itch; Shān Dòu root clears throat toxin.

Identity decides the job

MedicinalIdentityJob
Huái Mǐ 槐米Sophora japonica flower budCools Blood and stops bleeding, especially intestinal/hemorrhoidal bleeding.
Kǔ Shēn 苦参Sophora flavescens rootClears Damp-Heat, kills parasites and relieves itching.
Shān Dòu Gēn 山豆根Sophora tonkinensis rootClears Heat-toxin and benefits a severely swollen sore throat.

Exam and safety

Exam keyBleeding, Damp-Heat itching and toxic throat swelling are three separate targets.
SafetyShān Dòu Gēn has significant dose-related neurologic/GI toxicity and requires strict professional dosing; sore throat with airway difficulty is an emergency.
