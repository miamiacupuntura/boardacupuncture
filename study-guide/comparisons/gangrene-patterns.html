Pathology or symptom comparison

Gangrene Stage Comparison

Diagnostic orientation

Core ideaThis is a historical stage comparison. Gangrene means tissue death from ischemia or infection and requires immediate hospital treatment.
Exam focusIschemic pain and weak pulses versus tissue loss and nonhealing wounds

Comparative differentiation

Pattern or presentationDecisive cluesRepresentative formula/herbStrategy
Early Qi and Blood stagnationCold pale limb, heaviness/numbness, calf cramps with walking, weak local pulse, atrophy and dark-purple tongueYang He Tang + Tao Hong Si Wu TangHistorical strategy: warm Yang and move Blood.
Late Qi and Blood deficiencyDigit/tissue loss, nonhealing lesions, withered complexion, fatigue, dyspnea, cold, sweating, atrophy and pale tongueRen Shen Yang Rong Tang or Shi Quan Da Bu TangHistorical strategy: support Qi and Blood.

Study rule

How to distinguishCold exertional pain and weak pulse signals ischemia early; tissue loss and a nonhealing wound identifies advanced disease.
Memory cueCold pain + weak pulse—ischemia; black/lost tissue + nonhealing—advanced gangrene.
SafetyGangrene, black/blue tissue, a cold pulseless limb, rapidly spreading redness, foul drainage, fever or severe rest pain is an emergency. Vascular/surgical and antibiotic treatment cannot be delayed.
