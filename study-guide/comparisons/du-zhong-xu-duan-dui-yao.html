Appendix B pair · 31 of 51

Dù Zhòng + Xù Duàn

杜仲 + 续断

One picture, one job

PictureRubber-thread bark plus “reconnect the broken.”
Combined jobSupplement Liver and Kidney, strengthen sinews and bones and stabilize pregnancy.
CategoryStrengthen the Back · Calm the Fetus

How the pair works

HerbRole within the pair
Dù ZhòngTonifies Liver–Kidney Yang and strongly supports the low back and knees.
Xù DuànTonifies without excessive cloying, reconnects injured sinews/bones and calms the fetus.

Exam focus and safety

CluesSore weak low back and knees from deficiency; classically also threatened miscarriage.
Signature formulaShòu Tāi Wán emphasizes Xù Duàn; Dú Huó Jì Shēng Tāng emphasizes Dù Zhòng.
TrapDù Zhòng leans toward pure deficiency; Xù Duàn also addresses injury and Blood stasis around damaged tissue.
PreparationSalt-processing may direct either medicinal more strongly toward the Kidney when specified by the source.
SafetyPain or bleeding during pregnancy requires obstetric evaluation. Trauma with weakness, numbness or bowel/bladder change requires urgent assessment.

Current formula-roster boundary

Inside the current 63-formula Board roster: Du Huo Ji Sheng Tang. Outside it: Shou Tai Wan; it remains a recognition clue only.
