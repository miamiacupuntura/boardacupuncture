Ba Zhen Tang

Combined Qi and Blood deficiency.

High-yield summary

CategoryTonify Qi and Blood
PatternCombined Qi and Blood deficiency.
ActionCombines the functions of Si Jun Zi Tang and Si Wu Tang to supplement Qi and nourish Blood.
Decisive clueFatigue and poor appetite occur together with pallor, dizziness, palpitations or scanty menses.

Ingredients

Ren Shen, Bai Zhu, Fu Ling, Zhi Gan Cao, Shu Di Huang, Bai Shao, Dang Gui, Chuan Xiong, with Sheng Jiang and Da Zao in the decoction method.

Commercial preparations and lineages may vary; always verify the source being used.

Differentiation and memory

Do not confuseSi Jun Zi Tang is Qi-only; Si Wu Tang is Blood-focused; Shi Quan Da Bu Tang adds warming support.
Memory cueFour Gentlemen plus Four Substances equals Eight Treasures.

Safety

Fatigue or pallor may indicate anemia, bleeding, endocrine or cardiac disease and warrants appropriate testing.
