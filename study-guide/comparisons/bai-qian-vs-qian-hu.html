Herb comparison

Cynanchi stauntonii Rhizoma (bai qian) vs. Peucedani Radix (qian hu)

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueBai QianQian Hu
Shared cluesBoth direct rebellious Qi downward, alleviate cough and reduce profuse Phlegm. They are often combined as the “two Qian” (er qian).
Fast distinctionSlightly warm; best for obstructed Lung Qi with rattling Phlegm, stifling chest fullness and acute wheezing.Cool; clears Heat, releases the Exterior and disperses Wind; best for thick yellow Phlegm with simultaneous Exterior Wind-Heat.

Comparative answer key

CompareBai QianQian Hu
Botanical nameCynanchi stauntonii Rhizoma.Peucedani Radix.
CategoryWarm Herbs that Transform Cold-Phlegm.Herbs that Cool and Transform Phlegm-Heat.
TemperatureSlightly warm.Cool.
Primary presentationAccumulated obstruction of Lung Qi with profuse rattling Phlegm, chest fullness and acute wheezing.Cough with thick yellow Phlegm and simultaneous Exterior Wind-Heat.
Distinctive actionSmooths the flow of Lung Qi.Also disperses clumps and releases the Exterior while dispersing Wind.
Combination clueThe “two Qian” are commonly combined to descend rebellious Qi and reduce profuse Phlegm.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersTheir names and descending actions are similar, but Bai Qian favors obstructed Lung Qi with rattling and acute wheezing, whereas Qian Hu favors thick yellow Phlegm with Exterior Wind-Heat.
Memory cueBai Qian smooths obstructed Lung Qi; Qian Hu cools, disperses Wind and breaks up clumps.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), p. 436. Source Exam Key CMID 9039, questions 3–6.
