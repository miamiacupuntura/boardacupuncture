Herb comparison

Gui Ban vs. Lu Rong

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueGui BanLu Rong
Shared clueBoth augment the Kidneys, fortify the bones and treat atrophy or weakness of the sinews and bones. They may be combined for concurrent Yin and Yang deficiency.
Fast distinctionTonifies Kidney Yin and the Conception Vessel; continuous uterine bleeding with Heat.Tonifies Kidney Yang and the Governing Vessel; uterine bleeding from Yang deficiency.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersBoth strengthen the Kidney-bone axis, but the exam distinction is Yin versus Yang, Conception versus Governing Vessel, and hot versus cold-deficiency uterine bleeding.
Memory cueGui Ban: Yin–Conception–Heat; Lu Rong: Yang–Governing–Cold.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), p. 843. Source Exam Keys CMID 8962, question 3, and CMID 9005, question 7.

Possible exam questions · Study prompts

  • Which Kidney, bone and sinew actions do both substances share?
  • Which substance tonifies Yin, and which fortifies Yang?
  • Which substance affects the Conception Vessel, and which the Governing Vessel?
  • How does the uterine-bleeding presentation distinguish the two?
