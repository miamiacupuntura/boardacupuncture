Herb comparison

Chloriti Lapis (qing meng shi) vs. Haematitum (dai zhe shi)

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueQing Meng ShiDai Zhe Shi
Shared cluesBoth are heavy, sinking minerals that pacify the Liver, sedate convulsions, direct rebellious Qi downward and scour out Phlegm.
Fast distinctionMore intense, salty Phlegm-Heat mineral that clears Phlegm and food stagnation from the alimentary tract.Suppresses rebellious Qi and Liver Wind, enters the Blood level, cools Blood and stops bleeding.

Comparative answer key

CompareQing Meng Shi (Chloriti Lapis)Dai Zhe Shi (Haematitum)
Category in sourceHerbs that Transform Phlegm and Stop Coughing · Herbs that Cool and Transform Phlegm-Heat.Substances that Extinguish Wind and Stop Tremors.
Properties in this comparisonSalty; more intense in action.Bitter, sweet and neutral.
Primary emphasisMobilizes the alimentary tract and clears Phlegm and food stagnation.Suppresses rebellious Qi in the Penetrating Vessel and extinguishes Liver Wind.
Channels or levelsClears the channels of the Lungs, Liver and Stomach by removing the source of Phlegm.Enters the Blood level and primarily affects the Liver, Stomach and Pericardium channels.
Additional actionThe source emphasizes its stronger Phlegm-clearing action.Cools Blood and stops bleeding.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersBoth are heavy minerals that descend Qi and address Phlegm, so the digestive-Phlegm versus Blood-and-Liver distinction is essential.
Common confusionPhlegm and food stagnation in the alimentary tract favors Qing Meng Shi; Penetrating Vessel rebellion, Liver Wind or bleeding favors Dai Zhe Shi.
Source-data noteThis comparison calls Dai Zhe Shi “bitter, sweet, and neutral”; later questions in the same exam describe its “bitter coldness.” Both source descriptions are retained in their respective comparisons.
Source question formatsQuestions 1 and 2 are Matching.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), p. 398. Source Exam Key CMID 8942.
