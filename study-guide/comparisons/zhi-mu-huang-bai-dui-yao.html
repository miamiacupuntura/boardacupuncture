Appendix B pair · 12 of 51

Zhī Mǔ + Huáng Bǎi

知母 + 黄柏

One picture, one job

PictureTwo bitter-cold herbs put out the fire beneath an empty kettle.
Combined jobEnrich Yin while draining deficiency Fire from the Kidney and lower burner.
CategoryDrain Deficiency Fire

How the pair works

HerbRole within the pair
Zhī MǔClears deficiency Heat and nourishes Yin and fluids.
Huáng BǎiDrains ministerial Fire and Damp-Heat from the lower burner.

Exam focus and safety

CluesNight sweats, steaming-bone fever, afternoon Heat, seminal emission and other Empty-Heat signs.
Signature formulaZhī Bǎi Dì Huáng Wán; Dà Bǔ Yīn Wán.
TrapDo not confuse deficiency Fire with the four-greats excess pattern of Shí Gāo + Zhī Mǔ.
PreparationThe cold, bitter pair is normally embedded in a Yin-enriching formula rather than used as a stand-alone tonic.
SafetyUse cautiously with chronic loose stools, poor appetite or Spleen deficiency because the pair is bitter and cold.
