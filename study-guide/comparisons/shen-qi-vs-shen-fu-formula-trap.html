Shèn Qì Wán vs. Shēn Fù Tāng

Shèn 肾 means Kidney; Shēn 参 means ginseng—and the treatment intensity is very different.

Memory rule

AnchorKidney Pill warms chronic deficiency; Ginseng-Aconite is the collapse crash cart.

Decisive separation

FormulaArchitectureDecisive presentation
Shèn Qì Wán 肾气丸Liù Wèi core + Guì Zhī/Ròu Guì + processed Fù ZǐChronic Kidney Yang/Qi deficiency with water metabolism signs.
Shēn Fù Tāng 参附汤Rén Shēn + processed Fù ZǐDevastated Qi/Yang collapse with profuse sweat, cold limbs and faint pulse.

Exam and safety

Exam keyChronic Kidney deficiency versus acute collapse; character and tone prevent the mistake.
SafetyBoth use processed aconite. Collapse signs are a medical emergency; formula knowledge does not replace resuscitation.
