Wan Dai Tang

Dai vessel and Spleen deficiency with Dampness and constrained Liver Qi causing leukorrhea.

High-yield summary

CategoryStrengthen Spleen · Stop Vaginal Discharge
PatternDai vessel and Spleen deficiency with Dampness and constrained Liver Qi causing leukorrhea.
ActionStrengthens Spleen Qi, resolves Dampness, raises clear Yang and stops discharge.
Decisive clueCopious thin white vaginal discharge without strong odor, accompanied by fatigue, pallor and weak low back.

Ingredients

Bai Zhu, Shan Yao, Ren Shen, Bai Shao, Cang Zhu, Gan Cao, Chen Pi, Che Qian Zi, Chai Hu and Jing Jie Sui.

Commercial preparations and lineages may vary; always verify the source being used.

Differentiation and memory

Do not confuseYi Huang Tang treats yellow, sticky discharge from Damp-Heat with deficiency; Wan Dai Tang treats predominantly white discharge from deficiency and Dampness.
Memory cueWan Dai strengthens the belt vessel by rebuilding Spleen and drying the Damp discharge.

Safety

Foul odor, pelvic pain, fever, bleeding, pregnancy or possible sexually transmitted infection requires medical examination.
