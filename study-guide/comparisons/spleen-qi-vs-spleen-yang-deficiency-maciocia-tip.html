Spleen Qi Deficiency vs. Spleen Yang Deficiency

Recognize when weak transformation progresses to loss of warming and water movement.

Fast differentiation clues

ClueSpleen Qi DeficiencySpleen Yang Deficiency
SharedBoth cause poor appetite, fatigue, loose stools, abdominal weakness, pale tongue and a weak pulse.
Fast distinctionWeak digestion without pronounced Cold: fatigue, bloating and loose stool.Qi deficiency plus Cold: cold limbs, abdominal pain better with warmth/pressure, undigested food and edema.

Clinical table

CompareSpleen Qi DeficiencySpleen Yang Deficiency
TemperatureNeutral or mildly cold.Marked internal Cold.
Tongue / pulsePale with teeth marks; weak.Pale, swollen and wet; deep-slow-weak.
Formula associationSi Jun Zi Tang.Li Zhong Wan.

Exam and safety

Why it mattersSpleen Yang deficiency contains Spleen Qi deficiency plus Cold and often water retention.
Memory cueQi is weak; Yang is weak, cold and wet.
SafetyPersistent diarrhea, dehydration, weight loss, bleeding or sudden edema requires assessment.
