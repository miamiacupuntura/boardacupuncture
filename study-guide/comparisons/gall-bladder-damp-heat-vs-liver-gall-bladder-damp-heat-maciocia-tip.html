Gall Bladder vs. Liver–Gall Bladder Damp-Heat

Recognize when a Gall Bladder Damp-Heat picture extends into the Liver channel.

Fast differentiation clues

ClueGall Bladder Damp-HeatLiver–Gall Bladder Damp-Heat
SharedBoth cause hypochondriac fullness, bitter taste, nausea and a sticky yellow right-sided coating.
Fast distinctionGall Bladder-centered: digestive/biliary fullness and bitter taste dominate.Liver channel joins: genital rash/itching, dark urine, jaundice or stronger wiry irritability signs.

Clinical table

CompareGall Bladder Damp-HeatLiver–Gall Bladder Damp-Heat
Added pathwayGall Bladder and Shaoyang/biliary signs.Liver channel and lower-burner/genital signs join.
Formula associationYin Chen Hao-based strategy in the supplied table.Long Dan Xie Gan Tang.

Exam and safety

Why it mattersGenital/channel signs and stronger Liver manifestations identify the combined pattern.
Memory cueGall Bladder is bitter at the ribs; Liver–Gall Bladder also descends to the genitals.
SafetyJaundice, fever, severe pain, vomiting or genital lesions requires medical diagnosis rather than self-treatment.
