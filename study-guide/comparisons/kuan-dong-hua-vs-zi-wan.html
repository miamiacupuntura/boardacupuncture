Herb comparison

Kuan Dong Hua vs. Zi Wan

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueKuan Dong HuaZi Wan
Shared clueBoth are harmonious herbs that transform Phlegm, relieve cough and wheezing, and can be used for many types of cough.
Fast distinctionPrimarily moistens the Lungs and alleviates severe cough.More strongly opens, drains and directs Qi downward when Phlegm is difficult to expectorate.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersBoth can treat many cough patterns. Choose Kuan Dong Hua when cough severity and Lung moistening dominate; choose Zi Wan when downward drainage and expectoration are central.
Memory cueKuan Dong Hua calms the hard cough; Zi Wan opens and sends Phlegm downward.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), p. 441. Source Exam Keys: CMID 9007, questions 1–2; CMID 9132, questions 1–2.
