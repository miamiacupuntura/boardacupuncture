Formula comparison

Tao He Cheng Qi Tang vs. Tiao Wei Cheng Qi Tang

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueTao He Cheng Qi TangTiao Wei Cheng Qi Tang
Shared clueBoth contain Da Huang, Mang Xiao and Zhi Gan Cao and purge Heat downward, but only one targets Blood stasis.
Fast distinctionLower-burner Blood stasis with Heat: acute lower-abdominal pain/fullness, normal urination and agitation or gynecologic stasis signs.Yangming Heat and Dryness: constipation, thirst, irritability and a dry yellow tongue coating without defining Blood-stasis signs.

Comparative answer key

CompareTao He Cheng Qi TangTiao Wei Cheng Qi Tang
Primary pathologyBlood stasis with Heat.Bowel Heat and Dryness.
Distinct ingredientsTao Ren and Gui Zhi move Blood and open the lower burner.No Blood-moving pair; regulates Stomach and softens stool.
Decisive clueLower-abdominal stasis signs with normal urination.Dry constipation and Yangming Heat.
Exam shortcutCheng Qi plus Blood movement.Cheng Qi for dry stool.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersThe shared purgative trio can obscure the purpose. Tao He adds a Blood-stasis strategy; Tiao Wei remains focused on Heat-Dryness in the bowels.
Memory cueTao Ren marks Blood stasis; Tiao Wei marks dry Yangming stool.
SafetyBoth are strong purgative formulas and are inappropriate for self-treatment. Avoid in pregnancy; suspected obstruction, severe pain, vomiting or no passage of gas requires emergency assessment.
SourceSource bank: FWB 10.1, Comparison Formulas to Invigorate the Blood; corroborated with FWB 3.2 and individual profiles.
