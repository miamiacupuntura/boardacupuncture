You Gui Yin

Moderate Kidney Yang and Essence deficiency.

High-yield summary

CategoryTonify Kidney Yang and Essence
PatternModerate Kidney Yang and Essence deficiency.
ActionWarms Kidney Yang and replenishes Essence in a comparatively streamlined decoction.
Decisive clueColdness and low-back weakness with fatigue and urinary or sexual deficiency signs, but less severe depletion than You Gui Wan.

Ingredients

Shu Di Huang, Shan Zhu Yu, Shan Yao, Gou Qi Zi, Du Zhong, Zhi Gan Cao, Rou Gui and Zhi Fu Zi.

Commercial preparations and lineages may vary; always verify the source being used.

Differentiation and memory

Do not confuseYou Gui Wan includes additional Blood/Essence enrichers and is more potent; You Gui Yin is the moderate, simpler member of the pair.
Memory cueYou Gui Yin restores the Right with eight focused ingredients; the Wan adds more Essence-building weight.

Safety

Fu Zi requires correct processing and qualified supervision. Palpitations, numbness, vomiting or weakness after use requires urgent care.
