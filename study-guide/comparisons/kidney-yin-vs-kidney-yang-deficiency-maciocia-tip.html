Kidney Yin Deficiency vs. Kidney Yang Deficiency

Use temperature, fluids, tongue and pulse to separate the two foundational Kidney deficiency patterns.

Fast differentiation clues

ClueKidney Yin DeficiencyKidney Yang Deficiency
SharedBoth may cause low-back and knee weakness, tinnitus, dizziness, reproductive weakness and a deficient pulse.
Fast distinctionEmpty Heat and dryness: night sweating, dry mouth at night, five-center Heat and a red peeled tongue.Cold and weak transformation: cold back/limbs, abundant clear urine, edema and a pale wet tongue.

Clinical table

CompareKidney Yin DeficiencyKidney Yang Deficiency
Temperature / fluidsWarm at night, dry and scant fluids.Cold, wet and clear-abundant fluids.
Tongue / pulseRed, peeled; thin-rapid.Pale, swollen and wet; deep-slow-weak.
Formula associationZuo Gui Wan or Liu Wei Di Huang Wan.You Gui Wan or Jin Gui Shen Qi Wan.

Exam and safety

Why it mattersShared Kidney weakness does not decide the pattern; Heat-dry-red versus Cold-wet-pale does.
Memory cueYin deficiency is dry and hot; Yang deficiency is cold and wet.
SafetyBlood in urine, inability to urinate, severe flank pain, sudden weakness or major urinary change requires prompt medical assessment.

Review prompts

  • Which has night sweating?
  • Which has clear abundant urine?
  • How do the tongues differ?
