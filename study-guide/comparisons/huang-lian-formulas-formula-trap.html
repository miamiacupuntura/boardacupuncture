Huáng Lián Jiě Dú Tāng vs. Huáng Lián Ē Jiāo Tāng

One is a four-herb fire hose for excess toxin; the other reconnects Heart Fire above with depleted Yin below.

Memory rule

AnchorJiě Dú extinguishes excess; Ē Jiāo enriches what is empty.
