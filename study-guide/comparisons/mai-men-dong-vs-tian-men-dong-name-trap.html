Mài Mén Dōng 麦门冬 vs. Tiān Mén Dōng 天门冬

Both nourish Yin, but Mài is lighter and reaches Heart/Stomach while Tiān is colder and reaches Kidney.

Memory rule

AnchorTiān—Heaven—reaches all the way down to Kidney.

Correct identity

NameIdentityJob
Mài Mén Dōng 麦门冬Ophiopogonis RadixNourishes Lung, Stomach and Heart Yin; generates fluids.
Tiān Mén Dōng 天门冬Asparagi RadixMore strongly clears deficiency Heat and nourishes Lung-Kidney Yin.
