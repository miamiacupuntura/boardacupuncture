Exam focus and safety

CluesEarly fever with sore throat or hot, red, painful sores and boils.
Signature formulaYín Qiáo Sǎn.
TrapTheir shared toxin-clearing action does not replace drainage or medical treatment for a mature abscess.
PreparationThese light aromatic medicinals generally should not be subjected to unnecessarily prolonged boiling.
SafetyRapidly spreading redness, severe pain, high fever, streaking, facial infection or immune suppression requires medical evaluation.
