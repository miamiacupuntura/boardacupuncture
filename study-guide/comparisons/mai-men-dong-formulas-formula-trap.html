Formula trap 21 of 22

Mài Mén Dōng Tāng vs. Shā Shēn Mài Mén Dōng Tāng

One moistens Lung while descending rebellious Qi; the other restores Lung-Stomach fluids after warm dryness.

Memory rule

AnchorThe formula with Bàn Xià pushes rebellion down.

Decisive separation

FormulaArchitectureDecisive presentation
Mài Mén Dōng Tāng 麦门冬汤Large Mài Mén Dōng dose plus Bàn Xià, Rén Shēn, Gān Cǎo, Jīng Mǐ, Dà ZǎoLung atrophy/Stomach Yin injury with cough, retching or rebellious Qi.
Shā Shēn Mài Mén Dōng Tāng 沙参麦门冬汤Shā Shēn, Mài Dōng, Yù Zhú, Tiān Huā Fěn, Biǎn Dòu, Sāng Yè, Gān CǎoLung-Stomach dryness after warm-dry disease: dry cough, thirst and scant sputum.

Exam and safety

Exam keyRetching/rebellious Qi points to the Bàn Xià formula; post-warm dryness points to Shā Shēn version.
SafetyBoth are moistening and can aggravate Dampness or weak digestion; processed Bàn Xià is required in Mài Mén Dōng Tāng.
