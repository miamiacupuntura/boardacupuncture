Herb comparison

Alismatis Rhizoma (ze xie) vs. Polyporus (zhu ling)

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueZe XieZhu Ling
Shared cluesBoth enter the Kidney and Bladder, clear Heat, promote urination and treat edema with scanty, difficult urination.
Fast distinctionColder and better at clearing Heat, especially blazing ministerial Fire.More strongly facilitates removal of pathogenic Water.

Comparative answer key

CompareZe XieZhu Ling
Medicinal nameAlismatis Rhizoma.Polyporus.
Shared categoryHerbs that Drain Dampness.
Heat and Water emphasisCold and stronger at cooling Heat; especially indicated for blazing ministerial Fire.Stronger at facilitating removal of pathogenic Water.
Spermatorrhea clueDamp-Heat with Heat predominant, especially Heat disturbing the essence chamber from blazing ministerial Fire in the Kidney channel.Lower-burner Damp-Heat with Dampness predominant and turbid urine.
Water-removal mechanismDisseminates and unblocks Dampness in the internal organs.Separates and drains pathogenic influences from the exterior.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersBoth drain Water and may treat spermatorrhea, but Heat predominance and ministerial Fire favor Ze Xie; Dampness predominance, turbid urine and stronger Water removal favor Zhu Ling.
Memory cueZe Xie clears the Fire; Zhu Ling drains the Water.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), p. 274. Source Exam Keys: CMID 9121, questions 1–3; CMID 9126, questions 2–4.

Possible exam questions · Study prompts

  • Which Kidney-Bladder actions do both herbs share?
  • Which herb better cools blazing ministerial Fire?
  • Which herb more strongly removes pathogenic Water?
  • How do Heat-predominant and Dampness-predominant spermatorrhea distinguish them?
  • Which herb acts through the internal organs and which drains outward?
