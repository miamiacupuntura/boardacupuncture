Trap 1 of 22

Dà Jì 大蓟 vs. Dà Jǐ 大戟

One tone and one character separate a cooling hemostatic from a toxic harsh water-expellant.

Memory rule

Anchor蓟 has the grass radical and stops bleeding; 戟 is the weapon-like toxic expellant.

Correct identity

NameIdentityJob
Dà Jì 大蓟Cirsii japonici Herba / Japanese thistleCools Blood and stops bleeding; disperses stasis and reduces swellings.
Dà Jǐ 大戟Euphorbiae pekinensis RadixHarshly drives out water; toxic and used only under qualified supervision.

Exam and safety

Exam keyBleeding points to Dà Jì 大蓟. Severe retained fluid points to the very different Dà Jǐ 大戟.
Safety alertDà Jǐ 大戟 is toxic and traditionally incompatible with Gān Cǎo. Never substitute these names.
