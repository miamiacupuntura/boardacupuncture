Pattern comparison

Heart Fire vs. Heart Yin Deficiency

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueHeart FireHeart Yin Deficiency
Shared clueBoth disturb the Spirit and may cause insomnia, palpitations, anxiety, restlessness, a red tongue tip and rapid pulse.
Fast distinctionFull Fire: intense agitation, mouth/tongue ulcers, bitter taste, dark urine and a red tongue with yellow coat; pulse forceful-rapid.Yin deficiency: chronic insomnia, night sweating, five-center Heat, dry mouth and a red peeled tongue; pulse thin-rapid.

Comparative answer key

CompareHeart FireHeart Yin Deficiency
MechanismExcess Fire agitates Heart and Spirit.Heart Yin cannot cool and anchor the Spirit.
Mouth / urinePainful ulcers and dark scant urine.Dryness without strong ulcerative or urinary Fire.
Tongue / pulseRed tip/body with yellow coat; overflowing/forceful-rapid.Red tip, little coat/cracks; thin-rapid.
Formula examplesDao Chi San or Huang Lian Jie Du Tang by severity.Tian Wang Bu Xin Dan.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersInsomnia and red tip overlap. Ulcers, dark urine, coating and pulse force indicate Fire; night Heat, dryness and no coat indicate Yin deficiency.
Memory cueHeart Fire flares and sores; Heart Yin deficiency dries and wakes.
SafetyChest pain, fainting, sustained rapid/irregular heartbeat, mania, suicidality or severe sleep deprivation requires urgent medical assessment.
