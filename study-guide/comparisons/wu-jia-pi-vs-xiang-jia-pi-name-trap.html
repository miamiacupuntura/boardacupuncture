Trap 5 of 22

Wǔ Jiā Pí 五加皮 vs. Xiāng Jiā Pí 香加皮

Three similar market names hide different plants, actions and cardiac risk.

Memory rule

AnchorWǔ Jiā Pí strengthens; Xiāng Jiā Pí carries the cardiac-glycoside warning.

Correct identity

NameIdentityJob
Wǔ Jiā Pí / Nán Wǔ Jiā PíAcanthopanacis CortexExpels Wind-Damp, strengthens sinews/bones and supports Liver-Kidney.
Xiāng Jiā Pí / Běi Wǔ Jiā PíPeriplocae CortexStronger edema action but contains cardiac glycosides.
Cì Wǔ Jiā 刺五加Eleutherococci senticosi RadixA separate Qi-tonic/adaptogenic medicinal.

Exam and safety

Exam keyDo not collapse Nán Wǔ Jiā Pí, Běi Wǔ Jiā Pí and Cì Wǔ Jiā into one item.
Safety alertXiāng Jiā Pí may cause digoxin-like toxicity and has important cardiac-drug interactions.
