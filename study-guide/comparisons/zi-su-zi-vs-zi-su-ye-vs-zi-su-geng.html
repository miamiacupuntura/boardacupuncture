Herb comparison

Perillae Fructus (zi su zi) vs. Perillae Folium (zi su ye) vs. Perillae Caulis (zi su geng)

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueZi Su ZiZi Su YeZi Su Geng
Shared clueAll three regulate Qi, but each medicinal part has a different primary direction and category.
Fast distinctionFruit: directs Lung Qi downward, transforms Phlegm and moistens the Intestines.Leaf: releases the Exterior, harmonizes the Middle and stops nausea.Stem: unbinds Middle-Burner Qi and relieves chest and diaphragm obstruction.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersThe names share the Zi Su root, so the plant part identifies the action: fruit descends Lung Qi, leaf releases the Exterior and stem unbinds Qi in the chest and Middle Burner.
Memory cueSeed descends; leaf releases; stem unbinds.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), p. 445. Source Exam Keys: CMID 9131, questions 4–6; CMID 9133, questions 11–13.
