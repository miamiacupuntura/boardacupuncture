Pathology or symptom comparison

Cough Pattern Differentiation

Diagnostic orientation

Core ideaSputum color and quantity, dryness and exterior signs distinguish the main cough patterns.
Exam focusSputum, dryness, exterior signs and chronicity

Comparative differentiation

Pattern or presentationDecisive cluesRepresentative formula/herbStrategy
Wind-ColdScratchy throat, thin white sputum, chills, stuffy or runny noseSan Ao Tang or Zhi Sou SanDiffuse Lung Qi and dispel Wind-Cold.
Wind-HeatHeavy breathing, sticky yellow sputum, sore throat, thirst and sweatingSang Ju YinDispel Wind-Heat and diffuse the Lung.
Wind-DrynessDry cough, dry nose/throat, scant sputum or blood streaksSang Xing Tang; Xing Su San if Cold-DrynessCourse Wind and moisten the Lung.
Damp-PhlegmMorning cough, copious dilute white sputum, nausea and epigastric fullnessEr Chen Tang + San Zi Yang Qin TangStrengthen Spleen and transform Damp-Phlegm.
Liver Fire invading LungHacking cough linked to emotion, hypochondriac distention, bitter tasteXie Bai San + Dai Ge SanClear Liver Fire and drain the Lung.
Lung Yin deficiencyChronic dry cough, hoarseness, night sweats, tidal fever and scant blood-tinged sputumSha Shen Mai Dong TangNourish Yin and moisten Lung.

Study rule

How to distinguishWhite/thin suggests Cold, yellow/sticky suggests Heat, copious white suggests Damp-Phlegm, and scant dry sputum shifts attention to Dryness or Yin deficiency.
Memory cueCold—white; Heat—yellow; Dryness—parched; Damp—copious; Liver—emotion; Yin—chronic night Heat.
SafetyCoughing blood, shortness of breath, chest pain, low oxygen, unexplained weight loss or cough lasting more than three weeks requires medical evaluation.
