Appendix B pair · 24 of 51

Dāng Guī + Chuān Xiōng

当归 + 川芎

One picture, one job

PictureThe moving half of the Four Substances.
Combined jobNourish and invigorate Blood, regulate menstruation and stop pain from deficiency with stasis.
CategoryNourish and Invigorate Blood

How the pair works

HerbRole within the pair
Dāng GuīNourishes Blood while moving it gently and regulating menstruation.
Chuān XiōngMoves Blood and Qi more strongly and reaches upward to the head.

Exam focus and safety

CluesIrregular or painful periods, headache or postpartum pain with Blood deficiency and stasis.
Signature formulaFó Shǒu Sǎn; Sì Wù Tāng.
TrapDāng Guī supplies while Chuān Xiōng moves; too much movement is wrong when pure deficiency dominates.
PreparationUse within a balanced formula when deficiency and stasis coexist rather than treating all bleeding as stasis.
SafetyScreen warfarin, antiplatelets and bleeding disorders. Chuān Xiōng requires pregnancy caution; severe postpartum bleeding is an emergency.
