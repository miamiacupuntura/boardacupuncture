Bu Yang Huan Wu Tang

Qi deficiency with Blood stasis obstructing the channels, classically after stroke.

High-yield summary

CategoryTonify Qi · Invigorate Blood
PatternQi deficiency with Blood stasis obstructing the channels, classically after stroke.
ActionStrongly tonifies Qi, invigorates Blood and unblocks the channels.
Decisive cluePost-stroke hemiplegia or facial deviation with a pale tongue and weak pulse rather than an acute excess presentation.

Ingredients

Huang Qi in a very large dose, Dang Gui Wei, Chi Shao, Di Long, Chuan Xiong, Hong Hua and Tao Ren.

Commercial preparations and lineages may vary; always verify the source being used.

Differentiation and memory

Do not confuseTian Ma Gou Teng Yin addresses active Liver Yang/Wind; Bu Yang Huan Wu Tang addresses deficient Qi failing to move Blood in recovery.
Memory cueA large Huang Qi dose supplies the force; the Blood movers reopen the five tenths.

Safety

Any new facial droop, weakness, speech difficulty or severe headache is an emergency; this is not acute-stroke self-treatment.
