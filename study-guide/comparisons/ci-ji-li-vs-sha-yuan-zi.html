Herb comparison

Tribuli Fructus (ci ji li) vs. Astragali complanati Semen (sha yuan zi)

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueCi Ji LiSha Yuan Zi
Shared clueBoth may brighten the eyes and have historically confused names, but their appearance, plant part, category and actions differ.
Fast distinctionLarge spiny fruit; expels Wind and moves Liver constraint without tonifying.Tiny dark seed; tonifies Liver and Kidneys.

Comparative answer key

CompareCi Ji Li (Tribuli Fructus)Sha Yuan Zi (Astragali complanati Semen)
CategorySubstances that Extinguish Wind and Stop Tremors.Herbs that Tonify the Yang.
Part usedDried ripe fruit.Dried ripe seeds.
AppearanceLarge, light greenish-yellow, irregular and sharply protruding; “caltrop fruit.”Tiny brownish-black seeds with a light aroma.
Eye actionExpels Wind and dredges Liver constraint to brighten eyes; has no tonifying effect.Tonifies Liver and Kidneys to brighten eyes.
Name clueAlso called Bai Ji Li in the source.Historically called Bai Ji Li in some materia medica texts.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersHistorical naming overlap can imply equivalence, but the source stresses that the two substances are completely different.
Common confusionCi Ji Li is a spiny Wind-expelling fruit with no tonic effect; Sha Yuan Zi is a tiny Yang-tonifying seed.
Historical noteDuring the Song, Yuan and Ming dynasties, Bai Ji Li was sometimes used for Sha Yuan Zi, likely because the sprouts' leaves look similar.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), p. 803. Source Exam Key CMID 8923.
