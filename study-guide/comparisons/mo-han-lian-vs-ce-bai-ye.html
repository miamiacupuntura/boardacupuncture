Herb comparison

Ecliptae Herba (mo han lian) vs. Platycladi Cacumen (ce bai ye)

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueMo Han LianCe Bai Ye
Shared cluesBoth cool Blood, stop bleeding, and promote hair growth and color.
Cause of hair lossLiver and Kidney Yin deficiency.Blood Heat.

Comparative answer key

CompareMo Han Lian (Ecliptae Herba)Ce Bai Ye (Platycladi Cacumen)
CategoryHerbs that Tonify the Yin.Herbs that Regulate the Blood · Herbs that Stop Bleeding.
Properties/channelsCold, sweet and sour; Liver and Kidney.Bitter, astringent and slightly cold; Lung, Liver and Large Intestine.
Hair indicationNourishes Liver/Kidney Yin for graying hair or deficiency-related hair loss.Promotes hair growth when Blood Heat is the cause.
Associated signsDizziness and blurred vision from Liver/Kidney Yin deficiency.Blood-Heat bleeding, bloody cough or phlegm; may also transform phlegm and stop cough.
Formula clueEr Zhi Wan.Shi Hui San; Huai Hua San; Si Sheng Wan; Bai Ye Tang.
Bleeding overlapThe source includes vomiting blood, epistaxis, hematuria, bloody stool or uterine bleeding among their cooling and hemostatic indications.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersBoth can appear in bleeding and hair questions; the pattern behind the hair symptom separates them.
Common confusionYin-deficiency hair loss favors Mo Han Lian; Blood-Heat hair loss favors Ce Bai Ye.
Cautions in sourceMo Han Lian: Kidney/Spleen Deficiency Cold, Cold diarrhea or loose stools, and caution with Spleen Qi deficiency plus Food Stagnation. Ce Bai Ye: absence of Heat/Dampness, prolonged or high-dose nausea, vomiting or dizziness, deficiency bleeding with Blood Stasis, Spleen/Stomach Deficiency Cold, pregnancy and breastfeeding.
SafetyThis page records exam-source cautions and is not a prescribing or dosing guide.
Source question formatsQuestions 4–7 are Matching.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), p. 578. Source Exam Key CMID 8908.
