Herb comparison

Ginkgo Semen (bai guo) vs. Schisandrae Fructus (wu wei zi)

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueBai GuoWu Wei Zi
Shared clueBoth restrain the Lungs for cough or wheezing and address lower-burner leakage.
Fast distinctionLung-Heat wheezing with profuse Phlegm; lower-burner action is limited to turbid vaginal discharge from Dampness.Warmer and broader: chronic deficiency or Lung-Cold cough, plus Kidney, urinary, Essence, Intestinal, fluid and Spirit indications.

Comparative answer key

CompareBai GuoWu Wei Zi
Medicinal nameGinkgo Semen.Schisandrae Fructus.
CategoryHerbs that Stabilize and Bind.
Lung emphasisPrimarily restrains the Lungs to arrest wheezing.Primarily restrains the Lungs to stop cough.
Best respiratory patternWheezing from Lung Heat with profuse Phlegm.Chronic cough from Lung deficiency or cough and wheezing from Lung Cold with profuse Phlegm.
Lower-burner scopeLimited to turbid vaginal discharge, especially when Dampness predominates.Secures the lower burner and enriches Kidney Water for enuresis, urinary frequency, spermatorrhea and diarrhea.
Fluids, Heart and SpiritThe comparison does not emphasize these actions.Generates fluids and restrains Heart Qi for Qi-and-Yin injury with intense thirst, excessive sweating, irritability or insomnia.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersBoth restrain the Lungs and treat lower-burner pathology. Lung Heat with wheezing or Damp vaginal discharge favors Bai Guo; Lung Cold or deficiency with broader Kidney leakage and fluid-Spirit symptoms favors Wu Wei Zi.
Memory cueBai Guo binds Heat wheezing and discharge; Wu Wei Zi warms, secures Kidney leakage and calms the Spirit.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), p. 893. Source Exam Key CMID 9088, question 2.
