Herb comparison

Sang Piao Xiao vs. Fu Pen Zi

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueSang Piao XiaoFu Pen Zi
Shared clueBoth tonify, restrain and contain Kidney deficiency causing enuresis, urinary frequency, spermatorrhea or impotence.
Fast distinctionSalty and more strongly reinforces Yang.Sweet and neutral and more strongly enriches true Yin.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersThe indication list is nearly identical, so the test turns on whether the pattern calls for stronger Yang reinforcement or Yin nourishment.
Memory cueSang Piao Xiao supports Yang; Fu Pen Zi feeds Yin.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), p. 888. Source Exam Key CMID 8949, question 1.
