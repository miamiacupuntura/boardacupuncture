Formula comparison

Zhi Sou San vs. Ban Xia Bai Zhu Tian Ma Tang

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueZhi Sou SanBan Xia Bai Zhu Tian Ma Tang
Shared clueBoth regulate Lung Qi and transform Phlegm, but their defining symptoms and target systems are very different.
Fast distinctionCough dominates after an Exterior Wind-Cold invasion; the remaining pathogen constrains the Lungs.Dizziness, vertigo, nausea and headache dominate because Spleen deficiency generates Damp-Phlegm and internal Wind.

Comparative answer key

CompareZhi Sou SanBan Xia Bai Zhu Tian Ma Tang
Primary targetLungs and residual Exterior pathogen.Spleen, Liver and head.
Main symptomPersistent cough with thin sputum.Vertigo or dizziness with nausea and chest/epigastric fullness.
Therapeutic emphasisGently disperses Wind, disseminates Lung Qi and stops cough.Dries Dampness, transforms Phlegm, strengthens Spleen and extinguishes Wind.
Exam shortcutThe cold is fading; the cough remains.Phlegm rises with Wind to the head.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersA shared Phlegm label can distract from the clinical center: respiratory persistence versus neurologic dizziness generated by Damp-Phlegm.
Memory cueZhi Sou stops the leftover cough; Ban Xia–Tian Ma stops the spinning.
SafetySudden severe dizziness, new neurologic deficits, fainting, chest pain or coughing blood requires urgent medical assessment.
SourceSource bank: FWB 6.2 and FWB 6.2.1, Comparison Formulas to Transform Phlegm; corroborated with individual profiles.
