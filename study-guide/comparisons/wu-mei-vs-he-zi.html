Herb comparison

Wu Mei vs. He Zi

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueWu MeiHe Zi
Shared clueBoth are sour, binding herbs that restrain Lung Qi for chronic deficiency cough and enter Large Intestine for chronic diarrhea or dysentery.
Fast distinctionGenerates fluids, augments Stomach and expels roundworms.Directs Fire downward, benefits throat and restores or unbinds the voice.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersTheir shared sour binding actions cover the same Lung and Intestinal patterns, so the extra clue decides: fluids, Stomach or parasites indicate Wu Mei; throat, voice or downward-directed Fire indicate He Zi.
Memory cueWu Mei makes fluids and removes worms; He Zi helps throat and voice.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), p. 864. Source Exam Key CMID 8972, question 1.
