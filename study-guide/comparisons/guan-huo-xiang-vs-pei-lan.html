Herb comparison

Guan Huo Xiang vs. Pei Lan

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueGuan Huo XiangPei Lan
Shared clueBoth aromatically transform Dampness, dispel Summerheat and may be used together.
Fast distinctionMore dispersing; better when externally contracted Cold occurs during hot summer weather.Milder, more harmonious and less dispersing; superior for clearing foul turbidity.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersThe herbs share category and Summerheat-Dampness use. Stronger Exterior-Cold dispersal and vomiting control favor Guan Huo Xiang; mild harmony and removal of foulness favor Pei Lan.
Memory cueHuo Xiang disperses and stops vomiting; Pei Lan harmonizes and clears foulness.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), pp. 475 and 478. Source Exam Key CMID 8984, questions 1 and 3.
