Herb comparison

Kochiae Fructus (di fu zi) vs. Sophorae flavescentis Radix (ku shen)

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueDi Fu ZiKu Shen
Shared cluesBoth clear Damp-Heat, expel Wind, alleviate itching and facilitate urination. They may be combined for itchy skin or gynecological Damp-Heat and painful urinary dribbling.
Fast distinctionSuperior for expelling Wind, resolving Dampness and relieving itching.Bitter and cold; more powerfully clears Damp-Heat and treats Heat-toxin sores, dysentery or jaundice.

Comparative answer key

CompareDi Fu Zi (Kochiae Fructus)Ku Shen (Sophorae flavescentis Radix)
CategoryHerbs that Drain Dampness.Herbs that Clear Heat and Dry Dampness.
Shared actionsClear Damp-Heat, expel Wind, alleviate itching and facilitate urination.
Primary emphasisExpels Wind, resolves Dampness and alleviates itching more effectively.More powerfully clears Damp-Heat through its bitter, cold nature.
External clueItchy skin disorders and gynecological pruritus.Itchy sores caused by Heat toxin.
Internal cluePainful urinary dribbling from Damp-Heat in the Bladder.Dysentery and jaundice from overabundant Damp-Heat.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersThe two herbs share four actions and several indications, so relative strength and the dominant symptom are decisive.
Common confusionWind-Damp itching favors Di Fu Zi; stronger Damp-Heat, Heat toxin, dysentery or jaundice favors Ku Shen.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), p. 295. Source Exam Keys CMID 8930, questions 1–2, and CMID 9006, questions 6–7.
