Substance comparison

Glauberitum (han shui shi) vs. Gypsum fibrosum (shi gao)

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueHan Shui ShiShi Gao
Shared cluesBoth clear Excess Heat from the Yang-Brightness Qi level and may be combined for high fever, insatiable thirst and a large, forceful pulse.
Fast distinctionSalty quality, Kidney entry, edema and softening hardness.Lung entry, Heat cough or wheezing, and release of Heat from skin and muscles.

Comparative answer key

CompareHan Shui ShiShi Gao
Medicinal nameGlauberitum.Gypsum fibrosum.
CategoryHerbs that Clear Heat and Drain Fire.
Shared actionClear Yang-Brightness Qi-level Excess Heat.
Taste and channel clueSalty as well as acrid; enters the Kidneys.Acrid and sweet; also enters the Lungs.
Distinctive actionTreats edema and softens, mobilizes and disperses areas of hardness.Treats cough or wheezing from Lung Heat and releases pathogenic Heat from the skin and muscle layers.
Exam presentationBlazing Heat plus edema or hardness.Blazing Heat plus Lung symptoms or Heat lodged in skin and muscles.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersThe shared high fever and thirst do not separate them. Kidney, edema and hardness clues favor Han Shui Shi; Lung, cough, wheezing or muscle-layer clues favor Shi Gao.
Memory cueHan Shui Shi softens and drains edema; Shi Gao clears Lung and muscle Heat.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), p. 104. Source Exam Key CMID 9074, question 6.
