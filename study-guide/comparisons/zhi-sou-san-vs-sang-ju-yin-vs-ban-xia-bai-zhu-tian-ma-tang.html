Three Wind and Phlegm paths · FWB Transform Phlegm

Zhi Sou San vs. Sang Ju Yin vs. Ban Xia Bai Zhu Tian Ma Tang

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueZhi Sou SanSang Ju YinBan Xia Bai Zhu Tian Ma Tang
Shared clueAll may appear in questions using Wind, Phlegm or Lung-Qi language, but their disease location and dominant symptom differ.
Fast distinctionResidual external Wind-Cold: the acute illness is fading but cough persists.Early mild Wind-Heat: cough with mild fever, thirst or sore throat.Internal Wind-Phlegm: dizziness and vertigo dominate rather than cough.

Comparative answer key

CompareZhi Sou SanSang Ju YinBan Xia Bai Zhu Tian Ma Tang
Disease locationLung with a residual Exterior pathogen.Lung defensive level during early Wind-Heat.Spleen-Liver axis with Phlegm and internal Wind rising to the head.

Exam shortcutThe cold is leaving; cough remains.Warm cough is just beginning.The room is spinning.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersThe word “Wind” does not identify the formula. First decide whether the pathogen is residual Wind-Cold, new Wind-Heat or internally generated Wind carrying Phlegm upward.
Memory cueZhi Sou = leftover cough; Sang Ju = early hot cough; Ban Xia–Tian Ma = spinning.
SafetyShortness of breath, chest pain, coughing blood, high fever or sudden vertigo with neurologic deficits requires medical assessment.
