Misleading name 5 of 6

Xī Jiǎo Dì Huáng Tāng 犀角地黄汤

The historical title says rhinoceros horn, but modern lawful practice does not use rhino horn.

Name versus reality

What it suggestsA literal reading appears to require Xī Jiǎo (rhinoceros horn).
RealityShuǐ Niú Jiǎo (water-buffalo horn), with adjusted dosing where permitted, replaces the illegal/obsolete Xī Jiǎo alongside Shēng Dì, Chì Sháo and Mǔ Dān Pí.

How to answer

Decisive clueHeat entering nutritive/Blood levels with bleeding, rash, high fever or delirium.
MemoryKeep the historical formula name; replace the prohibited wildlife substance.

Safety

AlertNever source rhinoceros horn. High fever, delirium or significant bleeding is an emergency requiring biomedical care.
