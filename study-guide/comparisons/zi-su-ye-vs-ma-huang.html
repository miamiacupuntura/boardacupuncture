Herb comparison

Zi Su Ye vs. Ma Huang

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueZi Su YeMa Huang
Shared clueBoth are warm, acrid Exterior-releasing herbs that disperse Wind-Cold and induce sweating.
Fast distinctionAromatic and gentler; moves Lung-Spleen Qi, harmonizes the Middle and calms the fetus.Much stronger at sweating; treats Exterior excess, wheezing, Bi pain and Wind-Cold edema.

Comparative answer key

CompareZi Su YeMa Huang
Medicinal namePerillae Folium.Ephedrae Herba.
Properties and channelsWarm, acrid and aromatic; Lung and Spleen.Warm, acrid and slightly bitter; Lung and Urinary Bladder.
Relative strengthWeaker at dispersing and inducing sweat.Strongly disperses and induces sweating; not classified as an aromatic herb.
Distinctive actionsMoves chest and Stomach Qi for nausea, vomiting, morning sickness or seafood poisoning.Treats Wind-Cold Exterior excess, wheezing, Bi pain and edema.
Part usedDried leaf, sometimes with young branches.Dried herbaceous stems.
Dose and caution3–9 g; do not decoct for a long time.3–9 g; caution with deficiency sweating or wheezing, hypertension and insomnia.
Formula cluesXing Su San; Xiang Su San.Ma Huang Tang; Ge Gen Tang; Ma Xing Shi Gan Tang; Ding Chuan Tang.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersBoth release Wind-Cold, but Middle-Jiao Qi or pregnancy clues favor Zi Su Ye, while strong sweating, wheezing or edema favors Ma Huang.
Memory cueZi Su Ye moves Qi gently; Ma Huang forcefully opens the Exterior.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), p. 13. Source Exam Keys: CMID 9016, questions 1–5; CMID 9133, questions 1–5.
