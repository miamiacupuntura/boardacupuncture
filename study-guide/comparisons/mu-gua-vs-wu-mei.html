Herb comparison

Chaenomelis Fructus (mu gua) vs. Mume Fructus (wu mei)

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueMu GuaWu Mei
Shared cluesBoth are sour, astringent and warm.
Fast distinctionDredges and drains Damp-Heat from the Spleen, Stomach, sinews and bones.More broadly restrains and binds the Lungs and Intestines and drives parasites into hiding.

Comparative answer key

CompareMu Gua (Chaenomelis Fructus)Wu Mei (Mume Fructus)
CategoryHerbs that Dispel Wind-Dampness.Herbs that Stabilize and Bind.
Shared qualitiesSour, astringent and warm.
Primary emphasisDredges and drains Damp-Heat from the Spleen, Stomach, sinews and bones.Uses its restraining and astringent nature across several leakage patterns.
Lung and Intestine clueCan restrain depleted and dispersed primal Qi of the Spleen and Lungs, but is not equal to Wu Mei in the other listed binding patterns.Enters the Lungs to restrain and the Intestines to bind after Intestinal foulness has been expelled.
Sinew and parasite clueFocuses on Damp-Heat affecting the sinews and bones.Softens the sinews and bones and drives parasites into hiding.
Additional source cluesThe comparison centers on Damp-Heat drainage.The source also lists treatment of unhealthy flesh and toxic lesions and drawing out embedded splinters.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersThe shared sour, warm and astringent qualities can obscure different priorities: Mu Gua drains Damp-Heat, while Wu Mei more broadly stabilizes leakage and restrains.
Memory cueMu Gua drains Dampness; Wu Mei binds and restrains.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), p. 864. Source Exam Keys CMID 9023, questions 5–8, and CMID 9087, questions 1–3 and 5.
