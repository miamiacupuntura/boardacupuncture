Tian Ma Gou Teng Yin vs. Zhen Gan Xi Feng Tang

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueTian Ma Gou Teng YinZhen Gan Xi Feng Tang
Shared clueBoth calm the Liver, subdue ascendant Yang and extinguish internal Wind, often with headache, dizziness, tinnitus or elevated blood pressure.
Fast distinctionLiver-Yang rising with internal Wind and Heat: headache, dizziness, insomnia and irritability.More severe Yang ascendance and Wind with red face, intense vertigo, tremor, weakness or signs threatening Wind-stroke.

Comparative answer key

CompareTian Ma Gou Teng YinZhen Gan Xi Feng Tang
SeverityModerate to significant Liver-Yang/Wind pattern.Severe, forceful ascendance with greater risk of collapse into Wind-stroke.
Primary directionClears Heat, calms Liver Yang and extinguishes Wind.Heavily anchors Yang, extinguishes Wind and nourishes deficient Liver-Kidney Yin.
Decisive clueHeadache, dizziness, tinnitus, insomnia and a wiry-rapid pulse.Severe vertigo, red face, tremor, numbness or weakness with a forceful wiry pulse.
Exam shortcutHot, restless Liver-Yang pattern.Heavy anchoring for a dangerous upward surge.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersThe formulas overlap in diagnosis; severity and force distinguish them. Neurologic deficits are not merely a formula clue—they are an emergency sign.
Memory cueTian Ma calms the climb; Zhen Gan weighs down the surge.
SafetySudden severe headache, facial droop, one-sided weakness, speech difficulty, loss of balance or altered consciousness requires emergency stroke evaluation.
SourceSource bank: FWB 14.1 series, Comparison Formulas to Expel Wind; corroborated with the individual formula profiles.
