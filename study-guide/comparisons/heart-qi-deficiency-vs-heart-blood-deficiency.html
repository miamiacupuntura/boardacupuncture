Pattern comparison

Heart Qi Deficiency vs. Heart Blood Deficiency

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueHeart Qi DeficiencyHeart Blood Deficiency
Shared clueBoth may cause palpitations, fatigue, anxiety, pale complexion and a weak pulse.
Fast distinctionFunction is weak: palpitations worse with exertion, shortness of breath, spontaneous sweating and general fatigue.Nourishment is weak: insomnia, dream-disturbed sleep, poor memory, dizziness and emotional unease with a pale thin tongue.

Comparative answer key

CompareHeart Qi DeficiencyHeart Blood Deficiency
Primary failureHeart Qi cannot propel and govern effectively.Heart Blood cannot nourish and house the Spirit.
Decisive clueExertional breathlessness and sweating.Insomnia, forgetfulness and dizziness.
Tongue / pulsePale; weak, possibly irregular.Pale and thin/dry; thin or choppy.
Formula examplesZhi Gan Cao Tang or Sheng Mai San by presentation.Gui Pi Tang or Suan Zao Ren Tang by root pattern.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersPalpitations overlap. Exertional functional weakness points to Qi; sleep, memory and undernourished Spirit signs point to Blood.
Memory cueHeart Qi cannot pump; Heart Blood cannot house Shen.
SafetyChest pain, fainting, sustained irregular rhythm, severe breathlessness or new palpitations requires prompt medical assessment.

Possible exam questions · Study prompts
