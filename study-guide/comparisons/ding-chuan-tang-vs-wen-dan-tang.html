Formula comparison

Ding Chuan Tang vs. Wen Dan Tang

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueDing Chuan TangWen Dan Tang
Shared clueBoth clear Phlegm-Heat, regulate Qi and may present with chest oppression, a greasy yellow coating and a slippery-rapid pulse.
Fast distinctionLung Phlegm-Heat with an Exterior constraint: wheezing, cough and thick yellow sputum dominate.Gallbladder-Stomach Phlegm-Heat disturbing the Spirit: insomnia, irritability, easy fright, nausea and bitter taste dominate.

Comparative answer key

CompareDing Chuan TangWen Dan Tang
Primary targetLungs and Exterior.Gallbladder, Stomach and Spirit.
Qi dynamicDescends rebellious Lung Qi to arrest wheezing.Harmonizes Stomach, regulates Gallbladder Qi and settles the Spirit.
Decisive clueSevere wheezing with abundant thick sputum.Insomnia or easy fright with bitter taste and nausea.
Exam shortcutHot Phlegm blocks breathing.Hot Phlegm disrupts sleep and digestion.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersThe shared Phlegm-Heat mechanism is localized differently. Respiratory obstruction points to Ding Chuan; psycho-digestive disturbance points to Wen Dan.
Memory cueDing Chuan arrests the wheeze; Wen Dan settles the disturbed Gallbladder.
SafetyDing Chuan Tang contains Ma Huang and requires cardiovascular and medication screening. Severe breathing difficulty is an emergency.
SourceSource bank: FWB 9.1.1, Comparison Formulas to Regulate the Qi; corroborated with FWB 6.2 and individual profiles.
