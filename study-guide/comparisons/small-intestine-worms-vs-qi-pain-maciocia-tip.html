Small Intestine Worm Infestation vs. Qi Pain

Distinguish suspected infestation from a functional Qi-movement pain pattern.

Fast differentiation clues

ClueWorm InfestationSmall Intestine Qi Pain
SharedBoth may cause recurrent abdominal pain, distention, borborygmi and appetite disturbance.
Fast distinctionInfestation requires exposure/systemic clues and confirmation: weight change, sallow color, rectal itching or stool findings.Qi Pain is defined by twisting, mobile pain and a deep wiry pulse without parasite evidence.

Exam and safety

Why it mattersA symptom pattern should not be labeled parasitic without evidence.
Memory cueWorms need proof; Qi Pain needs a movement pattern.
SafetyPersistent pain, blood, fever, anemia, weight loss or suspected parasites requires clinician-directed testing.
