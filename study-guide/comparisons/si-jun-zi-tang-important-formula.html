Si Jun Zi Tang

Spleen/Stomach Qi deficiency.

High-yield summary

CategoryTonify Spleen and Stomach Qi
PatternSpleen/Stomach Qi deficiency.
ActionTonifies Qi, strengthens the Spleen and supports transformation and transportation.
Decisive cluePale face, low voice, reduced appetite, loose stools, fatigue and a weak pulse.

Ingredients

Ren Shen, Bai Zhu, Fu Ling and Zhi Gan Cao.

Commercial preparations and lineages may vary; always verify the source being used.

Differentiation and memory

Do not confuseShen Ling Bai Zhu San adds Damp-draining and diarrhea-stopping herbs; Si Jun Zi Tang is the foundational four-herb Qi tonic.
Memory cueFour Gentlemen: tonify, strengthen, drain Dampness and harmonize.

Safety

Persistent fatigue or appetite loss may reflect anemia, infection, endocrine, gastrointestinal or malignant disease and warrants evaluation.
