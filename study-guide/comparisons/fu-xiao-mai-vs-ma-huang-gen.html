Herb comparison

Fu Xiao Mai vs. Ma Huang Gen

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueFu Xiao MaiMa Huang Gen
Shared clueBoth stop sweating and are often combined for night sweats or spontaneous sweating.
Fast distinctionAugments Qi and eliminates Heat while stopping sweating.Only restrains outward dispersal; it does not support normal Qi or clear Heat.

Comparative answer key

CompareFu Xiao MaiMa Huang Gen
Medicinal nameTritici Fructus levis.Ephedrae Radix.
CategoryHerbs that Stabilize and Bind.Herbs that Stabilize and Bind.

Additional benefitSupports normal Qi, cools the Heart and reduces Heat.No ability to support normal Qi, cool the Heart or reduce Heat.
Shared indicationsNight sweats and spontaneous sweating.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersBoth can stop the same sweating symptoms, but only Fu Xiao Mai addresses deficiency and Heat while restraining sweat.
Memory cueFu Xiao Mai supports and cools; Ma Huang Gen simply closes.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), p. 895. Source Exam Key CMID 8951, question 1.
