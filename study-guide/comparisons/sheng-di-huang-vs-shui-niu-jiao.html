Herb comparison

Rehmanniae Radix (sheng di huang) vs. Bubali Cornu (shui niu jiao)

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueSheng Di HuangShui Niu Jiao
Shared cluesBoth clear Heat and cool the Blood and are often combined for excess Heat in the Blood.
Fast distinctionBetter when Blood Heat is accompanied by Yin or Blood deficiency.Preferred when excess Blood Heat and toxicity predominate.

Comparative answer key

CompareSheng Di HuangShui Niu Jiao
Medicinal nameRehmanniae Radix.Bubali Cornu.
Common categoryHerbs that Clear Heat and Cool the Blood.
Shared actionClear excess Heat from the Blood level and cool the Blood.
Primary emphasisCools Blood while enriching Yin and Blood.Strongly addresses Blood Heat with toxicity.
Decisive clueBlood Heat plus Yin or Blood deficiency.Blood Heat and toxicity predominating.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersThe distinction is whether the Blood-Heat pattern includes deficiency requiring nourishment or excess toxicity requiring stronger clearing.
Memory cueSheng Di Huang cools and nourishes; Shui Niu Jiao cools toxicity.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), p. 122. Source Exam Key CMID 9060, question 2, and CMID 9071, question 2.

Possible exam questions · Study prompts

  • Which category and actions do both medicinals share?
  • Which is preferred when Yin or Blood deficiency accompanies Blood Heat?
  • Which is preferred when toxicity predominates?
  • Why are the two often combined?
