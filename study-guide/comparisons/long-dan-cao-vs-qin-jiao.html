Herb comparison

Long Dan Cao vs. Qin Jiao

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueLong Dan CaoQin Jiao
Shared clueBoth are Gentiana species that eliminate Dampness, reduce Heat and treat Damp-Heat jaundice.
Fast distinctionVery bitter and very cold; drains Excess Fire from the Liver and clears lower-burner Damp-Heat.Not overly drying; expels Wind-Dampness for painful obstruction and treats steaming-bone tidal fever.

Comparative answer key

CompareLong Dan CaoQin Jiao
Medicinal nameGentianae Radix.Gentianae Macrophyllae Radix.
CategoryHerbs that Clear Heat and Dry Dampness.Herbs that Dispel Wind-Dampness.
Drying and temperature clueVery bitter and very cold.Expels Wind-Dampness without being overly drying.

Distinctive indicationsRed eyes, vertigo, tinnitus, Liver-Heat spasms, or lower-burner vaginal swelling and itching.Steaming-bone disorder with tidal fever.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersThe same Gentiana family and jaundice indication can hide different categories: intense Liver and lower-burner Heat clearing versus gentle Wind-Damp obstruction treatment.
Memory cueLong Dan Cao drains fierce Liver Fire; Qin Jiao frees painful Wind-Dampness.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), p. 354. Source Exam Keys CMID 8999, questions 2 and 4–6, and CMID 9033, questions 1 and 8–10.
