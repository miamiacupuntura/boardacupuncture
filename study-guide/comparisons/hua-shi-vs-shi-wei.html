Substance comparison

Talcum (hua shi) vs. Pyrrosiae Folium (shi wei)

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueHua ShiShi Wei
Shared clueBoth are cold, drain Dampness and unblock hot or stony painful urinary dribbling.
Fast distinctionSlippery Dampness facilitation plus Summerheat and thirst.Bleeding in the urine, or Lung Heat with cough.

Comparative answer key

CompareHua ShiShi Wei
Botanical or medicinal nameTalcum.Pyrrosiae Folium.
CategoryHerbs that Drain Dampness.Herbs that Drain Dampness.

Primary emphasisSlippery and facilitates Dampness; especially suited to Damp-Heat or stony painful urinary dribbling.Cools Blood and stops bleeding; especially suited to painful urinary dribbling with bleeding.
Additional actionCools Summerheat and alleviates thirst.Cools Lungs, stops cough and alleviates bleeding.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersBoth may appear in hot painful urination, but Summerheat and Dampness favor Hua Shi, while hematuria or Lung cough identifies Shi Wei.
Memory cueHua Shi drains slippery Dampness; Shi Wei stops urinary Blood and Lung cough.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), p. 293. Source Exam Key CMID 8989, questions 8–9, and CMID 9069, questions 1–2.
