Formula comparison

Wu Ling San vs. Zhen Wu Tang

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueWu Ling SanZhen Wu Tang
Shared clueBoth warm and transform water metabolism and may treat edema, urinary difficulty, dizziness or palpitations.
Fast distinctionBladder Qi transformation fails, often in a Taiyang pattern; thirst and urinary difficulty dominate.Spleen-Kidney Yang deficiency is deeper; Cold, edema, heaviness and loose stools dominate.

Comparative answer key

CompareWu Ling SanZhen Wu Tang
DepthFunctional water regulation at Bladder/Taiyang level.Constitutional Yang deficiency with retained water.
Primary actionPromotes urination and transforms Qi.Warms Yang and promotes urination.
Decisive clueThirst but fluids are not transformed properly.Cold limbs, abdominal pain, heaviness and weakness.
Exam shortcutWater gate will not open.Fire is too weak to move water.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersBoth drain water, but Zhen Wu Tang is chosen when the warming/tonifying need is deeper and more systemic.
Memory cueWu Ling opens transformation; Zhen Wu rekindles Yang to move water.
SafetyZhen Wu Tang contains Fu Zi and requires processed material and professional supervision. Acute urinary retention or breathlessness with edema needs urgent care.
