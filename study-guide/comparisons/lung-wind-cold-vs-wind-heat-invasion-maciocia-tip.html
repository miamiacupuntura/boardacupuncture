Lung Invasion by Wind-Cold vs. Wind-Heat

Use chills, throat, discharge and pulse speed to separate the two common Exterior invasions.

Fast differentiation clues

ClueWind-Cold Invading LungWind-Heat Invading Lung
SharedBoth begin acutely with cough, fever, aversion to wind/cold, headache and a floating pulse.
Fast distinctionCold dominates: severe chills, mild fever, no thirst, clear discharge and floating-tight pulse.Heat dominates: fever stronger, chills mild, sore throat, thirst, yellow discharge and floating-rapid pulse.

Clinical table

CompareWind-Cold Invading LungWind-Heat Invading Lung
SecretionsClear, thin and white.Yellower, thicker or scant.
Tongue / pulseThin white coat; floating-tight.Red tip/edges, thin coat; floating-rapid.
Formula associationMa Huang Tang or Xiao Qing Long Tang when water signs fit.Sang Ju Yin for mild cough or Yin Qiao San for stronger throat signs.

Exam and safety

Why it mattersBoth are Exterior; temperature and secretion quality determine Cold versus Heat.
Memory cueCold is clear and tight; Heat is sore and rapid.
SafetyBreathing difficulty, chest pain, confusion, dehydration or low oxygen requires urgent care.
