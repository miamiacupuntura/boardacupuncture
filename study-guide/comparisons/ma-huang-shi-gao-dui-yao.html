How the pair works

HerbRole within the pair
Má HuángOpens and diffuses constrained Lung Qi.
Shí GāoClears blazing Lung Heat and restrains the sweating tendency when used in the larger dose.

Exam focus and safety

CluesFever, thirst, cough and wheezing with Heat signs, with or without sweating.
Signature formulaMá Xìng Shí Gān Tāng; Dà Qīng Lóng Tāng.
TrapDo not label the pair simply warming because it contains Má Huáng; the larger Shí Gāo dose redirects it toward Lung Heat.
PreparationCrush Shí Gāo and decoct it first.
SafetyMá Huáng requires cardiovascular and drug-interaction screening. Severe wheezing or breathing difficulty is an emergency.

Complete formula, Duì Yào, or formula + pair?

Use the complete formula when the case reproduces a full pattern architecture with root, branches, and protection. Consider the pair for a narrow objective; use it as a modification when a base formula fits but lacks one function. The pair does not automatically replace the formula.
