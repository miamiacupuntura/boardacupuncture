Herb comparison

Glehniae/Adenophorae Radix (sha shen) vs. Lilii Bulbus (bai he)

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueSha ShenBai He
Shared cluesBoth nourish Yin, clear the Lungs, moisten Dryness and stop dry cough.
Fast distinctionBetter augments the Stomach, generates fluids, stops thirst and expels phlegm.Moistens the Lungs for deficiency cough with blood, clears the Heart and quiets the Spirit.

Comparative answer key

CompareSha ShenBai He
Medicinal nameGlehniae/Adenophorae Radix.Lilii Bulbus.
Common categoryHerbs that Tonify the Yin.
Shared actionsNourish Yin, clear the Lungs, moisten Dryness and stop cough.
Stomach and fluidsBetter augments the Stomach, generates fluids and stops thirst.Primarily emphasizes Lung and Heart rather than Stomach-fluid generation.
Lung distinctionClears the Lungs and expels phlegm to stop cough.Moistens the Lungs and treats cough with blood from deficiency consumption.
Heart and SpiritNot its distinguishing action.Clears the Heart and quiets the Spirit.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersBoth treat dry cough from Yin injury. Thirst, Stomach fluids or phlegm point to Sha Shen; cough with blood, Heart Heat or Spirit disturbance point to Bai He.
Memory cueSha Shen supports Stomach fluids and expels phlegm; Bai He supports Lung, Heart and Spirit.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), p. 835. Source Exam Key CMID 9058, question 2.
