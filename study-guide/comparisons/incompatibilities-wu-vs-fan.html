Chapter 4 of 7

Xiāng Wù vs. Xiāng Fǎn

The decisive issue is reduced efficacy versus increased danger.

Answer key

Xiāng Wù 相恶Mutual antagonism/aversion: one herb reduces another’s therapeutic effect. Think wasted treatment, not a newly toxic pair.
Xiāng Fǎn 相反Mutual incompatibility: the traditional combination is considered capable of producing toxicity or serious adverse effects.

Classic contrast

ExampleClassificationReason
Rén Shēn + Lái Fú ZǐXiāng WùQi supplementation is opposed by an herb that breaks and descends Qi.
Wū Tóu/Fù Zǐ + Bàn XiàXiāng FǎnBelongs to the traditional Shí Bā Fǎn forbidden groups.

Translation warning

English sources vary between “antagonism,” “incompatibility,” “aversion” and “opposition.” On exams, anchor to the Chinese term and mechanism: Wù = diminished effect; Fǎn = harmful combination.
