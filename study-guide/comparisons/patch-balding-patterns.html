Pathology or symptom comparison

Patch Balding Pattern Differentiation

Diagnostic orientation

Core ideaOnset and systemic signs distinguish the two historical presentations of patchy hair loss.
Exam focusSudden itchy patches versus gradual loss with constraint and stasis signs

Comparative differentiation

Pattern or presentationDecisive cluesRepresentative formula/herbStrategy
Wind with dryness of BloodSudden round/irregular patches with scalp itch, dizziness, insomnia, pale tongue and weak-thready pulseShen Ying Yang Zhen TangNourish Blood and dispel Wind.
Qi stagnation and Blood stasisGradual prolonged loss with dull complexion, chest/hypochondriac distention, dark tongue and stasis spotsXiao Yao San + Tong Qiao Huo Xue TangRegulate Qi and invigorate Blood.

Study rule

How to distinguishSudden itchy patches with pale deficiency signs indicate Blood dryness/Wind; chronic loss with constraint and purple stasis signs indicates Qi-Blood obstruction.
Memory cueSudden, itchy and pale—Blood-Wind; gradual, constrained and purple—Qi-Blood stasis.
SafetyPatchy hair loss needs evaluation for alopecia areata, fungal infection, thyroid disease, nutritional deficiency and medication effects. Scaling, inflammation, scarring or rapid widespread loss needs prompt dermatology review.
