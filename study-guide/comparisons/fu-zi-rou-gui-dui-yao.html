Exam focus and safety

CluesCold low back and knees, frequent clear urine, impotence and chronic lower-burner Cold.
Signature formulaYòu Guī Wán; later versions of Shèn Qì Wán.
TrapCompared with Fù Zǐ + Gān Jiāng, this pair treats chronic Kidney-Yang deficiency rather than acute collapse.
PreparationUse prepared Fù Zǐ with required pre-decoction; add Ròu Guì late or use it as powder when directed.
SafetyFù Zǐ is toxic and the pair is contraindicated in pregnancy. Classical 19 Wèi: Ròu Guì is incompatible with Chì Shí Zhī.

Complete formula, Duì Yào, or formula + pair?

Use the complete formula when the case reproduces a full pattern architecture with root, branches, and protection. Consider the pair for a narrow objective; use it as a modification when a base formula fits but lacks one function. The pair does not automatically replace the formula.
