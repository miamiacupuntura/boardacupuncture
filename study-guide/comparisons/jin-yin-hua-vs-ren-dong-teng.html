Plant-part comparison

Jin Yin Hua vs. Ren Dong Teng

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueJin Yin HuaRen Dong Teng
Shared clueBoth are sweet and cold, enter the Large Intestine, Lung and Stomach, and clear Heat while resolving toxicity.
Fast distinctionThe flower is stronger at cooling and resolving toxicity.The stem is weaker for toxicity but expels Wind-Heat from channels, unblocks collaterals, soothes sinews and stops pain.

Comparative answer key

CompareJin Yin HuaRen Dong Teng
Medicinal name and plant partLonicerae Flos; flower.Lonicerae Caulis; stem.
Shared categoryHerbs that Clear Heat and Resolve Toxicity.

Primary indicationsExternally contracted Wind-Heat, all stages of warm disease, and hot painful sores or swellings.Wind-Heat painful obstruction, abscesses, sores, breast inflammation and Blood Heat.
Bleeding clueThe charred flower cools Blood and stops dysenteric bleeding from Heat toxin.The source associates the stem with early periods or excessive bleeding from Blood Heat.
Additional source-specific clueThe source also assigns deficiency tonification, Blood enrichment and thirst relief.The source assigns adjunct gynecological use for hot abdominal pain and breast inflammation.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersThe same plant provides a flower and a stem with shared Heat-toxin actions. Stronger toxicity clearing and warm-disease clues favor the flower; channel pain, sinews and Wind-Heat Bi favor the stem.
Memory cueThe flower clears toxicity; the stem travels through channels.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), p. 152. Source Exam Key CMID 8992, questions 2–5.
