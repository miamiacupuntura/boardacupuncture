Herb comparison

Apocyni veneti Folium (luo bu ma ye) vs. Gastrodiae Rhizoma (tian ma)

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueLuo Bu Ma YeTian Ma
Shared clueBoth calm and restrain the Liver.
Fast distinctionCool; clears Heat, promotes urination and is associated with elevated blood pressure.Neutral; extinguishes Wind, stops spasms and unblocks collaterals to relieve pain.

Comparative answer key

CompareLuo Bu Ma YeTian Ma
Medicinal nameApocyni veneti Folium.Gastrodiae Rhizoma.
CategorySubstances that Calm the Liver and Extinguish Wind.
Nature and tasteSweet, bitter and cool.Sweet and neutral.
Primary emphasisCalms the Liver, clears Heat and traditionally reduces elevated blood pressure.Extinguishes Wind and stops spasms.
Fluid or collateral actionPromotes urination; used for Damp-Heat edema with urinary difficulty.Dispels Wind and unblocks collaterals to stop pain.
Decisive indicationLiver disturbance with Heat, edema or difficult urination.Spasms, Wind-Damp painful obstruction or numbness of the limbs.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersBoth calm the Liver, but Heat, edema and urination clues identify Luo Bu Ma Ye; spasms, painful obstruction and limb numbness identify Tian Ma.
Memory cueLuo Bu Ma Ye cools and drains; Tian Ma stops Wind and unblocks.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), p. 977. Source Exam Key CMID 9078, question 2.
