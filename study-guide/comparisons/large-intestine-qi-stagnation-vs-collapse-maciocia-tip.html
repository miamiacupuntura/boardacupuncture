Batch 8 · Large Intestine · Tip 4 of 10

Large Intestine Qi Stagnation vs. Collapse

Separate obstructed bowel movement from deficient Qi failing to hold the rectum.

Fast differentiation clues

ClueLarge Intestine Qi StagnationLarge Intestine Collapse
SharedBoth reflect disordered Large Intestine Qi and may cause difficult or abnormal defecation.
Fast distinctionStagnation: distention, pain and small bitty stools from blocked movement.Collapse: chronic diarrhea, bearing down and rectal prolapse from weak sinking Qi.

Clinical table

CompareLarge Intestine Qi StagnationLarge Intestine Collapse
MechanismQi cannot move through.Qi cannot lift and hold.
Formula associationChai Hu Shu Gan Tang.Bu Zhong Yi Qi Tang.

Exam and safety

Why it mattersPainful distention signals obstruction; prolapse and bearing down signal sinking deficiency.
Memory cueStagnant Qi will not move; collapsed Qi will not hold.
SafetyNew prolapse, severe pain, rectal bleeding, fever or inability to pass stool/gas requires examination.
