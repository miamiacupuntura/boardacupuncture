Pathology or symptom comparison

Asthma Pattern Differentiation

Diagnostic orientation

Core ideaFirst decide whether the attack is acute excess or chronic deficiency; then use sputum and constitutional signs.
Exam focusAcute excess versus chronic deficiency

Comparative differentiation

Pattern or presentationDecisive cluesRepresentative formula/herbStrategy
Wind-Cold constraining LungAcute wheeze, chest oppression, thin white or foamy sputum and floating-tight pulseMa Huang TangRelease Wind-Cold and ventilate Lung.
Phlegm-Heat in LungLoud wheeze, thick yellow sputum, fever, thirst and slippery-rapid pulseSang Bai Pi TangClear Lung Heat and transform Phlegm.
Lung Qi deficiencyWeak chronic wheeze, feeble voice, spontaneous sweating and sensitivity to draftsSheng Mai Yin + Bu Fei TangSupplement Lung Qi and stabilize breathing.
Kidney deficiency failing to grasp QiPersistent exertional wheeze, shallow inhalation, weak back and cold limbsJin Gui Shen Qi WanWarm Kidney and assist grasping of Qi.

Study rule

How to distinguishAcute exterior signs indicate Wind-Cold; yellow phlegm indicates Heat; weak voice/sweat indicates Lung deficiency; difficulty inhaling with lumbar weakness indicates Kidney deficiency.
Memory cueCold—acute white; Heat—acute yellow; Lung—weak exhale; Kidney—cannot inhale deeply.
SafetySevere breathlessness, inability to speak full sentences, blue lips, altered consciousness or poor response to rescue medication is an emergency. Do not replace prescribed inhalers.
