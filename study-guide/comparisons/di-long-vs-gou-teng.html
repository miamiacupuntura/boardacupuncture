Herb comparison

Di Long vs. Gou Teng

Study tip · Both cool Heat-Wind

ClueDi LongGou Teng
Shared cluesBoth are cold Liver-Wind medicines for tremor or convulsion with Heat signs.
Fast distinctionAnimal medicine that tunnels through collaterals: paralysis, numbness, fixed pain, wheezing or difficult urination.Gentler hooked twig centered on Liver Heat: dizziness, headache, irritability, red eyes and febrile childhood convulsion.

Comparative answer key

CompareDi LongGou Teng
Medicinal namePheretima.Uncariae Ramulus Cum Uncis.
Nature and tasteSalty and cold.Sweet and cool.
Primary routeClears Heat, extinguishes Wind and penetrates blocked channels.Clears Liver Heat, extinguishes Wind and calms rising Liver Yang.
Decisive clueHemiplegia, facial paralysis, stiff painful numb limbs or deep collateral obstruction.Heat-centered dizziness/headache, red eyes, irritability or childhood fever.
Additional actionsCalms wheezing and promotes urination.Calms the Pericardium and is less harsh for common Heat-Wind patterns.
Yang index60 for Heat-Wind; 50 for opening collaterals.70 for cooling Liver Heat and extinguishing Wind.

Formula boundary and safety

Current rosterGou Teng is co-chief in Tian Ma Gou Teng Yin. Di Long is not chief or co-chief in the current 63-formula roster.
PreparationGou Teng is added near the end; Di Long requires authenticated, quality-controlled animal material.
Emergency boundaryFirst seizure, sudden weakness, facial droop, speech difficulty or loss of consciousness requires emergency services.
