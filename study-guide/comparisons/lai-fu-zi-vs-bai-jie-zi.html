Herb comparison

Lai Fu Zi vs. Bai Jie Zi

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueLai Fu ZiBai Jie Zi
Shared clueBoth excel at reducing Phlegm in cough and wheezing.
Fast distinctionGentle and neutral; works within the organs and may be used in either hot or cold disorders.Warm and extremely drying; reaches the channels, collaterals and Phlegm between skin and membranes, but only for Cold-Phlegm.

Comparative answer key

CompareLai Fu ZiBai Jie Zi
Medicinal nameRaphani Semen; dry-fried in the source comparison.Sinapis Semen.
CategoryHerbs that Relieve Food Stagnation.Warm Herbs that Transform Cold-Phlegm.
NatureGentle and neutral.Warm and extremely drying.
Level of actionPrimarily acts within the organs: Lungs, Spleen and Large Intestine.Primarily acts at the channels and collaterals, including between the skin and membranes.
Qi actionRegulates organ Qi dynamics, dispels fullness and eliminates Food Stagnation.Promotes Qi dynamics in the channels and collaterals.
Thermal useMay be used for either hot or cold disorders.Use only for Cold-Phlegm; inappropriate for Phlegm-Heat.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersBoth reduce Phlegm and treat cough, but temperature and anatomical level decide the answer: neutral organ-level regulation versus strongly warming channel-level dispersion.
Memory cueLai Fu Zi works inside and tolerates hot or cold; Bai Jie Zi reaches outward and requires Cold-Phlegm.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), p. 503. Source Exam Key CMID 9008, questions 1–3.
