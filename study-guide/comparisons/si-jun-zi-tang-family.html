Si Jun Zi Tang Family

Root formula and architecture

RootSi Jun Zi Tang is the foundational Spleen-Qi formula: supplement Qi, strengthen the Spleen and support fluid transformation.
ArchitectureCore: Ren Shen, Bai Zhu, Fu Ling and Zhi Gan Cao. The family changes by adding Phlegm transformation, Qi movement, Damp drainage, warming or lifting.

Study rule

How to chooseStart with Spleen-Qi deficiency, then identify the complication: Phlegm, stagnation, Damp diarrhea, Cold or sinking.
Memory cueFour tonify; Six transform Phlegm; Xiang Sha moves; Shen Ling drains; Li Zhong warms; Bu Zhong lifts.
SafetyTonifying formulas can trap an unresolved pathogen or worsen Food Stagnation. Processed Ban Xia is required in formulas that contain it.
