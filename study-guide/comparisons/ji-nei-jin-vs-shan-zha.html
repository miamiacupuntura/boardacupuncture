Herb comparison

Galli Gigerii Endothelium Corneum (ji nei jin) vs. Crataegi Fructus (shan zha)

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueJi Nei JinShan Zha
Shared clueBoth strongly reduce Food Stagnation.
Fast distinctionAll types of Food Stagnation; also strengthens the Stomach, halts enuresis and dissolves stones.Especially meat stagnation; also breaks Qi obstruction and disperses Blood stasis.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersThe shared Food-Stagnation action is strong in both. “All foods,” stones or enuresis identify Ji Nei Jin; meat, Blood stasis, amenorrhea or obstructive chest pain identify Shan Zha.
Memory cueJi Nei Jin handles every meal and stones; Shan Zha handles meat and stasis.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), p. 505. Source Exam Keys CMID 8990 and CMID 9055, questions 1–2.
