Pathology or symptom comparison

Urinary Retention Pattern Differentiation

Diagnostic orientation

Core ideaHeat, respiratory signs, emotion, fixed lower-abdominal pain and weak urinary force distinguish the branches.
Exam focusAcute obstruction, location and deficient urinary force

Comparative differentiation

Pattern or presentationDecisive cluesRepresentative formula/herbStrategy
Bladder Damp-HeatHot scant urine/retention, lower-abdominal fullness, thirst and yellow greasy coatBa Zheng SanClear Damp-Heat and free urine.
Lung Heat congestionRetention with dry throat, chest Heat, thirst, rapid breathing or coughQing Fei YinClear Lung Heat and open water passages.
Liver-Qi stagnationDifficult urine with emotional constraint and lower-abdominal/hypochondriac distentionChen Xiang SanSoothe Liver and regulate Qi.
Blood stasis obstructionDribbling/retention with fixed lower-abdominal pain and purple tongueDai Di Dang WanInvigorate Blood and dispel stasis.
Spleen-Qi deficiencyBearing-down fullness, periodic urge, fatigue, poor appetite and weak voiceBu Zhong Yi Qi Tang + Wu Ling SanRaise Qi and promote urination.
Kidney-Yang exhaustionWeak urinary force with cold, fatigue and cold aching low back/kneesJi Sheng Shen Qi WanWarm Kidney Yang and transform water.

Study rule

How to distinguishHot urine indicates Bladder; respiratory Heat indicates Lung; emotion/distention indicates Liver; fixed purple pain indicates Blood; bearing-down fatigue indicates Spleen; weak cold stream indicates Kidney.
Memory cueHot—Bladder; chest—Lung; emotion—Liver; fixed—Blood; sinking—Spleen; cold weak stream—Kidney.
SafetyAcute inability to urinate is a medical emergency because obstruction can injure the kidneys. Severe pain, fever, vomiting or blood increases urgency.
