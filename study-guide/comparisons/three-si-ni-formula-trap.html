The Three Sì Nì Formulas

The same “frigid extremities” name hides Yang collapse, constrained Qi or Blood deficiency with channel Cold.

Memory rule

AnchorTāng = tanked Yang; Sǎn = stuck Qi; Dāng Guī = too little Blood to carry warmth.

Decisive separation

FormulaArchitectureDecisive presentation
Sì Nì Tāng 四逆汤Fù Zǐ + Gān Jiāng + Zhì Gān CǎoYang collapse: profound cold, curled posture and deep-faint pulse.
Sì Nì Sǎn 四逆散Chái Hú + Zhǐ Shí + Bái Sháo + Zhì Gān CǎoQi constraint prevents warmth reaching fingers; wiry pulse and internal tension.
Dāng Guī Sì Nì Tāng 当归四逆汤Dāng Guī, Guì Zhī, Bái Sháo, Xì Xīn, Tōng Cǎo, Dà Zǎo, Zhì Gān CǎoBlood deficiency with channel Cold/Raynaud-like extremities.

Exam and safety

Exam keyAsk why the hands are cold—not which title contains Sì Nì.
SafetySì Nì Tāng contains processed aconite and requires expert preparation. Dāng Guī Sì Nì Tāng contains Xì Xīn and does not contain Fù Zǐ.
