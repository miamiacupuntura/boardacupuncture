High-Risk & Toxic Herbs

Identify toxic constituents, required processing and characteristic overdose signals.

Core rule

RuleVerify species, part, processing, dose, duration and patient risk every time a high-risk medicinal is considered.

High-risk medicinals

Group / stepWhat to knowSafe decision
Fù Zǐ / Chuān Wū / Cǎo WūAconitine-type alkaloids can cause paresthesia, weakness, hypotension and lethal ventricular arrhythmias.Authenticated processed material and expert supervision only; never use raw or improvise processing.
Mǎ Qián ZǐStrychnos alkaloids can cause painful rigidity, spasms and seizures with preserved awareness.Narrow therapeutic range; professional processed products only where legally permitted.
Bàn Xià / Tiān Nán Xīng / Bái Fù ZǐRaw Araceae material can cause oral burning, swelling and airway injury.The specified processed form is part of the identity; raw material is not interchangeable.
Kǔ Xìng Rén and cyanogenic kernelsAmygdalin may release cyanide, especially when raw, crushed or excessive.Use authenticated processed material and professional dosing; keep away from children.
Xiāng Jiā PíPeriploca contains cardiac glycosides with digoxin-like rhythm toxicity.Avoid with cardiac glycosides or rhythm disease unless specialist-supervised.
Léi Gōng TéngTripterygium can cause marrow, hepatic, renal, reproductive and immune toxicity.Requires specialist indication and laboratory monitoring; contraindicated in pregnancy.
Gān Suì / Dà Jǐ / Yuán Huā / Bā DòuDrastic purgation may cause severe fluid loss, electrolyte disturbance and tissue injury.Short-term expert use only; avoid in pregnancy, frailty, dehydration and suspected obstruction.

Overdose signals

Group / stepWhat to knowSafe decision
Cardiac/neurologicNumb mouth, spreading tingling, weakness, palpitations, irregular pulse, fainting, spasms or seizures.Stop exposure and seek emergency care/poison control immediately.
Airway/organ injuryTongue or throat swelling, persistent vomiting, jaundice, reduced urine, edema or unusual bleeding.Preserve the package and obtain urgent medical evaluation.

When to act immediately

EmergencyDo not induce vomiting or attempt home detoxification. Bring the package, prescription, batch, dose and timing to emergency care.

Educational use: this library does not replace medical evaluation, poison control, local regulation or pharmacist consultation.
