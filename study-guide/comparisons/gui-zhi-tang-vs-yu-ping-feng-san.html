Gui Zhi Tang vs. Yu Ping Feng San

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueGui Zhi TangYu Ping Feng San
Shared clueBoth can involve spontaneous sweating and aversion to wind, but one treats an active Exterior pattern while the other strengthens a chronically weak Exterior.
Fast distinctionActive Wind-Cold invasion with fever, chills and a floating-moderate pulse.No active invasion: chronic spontaneous sweating, weak resistance and recurrent colds.

Comparative answer key

CompareGui Zhi TangYu Ping Feng San
CategoryFormula that releases the Exterior.Formula that tonifies Qi and stabilizes the Exterior.
Primary actionHarmonizes Ying and Wei while releasing Wind-Cold.Augments Defensive Qi, secures the Exterior and stops sweating.
Timing clueUse for the current acute pattern.Use between episodes for constitutional prevention.
Decisive signsFever and chills, headache, sweating, floating-moderate pulse.Recurrent colds, spontaneous sweating, pale complexion, weak pulse.
Exam trapNot a preventive tonic.Not the choice during a strong active Exterior pathogen.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersThe shared sweating clue can mislead. First determine whether a pathogen is currently present or whether the Exterior is simply too weak to defend and secure itself.
Memory cueGui Zhi treats the visitor; Yu Ping Feng repairs the screen.
SafetyAn acute febrile illness with breathing difficulty, dehydration or rapid deterioration requires medical assessment.
