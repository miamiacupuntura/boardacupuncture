Batch 7 · Small Intestine · Tip 7 of 10

Small Intestine Full Heat vs. Heart Fire

Use urinary pain versus prominent Spirit and Heart signs within the paired-organ Heat pathway.

Fast differentiation clues

ClueSmall Intestine Full HeatHeart Fire
SharedBoth can cause tongue ulcers, thirst, restlessness, red tongue tip and Heat transferring along the paired channel.
Fast distinctionSmall Intestine focus: scant dark painful urination and lower abdominal discomfort.Heart focus: palpitations, insomnia, agitation and more prominent Spirit disturbance.

Clinical table

CompareSmall Intestine Full HeatHeart Fire
Decisive systemUrinary passage and lower burner.Heart rhythm, sleep and Spirit.
Formula associationDao Chi San, Dao Chi Qing Xin Tang or Liang Ge San.Xie Xin Tang or Dao Chi San by presentation.

Exam and safety

Why it mattersUrinary burning localizes Heat to Small Intestine; palpitations/insomnia localize it to Heart.
Memory cueSmall Intestine Fire burns urine; Heart Fire agitates Shen.
SafetyFever with painful urination, flank pain, confusion, chest pain or sustained palpitations requires medical evaluation.
