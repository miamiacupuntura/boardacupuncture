Small Intestine Qi Pain vs. Qi Tied

Separate intermittent twisting Qi pain from a sudden severe obstructive presentation.

Fast differentiation clues

ClueSmall Intestine Qi PainSmall Intestine Qi Tied
SharedBoth cause lower abdominal pain with a deep wiry pulse and impaired Qi movement.
Fast distinctionQi Pain: twisting or cramping pain with borborygmi, often intermittent.Qi Tied: sudden severe pain with constipation, vomiting and signs of complete blockage.

Clinical table

CompareSmall Intestine Qi PainSmall Intestine Qi Tied
SeverityVariable and mobile/intermittent.Severe, fixed and acute.
Bowel passageMay continue despite discomfort.Constipation or failure to pass stool/gas.
Formula associationChai Hu Shu Gan Tang.Zhi Shi Dao Wan or Tian Tai Wu Yao San in the supplied table.

Exam and safety

Why it mattersVomiting plus obstipation transforms a Qi-pain differential into a possible obstruction emergency.
Memory cueQi Pain twists; Qi Tied locks.
SafetySevere pain, persistent vomiting, swollen abdomen or inability to pass stool/gas may be bowel obstruction—seek emergency care.
