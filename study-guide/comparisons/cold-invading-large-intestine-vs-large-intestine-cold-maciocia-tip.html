Cold Invading vs. Large Intestine Cold

Separate acute excess Cold diarrhea from a chronic deficient-Cold bowel pattern.

Fast differentiation clues

ClueCold Invading Large IntestineLarge Intestine Cold
SharedBoth cause loose stool, abdominal pain, cold limbs and a slow pulse.
Fast distinctionInvasion: sudden sharp/cramping pain and diarrhea after Cold exposure with a deep-tight pulse.Deficient Cold: chronic loose stool, dull pain, fatigue and a fine weak pulse.

Exam and safety

Why it mattersOnset and pulse force separate an acute pathogen from chronic weakness.
Memory cueInvading Cold strikes tight; deficient Cold lingers weak.
SafetySevere pain, fever, blood, persistent diarrhea or dehydration requires medical assessment.
