Herb comparison

Ziziphi spinosae Semen (suan zao ren) vs. Polygalae Radix (yuan zhi)

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueSuan Zao RenYuan Zhi
Shared cluesBoth enter the Heart, calm the Spirit and treat anxiety palpitations and insomnia.
Fast distinctionNourishes Heart Yin and Liver Blood; sourly restrains in deficiency insomnia.Warms and unblocks to restore Heart-Kidney communication; cognition and resolve clues.

Comparative answer key

CompareSuan Zao RenYuan Zhi
Medicinal nameZiziphi spinosae Semen.Polygalae Radix.
CategoryHerbs that Nourish the Heart and Calm the Spirit.
Nature and tasteSour, sweet and neutral.Acrid, bitter and warm.
MechanismNourishes Heart Yin and Liver Blood; its sour-contracting nature generates fluids and restrains excessive dispersal of the Spirit.Assists Heart Yang and Heart Qi and reestablishes harmonious Heart-Kidney communication.
Best insomnia clueDeficiency irritability and insomnia with Yin or Blood deficiency.Palpitations and insomnia from Heart-Kidney disconnection.
Additional clueRestrains deficiency sweating and generates fluids.Strengthens resolve and treats confusion, poor concentration and forgetfulness; also transforms Phlegm.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersBoth calm the Spirit, but the deficiency-nourishing and restraining profile identifies Suan Zao Ren; warming, unblocking and cognitive clues identify Yuan Zhi.
Memory cueSuan Zao Ren nourishes and holds; Yuan Zhi opens and reconnects.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), p. 932. Source Exam Keys CMID 9072 and CMID 9116, question 3.
