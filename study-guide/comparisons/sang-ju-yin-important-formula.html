Sang Ju Yin

Mild early-stage Wind-Heat attacking the Lung with cough as the chief symptom.

High-yield summary

CategoryDisperse Wind-Heat · Stop Cough
PatternMild early-stage Wind-Heat attacking the Lung with cough as the chief symptom.
ActionDisperses Wind-Heat, clears the Lung and stops cough.
Decisive clueCough is more prominent than fever or thirst in a mild Wind-Heat presentation.

Ingredients

Sang Ye, Ju Hua, Xing Ren, Lian Qiao, Bo He, Jie Geng, Sheng Gan Cao and Lu Gen.

Commercial preparations and lineages may vary; always verify the source being used.

Differentiation and memory

Do not confuseYin Qiao San treats stronger Wind-Heat with sore throat and Heat-Toxin; Sang Ju Yin is milder and cough-centered.

Safety

Breathing difficulty, chest pain, persistent high fever, blood, low oxygen or worsening cough requires medical assessment.
