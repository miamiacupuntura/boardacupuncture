Gui Zhi Tang Family

Root formula and architecture

RootGui Zhi Tang releases Wind-Cold from an Exterior-deficiency presentation while harmonizing the nutritive and protective levels.
ArchitectureGui Zhi warms and releases; Bai Shao preserves Yin and moderates the exterior; Sheng Jiang, Da Zao and Zhi Gan Cao harmonize. The Gui Zhi–Bai Shao balance is the family axis.

Study rule

How to chooseKeep the Gui Zhi–Bai Shao harmony in view; the added symptom identifies the branch—neck, Lung, leakage or abdominal deficiency Cold.
Memory cueGe Gen = neck; Hou Po/Xing Ren = Lung; Long Gu/Mu Li = leakage; Yi Tang = abdomen; Huang Qi = deeper deficiency.
SafetyDo not confuse this spontaneous-sweating Exterior-deficiency picture with a closed, no-sweat Ma Huang Tang presentation. Yi Tang-containing formulas require dietary/glucose review.
