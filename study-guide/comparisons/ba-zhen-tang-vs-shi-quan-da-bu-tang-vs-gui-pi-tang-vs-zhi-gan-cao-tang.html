Ba Zhen Tang vs. Shi Quan Da Bu Tang vs. Gui Pi Tang vs. Zhi Gan Cao Tang

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueBa Zhen TangShi Quan Da Bu TangGui Pi TangZhi Gan Cao Tang

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersThe shared deficiency signs are nonspecific. Identify the complication: Cold, Shen/bleeding involvement or failure to nourish the Heart rhythm.
Memory cueBa Zhen builds eight; Shi Quan warms ten; Gui Pi returns Blood; Zhi Gan Cao restores rhythm.
SafetyHeavy bleeding, fainting, chest pain, significant shortness of breath or a new irregular heartbeat requires prompt medical evaluation.
