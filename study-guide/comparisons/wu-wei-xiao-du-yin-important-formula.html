Wu Wei Xiao Du Yin

Toxic Heat accumulation causing boils, carbuncles and hot swellings.

High-yield summary

CategoryClear Toxic Heat · Reduce Sores
PatternToxic Heat accumulation causing boils, carbuncles and hot swellings.
ActionClears Heat, resolves toxicity, cools Blood and disperses abscesses.
Decisive clueMultiple red, hot, painful boils or carbuncles with fever, thirst and a rapid pulse.

Ingredients

Jin Yin Hua, Ye Ju Hua, Pu Gong Ying, Zi Hua Di Ding and Zi Bei Tian Kui (Tian Kui Zi).

Commercial preparations and lineages may vary; always verify the source being used.

Differentiation and memory

Do not confuseXian Fang Huo Ming Yin is more strongly Blood- and Qi-moving for an early localized abscess; Wu Wei Xiao Du Yin is a focused five-herb Toxic-Heat formula.
Memory cueFive flowers and herbs detoxify fiery skin swellings.

Safety

Rapidly spreading redness, severe pain, fever, streaking, facial involvement or immunosuppression requires urgent medical care and possible drainage/antibiotics.
