Pathology or symptom comparison

Chronic Nasal Congestion Pattern Differentiation

Diagnostic orientation

Core ideaDischarge quality and systemic signs distinguish the branches; persistent unilateral or complicated sinus disease requires biomedical assessment.
Exam focusDischarge color/odor, facial pain, Heat and deficiency signs

Comparative differentiation

Pattern or presentationDecisive cluesRepresentative formula/herbStrategy
Lung Wind-HeatFever/chills, headache, blocked nose and turbid yellow fishy discharge, sometimes coughCang Er Zi SanDispel Wind-Heat and open nasal orifices.
Gallbladder HeatCopious thick foul yellow discharge with severe brow/cheek pressure, bitter mouth, insomnia and irritabilityLong Dan Xie Gan TangDrain GB Heat and clear turbidity.
Spleen-Stomach Damp-HeatHeavy yellow discharge, severe continuous blockage, smell loss, swollen nasal cavity, heavy head and greasy yellow coatHuang Qin Hua Shi Tang + Cang Er Zi SanClear Damp-Heat and open orifices.
Lung deficiency-ColdIntermittent blockage with clear sticky discharge worse in Wind/Cold, dyspnea, weak voice and spontaneous sweatWen Fei TangWarm/support Lung Qi and dispel Cold.
Spleen-Qi deficiencyThick milky non-foul discharge, severe blockage/smell loss, heavy limbs/head, fatigue, poor appetite and loose stoolShen Ling Bai Zhu SanSupport Spleen Qi and drain Dampness.

Study rule

How to distinguishYellow after exterior signs is Lung Wind-Heat; foul with brow/cheek pain GB Heat; heavy/swollen/greasy Damp-Heat; clear/cold/weak Lung; milky with digestive weakness Spleen.
Memory cueCold then yellow—Lung; foul facial pain—GB; heavy greasy—Damp; clear cold—Lung deficiency; milky tired—Spleen.
SafetyEye swelling, vision change, severe frontal headache, neurologic signs, high fever or facial swelling is urgent. Symptoms over 10 days, recurrent unilateral discharge, bleeding or persistent smell loss needs medical/ENT evaluation.
