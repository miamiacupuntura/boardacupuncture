Herb comparison

Fu Ling vs. Zhu Ling

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueFu LingZhu Ling
Shared clueBoth belong to Herbs that Drain Dampness and promote urination.
Fast distinctionMilder urination effect, but also tonifies the Spleen and calms the Spirit.Promotes urination more strongly but does not tonify.

Comparative answer key

CompareFu LingZhu Ling
Medicinal namePoria.Polyporus.
CategoryHerbs that Drain Dampness.Herbs that Drain Dampness.
Urination effectRelatively mild.Stronger than Fu Ling.
Additional actionsTonifies the Spleen, harmonizes the Middle and calms the Spirit.Does not tonify.
Nature and channels in sourceThe comparison emphasizes its broad tonifying and calming scope.Neutral; Kidney and Urinary Bladder.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersStronger urination alone points to Zhu Ling; a broader pattern requiring Spleen support or Spirit calming points to Fu Ling.
Memory cueZhu Ling drains more; Fu Ling does more.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), p. 272. Source Exam Keys: CMID 8954, question 2; CMID 9126, question 1.
