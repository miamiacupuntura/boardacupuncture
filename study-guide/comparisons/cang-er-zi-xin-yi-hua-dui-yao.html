Appendix B pair · 6 of 51

Cāng Ěr Zǐ + Xīn Yí Huā

苍耳子 + 辛夷花

One picture, one job

PictureThe burr and fuzzy bud nose crew.
Combined jobDispel Wind and open the nasal passages for congestion, sinus pressure and reduced smell.
CategoryOpen the Nasal Passages

How the pair works

HerbRole within the pair
Cāng Ěr ZǐStrongly opens the nose and dispels Wind-Damp from the head.
Xīn Yí HuāDirects aromatic opening action to the nose and sinus region.

Exam focus and safety

CluesStuffy nose, frontal/sinus headache and loss or reduction of smell.
Signature formulaCāng Ěr Zǐ Sǎn.
TrapThis pair treats obstruction; discharge color and whole pattern still determine whether Cold, Heat or Dampness must also be addressed.
PreparationWrap Xīn Yí Huā during decoction because its hairs irritate the throat.
SafetyCāng Ěr Zǐ is toxic and requires professional dosing. Facial swelling, visual changes, severe headache, high fever or neurologic signs needs urgent care.
