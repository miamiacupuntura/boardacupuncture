Formula comparison

Bu Zhong Yi Qi Tang vs. Gui Pi Tang

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueBu Zhong Yi Qi TangGui Pi Tang
Shared clueBoth tonify Spleen Qi and may treat fatigue, poor appetite, loose stools and pale weakness.
Fast distinctionMiddle Qi sinks: prolapse, chronic diarrhea, bearing-down sensation or Qi-deficiency fever.Spleen Qi fails to make/hold Blood and Heart Blood is deficient: insomnia, palpitations, poor memory or bleeding.

Comparative answer key

CompareBu Zhong Yi Qi TangGui Pi Tang
Primary directionRaises clear Yang and lifts sunken Qi.Tonifies Spleen Qi, nourishes Heart Blood and calms the Spirit.
Decisive clueProlapse or bearing-down symptoms.Spirit disturbance or abnormal bleeding.
Typical signsFatigue worse with exertion, weak voice, spontaneous sweating, chronic diarrhea.Fatigue, poor appetite, insomnia, palpitations, forgetfulness, easy bruising.
Exam shortcutQi is sinking.Qi is not producing or containing Blood.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersBoth start from Spleen-Qi deficiency. The downstream failure—sinking versus Heart-Blood deficiency/bleeding—chooses the formula.
Memory cueBu Zhong lifts; Gui Pi builds and holds Blood.
SafetyNew prolapse, unexplained bleeding, black stools or severe anemia signs require medical evaluation.
SourceSource bank: FWB 8.2 series, Comparison Formulas to Tonify; corroborated with the Classic Formulas profiles.
