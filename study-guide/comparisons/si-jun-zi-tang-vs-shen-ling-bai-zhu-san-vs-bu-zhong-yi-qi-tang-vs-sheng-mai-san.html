Study guide · Exam focus

Why it matters“Fatigue” appears in all four. The secondary feature—Dampness, sinking, or Yin-fluid depletion—determines how the tonic strategy must be modified.
Memory cueSi Jun builds; Shen Ling drains; Bu Zhong lifts; Sheng Mai restores the pulse and fluids.
SafetyNew prolapse, persistent diarrhea, unexplained fever, fainting, chest pain or significant palpitations requires medical evaluation.
