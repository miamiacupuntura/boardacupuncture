Herb comparison

Eriobotryae Folium (pi pa ye) vs. Pinelliae Rhizoma preparatum (zhi ban xia)

Study tip · Clues to help answer

CluePi Pa YeZhi Ban Xia
Shared clueBoth treat Lung and Stomach disorders and restore downward movement.
Fast distinctionCools Lung and Stomach Heat; best for Phlegm-Heat, Lung Dryness and Heat-driven rebellious Stomach Qi.Warms and transforms Damp-Phlegm through the Spleen; best for profuse sputum and Damp-Phlegm obstructing Stomach-Qi descent.

Comparative answer key

ComparePi Pa YeZhi Ban Xia
Botanical nameEriobotryae Folium.Pinelliae Rhizoma preparatum.
CategoryHerbs that Relieve Coughing and Wheezing.Warm Herbs that Transform Cold-Phlegm.
Lung actionCools the Lungs, stops cough and transforms Phlegm.Transforms Dampness and Phlegm through its action on the Spleen.
Cough cluePhlegm-Heat or cough from Lung Dryness.Cough with profuse sputum from Damp-Phlegm.
Stomach actionCools Stomach Heat so excessive Yang does not carry Stomach Qi upward.Transforms Dampness and Phlegm so Stomach Qi can descend properly.
Nausea mechanismStops Heat-driven rebellion.Stops Damp-Phlegm obstruction of descent.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersBoth address Lung and Stomach rebellion, but the mechanism differs: Heat or Dryness points to Pi Pa Ye, whereas Spleen-generated Damp-Phlegm with profuse sputum points to Zhi Ban Xia.
Memory cuePi Pa Ye cools Heat and Dryness; Zhi Ban Xia dries Damp-Phlegm.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), p. 447. Source Exam Key CMID 9028, questions 4–6.

Possible exam questions · Study prompts

  • Which organ systems do both herbs treat?
  • Which herb treats Phlegm-Heat or cough from Lung Dryness?
  • Which herb treats profuse sputum from Damp-Phlegm?
  • How does each herb restore the descent of Stomach Qi?
