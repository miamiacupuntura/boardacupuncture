Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersAll can show Wind-Cold, so the internal branch matters more than the shared exterior signs.
Memory cueJiu Wei = Damp; Da Qing = Heat; Xiao Qing = cold Fluids.
SafetyThese are strong formulas containing potent warming or dispersing herbs. Breathing difficulty, low oxygen, severe dehydration, chest pain or persistent high fever requires medical care.
