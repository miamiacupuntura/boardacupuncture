Batch 1 · Heart and Pericardium · Tip 1 of 10

Heart Qi Deficiency vs. Heart Yang Deficiency vs. Heart Yang Collapse

Follow the progression from weak Heart function to Cold and then to a medical emergency with devastated Yang.

Fast differentiation clues

ClueHeart Qi DeficiencyHeart Yang DeficiencyHeart Yang Collapse
SharedAll may cause palpitations, fatigue, shortness of breath and a weak pulse because Heart propulsion is impaired.
Fast distinctionWeak function without marked Cold: exertional palpitations, fatigue and spontaneous sweating.Qi deficiency plus Cold: cold limbs, chest discomfort and a deep-weak or irregular pulse.Sudden severe failure: icy limbs, blue lips, profuse cold sweat, altered consciousness and a hidden-minute pulse.

Clinical table

CompareHeart Qi DeficiencyHeart Yang DeficiencyHeart Yang Collapse
SeverityChronic functional deficiency.Deeper chronic deficiency with Cold.Critical decompensation.
Tongue / pulsePale; empty or weak.Pale-purple and wet; deep-weak, slow or irregular.Pale-blue or purple; minute, hidden or disappearing.
Formula associationYang Xin Tang by presentation.Zhi Gan Cao Tang when Qi-Yang weakness accompanies an irregular pulse.Shen Fu Tang or Si Ni Tang are classical associations, not substitutes for emergency care.

Exam and safety

Why it mattersCold separates Qi from Yang deficiency; cyanosis, cold sweat and a vanishing pulse identify collapse.
Memory cueQi is weak; Yang is weak and cold; collapse is cold and failing.
SafetyBlue lips, fainting, severe breathlessness, chest pain, cold sweat or a very slow/irregular pulse requires emergency services.
