Juan Bi Tang

Acute Wind-Cold-Damp painful obstruction, often affecting the shoulders and upper limbs.

High-yield summary

CategoryDispel Wind-Damp · Relieve Painful Obstruction
PatternAcute Wind-Cold-Damp painful obstruction, often affecting the shoulders and upper limbs.
ActionDispels Wind, eliminates Dampness, opens the channels and relieves pain.
Decisive clueAcute migrating or fixed shoulder/arm pain and heaviness that worsens in cold, damp weather.

Ingredients

Qiang Huo, Fang Feng, Jiang Huang, Dang Gui, Chi Shao, Huang Qi, Zhi Gan Cao and Sheng Jiang.

Commercial preparations and lineages may vary; always verify the source being used.

Differentiation and memory

Do not confuseDu Huo Ji Sheng Tang treats chronic lower-body Bi with Liver/Kidney and Qi/Blood deficiency; Juan Bi Tang is more acute and upper-body focused.
Memory cueQiang Huo and Jiang Huang pull Wind-Damp pain out of shoulders and arms.

Safety

Hot swollen joint, fever, recent trauma, chest-related arm pain or sudden weakness requires prompt medical evaluation.
