Herb comparison

Stemonae Radix (bai bu) vs. Asparagi Radix (tian men dong)

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueBai BuTian Men Dong
Shared clueBoth enter the Lungs and treat cough, and their roots were historically compared by shape.
Fast distinctionWarm, bitter and drying; favors cold cough and also kills parasites.Cold, sweet and moistening; favors hot or dry cough from Yin deficiency.

Comparative answer key

CompareBai BuTian Men Dong
Medicinal nameStemonae Radix.Asparagi Radix.
CategoryHerbs that Relieve Coughing and Wheezing.Herbs that Tonify the Yin.
Nature and tasteWarm, bitter and drying.Cold, sweet and moistening.
Cough patternCold cough, especially when a warm cough-suppressing action is needed.Hot or dry cough involving Lung-Yin deficiency and Dryness.
Distinctive actionKills parasites in addition to stopping cough.Strongly nourishes Yin, clears Heat and moistens Dryness.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersBoth may answer a cough question, but temperature and moisture decide it: warm-drying and parasite clues identify Bai Bu; cold-moistening and Yin-deficiency Dryness identify Tian Men Dong.
Memory cueBai Bu warms and dries cold cough; Tian Men Dong cools and moistens hot dry cough.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), p. 449. Source Exam Key CMID 9079, question 1.
