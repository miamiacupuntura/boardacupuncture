Liu Wei Di Huang Wan Family

Root formula and architecture

RootLiu Wei Di Huang Wan balances three tonifying herbs with three draining/regulating herbs for Liver-Kidney Yin deficiency.
ArchitectureThe six-herb base stays recognizable. Additions identify the branch; Fu Zi and Rou Gui reverse the direction toward Kidney Yang and Qi transformation.

Study rule

How to chooseBase Yin, then ask: Fire, eyes or Yang/water? Ba Wei Di Huang Wan and Jin Gui Shen Qi Wan are treated here as names from the same eight-ingredient lineage.
Memory cueLiu Wei = Yin; Zhi Bai = Fire; Qi Ju = eyes; Ba Wei/Jin Gui = Yang and water.
SafetyRich Yin formulas can worsen Dampness or loose stools. Kidney-Yang members contain processed Fu Zi and require professional supervision.
