Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersThe three names and warming actions are easy to confuse, but the source assigns a distinct recognition clue to each: Wind, Cold, or severe stubborn obstruction with extreme toxicity.
Common confusionZhi Chuan Wu is the Wind-focused and more dispersing comparison; Zhi Fu Zi is the Cold-focused option compatible with tonifying herbs; Zhi Cao Wu carries the strongest toxicity warning.
Shared incompatibilityThe source question states that they are incompatible with Bai Ji (Bletillae Rhizoma).
SafetyThis is an exam-review comparison, not a preparation or dosing guide. The source explicitly describes Zhi Cao Wu as extremely toxic.
Source-data noteThe source spells Zhi Fu Zi as “zhi fui zi.” This note uses the standardized pinyin spelling while preserving the keyed distinction.
Source question formatsQuestions 1 and 2 are Matching.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), p. 676. Source Exam Key CMID 8907.
