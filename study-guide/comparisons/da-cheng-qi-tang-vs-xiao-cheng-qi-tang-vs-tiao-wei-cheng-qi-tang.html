Da Cheng Qi Tang vs. Xiao Cheng Qi Tang vs. Tiao Wei Cheng Qi Tang

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueDa Cheng Qi TangXiao Cheng Qi TangTiao Wei Cheng Qi Tang
Shared clueAll three clear Heat and move stool in Yangming bowel excess. They differ in severity, abdominal findings and the balance between moving Qi and softening Dryness.
Fast distinctionSevere obstruction: focal distention, fullness, Dryness and solidity.Moderate obstruction: focal distention and fullness without the same degree of Dryness.Heat-Dryness and irritability without pronounced focal distention or fullness.

Comparative answer key

CompareDa Cheng Qi TangXiao Cheng Qi TangTiao Wei Cheng Qi Tang
Relative strengthStrongest of the three.Moderate.Gentler Qi movement; emphasizes Heat and Dryness.
Key ingredientsDa Huang, Mang Xiao, Zhi Shi and Hou Po.Da Huang, Zhi Shi and Hou Po; no Mang Xiao.Da Huang, Mang Xiao and Zhi Gan Cao; no Zhi Shi or Hou Po.
Abdominal clueMarked distention, fullness, hardness and pain.Distention and fullness with constipation, but less severe hardness.Constipation and irritability with less marked distention/fullness.
Tongue coatDark yellow to black and very dry.Deep yellow to brown and dry.Yellow and dry, generally less severe.
PulseRapid, deep and forceful.Rapid and slippery.Usually rapid; less forceful obstruction.
Exam wordingSevere Yangming Fu-organ excess.Moderate Yangming Fu-organ excess.Yangming Heat and Dryness needing regulation of the Stomach.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersThe names identify a graded family. Da Cheng Qi combines forceful Qi movement with softening; Xiao omits the salt that softens hardness; Tiao Wei omits the strong Qi movers and adds licorice.
Memory cueDa = all four and severe; Xiao = no Mang Xiao; Tiao Wei = regulate with Gan Cao.
SafetyThese are strong purgative formulas for professional use. They are not appropriate for self-treatment and require caution or avoidance in pregnancy, frailty, dehydration, and suspected bowel obstruction.
SourceSource bank: FWB 3.2, Comparison Formulas to Purge; corroborated with the Classic Formulas profiles and Chinese Herbal Medicine: Formulas & Strategies, 2nd ed.
