Dizziness and Vertigo Pattern Differentiation

Diagnostic orientation

Core ideaHead pressure and anger point upward, pallor points to Qi-Blood deficiency, lumbar signs point to Kidney, and heaviness/nausea point to Phlegm.
Exam focusUpward Yang, deficient nourishment and Phlegm obstruction

Study rule

How to distinguishAnger/head distention identifies Liver Yang; pallor/fatigue identifies Qi-Blood; lumbar-tinnitus signs identify Kidney; heaviness and greasy coat identify Phlegm, with yellow/bitter signs marking Heat.
Memory cueUpward—Liver; pale—Qi/Blood; back—Kidney; heavy—Phlegm; yellow—Phlegm-Heat.
SafetySudden vertigo with one-sided weakness, speech difficulty, severe headache, fainting, chest pain or inability to walk requires emergency stroke/cardiac assessment.
