Chuan Xiong Cha Tiao San

External Wind attacking the head and channels.

High-yield summary

CategoryDispel External Wind · Stop Headache
PatternExternal Wind attacking the head and channels.
ActionDisperses Wind, relieves pain and directs treatment to different regions of the head.
Decisive clueAcute headache with exterior signs; the guiding herbs cover vertex, frontal and occipital locations.

Ingredients

Chuan Xiong, Jing Jie, Qiang Huo, Bai Zhi, Gan Cao, Xi Xin, Fang Feng and Bo He; traditionally taken with green tea.

Commercial preparations and lineages may vary; always verify the source being used.

Differentiation and memory

Do not confuseXue Fu Zhu Yu Tang treats fixed chronic headache from Blood stasis; this formula treats an acute Wind presentation.
Memory cueChuan Xiong reaches the vertex; the formula tunes every headache location with tea.

Safety

Thunderclap headache, neurologic deficit, fever with neck stiffness, trauma or pregnancy-related severe headache is an emergency.
