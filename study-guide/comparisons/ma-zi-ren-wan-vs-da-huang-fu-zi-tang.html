Ma Zi Ren Wan vs. Da Huang Fu Zi Tang

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueMa Zi Ren WanDa Huang Fu Zi Tang
Shared clueBoth unblock constipation, but one moistens dry intestines while the other warms and purges internal Cold accumulation.
Fast distinctionDry, hard stool from intestinal Dryness and Heat, often in a relatively deficient patient.Constipation with acute Cold accumulation, abdominal/flank pain and a deep-tight pulse.

Comparative answer key

CompareMa Zi Ren WanDa Huang Fu Zi Tang
TemperatureWarm intestinal Dryness/Heat pattern.Internal Cold accumulation.
Decisive clueHard dry stool, thirst and frequent urination; dryness dominates.Severe abdominal or flank pain, cold limbs or aversion to cold; Cold pain dominates.
Treatment directionMoisten intestines, drain mild Heat and move Qi.Warm the interior, disperse Cold and purge accumulation.
Key ingredientsHuo Ma Ren with Xing Ren, Bai Shao and a reduced Cheng Qi unit.Da Huang with processed Fu Zi and Xi Xin.
PulseOften slippery/rapid or deficient depending on constitution.Deep and tight.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersDa Huang does not always mean Heat. In Da Huang Fu Zi Tang, the purgative is paired with intensely warming herbs to clear a Cold obstruction.
Memory cueMa Zi moistens dry Heat; Fu Zi warms Cold blockage.
SafetySevere pain, vomiting, distention, blood or inability to pass gas may indicate obstruction and requires urgent evaluation. Fu Zi must be correctly processed and professionally prescribed.
