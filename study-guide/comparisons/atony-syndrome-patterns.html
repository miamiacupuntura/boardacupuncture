Pathology or symptom comparison

Atony Syndrome Pattern Differentiation

Diagnostic orientation

Core ideaFlaccidity and limb weakness are sorted by acute dryness/Heat, Damp-Heat heaviness or progressive deficiency signs.
Exam focusAcute Heat or Dampness versus progressive digestive or Kidney deficiency

Comparative differentiation

Pattern or presentationDecisive cluesRepresentative formula/herbStrategy
Lung Heat injures fluidsWeak/flaccid limbs with fever, dry cough/throat, thirst, scant urine and dry yellow tongueQing Zao Jiu Fei TangClear Heat, moisten Lung and generate fluids.
Damp-Heat invasionHeavy weak limbs with numbness/slight swelling, fever, chest fullness, dark urine and yellow sticky coatJia Wei Er Miao SanClear Heat and drain Dampness.
Spleen-Stomach Qi deficiencyProgressive weakness/atrophy with poor appetite, loose stool, fatigue, dyspnea and pale tongueShen Ling Bai Zhu SanTonify Spleen-Stomach and boost Qi.
Liver-Kidney Yin depletionWeak limbs with weak back/knees, vertigo, tinnitus, dry throat and red scant-coated tongueHu Qian WanNourish Liver-Kidney Yin and clear deficient Heat.

Study rule

How to distinguishDry febrile signs point to Lung fluids; heavy swollen sticky signs to Damp-Heat; digestion/pallor to Spleen Qi; lumbar weakness/tinnitus/empty Heat to Liver-Kidney Yin.
Memory cueDry-fever—Lung; heavy-sticky—Damp-Heat; thin-tired digestion—Spleen; back-ears-empty Heat—Kidney Yin.
SafetySudden or rapidly progressive weakness, one-sided weakness, breathing/swallowing difficulty, loss of bladder/bowel control or inability to walk is an emergency.
