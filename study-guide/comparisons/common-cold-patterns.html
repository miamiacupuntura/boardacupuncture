Pathology or symptom comparison

Common Cold Pattern Differentiation

Diagnostic orientation

Core ideaThe decisive sequence is temperature, sweating, secretions and the patient’s underlying constitution.
Exam focusExterior pathogen, temperature and constitution

Comparative differentiation

Pattern or presentationDecisive cluesRepresentative formula/herbStrategy
Wind-Cold invasionStrong chills, mild fever, no sweat, body aches, thin white dischargeJing Fang Bai Du SanDispel Wind, scatter Cold and release the Exterior.
Wind-Heat invasionMore fever, mild chills, sweating, sore throat, thirst, yellow dischargeYin Qiao SanDispel Wind, clear Heat and release the Exterior.
Summerheat-Damp invasionHeavy body, nausea, chest oppression, loose stool, scanty dark urineXin Jia Xiang Ru YinClear Summerheat and transform Dampness.
Wind-Cold with Qi deficiencyChills and fever with fatigue, short breath and weak voiceShen Su YinRelease the Exterior while supporting Qi.
Exterior disorder with Yin deficiencyFever/chills with dry cough, thirst, dry mouth and five-center HeatJia Jian Wei Rui TangRelease the Exterior while nourishing Yin.

Study rule

How to distinguishCold has stronger chills and white discharge; Heat has sore throat and yellow discharge; heaviness/GI signs point to Summerheat-Damp; constitutional weakness identifies the deficiency branches.
Memory cueCold—closed; Heat—throat; Summer—heavy; Qi deficiency—tired; Yin deficiency—dry.
SafetyBreathing difficulty, confusion, dehydration, persistent high fever or rapid deterioration requires medical evaluation.
