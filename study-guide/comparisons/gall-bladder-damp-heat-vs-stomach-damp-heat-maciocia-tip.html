Gall Bladder vs. Stomach Damp-Heat

Use lateral bitter-biliary signs versus central epigastric digestive signs.

Fast differentiation clues

ClueGall Bladder Damp-HeatStomach Damp-Heat
SharedBoth cause nausea, heaviness, poor appetite, sticky yellow coating and a slippery-rapid pulse.
Fast distinctionGall Bladder: hypochondriac fullness, bitter taste and right-sided coat.Stomach: epigastric fullness, sticky taste, reflux or vomiting and central coat.

Clinical table

CompareGall Bladder Damp-HeatStomach Damp-Heat
LocationLateral/right hypochondrium.Central epigastrium.
Formula associationYin Chen Hao-based strategy in the supplied table.Lian Po Yin.

Exam and safety

Why it mattersLocation and taste direct the pattern when Heat and Dampness signs overlap.
Memory cueGall Bladder is bitter at the side; Stomach is full at the center.
SafetySevere pain, jaundice, fever, persistent vomiting, blood or dehydration requires prompt medical care.
