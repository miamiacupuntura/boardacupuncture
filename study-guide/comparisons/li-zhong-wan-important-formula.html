Li Zhong Wan

Deficiency-Cold of the Spleen and Stomach.

High-yield summary

CategoryWarm and Tonify the Middle Burner
PatternDeficiency-Cold of the Spleen and Stomach.
ActionWarms the Middle Burner, dispels Cold, tonifies Qi and strengthens the Spleen.
Decisive clueChronic diarrhea or vomiting with abdominal pain relieved by warmth/pressure, no thirst and cold extremities.

Ingredients

Ren Shen, Gan Jiang, Bai Zhu and Zhi Gan Cao.

Commercial preparations and lineages may vary; always verify the source being used.

Differentiation and memory

Do not confuseSi Ni Tang rescues devastated Yang with a more severe systemic collapse picture; Li Zhong Wan centers on Spleen/Stomach deficiency-Cold.
Memory cueRegulate the center: warm with Gan Jiang and rebuild with the other three.

Safety

Severe abdominal pain, persistent vomiting, blood, fever, fainting or dehydration requires urgent medical care.
