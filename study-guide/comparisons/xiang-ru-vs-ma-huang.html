Exam trap · Category correction

Xiang Ru vs. Ma Huang

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueXiang RuMa Huang
Shared clueBoth are acrid and warm, induce sweating for externally contracted Cold and promote urination.
Fast distinction“Ephedra of summer”; assists Yang Qi in throwing off Cold while harmonizing the Spleen and transforming Dampness.Directly vents the Exterior, disseminates Lung Qi and more strongly induces sweating and disperses Cold.

Study guide · Exam focus

Exam trapCMID 9016 labels Xiang Ru as “Herbs for Regulating Qi.” Its correct category is Warm, Acrid Herbs that Release the Exterior.
Why it mattersSpleen-Dampness and summer context favor Xiang Ru; stronger direct sweating and Lung cough treatment favor Ma Huang.
Memory cueXiang Ru is summer's gentler Ephedra; Ma Huang directly vents the Exterior.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), p. 38. Source Exam Key CMID 9016, questions 6–7; category corrected here.
