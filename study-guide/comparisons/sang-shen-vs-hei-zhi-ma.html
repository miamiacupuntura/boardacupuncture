Herb comparison

Mori Fructus (sang shen) vs. Sesami Semen nigrum (hei zhi ma)

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueSang ShenHei Zhi Ma
Shared clueBoth are foods and medicinals that tonify Liver and Kidneys, enrich Yin, augment Blood, moisten Dryness and lubricate Intestines.
Fast distinctionCrimson-purple and cooling; primarily enters the Blood level.Black and neutral; primarily enters Kidneys to replenish Essence and augment marrow.

Comparative answer key

CompareSang ShenHei Zhi Ma

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersTheir shared food-medicine and moistening roles are nearly identical, so color and temperature decide: crimson-purple/cool points to Blood-level Sang Shen; black/neutral points to Kidney-Essence Hei Zhi Ma.
Memory cuePurple Sang Shen enters Blood; black Hei Zhi Ma enters Kidney.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), p. 762. Source Exam Keys CMID 8973 and CMID 9047, questions 1–2.
