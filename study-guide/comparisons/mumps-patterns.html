Pathology or symptom comparison

Mumps Pattern Differentiation

Diagnostic orientation

Core ideaParotid swelling with fever requires infectious diagnosis and public-health management; pattern clues describe relative severity.
Exam focusParotid swelling quality, fever intensity and complications

Comparative differentiation

Pattern or presentationDecisive cluesRepresentative formula/herbStrategy
External epidemic Warm-ToxinMild fever and chills with unilateral or bilateral springy, tender parotid swelling, indistinct border, little redness, difficult chewing and sore throatYin Qiao SanDisperse Wind-Heat and resolve toxin.
Internal epidemic Heat-ToxinHot, red, very painful swelling with high fever, headache, irritability, thirst, sore throat, dark urine, dry stool or vomiting; severe cases may affect testes or consciousnessPu Ji Xiao Du YinClear intense Heat-Toxin and reduce swelling.

Study rule

How to distinguishMild systemic illness with springy non-red swelling is the external stage; high fever with red painful swelling and systemic toxicity is internal Heat-Toxin.
Memory cueSpringy and pale = external; hot, red and toxic = internal.
SafetyMumps is vaccine-preventable and commonly reportable. Arrange testing and isolation according to public-health guidance. Testicular pain/swelling, severe headache or stiff neck, confusion, dehydration, hearing loss or breathing difficulty needs urgent care.
