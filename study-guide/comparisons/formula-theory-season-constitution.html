Theory 9 of 12

Season, Climate & Constitution

Modify intensity without losing the core treatment principle.

Core rule

RuleSeason and constitution modify the prescription; they do not replace the diagnosed pattern.

Environmental modifiers

ConceptWhat it meansApplication
Hot/dry conditionsStrong warming, dispersing, drying and fluid-draining actions may consume fluids more readily.Protect fluids and avoid unnecessary intensity.
Cold conditionsVery cold, bitter or strongly descending actions may be harder to tolerate in a cold constitution.Balance only when the pattern supports it.
Humid conditionsDampness may require stronger aromatic transformation, Qi movement or digestive support.Do not mistake climate for proof of internal Dampness.
Seasonal transitionThe same chronic patient may present a different branch across seasons.Recheck tongue, pulse and current symptoms before repeating.

Constitutional modifiers

ConceptWhat it meansApplication
Deficiency/frailtyHarsh dispersing or purging can damage Qi, Blood, Yin or fluids.Use the minimum effective intensity and protect the root.
Heat/Yang tendencyWarm acrid ascent can aggravate irritability, dryness or Yang rising.Reduce or counterbalance when consistent with the pattern.
Stress/exhaustionHighly stimulating or strongly tonifying prescriptions may be poorly tolerated.Stabilize sleep, digestion and Shen when clinically relevant.
Menstruation/recent blood lossStrong Blood movement requires context-specific caution.Review bleeding pattern, anemia risk and medications.

Responsible use

Educational summary for formula analysis and exam preparation. Doses, toxic preparations, interactions and clinical decisions require current references and professional supervision.
