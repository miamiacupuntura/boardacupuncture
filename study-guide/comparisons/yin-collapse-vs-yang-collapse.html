Pattern comparison

Yin Collapse vs. Yang Collapse

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueYin CollapseYang Collapse
Shared clueBoth are critical depletion states with profuse sweating, extreme weakness, altered responsiveness and a minute pulse.
Fast distinctionFluids/Yin exhausted: warm sticky or oily sweat, hot skin, intense thirst, restlessness, red dry tongue and rapid minute pulse.Yang devastated: profuse cold sweat, icy limbs, pale or bluish face, little thirst or desire for warm drinks, pale wet tongue and faint slow pulse.

Comparative answer key

CompareYin CollapseYang Collapse
TemperatureHeat and dryness predominate.Profound Cold and loss of warming predominate.
SweatWarm, sticky or oily.Cold, copious and clear.
Tongue / pulseRed-dry; minute-rapid.Pale-wet; minute-faint-slow.
Classical formula associationSheng Mai San in Qi-Yin depletion contexts.Si Ni Tang in devastated-Yang theory; Fu Zi requires expert emergency-level use.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersBoth can sweat and collapse. Temperature of skin/sweat, thirst, tongue moisture and pulse speed determine Yin versus Yang failure.
Memory cueYin collapse is hot and sticky; Yang collapse is cold and pouring.
SafetyBoth descriptions represent medical emergencies such as shock, severe dehydration, sepsis or cardiac illness. Call emergency services; do not attempt herbal self-treatment.

Possible exam questions · Study prompts
