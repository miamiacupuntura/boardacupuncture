Bladder Damp-Heat vs. Small Intestine Full Heat

Use turbidity and heaviness versus Heart-channel mouth ulcers to distinguish two burning urinary patterns.

Fast differentiation clues

ClueBladder Damp-HeatSmall Intestine Full Heat
SharedBoth cause burning, scant dark difficult urination and a red tongue with yellow coat.
Fast distinctionBladder Damp-Heat: turbid/foul urine, heaviness and a thicker sticky coat.Small Intestine Heat: tongue/mouth ulcers, thirst and Heart-channel Heat signs.

Clinical table

CompareBladder Damp-HeatSmall Intestine Full Heat
Added clueDampness makes urine turbid and body heavy.Paired Heart pathway links oral ulcers to urine.
Formula associationBa Zheng San.Dao Chi San, Dao Chi Qing Xin Tang or Liang Ge San.

Exam and safety

Why it mattersTurbidity identifies Dampness; oral ulcers identify the Heart–Small Intestine pathway.
Memory cueBladder Damp-Heat clouds urine; Small Intestine Heat links mouth to urine.
SafetyFever, flank pain, blood, vomiting, pregnancy or urinary retention requires urgent medical care.
