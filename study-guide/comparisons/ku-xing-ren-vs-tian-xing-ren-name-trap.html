Trap 9 of 22

Kǔ Xìng Rén 苦杏仁 vs. Tián Xìng Rén 甜杏仁

Bitter and sweet apricot kernels differ in medicinal strength and cyanogenic risk.

Memory rule

AnchorKǔ is bitter, stronger and riskier; Tián is sweet and milder.

Correct identity

NameIdentityJob
Kǔ Xìng Rén 苦杏仁Bitter apricot kernelDescends Lung Qi, stops cough/wheezing and moistens Intestines.
Tián Xìng Rén 甜杏仁Sweet apricot kernelFood-like, milder and mainly moistening.

Exam and safety

Exam keyA strong cough-and-wheeze indication usually refers to Kǔ Xìng Rén, not the sweet culinary kernel.
Safety alertKǔ Xìng Rén contains amygdalin and may release cyanide; use processed material and professional dosing. Keep raw kernels from children.
