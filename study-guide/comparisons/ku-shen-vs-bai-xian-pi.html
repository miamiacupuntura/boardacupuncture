Herb comparison

Ku Shen vs. Bai Xian Pi

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueKu ShenBai Xian Pi
Shared clueBoth clear lower-burner and skin Damp-Heat and promote urination; they may treat eczema, Damp sores, jaundice and vaginal discharge.
Fast distinctionExpels Wind and kills parasites; stronger for itching and gynecological pruritus.Enters muscles and flesh, unblocks Blood vessels and facilitates joint movement.

Comparative answer key

CompareKu ShenBai Xian Pi
Medicinal nameSophorae flavescentis Radix.Dictamni Cortex.
CategoryHerbs that Clear Heat and Dry Dampness.Herbs that Clear Heat and Dry Dampness.
Primary emphasisExpels Wind, kills parasites and stops itching.Unblocks Blood vessels in the muscles and flesh and facilitates joint movement.
Decisive indicationSkin itching, vaginal discharge and gynecological pruritus.Wind or Damp painful obstruction with Heat signs.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersThe two herbs overlap strongly in skin Damp-Heat; parasites and pruritus favor Ku Shen, while heated painful obstruction favors Bai Xian Pi.
Memory cueKu Shen stops parasitic itching; Bai Xian Pi frees heated joints.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), p. 199. Source Exam Key CMID 9006, question 4.
