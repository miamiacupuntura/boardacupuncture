Zēng Yè Tāng vs. Zēng Yè Chéng Qì Tāng

Increase Fluids floats the dry boat; adding Chéng Qì purgatives moves the stool when water alone fails.

Memory rule

AnchorZēng Yè adds water; Zēng Yè Chéng Qì adds water plus Dà Huáng/Máng Xiāo.

Decisive separation

FormulaArchitectureDecisive presentation
Zēng Yè Tāng 增液汤Xuán Shēn + Mài Mén Dōng + Shēng DìConstipation from severe fluid/Yin injury—“add water to float the boat.”
Zēng Yè Chéng Qì Tāng 增液承气汤Zēng Yè Tāng + Dà Huáng + Máng XiāoFluid injury plus Heat accumulation and dry hard stool requiring purgation.

Exam and safety

Exam keyIf enrichment alone cannot move the dry stool and excess Heat remains, add the Chéng Qì branch.
SafetyThe Chéng Qì version is a strong purge; rule out mechanical obstruction, pregnancy, severe frailty and dehydration.
