Xiao Chai Hu Tang

Half-exterior, half-interior Shaoyang disorder.

High-yield summary

CategoryHarmonize the Shaoyang
PatternHalf-exterior, half-interior Shaoyang disorder.
ActionHarmonizes Shaoyang, vents the pathogen, clears Heat and supports upright Qi.
Decisive clueAlternating chills and fever, bitter taste, dry throat, dizziness, hypochondriac fullness and nausea.

Ingredients

Chai Hu, Huang Qin, Ban Xia, Sheng Jiang, Ren Shen, Zhi Gan Cao and Da Zao.

Commercial preparations and lineages may vary; always verify the source being used.

Differentiation and memory

Do not confuseDa Chai Hu Tang adds purgative/Heat-clearing force for a more excess Shaoyang–Yangming presentation; Xiao Chai Hu Tang is the classic harmonizer.
Memory cueChai Hu vents the pivot; Huang Qin clears inside; the remaining herbs harmonize and protect Qi.

Safety

Persistent fever, jaundice, severe abdominal pain, dehydration, pregnancy or significant liver disease requires medical evaluation.
