Si Wu Tang vs. Xiao Yao San

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueSi Wu TangXiao Yao San
Shared clueBoth nourish Liver Blood and contain Dang Gui and Bai Shao; both are commonly tested in menstrual presentations.
Fast distinctionBlood deficiency is primary: pale complexion, dizziness, scant or irregular menses and a thin pulse.Liver-Qi constraint is primary: emotional symptoms, rib-side distention, PMS and digestive weakness.

Comparative answer key

CompareSi Wu TangXiao Yao San
Primary strategyNourish and regulate Blood.Spread Liver Qi, strengthen Spleen and nourish Blood.
Root patternBlood deficiency, sometimes with mild Blood stasis.Liver constraint overacting on a deficient Spleen with insufficient Blood.
Decisive cluePale, dry and undernourished Blood signs dominate.Mood changes, sighing, rib-side or breast distention and appetite changes dominate.
PulseThin or choppy.Wiry and weak.
Exam shortcutBuild Blood directly.Move Liver Qi while protecting Qi and Blood.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersMenstrual irregularity is shared. Emotional and digestive constraint points to Xiao Yao San; uncomplicated Blood deficiency points to Si Wu Tang.
Memory cueSi Wu fills the Blood; Xiao Yao frees the Liver and feeds the Blood.
SafetyHeavy, unexplained or pregnancy-related bleeding requires medical evaluation rather than formula selection alone.
