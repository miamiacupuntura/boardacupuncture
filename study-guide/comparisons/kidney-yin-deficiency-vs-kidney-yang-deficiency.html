Kidney Yin Deficiency vs. Kidney Yang Deficiency

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueKidney Yin DeficiencyKidney Yang Deficiency
Shared clueBoth may cause weak low back and knees, tinnitus, reproductive weakness, urinary changes and a deep weak pulse.
Fast distinctionEmpty Heat and dryness: night sweating, five-center heat, dry mouth, red peeled tongue.Cold and fluid failure: cold limbs, edema, frequent clear urine, pale swollen tongue.

Comparative answer key

CompareKidney Yin DeficiencyKidney Yang Deficiency
Thermal signHeat in the afternoon/night.Persistent internal Cold.
FluidsInsufficient fluids and Essence.Fluids accumulate because transformation is weak.
Tongue / pulseRed, little coat; thin-rapid-deep.Pale, swollen, wet; deep-slow-weak.
Formula examplesLiu Wei Di Huang Wan; Zuo Gui Wan.Jin Gui Shen Qi Wan; You Gui Wan.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersThe shared Kidney symptoms identify the organ; temperature, moisture and water metabolism identify Yin versus Yang.
Memory cueYin deficiency is dry and hot; Yang deficiency is wet and cold.
SafetyEdema, major urinary changes, infertility or persistent night sweats require biomedical assessment as well as pattern differentiation.

Possible exam questions · Study prompts
