Appendix B pair · 8 of 51

Gé Gēn + Shēng Má

葛根 + 升麻

One picture, one job

PicturePush the measles rash outward.
Combined jobRelease the muscle layer and vent an early rash that is failing to erupt fully.
CategoryVent Rashes · Release the Muscle Layer

How the pair works

HerbRole within the pair
Gé GēnReleases the muscle layer, raises clear Yang and generates fluids.
Shēng MáRaises and vents toxin/rash toward the surface.

Exam focus and safety

CluesEarly measles or another appropriately diagnosed rash that is incomplete or slow to emerge.
Signature formulaShēng Má Gé Gēn Tāng.
TrapStop the venting strategy once the rash is fully out; do not use it to force a late, dark or complicated rash.
PreparationUse as part of a professionally differentiated formula, not as isolated rash self-care.
SafetySuspected measles requires immediate public-health coordination and airborne isolation. Breathing difficulty, dehydration, confusion or seizure is an emergency.
