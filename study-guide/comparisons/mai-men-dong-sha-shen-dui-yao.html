麦门冬 + 沙参

One picture, one job

PictureTwo moist roots for a dry cough and dry mouth.
Combined jobNourish Lung and Stomach Yin, generate fluids and moisten dryness.
CategoryNourish Lung–Stomach Yin

How the pair works

HerbRole within the pair
Mài Mén DōngEnriches Lung, Stomach and Heart Yin while generating fluids.
Shā ShēnNourishes Lung and Stomach Yin and clears deficiency Heat from dryness.

Exam focus and safety

CluesDry cough, dry throat or mouth, thirst and scanty sputum from injured fluids.
Signature formulaShā Shēn Mài Mén Dōng Tāng; Yī Guàn Jiān.
TrapThis is dry Yin/fluid injury, not Damp-Phlegm with a greasy coat and copious sputum.
PreparationUse verified Shā Shēn species and formula-specific preparation; regional products are not automatically interchangeable.
SafetyShā Shēn is traditionally incompatible with Lí Lú. Use cautiously with marked Spleen deficiency, loose stools or Dampness.
