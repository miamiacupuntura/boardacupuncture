Liu Wei Di Huang Wan vs. Zhi Bai Di Huang Wan vs. Qi Ju Di Huang Wan

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueLiu Wei Di Huang WanZhi Bai Di Huang WanQi Ju Di Huang Wan
Shared clueAll use the Liu Wei Di Huang Wan base to nourish Liver-Kidney Yin. The added herbs reveal the branch pattern.
Fast distinctionFoundational Liver-Kidney Yin deficiency.The same deficiency with pronounced deficient Fire.The same deficiency with dry, blurred or weak eyes.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersThe two derivatives do not replace the base diagnosis; they add a decisive branch—Fire or eyes.
Memory cueLiu Wei is the root; Zhi Bai cools Fire; Qi Ju opens the eyes.
SafetyThese rich formulas may aggravate weak digestion, loose stools or Dampness. Eye symptoms with sudden vision loss, pain or neurologic signs require urgent evaluation.
