Cang Er Zi San

Wind obstructing the nasal passages.

High-yield summary

CategoryDispel Wind · Open the Nose
PatternWind obstructing the nasal passages.
ActionDisperses Wind, opens the nasal passages and relieves frontal sinus pain.
Decisive clueNasal obstruction with copious discharge, reduced smell and frontal or sinus pressure.

Ingredients

Cang Er Zi, Xin Yi Hua, Bai Zhi and Bo He; traditionally taken with scallion tea.

Commercial preparations and lineages may vary; always verify the source being used.

Differentiation and memory

Do not confuseXin Yi Qing Fei Tang is chosen when pronounced Lung Heat and toxic nasal signs dominate; Cang Er Zi San is the concise Wind-obstruction formula.
Memory cueCang Er Zi and Xin Yi Hua are the nasal-opening pair.

Safety

Cang Er Zi is toxic in excessive doses. Unilateral obstruction, facial swelling, high fever or visual change requires prompt assessment.
