Trap 11 of 22

Bǎi Hé 百合 vs. Bǎo Hé Wán 保和丸

A single herb and a digestive formula sound alike but belong to different clinical worlds.

Memory rule

AnchorBǎi Hé is lily bulb; Bǎo Hé Wán is the “Preserve Harmony” food-stagnation formula.

Correct identity

NameIdentityJob
Bǎi Hé 百合Lilii Bulbus / lily bulbNourishes Lung-Heart Yin, moistens dryness and calms the Spirit.
Bǎo Hé Wán 保和丸Multi-herb formulaReduces Food Stagnation and harmonizes the Stomach.

Exam and safety

Exam keyDry cough or Yin-related restlessness points to Bǎi Hé; focal food retention points to Bǎo Hé Wán.
Safety alertDo not prescribe from sound alone: one is an ingredient, the other a complete formula with a different treatment strategy.
