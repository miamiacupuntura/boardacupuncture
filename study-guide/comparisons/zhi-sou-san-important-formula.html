Zhi Sou San

Residual external Wind lodging in the Lung after an exterior illness.

High-yield summary

CategoryStop Cough · Release Residual Wind
PatternResidual external Wind lodging in the Lung after an exterior illness.
ActionStops cough, transforms Phlegm and gently releases residual exterior Wind.
Decisive cluePersistent cough after other exterior symptoms have largely resolved, with itchy throat and scant sputum.

Ingredients

Zi Wan, Bai Bu, Bai Qian, Jie Geng, Jing Jie, Chen Pi and Gan Cao.

Commercial preparations and lineages may vary; always verify the source being used.

Differentiation and memory

Do not confuseSang Ju Yin treats active early Wind-Heat with cough and fever; Zhi Sou San treats the lingering cough after the main exterior disease has passed.
Memory cueThree direct cough herbs meet Jie Geng and Jing Jie to clear the last Wind from the Lung.

Safety

Persistent or worsening cough, blood, fever, weight loss, chest pain, breathing difficulty or low oxygen requires medical assessment.
