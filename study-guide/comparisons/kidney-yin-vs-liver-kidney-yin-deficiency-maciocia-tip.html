Kidney Yin vs. Liver–Kidney Yin Deficiency

Recognize when Kidney-Yin depletion extends to the Liver and adds eye, tendon and menstrual signs.

Fast differentiation clues

ClueKidney Yin DeficiencyLiver–Kidney Yin Deficiency
SharedBoth cause tinnitus, dizziness, dry throat, night sweating, weak low back and a red peeled tongue.
Fast distinctionKidney-centered: low-back/knee weakness and urinary, sexual or Essence signs predominate.Liver joins: dry eyes, blurred vision, tendon weakness/cramps and scanty menstruation.

Clinical table

CompareKidney Yin DeficiencyLiver–Kidney Yin Deficiency
Added systemKidney Yin and Essence signs.Liver eyes, sinews and Blood regulation join Kidney signs.
Formula associationZuo Gui Wan or Liu Wei Di Huang Wan.Zuo Gui Wan or Qi Ju Di Huang Wan.

Exam and safety

Why it mattersDry eyes, visual change, tendons and menstruation—not dizziness alone—show Liver involvement.
Memory cueKidney Yin weakens the root; Liver–Kidney Yin also dries eyes and sinews.
SafetySudden visual change, severe headache, neurologic symptoms or abnormal uterine bleeding needs medical evaluation.
