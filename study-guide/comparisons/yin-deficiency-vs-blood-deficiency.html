Pattern comparison

Yin Deficiency vs. Blood Deficiency

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueYin DeficiencyBlood Deficiency
Shared clueBoth fail to nourish tissues and can cause dizziness, insomnia, dryness, scanty menses and a thin pulse.
Fast distinctionDryness plus empty Heat: night sweating, five-center heat and red peeled tongue.Pallor plus poor nourishment: blurred vision, numbness, cramps and pale tongue.

Comparative answer key

CompareYin DeficiencyBlood Deficiency
Thermal signEmpty Heat is common.No Heat unless another pattern is present.
Primary tissue clueDry mouth, throat, skin or mucosa.Pale complexion, lips and nails.
Tongue / pulseRed with little coat; thin-rapid.Pale; thin or choppy.
Formula examplesLiu Wei Di Huang Wan; Tian Wang Bu Xin Dan by organ.Si Wu Tang; Gui Pi Tang by organ.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersBlood is part of Yin, but exam patterns distinguish pale Blood undernourishment from red, dry Yin depletion.
Memory cueBlood deficiency is pale; Yin deficiency is dry and hot.
SafetyPersistent night sweats, unexplained weight loss, heavy bleeding or severe anemia symptoms require medical evaluation.
