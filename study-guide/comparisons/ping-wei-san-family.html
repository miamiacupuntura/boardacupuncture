Ping Wei San Family

Root formula and architecture

RootPing Wei San dries Dampness, moves Qi and harmonizes a distended, heavy Middle Jiao.
ArchitectureCang Zhu dries and strengthens transport; Hou Po and Chen Pi move Qi; Gan Cao harmonizes. Added aromatic, Exterior, Shaoyang or water-draining units define each branch.

Study rule

How to chooseStay with Ping Wei for pure Middle Damp; vomiting adds Ban Xia/Huo Xiang, Exterior signs choose Huo Xiang Zheng Qi, Shaoyang signs choose Chai Ping, and edema/urine signs choose Wei Ling.
Memory cuePing Wei dries; Bu Huan stops vomiting; Huo Xiang releases; Chai Ping harmonizes; Wei Ling drains.
SafetyThese drying aromatic formulas can injure Yin or fluids. Persistent vomiting/diarrhea, dehydration, severe abdominal pain, jaundice or rapidly worsening edema requires medical evaluation; Ban Xia must be processed.
