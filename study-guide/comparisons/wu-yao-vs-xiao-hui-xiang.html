Herb comparison

Linderae Radix (wu yao) vs. Foeniculi Fructus (xiao hui xiang)

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueWu YaoXiao Hui Xiang
Shared clueBoth are acrid, warm and aromatic; both warm the Liver and Kidneys, expel Cold and relieve pain.
Fast distinctionMoves Qi broadly through upper and lower body and adds urinary frequency or incontinence indications.Focuses on middle and lower burners, especially Liver-channel Cold with lower-abdominal or hernia-like pain.

Comparative answer key

CompareWu YaoXiao Hui Xiang
Medicinal nameLinderae Radix.Foeniculi Fructus.
CategoryHerbs that Regulate the Qi.Herbs that Warm the Interior and Expel Cold.
Shared properties and actionsAcrid, warm and aromatic; warm Liver and Kidneys, expel Cold and relieve pain.
Reach and directionDisseminates and unblocks Qi in the Spleen and Lungs above and reaches Liver, Kidneys and Bladder below.Does not reach the upper burner; concentrates on the middle and lower burners.
Primary emphasisPromotes Qi movement while also warming Liver and Kidneys.More strongly warms Liver and Kidneys, expels Cold and relieves pain while regulating Stomach Qi.
Decisive indicationCold and Qi stagnation with chest, flank, epigastric or abdominal pain; urinary frequency or incontinence from Kidney-Yang or Bladder deficiency Cold.Lower-abdominal pain along the Liver channel, especially Cold Shan or hernia-like bulging disorders.
Channel emphasisBladder, Kidney, Lung and Spleen.Kidney, Liver, Spleen and Stomach.
Formula cluesWu Yao Tang; Si Mo Tang; Ge Xia Zhu Yu Tang.Shao Fu Zhu Yu Tang.
Shared formulasNuan Gan Jian and Tian Tai Wu Yao San.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersBoth warm Cold in Liver-Kidney patterns and relieve pain. Broad Qi movement and urinary clues identify Wu Yao; stronger middle/lower warming and Liver-channel Cold pain identify Xiao Hui Xiang.
Memory cueWu Yao moves Qi everywhere; Xiao Hui Xiang warms Cold below.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), p. 698. Source Exam Keys CMID 9089, questions 13–22, and CMID 9098, questions 8–17.

Possible exam questions · Study prompts

  • Which properties and warming actions do both herbs share?
  • Which herb reaches both upper and lower body?
  • Which herb focuses on the middle and lower burners?
  • Which urinary clues favor Wu Yao, and which Liver-channel Cold clues favor Xiao Hui Xiang?
