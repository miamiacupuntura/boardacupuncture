Exam trap · Category correction

Rou Gui vs. Zhi Fu Zi vs. Lu Rong

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueRou GuiZhi Fu ZiLu Rong
Shared clueAll warm the Kidneys, fortify Yang and treat patterns of Kidney-Yang deficiency.
Fast distinctionAcrid, harsh, hot and drying: rapidly warm the Interior and expel excessive internal Cold.Sweet, warm, soft and moistening: gradually tonifies Yang, Essence, Blood and marrow.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersAll three warm Kidney Yang, but the exam separates rapid expulsion of internal Cold from gradual restoration of deficient Essence, Blood and marrow.
Exam trapCMID 9005 questions 4–6 incorrectly state that Zhi Fu Zi belongs to Herbs that Tonify the Yang. Its correct category is Herbs that Warm the Interior and Expel Cold; only Lu Rong belongs to Herbs that Tonify the Yang in this comparison.
Memory cueRou Gui and Zhi Fu Zi drive out Cold; Lu Rong rebuilds deeply.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), p. 766. Source Exam Keys CMID 8952, questions 2–8, and CMID 9005, questions 1–6. The category statement in questions 4–6 is corrected here.
