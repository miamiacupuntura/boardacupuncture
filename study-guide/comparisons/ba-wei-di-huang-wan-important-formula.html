Ba Wei Di Huang Wan

Kidney Yang deficiency with weakness of Essence and water transformation.

High-yield summary

CategoryWarm and Tonify Kidney Yang
PatternKidney Yang deficiency with weakness of Essence and water transformation.
ActionWarms Kidney Yang on a Liu Wei Di Huang Wan foundation and supports transformation of fluids.
Decisive clueCold low back/limbs, clear abundant urine or edema with a pale wet tongue and deep weak pulse.

Differentiation and memory
