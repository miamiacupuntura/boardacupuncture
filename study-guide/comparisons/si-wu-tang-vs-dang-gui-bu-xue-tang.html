Si Wu Tang vs. Dang Gui Bu Xue Tang

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueSi Wu TangDang Gui Bu Xue Tang
Shared clueBoth nourish Blood and contain Dang Gui, but they build Blood through different strategies.
Fast distinctionBlood deficiency with impaired circulation: pale or irregular menses, abdominal pain and a thin or choppy pulse.Severe Qi deficiency cannot generate/hold Blood, causing floating Yang: fever, thirst, red face and a large deficient pulse after blood loss or exhaustion.

Comparative answer key

CompareSi Wu TangDang Gui Bu Xue Tang
Root mechanismBlood is deficient and circulation is sluggish.Qi is too deficient to produce Blood and contain Yang.
Treatment directionNourish and regulate Blood directly.Strongly tonify Qi so Blood can be generated.
Key ingredientsShu Di Huang, Dang Gui, Bai Shao and Chuan Xiong.A high proportion of Huang Qi with a small amount of Dang Gui.
Decisive clueMenstrual/Blood signs with mild stasis.Heat-like floating-Yang signs with a large, forceless pulse.
Exam shortcutFour Blood herbs.Qi makes Blood.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersApparent Heat in Dang Gui Bu Xue Tang is not an excess-Heat pattern. The large but empty pulse and severe deficiency reveal floating Yang caused by loss of Qi and Blood.
Memory cueSi Wu builds and moves Blood; Dang Gui Bu Xue uses Huang Qi to manufacture Blood.
SafetyUnexplained fever, active heavy bleeding, fainting, shortness of breath or marked anemia symptoms requires medical evaluation.
SourceSource bank: FWB 8.2.2, Comparison Formulas to Tonify, activity 3; corroborated with individual formula profiles.
