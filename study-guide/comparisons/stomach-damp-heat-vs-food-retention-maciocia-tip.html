Batch 6 · Stomach · Tip 8 of 10

Stomach Damp-Heat vs. Food Retention

Use Heat, heaviness and coating quality versus a dietary trigger and sour regurgitation.

Fast differentiation clues

ClueStomach Damp-HeatFood Retention
SharedBoth cause epigastric fullness, nausea, poor appetite and a thick sticky or greasy coating.
Fast distinctionDamp-Heat: heaviness, bitter/sticky taste, Heat signs and a yellow sticky coat.Food Retention: overeating trigger, sour regurgitation, foul breath and undigested-food signs.

Clinical table

CompareStomach Damp-HeatFood Retention
Primary mechanismDampness and Heat obstruct the middle.Food accumulation blocks Stomach descent.
PulseSlippery-rapid or soggy-rapid.Slippery and forceful.
Formula associationLian Po Yin.Bao He Wan or Zhi Shi Dao Zhi Wan.

Exam and safety

Why it mattersBoth can have a thick coat; systemic Heat/heaviness versus sour dietary accumulation decides.
Memory cueDamp-Heat feels heavy and hot; retained food tastes sour and foul.
SafetyFever, jaundice, persistent vomiting, severe pain, dehydration or inability to pass stool/gas requires medical evaluation.
