Herb comparison

Huang Qi vs. Ren Shen

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueHuang QiRen Shen
Shared clueBoth are important primal-Qi tonics in the Herbs that Tonify the Qi category and enter the Spleen and Lung.
Fast distinctionQuicker, ascending action that raises Yang and addresses sinking Qi.Stronger, longer-lasting primal-Qi tonic that also nourishes Yin.
Unique action clueStops sweating, discharges pus, generates flesh and facilitates urination.Deeper and more sustained Qi support.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersBoth tonify primal Qi and share many formulas. Ascending Yang, sinking Qi and exterior or tissue-healing actions identify Huang Qi; greater strength, duration and Yin nourishment identify Ren Shen.
Memory cueHuang Qi raises and protects; Ren Shen restores more deeply and lasts longer.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), p. 721. Source Exam Key CMID 8981, questions 1–7.
