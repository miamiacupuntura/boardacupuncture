Phlegm Misting the Mind vs. Phlegm-Fire Harassing the Heart

Use activity level, temperature and tongue coating to separate cold-turbid closure from hot agitated disturbance.

Fast differentiation clues

CluePhlegm Misting the MindPhlegm-Fire Harassing the Heart
SharedBoth obstruct the Heart orifices and can cause abnormal behavior, confused speech and a slippery pulse.
Fast distinctionCold/turbid and withdrawn: dullness, depression, muttering, aphasia, stupor or rattling phlegm with a greasy white coat.Hot and explosive: agitation, shouting, uncontrolled laughter/crying, insomnia or mania with a greasy yellow coat.

Clinical table

ComparePhlegm Misting the MindPhlegm-Fire Harassing the Heart
BehaviorSlowed, withdrawn or unconscious.Agitated, restless or manic.
Tongue / pulseSwollen with sticky white coat; slippery-slow.Red with sticky yellow coat; slippery-rapid.
Formula associationDi Tan Tang by presentation.Wen Dan Tang or Gun Tan Wan by severity and constitution.

Exam and safety

Why it mattersBoth are Phlegm, but Fire activates and agitates while cold turbidity closes and suppresses.
Memory cueWhite Phlegm mutes; yellow Phlegm-Fire erupts.
SafetyNew psychosis, violent behavior, coma, stroke signs, seizure or sudden confusion requires emergency assessment.

Review prompts

  • Which presentation is withdrawn?
  • Which has a yellow greasy coat?
  • What emergency conditions can mimic both?
