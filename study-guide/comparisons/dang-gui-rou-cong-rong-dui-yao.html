HerbRole within the pair

Exam focus and safety

CluesConstipation in an older or depleted person with cold low back, weakness and frequent clear urine.
Signature formulaJì Chuān Jiān.
TrapLoose stools or Damp accumulation means the moistening direction is wrong.
PreparationWine-processing may strengthen Ròu Cōng Róng’s warming and moving direction when the formula specifies it.
SafetyAvoid self-treatment for severe abdominal pain, vomiting, rigid distention or no stool and no gas; these may indicate obstruction.
