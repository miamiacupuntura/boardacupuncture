Misleading name 2 of 6

Bǔ Yáng Huán Wǔ Tāng 补阳还五汤

Despite “supplement Yang,” the formula primarily tonifies Qi and moves Blood after stroke.

Name versus reality

What it suggestsThe title appears to promise a Kidney-Yang tonic.
RealityA very large Huáng Qí emphasis supplements Qi while Dāng Guī Wěi, Chì Sháo, Chuān Xiōng, Táo Rén, Hóng Huā and Dì Lóng move Blood and channels.

How to answer

Decisive clueChronic post-stroke weakness or hemiplegia from Qi deficiency with Blood stasis—not acute stroke treatment.
MemoryHuáng Qí supplies the pushing Qi; the remaining herbs reopen the “five tenths” of the body.

Safety

AlertSudden facial droop, one-sided weakness, speech change or severe headache is an emergency stroke protocol. Blood-moving herbs require anticoagulant/bleeding screening.
