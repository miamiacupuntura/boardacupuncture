Herb comparison

Akebiae Caulis (mu tong) vs. Junci Medulla (deng xin cao)

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueMu TongDeng Xin Cao
Shared cluesBoth clear and drain Heart Fire, direct Small Intestine Heat out through urine, and may be combined for hot painful urinary dribbling.
Fast distinctionStronger, bitter-cold action with oral ulcers, red eyes and Qi/Blood obstruction.Milder action with childhood night terrors and Lung-Heat sore throat.

Comparative answer key

CompareMu Tong (Akebiae Caulis)Deng Xin Cao (Junci Medulla)
Shared categoryHerbs that Drain Dampness.
Relative strengthBitter, cold and more powerful.Relatively milder.
Heart-Heat clueMouth/tongue ulcers and swollen red eyes.Children's night terrors due to Heart Heat.
Additional actionUnblocks Qi and Blood for amenorrhea, insufficient lactation or painful obstruction.Drains Lung Heat for sore throat.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersThe shared Heart/Small Intestine and urinary actions make strength and secondary indications decisive.
Common confusionOral ulcers, red eyes or Blood obstruction favors Mu Tong; childhood night terrors or Lung-Heat sore throat favors Deng Xin Cao.
How it may appearA “more powerful” clue selects Mu Tong; a “milder” clue selects Deng Xin Cao.
Source question formatsQuestion 3 in CMID 8919 and question 1 in CMID 8929 are Matching.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), p. 288. Source Exam Keys CMID 8919 and CMID 8929.
