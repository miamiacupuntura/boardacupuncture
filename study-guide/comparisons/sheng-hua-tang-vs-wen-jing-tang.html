Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueSheng Hua TangWen Jing Tang

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersThe actions overlap, but timing is decisive. Sheng Hua Tang is postpartum; Wen Jing Tang is a broader chronic uterine Cold-deficiency pattern.
Memory cueSheng Hua transforms after birth; Wen Jing warms the menstrual channel.
SafetyPostpartum hemorrhage, fever, foul lochia, dizziness or severe pain requires urgent obstetric evaluation.
