Herb comparison

E Jiao vs. He Shou Wu vs. Gui Ban Jiao

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueE JiaoHe Shou WuGui Ban Jiao
Shared clueAll are rich tonifying substances, but they emphasize Blood, Liver-hair actions or deepest Yin.
Fast distinctionBlood aspect, Lung nourishment and Dryness moistening.Liver Blood, Wind expulsion and blackening hair and beard.Powerfully tonifies deepest Yin and fills the Conception Vessel.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersThe three can appear in deep deficiency patterns, but Lung Dryness, Liver-hair signs and ultimate-Yin steaming Heat separate them.
Memory cueE Jiao moistens Lung; He Shou Wu blackens hair; Gui Ban Jiao fills deepest Yin.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), p. 757. Source Exam Key CMID 8948, question 8.
