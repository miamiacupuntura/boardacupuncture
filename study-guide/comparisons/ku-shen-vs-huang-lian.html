Herb comparison

Ku Shen vs. Huang Lian

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueKu ShenHuang Lian
Shared clueBoth are very bitter and cold, clear Heat, dry Dampness and address Heart-related problems.
Fast distinctionDrains Damp-Heat through urine, reaches the Small Intestine and kills Damp-Heat parasites.Primarily cools Fire in the Heart organ.

Comparative answer key

CompareKu ShenHuang Lian
Medicinal nameSophorae flavescentis Radix.Coptidis Rhizoma.

Elimination routeDrains Damp-Heat outward through the urine.No urinary-drainage distinction is assigned in these questions.

Parasite clueKills parasites generated by Damp-Heat.No corresponding parasite action is assigned here.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersBoth are bitter-cold Heart-related Damp-Heat herbs. Urination, Small Intestine, stronger drying or parasites point to Ku Shen; direct Heart-organ Fire points to Huang Lian.
Memory cueKu Shen drains out and dries; Huang Lian cools the Heart directly.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), p. 146. Source Exam Keys CMID 8980, questions 3–4, and CMID 9006, questions 2–3.
