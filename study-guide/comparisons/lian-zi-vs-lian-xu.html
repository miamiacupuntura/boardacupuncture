Herb comparison

Lian Zi vs. Lian Xu

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueLian ZiLian Xu
Shared clueBoth come from the lotus plant and belong to the Herbs that Stabilize and Bind.
Fast distinctionLotus seed; the source assigns it the shared category without the intensified astringency clue.Lotus stamen; more astringent and specifically stabilizes watery semen and uncontrolled spermatorrhea.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersThe names share “Lian,” and both stabilize and bind. The Exam Key distinguishes them by lotus part and the stronger astringency of Lian Xu.
Memory cueLian Zi is the lotus seed; Lian Xu is the more astringent lotus stamen.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), p. 882. Source Exam Key CMID 9010, question 1.
