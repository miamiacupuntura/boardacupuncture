Bladder Damp-Heat vs. Damp-Cold

Use burning, urine color and temperature to separate two obstructive urinary patterns.

Fast differentiation clues

ClueBladder Damp-HeatBladder Damp-Cold
SharedBoth cause difficult urination, lower abdominal heaviness and a slippery pulse.
Fast distinctionDamp-Heat: burning, dark scant urine, urgency and sticky yellow coat.Damp-Cold: turbid urine, marked heaviness, little burning and sticky white coat.

Clinical table

CompareBladder Damp-HeatBladder Damp-Cold
TemperatureHot and Damp.Cold and Damp.
UrineDark, scant and burning.Turbid, difficult and less painful.
Formula associationBa Zheng San.Ba Zheng San or Shi Wei San in the supplied table.

Exam and safety

Why it mattersBurning and dark urine identify Heat; turbidity with Cold/heaviness identifies Damp-Cold.
Memory cueDamp-Heat burns dark; Damp-Cold blocks turbid.
SafetyFever, flank pain, vomiting, blood in urine, pregnancy or inability to urinate requires urgent medical care.
