Long Dan Xie Gan Tang

Liver Fire blazing upward or Liver/Gallbladder Damp-Heat pouring downward.

High-yield summary

CategoryDrain Liver Fire · Clear Liver/GB Damp-Heat
PatternLiver Fire blazing upward or Liver/Gallbladder Damp-Heat pouring downward.
ActionDrains Liver Fire, clears Damp-Heat and protects Yin and Blood.
Decisive clueEither red painful eyes/headache and irritability above, or burning genital/urinary discharge and itching below, with a wiry rapid pulse.

Ingredients

Long Dan Cao, Huang Qin, Zhi Zi, Ze Xie, Mu Tong, Che Qian Zi, Dang Gui, Sheng Di Huang, Chai Hu and Sheng Gan Cao.

Commercial preparations and lineages may vary; always verify the source being used.

Differentiation and memory

Do not confuseJia Wei Xiao Yao San clears milder Heat from Liver constraint with deficiency; Long Dan Xie Gan Tang treats a strong excess Fire/Damp-Heat picture.
Memory cueLong Dan drains the Liver; three urinary herbs guide Damp-Heat down and out.

Safety

Severe eye pain/vision change, jaundice, fever, pelvic pain, pregnancy or blood in urine requires prompt medical evaluation.
