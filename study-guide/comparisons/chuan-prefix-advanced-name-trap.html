Chuān 川 Is an Address, Not a Family

Chuān usually signals Sichuan origin or tradition; the medicinals are botanically unrelated.

Memory rule

AnchorRead the word after Chuān—the address does not tell the job.

Exam and safety

Exam keySichuan prefix never licenses family-level substitution.
SafetyChuān Wū is aconite-toxic; Chuān Liàn Zǐ has liver-toxicity concerns; Chuān Mù Tōng requires exact botanical verification.
