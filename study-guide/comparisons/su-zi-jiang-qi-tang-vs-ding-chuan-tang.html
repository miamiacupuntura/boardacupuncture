Formula comparison

Su Zi Jiang Qi Tang vs. Ding Chuan Tang

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueSu Zi Jiang Qi TangDing Chuan Tang
Shared clueBoth descend rebellious Lung Qi, transform Phlegm and relieve cough or wheezing; both may contain Su Zi, Ban Xia and Gan Cao.
Fast distinctionExcess above, deficiency below: Cold-Phlegm with weak Kidney grasping and copious thin white sputum.Exterior Wind-Cold with internal Phlegm-Heat: wheezing with thick yellow sputum and mixed Cold/Heat signs.

Comparative answer key

CompareSu Zi Jiang Qi TangDing Chuan Tang
Thermal patternCold-Phlegm with lower-burner deficiency.Phlegm-Heat constrained by external Wind-Cold.
Sputum clueCopious, thin and white.Copious, thick and yellow.
Distinct actionWarms the lower burner and helps Kidneys grasp Qi while directing Lung Qi down.Clears Phlegm-Heat, releases external constraint and arrests wheezing.
Decisive signsShortness of breath worse with exertion, cold limbs or low-back weakness.Fever/chills, chest constraint and loud wheezing with Heat signs.
Exam shortcutCold above + deficiency below.Cold outside + Heat-Phlegm inside.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersBoth treat wheezing with Phlegm. Sputum color and the lower-burner deficiency signs separate them quickly.
Memory cueSu Zi descends cold Qi and supports below; Ding Chuan clears hot Phlegm and stops wheeze.
SafetyDing Chuan Tang contains Ma Huang and requires cardiovascular and medication screening. Severe or rapidly worsening breathing difficulty is an emergency.
