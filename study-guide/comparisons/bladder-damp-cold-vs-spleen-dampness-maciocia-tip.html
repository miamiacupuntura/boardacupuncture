Bladder Damp-Cold vs. Spleen Dampness

Use urinary turbidity versus digestive dysfunction to locate a cold Damp pattern.

Fast differentiation clues

ClueBladder Damp-ColdSpleen Dampness
SharedBoth cause heaviness, a sticky white coat, nausea and a slippery or soggy pulse.
Fast distinctionBladder: difficult turbid urination and lower-abdominal heaviness.Spleen: poor appetite, epigastric fullness, loose stool and whole-body heaviness.

Clinical table

CompareBladder Damp-ColdSpleen Dampness
LocationLower burner and urinary passage.Middle burner and digestion.
Formula associationBa Zheng San or Shi Wei San in the supplied table.Ping Wei San or Huo Xiang Zheng Qi San.

Exam and safety

Why it mattersUrine versus stool/appetite localizes otherwise similar Damp-Cold signs.
Memory cueBladder Dampness clouds urine; Spleen Dampness burdens digestion.
SafetyUrinary retention, fever, flank pain, persistent diarrhea or dehydration requires assessment.
