Herb comparison

Pao Jiang vs. Gan Jiang vs. Sheng Jiang

Study tip · Clues to help answer

CluePao JiangGan JiangSheng Jiang
Shared clueAll are ginger preparations, but processing changes their strongest clinical direction.
Fast distinctionBlast-fried: stops bleeding from Deficiency Cold.Dried: most strongly warms the Middle and expels Interior Cold.Fresh: promotes sweating and disperses Exterior Cold.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersThe plant is the same, but blast-fried, dried and fresh forms point respectively to bleeding, Interior Cold and Exterior Cold.
Memory cuePao Jiang stops Blood; Gan Jiang warms within; Sheng Jiang releases outside.
