Ping Wei San

Damp-Cold stagnation obstructing the Spleen and Stomach.

High-yield summary

CategoryDry Dampness · Harmonize the Stomach
PatternDamp-Cold stagnation obstructing the Spleen and Stomach.
ActionDries Dampness, strengthens the Spleen, moves Qi and harmonizes the Stomach.
Decisive clueEpigastric/abdominal fullness, poor appetite, nausea, heaviness and a thick greasy white coat.

Ingredients

Cang Zhu, Hou Po, Chen Pi and Zhi Gan Cao, prepared with Sheng Jiang and Da Zao.

Commercial preparations and lineages may vary; always verify the source being used.

Differentiation and memory

Do not confuseBao He Wan requires retained food with sour-foul signs; Ping Wei San centers on Dampness and a greasy coat.
Memory cueCang Zhu dries, Hou Po opens fullness, Chen Pi moves and Gan Cao harmonizes.

Safety

Persistent vomiting, severe pain, gastrointestinal bleeding, dehydration or unexplained weight loss requires medical evaluation.
