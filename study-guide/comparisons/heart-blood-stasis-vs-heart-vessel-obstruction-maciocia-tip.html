Heart Blood Stasis vs. Heart Vessel Obstruction

Distinguish a specific Blood-stasis mechanism from the broader vessel-obstruction category that may include Phlegm and Cold.

Fast differentiation clues

ClueHeart Blood StasisHeart Vessel Obstruction
SharedBoth may cause fixed chest pain, palpitations, purple lips/tongue and a choppy or irregular pulse.
Fast distinctionPure stasis emphasis: stabbing pain, worse at night, dark complexion and prominent purple spots or sublingual veins.Broader obstruction: stasis signs plus Phlegm, heaviness, sticky coat, Cold aggravation, wheeze or inability to lie flat.

Clinical table

CompareHeart Blood StasisHeart Vessel Obstruction
ScopeOne defined mechanism: Blood is static.Umbrella pattern: Blood stasis, Phlegm, Cold and Qi stagnation may combine.
Formula associationXue Fu Zhu Yu Tang by presentation.Gua Lou Xie Bai Ban Xia Tang, Zhi Shi Xie Bai Gui Zhi Tang or a stasis formula according to mechanism.

Exam and safety

Why it mattersHeart Blood stasis can be one component of Heart vessel obstruction; the latter asks which additional factor blocks the chest.
Memory cueStasis is one blocker; vessel obstruction names the whole traffic jam.
SafetyClassical pattern labels cannot rule out acute coronary syndrome, pulmonary embolism or arrhythmia. New chest pain is an emergency until assessed.

Review prompts
