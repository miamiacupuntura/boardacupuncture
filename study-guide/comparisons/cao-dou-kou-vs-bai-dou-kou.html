Herb comparison

Alpiniae katsumadai Semen (cao dou kou) vs. Amomi Fructus rotundus (bai dou kou)

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueCao Dou KouBai Dou Kou
Shared cluesBoth mobilize Qi, ease the Stomach, warm the middle and transform Dampness.
Decisive clueWarmer and better at drying turbid Dampness.Stronger Qi mobilization and effective dissemination of Lung Qi.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersThe herbs share all four headline functions, so relative strength in Qi movement, Lung dissemination, warmth and drying determines the answer.
Common confusionCao Dou Kou still mobilizes Qi, but less strongly; Bai Dou Kou still warms and transforms Dampness, but the source emphasizes its Qi-moving and Lung-disseminating advantage.
Source-data noteThe question labels the common category “Herbs that Relieve Food Stagnation,” while the exam title places Cao Dou Kou under “Aromatic Herbs that Transform Dampness.” The comparison therefore relies on the explicit shared functions rather than silently choosing between those labels.
How it may appearPronounced turbid Dampness requiring greater warmth favors Cao Dou Kou. A Lung Qi or stronger Qi-mobilization clue favors Bai Dou Kou.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), p. 485. Source Exam Key CMID 8905.
