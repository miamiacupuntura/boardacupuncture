Herb comparison

Mume Fructus (wu mei) vs. Schisandrae Fructus (wu wei zi)

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueWu MeiWu Wei Zi
Shared clueBoth restrain the Lungs to alleviate cough, generate fluids to relieve thirst and bind the Intestines to stop diarrhea.
Fast distinctionStronger for generating fluids, thirst, Stomach disharmony and roundworms.Stronger for restraining Lung Qi; also augments Qi, grasps Kidney Qi, secures Essence, stops sweating and calms the Spirit.

Comparative answer key

CompareWu MeiWu Wei Zi
Medicinal nameMume Fructus.Schisandrae Fructus.
CategoryHerbs that Stabilize and Bind.
Shared actionsRestrain Lung Qi, generate fluids and bind the Intestines.
Primary strengthMore strongly generates fluids and alleviates thirst.More powerfully restrains Lung Qi to stop cough.
Distinctive actionsHarmonizes the Stomach and expels roundworms.Augments Qi, grasps Kidney Qi, secures Essence, inhibits sweating and quiets the Spirit.
Additional bleeding clueImportant in irregular uterine bleeding or Blood in the urine.The comparison emphasizes Essence, sweating and Spirit rather than bleeding.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersBoth restrain Lung and Intestine and generate fluids. Thirst, Stomach, roundworm or bleeding clues favor Wu Mei; stronger cough restraint plus Kidney, Essence, sweating or Spirit clues favor Wu Wei Zi.
Memory cueWu Mei makes fluids and handles worms; Wu Wei Zi holds Lung Qi, Essence and sweat.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), p. 860. Source Exam Keys CMID 9087, question 4, and CMID 9088, question 1.

Possible exam questions · Study prompts

  • Which herb more strongly generates fluids and relieves thirst?
  • Which herb more strongly restrains Lung Qi?
  • Which herb grasps Kidney Qi, secures Essence and inhibits sweating?
