Pattern comparison

Taiyang vs. Yangming vs. Shaoyang

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueTaiyangYangmingShaoyang
Shared clueAll are Yang-stage presentations with fever, but location, fever-chill relationship and pulse identify the stage.
Fast distinctionExterior: fever/chills, occipital headache, stiff neck and floating pulse; sweat status separates excess from deficiency.Interior: high fever without chills, profuse sweat, great thirst or bowel excess with a strong/deep pulse.Pivot: alternating chills and fever, bitter taste, dry throat, dizziness, rib-side fullness and wiry pulse.

Comparative answer key

CompareTaiyangYangmingShaoyang
LocationExterior and channels.Interior Qi or bowel.Half-exterior, half-interior pivot.
Temperature clueFever with aversion to cold.Strong Heat without exterior chills.Alternating chills and fever.
PulseFloating.Flooding/forceful or deep-excess.Wiry.
Formula examplesMa Huang Tang or Gui Zhi Tang by sweating.Bai Hu Tang or a Cheng Qi Tang by Qi/bowel pattern.Xiao Chai Hu Tang.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersFever alone does not locate the stage. Exterior aversion, pure interior Heat or alternating pivot signs solve the comparison.
Memory cueTaiyang is outside; Yangming is inside and blazing; Shaoyang swings at the hinge.
SafetyHigh fever, confusion, severe dehydration, stiff neck, breathing difficulty, rigid abdomen or inability to pass stool/gas requires urgent medical evaluation.
