Bei Mu Gua Lou San

Dry Phlegm obstructing the Lung.

High-yield summary

CategoryMoisten Dryness · Transform Phlegm
PatternDry Phlegm obstructing the Lung.
ActionMoistens the Lung, clears Heat, regulates Qi and transforms dry Phlegm.
Decisive clueDry cough with scant, thick sputum that is difficult to expectorate, plus a dry throat.

Ingredients

Chuan Bei Mu, Gua Lou, Tian Hua Fen, Fu Ling, Ju Hong and Jie Geng.

Commercial preparations and lineages may vary; always verify the source being used.

Differentiation and memory

Do not confuseBai He Gu Jin Tang emphasizes chronic Lung–Kidney Yin deficiency; Bei Mu Gua Lou San emphasizes obstructing dry Phlegm.
Memory cueBei Mu transforms the Phlegm; Gua Lou and Tian Hua Fen restore moisture.

Safety

Persistent cough, coughing blood, fever, breathlessness or low oxygen requires medical evaluation.
