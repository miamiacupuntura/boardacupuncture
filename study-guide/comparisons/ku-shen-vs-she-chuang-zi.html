Herb comparison

Ku Shen vs. She Chuang Zi

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueKu ShenShe Chuang Zi
Shared external actionsBoth dry Dampness, expel Wind, kill parasites and stop itching when used externally.
Fast distinctionVery bitter and cold; internally clears Damp-Heat and facilitates urination.Acrid, bitter and warm; internally strongly warms the Kidneys.

Comparative answer key

CompareKu ShenShe Chuang Zi
Medicinal nameSophorae flavescentis Radix.Cnidii Fructus.
CategoryHerbs that Clear Heat and Dry Dampness.Substances for Topical Application.
Properties in the sourceVery bitter and very cold.Acrid, bitter and warm.
Internal actionClears Heat, dries Dampness and facilitates urination.Strongly warms the Kidneys.
Internal indicationsDamp-Heat dysentery, jaundice and urinary difficulty.Kidney-Cold patterns requiring warming.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersTheir external actions overlap almost completely, but their internal thermal directions are opposite: Ku Shen uses its cold nature to clear Damp-Heat, while She Chuang Zi warms the Kidneys.
Memory cueKu Shen is cold and drains; She Chuang Zi is warm and warms the Kidneys.
Name correctionThe source uses “she chuan zi” once; the standardized pinyin used here is She Chuang Zi.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), pp. 1026–1027. Source Exam Key CMID 9006, questions 5 and 8.

Possible exam questions · Study prompts
