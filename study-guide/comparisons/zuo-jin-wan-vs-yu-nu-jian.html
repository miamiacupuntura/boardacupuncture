Zuo Jin Wan vs. Yu Nu Jian

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueZuo Jin WanYu Nu Jian
Shared clueBoth clear pathogenic Fire affecting the Stomach and can present with thirst, irritability, epigastric discomfort or rebellious Stomach Qi.
Fast distinctionLiver Fire attacks Stomach: acid regurgitation, bitter taste, hypochondriac irritability and vomiting.Stomach Fire with Kidney Yin deficiency: toothache, bleeding gums, intense hunger/thirst and loose teeth.

Comparative answer key

CompareZuo Jin WanYu Nu Jian

Decisive clueAcid reflux with Liver-Qi/Fire signs.Dental and gum signs with Yin deficiency.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersThe shared Stomach Heat signs are not enough. Acid rebellion plus Liver signs points to Zuo Jin; mouth/teeth plus Yin deficiency points to Yu Nu Jian.
Memory cueZuo Jin redirects Liver Fire; Yu Nu cools Stomach and restores Kidney water.
SafetyVomiting blood, black stools, severe abdominal pain or spreading dental infection requires urgent medical care.
