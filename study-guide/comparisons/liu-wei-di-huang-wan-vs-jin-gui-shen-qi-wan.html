Formula comparison

Liu Wei Di Huang Wan vs. Jin Gui Shen Qi Wan

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueLiu Wei Di Huang WanJin Gui Shen Qi Wan
Shared clueBoth use the six-herb Liu Wei Di Huang Wan base to address Kidney deficiency, with low-back weakness, dizziness, tinnitus or urinary changes.
Fast distinctionKidney-Liver Yin deficiency: night sweats, dry mouth, five-center Heat and a red tongue with little coat.Kidney Yang deficiency: cold limbs, edema, urinary difficulty or copious clear urine and a pale swollen tongue.

Comparative answer key

CompareLiu Wei Di Huang WanJin Gui Shen Qi Wan
Thermal directionNourishes Yin; suited to deficiency-Heat signs.Warms and assists Kidney Yang; suited to Cold and water-metabolism signs.
ConstructionThe foundational six-herb formula.The six-herb base plus warming Yang assistants, classically Gui Zhi and Fu Zi.
Decisive signsNight sweating, dry throat, heat sensations, seminal emission.Cold extremities, edema, weak low back, urinary dysfunction.
Tongue and pulseRed with scant coat; thin and rapid.Pale, swollen and moist; deep and weak.
Exam shortcutDeficient water: nourish Yin.Weak fire at the gate of vitality: warm Yang and transform water.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersThe formulas share a base, but the added warming medicinals reverse the diagnostic direction. Heat/dryness points to Liu Wei; Cold/fluid failure points to Jin Gui Shen Qi.
Memory cueLiu Wei builds Kidney water; Jin Gui Shen Qi adds fire to move water.
SafetyJin Gui Shen Qi Wan includes processed Fu Zi in classical composition and requires qualified professional use and reliable sourcing. It is not a home-dosing formula.
SourceSource bank: FWB 8.2 series, Comparison Formulas to Tonify; corroborated with the Classic Formulas profiles and individual formula files.
