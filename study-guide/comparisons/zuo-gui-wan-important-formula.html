Zuo Gui Wan

Severe Kidney Yin and Essence deficiency.

High-yield summary

CategoryStrongly Nourish Kidney Yin and Essence
PatternSevere Kidney Yin and Essence deficiency.
ActionDeeply enriches Kidney Yin, replenishes Essence and strengthens the low back and knees.
Decisive clueSevere dizziness, tinnitus, weak low back/knees, night sweats and reproductive or developmental deficiency signs.

Ingredients

Shu Di Huang, Shan Yao, Gou Qi Zi, Shan Zhu Yu, Chuan Niu Xi, Tu Si Zi, Lu Jiao Jiao and Gui Ban Jiao.

Commercial preparations and lineages may vary; always verify the source being used.

Differentiation and memory

Do not confuseZuo Gui Yin is the lighter decoction for moderate Yin/Essence deficiency; Zuo Gui Wan is richer and more potent for severe depletion.
Memory cueRestore the Left with a rich pill that combines plant, seed, antler and shell Essence.

Safety

Marked fatigue, neurologic symptoms, infertility, delayed development or persistent night sweats requires appropriate biomedical evaluation.
