Herb comparison

Du Zhong vs. Ba Ji Tian vs. Gou Ji

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueDu ZhongBa Ji TianGou Ji
Shared cluesAll three tonify Liver and Kidneys, strengthen sinews and bones, expel Wind-Dampness and belong to Herbs that Tonify the Yang.
Fast distinctionPrimarily tonifies Liver and Kidneys.Primarily expels Wind, Cold and Dampness.Combines both actions but is stronger at expelling than tonifying.

Comparative answer key

CompareDu ZhongBa Ji TianGou Ji
Shared categoryHerbs that Tonify the Yang.
Shared actionsTonify Liver and Kidneys, strengthen sinews and bones, and expel Wind-Dampness.
Relative emphasisTonification.Expulsion of pathogenic Wind, Cold and Dampness.Combines tonification and expulsion, with stronger expelling action.
Primary clinical clueLiver-Kidney deficiency with weak or painful lower back and knees.Kidney Yang deficiency with Wind-Dampness and reproductive or Cold-Womb signs.Lower-back, knee or lower-extremity pain from either Wind-Cold-Damp invasion or Liver-Kidney deficiency.
Formula or use clueDu Huo Ji Sheng Tang and You Gui Wan.Er Xian Tang.The source notes particular benefit for older adults and frequent use as a single herb.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersAll three share the same broad category and musculoskeletal actions, so their relative tonifying and pathogen-expelling strength is the key.
Memory cueDu Zhong tonifies; Ba Ji Tian expels; Gou Ji combines both but leans toward expelling.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), p. 794. Source Exam Key CMID 8938, questions 7–8.

Possible exam questions · Study prompts

  • Which category and actions do all three herbs share?
  • Which herb primarily tonifies Liver and Kidneys?
  • Which herb primarily expels Wind-Cold-Dampness?
  • Which herb combines both actions and particularly benefits older adults?
