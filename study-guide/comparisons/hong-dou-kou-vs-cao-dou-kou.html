Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersThe comparison is almost entirely shared; the testable distinction is Hong Dou Kou's greater force for severe internal stasis.
Common confusionThe source question labels the common category “Herbs that Relieve Food Stagnation,” while the exam title places Cao Dou Kou under “Aromatic Herbs that Transform Dampness.” This note preserves the shared actions without resolving that source inconsistency by assumption.
How it may appearA phrase such as “somewhat more forceful” or “more extreme internal stasis” selects Hong Dou Kou.
Source question formatQuestion 1 is Matching.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), p. 484. Source Exam Key CMID 8905.
