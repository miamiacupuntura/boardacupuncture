Herb comparison

Zingiberis Rhizoma (gan jiang) vs. Evodiae Fructus (wu zhu yu)

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueGan JiangWu Zhu Yu
Shared clueBoth are hot, acrid herbs that warm the Middle and disperse Cold for epigastric or abdominal Cold pain.
Fast distinctionFocuses upward and transforms thin mucus in the Lungs.Focuses downward on Liver-channel Cold, the Womb and lower abdomen.

Comparative answer key

CompareGan JiangWu Zhu Yu
Medicinal name and partZingiberis Rhizoma · dried ginger rhizome.Evodiae Fructus · dried, nearly ripe Evodia fruit.
CategoryHerbs that Warm the Interior and Expel Cold.Herbs that Warm the Interior and Expel Cold.
PropertiesHot and acrid.Hot, acrid, bitter and slightly toxic.
Burner emphasisUpper burner.Lower burner.
Decisive indicationCold thin mucus in the Lung with cough; also Middle Cold, Cold-pattern bleeding and Yang collapse.Cold bulging disorder, Liver-channel pain, Cold Womb, irregular menstruation or lower-abdominal Cold pain.
Formula cluesLi Zhong Wan; Da Jian Zhong Tang; Bai Ye Tang; Si Ni Tang; Ling Gan Wu Wei Jiang Xin Tang.Wu Zhu Yu Tang; Zuo Jin Wan; Si Shen Wan; Wen Jing Tang.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersBoth quickly scatter Interior Cold, but Lung mucus and the upper burner favor Gan Jiang, whereas Liver, Womb and lower-abdominal Cold favor Wu Zhu Yu.
Memory cueGan Jiang goes up to Lung; Wu Zhu Yu warms below.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), p. 689. Source Exam Keys CMID 8965, questions 2–7, and CMID 9090, questions 2–7.
