Herb comparison

Hai Piao Xiao vs. Sang Piao Xiao

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueHai Piao XiaoSang Piao Xiao
Shared clueBoth enter the Kidney channel and retain Essence.
Fast distinctionBroadly stops bleeding, Dampness, discharge, acidity and pain, but does not tonify.Tonifies the Kidneys and more strongly secures Essence and contains urine.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersBoth retain Essence, but Hai Piao Xiao is the broad non-tonifying choice for bleeding, discharge, acidity or pain, while Sang Piao Xiao is the Kidney-tonifying choice for stronger Essence and urine containment.
Memory cueHai Piao Xiao handles many leaks; Sang Piao Xiao supports the Kidneys.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), p. 899. Source Exam Key CMID 8971, question 1.
