Rehmannia 地黄 — Fresh, Raw-Dried, Prepared

Drying and steaming shift Rehmannia from intense cooling toward rich Blood-Essence tonification.

One picture, one rule

RuleFresh is coldest; Shēng cools/nourishes; Shú deeply supplements.

The part decides the job

MedicinalPart / identityJob
Xiān Dì Huáng 鲜地黄Fresh rootMost strongly clears Heat, cools Blood and generates fluids.
Shēng Dì Huáng 生地黄Raw-dried rootCools Blood and nourishes Yin/fluids.
Shú Dì Huáng 熟地黄Wine-steamed prepared rootTonifies Blood, nourishes Kidney Essence and fills Marrow.

Exam and safety

Exam keyAcute hot fluid injury = fresh; Blood Heat/Yin injury = Shēng; chronic Blood/Essence deficiency = Shú.
SafetyShú Dì is rich and cloying and may worsen Spleen deficiency, Dampness, fullness or loose stools.
