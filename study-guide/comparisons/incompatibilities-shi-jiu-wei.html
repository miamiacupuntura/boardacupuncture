Shí Jiǔ Wèi: Nine Forbidden Clauses

The traditional title counts 19 medicinals arranged in nine clauses; translations may call them antagonisms, counteractions or incompatibilities.

Terminology trapXiāng Wèi is beneficial toxicity mitigation; Shí Jiǔ Wèi is the prohibited historical list.
Counting trapNineteen medicinals, nine clauses—not nineteen individual pairs.

Responsible use

Several entries are obsolete, prohibited or protected. This list is for historical literacy and exam preparation—not a prescribing authorization. Confirm current law, pharmacopoeial identity, processing and toxicology.
