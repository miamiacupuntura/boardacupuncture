Pattern comparison

Blood Heat vs. Blood Stasis

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueBlood HeatBlood Stasis
Shared clueBoth can cause abnormal bleeding, menstrual disorders, red-purple skin lesions and a darkened tongue.
Fast distinctionHeat agitates Blood: bright-red rapid bleeding, fever, thirst and a rapid pulse.Obstruction prevents movement: fixed stabbing pain, dark clots, masses and purple tongue.

Comparative answer key

CompareBlood HeatBlood Stasis
Primary dynamicBlood moves recklessly from Heat.Blood fails to move.
BleedingBright red, fresh and often profuse.Dark, clotted or intermittent.
Tongue / pulseRed/crimson; rapid.Purple/dusky with spots; wiry/choppy.
Formula examplesXi Jiao Di Huang Tang with modern Shui Niu Jiao substitution.Xue Fu Zhu Yu Tang; Gui Zhi Fu Ling Wan by location.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersBoth can bleed, but Heat accelerates and brightens while stasis darkens, clots and fixes pain.
Memory cueHeat makes Blood run; stasis makes Blood stop.
SafetyHeavy bleeding, coughing/vomiting blood, black stool, pregnancy bleeding or severe pain requires urgent care.
