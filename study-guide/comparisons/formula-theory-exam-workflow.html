Formula Analysis Exam Workflow

Use a repeatable sequence to solve composition and modification questions.

Core rule

RuleSolve the mechanism first; ingredient recognition comes second.

Eight-step method

ConceptWhat it meansApplication
1 · Define the patternOrgan/system, level, temperature, deficiency/excess and pathogenic factor.Write the mechanism in one sentence.
2 · Find decisive signsSelect the tongue, pulse and symptom combination that separates close patterns.Ignore nonspecific noise.
3 · State the principleRelease, clear, warm, tonify, drain, transform, move, secure or harmonize.The verb should reverse the mechanism.
4 · Identify the chief unitWhich herb or pair directly performs the principal job?This anchors the answer.
5 · Map supporting rolesDeputy, helping/corrective/strategic assistants and envoy.Every ingredient needs a reason.
6 · Read balance and ratiosHot/cold, up/down, disperse/secure, dry/moisten and relative dose.Predict the net formula direction.
7 · Check safetyProcessing, pregnancy, interactions, toxicity, wildlife and patient context.Eliminate answers that fit the pattern but fail safety.
8 · Define reassessmentWhat change proves success, failure or need to transition?A formula is tied to a phase, not a diagnosis label forever.

Modification rule

ConceptWhat it meansApplication
AddA real secondary mechanism or important symptom is untreated.Add the smallest functional unit that fits.
RemoveAn ingredient no longer has a target or creates a new imbalance.Preserve the chief strategy while simplifying.
ReplaceThe same job is needed but temperature, strength, direction or safety must change.Compare function plus clinical texture, not category alone.
Change ratioThe formula is correct but its emphasis is wrong.Ratio change can transform the clinical identity.

Responsible use

Educational summary for formula analysis and exam preparation. Doses, toxic preparations, interactions and clinical decisions require current references and professional supervision.
