Xiang Ru San vs. Huo Xiang Zheng Qi San

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueXiang Ru SanHuo Xiang Zheng Qi San
Shared clueBoth address Exterior Wind-Cold complicated by Dampness in summer and may include headache, chills, heaviness and digestive upset.
Fast distinctionExterior signs dominate: chills, fever, no sweating and heaviness after cold exposure in summer.Middle-burner Dampness dominates: nausea, vomiting, diarrhea and epigastric fullness.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersBoth combine Exterior and Dampness. Decide whether the surface or the digestive middle burner is clinically louder.
Memory cueXiang Ru vents summer Cold-Damp; Huo Xiang rectifies the damp middle.
SafetySevere dehydration, high fever, bloody diarrhea or inability to keep fluids down requires medical care.
