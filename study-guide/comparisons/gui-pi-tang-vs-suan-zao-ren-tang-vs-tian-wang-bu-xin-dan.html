Gui Pi Tang vs. Suan Zao Ren Tang vs. Tian Wang Bu Xin Dan

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueGui Pi TangSuan Zao Ren TangTian Wang Bu Xin Dan
Shared clueAll may treat insomnia, palpitations, anxiety or poor memory when deficiency fails to nourish and settle the Spirit.
Fast distinctionSpleen Qi and Heart Blood deficiency: fatigue, poor appetite, pale complexion and possible bleeding.Liver Blood deficiency with deficiency Heat: irritability, dizziness and night sweating.Heart-Kidney Yin and Blood deficiency with Heart Fire: marked dryness, night sweating and stronger deficiency-Heat signs.

Comparative answer key

CompareGui Pi TangSuan Zao Ren TangTian Wang Bu Xin Dan
Root patternSpleen Qi deficiency with Heart Blood deficiency.Liver Blood deficiency with deficiency Heat.Heart and Kidney Yin-Blood deficiency with deficient Fire.
Decisive clueFatigue, poor appetite, easy bruising or abnormal bleeding.Irritability, dizziness and night sweats with insomnia.Dry mouth, night sweats, palpitations and pronounced Yin-deficiency Heat.
TonguePale.Red or pale-red, often with a thin coat.Red with little coating.
PulseThin and weak.Wiry or thin and rapid.Thin and rapid.
Exam shortcutQi cannot make/hold Blood.Liver Blood does not anchor the Hun.Heart-Kidney Yin cannot control Fire.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersThe symptom “insomnia” is not enough. Appetite and bleeding point to Gui Pi Tang; irritability with Liver-Blood clues points to Suan Zao Ren Tang; dryness and Heart-Kidney Yin deficiency point to Tian Wang Bu Xin Dan.
Memory cueGui Pi feeds Qi and Blood; Suan Zao Ren settles the Liver; Tian Wang connects Heart and Kidney Yin.
SafetyClassical Tian Wang Bu Xin Dan may list Zhu Sha (cinnabar). Modern study and clinical references should use cinnabar-free, quality-controlled versions; mercury-containing products are not recommended.
SourceSource bank: FWB 13.1 and FWB 13.1.1, Comparison Formulas to Calm the Mind; corroborated with the Classic Formulas profiles.
