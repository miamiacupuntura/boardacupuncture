Three clinical settings for Blood stasis · FWB Invigorate Blood

Xue Fu Zhu Yu Tang vs. Sheng Hua Tang vs. Fu Yuan Huo Xue Tang

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueXue Fu Zhu Yu TangSheng Hua TangFu Yuan Huo Xue Tang
Shared clueAll move Blood stasis and relieve fixed pain, but the clinical setting is decisive.
Fast distinctionChest Blood stasis with Qi stagnation: chest pain, palpitations, irritability or chronic headache.Postpartum Blood deficiency with Cold and retained stasis: abdominal pain and retained or scanty lochia.Traumatic Blood stasis: severe rib-side or flank pain after injury.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersPain quality may look similar. The setting—chest constraint, postpartum state or trauma—provides the highest-value clue.
Memory cueXue Fu = chest; Sheng Hua = postpartum; Fu Yuan = trauma.
SafetyPostpartum hemorrhage, chest pain or significant trauma requires urgent medical assessment. Classical Fu Yuan Huo Xue Tang used prohibited/protected pangolin scale and must use a legal substitute.
