Gall Bladder Deficiency vs. Damp-Heat

Contrast a deficient emotional presentation with a hot obstructive excess.

Fast differentiation clues

ClueGall Bladder DeficiencyGall Bladder Damp-Heat
SharedBoth may cause restlessness, sighing and disturbed decision-making.
Fast distinctionDeficiency: timidity, easy fright and indecision with a weak presentation.Damp-Heat: hypochondriac fullness, bitter taste, nausea and sticky yellow coating.

Clinical table

CompareGall Bladder DeficiencyGall Bladder Damp-Heat
NatureDeficient Gall Bladder Qi/Spirit support.Excess Dampness plus Heat.
PulseWeak or timid presentation; may be fine.Slippery-wiry-rapid.
Formula associationWen Dan Tang or An Shen Ding Zhi Wan.Yin Chen Hao-based strategy in the supplied table.

Exam and safety

Why it mattersBitter taste, yellow stickiness and physical fullness identify the excess pattern.
Memory cueDeficiency lacks nerve; Damp-Heat adds bitter yellow weight.
SafetyJaundice, fever, severe upper abdominal pain or persistent vomiting requires urgent assessment.
