Trap 7 of 22

Zhū Líng 猪苓 vs. Zhū Shā 朱砂

The shared Zhū sound links a mild diuretic fungus to mercury-containing cinnabar.

Memory rule

AnchorLíng is the draining fungus; Shā is mineral “sand” with mercury.

Correct identity

NameIdentityJob
Zhū Líng 猪苓PolyporusPromotes urination and drains Dampness.
Zhū Shā 朱砂Cinnabaris / cinnabarHeavy mineral that calms the Spirit and clears Heart fire in traditional use.

Exam and safety

Exam keyUrinary Dampness points to Zhū Líng; fright/Spirit disturbance historically points to Zhū Shā.
Safety alertZhū Shā contains mercury, is restricted/prohibited in many places and is not for casual or prolonged internal use.
