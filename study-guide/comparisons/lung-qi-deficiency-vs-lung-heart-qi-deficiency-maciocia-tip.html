Lung Qi Deficiency vs. Lung–Heart Qi Deficiency

Recognize when weak Lung Qi extends into the Heart and adds palpitations and circulatory signs.

Fast differentiation clues

ClueLung Qi DeficiencyLung–Heart Qi Deficiency
SharedBoth cause shortness of breath, weak voice, fatigue, spontaneous sweating and a pale tongue with empty pulse.
Fast distinctionLung signs dominate: weak cough, recurrent colds and low voice without prominent palpitations.Heart joins: palpitations, depression, sighing, bright-pale complexion and greater exertional intolerance.

Clinical table

CompareLung Qi DeficiencyLung–Heart Qi Deficiency
Added organ clueRespiration and Defensive Qi.Palpitations and circulation/Spirit symptoms join respiration.
Formula associationBu Fei Tang.Si Jun Zi Tang, Bao Yuan Tang or Sheng Mai San according to the supplied table and presentation.

Exam and safety

Why it mattersPalpitations are the decisive addition; shortness of breath alone does not establish Heart involvement.
Memory cueLung Qi weakens the voice; Lung–Heart Qi also unsettles the pulse.
SafetyPalpitations with fainting, chest pain, blue lips or breathlessness at rest requires urgent cardiac evaluation.
