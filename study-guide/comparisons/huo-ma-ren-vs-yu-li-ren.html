Herb comparison

Cannabis Semen (Huo Ma Ren) vs. Pruni Semen (Yu Li Ren)

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueHuo Ma RenYu Li Ren
Shared clueBoth are neutral, oil-rich seeds that moisten the Intestines and promote bowel movement.
Fast distinctionSweet and nourishing; stronger for deficiency-type dry constipation.Acrid, bitter and sweet; descends Qi, promotes urination and reduces edema in a more Excess-type pattern.

Comparative answer key

CompareHuo Ma RenYu Li Ren
Medicinal nameCannabis Semen.Pruni Semen.
PropertiesSweet and neutral.Acrid, bitter, sweet and neutral.
ChannelsLarge Intestine, Spleen and Stomach.Large Intestine, Small Intestine and Spleen.
Patient typeElderly, postpartum, post-febrile, chronically ill or deficient.More Excess-type constipation with Qi stagnation or edema.
Additional actionIts sweet quality nourishes while moistening.Moves Qi downward, promotes urination and reduces edema.
Yang figure 4.2Relative moistening index: 80.Relative moistening index: 60.
Current Appendix AListed.Not listed; module recognition only.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersBoth moisten dry stool, but constitutional deficiency points to Huo Ma Ren while Qi fullness, difficult urination or edema points to Yu Li Ren.
Memory cueHuo oils and nourishes; Yu rolls Qi and opens urine.
Formula boundaryNeither herb is a chief or co-chief in the current 63-formula Board roster.
SourceYang, Chinese Herbal Medicines: Comparisons and Characteristics, 2nd ed., Chapter 4; Bensky et al., Materia Medica, 3rd ed.
