Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersAll may be described simply as “Heat bleeding.” The anatomic outlet is the efficient discriminator.
Memory cueShi Hui—many; Ke Xue—cough; Huai Hua—stool; Xiao Ji—urine.
SafetyCoughing/vomiting blood, black stool, heavy rectal bleeding or visible blood in urine requires prompt medical evaluation and may be an emergency.
