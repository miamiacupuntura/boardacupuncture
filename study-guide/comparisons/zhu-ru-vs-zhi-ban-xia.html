Herb comparison

Bambusae Caulis in taeniam (zhu ru) vs. Pinelliae Rhizoma preparatum (zhi ban xia)

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueZhu RuZhi Ban Xia
Shared cluesBoth transform Phlegm, alleviate nausea and calm the Spirit; they are often combined to reinforce these actions.
Fast distinctionMoistening and cooling for Phlegm-Heat and nausea from Stomach Heat.Acrid, warm and drying for Cold-Phlegm, Damp-Phlegm and thin mucus.

Comparative answer key

CompareZhu RuZhi Ban Xia
Medicinal nameBambusae Caulis in taeniam.Pinelliae Rhizoma preparatum.
CategoryCool Herbs that Transform Phlegm-Heat.Warm Herbs that Transform Cold-Phlegm.
NatureMoistening and cooling.Acrid, warm and drying.
Primary Phlegm patternPhlegm-Heat.Cold-Phlegm, Damp-Phlegm and thin mucus.
Nausea clueNausea from Stomach Heat.Nausea associated with Cold or Phlegm-Dampness affecting the Spleen, Stomach and epigastrium.
Distinctive emphasisRelieves constraint and disperses clumped Phlegm-Heat affecting the Gallbladder.Dries and transforms Phlegm concentrated in the Spleen, Stomach and epigastrium.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersBoth may appear in a Phlegm-and-nausea question. Heat, a moistening action or Gallbladder constraint favors Zhu Ru; Cold, Dampness and a warm-drying action favor Zhi Ban Xia.
Memory cueZhu Ru cools clumped Heat; Zhi Ban Xia dries Cold-Damp Phlegm.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), p. 394. Source Exam Key CMID 9127, questions 1–2.

Possible exam questions · Study prompts

  • Which actions do Zhu Ru and Zhi Ban Xia share?
  • Which herb treats Phlegm-Heat and nausea from Stomach Heat?
  • Which herb treats Cold-Phlegm, Damp-Phlegm and thin mucus?
  • Which herb relieves Gallbladder constraint with clumped Phlegm-Heat?
  • How do their temperature and moisture qualities differ?
