Herb comparison

Carthami Flos (hong hua) vs. Persicae Semen (tao ren)

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueHong HuaTao Ren
Shared clueBoth dispel stasis, unblock menstruation, invigorate Blood and stop pain.
Fast distinctionLight flower that floats toward Exterior and upper body.Heavy seed that sinks toward Interior and lower body and moistens Intestines.

Comparative answer key

CompareHong HuaTao Ren
Botanical nameCarthami Flos.Persicae Semen.
Category boundaryOutside the current category list; included only for recognition.Current category list.
Properties and channelsWarm and acrid; Heart and Liver.Neutral, bitter and sweet; Heart, Large Intestine, Liver and Lung.
DirectionLight and floating; travels in Exterior and upper body. Excessively high doses may raise Blood into the head.Heavy and sinking; attacks Blood stagnation in Interior and lower body.
Distinctive actionsUnblocks menstruation; treats abdominal pain, masses, difficult delivery and traumatic pain; used topically for bedsores.Breaks Blood stasis, treats Lung or intestinal abscess, moistens Intestines for constipation and stops cough or wheezing.

Shared cautionContraindicated during pregnancy.
Current formula boundaryCo-chief in Xue Fu Zhu Yu Tang (#46), but outside this herb-category list.Co-chief in Tao He Cheng Qi Tang (#45) and Xue Fu Zhu Yu Tang (#46).

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersThe plant part predicts direction: a light flower reaches upward and outward, while a heavy seed sinks inward and downward and contributes oily Intestinal and Lung actions.
Memory cueHong Hua floats; Tao Ren sinks and moistens.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), p. 626. Source Exam Key CMID 8985, questions 4–8, and CMID 9076, questions 1–5.
