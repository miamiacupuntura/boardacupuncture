Er Miao San

Damp-Heat lodging in the Lower Burner.

High-yield summary

CategoryClear Lower Burner Damp-Heat
PatternDamp-Heat lodging in the Lower Burner.
ActionClears Heat and dries Dampness, especially from the lower extremities.
Decisive clueHot, swollen or painful legs/knees, weakness or heaviness below, or yellow foul discharge with a greasy yellow coat.

Ingredients

Huang Bai and Cang Zhu.

Commercial preparations and lineages may vary; always verify the source being used.

Differentiation and memory

Do not confuseBa Zheng San directs Damp-Heat through urination in painful urinary dysfunction; Er Miao San targets Damp-Heat lodged in the lower body.
Memory cueTwo marvels: Huang Bai clears Heat while Cang Zhu dries Dampness.

Safety

A hot swollen joint, rapidly spreading redness, fever or inability to bear weight requires urgent evaluation.
