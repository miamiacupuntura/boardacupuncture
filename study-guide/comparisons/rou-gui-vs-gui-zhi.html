Herb comparison

Rou Gui vs. Gui Zhi

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueRou GuiGui Zhi
Shared clueBoth come from the same species, warm nutritive Qi and Blood, assist Qi transformation and disperse congealed Cold.
Fast distinctionBark; enters inward and downward to warm the Interior and Kidney Yang.Twig; moves outward and upward to release Exterior Cold and unblock channels.

Comparative answer key

CompareRou GuiGui Zhi
Botanical nameCinnamomi Cortex.Cinnamomi Ramulus.
Plant partBark from the lower portion of the tree.Twig.
CategoryHerbs that Warm the Interior and Expel Cold.Warm, Acrid Herbs that Release the Exterior.
DirectionEnters the Lower Burner.Primarily moves upward through channels and collaterals.
Best useWarms the Interior and stops pain; tonifies Kidney Yang and restores fire at the Gate of Vitality.Disperses pathogenic Cold from the Exterior and warms and unblocks the channels and collaterals.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersThe plant part predicts the direction: lower bark warms deeply and restores Kidney fire, while the reaching twig moves upward and outward to release and unblock.
Memory cueRou Gui bark goes deep; Gui Zhi twig goes out.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), p. 686. Source Exam Key CMID 8963, questions 8–10.
