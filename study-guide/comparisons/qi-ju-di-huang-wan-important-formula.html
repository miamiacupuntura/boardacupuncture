Qi Ju Di Huang Wan

Liver and Kidney Yin deficiency affecting the eyes.

High-yield summary

CategoryNourish Liver/Kidney Yin · Benefit the Eyes
PatternLiver and Kidney Yin deficiency affecting the eyes.
ActionNourishes Liver/Kidney Yin and Essence and brightens the eyes.
Decisive clueDry, blurred or diminished vision with dizziness, tinnitus and low-back weakness.

Ingredients

Shu Di Huang, Shan Zhu Yu, Shan Yao, Ze Xie, Mu Dan Pi, Fu Ling, Gou Qi Zi and Ju Hua.

Commercial preparations and lineages may vary; always verify the source being used.

Differentiation and memory

Do not confuseLiu Wei Di Huang Wan is the general Yin-deficiency base; Qi Ju adds Gou Qi Zi and Ju Hua for eye manifestations.
Memory cueLiu Wei plus Qi and Ju: wolfberry and chrysanthemum focus the formula on vision.

Safety

Sudden vision loss, flashes/floaters, severe eye pain or neurologic symptoms requires urgent ophthalmic assessment.
