Poria 茯苓 — Body, Root-Hugging Core and Skin

Different portions of the pine-root fungus shift emphasis from Spleen to Spirit, edema or Damp-Heat.

Memory rule

AnchorBody supports Spleen; root-hugging core calms; skin drains swelling.

Identity decides the job

MedicinalIdentityJob
Fú Líng 茯苓White fungal bodyPromotes urination, strengthens Spleen and calms Spirit.
Fú Shén 茯神Poria portion surrounding pine rootEmphasizes calming Spirit and treating palpitations/insomnia.
Fú Líng Pí 茯苓皮Outer skinPromotes urination and reduces edema.
Chì Fú Líng 赤茯苓Pink/red layerTraditionally emphasizes Damp-Heat urinary patterns.
Tǔ Fú Líng 土茯苓Smilax root—not Poria fungusResolves toxicity and Dampness; distinct medicinal.
Zhū Líng 猪苓Polyporus—different fungusMore exclusively drains water without Spleen-tonic emphasis.

Exam and safety

Exam keyThe modifier identifies the portion or a completely different species.
SafetyDo not substitute Tǔ Fú Líng or Zhū Líng for Poria parts from name resemblance alone; consider additive diuretic effects.
