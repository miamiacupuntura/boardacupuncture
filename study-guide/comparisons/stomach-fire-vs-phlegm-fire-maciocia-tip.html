Stomach Fire vs. Stomach Phlegm-Fire

Recognize when Full Stomach Heat combines with Phlegm and mental disturbance.

Fast differentiation clues

ClueStomach FireStomach Phlegm-Fire
SharedBoth show epigastric Heat/fullness, a red tongue with yellow coat and a rapid forceful pulse.
Fast distinctionFire alone: burning, intense thirst for cold drinks, dry mouth and a dry yellow coat.Phlegm joins: oppression, reduced thirst, mucus in stools or sputum and mental disturbance with sticky coat.

Clinical table

CompareStomach FireStomach Phlegm-Fire
Added factorFull Heat scorches fluids.Heat condenses fluids into Phlegm that obstructs the Mind and middle.
Tongue / pulseYellow-dry coat; rapid-overflowing.Yellow-sticky coat; slippery-overflowing-rapid.
Formula associationQing Wei San, Tiao Wei Cheng Qi Tang or Liang Ge San.The supplied table uses the same formula family; full Phlegm signs must guide modification.

Exam and safety

Why it mattersMucus, stickiness, slipperiness and mental symptoms prove Phlegm beyond Fire alone.
Memory cueFire burns and dries; Phlegm-Fire burns and clouds.
SafetyConfusion, agitation, severe dehydration, GI bleeding or intense abdominal pain requires urgent medical evaluation.
