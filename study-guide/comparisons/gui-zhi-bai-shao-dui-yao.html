Guì Zhī + Bái Sháo

桂枝 + 白芍

One picture, one job

PictureOne pushes out; one holds in.
Combined jobHarmonize nutritive and protective Qi in Exterior deficiency and regulate tension between movement and containment.
CategoryHarmonize Ying and Wei

How the pair works

HerbRole within the pair
Guì ZhīWarms the channels and releases the muscle layer outward.
Bái SháoPreserves Yin and nutritive Qi so the Exterior does not leak excessively.

Exam focus and safety

CluesWind-Cold with spontaneous sweating, aversion to wind and a floating-moderate pulse.
Signature formulaGuì Zhī Tāng; Bái Sháo is doubled in Xiǎo Jiàn Zhōng Tāng.
TrapThis is not the closed, no-sweat excess pattern of Má Huáng + Guì Zhī.
PreparationThe relative dose changes the pair from Exterior harmonizing toward spasm/pain relief.
SafetyPersistent fever, breathing difficulty or dehydration requires medical evaluation; do not induce further sweating in a depleted patient.

Complete formula, Duì Yào, or formula + pair?

Use the complete formula when the case reproduces a full pattern architecture with root, branches, and protection. Consider the pair for a narrow objective; use it as a modification when a base formula fits but lacks one function. The pair does not automatically replace the formula.
