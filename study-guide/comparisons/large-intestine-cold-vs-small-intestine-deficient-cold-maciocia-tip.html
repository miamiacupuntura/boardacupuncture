Large vs. Small Intestine Deficient Cold

Use pain location and dominant bowel function to localize two overlapping Cold-deficiency patterns.

Fast differentiation clues

ClueLarge Intestine ColdSmall Intestine Deficient Cold
SharedBoth cause chronic loose stool/diarrhea, abdominal discomfort, cold limbs and a weak slow pulse.
Fast distinctionLarge Intestine focus: chronic loose stool and deficient bowel containment dominate.Small Intestine focus: lower abdominal pain and prominent borborygmi dominate.

Clinical table

CompareLarge Intestine ColdSmall Intestine Deficient Cold
Primary clueFinal stool formation and containment.Lower abdominal separation/movement with rumbling.
Formula associationLiang Fu Wan in the supplied table.Xiao Jian Zhong Tang or Shen Ling Bai Zhu San.

Exam and safety

Why it mattersThese patterns overlap; localization and the dominant symptom determine the study label.
Memory cueLarge Intestine loses the stool; Small Intestine rumbles below.
SafetyPersistent diarrhea, dehydration, blood/black stool, fever, severe pain or weight loss requires evaluation.
