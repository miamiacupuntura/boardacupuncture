Herb comparison

Cistanches Herba (rou cong rong) vs. Cynomorii Herba (suo yang)

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueRou Cong RongSuo Yang
Shared cluesBoth tonify Kidney Yang, moisten the Intestines and unblock the bowels. Both are warm and sweet and treat impotence, low-back weakness and dry constipation.
Fast distinctionGentler and better at augmenting Essence and Blood; favors female infertility.More strongly warms Kidney Yang and nourishes the sinews; favors male impotence and atrophy disorders.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersThe two herbs are similar and may substitute for one another, but Rou Cong Rong is gentler and more Essence-Blood oriented, while Suo Yang is stronger for Kidney Yang and sinews.
Memory cueRou Cong Rong: Essence, Blood and female infertility; Suo Yang: stronger Yang, sinews, male impotence and atrophy.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), p. 774. Source Exam Key CMID 9044, questions 1–3.
