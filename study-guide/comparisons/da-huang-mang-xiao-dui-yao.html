Dà Huáng + Máng Xiāo

大黄 + 芒硝

One picture, one job

PictureThe General pushes; the salt softens.
Combined jobPurge Heat accumulation and soften dry hardness in Yangming bowel excess.
CategoryPurge Heat · Soften Hardness

How the pair works

HerbRole within the pair
Dà HuángDrives accumulated Heat and stool forcefully downward.
Máng XiāoDraws water into the bowel and softens hard, dry stool.

Exam focus and safety

CluesDry hard stool, focal distention/fullness, tidal fever, delirium and a forceful excess pulse.
Signature formulaDà Chéng Qì Tāng; Tiáo Wèi Chéng Qì Tāng.
TrapThis is interior excess with hardness, not deficiency constipation needing moistening and tonification.
PreparationAdd Dà Huáng late when purgation is intended; dissolve Máng Xiāo in the strained decoction and never cook it directly.
SafetyContraindicated in pregnancy. Classical 19 Wèi warnings involve Máng Xiāo with Sān Léng and Liú Huáng. A rigid tender abdomen or no stool and gas is a surgical emergency.
