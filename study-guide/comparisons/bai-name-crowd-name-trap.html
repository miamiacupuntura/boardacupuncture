The Bǎi Crowd: Bǎi Bù, Bǎi Zǐ Rén, Huáng Bǎi

Three unrelated medicinals share the Bǎi sound but act on Lung, Heart or Damp-Heat.

Memory rule

AnchorBù stops cough; Zǐ Rén is the seed that sleeps; Huáng Bǎi is yellow bark.

Correct identity

NameIdentityJob
Bǎi Bù 百部Stemonae RadixMoistens Lung and stops cough; also addresses parasites.
Bǎi Zǐ Rén 柏子仁Platycladi SemenNourishes Heart Blood/Yin and calms insomnia.
Huáng Bǎi 黄柏Phellodendri CortexClears Lower-Jiao Damp-Heat and deficiency fire.

Exam and safety

Exam keyMatch the complete name and organ system; “Bǎi” alone identifies nothing.
Safety alertSpell and read Bǎi correctly—never convert it to Bǎo. Each item has different cautions and cannot be substituted.
