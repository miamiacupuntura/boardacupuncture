Herb comparison

Menthae haplocalycis Herba (bo he) vs. Chrysanthemi Flos (ju hua)

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueBo HeJu Hua
Shared cluesBoth are cool, acrid exterior-releasing herbs and may address Wind-Heat involving the head or eyes.
Decisive clueSore throat, rash venting or Liver Qi stagnation.Liver heat, red eyes, dizziness, Yang rising or hypertension.

Comparative answer key

CompareBo He (Field Mint)Ju Hua (Chrysanthemum Flower)
Shared categoryCool, Acrid Herbs that Release the Exterior.
Primary distinctionDisperses Wind-Heat, benefits the throat, vents rash and smooths Liver Qi.Has greater emphasis on the Liver and eyes.
Liver Yang clueWeaker at calming Yang.Stronger for red eyes, dizziness, Yang rising and hypertension.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersBoth may occur in head and eye presentations, but Ju Hua carries the stronger Liver-Yang pattern clue.
Common confusionBo He can clear heat from the eyes, yet it is not as strong as Ju Hua for calming rising Yang.
How it may appearHypertension, dizziness or Liver Yang rising favors Ju Hua. Sore throat, rash and Liver Qi stagnation favors Bo He.
Source question formatsQuestion 6 is Matching. Question 10 is Multiple Choice: the herb stronger for clearing Liver heat, brightening the eyes and calming rising Yang is Ju Hua. Questions 13–16 reinforce this in True/False review blocks.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), p. 51. Source Exam Key CMID 9277.
