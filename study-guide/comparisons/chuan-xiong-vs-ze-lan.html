Herb comparison

Chuanxiong Rhizoma (chuan xiong) vs. Lycopi Herba (ze lan)

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueChuan XiongZe Lan
Shared cluesBoth are aromatic and acrid, enter the Liver, invigorate Blood and stop pain.
Fast distinctionMoves Qi within Blood and ascends to head/eyes for Wind headache.Promotes fluid metabolism and urination when edema accompanies Blood stasis.

Comparative answer key

CompareChuan XiongZe Lan
Shared categoryHerbs that Regulate and Invigorate the Blood.
Properties/channelsWarm and acrid; Liver, Gallbladder and Pericardium.Slightly warm, bitter, acrid and aromatic; Liver and Spleen.
Primary extensionMoves Qi in Blood; ascends to head/eyes, expels Wind and stops headache.Facilitates urination and fluid metabolism for edema with Blood stasis.
Clinical emphasisBroad Blood stasis, especially menstrual problems and postpartum abdominal pain; various headaches, especially Wind-Cold.Qi stagnation with predominant Blood stasis and postoperative pain; the source notes combination with Hong Hua.
Formula cluesTao Hong Si Wu Tang; Sheng Hua Tang; Xue Fu Zhu Yu Tang; Chuan Xiong Cha Tiao San.No named formula is provided in these questions.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersBoth move Blood and stop pain, but Chuan Xiong adds an ascending Wind/headache action while Ze Lan adds fluid and edema treatment.
Common confusionHeadache and upward movement favors Chuan Xiong; edema plus stasis and urination favors Ze Lan.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), p. 618. Source Exam Keys CMID 8921 and CMID 9120, questions 1–3.
