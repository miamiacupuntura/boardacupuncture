Formula trap 18 of 22

Dìng Chuǎn Tāng vs. Dìng Zhì Wán

One arrests wheezing in the Lung; the other settles a timid, forgetful Heart-Spirit.

Memory rule

AnchorChuǎn = panting; Zhì = will.

Decisive separation

FormulaArchitectureDecisive presentation
Dìng Chuǎn Tāng 定喘汤Bái Guǒ, Má Huáng and herbs that clear Lung Heat/transform PhlegmPhlegm-Heat with constrained Lung: wheezing, cough and thick yellow sputum.
Dìng Zhì Wán 定志丸Rén Shēn, Fú Líng, Shí Chāng Pú, Yuǎn ZhìHeart-Qi deficiency with Phlegm: timidity, forgetfulness and easy fright.

Exam and safety

Exam keyRespiratory sound versus cognitive-emotional instability.
SafetySevere breathlessness, cyanosis, inability to speak or low oxygen is an emergency. Dìng Chuǎn contains Má Huáng and Bái Guǒ safety concerns.
