Herb comparison

Gua Lou vs. Bai Jie Zi

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueGua LouBai Jie Zi
Shared clueBoth facilitate the flow of Qi in the chest and scour Phlegm when turbid Phlegm obstructs Qi, causing chest pain and a stifling sensation.
Fast distinctionMoist and cold; treats Heat and Phlegm clumped in the chest.Warm, drying and powerful; treats Cold-Phlegm accumulated in the chest.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersThe herbs share a chest-Phlegm indication, but their temperatures are diametrically opposed: Gua Lou cools and moistens Phlegm-Heat, while Bai Jie Zi warms and dries Cold-Phlegm.
Memory cueGua Lou cools a hot chest; Bai Jie Zi warms a cold chest.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), p. 385. Source Exam Key CMID 8961, questions 1–2.
