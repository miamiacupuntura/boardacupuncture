Ding Chuan Tang

External Wind-Cold constraining the Lung with internal Phlegm-Heat.

High-yield summary

CategoryClear Lung Heat · Stop Wheezing
PatternExternal Wind-Cold constraining the Lung with internal Phlegm-Heat.
ActionDisseminates Lung Qi, clears Heat, transforms Phlegm and stops cough and wheezing.
Decisive clueWheezing with copious thick yellow sputum plus mild exterior Wind-Cold signs.

Ingredients

Bai Guo, Ma Huang, Su Zi, Gan Cao, Kuan Dong Hua, Xing Ren, Sang Bai Pi, Huang Qin and Ban Xia.

Commercial preparations and lineages may vary; always verify the source being used.

Differentiation and memory

Do not confuseXiao Qing Long Tang treats exterior Wind-Cold with internal cold Fluids; Ding Chuan Tang requires internal Phlegm-Heat.
Memory cueDing Chuan “stabilizes wheezing” by opening outside while clearing hot Phlegm inside.

Safety

Severe breathlessness, blue lips, chest pain, confusion or low oxygen is an emergency. Ma Huang and Bai Guo require professional dosing.
