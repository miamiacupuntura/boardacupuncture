Batch 7 · Small Intestine · Tip 4 of 10

Small Intestine Qi Tied vs. Full Heat

Separate mechanical-type obstruction signs from a Heat pattern transferring to urine.

Fast differentiation clues

ClueSmall Intestine Qi TiedSmall Intestine Full Heat
SharedBoth may cause acute abdominal pain, constipation and an excess presentation.
Fast distinctionQi Tied: sudden severe pain, vomiting, distention and failure to pass stool/gas.Full Heat: mouth/tongue ulcers, thirst and scant dark painful urination dominate.

Clinical table

CompareSmall Intestine Qi TiedSmall Intestine Full Heat
Primary blockageBowel Qi and contents are tied.Heat disturbs the paired Heart–Small Intestine pathway and fluids.
Formula associationZhi Shi Dao Wan or Tian Tai Wu Yao San in the supplied table.Dao Chi San, Dao Chi Qing Xin Tang or Liang Ge San.

Exam and safety

Why it mattersObstipation and vomiting indicate obstruction; oral and urinary burning indicate Full Heat.
Memory cueQi Tied blocks the bowel; Full Heat burns mouth and urine.
SafetyPossible obstruction, rigid abdomen, severe pain, vomiting or inability to pass gas requires emergency evaluation.
