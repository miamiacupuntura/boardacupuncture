Shēng Má Is Not Western Black Cohosh

Related genera/common labels do not make Asian Shēng Má and North American black cohosh interchangeable.

Memory rule

AnchorShēng Má rises and vents; black cohosh is a Western menopause supplement.

Exam keyA shared English genus history is not a prescribing equivalence.
