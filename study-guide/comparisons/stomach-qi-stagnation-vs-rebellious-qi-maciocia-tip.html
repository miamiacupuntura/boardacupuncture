Batch 6 · Stomach · Tip 7 of 10

Stomach Qi Stagnation vs. Stomach Qi Rebelling Upward

Separate Qi that is blocked in the epigastrium from Qi that has reversed upward.

Fast differentiation clues

ClueStomach Qi StagnationStomach Qi Rebelling Upward
SharedBoth cause epigastric discomfort, fullness, belching and impaired Stomach descent.
Fast distinctionStagnation: distention and constraint dominate; symptoms may vary with stress.Rebellion: nausea, vomiting, hiccup or forceful belching show clear upward counterflow.

Clinical table

CompareStomach Qi StagnationStomach Qi Rebelling Upward
DirectionQi is obstructed in place.Qi moves upward instead of descending.
Formula associationChen Xiang Jiang San or Ban Xia Hou Po Tang.Ding Xiang Shi Di Tang, Huo Xiang Zheng Qi San or Ban Xia Hou Po Tang by cause.

Exam and safety

Why it mattersNausea, vomiting and hiccup identify direction; fullness alone only shows stagnation.
Memory cueStagnation stops; rebellion rises.
SafetyPersistent vomiting, inability to keep fluids, blood/coffee-ground material, severe pain or dehydration requires urgent care.
