Large Intestine Dryness vs. Heat Obstruction

Separate chronic dry constipation from an acute painful excess blockage.

Fast differentiation clues

ClueLarge Intestine DrynessHeat Obstruction
SharedBoth can produce severe constipation and dry stool.
Fast distinctionDryness: chronic difficult stool with thin/dry constitution and little systemic Heat.Obstruction: acute constipation with marked pain/distention, fever and a big forceful pulse.

Clinical table

CompareLarge Intestine DrynessHeat Obstruction
Clinical tempoChronic and deficiency-weighted.Acute and excess.
Formula associationMoistening formulas such as Zeng Ye Tang or Wu Ren Wan.Tiao Wei Cheng Qi Tang in the supplied table.

Exam and safety

Why it mattersAcute pain, fever, distention and forceful pulse require an obstruction differential.
Memory cueDryness slows; obstruction stops.
SafetyAcute pain, vomiting, distention or inability to pass stool/gas can be obstruction and needs emergency care.
