Xiao Huo Luo Dan vs. Du Huo Ji Sheng Tang

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueXiao Huo Luo DanDu Huo Ji Sheng Tang
Shared clueBoth dispel Wind-Dampness, unblock the channels and treat chronic painful obstruction with numbness or limited movement.
Fast distinctionSevere, fixed and stabbing pain from Wind-Cold-Damp, Phlegm and Blood stasis.Long-standing pain with weakness, soreness and deficiency of Liver-Kidney, Qi and Blood.

Comparative answer key

CompareXiao Huo Luo DanDu Huo Ji Sheng Tang
PatternExcess obstruction: Cold-Damp plus Phlegm and stasis.Mixed excess-deficiency: Wind-Damp in a chronically deficient patient.
Pain clueIntense, fixed, sharp or boring pain.Chronic aching pain with weakness of back and knees.
Therapeutic emphasisStrongly warms, transforms Phlegm and invigorates Blood.Dispels Wind-Damp while tonifying Liver-Kidney and nourishing Qi-Blood.
Exam shortcutHard obstruction needs force.Old obstruction needs support.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersBoth address chronic Bi syndrome, but one attacks a severe obstructive excess while the other combines pathogen removal with tonification.
Memory cueXiao Huo Luo breaks blockage; Du Huo Ji Sheng supports the weak.
SafetyClassical Xiao Huo Luo Dan contains processed aconite and must not be self-prepared. New weakness, bowel/bladder changes or fever with joint swelling requires urgent assessment.
