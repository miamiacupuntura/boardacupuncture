Wu Pi San

Spleen Qi deficiency with excessive Dampness causing generalized skin edema.

High-yield summary

CategoryMove Qi · Reduce Skin Edema
PatternSpleen Qi deficiency with excessive Dampness causing generalized skin edema.
ActionPromotes urination, reduces edema, regulates Qi and strengthens fluid transformation.
Decisive clueGeneralized pitting skin edema with heaviness, abdominal fullness and reduced urination.

Ingredients

Sang Bai Pi, Chen Pi, Sheng Jiang Pi, Da Fu Pi and Fu Ling Pi.

Commercial preparations and lineages may vary; always verify the source being used.

Differentiation and memory

Do not confuseWu Ling San addresses Taiyang water accumulation and Bladder Qi transformation; Wu Pi San uses five peels for superficial generalized edema with Qi stagnation.
Memory cueFive peels treat swelling located in the skin.

Safety

New generalized edema may signal heart, kidney or liver disease; breathlessness, chest pain or low urine requires urgent care.
