Obsolete, Prohibited & Protected Substances

Recognize substances that should not be copied literally from historical formulas and select lawful strategies.

Core rule

RuleA classical source is historical evidence, not automatic legal or safety authorization for the original substance.

How to use the supplied FDA/USDA/CITES handout

Group / stepWhat to knowSafe decision
Historical regulatory snapshotThe handout explicitly dates its CITES information to October 10, 2010 and cites a 2009 proposed bill.Treat it as a historical study source, not a current consolidated federal list.
Different agencies, different rulesFDA product safety, USDA plant-pest import controls, CITES wildlife trade and the ESA are separate systems.A detention, permit requirement, dietary-supplement restriction and trade prohibition are not interchangeable.
Private supplier policy“Mayway USA does not carry as raw herbs” describes a supplier decision, not by itself a federal ban.Label commercial policy separately from government regulation.
Current verificationSpecies listings, exemptions, permits, product definitions and state practice rules change.Verify the scientific species and product against current FDA, USDA APHIS, CITES, USFWS and California primary sources before dispensing.
