Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersAll three may appear in Damp-Heat and jaundice questions, so identify the associated clue: parasites and itching, Liver-channel swelling, or chest irritability.
Memory cueKu Shen kills parasites; Long Dan Cao targets Liver Damp-Heat; Zhi Zi vents Heat and vexation.
