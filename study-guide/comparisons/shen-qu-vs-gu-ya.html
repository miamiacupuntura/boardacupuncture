Herb comparison

Shen Qu vs. Gu Ya

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueShen QuGu Ya
Shared clueBoth belong to Herbs that Relieve Food Stagnation and reduce retained food.
Fast distinctionStronger reducing and guiding-out action; moves Qi.Gentler, more harmonious and tonifying; strengthens Spleen Qi.

Comparative answer key

CompareShen QuGu Ya
Medicinal nameMassa medicata fermentata.Setariae (Oryzae) Fructus germinatus.
CategoryHerbs that Relieve Food Stagnation.Herbs that Relieve Food Stagnation.
Primary emphasisMore strongly reduces and guides out stagnation and promotes Qi movement.More harmoniously reduces stagnation while strengthening the Spleen and augmenting Qi.
Decisive indicationFood stagnation with epigastric or abdominal distention and explosive diarrhea.Weak Spleen and Stomach with poor digestion and loss of appetite.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersBoth reduce Food Stagnation, but forceful reduction with distention or explosive diarrhea favors Shen Qu, while deficiency and poor appetite favor Gu Ya.
Memory cueShen Qu reduces strongly; Gu Ya gently strengthens.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), p. 501. Source Exam Key CMID 8960, questions 2–3.
