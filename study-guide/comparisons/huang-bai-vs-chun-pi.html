Herb comparison

Phellodendri Cortex (huang bai) vs. Ailanthi Cortex (chun pi)

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueHuang BaiChun Pi
Shared cluesBoth clear Heat, dry Dampness and treat Damp-Heat vaginal discharge; the source notes that they are often combined.
Fast distinctionStrong bitter-cold Heat/Dampness clearing with broad application.Astringent binding plus Blood cooling for chronic leakage and bleeding.

Comparative answer key

CompareHuang Bai (Phellodendri Cortex)Chun Pi (Ailanthi Cortex)
CategoryHerbs that Clear Heat and Dry Dampness.Herbs that Stabilize and Bind.
Shared actionClear Heat, dry Dampness and address Damp-Heat vaginal discharge.
Primary emphasisVery bitter and cold, with strong cooling/drying and a broad range of applications.Restricts, binds and cools the Blood.
Distinctive indicationsDamp-Heat jaundice through disorders of ministerial Fire in the Kidney channel.Persistent uterine bleeding, Blood in stool from Blood Heat, and chronic dysenteric disorders.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersThe Damp-Heat overlap hides a categorical difference: Huang Bai clears/dries strongly, while Chun Pi stabilizes and binds.
Common confusionJaundice or Kidney ministerial Fire favors Huang Bai. Chronic dysentery or Heat-related uterine/stool bleeding favors Chun Pi.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), p. 874. Source Exam Keys CMID 8922 and CMID 8978, questions 2 and 4.
