Tao He Cheng Qi Tang vs. Gui Zhi Fu Ling Wan

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueTao He Cheng Qi TangGui Zhi Fu Ling Wan
Shared clueBoth invigorate Blood and treat lower-abdominal Blood stasis with pain, menstrual obstruction or irregular bleeding.
Fast distinctionStasis plus Heat is acute/forceful: lower-abdominal urgency, night fever, irritability or delirium, with normal urination.Stasis forms a chronic mass: fixed pelvic fullness, pain or persistent uterine bleeding.

CompareTao He Cheng Qi TangGui Zhi Fu Ling Wan
Primary mechanismBlood stasis and Heat bind in lower burner.Blood stasis obstructs the womb and forms a mass.
Distinct actionBreaks stasis and purges Heat downward.Gradually dispels stasis and reduces masses.
Formula clueContains Da Huang and Mang Xiao.Uses balanced warming/moving and cooling Blood herbs.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersBoth act in the lower abdomen. Heat and purgation point to Tao He Cheng Qi; a stable mass/persistent bleeding points to Gui Zhi Fu Ling.
Memory cueTao He Cheng Qi purges hot stasis; Gui Zhi Fu Ling resolves the mass.
SafetyBoth require pregnancy screening; Tao He Cheng Qi is strongly purgative. Acute pelvic pain, pregnancy bleeding or heavy hemorrhage requires urgent care.
