Exam focus and safety

CluesUterine bleeding or spotting with Cold/deficiency signs; classically threatened miscarriage.
Signature formulaJiāo Ài Tāng.
TrapThe pair is for deficiency-Cold bleeding, not bright-red excess-Heat bleeding.
PreparationMelt Ē Jiāo into the strained hot decoction; do not boil it directly with the other herbs.
SafetyBleeding during pregnancy requires obstetric co-management. Ē Jiāo is an animal product requiring authenticated, legal and ethically reviewed sourcing.
