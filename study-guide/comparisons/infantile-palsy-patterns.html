Pathology or symptom comparison

Infantile Palsy / Poliomyelitis Pattern Differentiation

Diagnostic orientation

Core ideaThis historical progression is for examination study only; suspected acute flaccid paralysis requires immediate public-health and hospital assessment.
Exam focusAcute febrile stage, flaccid paralysis and chronic atrophy

Study rule

How to distinguishSystemic respiratory-digestive symptoms precede channel pain and paralysis; persistent flaccidity suggests Qi/Blood impairment, while structural atrophy marks the chronic stage.
Memory cueFever → channel pain → flaccidity → atrophy.
SafetyPolio is vaccine-preventable. Any sudden limb weakness or acute flaccid paralysis is an emergency and reportable public-health concern; respiratory or swallowing weakness is immediately life-threatening. Classical Hu Qian Wan contains obsolete tiger bone and must not be used as written.
