Gall Bladder Dampness vs. Phlegm-Dampness

Separate localized lateral Dampness from broader Phlegm obstruction with sputum or dizziness.

Fast differentiation clues

ClueGall Bladder DampnessPhlegm-Dampness
SharedBoth cause heaviness, nausea, poor appetite, sticky coating and a slippery pulse.
Fast distinctionGall Bladder: hypochondriac fullness and unilateral coating predominate.Phlegm-Dampness: sputum, chest oppression, throat sensation, dizziness or mental clouding predominate.

Clinical table

CompareGall Bladder DampnessPhlegm-Dampness
DistributionLocalized to Gall Bladder/lateral region.Broader obstruction of Lung, chest, head or Mind.
Formula associationSan Ren Tang plus Yin Chen Hao in the supplied table.Er Chen Tang or Wen Dan Tang by presentation.

Exam and safety

Why it mattersMaterial Phlegm or upper-body obstruction distinguishes Phlegm-Dampness from localized Dampness.
Memory cueDampness weighs the side; Phlegm-Dampness rises and clogs.
SafetyBreathlessness, chest pain, confusion, sudden neurologic symptoms or persistent vomiting requires urgent evaluation.
