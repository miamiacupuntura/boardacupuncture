Clinical Herb–Drug Interaction Cases

Apply effect-based screening to common medication scenarios and define monitoring before treatment.

Core rule

RuleEvery interaction decision should end with an action: avoid, modify, monitor or coordinate.

Common clinical cases

Group / stepWhat to knowSafe decision
Warfarin + Blood moversAdditive bleeding and INR instability are possible.Coordinate before starting and schedule INR after any change.
Digoxin + Gān Cǎo or Xiāng Jiā PíLicorice-related hypokalemia can increase rhythm risk; Periploca has cardiac glycosides.Avoid Xiāng Jiā Pí and monitor potassium/rhythm when relevant.
Antihypertensives + Má HuángSympathomimetic stimulation can oppose BP control and raise pulse.Avoid with uncontrolled hypertension and reconcile stimulants.
Insulin/sulfonylurea + glucose-active tonicsGlucose may fall after formula or diet/appetite changes.Increase glucose checks and coordinate medication adjustment.
Sedatives/opioids + Shen-calming herbsDrowsiness, falls and respiratory impairment may add.Avoid alcohol/driving and assess dose, age and breathing risk.
Transplant/biologic therapy + immune-active tonicsImmune modulation may conflict with the therapeutic goal or drug levels.Specialist approval is required.
Diuretics + water-draining formulasVolume depletion, hypotension, kidney injury and electrolyte shifts may add.Monitor weight, pressure, renal function and electrolytes.

Document the plan

Group / stepWhat to knowSafe decision
Exposure and endpointRecord exact product, concentration and dose; define the measurable risk endpoint.Retain label/lot and specify bleeding, BP, glucose, rhythm or laboratory monitoring.
Owner and stop planState who reviews results and what symptoms or values trigger stopping.Give written instructions and communicate across practitioner, physician and pharmacist.

When to act immediately

EmergencyMajor bleeding, severe hypoglycemia, chest pain, syncope, breathing suppression, anaphylaxis or sudden neurologic symptoms requires emergency care.

Educational use: this library does not replace medical evaluation, poison control, local regulation or pharmacist consultation.
