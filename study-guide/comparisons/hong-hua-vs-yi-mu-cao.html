Herb comparison

Carthami Flos (hong hua) vs. Leonuri Herba (yi mu cao)

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueHong HuaYi Mu Cao
Shared clueBoth invigorate Blood and enter the Blood level of the Heart and Liver channels.
Fast distinctionWarm and stronger at invigorating Blood and breaking stasis.Slightly cold; cools Blood Heat and facilitates urination.

Comparative answer key

CompareHong HuaYi Mu Cao
Botanical nameCarthami Flos.Leonuri Herba.
Common nameSafflower flower.Chinese motherwort.
CategoryHerbs that Regulate and Invigorate Blood.Herbs that Regulate and Invigorate Blood.
Properties and channelsWarm and acrid; Heart and Liver.Slightly cold, acrid and bitter; Heart, Liver and Urinary Bladder.
Primary emphasisMore strongly invigorates Blood, reduces swelling and, in large doses, strongly breaks Blood stasis.Gently cools Blood Heat associated with stagnation and uniquely facilitates urination.
Clinical cluesMenstrual obstruction, abdominal pain or masses, difficult delivery, traumatic pain and topical use for bedsores.Blood-stasis menstrual problems, postpartum abdominal pain, external-injury swelling and pain, dysuria or edema.
CautionsContraindicated during pregnancy.Contraindicated during pregnancy; caution with Yin or Blood deficiency and excessive menstruation.
Formula cluesQi Li San; Bu Yang Huan Wu Tang; Ge Xia Zhu Yu Tang; Xue Fu Zhu Yu Tang; Tao Hong Si Wu Tang; Fu Yuan Huo Xue Tang.Tian Ma Gou Teng Yin; Ba Zhen Yi Mu Tang.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersBoth treat Blood stasis, but forceful warming and Blood movement favor Hong Hua, while Heat, edema, dysuria or Urinary-Bladder involvement favor Yi Mu Cao.
Memory cueHong Hua moves Blood strongly; Yi Mu Cao cools and urinates.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), p. 615. Source Exam Keys CMID 8985, questions 1–3, and CMID 9108, questions 1–3.
