Herb comparison

Polygoni multiflori Caulis (ye jiao teng) vs. Albiziae Cortex (he huan pi)

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueYe Jiao TengHe Huan Pi
Shared cluesBoth nourish the Heart and calm the Spirit.
Fast distinctionNourishes Heart-Liver Blood and treats Wind-related itching and skin disorders.Soothes Heart-Spleen Qi, invigorates Blood, stops pain and reconnects sinews and bones.

Comparative answer key

CompareYe Jiao TengHe Huan Pi
Shared categoryHerbs that Nourish the Heart and Calm the Spirit.
Shared actionCalm the Spirit.
Organ or substance emphasisNourishes Heart and Liver Blood.Soothes Heart and Spleen Qi.
Distinctive actionExpels Wind from the collaterals.Invigorates Blood and stops pain.
Distinctive indicationItching and skin disorders.Injury or damage requiring reconnection of sinews and bones.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersBoth calm the Spirit, but Blood nourishment with skin itching differs from Qi soothing with pain and traumatic injury.
Memory cueYe Jiao Teng: Blood and itching; He Huan Pi: Qi, pain and bones.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), p. 936. Source Exam Key CMID 8948, question 9.
