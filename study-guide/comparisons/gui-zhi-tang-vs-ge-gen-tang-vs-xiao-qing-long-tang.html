Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersThe shared Exterior presentation is only the starting point. Sweat identifies Gui Zhi Tang, marked upper-back rigidity identifies Ge Gen Tang, and internal thin fluids identify Xiao Qing Long Tang.
Memory cueGui Zhi leaks; Ge Gen loosens; Xiao Qing Long drains the cold dragon.
SafetyGe Gen Tang and Xiao Qing Long Tang contain Ma Huang; Xiao Qing Long Tang also contains processed Ban Xia and Xi Xin. Cardiovascular disease, pregnancy, frailty and medication use require professional screening.
