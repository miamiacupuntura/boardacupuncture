Liver Qi Stagnation vs. Stagnant Liver Qi Turning to Heat

Recognize when ordinary constraint acquires Heat signs and requires more than simply moving Qi.

Fast differentiation clues

ClueLiver Qi StagnationStagnation Turning to Heat
SharedBoth cause rib-side or chest distention, mood changes, sighing and a wiry pulse.
Fast distinctionConstraint without clear Heat: distention, depression, moodiness and menstrual irregularity.Constraint plus Heat: irritability, feeling hot, bitter taste, red tongue sides and a faster wiry pulse.

Clinical table

CompareLiver Qi StagnationStagnation Turning to Heat
TemperatureNeutral; Heat signs absent or slight.Heat emerges from prolonged constraint.
Tongue / pulseNormal or dusky; wiry.Red sides, possible yellow coat; wiry-rapid.
Formula associationXiao Yao San or Chai Hu Shu Gan San by deficiency/excess.Dan Zhi Xiao Yao San when Heat signs are decisive.

Exam and safety

Why it mattersThe diagnosis changes when constrained Qi generates measurable Heat—not merely when the patient feels stressed.
Memory cueStagnation presses; transformed Heat presses and burns.
SafetyPersistent pain, jaundice, fever, heavy bleeding or pregnancy-related symptoms need medical evaluation.
