Herb comparison

Astragali membranacei Radix (huang qi) vs. Astragali complanati Semen (sha yuan zi / sha yuan ji li)

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueHuang QiSha Yuan Zi
Name clueBoth come from the Astragalus genus, but they are different species and different medicinal parts.
Fast distinctionAstragalus root: tonifies Qi, raises Yang, stabilizes the Exterior and reduces edema.Astragalus seed: tonifies Liver-Kidney Yang, secures Essence and brightens the eyes.

Comparative answer key

CompareHuang QiSha Yuan Zi
Medicinal nameAstragali membranacei Radix; Radix Astragali.Astragali complanati Semen; also called Sha Yuan Ji Li.
Plant partRoot.Seed.
CategoryHerbs that Tonify the Qi.Herbs that Tonify the Yang.
Primary emphasisTonifies Qi, raises Yang, stabilizes the Exterior, supports Qi and Blood, and reduces edema.Tonifies the Liver and Kidneys, secures Essence and brightens the eyes.
Decisive indicationsFatigue, diarrhea, prolapse, spontaneous sweating, weak Protective Qi, postpartum Qi-Blood deficiency or edema.Frequent urination, premature ejaculation, vaginal discharge, aching lower back or cloudy vision.
Formula cluesBu Zhong Yi Qi Tang; Yu Ping Feng San; Bu Yang Huan Wu Tang; Dang Gui Bu Xue Tang; Fang Ji Huang Qi Tang.Jin Suo Gu Jing Wan.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersThe shared Astragalus name can obscure a major distinction: Huang Qi is a Qi-tonifying root, while Sha Yuan Zi is a Yang-tonifying seed with a Liver-Kidney and securing focus.
Memory cueHuang Qi root raises Qi; Sha Yuan Zi seed secures Essence and supports the eyes.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), p. 785. Source Exam Key CMID 9059, question 4.
