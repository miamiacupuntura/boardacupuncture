Herb comparison

Lian Zi vs. Qian Shi

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueLian ZiQian Shi
Shared clueBoth are sweet, neutral, astringent seeds that tonify the Spleen and Kidneys and bind chronic diarrhea or spermatorrhea.
Fast distinctionNourishes the Heart, restores Heart-Kidney communication and treats incessant uterine bleeding.Also eliminates Dampness and is better when deficiency with Dampness causes vaginal discharge.

Decisive indicationIncessant uterine bleeding.Deficiency with predominant Dampness, especially vaginal discharge.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersBoth seeds tonify and bind nearly identical deficiency patterns. Heart-Spirit and uterine-bleeding clues favor Lian Zi; a prominent Dampness component favors Qian Shi.
Memory cueLian Zi links Heart and Kidney; Qian Shi clears the Damp component.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), p. 884. Source Exam Keys CMID 9011, question 7, and CMID 9040, question 1.
