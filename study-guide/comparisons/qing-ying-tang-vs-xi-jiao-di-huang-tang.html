Qing Ying Tang vs. Xi Jiao Di Huang Tang

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueQing Ying TangXi Jiao Di Huang Tang
Shared clueBoth treat deep warm-disease Heat, cool the Blood, protect Yin and may present with fever worse at night, restlessness, rash or altered consciousness.
Fast distinctionNutritive-level Heat: early Shen disturbance, faint rash and residual Qi-level Heat.Blood-level Heat: bleeding, dense rash, severe delirium and Blood stasis.

Comparative answer key

CompareQing Ying TangXi Jiao Di Huang Tang
Disease levelYing/Nutritive level.Xue/Blood level.
Primary actionClears Ying Heat, nourishes Yin and vents Heat toward the Qi level.Cools Blood, stops bleeding and disperses stasis.
Decisive clueFever worse at night with irritability and indistinct rash.Hemorrhage or pronounced macular eruption with severe mental disturbance.
Exam shortcutDeep Heat entering Ying.Deeper Heat damaging Blood.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersThese are sequential depth patterns. Bleeding and pronounced Blood-level injury move the answer from Qing Ying Tang to Xi Jiao Di Huang Tang.
Memory cueQing Ying vents the nutritive level; Xi Jiao Di Huang cools damaged Blood.
SafetyXi Jiao (rhinoceros horn) is obsolete and illegal; modern references substitute Shui Niu Jiao. High fever, bleeding or altered consciousness is an emergency.
SourceSource bank: FWB 2.2 series and Heat-clearing materials; corroborated with formula references. Modern substitution: Shui Niu Jiao for Xi Jiao.
