Batch 6 · Formula 4 of 10

Shi Quan Da Bu Tang

Qi and Blood deficiency with mild deficiency-Cold.

High-yield summary

CategoryTonify Qi and Blood · Warm Yang
PatternQi and Blood deficiency with mild deficiency-Cold.
ActionStrongly tonifies Qi and Blood and gently warms Yang.
Decisive clueMarked fatigue, pallor and weakness with cold limbs or aversion to cold after chronic illness or blood loss.

Ingredients

Ren Shen, Bai Zhu, Fu Ling, Zhi Gan Cao, Shu Di Huang, Bai Shao, Dang Gui, Chuan Xiong, Huang Qi and Rou Gui, with Sheng Jiang and Da Zao in decoction.

Commercial preparations and lineages may vary; always verify the source being used.

Differentiation and memory

Do not confuseBa Zhen Tang tonifies Qi and Blood; Shi Quan Da Bu Tang adds Huang Qi and Rou Gui for stronger support and mild Cold.
Memory cueEight Treasures plus Huang Qi and Rou Gui make the Ten Complete Great Tonics.

Safety

Severe fatigue, pallor, fainting, unexplained weight loss or suspected bleeding requires diagnostic testing before tonic treatment.
