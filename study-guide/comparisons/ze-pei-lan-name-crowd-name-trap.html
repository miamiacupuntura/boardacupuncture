Zé Lán, Pèi Lán and Zé Xiè

Similar Lán/Xiè sounds separate Blood movement, aromatic Damp transformation and water drainage.

Memory rule

AnchorZé Lán moves the period; Pèi Lán is “worn” for aroma; Zé Xiè drains downward.
