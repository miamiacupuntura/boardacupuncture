Liu Wei Di Huang Wan vs. Qing Hao Bie Jia Tang

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueLiu Wei Di Huang WanQing Hao Bie Jia Tang
Shared clueBoth address Yin deficiency with deficiency-Heat signs and use a Rehmannia root, but their clinical setting and therapeutic direction differ.
Fast distinctionChronic Liver-Kidney Yin deficiency: soreness of the back and knees, dizziness, tinnitus and developmental or reproductive signs.Late-stage warm disease: night fever that subsides in the morning without sweating and a cool sensation deep in the bones.

Comparative answer key

CompareLiu Wei Di Huang WanQing Hao Bie Jia Tang
Root patternLiver and Kidney Yin deficiency.Heat lodged in the Yin level after warm disease with Yin and fluids injured.
Rehmannia clueShu Di Huang nourishes Yin and Essence.Sheng Di Huang clears Heat and cools the Blood while protecting Yin.
Therapeutic emphasisLong-term nourishment and balancing of Kidney-Liver Yin.Leads hidden Heat outward while nourishing Yin.
Decisive symptomChronic lumbar weakness, tinnitus or night sweating.Night fever that improves by morning without sweat.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersBoth can show deficiency Heat, but Liu Wei Di Huang Wan rebuilds a chronic deficient root; Qing Hao Bie Jia Tang clears a lingering pathogen from the Yin level.
Memory cueLiu Wei fills the Yin; Qing Hao leads hidden Heat out.
SafetyPersistent fever, unexplained weight loss or recurrent night sweats requires medical evaluation rather than self-treatment.
