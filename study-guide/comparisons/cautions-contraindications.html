Cautions & Contraindications

Guideline scope

All displayed content comes from individual-monograph warnings and official category rules in the guideline. This tool does not diagnose, prescribe or clear use during pregnancy or lactation.

How the level is determined

Individual monograph

Takes precedence when an herb card states a specific caution or contraindication.

Category rule

Applies when the guideline assigns a pregnancy rule to the complete category.

Category caution

Remains a caution and is not upgraded to a contraindication.

No inference

Herbs without a documented warning are omitted; absence does not prove safety.
