Educational framework, not a clinical calculator

This page organizes the supplied chart and corrects its interpretation boundaries. It does not diagnose, prescribe, clear use in pregnancy or lactation, or replace review of identity, dose, processing, the complete formula, medicines and medical conditions.

How to read the four levels

High risk

Avoid unsupervised use; identity, processing or toxicity may be critical.

Pregnancy/lactation

Source classification requiring individualized obstetric and herbal review.

Pattern caution

Traditional differentiation boundary, not universal toxicity.

Historical / source

Ambiguous or chart-specific wording preserved without turning it into a rule.
