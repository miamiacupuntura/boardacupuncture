Angelica — Blood, Forehead and Lower Body

Members of the carrot family share aroma but specialize in Blood, face/sinuses or lower-body Wind-Damp.

One picture, one rule

RuleDāng Guī = Blood; Bái Zhǐ = bright forehead; Dú Huó = lower-body Bi.

The part decides the job

MedicinalPart / identityJob
Dāng Guī 当归Angelica sinensis rootNourishes and invigorates Blood, regulates menses and relieves pain.
Bái Zhǐ 白芷Angelica dahurica rootExpels Wind, treats frontal/sinus pain, dries discharge and reduces swelling.
Dú Huó 独活Angelica pubescens rootDispels Wind-Damp with emphasis on lower-body Cold-Bi.
Qiāng Huó 羌活Notopterygium—not AngelicaSame carrot family; treats upper-body/occipital Wind-Cold-Damp.

Exam and safety

Exam keyBlood/gynecology = Dāng Guī; forehead/nose = Bái Zhǐ; lower body = Dú Huó; upper body = Qiāng Huó.
SafetyDāng Guī and other moving members require bleeding/drug and pregnancy screening. Botanical genus and shared family are not the same.
