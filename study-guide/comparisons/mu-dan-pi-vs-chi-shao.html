Herb comparison

Moutan Cortex (mu dan pi) vs. Paeoniae Radix rubra (chi shao)

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueMu Dan PiChi Shao
Shared cluesBoth cool the Blood and dispel stasis and are frequently combined for these purposes.
Fast distinctionMore cooling; clears both excess and deficiency Heat from the Blood; Heart focus.Stronger Blood invigoration and pain relief; clears excess Heat but not Yin-deficiency Heat; Liver focus.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersThe shared Blood-cooling and stasis-dispersing actions make the type of Heat and relative need for Blood movement decisive.
Common confusionDeficiency Heat points to Mu Dan Pi; stronger stasis pain and Blood invigoration points to Chi Shao.
How it may appearA Heart-focused Blood-Heat comparison or Yin-deficiency Heat favors Mu Dan Pi. A Liver-focused excess-Heat and painful-stasis presentation favors Chi Shao.
Source question formatQuestion 1 is Matching.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), p. 127. Source Exam Key CMID 8916.
