Si Wu Tang

Blood deficiency with impaired circulation or mild Blood stasis.

High-yield summary

CategoryNourish and Regulate Blood
PatternBlood deficiency with impaired circulation or mild Blood stasis.
ActionNourishes Blood and regulates its circulation.
Decisive cluePallor, dizziness, dry skin, palpitations and scant or irregular menses with a pale tongue and fine pulse.

Ingredients

Shu Di Huang, Bai Shao, Dang Gui and Chuan Xiong.

Commercial preparations and lineages may vary; always verify the source being used.

Differentiation and memory

Do not confuseDang Gui Bu Xue Tang uses strong Qi tonification to generate Blood; Si Wu Tang directly combines two nourishing and two regulating Blood herbs.
Memory cueShu Di and Bai Shao nourish; Dang Gui and Chuan Xiong nourish while keeping Blood moving.

Safety

Heavy bleeding, pregnancy-related bleeding, marked pallor, fainting or rapid heartbeat requires medical evaluation.
