Herb comparison

Jie Geng vs. Xing Ren

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueJie GengXing Ren
Shared clueBoth are Lung-channel, Qi-level herbs used together to restore normal Lung-Qi circulation.
Fast distinctionRaises and disseminates Lung Qi.Directs rebellious Lung Qi downward.
Symptom clueThroat symptoms and Phlegm.Cough and wheezing.

Comparative answer key

CompareJie GengXing Ren
Medicinal namePlatycodi Radix.Armeniacae Semen.
Broad shared categoryHerbs that Transform Phlegm and Stop Coughing.
Source subcategoryWarm Herbs that Transform Cold-Phlegm.Herbs that Relieve Coughing and Wheezing.
Qi directionAscending: raises and disseminates Lung Qi.Descending: directs rebellious Qi downward.
Primary actionsBenefits the throat and expels Phlegm.Alleviates cough and calms wheezing.
Shared clinical contextDisrupted Lung-Qi circulation from an externally contracted pathogenic factor or internal accumulation of Phlegm or thin mucus.
Combination logicTheir complementary ascending and descending actions restore the normal movement of Lung Qi.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersBoth address Lung Qi and Phlegm, but direction decides the match: throat benefit and upward dissemination identify Jie Geng; rebellious Qi, cough and wheezing identify Xing Ren.
Memory cueJie Geng lifts and opens; Xing Ren descends and calms.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), p. 431. Source Exam Keys CMID 8991, questions 1–2, and CMID 9102, questions 3–4.
