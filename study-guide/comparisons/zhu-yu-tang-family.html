Formula family

Zhu Yu Tang Family

Root formula and architecture

RootThe Zhu Yu formulas invigorate Blood and dispel stasis, but the decisive exam distinction is anatomical location and temperature.
ArchitectureTao Ren, Hong Hua, Chuan Xiong and Chi Shao recur across the family. Guiding and Qi-regulating herbs redirect the strategy to a specific region.

Study rule

How to chooseLocate first: head, chest, diaphragm, lower abdomen or channels. In the lower abdomen, Cold and improvement with warmth make Shao Fu decisive.
Memory cueTong Qiao = head; Xue Fu = chest; Ge Xia = diaphragm; Shao Fu = cold pelvis; Shen Tong = body pain.
SafetyStrong Blood-moving formulas require pregnancy, bleeding, anticoagulant and surgery review. New chest pain, neurologic deficit or severe abdominal pain requires urgent biomedical evaluation.
