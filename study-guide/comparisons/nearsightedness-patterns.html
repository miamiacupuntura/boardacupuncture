Pathology or symptom comparison

Nearsightedness Clinical Comparison

Diagnostic orientation

Core ideaMyopia is a refractive condition measured by eye examination; the source associates accompanying constitutional symptoms with Liver-Blood/Kidney-Essence depletion.
Exam focusBlurred distance vision versus warning signs of retinal disease

Comparative differentiation

Pattern or presentationDecisive cluesRepresentative formula/herbStrategy
Liver-Blood and Kidney-Essence depletionNear objects clear but distant objects blurred, with eye fatigue, headache, dizziness, tinnitus, weak back/knees and poor memoryQi Ju Di Huang WanHistorical strategy: support Liver-Blood/Kidney-Essence and brighten eyes.

Study rule

How to distinguishStable blurred distance vision that improves with corrective lenses is refractive; sudden distortion, field loss, flashes or floaters is not routine myopia.
Memory cueNear clear, far blurred = myopia; sudden curtain/flashes = retina until proven otherwise.
SafetyMyopia requires optometric/ophthalmic refraction and appropriate correction. Sudden flashes, many floaters, a curtain/shadow, distortion or rapid vision loss requires emergency retinal evaluation.
