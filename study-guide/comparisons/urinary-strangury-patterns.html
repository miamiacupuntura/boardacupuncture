Pathology or symptom comparison

Urinary Strangury Pattern Differentiation

Diagnostic orientation

Core ideaUrinary quality and pain identify the lin presentation; excess and deficiency must then be separated.
Exam focusBurning, stones, blood, turbidity and deficiency

Comparative differentiation

Pattern or presentationDecisive cluesRepresentative formula/herbStrategy
Heat linFrequent scant burning foul urine with thirst and yellow greasy coatBa Zheng SanClear Damp-Heat and free urine.
Stone linSand/stone, interrupted stream, severe sacral pain or hematuriaShi Wei SanClear Heat and expel stones.
Qi linPainful difficult urine with abdominal/hypochondriac distention; weak fatigue if deficientChen Xiang San; Bu Zhong Yi Qi Tang if deficientRegulate or supplement Qi.
Blood linBurning urination with blood/clots; slight pain plus empty Heat when deficientXiao Ji Yin Zi; Zhi Bai Di Huang Tang if deficientCool Blood or nourish Yin.
Turbid/unctuous linMilky rice-water urine; fatigue and lumbar weakness mark deficiencyCheng Shi Bi Xie Fen Qing Yin; Gao Lin Tang if deficientSeparate clear/turbid and secure Essence.
Taxation linDribbling after exertion with fatigue and weak low back/kneesWu Bi Shan Yao WanStrengthen Spleen and Kidney.

Study rule

How to distinguishBurning marks Heat, sudden stop marks Stone, distention marks Qi, red marks Blood, milky urine marks turbid lin, and exertional recurrence marks taxation.
Memory cueBurn—Heat; stop—Stone; distend—Qi; red—Blood; milk—Turbid; strain—Taxation.
SafetyFever with flank pain, inability to urinate, vomiting, pregnancy, visible blood or suspected obstruction/stone requires urgent medical evaluation.
