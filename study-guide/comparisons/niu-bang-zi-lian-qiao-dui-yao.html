Appendix B pair · 16 of 51

Niú Bàng Zǐ + Lián Qiào

牛蒡子 + 连翘

One picture, one job

PictureThe swollen-throat pair.
Combined jobDisperse Wind-Heat, resolve toxicity, benefit the throat and reduce swelling.
CategoryBenefit the Throat · Reduce Swelling

How the pair works

HerbRole within the pair
Niú Bàng ZǐVents Wind-Heat, opens the throat and promotes rash expression.
Lián QiàoClears toxicity and breaks up hot swelling and clumps.

Exam focus and safety

CluesMarked swollen sore throat, painful neck swelling or mumps-type presentation.
Signature formulaYín Qiáo Sǎn; Pǔ Jì Xiāo Dú Yǐn.
TrapCompared with Jīn Yín Huā + Lián Qiào, this pair emphasizes throat and swelling more strongly.
PreparationNiú Bàng Zǐ is commonly crushed before decoction.
SafetyNiú Bàng Zǐ can loosen stool. Drooling, inability to swallow, muffled voice, airway symptoms or severe dehydration requires urgent care.
