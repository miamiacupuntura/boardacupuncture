Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersThe herbs overlap in exterior Wind-Heat, but each has a high-yield secondary action.
Common confusionDo not choose only from the shared exterior symptom; match the throat, rash, eye, cough, neck, bowel or Liver clue.
How it may appearA matching question may list each herb once and ask for its distinctive clinical extension.
Source question formatsQuestion 2 is Matching. Questions 8–12 are Multiple Choice. Questions 13–16 are True/False review blocks containing flashcards, mnemonics, a master story and a comparison drill; the keyed answer for each block is True.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), p. 51. Source Exam Key CMID 9277.
