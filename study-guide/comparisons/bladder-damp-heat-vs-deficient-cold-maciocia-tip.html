Bladder Damp-Heat vs. Deficient and Cold

Separate hot painful obstruction from cold failure to contain urine.

Fast differentiation clues

ClueBladder Damp-HeatBladder Deficient and Cold
SharedBoth disturb urination and may cause frequency.
Fast distinctionExcess Heat: burning, urgency, dark scant difficult urine and yellow coat.Deficient Cold: frequent pale abundant urine, leakage, cold limbs and pale wet tongue.

Clinical table

CompareBladder Damp-HeatBladder Deficient and Cold
NatureHot obstructive excess.Cold containment deficiency.
Formula associationBa Zheng San.Suo Quan Wan, Sang Piao Xiao San or Tu Si Zi Wan.

Exam and safety

Why it mattersPainful dark scant urine is excess Heat; pale abundant leakage is deficient Cold.
Memory cueDamp-Heat cannot pass; deficient Cold cannot hold.
SafetyPainful urination may be infection. Fever, flank pain, blood, pregnancy or retention needs urgent assessment.

Review prompts

  • Which urine is abundant?
  • Which has burning?
