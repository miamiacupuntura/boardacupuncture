Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersAll nourish Liver-Kidney Yin, but Blood with Yang assistance favors Gou Qi Zi, Blood alone identifies Sang Shen here, and deficiency Heat points to Nu Zhen Zi.
Memory cueGou Qi Zi: Blood plus Yang; Sang Shen: Blood; Nu Zhen Zi: Yin plus cooling.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), p. 838. Source Exam Keys CMID 8957, question 6, and CMID 9026, question 2.
