Herb comparison

Shi Chang Pu vs. Niu Huang

Study tip · Two keys, two locks

ClueShi Chang PuNiu Huang
Shared clueBoth open the sensory orifices when Phlegm or Heat obstructs consciousness and the Spirit.
Fast distinctionWarm aromatic key: Damp-Phlegm fog, sticky coat, forgetfulness and Middle-Jiao obstruction.Cold emergency key: Hot toxic closure, high fever, convulsions, red face and thick yellow coat.

Formula boundary and safety

Current formula rosterNeither herb is chief or co-chief in the current 63-formula Board roster; outside-roster formulas are not linked.
PreparationPreserve Shi Chang Pu’s volatile oils; Niu Huang is used in tiny powdered doses rather than routine decoction.
Critical trapDo not use orifice-opening logic for Deficiency collapse: limpness, cold limbs, profuse sweat, shallow breathing, incontinence and a deep weak pulse.
Emergency boundaryLoss of consciousness, a new seizure, stroke signs or high fever with mental-status change requires emergency services.
