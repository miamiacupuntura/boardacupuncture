Pathology or symptom comparison

Four Herbs for Headache Location

Diagnostic orientation

Core ideaLocation supplies the fastest exam clue when selecting a guiding herb for headache.
Exam focusHeadache location and channel guidance

Comparative differentiation

Pattern or presentationDecisive cluesRepresentative formula/herbStrategy
VertexPain at the top of the headGao BenGuides to the vertex.
OccipitalPain at the back of the head or upper neckQiang HuoGuides to the Taiyang/Governing Vessel region.
FrontalForehead, brow or sinus-region painBai ZhiGuides to the Yangming frontal region.
Lateral or Blood stasisTemporal pain or fixed/stabbing headacheChuan XiongMoves Blood and reaches the head.

Study rule

How to distinguishChoose by location first; fixed or stabbing pain adds the Blood-stasis clue.
Memory cueGao Ben—top; Qiang Huo—back; Bai Zhi—front; Chuan Xiong—side or stasis.
SafetyA sudden thunderclap headache, neurologic deficit, fever with neck stiffness or headache after major trauma requires emergency evaluation.
