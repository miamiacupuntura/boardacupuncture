Four Herbs for Headache Location

Study tip · Clues to help answer

Location or patternGuiding herb
VertexGao Ben
Back of the headQiang Huo
FrontalBai Zhi
Lateral or involving Blood stagnationChuan Xiong

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersThe source states that headache treatment depends on assistant herbs guiding therapy into the appropriate channels.
Memory cueGao Ben—top; Qiang Huo—back; Bai Zhi—front; Chuan Xiong—side or stasis.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), p. 23. Source Exam Key CMID 8966, question 2.
