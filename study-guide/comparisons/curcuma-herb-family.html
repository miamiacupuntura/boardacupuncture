Curcuma — Three Faces and One Impostor

One genus warms the arms, cools the Heart/Liver or breaks masses; Sān Léng travels with it but is not Curcuma.

One picture, one rule

RuleJiāng Huáng warms; Yù Jīn cools; É Zhú breaks.

The part decides the job

MedicinalPart / identityJob
Jiāng Huáng 姜黄Turmeric rhizomeInvigorates Blood, moves Qi and unblocks shoulder/arm channels.
Yù Jīn 郁金Curcuma tuberous rootMoves Qi-Blood, cools Blood, opens Heart and benefits Gallbladder.
É Zhú 莪术Zedoary rhizomeBreaks Blood/Qi and disperses food accumulation or abdominal masses.
Sān Léng 三棱Sparganium rhizome—not CurcumaPartner of É Zhú; breaks Blood more strongly while É Zhú breaks Qi more.

Exam and safety

Exam keyUpper-limb pain = Jiāng Huáng; constrained Heat/jaundice = Yù Jīn; fixed mass = É Zhú/Sān Léng.
SafetyÉ Zhú and Sān Léng are contraindicated in pregnancy. Traditional incompatibilities: Dīng Xiāng–Yù Jīn and Máng Xiāo–Sān Léng.
