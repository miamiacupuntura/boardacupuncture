Family 6 of 20

Cinnamon 桂 — Twig Out, Bark In

The thin twig travels to surface and channels; thick bark descends to Kidney/Mìngmén.

One picture, one rule

RuleGuì Zhī goes out; Ròu Guì goes down and in.

The part decides the job

MedicinalPart / identityJob
Guì Zhī 桂枝TwigReleases muscle layer, harmonizes Yíng-Wèi, warms channels and transforms water.
Ròu Guì 肉桂BarkWarms Kidney Yang/Mìngmén, leads floating Fire home and supports Qi-Blood tonics.

Exam and safety

Exam keyExterior or channel Cold points to twig; deep Yang deficiency points to bark.
SafetyBoth require pregnancy and Heat/bleeding caution, especially Ròu Guì. Ròu Guì traditionally “fears” Chì Shí Zhī.
