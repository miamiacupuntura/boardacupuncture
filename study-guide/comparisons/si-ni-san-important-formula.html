Batch 6 · Formula 6 of 10

Si Ni San

Liver Qi constraint overacting on the Spleen and blocking Yang from reaching the extremities.

High-yield summary

CategoryHarmonize Liver and Spleen · Release Constraint
PatternLiver Qi constraint overacting on the Spleen and blocking Yang from reaching the extremities.
ActionDisperses constrained Qi, harmonizes Liver/Spleen and restores circulation to the limbs.
Decisive clueCold fingers and toes with a warm body, irritability and abdominal or hypochondriac discomfort.

Ingredients

Chai Hu, Bai Shao, Zhi Shi and Zhi Gan Cao.

Commercial preparations and lineages may vary; always verify the source being used.

Differentiation and memory

Do not confuseSi Ni Tang treats true Yang collapse with a cold body and deep faint pulse; Si Ni San treats Qi constraint with only the extremities cold.
Memory cueFour cold extremities do not always mean Cold: Si Ni San frees constrained Yang.

Safety

Cold limbs with chest pain, fainting, confusion, severe weakness or low blood pressure requires emergency assessment.
