Formula comparison

Wu Ling San vs. Zhu Ling Tang

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueWu Ling SanZhu Ling Tang
Shared clueBoth promote urination and treat fluid accumulation, sharing Fu Ling, Zhu Ling and Ze Xie.
Fast distinctionWarms Yang and restores Bladder Qi transformation.Clears Heat while promoting urination and protects injured Yin.

Comparative answer key

CompareWu Ling SanZhu Ling Tang
PatternTaiyang water accumulation from impaired Bladder Qi transformation.Water and Heat in the lower burner with Yin injury.
Distinct actionWarms Yang and transforms Qi.Clears Heat and nourishes Yin.
IngredientsFu Ling, Zhu Ling and Ze Xie are shared; Gui Zhi and Bai Zhu distinguish Wu Ling San.Fu Ling, Zhu Ling and Ze Xie are shared; E Jiao and Hua Shi distinguish Zhu Ling Tang.
Key signsUrinary difficulty, edema or fluid retention; thirst with difficulty processing ingested fluids.Urinary difficulty with thirst, fever or irritability; may include painful urination or blood in the urine.
Water clueMay vomit soon after drinking because fluids cannot be transformed.Desires and can take fluids, but thirst persists from Heat/Yin injury.
Tongue emphasisOften pale or normal with a white coating.Often red with a yellow coating when Heat is pronounced.
PulseFloating and rapid in the Taiyang presentation.Thin and rapid.
Exam shortcutThink “transformation failure.”Think “water + Heat + Yin injury.”

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersThe shared diuretic core can obscure the direction of treatment. Wu Ling San warms and transforms; Zhu Ling Tang drains while cooling and preserving fluids.
Memory cueWu Ling transforms; Zhu Ling clears and protects Yin.
SafetyUrinary retention, fever with flank pain, visible blood in urine, or inability to keep fluids down requires medical assessment rather than self-treatment.
SourceSource bank: FWB 5.2 and FWB 5.2.1, Comparison Formulas to Expel Damp; corroborated with the individual formula profiles.
