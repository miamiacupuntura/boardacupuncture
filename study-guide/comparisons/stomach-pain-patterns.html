Pathology or symptom comparison

Stomach Pain Pattern Differentiation

Diagnostic orientation

Core ideaTemperature, relationship to food or stress, and whether the pain is fixed provide the fastest distinctions.
Exam focusTemperature, pressure, food, emotion and fixed pain

Comparative differentiation

Pattern or presentationDecisive cluesRepresentative formula/herbStrategy
Stomach ColdAbrupt pain worse with Cold and relieved by warmth; no thirstLiang Fu WanScatter Cold and relieve pain.
Food StagnationPain worse with pressure, foul belching, reflux and relief after vomiting or gasBao He WanReduce food and move stagnation.
Liver Qi disrupting StomachDistending pain spreading to ribs, stress-related belching and refluxChai Hu Shu Gan SanSoothe Liver and harmonize Stomach.
Liver-Stomach HeatBurning acute pain, irritability, bitter dry taste and acid refluxZuo Jin WanClear Heat and harmonize Stomach.
Blood stasisFixed stabbing pain, purple tongue, hematemesis or black stoolShi Xiao San + Dan Shen YinInvigorate Blood and dispel stasis.
Stomach Yin deficiencyDull pain, dry mouth, dry stool and red dry tongueYi Guan Jian or Shao Yao Gan Cao TangNourish Yin and relieve pain.
Spleen-Stomach deficiency ColdDull pain relieved by warmth/pressure with fatigue, cold limbs and loose stoolHuang Qi Jian Zhong TangWarm and supplement the Middle.

Study rule

How to distinguishWarmth points to Cold; pressure worsens excess but relieves deficiency; stress points to Liver Qi; fixed stabbing pain points to Blood stasis; dryness points to Yin deficiency.
Memory cueCold—warm it; Food—foul; Liver—stress; Heat—burning; Blood—fixed; Yin—dry; Yang—weak and cold.
SafetyBlack stool, vomiting blood, severe persistent pain, weight loss, fainting, chest pressure or rigid abdomen requires urgent biomedical evaluation.
