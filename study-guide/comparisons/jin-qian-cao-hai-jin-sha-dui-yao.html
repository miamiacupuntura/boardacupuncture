Appendix B pair · 48 of 51

Jīn Qián Cǎo + Hǎi Jīn Shā

金钱草 + 海金沙

One picture, one job

PictureGold-coin grass dislodges the stone; sea-gold sand flushes the grit.
Combined jobPromote urination, unblock painful Lin and help expel urinary or biliary stones.
CategoryUnblock Lin · Expel Stones

How the pair works

HerbRole within the pair
Jīn Qián CǎoDislodges and expels stones while clearing Damp-Heat.
Hǎi Jīn ShāFlushes fine gravel through the urine and relieves burning pain.

Exam focus and safety

CluesStone-type urinary pain, burning urination or gallstone presentation with Damp-Heat.
Signature formulaSān Jīn Tāng.
TrapThe pair may support stone passage but does not establish stone size, obstruction or infection severity.
PreparationWrap Hǎi Jīn Shā in a cloth bag during decoction because its fine spores cloud the liquid.
SafetyFever with flank pain, vomiting, jaundice, severe colic or inability to urinate may signal obstruction/infection and requires emergency care.

Complete formula, Duì Yào, or formula + pair?

Use the complete formula when the case reproduces a full pattern architecture with root, branches, and protection. Consider the pair for a narrow objective; use it as a modification when a base formula fits but lacks one function. The pair does not automatically replace the formula.
