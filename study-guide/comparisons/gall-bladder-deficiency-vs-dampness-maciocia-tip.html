Gall Bladder Deficiency vs. Dampness

Separate a deficient decisiveness/Spirit picture from a heavy obstructive pattern.

Fast differentiation clues

ClueGall Bladder DeficiencyGall Bladder Dampness
SharedBoth may include sighing, uneasiness and reduced initiative.
Fast distinctionDeficiency: timidity, indecision, easy fright and lack of initiative without a thick sticky coat.Dampness: hypochondriac fullness, heaviness, nausea and a sticky unilateral coat.

Clinical table

CompareGall Bladder DeficiencyGall Bladder Dampness
Core problemWeak courage/decision and unsettled Spirit.Pathogenic Dampness obstructs Gall Bladder Qi.
Formula associationWen Dan Tang or An Shen Ding Zhi Wan in the supplied table.San Ren Tang plus Yin Chen Hao.

Exam and safety

Why it mattersPhysical heaviness and sticky coating establish Dampness; mental signs alone do not.
Memory cueDeficiency hesitates; Dampness weighs and coats.
SafetyNew severe anxiety, depression, confusion or inability to function requires appropriate mental-health assessment.
