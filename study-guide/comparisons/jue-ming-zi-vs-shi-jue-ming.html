Herb comparison

Jue Ming Zi vs. Shi Jue Ming

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueJue Ming ZiShi Jue Ming
Shared clueBoth clear Liver Heat and brighten the eyes; they may be combined for eye disorders and headaches involving the eyes.
Fast distinctionBitter and cold; better for cooling Liver Fire and also moistens the Intestines.Salty, cold and heavy; anchors rising Liver Yang and nourishes Liver Yin at the Blood level.

Comparative answer key

CompareJue Ming ZiShi Jue Ming
Botanical nameCassiae Semen.Haliotidis Concha.
CategoryHerbs that Clear Heat and Drain Fire.Substances that Extinguish Wind and Stop Tremors.
Properties in the sourceBitter and cold.Salty, cold and heavy.
Liver emphasisCools Liver Fire.Sedates ascendant Liver Yang and enters the Liver Blood level to nourish Yin.
Eye actionBrightens the eyes by clearing Liver Fire.Brightens the eyes while anchoring rising Yang.
Additional actionMoistens the Intestines.Its heavy shell anchors and settles.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersThe similar names and shared eye indication can obscure the key mechanism: clearing Liver Fire versus heavily anchoring Liver Yang.
Memory cueJue Ming Zi clears Fire and moistens; Shi Jue Ming is a heavy shell that anchors Yang.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), p. 112. Source Exam Key CMID 8996, questions 1–2.
