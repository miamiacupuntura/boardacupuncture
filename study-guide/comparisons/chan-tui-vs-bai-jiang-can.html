Herb comparison

Cicadae Periostracum (chan tui) vs. Bombyx batryticatus (bai jiang can)

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueChan TuiBai Jiang Can
Shared cluesBoth eliminate Wind and release spasms and may be combined for headache, swollen throat and convulsions from Wind-Heat in the Lung and Liver channels.
Decisive clueExterior Wind-Heat with rash venting or superficial visual obstruction.Internal Wind with phlegm transformation and clumping dispersal.

Comparative answer key

CompareChan Tui (Cicadae Periostracum)Bai Jiang Can (Bombyx batryticatus)
CategoryCool, Acrid Herbs that Release the Exterior.Substances that Extinguish Wind and Stop Tremors.
Shared actionEliminate Wind and release spasms.
Distinctive actionVents rashes and reduces superficial visual obstructions.Transforms phlegm and disperses clumping.
Recognition patternExterior-level Wind-Heat signs with rash or visual obstruction.Internal-Wind signs accompanied by phlegm or clumping.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersThe herbs share Wind- and spasm-related indications, but belong to different categories and extend the shared action in different directions.
Common confusionRash and superficial visual obstruction belong to Chan Tui; phlegm and clumping belong to Bai Jiang Can.
How it may appearA question may reverse the order of the herbs while testing the identical category and action distinctions.
Source question formatsQuestions 1 and 2 are Matching and contain the same comparison in reverse order.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), p. 983. Source Exam Key CMID 8918.

Possible exam questions · Study prompts
