Spleen Qi Deficiency vs. Spleen–Lung Qi Deficiency

Recognize when digestive Qi weakness extends upward to breathing and voice.

Fast differentiation clues

ClueSpleen Qi DeficiencySpleen–Lung Qi Deficiency
SharedBoth cause fatigue, poor appetite, pale tongue and a weak pulse.
Fast distinctionSpleen-centered: loose stool, bloating, weak limbs and possible Dampness.Combined pattern: the same digestive signs plus breathlessness, weak voice, cough or spontaneous sweating.

Clinical table

CompareSpleen Qi DeficiencySpleen–Lung Qi Deficiency
Added organ clueDigestive transport only.Respiratory weakness and Defensive-Qi failure.
Formula associationSi Jun Zi Tang.Si Jun Zi Tang-based support plus Lung-Qi strategy by presentation.

Exam and safety

Why it mattersBreathlessness and weak voice—not fatigue alone—identify Lung involvement.
Memory cueBazo fails below; Bazo–Pulmón also fades above.
SafetyBreathlessness at rest, chest pain, blue lips or low oxygen needs urgent evaluation.
