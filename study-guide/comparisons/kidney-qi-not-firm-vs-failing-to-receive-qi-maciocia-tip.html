Kidney Qi Not Firm vs. Kidneys Failing to Receive Qi

Separate failure to secure the lower orifices from failure to grasp descending Lung Qi.

Fast differentiation clues

ClueKidney Qi Not FirmKidneys Failing to Receive Qi
SharedBoth arise from weak Kidney Qi and may include low-back weakness, clear urination and a deep weak pulse.
Fast distinctionContainment fails below: dribbling, frequency, incontinence, vaginal discharge or emissions without dreams.Grasping fails above: breathlessness on exertion, weak inhalation, wheezing and sweating.

Clinical table

CompareKidney Qi Not FirmKidneys Failing to Receive Qi
Failed functionSecure urine and Essence.Receive and grasp descending Lung Qi.
Decisive clueLeakage from lower orifices.Difficulty inhaling, worse with exertion.
Formula associationJin Suo Gu Jing Wan, Fu Tu Dan or modified You Gui Wan.Shen Gen San, Su Zi Jiang Qi Tang or modified You Gui Wan.

Exam and safety

Why it mattersBoth are Kidney-Qi failures, but leakage localizes the first and inspiratory weakness the second.
Memory cueNot Firm leaks below; Not Receiving cannot draw breath down.
SafetyNew incontinence, urinary retention, saddle numbness, breathlessness at rest, chest pain or blue lips requires urgent assessment.
