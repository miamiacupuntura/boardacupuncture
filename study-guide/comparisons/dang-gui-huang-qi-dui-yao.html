Appendix B pair · 23 of 51

Dāng Guī + Huáng Qí

当归 + 黄芪

One picture, one job

PictureFive parts Qi make one part Blood.
Combined jobUse strongly supplemented Qi to generate Blood and support tissue recovery after loss or depletion.
CategorySupplement Qi to Generate Blood

How the pair works

HerbRole within the pair
Huáng QíAt the large dose, powerfully drives Qi and production of new Blood.
Dāng GuīSupplies and nourishes the Blood material while gently moving it.

Exam focus and safety

CluesBlood deficiency after blood loss, low-grade deficiency fever, fatigue or slow-healing sores.
Signature formulaDāng Guī Bǔ Xuè Tāng — Huáng Qí 5 : Dāng Guī 1.
TrapThe unusual 5:1 ratio is the exam key; equal doses miss the formula’s Qi-generates-Blood logic.
PreparationPreserve the classical dose relationship unless a qualified practitioner has a specific reason to modify it.
SafetyScreen anticoagulants/antiplatelets, diabetes medicines and immunosuppressants. Active or unexplained bleeding requires medical evaluation first.
