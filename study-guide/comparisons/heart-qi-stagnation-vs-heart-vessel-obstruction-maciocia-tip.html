Heart Qi Stagnation vs. Heart Vessel Obstruction

Separate functional chest constraint from a complex obstructive pattern with pain, Phlegm, Cold or Blood stasis.

Fast differentiation clues

ClueHeart Qi StagnationHeart Vessel Obstruction
SharedBoth may cause palpitations, chest oppression, sighing, depression and shortness of breath.
Fast distinctionConstraint predominates: variable oppression, sighing, emotional trigger and slight throat-lump sensation.Material obstruction predominates: fixed or stabbing chest pain, inability to lie flat, blue-purple lips/nails, cold hands and a knotted/choppy pulse.

Clinical table

CompareHeart Qi StagnationHeart Vessel Obstruction
MechanismQi movement in the chest is constrained.Qi, Blood, Phlegm and/or Cold physically obstruct Heart vessels.
Tongue / pulseNormal to pale-purple; wiry or slightly overflowing.Purple or swollen with sticky coat; wiry, choppy, slippery or knotted.
Formula associationBan Xia Hou Po Tang when Qi-Phlegm throat/chest constraint fits.Gua Lou Xie Bai formulas or Xue Fu Zhu Yu Tang according to the obstructing factor.

Exam and safety

Why it mattersFixed pain, cyanosis and an irregular pulse signal more than simple Qi constraint.
Memory cueQi stagnation squeezes; vessel obstruction fixes, stabs and darkens.
SafetyAny new chest pressure or pain—especially with breathlessness, sweating, nausea or radiation to arm/jaw—requires emergency cardiac evaluation.
