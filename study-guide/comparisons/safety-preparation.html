Preparation & Decoction

Processing and timing can change direction, potency and toxicity.

Core rule

RuleConfirm identity and required processing before deciding how the substance enters the formula.

Processing changes the medicinal

Group / stepWhat to knowSafe decision
Aconite: Fù Zǐ / Chuān Wū / Cǎo WūPrepared product only; requires qualified prescribing and prolonged pre-decoction according to the authenticated product and applicable standard.Raw or improperly prepared aconite can cause fatal arrhythmia and neurologic toxicity.
Bàn Xià / Tiān Nán XīngUse the specified processed form; processing reduces acrid irritant toxicity.Raw material is not an interchangeable substitute.
Shēng vs. Gān vs. Pào JiāngFresh ginger releases Exterior/stops vomiting; dried warms Interior; blast-fried emphasizes warm hemostasis.The processing word is part of the prescription.
Shēng Dì vs. Shú DìRaw-dried Rehmannia cools Blood/nourishes Yin; prepared Rehmannia tonifies Blood/Essence.Do not collapse raw and prepared forms.
Charred herbsCharring often redirects an herb toward stopping bleeding, but the endpoint and product identity matter.Burnt or uncontrolled homemade material is not standardized Páo Zhì.
Honey-fried herbsHoney-frying may increase moistening or tonic emphasis, as with Zhì Gān Cǎo or honey-fried Sāng Yè.Account for sugars and the exact prepared form.

Decoction timing

Group / stepWhat to knowSafe decision
Pre-decoctDense minerals/shells and specified toxic roots may need to begin earlier.Timing is substance-specific; “pre-decoct everything hard” is not a complete rule.
Add lateAromatic herbs and volatile medicinals such as Gōu Téng or Qīng Hāo may be added near the end when specified.Long boiling can reduce volatile or heat-sensitive activity.
Dissolve separatelyGelatins such as Ē Jiāo or Lù Jiǎo Jiāo are melted into the strained hot decoction.Direct boiling can scorch or clump the product.
Powder/pill rather than decoctionSome substances such as Qīng Dài or Lù Róng are traditionally administered in powder/pill form.Route and dose must follow a qualified prescription.
Crush before decoctionSome seeds or fruits release constituents better when cracked or crushed.Crushing also increases extraction and can increase toxicity; verify instructions.
Wrap before decoctionFine hairs, powders or floating materials may require a cloth bag.Prevents throat irritation and difficult straining.

When to act immediately

EmergencyNever attempt home detoxification of aconite or other toxic medicinals. Numb mouth, spreading weakness, vomiting, faintness, palpitations or an irregular pulse requires emergency care/poison control.

Educational use: this library does not replace medical evaluation, poison control, local regulation or pharmacist consultation.
