Preparation & Decoction

Processing and timing can change direction, potency and toxicity.

Core ruleConfirm identity and required processing before deciding how the substance enters the formula.

How does processing change the herb?Preparation can change action, direction and toxicity.
Study questionWhat to knowSafe decision
Why can Fù Zǐ, Chuān Wū and Cǎo Wū be fatal without correct processing?Prepared product only; requires qualified prescribing and prolonged pre-decoction according to the authenticated product and applicable standard.Raw or improperly prepared aconite can cause fatal arrhythmia and neurologic toxicity.
What makes processed Bàn Xià and Tiān Nán Xīng safer?Use the specified processed form; processing reduces acrid irritant toxicity.Raw material is not an interchangeable substitute.
How can ginger release the Exterior, warm the Interior or stop bleeding?Fresh ginger releases Exterior/stops vomiting; dried warms Interior; blast-fried emphasizes warm hemostasis.The processing word is part of the prescription.
How does processing transform Shēng Dì into Shú Dì?Raw-dried Rehmannia cools Blood/nourishes Yin; prepared Rehmannia tonifies Blood/Essence.Do not collapse raw and prepared forms.
Why can charring redirect an herb toward stopping bleeding?Charring often redirects an herb toward stopping bleeding, but the endpoint and product identity matter.Burnt or uncontrolled homemade material is not standardized Páo Zhì.
What can honey-frying add—and what risk must be considered?Honey-frying may increase moistening or tonic emphasis, as with Zhì Gān Cǎo or honey-fried Sāng Yè.Account for sugars and the exact prepared form.
Why and how are herbs processed?Processing can redirect effects, reduce toxicity, change properties and improve preparation.
Study questionPrincipleExamples from the chart
How can processing strengthen, moderate or redirect an effect?Traditional processing may emphasize, moderate or redirect an action; it does not universally make every medicinal “stronger.”Wine-processed Dang Gui emphasizes moving Blood; vinegar-processed Yan Hu Suo emphasizes Qi/Blood movement and pain relief; honey-prepared Kuan Dong Hua emphasizes moistening the Lung and stopping cough.
How can processing reduce toxicity or irritation?Processing can reduce irritating or toxic constituents, but only when the authenticated method and finished product are correct.Ban Xia is used in a specified processed form; ginger processing may emphasize harmonizing the Stomach and stopping vomiting.
How can processing change temperature, direction or therapeutic emphasis?Preparation can materially change temperature, direction and clinical emphasis.Sheng Di Huang is cooling and nourishes Yin; prepared Shu Di Huang is richer, warmer and emphasizes Blood/Essence tonification.
How does processing improve handling, storage and extraction?Drying, cutting, crushing and standardized heating can improve stability and extraction.Processing instructions remain part of the medicinal identity and cannot be replaced by improvised home preparation.

What changes when an herb is cut, crushed or ground?Cut, crush, grind or remove a nonmedicinal part.Particle size changes extraction and therefore exposure.
When can washing or soaking help—and when can it remove active material?Wash, soak, moisten or levigate when specified.Excess washing can discard active material; follow the authenticated standard.
Why is dry-frying (Chao) not the same as simply burning an herb?Dry, brown or scorch to modify digestion, storage or clinical emphasis.Charred processing is controlled Pao Zhi, not simply burning an herb.
How does moist heat transform an herb?Steam, boil or otherwise prepare with moist heat.Shu Di Huang is a prepared Rehmannia product, not an interchangeable synonym for Sheng Di Huang.
Why process an herb with honey—and what exposure must be considered?Traditionally emphasizes tonifying, moistening or cough-relieving actions.Kuan Dong Hua and Zhi Gan Cao are common study examples; consider sugar exposure.
Why use vinegar instead of salt for Yan Hu Suo?Traditionally emphasizes Liver affinity, Qi/Blood movement and pain relief.Yan Hu Suo is classically vinegar-processed, not salt-processed.
What does wine processing emphasize?Traditionally emphasizes Blood movement, channel access or upward movement.Dang Gui is a standard examination example.
How can ginger juice reduce irritation and support the Stomach?Traditionally moderates cold/irritating qualities and supports the Stomach.Processed Ban Xia is the safety-relevant example; raw Ban Xia is not substituted.
Why does salt-water processing direct action downward?Traditionally directs action toward the Kidney and lower body.Salt processing belongs to liquid-excipient processing, not dry-frying.
Which two processing errors must not be memorized?Yan Hu Suo is classically vinegar-processed, not salt-processed. Salt belongs under liquid-excipient processing. Fu Ling coated with Zhu Sha is a historical reference only: Zhu Sha contains mercury and is excluded from current safe study recommendations.
When is each herb added?Timing can change extraction, safety and the intended action.
Study questionWhat to knowSafe decision
Why are some substances pre-decocted?Dense minerals/shells and specified toxic roots may need to begin earlier.Timing is substance-specific; “pre-decoct everything hard” is not a complete rule.
Which herbs lose activity if they are boiled too long?Aromatic herbs and volatile medicinals such as Gōu Téng or Qīng Hāo may be added near the end when specified.Long boiling can reduce volatile or heat-sensitive activity.
Why are gelatins dissolved after straining?Gelatins such as Ē Jiāo or Lù Jiǎo Jiāo are melted into the strained hot decoction.Direct boiling can scorch or clump the product.
Why are some medicinals taken as powder or pill instead of decoction?Some substances such as Qīng Dài or Lù Róng are traditionally administered in powder/pill form.Route and dose must follow a qualified prescription.
When does crushing improve extraction—and increase risk?Some seeds or fruits release constituents better when cracked or crushed.Crushing also increases extraction and can increase toxicity; verify instructions.
Which materials should be wrapped before decoction?Fine hairs, powders or floating materials may require a cloth bag.Prevents throat irritation and difficult straining.
When does a reaction require immediate action?Toxicity symptoms require emergency care or poison control.
Which symptoms require emergency care after a toxic medicinal?Never attempt home detoxification of aconite or other toxic medicinals. Numb mouth, spreading weakness, vomiting, faintness, palpitations or an irregular pulse requires emergency care/poison control.
