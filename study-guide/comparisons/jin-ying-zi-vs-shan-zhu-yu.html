Herb comparison

Rosae laevigatae Fructus (jin ying zi) vs. Corni Fructus (shan zhu yu)

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueJin Ying ZiShan Zhu Yu
Shared clueBoth are sour and astringent and stabilize the lower burner for deficiency leakage.
Fast distinctionStabilizes and binds but does not tonify; also binds chronic diarrhea.Stabilizes while strongly tonifying Essence and Yang Qi; also stops sweating and supports collapse.

Comparative answer key

CompareJin Ying ZiShan Zhu Yu
Medicinal nameRosae laevigatae Fructus.Corni Fructus.
Shared categoryHerbs that Stabilize and Bind.
Shared properties and actionSour and astringent; conserve, hold and stabilize the lower burner.
Shared leakage indicationsSpermatorrhea, urinary incontinence and thin watery vaginal discharge from deficiency.
Tonifying actionOnly stabilizes; it does not tonify.Strongly tonifies Essence and Yang Qi while retaining Essence.
Distinctive actionsBinds the Intestines and stops chronic diarrhea.Stops sweating, supports collapse and gently stabilizes menstrual bleeding.
Decisive exam clueStabilization without tonification; chronic diarrhea.Stabilization plus tonification; sweating, collapse or menstrual bleeding.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersThe two herbs share the same category and lower-burner leakage indications. Jin Ying Zi binds without tonifying and can address chronic diarrhea; Shan Zhu Yu both binds and tonifies.
Memory cueJin Ying Zi binds only; Shan Zhu Yu binds and builds.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), p. 886. Source Exam Keys CMID 8993 and CMID 9056, questions 1–2.
