Batch 5 · Formula 7 of 10

Run Chang Wan

Blood deficiency causing dry, desiccated intestines.

High-yield summary

CategoryNourish Blood · Moisten the Intestines
PatternBlood deficiency causing dry, desiccated intestines.
ActionNourishes Blood, enriches fluids, moistens the intestines and moves stool.
Decisive clueDry constipation after illness, childbirth or blood loss with pallor, dizziness and dry skin.

Ingredients

Dang Gui, Sheng Di Huang, Tao Ren, Huo Ma Ren and Zhi Ke.

Commercial preparations and lineages may vary; always verify the source being used.

Differentiation and memory

Do not confuseMa Zi Ren Wan treats Dryness with Heat and more excess; Run Chang Wan treats deficient Blood and fluids.
Memory cueNourish with Dang Gui and Sheng Di; moisten with two seeds; move with Zhi Ke.

Safety

New severe constipation, vomiting, abdominal distention, bleeding or inability to pass gas requires urgent evaluation.
