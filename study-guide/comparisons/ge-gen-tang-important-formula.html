Ge Gen Tang

Excess Wind-Cold exterior pattern with stiffness of the upper back and neck.

High-yield summary

CategoryRelease Wind-Cold Exterior · Relax the Neck
PatternExcess Wind-Cold exterior pattern with stiffness of the upper back and neck.
ActionReleases the exterior, induces sweating, relaxes the muscle layer and raises clear Yang.
Decisive clueFever and chills without sweating plus marked stiff neck and upper back.

Ingredients

Ge Gen, Ma Huang, Gui Zhi, Sheng Jiang, Zhi Gan Cao, Bai Shao and Da Zao.

Commercial preparations and lineages may vary; always verify the source being used.

Differentiation and memory

Do not confuseGui Zhi Tang treats a deficient exterior with spontaneous sweating; Ge Gen Tang is an excess presentation without sweating and with neck stiffness.
Memory cueGe Gen raises fluids into the tight neck while Ma Huang opens the closed exterior.

Safety

Fever with severe neck stiffness, confusion, rash or intense headache may indicate meningitis and requires emergency care. Ma Huang requires caution.
