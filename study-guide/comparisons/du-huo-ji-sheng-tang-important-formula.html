Du Huo Ji Sheng Tang

Chronic Wind-Cold-Damp painful obstruction with Liver/Kidney and Qi/Blood deficiency.

High-yield summary

CategoryDispel Wind-Damp · Tonify Liver and Kidney
PatternChronic Wind-Cold-Damp painful obstruction with Liver/Kidney and Qi/Blood deficiency.
ActionDispels Wind-Damp, relieves pain, benefits sinews and bones, and tonifies underlying deficiency.
Decisive clueChronic low-back and leg pain, heaviness or numbness in an older or deficient patient, worse with cold and damp.

Ingredients

Du Huo, Sang Ji Sheng, Du Zhong, Niu Xi, Xi Xin, Qin Jiao, Fu Ling, Rou Gui, Fang Feng, Chuan Xiong, Ren Shen, Gan Cao, Dang Gui, Bai Shao and Shu Di Huang.

Commercial preparations and lineages may vary; always verify the source being used.

Differentiation and memory

Do not confuseJuan Bi Tang treats more acute Wind-Cold-Damp Bi, especially in the upper body; Du Huo Ji Sheng Tang is chronic and deficiency-based.
Memory cueDu Huo clears lower-body Bi; Ji Sheng supports the deficient Liver–Kidney root.

Safety

New weakness, saddle numbness, loss of bladder/bowel control, fever or traumatic back pain requires urgent evaluation.
