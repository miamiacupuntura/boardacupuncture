Formula 26 of 63

Zhen Wu Tang

High caution

Spleen and Kidney Yang deficiency with internal water retention.

Diagnostic navigation

CategoryExpel Dampness
Clinical routeFluids & urination · Digestion & abdomen
PatternSpleen and Kidney Yang deficiency with internal water retention.
Decisive clueEdema, scant urine, heavy painful limbs, abdominal pain/diarrhea and dizziness with a pale swollen wet tongue.

Essential architecture

King herb(s)Fu Zi
ActionWarms Yang, promotes urination, transforms water and stabilizes the interior.
IngredientsFu Ling, Bai Shao, Sheng Jiang, Bai Zhu and Zhi Fu Zi.

Safety and provenance

Fu Zi requires correct processing. Sudden edema, low urine, breathlessness, chest pain, confusion or fainting requires urgent care.
