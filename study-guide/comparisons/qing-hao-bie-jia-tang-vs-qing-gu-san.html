Qing Hao Bie Jia Tang vs. Qing Gu San

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueQing Hao Bie Jia TangQing Gu San
Shared clueBoth nourish Yin and clear deficiency Heat with low-grade fever, emaciation or night Heat.
Fast distinctionLate-stage febrile disease: Heat smolders at night and recedes without sweating.Chronic Liver-Kidney Yin deficiency: afternoon fever and steaming-bone sensation.

CompareQing Hao Bie Jia TangQing Gu San

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersBoth say Yin deficiency and Empty Heat. The disease history and timing of fever are the decisive clues.
Memory cueQing Hao opens the night; Qing Gu cools the bones in the afternoon.
SafetyPersistent fever, drenching sweats, weight loss or bone pain requires medical investigation for infection, malignancy or inflammatory disease.
