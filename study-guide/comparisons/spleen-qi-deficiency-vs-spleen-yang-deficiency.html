Pattern comparison

Spleen Qi Deficiency vs. Spleen Yang Deficiency

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueSpleen Qi DeficiencySpleen Yang Deficiency
Shared clueBoth cause fatigue, poor appetite, loose stools, abdominal weakness, pale tongue and a weak pulse.
Fast distinctionWeak digestion without strong Cold: fatigue and loose stool predominate.Qi deficiency plus Cold: abdominal pain better with warmth/pressure, cold limbs and edema.

Comparative answer key

CompareSpleen Qi DeficiencySpleen Yang Deficiency
ProgressionEarlier or milder functional deficiency.Deeper deficiency with loss of warming function.
Cold signsAbsent or mild.Pronounced.
Tongue / pulsePale with teeth marks; weak.Pale, swollen and wet; deep-slow-weak.
Formula examplesSi Jun Zi Tang; Shen Ling Bai Zhu San.Li Zhong Wan; Zhen Wu Tang if water accumulates.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersSpleen-Yang deficiency includes Spleen-Qi deficiency plus Cold and often water retention.
Memory cueQi is weak; Yang is weak and cold.
SafetyPersistent diarrhea, dehydration, weight loss, bleeding or sudden edema needs medical assessment.
