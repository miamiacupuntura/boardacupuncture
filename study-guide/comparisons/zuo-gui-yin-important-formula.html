Zuo Gui Yin

Moderate Kidney Yin and Essence deficiency.

High-yield summary

CategoryNourish Kidney Yin and Essence
PatternModerate Kidney Yin and Essence deficiency.
ActionNourishes Kidney Yin and Essence with a simpler, lighter decoction.
Decisive clueDizziness, tinnitus, dry mouth, weak low back/knees and night sweating without the profound depletion calling for Zuo Gui Wan.

Ingredients

Shu Di Huang, Shan Zhu Yu, Shan Yao, Gou Qi Zi, Fu Ling and Zhi Gan Cao.

Commercial preparations and lineages may vary; always verify the source being used.

Differentiation and memory

Do not confuseZuo Gui Wan adds animal substances and stronger Essence builders for severe deficiency; Zuo Gui Yin is the moderate six-herb option.
Memory cueRestore the Left gently: four nourish, Fu Ling balances and licorice harmonizes.

Safety

Persistent night sweats, weight loss, new hearing/vision changes or severe weakness requires medical evaluation.
