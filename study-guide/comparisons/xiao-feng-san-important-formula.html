Xiao Feng San

Wind-Damp-Heat lodged in the skin with underlying Blood dryness.

High-yield summary

CategoryDispel Wind · Clear Damp-Heat from Skin
PatternWind-Damp-Heat lodged in the skin with underlying Blood dryness.
ActionDispels Wind, clears Heat, drains Dampness, nourishes Blood and stops itching.
Decisive clueIntensely itchy, red, weeping or spreading rash that changes location or worsens with heat.

Ingredients

Jing Jie, Fang Feng, Niu Bang Zi, Chan Tui, Cang Zhu, Ku Shen, Mu Tong, Shi Gao, Zhi Mu, Sheng Di Huang, Dang Gui, Hei Zhi Ma and Gan Cao.

Commercial preparations and lineages may vary; always verify the source being used.

Differentiation and memory

Do not confuseDang Gui Yin Zi treats chronic dry itching from Blood deficiency with less Damp-Heat; Xiao Feng San treats active red, weeping Wind-Damp-Heat.
Memory cueDispel moving Wind, dry weeping Dampness, clear red Heat and nourish dry Blood.

Safety

Facial swelling, breathing difficulty, blistering, mucosal lesions, fever or rapidly spreading rash requires urgent medical care.
