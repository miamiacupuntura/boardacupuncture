Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersA purple tongue or fixed pain only establishes stasis. The site and whether Heat, Qi stagnation or Qi deficiency drives it identify the prescription.
Memory cueTao He = lower Heat; Xue Fu = chest; Shi Xiao = pain; Bu Yang = weak channels.
SafetySudden weakness, facial droop or speech difficulty is a stroke emergency. Chest pain, severe abdominal pain or active bleeding also requires urgent evaluation.
