Pathology or symptom comparison

Palpitations Pattern Differentiation

Diagnostic orientation

Core ideaEmotional fright, pallor, empty Heat, Cold, fluid signs and fixed chest pain separate the principal branches.
Exam focusSpirit, temperature, fluids and chest obstruction

Comparative differentiation

Pattern or presentationDecisive cluesRepresentative formula/herbStrategy
Heart-Gallbladder Qi deficiencyEasily frightened, timid, insomnia and profuse dreamsAn Shen Ding Zhi WanSupplement Qi, settle fright and calm Spirit.
Heart Blood deficiencyPalpitations with dizziness, pallor, fatigue and weak-thready pulseGui Pi TangSupplement Qi-Blood and nourish Heart.
Yin deficiency with HeatPalpitations, insomnia, five-center Heat, tinnitus and red peeled tongueTian Wang Bu Xin DanNourish Yin and descend Fire.
Heart Yang deficiencyPalpitations, chest oppression, short breath, cold limbs and pale tongueGui Zhi Gan Cao Long Gu Mu Li TangWarm Heart Yang and calm Spirit.
Phlegm-fluid oppressing HeartPalpitations with dizziness, mucus, edema, scant urine and greasy coatLing Gui Zhu Gan TangWarm Yang and transform Phlegm-fluid.
Blood stasis obstructing HeartChest pain/oppression, purple lips or nails and intermittent pulseTao Ren Hong Hua JianInvigorate Blood and open connections.

Study rule

How to distinguishFright points to Heart-Gallbladder; pallor to Blood; empty Heat to Yin; Cold to Yang; edema/mucus to fluid; fixed chest pain and purple signs to stasis.
Memory cueFright—Qi; pale—Blood; hot—Yin; cold—Yang; wet—Phlegm; purple pain—stasis.
SafetyPalpitations with chest pain, fainting, severe shortness of breath, sustained rapid/irregular pulse or neurologic symptoms requires urgent cardiac evaluation.
