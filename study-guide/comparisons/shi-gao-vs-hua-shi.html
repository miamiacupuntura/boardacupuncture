Substance comparison

Shi Gao vs. Hua Shi

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueShi GaoHua Shi
Shared internal clueBoth clear Heat and alleviate thirst.
Fast internal distinctionYang-Brightness Heat has dried fluids.Summerheat with Dampness and dark, difficult urine.
Shared external cluePrepared Shi Gao and Hua Shi may be applied topically to dispel Dampness and inhibit sores.
Fast external distinctionChronic sores that have not healed.Eczema, toxic rash or weeping lesions with severe itching.

Comparative answer key

CompareShi GaoHua Shi
Medicinal nameGypsum fibrosum; prepared form: Gypsum fibrosum preparatum (duan shi gao).Talcum.
CategoryHerbs that Clear Heat and Drain Fire.Herbs that Drain Dampness.
How thirst is relievedClears intense Yang-Brightness Heat so depleted fluids can recover.Harmonizes Spleen and Stomach while leaching Dampness.
Internal indicationPathogenic Heat at the Yang-Brightness stage with irritability, thirst and dried fluids.Summerheat-Dampness with thirst and dark urine that passes with difficulty.
Incorrect pattern clueThe source associates it with Dry Heat consuming fluids.Inappropriate for thirst caused by Dry Heat.
Topical formPrepared or calcined Duan Shi Gao.Hua Shi.
Topical indicationChronic sores that have failed to heal.Eczema, toxic rashes and weeping skin conditions with severe itching.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersThe symptom “thirst” does not decide the answer by itself. Dry intense Heat points to Shi Gao; Damp Summerheat with urinary difficulty points to Hua Shi. Topically, chronic nonhealing sores differ from weeping itchy lesions.
Memory cueShi Gao clears dry blazing Heat; Hua Shi drains wet Summerheat.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), p. 282. Source Exam Key CMID 8989, questions 1–7.
