Seminal Emission Pattern Differentiation

Diagnostic orientation

Core ideaHeat/Cold, relation to overwork and urinary burning distinguish failure to secure Essence.
Exam focusHeat versus Cold, fatigue and urinary signs

Study rule

How to distinguishEmpty Heat points to Yin; cold spontaneous leakage to Yang; overwork/digestion to Qi; burning cloudy urine to Damp-Heat.
Memory cueHot-dry—Yin; cold-leaking—Yang; tired-after-work—Qi; burning-cloudy—Damp-Heat.
SafetyPain, blood, fever, genital discharge, urinary retention or new persistent symptoms requires medical evaluation for infection or urologic disease.
