Bladder Damp-Heat vs. Stone Lin

Recognize when a painful urinary pattern includes colicky pain or evidence of a stone.

Fast differentiation clues

ClueBladder Damp-HeatStone Lin
SharedBoth may cause burning, dark difficult urination, urgency and blood in urine.
Fast distinctionDamp-Heat: burning/urgency with sticky yellow coat and possible infection signs.Stone Lin: severe colicky flank-to-groin pain, gravel/stone passage or imaging evidence.

Clinical table

CompareBladder Damp-HeatStone Lin
Diagnostic priorityUrinalysis/culture when infection is possible.Imaging and medical assessment for obstruction/stone.
Formula associationBa Zheng San.Jin Qian Cao-centered stone-Lin strategies in the supplied herb material.

Exam and safety

Why it mattersColicky radiation and objective stone evidence distinguish Stone Lin from a general Damp-Heat pattern.
Memory cueDamp-Heat burns; Stone Lin cuts and travels.
SafetySevere flank pain, fever, vomiting, one kidney, pregnancy, reduced urine or blood requires urgent evaluation.
