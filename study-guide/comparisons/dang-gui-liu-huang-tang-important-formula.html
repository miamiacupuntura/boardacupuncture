Dang Gui Liu Huang Tang

Yin deficiency with blazing Empty Fire causing night sweating.

High-yield summary

CategoryNourish Yin · Clear Fire · Stop Sweating
PatternYin deficiency with blazing Empty Fire causing night sweating.
ActionNourishes Yin and Blood, clears deficiency Fire, tonifies Qi and secures the exterior.
Decisive clueProfuse night sweats with feverishness, irritability, dry mouth and a red tongue.

Ingredients

Dang Gui, Sheng Di Huang, Shu Di Huang, Huang Lian, Huang Qin, Huang Bai and Huang Qi.

Commercial preparations and lineages may vary; always verify the source being used.

Differentiation and memory

Do not confuseMu Li San treats spontaneous or night sweating from Qi deficiency with a weaker Heat picture; Dang Gui Liu Huang Tang requires strong Yin-deficiency Fire.
Memory cueThree yellow Huangs clear Fire; the two Di Huangs and Dang Gui restore Yin and Blood.

Safety

Drenching night sweats with fever, cough, swollen nodes or weight loss requires prompt medical investigation.
