青蒿 + 鳖甲

One picture, one job

PictureThe turtle digs in; the wormwood leads Heat out.
Combined jobEnrich Yin and vent Heat lurking deep in the Yin level after warm disease.
CategoryVent Heat Lurking in Yin

How the pair works

HerbRole within the pair
Qīng HāoLightly vents hidden Heat outward without damaging Yin.
Biē JiǎEnters deeply, enriches Yin and searches out lurking Heat.

Exam focus and safety

CluesFever at night, cool by morning and no sweating as the fever recedes.
Signature formulaQīng Hāo Biē Jiǎ Tāng.
TrapThis is lurking Heat after warm disease, not ordinary Yin-deficiency night sweats or full Qi-level Heat.
PreparationCrush and decoct Biē Jiǎ first; add Qīng Hāo late and do not boil it for long.
SafetyBiē Jiǎ requires pregnancy caution and verified legal/ethical sourcing. Persistent or recurrent fever requires medical diagnosis.
