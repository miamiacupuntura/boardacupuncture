Pathology or symptom comparison

Erysipelas Pattern Differentiation

Diagnostic orientation

Core ideaThe source calls this Cinnabar Toxin (Dan Du); biomedically, erysipelas is a bacterial skin infection requiring antibiotics.
Exam focusDistribution of a rapidly spreading hot, sharply demarcated rash

Comparative differentiation

Pattern or presentationDecisive cluesRepresentative formula/herbStrategy
Wind-Heat invasionRapid sharply demarcated hot burning red patch on head/face with chills, fever and headachePu Ji Xiao Du YinHistorical strategy: dispel Wind, clear Heat and resolve toxin.
Damp-Heat accumulationLower-limb rash with fever, thirst, chest stuffiness, poor appetite, constipation, dark urine and yellow sticky coatBi Xie Sheng Shi Tang + Wu Shen TangHistorical strategy: clear Heat and drain Dampness.
Liver FireRash over chest/hypochondria with headache, red eyes, bitter taste, irritability and dark urineLong Dan Xie Gan TangHistorical strategy: drain Liver Fire and cool Blood.

Study rule

How to distinguishHead/face plus exterior signs maps to Wind-Heat; lower limb plus sticky Damp signs to Damp-Heat; chest/flank plus red eyes and irritability to Liver Fire.
Memory cueFace—Wind-Heat; legs—Damp-Heat; flanks + anger—Liver Fire.
SafetySuspected erysipelas/cellulitis needs same-day medical assessment and antibiotics. Rapid spread, face/eye involvement, severe pain, fever, confusion, blisters or immunosuppression requires urgent care.
