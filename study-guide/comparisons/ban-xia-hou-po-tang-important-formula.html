Ban Xia Hou Po Tang

Liver Qi and Phlegm stagnation lodging in the throat—Plum-Pit Qi.

High-yield summary

CategoryMove Qi · Transform Phlegm
PatternLiver Qi and Phlegm stagnation lodging in the throat—Plum-Pit Qi.
ActionMoves Qi, disperses constraint, transforms Phlegm and redirects rebellious Qi.
Decisive clueA persistent lump-in-throat sensation that cannot be swallowed or coughed out, often varying with emotion.

Ingredients

Ban Xia, Hou Po, Fu Ling, Sheng Jiang and Zi Su Ye.

Commercial preparations and lineages may vary; always verify the source being used.

Differentiation and memory

Do not confuseCang Er Zi San treats nasal obstruction; this formula treats throat constraint from Qi–Phlegm interaction.
Memory cueBan Xia transforms the material; Hou Po opens the constrained space.

Safety

True difficulty swallowing, choking, weight loss, neck mass or progressive hoarseness requires medical examination.
