Large Intestine Collapse vs. Spleen Qi Sinking

Distinguish the local prolapse manifestation from its broader middle-Qi root.

Fast differentiation clues

ClueLarge Intestine CollapseSpleen Qi Sinking
SharedBoth cause chronic diarrhea, bearing down, rectal prolapse and a weak submerged pulse.
Fast distinctionLarge Intestine label emphasizes the local bowel manifestation.Spleen sinking emphasizes the root: poor appetite, fatigue and possible prolapse of other organs.

Exam and safety

Why it mattersThese are often root and branch rather than mutually exclusive diagnoses.
