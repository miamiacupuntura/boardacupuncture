Pathology or symptom comparison

Round Corneal Nebula Pattern Comparison

Diagnostic orientation

Core ideaThe historical label describes unclear vision; modern examination must identify whether the problem is corneal opacity, cataract, retina, optic nerve or refraction.
Exam focusBlurred vision with deficiency, Heat or Dampness clues

Comparative differentiation

Pattern or presentationDecisive cluesRepresentative formula/herbStrategy
Liver-Kidney deficiencyBlurred vision with dizziness, tinnitus, weak back/knees and red scant-coated tongueQi Ju Di Huang WanSupport Liver-Kidney and nourish Blood-Essence.
Spleen-Qi deficiencyBlurred vision with fatigue, sallow complexion, poor appetite, loose stool and pale tongueBu Zhong Yi Qi TangSupport Spleen Qi.
Upper-burner Liver HeatBlurred vision with headache, eye discomfort, excess mucus/tears, bitter mouth and dry throatShi Jue Ming SanClear Liver Heat.
Yin depletion with Damp-HeatDry blurred eyes with irritability, halitosis, difficult stool and red tongue with greasy yellow coatGan Lu YinNourish Yin, clear Heat and transform Dampness.

Study rule

How to distinguishBack/tinnitus indicates Liver-Kidney; digestion/fatigue Spleen; headache/bitter mouth Liver Heat; dryness plus greasy Heat Yin depletion with Damp-Heat.
Memory cueBack/ears—Liver-Kidney; digestion—Spleen; bitter/headache—Liver Heat; dry + greasy—Yin/Damp-Heat.
SafetyBlurred vision or visible corneal opacity requires eye examination. Pain, redness, photophobia, trauma, contact-lens use, rapid worsening or any sudden loss requires urgent ophthalmic care.
SourceInternal Medicine ophthalmology section: Round Corneal Nebula (Yuan Yi Nei Zhang).
