Formula comparison

Gu Jing Wan vs. Wen Jing Tang

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueGu Jing WanWen Jing Tang
Shared clueBoth regulate uterine bleeding in deficiency patterns and may include prolonged or irregular menstruation.
Fast distinctionDeficiency Heat: dark-red bleeding, heat sensations, dry mouth and a red tongue.Deficiency Cold with stasis: pale/dark bleeding, cold lower abdomen, pain and a pale or dusky tongue.

Comparative answer key

CompareGu Jing WanWen Jing Tang
Thermal patternKidney Yin deficiency with Heat.Uterine Cold with Blood deficiency and stasis.
Primary actionNourishes Yin, clears Heat and stabilizes bleeding.Warms channels, nourishes/moves Blood and regulates menses.
Decisive clueHeat signs with bleeding.Cold pain and irregular flow with deficiency.
Exam shortcutSecure hot bleeding.Warm cold, stagnant bleeding.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersBoth involve chronic uterine bleeding, so temperature is decisive: Heat and Yin deficiency versus Cold with Blood deficiency/stasis.
Memory cueGu Jing cools and secures; Wen Jing warms and moves.
SafetyHeavy bleeding, pregnancy bleeding, dizziness, fainting or postmenopausal bleeding requires urgent gynecologic evaluation.
SourceSource bank: FWB 11.1 and FWB 12.1 comparisons; corroborated with formula references.
