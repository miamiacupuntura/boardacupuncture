Herb comparison

Chu Shi Zi vs. Gou Qi Zi

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueChu Shi ZiGou Qi Zi
Shared clueBoth are sweet, moistening herbs that enrich Liver-Kidney Yin and Blood for weak lower back and legs, dizziness and visual disturbance.
Fast distinctionCold; clears Liver Heat affecting the eyes and promotes urination.Neutral; tonifies Essence and moistens the Lungs to treat cough.

Comparative answer key

CompareChu Shi ZiGou Qi Zi
Botanical nameBroussonetiae Fructus.Lycii Fructus.
CategoryHerbs that Tonify the Yin.Herbs that Tonify the Blood.
NatureCold.Neutral.
Eye-related emphasisClears Liver-channel Heat affecting the eyes.Nourishes Liver Blood and Yin for visual disturbance.
Distinctive extensionPromotes urination.Tonifies Essence and moistens the Lungs for cough.
Shared indicationsWeak or aching lower back and legs, dizziness, lightheadedness and visual disturbance.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersBoth treat visual and Liver-Kidney deficiency signs, but Heat with urination favors Chu Shi Zi, while Essence or Lung-Dryness cough favors Gou Qi Zi.
Memory cueChu Shi Zi clears and drains; Gou Qi Zi nourishes and moistens.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), p. 849. Source Exam Key CMID 8957, questions 4–5 and 7.
