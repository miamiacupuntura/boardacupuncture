Herb comparison

Pinelliae Rhizoma preparatum (zhi ban xia) vs. Arisaematis Rhizoma preparatum (zhi tian nan xing)

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueZhi Ban XiaZhi Tian Nan Xing
Shared clueBoth dry Dampness and transform Phlegm, including Phlegm-Dampness involving the Spleen and Stomach.
Fast distinctionSpleen-Stomach Phlegm-Dampness, nausea and focal distention.Liver-channel Wind-Phlegm, spasms and obstruction in the channels and collaterals.

Comparative answer key

CompareZhi Ban XiaZhi Tian Nan Xing
Medicinal namePinelliae Rhizoma preparatum.Arisaematis Rhizoma preparatum.
CategoryWarm Herbs that Transform Cold-Phlegm.
Primary Phlegm patternPhlegm-Dampness, especially in the Spleen and Stomach.Wind-Phlegm, especially in the Liver channel, channels and collaterals.
Distinctive actionsAlleviates nausea and reduces focal distention.Stops spasms, disperses Blood and reduces swelling.
Acrid and drying qualityAcrid but comparatively conserving.More dispersing and acutely drying, without the same conserving quality.
Combination priorityPrimary for Phlegm-Dampness; assistant for Wind-Phlegm.Assistant for Phlegm-Dampness; primary for Wind-Phlegm.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersBoth transform Damp-Phlegm, so the location and associated symptoms identify the answer: digestive Phlegm-Dampness favors Zhi Ban Xia; Wind-Phlegm in the channels favors Zhi Tian Nan Xing.
Memory cueBan Xia centers on the Stomach; Tian Nan Xing disperses Wind-Phlegm through the channels.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), p. 419. Source Exam Key CMID 9080, questions 1–3.
