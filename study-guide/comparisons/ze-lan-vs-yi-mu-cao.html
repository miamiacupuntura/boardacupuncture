Herb comparison

Lycopi Herba (ze lan) vs. Leonuri Herba (yi mu cao)

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueZe LanYi Mu Cao
Shared cluesBoth invigorate Blood, facilitate urination and may be used for toxic swellings, although their mechanisms differ.
Fast distinctionGentle, warm and aromatic; harmoniously moves Blood in either hot or cold stasis patterns.Stronger and cooling; better when Blood Heat combines with Blood stasis.

Comparative answer key

CompareZe LanYi Mu Cao
Medicinal nameLycopi Herba.Leonuri Herba.
Shared categoryHerbs that Regulate the Blood · Herbs that Invigorate the Blood.
Shared actionsInvigorate Blood and facilitate urination; both may be used for toxic swellings.
Temperature and strengthGentle, warm and aromatic; suitable for either hot or cold Blood-stasis disorders.Cooling and stronger at both invigorating Blood and facilitating urination.
Swelling mechanismReduces swelling through gentle, warm, aromatic promotion of Blood movement; does not resolve toxicity directly.Better suited to Blood Heat combined with Blood stasis and has a stronger effect on swelling.
Modern-research association in the exam materialAntigonadotropic and antioxidant effects, atherosclerosis prevention, mild hyperthyroidism and an immunoregulatory polysaccharide are listed.Use in polycystic ovary syndrome (PCOS) is listed.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersBoth move Blood and fluids. A gentle herb usable across hot or cold stasis patterns favors Ze Lan; stronger cooling activity for Blood Heat with stasis favors Yi Mu Cao.
Memory cueZe Lan moves gently and harmoniously; Yi Mu Cao cools and moves more strongly.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), p. 615. Source Exam Key CMID 9108, questions 4–5.
