Formula family

Ma Huang Tang Family

Root formula and architecture

RootMa Huang Tang establishes the family logic: strongly open a closed Wind-Cold Exterior, disseminate Lung Qi and relieve wheezing.
ArchitectureCore axis: Ma Huang opens and disseminates; Xing Ren descends Lung Qi. Gui Zhi strengthens Exterior warming, while Shi Gao redirects the family toward Heat.

Study rule

How to chooseFirst ask whether the Exterior is closed. Then decide whether the branch is pure Cold, internal Heat or thin-fluid retention.
Memory cueBase closes sweat; Da Qing adds Heat; Xiao Qing adds water; Ma Xing Shi Gan becomes Lung Heat.
SafetyEvery member contains Ma Huang and requires cardiovascular, stimulant and medication screening. Xiao Qing Long Tang also uses processed Ban Xia and Xi Xin.
