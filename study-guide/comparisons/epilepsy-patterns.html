Pathology or symptom comparison

Epilepsy Pattern Differentiation

Diagnostic orientation

Core ideaSeizures require neurologic diagnosis; the TCM comparison organizes accompanying signs into excess Phlegm/Fire or chronic deficiency branches.
Exam focusSeizure presentation plus Phlegm, Heat or chronic deficiency signs

Comparative differentiation

Pattern or presentationDecisive cluesRepresentative formula/herbStrategy
Wind-Phlegm obstructionDizziness/chest oppression before sudden collapse, convulsions, foaming and white sticky coatDing Xian WanExpel Phlegm, extinguish Wind and open orifices.
Internal Phlegm-FireSeizure with irritability, insomnia, yellow difficult phlegm, bitter mouth, constipation and red greasy tongueLong Dan Xie Gan Tang + Di Tan TangDrain Fire, transform Phlegm and open orifices.
Liver-Kidney Yin deficiencyChronic epilepsy with vertigo, insomnia, forgetfulness, weak back/knees and red scant-coated tongueZuo Gui WanNourish Liver-Kidney Yin and subdue Yang.
Spleen-Stomach Qi deficiencyChronic epilepsy with fatigue, poor appetite, loose stools, nausea and pale tongueLiu Jun Zi TangStrengthen Spleen, boost Qi and transform turbidity.

Study rule

How to distinguishWhite-sticky collapse marks Wind-Phlegm; red-yellow-irritable marks Phlegm-Fire; weak back with empty Heat marks Yin; fatigue/digestion with pallor marks Spleen Qi.
Memory cueWhite-sticky—Wind-Phlegm; red-greasy—Fire; red-thin + back—Yin; pale-tired + digestion—Qi.
SafetyDo not replace antiseizure medication. A seizure lasting 5 minutes, repeated seizures without recovery, first seizure, injury, pregnancy or breathing difficulty is an emergency.
