Herb comparison

Hu Huang Lian vs. Huang Lian

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueHu Huang LianHuang Lian
Shared clueBoth are described as very bitter, very cold and “pure Yin”; both cool Heat and Blood, drain Fire and dry Dampness.
Fast distinctionMore intensely bitter-cold, heavy, black and rapidly downward-directing; excels at lower-burner Damp-Heat.The standard Coptis herb for clearing Heat and drying Dampness.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersThe similar names and shared bitter-cold actions make category and direction decisive: Hu Huang Lian belongs to deficiency-Heat herbs and moves strongly downward toward the lower burner.
Memory cueHu Huang Lian is heavier, colder and lower; Huang Lian clears Damp-Heat more generally.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), p. 229. Source Exam Key CMID 8980, questions 5–6.
