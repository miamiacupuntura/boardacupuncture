Herb comparison

Panacis quinquefolii Radix (xi yang shen) vs. Pseudostellariae Radix (tai zi shen) vs. Ginseng Radix (ren shen) vs. Codonopsis Radix (dang shen)

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueXi Yang ShenTai Zi ShenRen ShenDang Shen
Shared focusAll support Qi and the production or preservation of fluids, but their strength, temperature and organ emphasis differ.
Fast distinctionCool; Qi and Yin deficiency with Heat or fluid injury.Gentle; Lung-Spleen Qi deficiency with fluid injury.Strongest restoration of Yuan Qi.Moderate Spleen-Lung Qi tonic; supports Blood and fluids indirectly.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it matters“Tonifies Qi” is not enough to choose the answer. Temperature, Yuan-Qi strength, direct fluid generation and Lung-Spleen focus separate the four.
Memory cueXi Yang Shen cools and nourishes Yin; Tai Zi Shen is gentle and moistening; Ren Shen restores Yuan Qi; Dang Shen supports Spleen-Lung Qi.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), p. 718. Source Exam Key CMID 9082, questions 1–3.
