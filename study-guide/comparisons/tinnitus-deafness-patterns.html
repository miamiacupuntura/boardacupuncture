Pathology or symptom comparison

Tinnitus and Deafness Pattern Differentiation

Diagnostic orientation

Core ideaOnset and associated signs distinguish excess from deficiency; sudden sensorineural hearing loss is a time-critical medical diagnosis.
Exam focusSudden versus gradual onset, sound quality, excess Heat and deficiency clues

Comparative differentiation

Study rule

How to distinguishSudden loud/angry is Fire; cold-to-ear blockage is Wind-Heat; heavy/phlegmy is Phlegm-Fire; gradual night cicadas with weak back is Kidney; overwork/digestion is Spleen Qi.
Memory cueRoar—Fire; cold then blocked—Wind; heavy-phlegmy—Phlegm; cicadas at night—Kidney; tired after work—Spleen.
SafetySudden hearing loss, especially in one ear, needs same-day emergency ENT assessment. Pulsatile tinnitus, neurologic symptoms, severe vertigo, ear trauma or new unilateral tinnitus also requires medical evaluation.
SourceInternal Medicine ENT section: Tinnitus and Deafness (Er Ming, Er Long).
