Theory 5 of 12

From Pattern to Treatment Principle

Convert diagnosis into a prioritized, adaptable formula plan.

Core rule

RuleCorrect sequencing is pattern → treatment principle → strategy → ingredients → monitoring.

Build the diagnosis

ConceptWhat it meansApplication
Principal patternThe dominant pathomechanism explains the defining symptom cluster.It determines the chief treatment principle.
Secondary developmentA branch may arise from the principal pattern and resolve when the root mechanism is corrected.Do not overbuild the formula for every dependent symptom.
Coexisting patternA second independent mechanism may need direct treatment but have lower immediate priority.Decide whether to treat now, protect against it or defer it.
Accompanying symptomA distressing symptom may deserve an assistant without defining the diagnosis.Relief should not overturn the principal strategy.
Tongue and pulseThey help confirm mechanism, depth, temperature, deficiency/excess and prognosis.Use them to test the hypothesis, not as isolated answer keys.

Adapt over time

ConceptWhat it meansApplication
Acute priorityA severe branch such as internal Wind may temporarily lead treatment.Stabilize urgent dynamics before emphasizing the chronic root.
ReassessmentWhen the dominant pattern changes, the treatment principle must change.Do not continue a successful acute formula after its target is gone.
Prevent predictable injuryStrong Heat may damage Qi/Yin; Liver constraint may affect Spleen.Add protection only when the mechanism and constitution justify it.

Responsible use

Educational summary for formula analysis and exam preparation. Doses, toxic preparations, interactions and clinical decisions require current references and professional supervision.
