Hóng Huā 红花 vs. Zàng/Xī Hóng Huā 藏红花/西红花

Safflower and saffron share “red flower” language but differ in species, potency, cost and secondary actions.

Memory rule

AnchorPlain Hóng Huā is safflower; geographic Hóng Huā is saffron.

Identities

NameIdentityJob
Hóng Huā 红花Carthamus tinctorius / safflowerInvigorates Blood, dispels stasis and regulates menses.
Zàng/Xī Hóng Huā 藏红花/西红花Crocus sativus / saffronInvigorates and cools Blood and may calm Spirit; used in much smaller quantities.

Exam and safety

Exam keyCrocus identity and geographic prefix distinguish saffron from common safflower.
SafetyBoth are contraindicated in pregnancy. Saffron is costly and commonly adulterated with dyed safflower; require authenticated sourcing.
