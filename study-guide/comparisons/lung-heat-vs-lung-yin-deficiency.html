Pattern comparison

Lung Heat vs. Lung Yin Deficiency

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueLung HeatLung Yin Deficiency
Shared clueBoth can cause cough, thirst, dry throat, red tongue signs and a rapid pulse.
Fast distinctionExcess Heat: loud cough, thick yellow sputum, fever, thirst, chest pain and a red tongue with yellow coat.Deficient Yin: chronic dry weak cough, scant sticky sputum, hoarseness, afternoon Heat, night sweats and a red peeled tongue.

Comparative answer key

CompareLung HeatLung Yin Deficiency
SputumThick, yellow and difficult or profuse.Scant, sticky or absent; possibly blood-streaked from dryness.
Systemic signAcute fever and strong thirst.Chronic dryness and Empty Heat.
Tongue / pulseRed with yellow coat; rapid and forceful/slippery.Red, dry, little coat; thin-rapid.
Formula examplesMa Xing Shi Gan Tang or Qing Qi Hua Tan Wan.Bai He Gu Jin Tang or Sha Shen Mai Dong Tang.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersBoth are hot Lung patterns. Sputum abundance, disease timing, tongue coat and pulse strength distinguish excess Heat from depleted Lung Yin.
Memory cueLung Heat cooks yellow Phlegm; Lung Yin deficiency dries the cough.
SafetyBreathing difficulty, chest pain, coughing blood, low oxygen, high fever or cough lasting more than three weeks needs medical assessment.

Possible exam questions · Study prompts

  • Which causes hoarseness and night sweats?
  • How does the tongue coat differ?
