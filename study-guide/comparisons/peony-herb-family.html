Peony — White Nourishes, Red Drains

Processing and species separate a nourishing white root, cooling red root and tree-peony bark.

One picture, one rule

RuleWhite supplements; red drains; tree bark clears deeper Heat.

The part decides the job

MedicinalPart / identityJob
Bái Sháo 白芍Peeled/boiled white peony rootNourishes Blood, preserves Yin, softens Liver and relieves pain.
Chì Sháo 赤芍Red peony rootCools and invigorates Blood, clears stasis and relieves pain.
Mǔ Dān Pí 牡丹皮Tree-peony root barkCools Blood, clears deficiency Heat and moves stasis.

Exam and safety

Exam keyBlood deficiency/tension = Bái; hot Blood stasis = Chì; deficiency Heat without sweating = Mǔ Dān Pí.
SafetyBlood-moving members require bleeding and pregnancy caution. Do not substitute roots solely because all are “peony.”
