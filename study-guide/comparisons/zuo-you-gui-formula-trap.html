Formula trap 7 of 22

Zuǒ Guī, Yòu Guī and Zuǒ Jīn

Left and Right restoration tonify Kidney Yin/Yang; Left Metal is a different Liver-Stomach formula.

Memory rule

AnchorLeft = less Heat; Right = roaring Fire; Left Metal = Liver on the left attacking Stomach.

Decisive separation

FormulaArchitectureDecisive presentation
Zuǒ Guī Wán 左归丸Rich Kidney Yin/Essence tonicYin and Essence deficiency without the draining assistants of Liù Wèi.
Yòu Guī Wán 右归丸Kidney Yang/Mìngmén tonic with Ròu Guì and processed Fù ZǐDeep Yang deficiency with Cold.
Zuǒ Jīn Wán 左金丸Huáng Lián 6 : Wú Zhū Yú 1Liver Fire attacking Stomach with acid regurgitation/vomiting.

Exam and safety

Exam keyGuī means restore in the first two; Jīn changes the formula family completely.
SafetyYòu Guī contains processed aconite and warming agents. Zuǒ Jīn is strongly bitter-cold/hot in a precise ratio and is not a general reflux remedy.
