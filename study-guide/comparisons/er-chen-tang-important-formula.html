Er Chen Tang

Damp-Phlegm from Spleen dysfunction affecting the Middle Burner or Lung.

High-yield summary

CategoryDry Dampness · Transform Phlegm
PatternDamp-Phlegm from Spleen dysfunction affecting the Middle Burner or Lung.
ActionDries Dampness, transforms Phlegm, regulates Qi and harmonizes the Middle Burner.
Decisive clueCopious white sputum, chest or epigastric fullness, nausea and a greasy white tongue coat.

Ingredients

Ban Xia, Ju Hong (or Chen Pi), Fu Ling and Zhi Gan Cao; Sheng Jiang and Wu Mei are used in the traditional preparation.

Commercial preparations and lineages may vary; always verify the source being used.

Differentiation and memory

Do not confuseWen Dan Tang modifies this strategy for Phlegm-Heat disturbing Gallbladder/Stomach and the Spirit; Er Chen Tang is foundational Damp-Phlegm.
Memory cueThe “two aged” ingredients—Ban Xia and Chen Pi—dry and move Phlegm.

Safety

This drying formula is inappropriate for unconfirmed dry cough or Yin deficiency. Persistent cough or breathing difficulty requires evaluation.
