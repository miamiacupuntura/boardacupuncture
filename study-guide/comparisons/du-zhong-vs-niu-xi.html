Herb comparison

Eucommiae Cortex (du zhong) vs. Achyranthis bidentatae Radix (niu xi)

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueDu ZhongNiu Xi
Shared cluesThe combination treats low-back and extremity pain or weakness from painful obstruction or Kidney deficiency and appears in Du Huo Ji Sheng Tang.
Fast distinctionYang tonic that strengthens Liver, Kidney, bones and tendons and calms the fetus.Blood invigorator that strengthens sinews and bones, treats lower-Jiao Damp-Heat and directs Blood downward.

Comparative answer key

CompareDu Zhong (Eucommiae Cortex)Niu Xi (Achyranthis bidentatae Radix)
CategoryHerbs that Tonify the Yang.Herbs that Invigorate the Blood.
Properties and channelsWarm, sweet and slightly acrid; Liver and Kidney.Neutral, bitter and sour; Liver and Kidney.
Lower-body levelGoverns the Qi level of the lower body.Governs the Blood level of the lower body and travels through the Qi and Blood of the channels and collaterals.
Shared musculoskeletal useStrengthen bones or sinews and address low-back pain associated with Liver-Kidney deficiency.
Primary emphasisTonifies Liver and Kidney and strengthens bones and tendons.Invigorates Blood and strengthens sinews and bones.
Distinctive actionsCalms the fetus to prevent miscarriage; the source states that the fried herb is more effective than the raw herb.Moves Blood downward and clears lower-Jiao Damp-Heat.
Distinctive indicationsLiver-Kidney weakness of the low back and extremities.Blood stasis with dysmenorrhea, lochia or amenorrhea; painful obstruction, stone dysuria or leukorrhea; upper-body Blood Heat, deficiency Heat or rising Liver Yang.
Formula cluesDu Huo Ji Sheng Tang and You Gui Wan.Du Huo Ji Sheng Tang, Yu Nu Jian and Zhen Gan Xi Feng Tang.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersBoth support the low back, bones and sinews, but one tonifies Yang while the other moves Blood and directs it downward.
Common confusionFetal calming and direct Liver-Kidney tonification favor Du Zhong; gynecological stasis, lower-Jiao Damp-Heat or downward Blood movement favor Niu Xi.
Formula contextDu Huo Ji Sheng Tang is categorized as a formula that dispels Wind-Dampness and treats chronic painful obstruction with Liver-Kidney and Qi-Blood deficiency.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), p. 791. Source Exam Keys CMID 8937, CMID 8938 and CMID 8977, questions 9–11.
