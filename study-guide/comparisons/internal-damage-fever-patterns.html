Pathology or symptom comparison

Internal Damage Fever Pattern Differentiation

Diagnostic orientation

Core ideaThe comparison concerns chronic or recurrent feverish sensations after external infection and other biomedical causes have been evaluated.
Exam focusTiming, emotional triggers, exertion, fixed pain and empty-Heat signs

Comparative differentiation

Pattern or presentationDecisive cluesRepresentative formula/herbStrategy
Qi stagnation feverFeverishness changes with mood; depression/irritability, sighing, hypochondriac distention and wiry-rapid pulseDan Zhi Xiao Yao SanSoothe Liver, resolve constraint and clear Heat.
Blood stasis feverAfternoon/night tidal fever with fixed pain or masses, sallow scaly skin and dark-purple tongueXue Fu Zhu Yu TangInvigorate Blood, dispel stasis and clear Heat.
Qi deficiency feverFever worse after exertion with fatigue, dyspnea, spontaneous sweat, poor appetite and weak pulseBu Zhong Yi Qi TangTonify Qi and strengthen Spleen.
Blood deficiency feverMild fever with vertigo, fatigue, palpitations, pale lips/nails and pale tongueGui Pi TangBenefit Qi and nourish Blood.
Yin deficiency feverAfternoon/night tidal fever, five-center Heat, night sweat, dry mouth and red scant-coated tongueQing Gu SanNourish Yin and clear deficient Heat.

Study rule

How to distinguishMood links to Qi; fixed purple pain to stasis; exertion/sweat to Qi deficiency; pallor/palpitations to Blood; night Heat/dryness to Yin.
Memory cueMood—Qi; fixed-purple—stasis; exertion—Qi deficiency; pale-palpitations—Blood; night-dry—Yin.
SafetyPersistent or recurrent fever requires medical evaluation for infection, inflammation, malignancy, medication reaction and endocrine disease. Confusion, stiff neck, dyspnea or severe dehydration is urgent.
