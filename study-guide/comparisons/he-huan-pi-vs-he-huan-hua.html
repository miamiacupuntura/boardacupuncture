Herb comparison

He Huan Pi vs. He Huan Hua

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueHe Huan PiHe Huan Hua
Shared clueBoth come from the same tree, are sweet and neutral, augment the Heart and Spleen, relieve constraint and calm the Spirit.
Fast distinctionBark; moves at the Blood level to stop pain and reduce swelling.Flower; moves at the Qi level to soothe Liver, harmonize Stomach and promote sleep.

Comparative answer key

CompareHe Huan PiHe Huan Hua
Botanical nameAlbiziae Cortex.Albiziae Flos.
Plant partAlbizia or mimosa bark.Albizia, silktree or mimosa flower.
CategoryHerbs that Nourish the Heart and Calm the Spirit.Herbs that Nourish the Heart and Calm the Spirit.
Level of movementGreater tendency to move at the Blood level.Moves at the Qi level.
Primary emphasisCalms the Spirit, relieves constraint, invigorates Blood, stops pain and reduces swelling.Relieves stagnant Liver Qi, promotes Qi in the collaterals, harmonizes and unbinds the Stomach, and calms the Shen.
Additional cluesPain and swelling.Soothing Liver, promoting memory and sleep, and brightening the eyes.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersBoth calm constraint from the same tree, but Blood-level pain or swelling favors the bark, while Liver-Qi and Stomach constraint with sleep, memory or eye clues favors the flower.
Memory cueHe Huan Pi bark moves Blood; He Huan Hua flower moves Qi.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), pp. 543 and 938. Source Exam Key CMID 8974, questions 1–3 and 5.

  • Which substance better soothes Liver Qi, unbinds the Stomach and promotes sleep?
