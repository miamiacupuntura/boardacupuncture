Qing Hao vs. Yin Chai Hu vs. Bai Wei

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersThe distinction depends on where Heat is directed and which extra systems are treated: Qing Hao vents and clears Summerheat, Yin Chai Hu cools internally, and Bai Wei adds Lung cooling plus urination.
Memory cueQing Hao vents Summerheat; Yin Chai Hu cools within; Bai Wei vents, cools Lung and moves urine.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), p. 225. Source Exam Key CMID 9035, question 3.
