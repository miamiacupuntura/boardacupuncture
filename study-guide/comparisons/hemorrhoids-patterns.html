Pathology or symptom comparison

Hemorrhoids Pattern Differentiation

Diagnostic orientation

Core ideaColor, pain/Heat and constitutional strength distinguish the two source presentations; rectal bleeding still requires biomedical evaluation.
Exam focusBright painful hot bleeding versus pale prolapse with fatigue

Comparative differentiation

Pattern or presentationDecisive cluesRepresentative formula/herbStrategy
Blood stagnation with Damp-HeatBright-red bleeding, anal swelling/pain/itch, thirst, dark urine, constipation and yellow sticky coatZhi Tong Ru Shen TangClear Damp-Heat and invigorate Blood.
Qi deficiency with prolapsePale bleeding, protruding hemorrhoids, bearing-down heaviness, fatigue, dyspnea, poor appetite and pale tongueBu Zhong Yi Qi TangBoost Spleen Qi and raise the fallen.

Study rule

How to distinguishBright-red, painful, itchy and hot indicates Damp-Heat; pale blood, prolapse, bearing-down and fatigue indicates Qi deficiency.
Memory cueBright-hot-painful—Damp-Heat; pale-prolapsed-tired—Qi deficiency.
SafetyNew or persistent rectal bleeding, black stool, anemia, weight loss, bowel-habit change, severe pain or bleeding after age 45 requires medical evaluation; heavy bleeding is urgent.
