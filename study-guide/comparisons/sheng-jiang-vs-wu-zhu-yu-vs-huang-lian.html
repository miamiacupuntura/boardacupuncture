Herb comparison

Zingiberis Rhizoma recens (sheng jiang) vs. Evodiae Fructus (wu zhu yu) vs. Coptidis Rhizoma (huang lian)

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueSheng JiangWu Zhu YuHuang Lian
Shared clueAll three alleviate nausea, but each matches a different etiology.
Fast distinctionClear-fluid vomiting from Stomach Cold.Acid regurgitation from Liver Cold affecting the Stomach.Bile vomiting from Damp-Heat or Heat in the Stomach.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersNausea is shared, so the fluid, taste and temperature clues identify the answer: clear fluids and Cold, sour reflux and Liver Cold, or bile and Damp-Heat.
Memory cueSheng Jiang: clear and cold; Wu Zhu Yu: sour and Liver Cold; Huang Lian: bile and Heat.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), p. 689. Source Exam Keys CMID 8980, questions 1 and 7–14; CMID 9061, questions 1 and 13–20; and CMID 9090, questions 1 and 8–15.

Possible exam questions · Study prompts

  • Which nausea etiology belongs to each herb?
  • Which nature, taste and category distinguish the three?
  • Which herb treats clear-fluid vomiting, acid reflux or bile vomiting?
  • Which formula roles identify each herb?
