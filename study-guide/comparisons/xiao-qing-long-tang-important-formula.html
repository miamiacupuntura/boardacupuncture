Xiao Qing Long Tang

Exterior Wind-Cold with congested cold Phlegm-Fluids in the Lung.

High-yield summary

CategoryRelease Wind-Cold · Warm Cold Fluids
PatternExterior Wind-Cold with congested cold Phlegm-Fluids in the Lung.
ActionReleases the exterior, warms the Lung, transforms cold Fluids and stops cough/wheezing.
Decisive clueChills without sweating, cough/wheezing and copious thin clear or frothy sputum.

Ingredients

Ma Huang, Gui Zhi, Gan Jiang, Xi Xin, Wu Wei Zi, Bai Shao, Ban Xia and Zhi Gan Cao.

Commercial preparations and lineages may vary; always verify the source being used.

Differentiation and memory

Do not confuseDing Chuan Tang has internal Phlegm-Heat and thick yellow sputum; Xiao Qing Long Tang has cold, thin, clear Fluids.
Memory cueThe Little Green Dragon opens the exterior and dries a Lung flooded with cold water.

Safety

Severe breathing difficulty, blue lips, chest pain or low oxygen is an emergency. Ma Huang and Xi Xin require professional dosing.
