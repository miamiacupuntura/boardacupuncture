Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersTwo formulas clear the Lung, but Ma Xing Shi Gan restores diffusion in a more acute pattern while Xie Bai drains constrained Lung Heat. Xie Huang changes the organ entirely.
Memory cueMa Xing = acute hot wheeze; Xie Bai = Lung white; Xie Huang = Spleen yellow.
SafetyBreathing difficulty, low oxygen, chest pain, high fever, facial swelling or inability to swallow requires urgent care. Ma Huang requires cardiovascular screening.
