Gui Zhi Tang

Deficient exterior Wind-Cold with disharmony between nutritive and protective Qi.

High-yield summary

CategoryRelease the Muscle Layer · Harmonize Ying and Wei
PatternDeficient exterior Wind-Cold with disharmony between nutritive and protective Qi.
ActionReleases the muscle layer, regulates Ying/Wei and harmonizes the exterior.
Decisive clueFever and chills with spontaneous sweating, aversion to Wind and a floating moderate or frail pulse.

Ingredients

Gui Zhi, Bai Shao, Sheng Jiang, Da Zao and Zhi Gan Cao.

Commercial preparations and lineages may vary; always verify the source being used.

Differentiation and memory

Do not confuseMa Huang Tang treats an excess exterior pattern without sweating; Gui Zhi Tang treats a deficient exterior with spontaneous sweating.
Memory cueGui Zhi opens Wei; Bai Shao gathers Ying; ginger, dates and licorice harmonize them.

Safety

High fever, breathing difficulty, confusion, dehydration or rapidly worsening illness requires medical evaluation.
