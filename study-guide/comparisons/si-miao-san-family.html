Si Miao San Family

Root formula and architecture

RootEr Miao San establishes the pair: Cang Zhu dries Dampness while Huang Bai clears lower-burner Damp-Heat.
ArchitectureNiu Xi turns the pair toward the knees and legs and invigorates circulation; Yi Yi Ren increases Damp drainage and supports the Spleen.

Study rule

How to chooseTwo clears and dries; Niu Xi makes Three and directs to the legs; Yi Yi Ren makes Four and increases Damp drainage.
Memory cueTwo dry; Three descend; Four drain.
SafetyAvoid assuming every red or swollen joint is Damp-Heat. Acute monoarthritis with fever, inability to bear weight, calf swelling or neurologic weakness needs urgent evaluation. Niu Xi requires pregnancy caution.
