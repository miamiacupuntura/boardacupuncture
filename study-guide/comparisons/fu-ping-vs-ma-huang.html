Herb comparison

Fu Ping vs. Ma Huang

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueFu PingMa Huang
Shared clueBoth disseminate Lung Qi, open pores, induce sweating and promote urination for fever without sweating, urinary difficulty or edema.
Fast distinctionAcrid and cold for Wind-Heat, edema, Damp-Heat, rashes and itching; little effect on cough.Acrid and warm for Wind-Cold Exterior excess; strongly controls wheezing and cough.

Comparative answer key

CompareFu PingMa Huang
Medicinal nameSpirodelae Herba.Ephedrae Herba.
CategoryCool, Acrid Herbs that Release the Exterior.Warm, Acrid Herbs that Release the Exterior.
Thermal patternWind-Heat Exterior pattern without sweating.Wind-Cold Exterior excess pattern without sweating.
Urinary and edema cluePromotes urination for edema or Damp-Heat.Unblocks fluid pathways and promotes urination.
Skin clueExpels Wind to clear rashes and stop itching.No corresponding rash-and-itching emphasis here.
Respiratory clueLittle or no effect on cough or wheezing.Controls wheezing and stops cough.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersThe shared sweating and urination actions make temperature and the secondary symptom decisive: rash and itching versus cough and wheezing.
Memory cueFu Ping is cool and clears the skin; Ma Huang is warm and opens the Lungs.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), p. 68. Source Exam Key CMID 9016, question 9.
