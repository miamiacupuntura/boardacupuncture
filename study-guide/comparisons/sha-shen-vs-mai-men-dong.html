Exam trap · Wording correction

Sha Shen vs. Mai Men Dong

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueSha ShenMai Men Dong
Shared clueBoth nourish Yin, moisten Dryness, generate Stomach fluids, nourish Lung Yin and clear Lung Heat.
Fast distinctionLighter and stronger at clearing Lung Heat and nourishing Lung Yin.Heavier and moister; stronger at nourishing Stomach Yin, generating fluids, clearing Heart Heat and eliminating irritability.

Study guide · Exam focus

Exam trapCMID 9012 says “Stomach Yang fluids.” In this Yin-tonic comparison, the clinically coherent distinction is that Mai Men Dong better nourishes Stomach Yin and generates fluids.
Why it mattersLung Heat and a lighter action favor Sha Shen; Stomach dryness, heavier moistening or Heart irritability favor Mai Men Dong.
Memory cueSha Shen is lighter for Lung Heat; Mai Men Dong is heavier for Stomach fluids and Heart irritability.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), p. 824. Source Exam Key CMID 9012, question 1; wording corrected here.
