Blue-Eye Blindness (Optic Atrophy) Comparison

Diagnostic orientation

Core ideaThe source describes dryness and vision loss with optic-atrophy fundus changes. These historical formulas do not reverse established optic-nerve damage.
Exam focusFundus-confirmed optic damage with constitutional or causal clues

Comparative differentiation

Pattern or presentationDecisive cluesRepresentative formula/herbStrategy
Liver-Kidney deficiencyVision loss with dizziness, tinnitus, weak back/knees, seminal emission and red scant-coated tongueMing Mu Di Huang WanHistorical strategy: support Liver-Kidney.
Qi and Blood deficiencyVision loss with fatigue, dyspnea, palpitations, insomnia, poor appetite, loose stool and pale tongueRen Shen Yang Rong TangHistorical strategy: support Qi and Blood.
Liver-Qi stagnationVision loss/distending eye pain linked to emotional stress, bitter taste and costal painXiao Yao SanHistorical strategy: soothe Liver Qi.
Qi and Blood stasisVision loss after head/eye trauma with headache, poor memory, dark tongue and rough pulseXue Fu Zhu Yu TangHistorical strategy: move Qi-Blood.

Study rule

How to distinguishWeak back/tinnitus marks Liver-Kidney; global fatigue/pallor Qi-Blood; emotional distention Liver-Qi; trauma/dark-purple signs stasis—but the optic diagnosis must be biomedical.
Memory cueBack/ears—Liver-Kidney; pale/tired—Qi-Blood; emotion/distention—Liver-Qi; trauma/purple—stasis.
SafetyOptic atrophy and unexplained vision loss require ophthalmology/neuro-ophthalmology evaluation. Sudden vision loss, eye pain, neurologic symptoms or new field defect is an emergency; do not delay disease-specific treatment.
SourceInternal Medicine ophthalmology section: Blue-Eye Blindness (Qing Mang).
