Herb comparison

Gao Liang Jiang vs. Gan Jiang

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueGao Liang JiangGan Jiang
Shared clueBoth are hot, pungent herbs that warm the Middle and disperse Cold.
Fast distinctionWarms Stomach Cold with epigastric pain, nausea and belching.Warms Spleen Cold with abdominal pain and diarrhea.

Comparative answer key

CompareGao Liang JiangGan Jiang
Botanical and English nameAlpiniae officinarum Rhizoma · lesser galangal rhizome.Zingiberis Rhizoma · dried ginger.
CategoryHerbs that Warm the Interior and Expel Cold.Herbs that Warm the Interior and Expel Cold.
Shared propertiesHot and pungent.
Organ emphasisStomach; source also lists Spleen affinity.Spleen.
Decisive indicationEpigastric or abdominal pain with rebellious Qi, nausea or belching.Abdominal pain and diarrhea from Spleen Cold.
Formula cluesLiang Fu Wan; Tian Tai Wu Yao San.Li Zhong Wan; Si Ni Tang; Gui Zhi Jia Long Gu Mu Li Tang; Wu Mei Wan; Shao Fu Zhu Yu Tang.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersBoth warm the Middle, but nausea and belching point to Stomach-focused Gao Liang Jiang, while diarrhea points to Spleen-focused Gan Jiang.
Memory cueGao Liang Jiang settles the Stomach; Gan Jiang warms the Spleen.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), p. 694. Source Exam Key CMID 8965, questions 8–12, and CMID 8967, questions 8–12.
