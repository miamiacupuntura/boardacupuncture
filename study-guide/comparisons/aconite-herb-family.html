Family 8 of 20

Aconite 乌头 — Daughter, Mother, Wild Aunt

All three are dangerous; the wild root is generally the fiercest.

One picture, one rule

RuleFù Zǐ rescues Yang; Chuān Wū and Cǎo Wū attack severe Cold pain.

The part decides the job

MedicinalPart / identityJob
Fù Zǐ 附子Prepared lateral daughter rootRestores devastated Yang, supplements Fire, disperses Cold and stops pain.
Chuān Wū 川乌Prepared cultivated mother rootDispels Wind-Damp, warms channels and treats severe Cold-Bi pain.
Cǎo Wū 草乌Prepared wild aconite rootVery potent Cold-dispersing analgesic; highest toxicity concern.

Exam and safety

Exam keyCollapse/Yang devastation favors Fù Zǐ; severe fixed Cold-Bi pain points to Wū Tóu roots.
SafetyPrepared material only, with required long pre-decoction and expert dosing. Contraindicated in pregnancy and incompatible with the traditional 18-Fǎn group. Numb mouth, palpitations or irregular pulse is an emergency.
