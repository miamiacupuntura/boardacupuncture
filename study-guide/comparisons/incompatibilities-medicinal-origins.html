Legal, ethical and clinical boundary

Origin labels are for identification and exam study. They do not authorize sourcing, possession, importation or prescribing. Protected wildlife, human-derived materials, toxic minerals and obsolete substances require current legal, ethical, infection-control and toxicology review. Use lawful substitutes where applicable.
