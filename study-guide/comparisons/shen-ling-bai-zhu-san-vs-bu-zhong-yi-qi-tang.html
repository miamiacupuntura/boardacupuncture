Formula comparison

Shen Ling Bai Zhu San vs. Bu Zhong Yi Qi Tang

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueShen Ling Bai Zhu SanBu Zhong Yi Qi Tang
Shared clueBoth tonify Spleen Qi and treat fatigue, poor appetite, loose stools, pale tongue and a weak pulse.
Fast distinctionSpleen-Qi deficiency fails to transform fluids: chronic diarrhea, weight loss and Dampness predominate.Middle Qi sinks: prolapse, bearing-down sensation, chronic organ descent or Qi-deficiency fever predominate.

Comparative answer key

CompareShen Ling Bai Zhu SanBu Zhong Yi Qi Tang
Qi failureTransportive failure with Damp accumulation.Elevating and holding failure with sinking.
Distinct actionStrengthens Spleen, leaches Dampness and stops diarrhea.Raises clear Yang and lifts sunken Qi.
Decisive clueChronic loose stool with Dampness.Prolapse or bearing-down sensation.
Exam shortcutWeak Spleen cannot move water.Weak middle cannot hold organs up.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersBoth start from Spleen-Qi deficiency, but the failed function differs: transport of fluids versus lifting and containment.
Memory cueShen Ling drains and stops diarrhea; Bu Zhong lifts.
SafetyBlood in stool, significant weight loss, dehydration, a painful non-reducible prolapse or persistent fever requires medical evaluation.
SourceSource bank: FWB 8.2.2, Comparison Formulas to Tonify; corroborated with individual formula profiles.
