Batch 7 · Small Intestine · Tip 1 of 10

Small Intestine Full Heat vs. Deficient Cold

Use temperature, urine, stool and tongue to separate opposite Small Intestine patterns.

Fast differentiation clues

ClueSmall Intestine Full HeatSmall Intestine Deficient and Cold
SharedBoth may cause abdominal pain and disturbed intestinal function.
Fast distinctionFull Heat: tongue/mouth ulcers, scant dark painful urine, thirst and a red tongue.Deficient Cold: dull pain, borborygmi, chronic diarrhea, cold limbs and a pale wet tongue.

Clinical table

CompareSmall Intestine Full HeatSmall Intestine Deficient and Cold
TemperatureHot and excess.Cold and deficient.
Urine / stoolDark, scant and painful urine.Loose or watery stool with undigested food.
Formula associationDao Chi San, Dao Chi Qing Xin Tang or Liang Ge San.Xiao Jian Zhong Tang or Shen Ling Bai Zhu San.

Exam and safety

Why it mattersUrinary burning and ulcers point to Heat; chronic diarrhea and cold limbs point to deficient Cold.
Memory cueHeat burns urine; Cold loosens stool.
SafetySevere abdominal pain, fever, blood, dehydration or inability to urinate requires prompt medical evaluation.
