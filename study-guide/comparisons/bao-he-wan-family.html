Formula family

Bao He Wan Family

Root formula and architecture

RootBao He Wan is the balanced starting point for acute Food Stagnation: digest accumulation, regulate Qi and harmonize the Stomach.
ArchitectureThe family is organized by what complicates the retained food: nothing beyond excess, Heat-Damp and constipation, or chronic Spleen-Qi deficiency.

Study rule

How to chooseRecent dietary excess is Bao He; strong Heat-Damp and bowel obstruction is Zhi Shi Dao Zhi; recurrent stagnation in a weak patient is Jian Pi.
Memory cueBao He clears the meal; Dao Zhi pushes hot blockage; Jian Pi repairs digestion.
SafetySevere or localized abdominal pain, rigid abdomen, persistent vomiting, blood in stool, weight loss or inability to pass stool and gas requires prompt medical evaluation.
