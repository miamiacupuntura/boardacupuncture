Dang Gui Si Ni Tang Family

Root formula and architecture

RootDang Gui Si Ni Tang warms the channels, disperses Cold and nourishes Blood when cold extremities arise from Blood deficiency and channel obstruction rather than Yang collapse.
ArchitectureDang Gui and Bai Shao nourish Blood; Gui Zhi and Xi Xin warm channels; Tong Cao opens circulation; Da Zao and Zhi Gan Cao support the deficient interior. Wu Zhu Yu and Sheng Jiang deepen the warming-descending action.

Study rule

How to chooseCold hands and feet plus Blood-deficiency signs choose the base; add Wu Zhu Yu and Sheng Jiang when internal Cold produces digestive rebellion or abdominal pain.
Memory cueDang Gui Si Ni warms the channels; Wu Zhu Yu-Sheng Jiang warms the interior too.
SafetyDo not confuse channel Cold with shock or acute vascular occlusion. Sudden one-sided cold/pale limb, severe pain, fainting or unstable vital signs is an emergency. Xi Xin and Wu Zhu Yu require professional dosing.
