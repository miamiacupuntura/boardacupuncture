Herb comparison

Shan Yao vs. Lian Zi

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueShan YaoLian Zi
Shared clueBoth are sweet and neutral, supplement the Spleen and Kidneys, bind the Intestines and stabilize the lower burner.
Fast distinctionPrimarily tonifies Qi and Yin and extends to the Lung for deficiency cough.Primarily stabilizes and binds; nourishes the Heart, quiets the Spirit and stops bleeding.

Comparative answer key

CompareShan YaoLian Zi
Medicinal nameDioscoreae Rhizoma.Nelumbinis Semen.
Common nameChinese yam or Chinese potato.Lotus seed.
CategoryHerbs that Tonify the Qi.Herbs that Stabilize and Bind.
Properties and channelsSweet and neutral; Spleen, Lung and Kidney.Sweet, astringent and neutral; Kidney, Spleen and Heart.
Distinctive actionAugments Qi and Yin of the Lungs, Spleen and Kidneys; stops deficiency-consumption cough.Nourishes the Heart, calms the Spirit, reconnects Heart and Kidneys and stops bleeding.
Shared indicationsSpleen-deficiency diarrhea; Kidney deficiency with spermatorrhea, enuresis or vaginal discharge; wasting-thirsting disorder.
Formula cluesShen Ling Bai Zhu San; Liu Wei Di Huang Wan; Zuo Gui Yin.Shen Ling Bai Zhu San; Jin Suo Gu Jing Wan.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersThe herbs overlap in Spleen-Kidney deficiency and lower-burner leakage. Lung Qi/Yin and deficiency cough identify Shan Yao; Heart-Spirit or bleeding clues identify Lian Zi.
Memory cueShan Yao tonifies Lung, Spleen and Kidney; Lian Zi binds while calming the Heart.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), pp. 878–880. Source Exam Key CMID 9011, questions 1–6.
