Herb comparison

Erythrinae Cortex (hai tong pi) vs. Xanthii Fructus (cang er zi)

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueHai Tong PiCang Er Zi
Shared cluesBoth expel Wind, eliminate Dampness, relieve pain and itching, and treat Wind-Damp joint pain, skin disorders or toothache.
Decisive temperatureCooling; favors hot painful obstruction and Damp-Heat in the lower body.Warm; favors cold painful obstruction and externally contracted Wind-Cold.

Comparative answer key

CompareHai Tong Pi (Erythrinae Cortex)Cang Er Zi (Xanthii Fructus)
CategoryHerbs that Dispel Wind-Dampness.Warm, Acrid Herbs that Release the Exterior.
Relative emphasisMore cooling and excels at eliminating Dampness.Dispels Wind more than Dampness and also induces sweating.
Painful obstructionMore appropriate for hot painful obstruction.More appropriate for cold painful obstruction.
Distinctive presentationDamp-Heat lodged in the lower body with burning pain in the lower back or legs.Wind-Cold with headache, blocked nose or sinusitis; releases the exterior and unblocks the nose.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersThe two herbs share many Wind-Damp pain and skin indications, but their category, temperature and relative Wind-versus-Damp emphasis differ.
Common confusionHai Tong Pi belongs to Wind-Damp-dispelling herbs and is more Damp-focused; Cang Er Zi belongs to exterior-releasing herbs and is more Wind-focused.
How it may appearBurning lower-body pain from Damp-Heat favors Hai Tong Pi. Headache, nasal blockage and sinusitis from Wind-Cold favors Cang Er Zi.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), p. 329. Source Exam Key CMID 8912.

Possible exam questions · Study prompts

  • Which herb induces sweating and unblocks the nose?
  • Which herb matches hot obstruction, and which matches cold obstruction?
