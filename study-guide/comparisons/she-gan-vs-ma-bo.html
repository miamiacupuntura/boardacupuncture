Herb comparison

Belamcandae Rhizoma (she gan) vs. Lasiosphaera/Calvatia (ma bo)

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueShe GanMa Bo
Shared clueBoth clear Heat, benefit the throat, resolve toxicity and disperse clumped Heat for throat swelling and pain.
Fast distinctionBitter and cold; directs pathogenic Heat downward and transforms Phlegm.Lightweight, acrid and neutral; disperses Wind-Heat and stops bleeding.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersBoth target painful throat swelling. Phlegm and downward direction favor She Gan; Wind-Heat dispersal and epistaxis favor Ma Bo.
Memory cueShe Gan sends Heat and Phlegm down; Ma Bo disperses Wind-Heat and stops Blood.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), p. 212. Source Exam Keys CMID 9015, question 2, and CMID 9065, question 1.
