Herb comparison

Pini Lignum nodi (song jie) vs. Mori Ramulus (sang zhi)

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueSong JieSang Zhi
Shared cluesBoth dispel Wind-Dampness and treat painful obstruction in the sinews, bones and joints.
Fast distinctionWarm and drying; select when Cold accompanies Wind-Dampness, not Heat.Neutral; suitable whether the painful obstruction involves Cold or Heat.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersBoth treat Wind-Damp painful obstruction, so temperature is decisive: Song Jie is restricted to Cold patterns, while neutral Sang Zhi can fit either Cold or Heat.
Memory cueSong Jie warms and dries Cold; Sang Zhi stays neutral for Cold or Heat.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), p. 368. Source Exam Key CMID 9049, questions 9–10.
