Herb comparison

Han Fang Ji vs. Guang Fang Ji

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueHan Fang JiGuang Fang Ji
Shared clueBoth promote urination, reduce edema, regulate the water passages and enter the Bladder, Spleen and Kidney channels.
Fast distinctionStephania root; lower-jiao Water-Dampness and red, hot, swollen joints.Aristolochia root; toxic and banned because of aristolochic acid.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersThe names are easily confused, but the botanical identity is decisive: Han Fang Ji is Stephaniae tetrandrae Radix; Guang Fang Ji is the toxic, banned Aristolochiae Fangchi Radix.
Memory cueHan Fang Ji = Stephania; Guang Fang Ji = Aristolochia danger.
SourceSource Exam Key CMID 8969, question 1. The source review specifically warns that Guang Fang Ji contains the nephrotoxin aristolochic acid.
