Zhi Gan Cao Tang

Heart Qi, Blood, Yin and Yang deficiency causing an irregular or knotted pulse.

High-yield summary

CategoryTonify Qi and Blood · Restore the Pulse
PatternHeart Qi, Blood, Yin and Yang deficiency causing an irregular or knotted pulse.
ActionAugments Qi, nourishes Blood/Yin, warms Heart Yang and restores the pulse.
Decisive cluePalpitations with an irregular pulse, fatigue, shortness of breath and a dry, deficient presentation.

Ingredients

Zhi Gan Cao, Sheng Jiang, Gui Zhi, Ren Shen, Sheng Di Huang, E Jiao, Mai Men Dong, Huo Ma Ren and Da Zao.

Commercial preparations and lineages may vary; always verify the source being used.

Differentiation and memory

Do not confuseSheng Mai San treats Qi/Yin collapse with weak pulse and sweating; Zhi Gan Cao Tang is the classic complex formula for irregular pulse from mixed Heart deficiency.
Memory cueHoney-fried licorice leads a balanced team that fills vessels, warms Heart and reconnects the pulse.

Safety

New palpitations, chest pain, fainting, shortness of breath or sustained irregular heartbeat requires urgent cardiac evaluation. Gan Cao may affect potassium and blood pressure.
