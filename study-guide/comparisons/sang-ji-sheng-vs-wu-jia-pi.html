Herb comparison

Taxilli Herba (sang ji sheng) vs. Acanthopanacis Cortex (wu jia pi)

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueSang Ji ShengWu Jia Pi
Shared cluesBoth dispel Wind-Dampness, tonify the Liver and Kidneys, and strengthen the sinews and bones.
Fast distinctionNeutral, moistening and milder; nourishes Blood and stabilizes pregnancy.Acrid, bitter and warm; more strongly expels pathogens, warms Yang and promotes urination.

Comparative answer key

CompareSang Ji ShengWu Jia Pi

Additional actionStabilizes pregnancy.Promotes urination.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersBoth strengthen the same organ and musculoskeletal systems. Blood nourishment, mildness and pregnancy support indicate Sang Ji Sheng; warmth, stronger pathogen expulsion and urination indicate Wu Jia Pi.
Memory cueSang Ji Sheng moistens, nourishes Blood and stabilizes pregnancy; Wu Jia Pi warms, expels and drains.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), p. 339. Source Exam Keys CMID 9052, questions 4–6, and CMID 9086, questions 1–3.
