Family 19 of 20

Panax — Ginseng, American Ginseng and Sān Qī

The Panax genus contains a warm Qi rescue, cool Qi-Yin tonic and hemostatic that moves stasis.

One picture, one rule

RuleRén Shēn raises Qi; Xī Yáng Shēn cools and moistens; Sān Qī stops bleeding without trapping stasis.

The part decides the job

MedicinalPart / identityJob
Rén Shēn 人参Panax ginseng rootPowerfully tonifies original Qi, rescues collapse and supports Lung/Spleen.
Xī Yáng Shēn 西洋参Panax quinquefolius rootTonifies Qi and Yin while clearing deficiency Heat.
Sān Qī 三七Panax notoginseng rootStops bleeding, disperses stasis, reduces swelling and pain.
Dǎng Shēn / Tài Zǐ ShēnNot PanaxSeparate, gentler Qi tonics from other botanical families.

Exam and safety

Exam keyCold collapse = Rén; Qi-Yin deficiency with Heat = Xī Yáng; traumatic/stasis bleeding = Sān Qī.
SafetyAll can interact with anticoagulant, antiplatelet, glucose or blood-pressure therapy; Sān Qī requires bleeding/pregnancy caution.
