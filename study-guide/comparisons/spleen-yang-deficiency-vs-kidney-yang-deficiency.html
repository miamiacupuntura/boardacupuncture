Spleen Yang Deficiency vs. Kidney Yang Deficiency

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueSpleen Yang DeficiencyKidney Yang Deficiency
Shared clueBoth cause internal Cold, fatigue, cold limbs, edema, pale swollen wet tongue and a deep slow weak pulse.
Fast distinctionDigestive center fails: poor appetite, cold abdominal pain, loose stool with undigested food and edema from weak transport.Mingmen and lower burner fail: weak low back/knees, dawn diarrhea, frequent clear urine, edema, reproductive weakness and pronounced cold.

Comparative answer key

CompareSpleen Yang DeficiencyKidney Yang Deficiency
Primary sphereDigestion and Spleen fluid transport.Urinary, reproductive, Essence and whole-body warming.
Decisive clueCold digestive symptoms dominate.Low back/knee, urinary or reproductive signs dominate.
RelationshipCan progress into Kidney Yang deficiency.Often includes Spleen Yang weakness when advanced.
Formula examplesLi Zhong Wan.Jin Gui Shen Qi Wan, You Gui Wan or Zhen Wu Tang by presentation.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersCold and edema overlap. Location and depth decide: Middle-Jiao digestion points to Spleen; lower burner, Essence and Mingmen point to Kidney.
Memory cueSpleen Yang warms the pot; Kidney Yang powers the furnace and water gate.
SafetyRapidly worsening edema, shortness of breath, very low urine output, severe weakness or hypothermia requires urgent medical evaluation.
