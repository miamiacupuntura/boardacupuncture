Da Bu Yin Wan

Severe Liver/Kidney Yin and Essence deficiency with blazing Empty Heat.

High-yield summary

CategoryNourish Yin · Descend Deficiency Fire
PatternSevere Liver/Kidney Yin and Essence deficiency with blazing Empty Heat.
ActionEnriches Yin, nourishes Essence and drains deficiency Fire.
Decisive clueSteaming-bone fever, night sweats and heat in the palms/soles with weakness of the low back and knees.

Ingredients

Shu Di Huang, Gui Ban, Huang Bai and Zhi Mu; the traditional preparation uses honey and pig spinal cord as binding vehicles.

Commercial preparations and lineages may vary; always verify the source being used.

Differentiation and memory

Do not confuseZhi Bai Di Huang Wan treats a milder Kidney Yin deficiency with Empty Heat; Da Bu Yin Wan is stronger and more fire-draining.
Memory cueGreatly tonify Yin while Zhi Mu and Huang Bai pull Empty Fire downward.

Safety

Persistent fever, drenching night sweats, weight loss or bone pain requires evaluation for infection, malignancy or endocrine disease.
