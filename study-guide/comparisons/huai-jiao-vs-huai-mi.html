Herb comparison

Huai Jiao vs. Huai Mi

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueHuai JiaoHuai Mi
Shared clueBoth cool the Blood, stop bleeding and treat the eyes.
Fast distinctionThe fruit sinks and directs Fire downward.The lighter flower bud travels upward and is stronger at stopping bleeding.
Eye clueMoistens the Liver while directing Liver Fire downward.Clears Liver-channel Heat or Fire causing red, swollen eyes.

Comparative answer key

CompareHuai JiaoHuai Mi
Medicinal nameSophorae Fructus.Sophorae Flos immaturus.
Shared categoryHerbs that Regulate the Blood · Herbs that Stop Bleeding.
MovementSinks directly downward, moves Blood and directs Qi downward.Its lighter weight enables it to travel upward more effectively.
Eye actionBrightens the eyes by moistening the Liver while directing Fire downward.Clears Heat from Blood stored in the Liver, relieving red and swollen eyes from Liver-channel Heat or Fire.
Stronger emphasisBetter at draining Heat and descending Fire.Better at stopping bleeding.
Source-specific cautionThe source also attributes labor-inducing and fetus-aborting actions to Huai Jiao.No corresponding action is assigned to Huai Mi in these questions.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersThe names are similar and both herbs treat bleeding and the eyes. The key distinction is direction: Huai Jiao descends Heat and Fire, while the lighter Huai Mi travels upward and stops bleeding more strongly.
Memory cueJiao goes down; Mi moves up and stops bleeding.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), p. 576. Source Exam Key CMID 8976, questions 2–5.
