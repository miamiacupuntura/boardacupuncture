Yin Qiao San

Early-stage Wind-Heat attacking the Lung and Wei level.

High-yield summary

CategoryDisperse Wind-Heat · Clear Toxic Heat
PatternEarly-stage Wind-Heat attacking the Lung and Wei level.
ActionDisperses Wind-Heat, clears Heat/Toxin and benefits the throat.
Decisive clueFever greater than chills, sore throat, thirst and headache with little or no sweating.

Ingredients

Jin Yin Hua, Lian Qiao, Bo He, Niu Bang Zi, Jing Jie Sui, Dan Dou Chi, Jie Geng, Zhu Ye and Sheng Gan Cao, with Lu Gen in decoction.

Commercial preparations and lineages may vary; always verify the source being used.

Differentiation and memory

Do not confuseSang Ju Yin treats a milder Wind-Heat presentation dominated by cough; Yin Qiao San treats stronger fever and sore throat.
Memory cueSilver flower and forsythia clear the hot exterior while mint and burdock open throat and surface.

Safety

Breathing difficulty, drooling/inability to swallow, severe dehydration, rash, confusion or persistent high fever requires urgent care.
