Tiān Má + Gōu Téng

天麻 + 钩藤

One picture, one job

PictureThe Wind-and-dizziness pair.
Combined jobCalm the Liver, extinguish internal Wind, stop dizziness and headache and ease spasms.
CategoryCalm the Liver · Extinguish Wind

How the pair works

HerbRole within the pair
Tiān MáNeutral and versatile; extinguishes Wind and treats dizziness across deficiency or excess patterns.
Gōu TéngCool; clears Liver Heat, subdues rising Yang and relieves tremor or spasm.

Exam focus and safety

CluesLiver-Yang-rising headache, dizziness, hypertension pattern, tremor or muscular spasm.
Signature formulaTiān Má Gōu Téng Yǐn.
TrapTiān Má can address many dizziness patterns; Gōu Téng makes the pair especially appropriate when Heat or rising Yang is present.
PreparationAdd Gōu Téng late and boil it only briefly to preserve its Wind-extinguishing activity.
SafetyMonitor blood pressure and prescription-drug effects. Sudden severe headache, one-sided weakness, facial droop or slurred speech requires immediate stroke protocol.
