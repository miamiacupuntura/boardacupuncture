Ban Xia Bai Zhu Tian Ma Tang

Wind-Phlegm rising from Spleen deficiency and Dampness.

High-yield summary

CategoryTransform Phlegm · Extinguish Wind
PatternWind-Phlegm rising from Spleen deficiency and Dampness.
ActionDries Dampness, transforms Phlegm, strengthens Spleen and calms internal Wind.
Decisive clueDizziness/vertigo with nausea, heavy head, copious Phlegm and a slippery or wiry-slippery pulse.

Differentiation and memory

Do not confuseTian Ma Gou Teng Yin treats Liver Yang/Wind with Heat; this formula is Damp-Phlegm and Spleen-deficiency weighted.
Memory cueBan Xia dries Phlegm; Tian Ma quiets Wind; Bai Zhu supports the source.

Safety

Sudden severe vertigo, weakness, speech change, severe headache or inability to walk may indicate stroke and is an emergency.
