Study guide · Exam focus

Why it matters“Dry constipation” is incomplete. Heat/excess, Blood deficiency and Kidney-Yang deficiency require different therapeutic directions.
Memory cueMa Zi = Heat; Run Chang = Blood; Ji Chuan = Kidney Yang.
SafetyNo stool or gas with severe distention, persistent vomiting, rectal bleeding or acute severe pain may indicate obstruction or another emergency. Strong laxative use is not appropriate for self-treatment.
