Ba Zheng San

Damp-Heat clumping in the Lower Burner with painful urinary dysfunction.

High-yield summary

CategoryClear Damp-Heat from the Lower Burner
PatternDamp-Heat clumping in the Lower Burner with painful urinary dysfunction.
ActionClears Heat, drains Dampness and promotes urination.
Decisive clueBurning, dark, scant or difficult urination with urgency and a sticky yellow coat.

Ingredients

Qu Mai, Bian Xu, Mu Tong, Che Qian Zi, Hua Shi, Zhi Zi, Da Huang and Zhi Gan Cao; Deng Xin Cao is commonly added as a guiding herb.

Commercial preparations and lineages may vary; always verify the source being used.

Differentiation and memory

Do not confuseSuo Quan Wan treats frequent pale abundant urine from deficiency-Cold, the opposite thermal and excess/deficiency picture.
Memory cueBa Zheng rectifies eight directions of hot urinary blockage.

Safety

Fever, flank pain, vomiting, pregnancy, blood in urine or urinary retention requires urgent medical care.
