Hypertension Pattern Differentiation

Diagnostic orientation

Core ideaBlood-pressure measurement establishes the biomedical condition; TCM signs differentiate the accompanying presentation, not the numeric diagnosis itself.
Exam focusTCM presentation alongside biomedical blood-pressure management

Comparative differentiation

Yin and Yang deficiencyMovement-aggravated dizziness, blurred vision, tinnitus, palpitations, dyspnea and lumbar weaknessEr Xian TangSupport Kidney Yin and Yang.

Study rule

How to distinguishEmpty Heat/lumbar weakness marks Yin deficiency; red angry excess marks Liver Fire; heaviness/greasy coat marks Phlegm-Damp; mixed weakness with both Yin and Yang signs marks combined deficiency.
Memory cueHot-empty—Yin; red-angry—Fire; heavy-greasy—Phlegm; mixed weak—Yin and Yang.
SafetyHerbs do not replace prescribed antihypertensives. Blood pressure at or above 180/120 with chest pain, neurologic symptoms, severe headache or shortness of breath is an emergency.
