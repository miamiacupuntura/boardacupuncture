Formula comparison

Wu Ling San vs. Zhu Ling Tang vs. Wu Pi San

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueWu Ling SanZhu Ling TangWu Pi San
Shared clueAll promote urination and treat fluid retention, but they direct treatment toward different mechanisms and tissue levels.
Fast distinctionBladder Qi-transformation failure with thirst and urinary difficulty.Water-Heat in the lower burner with Yin injury and irritability or urinary Heat.Superficial generalized edema with heavy limbs, fullness and Qi stagnation.

Comparative answer key

CompareWu Ling SanZhu Ling TangWu Pi San
MechanismTaiyang water accumulation; Yang cannot transform fluids.Water combines with Heat and damages Yin.Qi stagnation prevents movement of water in the skin and subcutaneous tissues.
Distinct actionWarms Yang and transforms Qi.Clears Heat, promotes urination and nourishes Yin.Moves Qi and reduces edema with peel medicinals.
Decisive clueThirst but difficulty processing fluids.Heat signs, thirst and possible painful or bloody urination.Pitting edema with abdominal/epigastric fullness.
Exam shortcutTransform water.Drain hot water while protecting Yin.Move superficial water by moving Qi.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it matters“Edema” alone is not enough. Decide whether the failed step is Qi transformation, Heat injuring Yin, or Qi stagnation trapping water superficially.
Memory cueWu Ling transforms; Zhu Ling cools; Wu Pi moves the skin.
SafetySudden edema, breathlessness, chest pain, no urine, fever with flank pain or edema during pregnancy requires prompt medical evaluation.
