Clove Sores (Furuncles) Comparison

Diagnostic orientation

Core ideaThe source gives one principal pattern; clinically, the crucial distinction is a small localized boil versus abscess/cellulitis requiring drainage or antibiotics.
Exam focusHard-rooted pustular lesion with local Heat and systemic infection signs

Comparative differentiation

Pattern or presentationDecisive cluesRepresentative formula/herbStrategy
Exuberant Fire toxinSmall hard-rooted yellow/purple lesion becoming blister/pustule, red-hot swelling, pain/itch/numbness, fever, thirst and dark urineHuang Lian Jie Du TangHistorical strategy: clear Heat and resolve toxin.

Study rule

How to distinguishA hard-centered lesion becoming red, hot, swollen and pustular corresponds to Fire toxin; spreading redness or fluctuance raises concern for abscess/cellulitis.
Memory cueHard seed → hot red pustule = Fire-toxin sore.
SafetyDo not squeeze facial lesions. Rapid spread, severe pain, fever, red streaks, immune suppression or a soft/fluctuant center needs same-day care; many abscesses require incision and drainage.
