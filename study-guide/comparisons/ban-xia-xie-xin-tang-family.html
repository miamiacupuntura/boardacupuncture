Formula family

Ban Xia Xie Xin Tang Family

Root formula and architecture

RootBan Xia Xie Xin Tang harmonizes Cold and Heat clashing in the Middle Jiao and opens painless focal epigastric distention.
ArchitectureBan Xia and Gan Jiang warm and descend; Huang Qin and Huang Lian clear Heat; Ren Shen, Da Zao and Zhi Gan Cao support deficient Middle Qi. Changing ginger or licorice emphasis reveals the branch.

Study rule

How to chooseThe painless epigastric knot is shared. More water, vomiting and belching selects Sheng Jiang; greater deficiency and relentless diarrhea selects Gan Cao.
Memory cueBan Xia balances; Sheng Jiang disperses water; Gan Cao rebuilds the center.
SafetyBan Xia must be properly processed. Persistent vomiting or diarrhea, dehydration, GI bleeding, severe pain, fever or weight loss requires medical evaluation.
