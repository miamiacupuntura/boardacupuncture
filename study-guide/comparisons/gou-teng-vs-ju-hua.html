Herb comparison

Gou Teng vs. Ju Hua

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueGou TengJu Hua
Shared clueBoth calm the Liver and disperse Wind-Heat for headache, dizziness or red eyes.
Fast distinctionPrimarily extinguishes internal Wind and stops seizures.Primarily disperses external Wind and drains Heat.

Comparative answer key

CompareGou TengJu Hua
Botanical nameUncariae Ramulus cum Uncis.Chrysanthemi Flos.
CategorySubstances that Extinguish Wind and Stop Tremors.Cool, Acrid Herbs that Release the Exterior.
Primary actionExtinguishes Wind and stops seizures.Disperses Wind and drains Heat.
Shared Liver patternWind-Fire ascending along the Liver channel with headache or dizziness.
Shared exterior patternExternally contracted disease with headache and red eyes.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersThe same headache, dizziness and red-eye presentation can appear, but seizures and internal Wind favor Gou Teng while external Wind-Heat dispersal favors Ju Hua.
Memory cueGou Teng stops internal Wind; Ju Hua scatters external Wind-Heat.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), p. 969. Source Exam Keys CMID 8958, question 1, and CMID 8995, question 4.
