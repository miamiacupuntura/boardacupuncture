Appendix B pair · 10 of 51

Zhī Zǐ + Dàn Dòu Chǐ

栀子 + 淡豆豉

One picture, one job

PictureA cold bitter fruit and fermented bean let leftover Heat escape.
Combined jobClear and vent constrained residual Heat from the chest after an unresolved febrile process.
CategoryVent Constrained Heat from the Chest

How the pair works

HerbRole within the pair
Zhī ZǐClears Fire from the three burners and relieves irritability.
Dàn Dòu ChǐLightly vents lingering Heat outward from the chest and Exterior.

Exam focus and safety

CluesRestlessness, insomnia and stuffy chest after a fever, without the four-greats picture of blazing Qi-level Heat.
Signature formulaZhī Zǐ Chǐ Tāng.
TrapDo not confuse residual constrained Heat with full Yangming Heat requiring Shí Gāo + Zhī Mǔ.
PreparationDàn Dòu Chǐ is a prepared/fermented soybean medicinal, not ordinary food soy.
SafetyPersistent fever, chest pain, breathing difficulty, confusion or severe insomnia with behavioral change requires medical evaluation.
