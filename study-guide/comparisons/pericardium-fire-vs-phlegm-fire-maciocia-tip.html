Pericardium Fire vs. Phlegm-Fire Harassing the Pericardium

Separate direct Fire signs from Fire complicated by Phlegm, oppression and marked mental disturbance.

Fast differentiation clues

CluePericardium FirePhlegm-Fire Harassing the Pericardium
SharedBoth may cause palpitations, chest tightness, insomnia, agitation and a rapid pulse.
Fast distinctionFire dominates: strong thirst, bitter taste, red tongue and irritability without a markedly greasy coat.Phlegm-Fire dominates: chest oppression, nausea or sputum plus confused, manic or erratic behavior and a greasy yellow coat.

Clinical table

ComparePericardium FirePhlegm-Fire Harassing the Pericardium
Added pathogenHeat/Fire without prominent Phlegm.Heat/Fire plus obstructing Phlegm.
Tongue / pulseRed with yellow coat; rapid-forceful.Red, swollen, sticky yellow coat; slippery-rapid.
Formula associationXie Xin Tang by presentation.Wen Dan Tang or another Phlegm-Heat strategy by presentation.

Exam and safety

Why it mattersA greasy coat, slippery pulse and chest/mental obstruction identify the added Phlegm component.
Memory cueFire agitates; Phlegm-Fire agitates and clogs.
SafetySevere agitation, psychosis, confusion, fever or danger to self/others requires urgent medical and psychiatric assessment.

Review prompts

  • Which has a slippery pulse?
  • Which has more nausea and oppression?
  • What shows the added Phlegm?
