Gall Bladder Dampness vs. Damp-Heat

Use temperature, taste and coating color to separate two heavy Gall Bladder patterns.

Fast differentiation clues

ClueGall Bladder DampnessGall Bladder Damp-Heat
SharedBoth cause hypochondriac fullness, heaviness, nausea and a sticky unilateral tongue coating.
Fast distinctionDampness dominates: heaviness and sticky coat without strong Heat signs.Heat joins: bitter taste, yellow sticky coat, thirst or irritability and a faster pulse.

Clinical table

CompareGall Bladder DampnessGall Bladder Damp-Heat
TemperatureNeutral or mildly cool.Hot and Damp.
Tongue / pulseSticky unilateral coat; slippery or soggy.Thick sticky yellow coat, often right-sided; slippery-rapid.
Formula associationSan Ren Tang plus Yin Chen Hao in the supplied table.Yin Chen Hao-based strategy in the supplied table.

Exam and safety

Why it mattersYellow coat, bitter taste and rapid pulse prove Heat beyond Dampness alone.
Memory cueDampness feels heavy; Damp-Heat feels heavy, bitter and yellow.
SafetyFever, jaundice, severe right-upper abdominal pain or persistent vomiting needs urgent medical evaluation.
