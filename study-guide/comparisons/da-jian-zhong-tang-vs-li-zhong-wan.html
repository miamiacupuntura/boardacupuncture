Formula comparison

Da Jian Zhong Tang vs. Li Zhong Wan

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueDa Jian Zhong TangLi Zhong Wan
Shared clueBoth warm and tonify the middle burner for deficiency-Cold with abdominal pain and digestive dysfunction.
Fast distinctionSevere internal Cold: intense abdominal pain, vomiting, inability to eat and visible or palpable abdominal movement.Moderate chronic deficiency-Cold: dull pain, loose stool, poor appetite and fatigue.

Comparative answer key

CompareDa Jian Zhong TangLi Zhong Wan
Relative strengthStronger and more urgent warming.Gentler foundational warming-tonification.
IngredientsRen Shen and Gan Jiang are shared; Shu Jiao and Yi Tang strongly disperse Cold and relieve pain.Ren Shen and Gan Jiang are shared; Bai Zhu and Zhi Gan Cao emphasize Spleen support.
Decisive clueSevere pain and rebellious Qi with marked Cold.Chronic diarrhea and weakness.
Exam shortcutMajor Cold pain.Routine middle deficiency-Cold.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersThese formulas share a deficient middle but differ in intensity. Da Jian Zhong treats severe Cold obstruction and rebellion, not merely chronic weak digestion.
Memory cueDa Jian Zhong for dramatic Cold pain; Li Zhong for chronic cold weakness.
SafetySevere or rigid abdominal pain, persistent vomiting, abdominal distention with no stool/gas, fever or bleeding is an emergency, not a home-treatment pattern.
SourceSource bank: FWB 7.1 and FWB 7.1.1, Comparison Formulas to Warm the Interior; corroborated with formula references.
