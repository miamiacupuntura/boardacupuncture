Herb comparison

Cyathulae Officinalis Radix (chuan niu xi) vs. Achyranthis bidentatae Radix (niu xi)

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueChuan Niu XiNiu Xi
Shared cluesBoth are neutral and bitter, enter the Kidney and Liver, break up Blood, guide Blood downward, unblock menstruation, stop pain and affect the sinews.
Fast distinctionSweet secondary flavor; Sichuan Cyathula Root.Sour secondary flavor; Two-toothed Achyranthes.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersThe source gives many shared features, so the secondary flavor and English/botanical name are the decisive matching clues.
Common confusionBoth may be called “Ox knee.” Chuan Niu Xi is sweet and linked to Sichuan Cyathula; Niu Xi is sour and linked to Two-toothed Achyranthes.
Source-data noteThe exam title uses “Cyathulae Radix,” while the keyed comparison uses “Cyathulae Officinalis Radix.” This note preserves the label used in the answer key.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), p. 639. Source Exam Key CMID 8920.
