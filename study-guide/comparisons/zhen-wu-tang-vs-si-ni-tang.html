Formula comparison

Zhen Wu Tang vs. Si Ni Tang

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueZhen Wu TangSi Ni Tang
Shared clueBoth warm Yang and may contain Fu Zi and ginger, but their therapeutic targets differ.
Fast distinctionYang deficiency fails to transform water: edema, scanty urine, heaviness, dizziness or palpitations.Yang collapse fails to warm the body: severe cold limbs, diarrhea, exhaustion and a faint pulse.

Comparative answer key

CompareZhen Wu TangSi Ni Tang
Primary problemSpleen-Kidney Yang deficiency with retained water.Severe internal Cold with devastated or collapsing Yang.
Therapeutic emphasisWarm Yang and promote urination to transform water.Restore Yang and rescue collapse.
Fluid clueEdema, urinary difficulty, heaviness and fluid retention are central.Fluid retention is not the defining feature.
Formula constructionFu Zi with Bai Zhu, Fu Ling, Bai Shao and Sheng Jiang targets Yang deficiency with water.Fu Zi with Gan Jiang and Zhi Gan Cao concentrates on restoring devastated Yang.
Ginger distinctionUses Sheng Jiang in the standard formula.Uses Gan Jiang in the standard formula.
Exam shortcutCold + water.Cold + collapse.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersBoth are warming formulas, so the discriminating question is whether water accumulation or systemic collapse dominates.
Memory cueZhen Wu moves cold water; Si Ni restores failing fire.
SafetyBoth classically involve Fu Zi. Contemporary safe practice requires processed material, verified sourcing and qualified professional supervision; suspected collapse is an emergency.
SourceSource bank: FWB 5.2.1 and FWB 7.1, Comparison Formulas to Expel Damp / Warm the Interior; corroborated with Classic Formulas profiles.
