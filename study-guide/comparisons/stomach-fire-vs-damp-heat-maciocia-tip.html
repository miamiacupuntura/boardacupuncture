Stomach Fire vs. Stomach Damp-Heat

Separate dry, intense Full Heat from Heat burdened by Dampness and heaviness.

Fast differentiation clues

ClueStomach FireStomach Damp-Heat
SharedBoth cause epigastric Heat/fullness, thirst, red tongue, yellow coat and a rapid pulse.
Fast distinctionFire is dry and forceful: burning, strong thirst for cold drinks, foul breath and yellow-dry coat.Damp-Heat is heavy and sticky: fullness, nausea, heaviness, reduced thirst and sticky-yellow coat.

Clinical table

CompareStomach FireStomach Damp-Heat
Fluid qualityHeat dries fluids.Dampness makes fluids turbid and sticky.
PulseRapid and overflowing/forceful.Slippery-rapid or soggy-rapid.
Formula associationQing Wei San, Tiao Wei Cheng Qi Tang or Liang Ge San.Lian Po Yin.

Exam and safety

Why it mattersDryness and strong thirst indicate Fire; heaviness, nausea and stickiness indicate Damp-Heat.
Memory cueFire burns dry; Damp-Heat simmers sticky.
SafetySevere pain, persistent vomiting, GI bleeding, high fever, jaundice or dehydration requires prompt medical care.

Review prompts

  • Which wants cold liquids?
  • Which feels heavy?
  • How do the pulses differ?
