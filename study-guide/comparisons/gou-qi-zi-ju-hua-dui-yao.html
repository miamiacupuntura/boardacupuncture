Appendix B pair · 28 of 51

Gǒu Qǐ Zǐ + Jú Huā

枸杞子 + 菊花

One picture, one job

PictureGoji feeds the eyes from below; chrysanthemum clears them from above.
Combined jobNourish Liver and Kidney Yin and brighten deficient, dry or blurred eyes.
CategoryNourish Liver–Kidney · Brighten Eyes

How the pair works

HerbRole within the pair
Gǒu Qǐ ZǐNourishes Liver–Kidney Yin and Blood to support vision.
Jú HuāClears mild Liver Heat and directly brightens the eyes.

Exam focus and safety

CluesDry, blurry or tired eyes with dizziness and other Liver–Kidney deficiency signs.
Signature formulaQǐ Jú Dì Huáng Wán.
TrapJú Huā with Gǒu Qǐ Zǐ treats deficient eyes; with Sāng Yè it treats excess Wind-Heat red eyes.
PreparationAdd delicate Jú Huā late rather than subjecting it to an unnecessarily long boil.
SafetyGǒu Qǐ Zǐ can interact with warfarin. Sudden vision loss, severe eye pain, halos or neurologic symptoms requires urgent evaluation.
