Spleen Qi Deficiency vs. Sinking Qi vs. Qi Not Holding Blood

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueSpleen Qi DeficiencySinking Spleen QiSpleen Qi Not Holding Blood
Shared clueAll share fatigue, poor appetite, loose stools, pale tongue and a weak pulse because Spleen Qi is deficient.
Fast distinctionBasic transformation failure: digestive weakness and fatigue without a defining downstream failure.Upbearing failure: prolapse, bearing-down sensation, chronic diarrhea or worse symptoms on standing/exertion.Containment failure: easy bruising, prolonged menses or other pale, slow bleeding.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersThe root signs overlap. The exam asks what Spleen Qi fails to do: digest, lift or contain Blood.
Memory cueDeficient Qi cannot transform; sinking Qi cannot lift; uncontained Qi cannot hold Blood.
SafetyNew prolapse, heavy or unexplained bleeding, black stool, fainting or signs of anemia requires medical evaluation.
