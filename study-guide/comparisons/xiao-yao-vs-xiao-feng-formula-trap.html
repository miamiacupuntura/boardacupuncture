Formula trap 13 of 22

Xiāo Yáo Sǎn vs. Xiāo Fēng Sǎn

“Free and Easy” treats internal constraint; “Disperse Wind” treats an itchy, weeping skin pattern.

Memory rule

AnchorYáo frees the emotions; Fēng disperses the rash.

Decisive separation

FormulaArchitectureDecisive presentation
Xiāo Yáo Sǎn 逍遥散Chái Hú, Dāng Guī, Bái Sháo, Bái Zhú, Fú Líng, Zhì Gān Cǎo plus Bò Hé/gingerLiver-Qi constraint with Spleen and Blood deficiency.
Xiāo Fēng Sǎn 消风散Wind-dispersing, Heat-clearing, Damp-drying and Blood-nourishing combinationItchy, weeping, erythematous rash from Wind-Damp-Heat with Blood involvement.

Exam and safety

Exam keyMood, ribs and menses = Xiāo Yáo; skin itching and exudate = Xiāo Fēng.
SafetyA rapidly spreading rash, mucosal lesions, fever, facial swelling or breathing difficulty requires urgent medical evaluation.
