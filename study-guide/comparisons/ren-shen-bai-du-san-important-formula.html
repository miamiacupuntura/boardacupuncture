Ren Shen Bai Du San

External Wind-Cold-Damp invasion in a patient with underlying Qi deficiency.

High-yield summary

CategoryRelease Exterior · Support Qi
PatternExternal Wind-Cold-Damp invasion in a patient with underlying Qi deficiency.
ActionDisperses Wind-Cold-Damp, releases the exterior, regulates Qi and supports upright Qi.
Decisive clueFever/chills without sweating, headache, heavy aching body and nasal congestion in a weak or convalescent patient.

Ingredients

Ren Shen, Fu Ling, Zhi Gan Cao, Chuan Xiong, Qiang Huo, Du Huo, Qian Hu, Chai Hu, Jie Geng and Zhi Ke, prepared with Sheng Jiang and Bo He.

Commercial preparations and lineages may vary; always verify the source being used.

Differentiation and memory

Do not confuseJing Fang Bai Du San omits Ren Shen and suits a stronger constitution; Ren Shen Bai Du San explicitly supports Qi deficiency.
Memory cueBai Du expels the pathogen; Ren Shen protects the deficient host.

Safety

Breathing difficulty, persistent high fever, confusion, dehydration or low oxygen requires medical evaluation.
