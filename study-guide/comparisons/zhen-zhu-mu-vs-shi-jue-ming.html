Herb comparison

Margaritiferae Concha usta (zhen zhu mu) vs. Haliotidis Concha (shi jue ming)

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueZhen Zhu MuShi Jue Ming
Shared cluesBoth cool Liver Heat and sedate ascendant Liver Yang and may be combined for these disorders.
Fast distinctionEnters the Heart as well as the Liver and therefore also calms the Spirit.Enters only the Liver channel.

Comparative answer key

CompareZhen Zhu MuShi Jue Ming
Medicinal nameMargaritiferae Concha usta · mother-of-pearl.Haliotidis Concha · abalone shell.
CategorySubstances that Anchor, Settle and Calm the Spirit.Substances that Extinguish Wind and Stop Tremors.
Shared actionsCool Liver Heat and sedate ascendant Liver Yang.
Channel distinctionEnters the Heart and Liver channels.Enters only the Liver channel.
Additional actionCalms the Spirit because it also enters the Heart.The comparison does not assign it the additional Heart-Spirit action.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersThe Liver actions overlap. A Heart-channel or Spirit-calming clue identifies Zhen Zhu Mu; a Liver-only clue identifies Shi Jue Ming.
Memory cueZhen Zhu Mu reaches Heart and Liver; Shi Jue Ming stays with Liver.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), p. 919. Source Exam Key CMID 9130, question 2.
