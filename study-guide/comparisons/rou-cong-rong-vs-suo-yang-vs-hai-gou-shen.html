Historical substance comparison

Cistanches Herba (rou cong rong) vs. Cynomorii Herba (suo yang) vs. Callorhini Testes et Penis (hai gou shen)

Conservation notice

Hai Gou ShenThis traditional source material comes from a protected marine mammal. It is included only to understand the historical exam comparison and is not presented as a clinical option.

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueRou Cong RongSuo YangHai Gou Shen
Shared historical focusAll were described as salty substances that augment Essence and tonify Kidney Yang for male impotence.
Fast distinctionLarge Intestine and constipation.Large Intestine and constipation, plus strongest sinew support.Historical Liver-Kidney clue with lower-back and knee weakness.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersConstipation identifies Rou Cong Rong or Suo Yang; stronger sinew support identifies Suo Yang. The lower-back and knee clue identifies Hai Gou Shen only within the historical comparison.
Memory cueRou Cong Rong moistens; Suo Yang strengthens sinews; Hai Gou Shen is historical only.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), p. 809. Source Exam Key CMID 9075, question 4.
