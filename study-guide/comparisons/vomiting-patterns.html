Pathology or symptom comparison

Vomiting Pattern Differentiation

Diagnostic orientation

Core ideaOnset, vomit contents, epigastric signs and the tongue coating distinguish acute excess from chronic deficiency.
Exam focusContents, triggers, coating and constitutional signs

Comparative differentiation

Pattern or presentationDecisive cluesRepresentative formula/herbStrategy
Exterior pathogen invading StomachSudden vomiting with chills, fever, body aches and chest oppressionHuo Xiang Zheng Qi SanRelease Exterior and transform turbidity.
Food StagnationVomiting undigested food, foul belching, reflux and thick greasy coatBao He WanReduce food and guide stagnation out.
Liver Qi disrupting StomachStress-related vomiting, belching, reflux and hypochondriac painBan Xia Hou Po Tang or Zuo Jin Wan when Heat dominatesSoothe Liver and descend Stomach Qi.
Phlegm-Damp obstructionClear fluid/mucus, dizziness, palpitations and white greasy coatXiao Ban Xia Tang + Ling Gui Zhu Gan TangWarm and transform Phlegm-fluid.
Stomach Yin deficiencyRepeated retching, thirst, dry mouth, hunger without desire to eatMai Men Dong TangNourish Stomach Yin and descend Qi.
Spleen-Stomach deficiency ColdVomiting after eating with fatigue, cold limbs, loose stool and pale tongueLi Zhong TangWarm Middle and strengthen Spleen.

Study rule

How to distinguishSudden fever/chills indicates Exterior; undigested food and foul odor indicates Food; emotion indicates Liver; clear mucus with greasy coat indicates Phlegm; dry retching indicates Yin deficiency.
Memory cueExterior—sudden; Food—foul; Liver—stress; Phlegm—clear mucus; Yin—dry retching; Cold—weak and pale.
SafetyBlood or coffee-ground material, severe dehydration, persistent bilious vomiting, severe headache, rigid abdomen or inability to keep fluids down requires urgent assessment.
