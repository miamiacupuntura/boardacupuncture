Herb comparison

Isatidis Radix (bei ban lan gen) vs. Sophorae tonkinensis Radix (shan dou gen)

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueBei Ban Lan GenShan Dou Gen
Shared cluesBoth clear Heat, resolve toxicity, soothe the throat, and treat throat swelling and pain.
Fast distinctionCools the Blood and expels Wind-Heat; choose it for rashes in warm-pathogen disease.Cools above and below, disperses clumps, dries Dampness and kills parasites.

Comparative answer key

CompareBei Ban Lan GenShan Dou Gen
Medicinal nameIsatidis Radix.Sophorae tonkinensis Radix.
Common categoryHerbs that Clear Heat and Resolve Toxicity.
Channel emphasisPrimarily Heart, Lung and Stomach.Lung and Large Intestine.
Primary emphasisResolves toxicity, cools the Blood and expels Wind-Heat.Cools Heat both above and below, disperses clumps, dries Dampness and kills parasites.
Decisive indicationRashes associated with warm-pathogen diseases.Throat swelling with clumping, Dampness or parasite clues.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersBoth fit painful, swollen throat. Blood Heat, Wind-Heat or rash points to Bei Ban Lan Gen; clumps, Dampness, parasites or simultaneous upper-and-lower Heat point to Shan Dou Gen.
Memory cueBei Ban Lan Gen cools Blood and clears rashes; Shan Dou Gen disperses clumps and dries Dampness.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), p. 208. Source Exam Key CMID 9053, question 2.
