Xiao Chai Hu Tang vs. Da Chai Hu Tang

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueXiao Chai Hu TangDa Chai Hu Tang
Shared clueBoth harmonize Shaoyang and may show alternating chills and fever, hypochondriac fullness, bitter taste, nausea and a wiry pulse.
Fast distinctionPrimarily Shaoyang: alternating chills and fever without a strong bowel-excess pattern.Shaoyang plus Yangming excess: abdominal or epigastric fullness and pain, persistent vomiting or constipation.

Comparative answer key

CompareXiao Chai Hu TangDa Chai Hu Tang
Pattern depthHalf-exterior/half-interior Shaoyang disorder.Shaoyang disorder combined with interior excess.
Distinct actionHarmonizes the Shaoyang pivot and supports upright Qi.Harmonizes Shaoyang while draining interior Heat and accumulation.
Decisive clueAlternating chills and fever dominate.Constipation and abdominal fullness/pain join the Shaoyang signs.
Formula constructionContains Qi-supporting Ren Shen and Gan Cao.Adds Da Huang and Zhi Shi to drain and move the interior.
Exam shortcutShaoyang only.Shaoyang + Yangming bowel excess.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersThe larger formula is not merely a stronger Xiao Chai Hu Tang; it adds a second disease location that requires downward drainage.
Memory cueXiao turns the pivot; Da turns the pivot and opens the bowel.
SafetyDa Chai Hu Tang has a purgative component and is not for self-treatment. Severe abdominal pain or suspected obstruction requires urgent assessment.
