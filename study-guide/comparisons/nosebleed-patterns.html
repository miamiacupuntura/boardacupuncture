Pathology or symptom comparison

Nosebleed Pattern Differentiation

Diagnostic orientation

Core ideaBlood color/volume and accompanying system signs organize the TCM comparison; first aid and cause assessment come first clinically.
Exam focusBlood color and amount plus respiratory, digestive, Liver or deficiency signs

Comparative differentiation

Pattern or presentationDecisive cluesRepresentative formula/herbStrategy
Lung HeatModerate bright-red bleeding with dry/hot nasal cavity, fever, cough and dry mouthSang Ju YinClear Lung Heat and cool Blood.
Stomach HeatLarge bright/dark-red bleed with dry nose, halitosis, intense thirst, dry stool and surging pulseXi Jiao Di Huang Tang (use Shui Niu Jiao substitute)Clear Stomach Fire and cool Blood.
Liver FireLarge dark-red bleed with headache, vertigo, bitter mouth, red eyes, irritability and wiry-rapid pulseLong Dan Xie Gan TangDrain Liver Fire and cool Blood.
Liver-Kidney Yin deficiencySmall intermittent recurrent bleed with dryness, tidal fever, night sweat, tinnitus and five-center HeatZhi Bai Di Huang WanNourish Yin and clear deficient Fire.
Spleen fails to contain BloodVariable light-red bleeding with pallor, poor appetite, fatigue, weak voice and weak pulseGui Pi TangStrengthen Spleen Qi and contain Blood.

Study rule

How to distinguishRespiratory dryness is Lung; large bleed with thirst/halitosis Stomach; dark blood with anger/red eyes Liver; recurrent small hot-dry bleed Yin; pale tired bleed Spleen.
Memory cueCough-dry—Lung; thirst-breath—Stomach; angry-red eyes—Liver; small-night Heat—Yin; pale-tired—Spleen.
SafetySit forward and pinch the soft nose continuously for 15 minutes. Bleeding that continues after two attempts, is heavy, follows major trauma, causes faintness, or occurs with anticoagulants requires urgent care.
