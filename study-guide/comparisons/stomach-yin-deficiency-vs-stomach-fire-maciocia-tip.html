Batch 6 · Stomach · Tip 3 of 10

Stomach Yin Deficiency vs. Stomach Fire

Separate deficient dryness with little coating from a forceful Full-Heat presentation.

Fast differentiation clues

ClueStomach Yin DeficiencyStomach Fire
SharedBoth may cause epigastric discomfort, dry mouth, thirst, a red tongue and a rapid pulse.
Fast distinctionDeficient dryness: dull pain, poor appetite and a peeled rootless center with thin pulse.Full Heat: burning pain, strong thirst for cold liquids, foul breath and thick yellow or yellow-dry coat.

Clinical table

CompareStomach Yin DeficiencyStomach Fire
Heat typeEmpty Heat from depleted fluids.Excess Fire in the Stomach.
Tongue / pulseRed center without root/coating; thin-rapid.Red center with thick yellow-dry coat; rapid-overflowing.
Formula associationSha Shen Mai Dong Tang or Yi Wei Tang.Qing Wei San, Tiao Wei Cheng Qi Tang or Liang Ge San by presentation.

Exam and safety

Why it mattersCoating and force decide: Yin deficiency removes the coat; Full Fire makes it thick, yellow and dry.
Memory cueYin deficiency leaves no coat; Fire scorches a yellow coat.
SafetySevere or persistent burning pain, vomiting blood, black stool, fainting or weight loss requires urgent evaluation.
