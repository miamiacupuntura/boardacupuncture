Herb comparison

Fang Feng vs. Jing Jie

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueFang FengJing Jie
Shared clueBoth eliminate Wind, release the Exterior and disperse either Wind-Cold or Wind-Heat.
Fast distinctionReaches deeper Wind inside the membranes, bones and flesh and also overcomes Dampness.Acts more lightly and superficially on the skin, muscles and Blood.

Comparative answer key

CompareFang FengJing Jie
Botanical nameSaposhnikoviae Radix.Schizonepetae Herba.
CategoryWarm, Acrid Herbs that Release the Exterior.Warm, Acrid Herbs that Release the Exterior.
PropertiesAcrid, sweet and slightly warm.Acrid and slightly warm.
ChannelsLiver, Urinary Bladder and Spleen.Lung and Liver.
Depth of actionStronger for Wind deep in the body; reaches inside the membranes and into bones and flesh.Weaker and more superficial; expels Wind from the skin, muscles and Blood but does not reach bones and flesh.
Additional emphasisAppropriate for many types of Wind and can overcome Dampness.Its light, spreading nature disperses Wind-Heat from the Blood.
Individual formula cluesYu Ping Feng San; Gui Zhi Shao Yao Zhi Mu Tang; Juan Bi Tang; Du Huo Ji Sheng Tang.Yin Qiao San; Huai Hua San; Zhi Sou San.
Shared formula cluesFang Feng Tong Sheng San and Xiao Feng San.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersBoth release the Exterior and treat Wind-Cold or Wind-Heat; the decisive distinction is deep Wind with Dampness versus light, superficial Wind.
Memory cueFang Feng follows Wind deep; Jing Jie stays light and superficial.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), p. 19. Source Exam Keys CMID 8947, questions 1–4, and CMID 8994, questions 6–9.
