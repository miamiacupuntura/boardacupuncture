Pattern comparison

Kidney Qi Not Firm vs. Kidney Failing to Grasp Qi

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueKidney Qi Not FirmKidney Failing to Grasp Qi
Shared clueBoth arise from Kidney-Qi deficiency and may show weak low back/knees, fatigue, urinary weakness and a deep weak pulse.
Fast distinctionLower gates leak: frequent clear urine, incontinence, enuresis, spermatorrhea or chronic vaginal discharge.Respiration cannot descend: chronic wheezing or dyspnea worse on inhalation and exertion, with short breath and weak low back.

Comparative answer key

CompareKidney Qi Not FirmKidney Failing to Grasp Qi
Failed functionConsolidate Bladder, Essence and lower orifices.Receive and anchor Lung Qi during inhalation.
System emphasisUrinary and reproductive leakage.Respiratory weakness rooted below.
Decisive clueLoss or leakage.Difficulty drawing breath in.
Formula examplesSuo Quan Wan or Jin Suo Gu Jing Wan by leakage.Ren Shen Ge Jie San or Su Zi Jiang Qi Tang by presentation.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersBoth are Kidney-Qi failures, but one loses through the lower gates while the other fails to pull Lung Qi downward.
Memory cueNot firm leaks below; not grasping loses the inhale.
SafetyNew incontinence, urinary retention, saddle numbness, severe breathing difficulty, blue lips or rapidly worsening asthma requires urgent assessment.
