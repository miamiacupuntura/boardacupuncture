Herb comparison

Alismatis Rhizoma (ze xie) vs. Akebiae Caulis (mu tong)

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueZe XieMu Tong
Shared cluesBoth drain Dampness, promote flow through the fluid pathways, and may be combined for internal Water/Dampness stoppage.
Fire distinctionMinisterial Fire: Kidney channel.Sovereign Fire: Heart, with Small Intestine involvement.

Comparative answer key

CompareZe Xie (Alismatis Rhizoma)Mu Tong (Akebiae Caulis)
Shared categoryHerbs that Drain Dampness.
Shared fluid actionPromote flow in the fluid pathways when smooth fluid movement is obstructed.
Type of FireClears ministerial Fire.Clears sovereign Fire.
Channel focusKidney.Heart and Small Intestine.
Additional actionThe source comparison centers its distinction on Kidney ministerial Fire.Also unblocks the flow of Qi and Blood.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersThe fluid-draining action is shared; the tested distinction is ministerial versus sovereign Fire.
Common confusionKidney Fire points to Ze Xie; Heart/Small Intestine Fire and Qi/Blood flow point to Mu Tong.
TerminologyThe source defines sovereign Fire as Heart Fire and ministerial Fire as Kidney Fire.
Source question formatsQuestions 1 and 2 are Matching.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), p. 284. Source Exam Key CMID 8919.
