Phlegm-Heat vs. Dry-Phlegm in the Lung

Use sputum quantity and moisture to distinguish sticky hot obstruction from dryness with scant Phlegm.

Fast differentiation clues

CluePhlegm-HeatDry-Phlegm
SharedBoth can cause difficult expectoration, chest oppression, cough and a rapid pulse.
Fast distinctionHeat and abundance: thick yellow/green sticky sputum, thirst, greasy yellow coat and slippery-rapid pulse.Dryness and scarcity: dry cough with occasional scant tenacious sputum, dry mouth/throat and a dry tongue.

Clinical table

ComparePhlegm-HeatDry-Phlegm
Fluid stateFluids cook into thick hot Phlegm.Fluids are depleted, leaving scant sticky residue.
Formula associationQing Qi Hua Tan Wan/Tang.Bei Mu Gua Lou San.

Exam and safety

Why it mattersBoth can be sticky; quantity and systemic dryness distinguish them.
Memory cuePhlegm-Heat is thick and plentiful; Dry-Phlegm is scant and stubborn.
SafetyCoughing blood, persistent fever, weight loss, chest pain or breathing difficulty requires evaluation.

Review prompts

  • Which has abundant yellow sputum?
  • Which has a dry tongue?
  • Why can both be hard to expectorate?
