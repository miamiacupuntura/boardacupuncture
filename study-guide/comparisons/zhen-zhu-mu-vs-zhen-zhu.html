Herb comparison

Margaritiferae Concha usta (zhen zhu mu) vs. Margarita (zhen zhu)

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueZhen Zhu MuZhen Zhu
Shared cluesPearl and mother-of-pearl have similar anchoring, settling and Spirit-calming actions.
Fast distinctionPrimarily calms ascendant Liver Yang and cools Liver Heat affecting the eyes.Retains the additional ability to nourish Yin and generate flesh.

Comparative answer key

CompareZhen Zhu MuZhen Zhu
Medicinal nameMargaritiferae Concha usta · mother-of-pearl.Margarita · pearl.
Shared categorySubstances that Anchor, Settle and Calm the Spirit.
Primary emphasisCalms ascendant Liver Yang and cools Liver Heat.Calms the Spirit while additionally nourishing Yin and generating flesh.
Key indicationsHeadache, dizziness, tinnitus and insomnia from ascendant Liver Yang; red eyes and photophobia from Liver Heat.The distinguishing clues in this comparison are Yin nourishment and flesh generation.
LimitationLacks the ability to nourish Yin and generate flesh.Not assigned that limitation in the comparison.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersThe names and actions are similar. Liver-Yang or Liver-Heat eye clues point to Zhen Zhu Mu; nourishing Yin or generating flesh points to Zhen Zhu.
Memory cueZhen Zhu Mu anchors Liver Yang; Zhen Zhu nourishes and generates flesh.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), p. 917. Source Exam Keys CMID 9129 and CMID 9130, question 1.
