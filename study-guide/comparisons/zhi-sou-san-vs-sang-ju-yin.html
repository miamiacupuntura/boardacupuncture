Formula comparison

Zhi Sou San vs. Sang Ju Yin

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueZhi Sou SanSang Ju Yin
Shared clueBoth release a mild Exterior pathogen and direct Lung Qi downward to stop cough; cough is the dominant symptom.
Fast distinctionResidual Wind-Cold cough after most Exterior signs have faded; little fever and thin white sputum.Early Wind-Heat attacking the Lung; mild fever, thirst, sore throat and a floating-rapid pulse.

Comparative answer key

CompareZhi Sou SanSang Ju Yin
PathogenResidual Wind-Cold with constrained Lung Qi.Wind-Heat at the Lung defensive level.
Thermal clueLittle or no Heat; sputum is thin and white.Mild Heat, thirst or sore throat; sputum may be yellow.
Primary actionStops cough while gently dispersing the remaining pathogen.Disperses Wind-Heat and clears Lung Heat to stop cough.
Exam shortcutThe cold is nearly gone, but the cough remains.A mild warm-disease cough has just begun.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersBoth are cough-centered and relatively gentle. Temperature and timing separate a lingering Wind-Cold cough from the early stage of Wind-Heat.
Memory cueZhi Sou = cough after Cold; Sang Ju = cough at the start of Heat.
SafetyShortness of breath, chest pain, coughing blood, high fever or cough lasting more than three weeks requires medical assessment.
SourceSource bank: FWB 6.2, Comparison Formulas to Transform Phlegm; corroborated with exterior-releasing materials and individual profiles.
