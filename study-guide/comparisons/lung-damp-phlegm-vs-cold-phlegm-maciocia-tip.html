Damp-Phlegm vs. Cold-Phlegm in the Lung

Separate chronic white sticky Phlegm from colder, thinner and more watery sputum.

Fast differentiation clues

ClueDamp-PhlegmCold-Phlegm
SharedBoth cause chronic cough, copious white sputum, chest oppression, greasy white coat and a slippery pulse.
Fast distinctionDampness dominates: thick white sticky sputum, heaviness, poor appetite and nausea.Cold dominates: thin watery sputum, chest coldness, cold limbs and a slower pulse; worse with cold exposure.

Clinical table

CompareDamp-PhlegmCold-Phlegm
SputumWhite, thick and sticky.White, thin, watery or frothy.
Tongue / pulseSwollen, greasy white; slippery or soggy.Wet swollen, white coat; slippery-slow.
Formula associationEr Chen Tang.She Gan Ma Huang Tang, Ling Gui Zhu Gan Tang or San Zi Yang Qin Tang by presentation.

Exam and safety

Why it mattersBoth are white Phlegm; texture and systemic Cold signs decide.
Memory cueDamp-Phlegm is sticky; Cold-Phlegm is watery and chilly.
SafetyBreathlessness, frothy sputum, leg swelling or inability to lie flat can indicate heart failure and needs urgent care.

Review prompts

  • Which sputum is sticky?
  • Which has cold limbs?
  • Why can frothy sputum be urgent?
