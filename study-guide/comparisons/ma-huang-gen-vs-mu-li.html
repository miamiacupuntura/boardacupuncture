Herb comparison

Ephedrae Radix (ma huang gen) vs. Ostreae Concha (mu li)

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueMa Huang GenMu Li
Shared clueBoth restrain and inhibit sweating associated with Qi- and Yin-deficiency disorders.
Fast distinctionSpecialized action: restrains outward discharge to stop sweating.Broader heavy substance: anchors Yang, secures and binds, quiets the Spirit and especially treats Yin-deficiency night sweats.

Comparative answer key

CompareMa Huang Gen (Ephedrae Radix)Mu Li (Ostreae Concha)
CategoryHerbs that Stabilize and Bind.Substances that Anchor, Settle, and Calm the Spirit.
Shared actionRestrain and inhibit deficiency sweating.
ScopeLimited in this comparison to restraining sweating.Has a wide range of anchoring, securing, binding and Spirit-calming actions.
MechanismRestrains outward discharge.Uses its heavy nature to inhibit Yin and anchor Yang.
Decisive indicationDeficiency sweating when a specialized sweat-restraining herb is sought.Especially Yin-deficiency night sweats with a need to anchor Yang or quiet the Spirit.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersBoth stop deficiency sweating, but Ma Huang Gen is narrowly restraining while Mu Li combines sweat control with broader anchoring and Spirit-calming functions.
Memory cueMa Huang Gen closes sweating; Mu Li anchors and binds.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), p. 897. Source Exam Key CMID 9019, question 3.
