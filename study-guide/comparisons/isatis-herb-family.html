Isatis — Leaf, Root and Indigo Powder

One dye plant gives a Blood-cooling leaf, throat-focused root and concentrated indigo powder.

One picture, one rule

RuleLeaf to Blood/skin; root to throat; blue powder to Liver Fire.

The part decides the job

MedicinalPart / identityJob
Dà Qīng Yè 大青叶LeafClears Heat-toxin and cools Blood for rashes/blotches.
Bǎn Lán Gēn 板蓝根RootClears Heat-toxin with emphasis on throat, mumps and head/neck swelling.
Qīng Dài 青黛Indigo powderCools Blood, clears Liver Fire and stops Heat-type bleeding/cough.

Exam and safety

Exam keyRash = leaf; swollen sore throat = root; Liver Fire or coughing Blood = indigo.
SafetyQīng Dài is not normally decocted; administer in pill/powder as professionally directed. Bitter-cold members can injure weak digestion.
