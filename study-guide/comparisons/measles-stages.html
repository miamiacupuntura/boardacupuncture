Measles Stages and Complications

Diagnostic orientation

Core ideaMeasles staging may aid exam recall, but suspected measles requires immediate infection-control and public-health coordination.
Exam focusRash sequence, stage changes and emergency complication clues

Comparative differentiation

Pattern or presentationDecisive cluesRepresentative formula/herbStrategy
Febrile prodromeFever, chills, coryza, cough, frontal headache, red tearing eyes and fatigue; Koplik spots may appear after 2–3 daysXuan Du Fa Biao TangRelease the exterior and vent the eruption.
Eruption stagePersistent fever; rash begins at hairline or behind ears and spreads over face, trunk and limbs with thirst, red eyes and coughQing Jie Tou Biao TangClear Heat and promote complete eruption.
Recovery stageFever recedes, rash fades in the same order with darkening or fine desquamation, while hoarseness, dry cough and thirst may remainSha Shen Mai Dong TangNourish Lung-Stomach Yin and fluids.

Complication—throat Heat-ToxinSwollen painful throat, hoarse voice and barking coughQing Yan Xia Tan TangClear throat Heat-Toxin and transform Phlegm.
Complication—Heat-Toxin enters Heart-LiverHigh fever, delirium, confluent purple-red rash, nasal flaring, impaired consciousness or convulsionsLing Jiao Gou Teng Tang—historical formulaHistorical strategy: cool Heat and extinguish Wind.

Study rule

How to distinguishCoryza-cough-conjunctivitis precede a head-to-toe rash; fading follows the same order. Rash suppression, breathing distress or neurologic change signals complication.
Memory cueThree Cs + Koplik → descending rash → fading; lung or brain signs = emergency.
SafetyMeasles is highly contagious, vaccine-preventable and immediately reportable in many jurisdictions. Call ahead for same-day medical evaluation and use airborne isolation. Breathing difficulty, dehydration, confusion or seizure is an emergency. Classical Ling Jiao is an endangered/obsolete animal substance; vitamin A is only under medical guidance.
