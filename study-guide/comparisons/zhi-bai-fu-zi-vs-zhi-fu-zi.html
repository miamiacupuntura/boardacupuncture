Herb comparison

Zhi Bai Fu Zi vs. Zhi Fu Zi

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueZhi Bai Fu ZiZhi Fu Zi
Shared clueBoth are acrid warming medicinals whose similar names conceal different categories and directions.
Fast distinctionAscends to expel Wind, Dampness and Cold from the upper burner.Mobilizes the lower burner, warms the Gate of Vitality and restores devastated Yang.

Comparative answer key

CompareZhi Bai Fu ZiZhi Fu Zi
Botanical nameTyphonii Rhizoma preparatum.Aconiti Radix lateralis preparata.
CategoryWarm Herbs that Transform Cold-Phlegm.Herbs that Warm the Interior and Expel Cold.
NatureAcrid and warm.Acrid and extremely hot.
Direction and burnerAscending; upper burner.Mobilizes and warms the lower burner.
Primary emphasisExpels Wind, Dampness and Cold.Warms the Gate of Vitality, disperses Yin Cold-Dampness and restores devastated Yang.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersThe names differ by only one syllable, but one transforms Cold-Phlegm above while the other rescues Yang and warms below.
Memory cueBai Fu Zi rises; Fu Zi fires the lower burner.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), p. 422. Source Exam Key CMID 8952, question 1.
