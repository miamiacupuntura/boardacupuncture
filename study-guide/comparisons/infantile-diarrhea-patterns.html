Pathology or symptom comparison

Infantile Diarrhea Pattern Differentiation

Diagnostic orientation

Core ideaStool quality and systemic signs organize the TCM comparison; hydration status and infectious causes take priority in infants and children.
Exam focusStool odor/color, fever, food relation and chronic Cold-deficiency signs

Comparative differentiation

Pattern or presentationDecisive cluesRepresentative formula/herbStrategy
Food stagnationFoul runny stool after distention/pain, relieved by bowel movement, belching and vomiting undigested foodBao He WanReduce food stagnation.
Damp-HeatYellow foul stool with mucus, abdominal pain, fever, thirst, burning anus and dark scant urineGe Gen Qin Lian TangClear Damp-Heat.
Wind-ColdThin clear foamy non-foul stool with pain, borborygmus, fever/chills and white greasy coatHuo Xiang Zheng Qi SanDispel Wind-Cold.
Spleen-Qi deficiencyPale liquid stool after meals, variable chronic course, poor appetite, weight loss and fatigueShen Ling Bai Zhu SanStrengthen Spleen Qi.
Spleen-Kidney Yang deficiencyProlonged clear stool with undigested food, cold limbs, pallor, listlessness and possible anal prolapseFu Zi Li Zhong Tang + Si Shen WanHistorical strategy: warm Spleen-Kidney Yang.

Study rule

How to distinguishFoul/undigested with relief is food; yellow-burning Heat; clear-foamy with chills Cold; post-meal chronic fatigue Spleen; prolonged watery/cold/undigested Yang deficiency.
Memory cueFoul-food; yellow-burn—Damp-Heat; clear-foam—Cold; after meals—Spleen; prolonged cold—Yang.
SafetyInfants dehydrate quickly. Few wet diapers, dry mouth/no tears, lethargy, sunken eyes, blood, bilious vomiting, severe pain, fever in a young infant or inability to drink requires urgent pediatric care. Aconite formulas are not for home use.
SourceInternal Medicine pediatrics section: Infantile Diarrhea (Xiao Er Fu Xie).
