Si Jun Zi Tang vs. Li Zhong Wan

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueSi Jun Zi TangLi Zhong Wan
Shared clueBoth tonify Spleen Qi and treat fatigue, pale complexion, poor appetite, loose stool and a weak pulse; they share Ren Shen, Bai Zhu and Gan Cao.
Fast distinctionSimple Spleen-Qi deficiency without pronounced Cold.Spleen-Stomach deficiency-Cold with abdominal pain, vomiting or diarrhea and cold limbs.

Comparative answer key

CompareSi Jun Zi TangLi Zhong Wan
Thermal patternNeutral Qi deficiency.Deficiency-Cold of the middle burner.
Distinct ingredientFu Ling drains Dampness and supports Spleen.Gan Jiang warms the middle and expels Cold.
Decisive clueFatigue and weak digestion predominate.Cold pain improved by warmth and pressure predominates.
Exam shortcutTonify Qi.Tonify Qi and warm Cold.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersLi Zhong Wan resembles a warming adaptation of the basic Spleen-Qi tonic strategy. Strong Cold signs—not merely loose stool—justify the switch.
Memory cueSi Jun Zi builds Qi; Li Zhong adds dry ginger heat.
SafetyPersistent vomiting, severe abdominal pain, gastrointestinal bleeding or dehydration requires medical assessment.
