Pattern comparison

Heart Qi Deficiency vs. Heart Yang Deficiency

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueHeart Qi DeficiencyHeart Yang Deficiency
Shared clueBoth cause palpitations, shortness of breath, fatigue, spontaneous sweating and a weak pulse.
Fast distinctionWeak function without strong Cold: symptoms worsen with exertion.Qi deficiency plus Cold and Blood movement failure: chest pain, cold limbs and bluish-purple lips.

Comparative answer key

CompareHeart Qi DeficiencyHeart Yang Deficiency
Thermal signNeutral or mildly cold.Marked internal Cold.
Blood movementWeak circulation without defining stasis.Cold slows Blood, producing chest obstruction/stasis.
Tongue / pulsePale; weak.Pale or purple, wet; deep-weak, possibly knotted.
Formula examplesSheng Mai San when Qi-Yin are injured; individualized Heart-Qi tonics.Gui Zhi Gan Cao Tang or stronger rescue strategies by severity.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersHeart-Yang deficiency adds Cold and impaired Blood movement to Heart-Qi weakness.
Memory cueHeart Qi cannot pump strongly; Heart Yang cannot warm the circulation.
SafetyChest pain, fainting, breathlessness or irregular heartbeat requires urgent cardiac evaluation.

Possible exam questions · Study prompts

  • Which has pronounced Cold?
  • Which may produce purple lips?
