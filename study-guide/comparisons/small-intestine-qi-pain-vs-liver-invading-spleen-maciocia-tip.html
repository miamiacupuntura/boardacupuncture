Small Intestine Qi Pain vs. Liver Qi Invading the Spleen

Use pain location and stool-emotion relationships to distinguish two Qi-driven abdominal patterns.

Fast differentiation clues

ClueSmall Intestine Qi PainLiver Qi Invading Spleen
SharedBoth cause abdominal pain/distention, borborygmi and a wiry pulse, often influenced by stress.
Fast distinctionSmall Intestine focus: twisting lower abdominal pain is the chief sign.Liver–Spleen focus: hypochondriac tension, irritability and alternating constipation/diarrhea stand out.

Clinical table

CompareSmall Intestine Qi PainLiver Qi Invading Spleen
DistributionPrimarily lower abdomen.Hypochondrium plus abdomen and stool regulation.
Formula associationChai Hu Shu Gan Tang.Xiao Yao San.

Exam and safety

Why it mattersRib-side/emotional signs and alternating stool prove Liver–Spleen involvement beyond localized Qi pain.
Memory cueSmall Intestine twists below; Liver–Spleen pulls the sides and changes the stool.
SafetyNew severe pain, fever, blood, persistent vomiting, weight loss or bowel obstruction signs requires medical assessment.

Review prompts
