Xiao Yao San

Liver Qi stagnation with Liver Blood and Spleen Qi deficiency.

High-yield summary

CategoryCourse Liver Qi · Strengthen Spleen · Nourish Blood
PatternLiver Qi stagnation with Liver Blood and Spleen Qi deficiency.
ActionSpreads Liver Qi, nourishes Blood and strengthens the Spleen.
Decisive clueMood-related hypochondriac discomfort, irregular menses, fatigue and poor appetite that fluctuate with stress.

Ingredients

Commercial preparations and lineages may vary; always verify the source being used.
