Herb comparison

Glehniae Radix (bei sha shen) vs. Adenophorae Radix (nan sha shen) vs. Adenophorae Radix recens (xian sha shen)

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueBei Sha ShenNan Sha ShenXian Sha Shen
Shared cluesAll are sweet and cold, clear Heat, nourish Yin, generate fluids, and enter the Lung and Stomach channels.
Fast distinctionHeavier and firmer; strongest for Stomach Yin and intense thirst.Lighter and slightly bitter; better for Lung Yin cough with phlegm.Fresh and very juicy; strongest for clearing Heat and generating fluids.

Comparative answer key

CompareBei Sha ShenNan Sha ShenXian Sha Shen
Medicinal nameGlehniae Radix.Adenophorae Radix.Adenophorae Radix recens.
Common categoryHerbs that Tonify the Yin.
Shared properties and channelsSweet and cold; Lung and Stomach.
Form and textureHeavier and firmer.Lighter, less dense and slightly bitter.Freshly harvested or kept fresh; very juicy.
Primary emphasisMore strongly enriches Yin, especially Stomach Yin.Better expels phlegm while nourishing Lung Yin and cooling Heat.Especially good at clearing Heat and generating fluids.
Decisive indicationFluid injury from warm or febrile disease, or Stomach-Yin deficiency with intense thirst.Chronic cough from Lung-Yin deficiency, particularly with phlegm.Heat with marked fluid injury and thirst.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersThe three varieties share the same foundation, so weight, texture and freshness identify whether Stomach Yin, Lung phlegm-cough or rapid fluid generation is emphasized.
Memory cueBei is heavy for Stomach Yin; Nan is light for Lung phlegm; Xian is fresh for fluids.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), p. 817. Source Exam Key CMID 9058, question 1.

Possible exam questions · Study prompts

  • Which properties, channels and actions do all three share?
  • Which variety most strongly enriches Stomach Yin?
  • Which variety better expels phlegm in chronic Lung-Yin cough?
  • Which fresh variety best clears Heat and generates fluids?
