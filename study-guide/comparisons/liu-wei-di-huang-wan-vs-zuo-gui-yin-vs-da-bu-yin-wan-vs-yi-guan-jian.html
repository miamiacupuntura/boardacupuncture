Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersThe common Yin-deficiency label is incomplete. Determine whether the case is foundational, Essence-depleted, dominated by deficiency Fire or complicated by constrained Liver Qi.
Memory cueLiu Wei balances; Zuo Gui restores; Da Bu Yin cools Fire; Yi Guan nourishes and moves.
SafetyRich Yin tonics may aggravate Dampness or weak digestion. Persistent fever, weight loss, severe pain or gastrointestinal bleeding requires medical evaluation.
