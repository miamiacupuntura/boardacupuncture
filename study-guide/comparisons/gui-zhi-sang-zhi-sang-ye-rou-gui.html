Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersSimilar plant-part names can conceal four different categories. First identify cinnamon versus mulberry, then twig, leaf or bark.
Memory cueCinnamon twig exits; cinnamon bark enters. Mulberry twig drains Wind-Damp; mulberry leaf cools the Exterior.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), p. 356. Source Exam Keys CMID 8963, questions 2 and 7, and CMID 9048, question 1.
