Màn Jīng Zǐ Is Not Chasteberry

Two Vitex species share a genus but belong to different medical traditions and indications.

Memory rule

AnchorMàn Jīng clears the head; chasteberry targets the Western reproductive axis.

Identities

NameIdentityJob
Màn Jīng Zǐ 蔓荆子Vitex trifolia / V. rotundifolia fruitDisperses Wind-Heat and clears head/eyes.
ChasteberryVitex agnus-castus fruitWestern botanical used for selected menstrual/premenstrual indications.

Exam and safety

Exam keyVitex on the label is incomplete—species decides the drug.
SafetyChasteberry may interact with hormonal/dopaminergic therapy; Màn Jīng Zǐ cannot be substituted from a Western supplement bottle.
