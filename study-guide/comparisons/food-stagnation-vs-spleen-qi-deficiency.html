Pattern comparison

Food Stagnation vs. Spleen Qi Deficiency

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueFood StagnationSpleen Qi Deficiency
Shared clueBoth cause poor appetite, abdominal fullness, belching and bowel changes, and chronic Spleen deficiency can permit Food Stagnation.
Fast distinctionAcute excess after overeating: focal fullness/pain, sour belching, foul stool and thick coat.Chronic deficiency: fatigue, soft abdominal weakness, loose stools and pale teeth-marked tongue.

Comparative answer key

CompareFood StagnationSpleen Qi Deficiency
OnsetAcute and linked to food intake.Chronic and constitutional.
AbdomenFull, painful and dislikes pressure.Weak, dull discomfort often improved by pressure.
Tongue / pulseThick greasy coat; slippery/full.Pale, teeth-marked; weak.
Formula examplesBao He Wan.Si Jun Zi Tang; Shen Ling Bai Zhu San.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersBoth reduce appetite, but excess retention is full and foul while deficiency is weak and chronic.
Memory cueFood Stagnation is stuffed; Spleen deficiency is tired.
SafetySevere pain, persistent vomiting, inability to pass stool/gas, blood or major weight loss requires medical evaluation.

Possible exam questions · Study prompts

  • Which follows overeating?
  • Which has sour belching and foul stool?
  • Which has a weak pulse?
