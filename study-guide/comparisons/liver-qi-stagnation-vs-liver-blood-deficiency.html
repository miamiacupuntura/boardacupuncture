Pattern comparison

Liver Qi Stagnation vs. Liver Blood Deficiency

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueLiver Qi StagnationLiver Blood Deficiency
Shared clueBoth can affect menstruation, mood, eyes, sinews and the rib-side because both involve Liver function.
Fast distinctionDistention and movement: irritability, sighing, rib-side fullness and symptoms that fluctuate with emotion.Failure to nourish: dizziness, blurred vision, numbness, cramps, scanty menses and pale signs.

Comparative answer key

CompareLiver Qi StagnationLiver Blood Deficiency
PathomechanismLiver Qi fails to course freely.Liver Blood is insufficient to nourish and anchor.
PainDistending, wandering or emotionally aggravated.Dull discomfort with cramps, numbness or dryness.
Tongue / pulseTongue may be normal or dusky; pulse wiry.Pale tongue; pulse thin or choppy.
Formula examplesChai Hu Shu Gan San; Xiao Yao San when deficiency coexists.Si Wu Tang; Ba Zhen Tang when Qi deficiency coexists.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it matters“Liver” is not enough. Stagnation produces pressure and variability; Blood deficiency produces poor nourishment and pale, dry signs.
Memory cueQi stagnation presses; Blood deficiency fails to feed.
SafetySevere abdominal pain, jaundice, heavy bleeding or pregnancy-related pain requires medical evaluation.
