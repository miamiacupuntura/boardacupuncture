Herb comparison

Olibanum (ru xiang) vs. Myrrha (mo yao)

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueRu XiangMo Yao
Shared cluesBoth invigorate Blood, stop pain, reduce swelling and generate flesh. They are commonly paired so treatment reaches both the Qi and Blood levels.
Fast distinctionWarm and piercingly aromatic; acts more on Qi, more powerfully invigorates Blood and extends the sinews.Neutral and bitter; acts more on Blood, more strongly breaks up Blood and disperses stagnation.

Comparative answer key

CompareRu Xiang (Olibanum)Mo Yao (Myrrha)

Primary levelActs more on the Qi level.Acts more on the Blood level.
Nature and characterWarm and piercingly aromatic.Neutral and bitter.

Western nameFrankincense.Myrrh.

Current formula boundaryNeither herb is a chief or co-chief ingredient in the current 63-formula Board roster.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersThe pair shares trauma- and pain-focused actions, but Qi-level movement and sinew extension point to Ru Xiang, while stronger Blood-level breaking and stasis dispersion point to Mo Yao.
Memory cueRu Xiang reaches Qi and sinews; Mo Yao breaks Blood stasis.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), p. 637. Source Exam Keys CMID 9017 and CMID 9042, questions 1–5.
