Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersThese formulas share a Ban-Xia-centered strategy. The organ target and dominant symptom—not the word “Phlegm” alone—determine the answer.
Memory cueEr Chen coughs; Wen Dan cannot sleep; Ban Xia–Tian Ma spins.
SafetySudden neurologic deficits, severe breathing difficulty, chest pain or altered consciousness requires urgent medical assessment.
