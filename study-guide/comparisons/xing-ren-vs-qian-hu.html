Herb comparison

Armeniacae Semen (xing ren) vs. Peucedani Radix (qian hu)

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueXing RenQian Hu
Shared cluesBoth enter the Lung channel and direct rebellious Lung Qi downward.
Fast distinctionWarm; dispels Wind-Cold and relieves wheezing with copious white Phlegm.Cool; drains Lung Heat and expels Wind-Heat, especially with sticky yellow Phlegm.

Comparative answer key

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersBoth descend rebellious Lung Qi, so temperature and Phlegm color are decisive: warm with white Phlegm points to Xing Ren; cool with sticky yellow Phlegm points to Qian Hu.
Memory cueXing Ren: warm with white Phlegm; Qian Hu: cool with yellow Phlegm.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), p. 377. Source Exam Key CMID 9039, questions 1–2.
