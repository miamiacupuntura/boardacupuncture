Herb comparison

Huang Qin vs. Shi Gao

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueHuang QinShi Gao
Shared clueBoth cool Lung Heat and drain Excess Fire at the Qi level.
Fast distinctionBitter-cold and focused on clearing Heat while drying Dampness.Acrid, sweet and very cold; vents Heat from the muscle layer and permits fluids to regenerate.
Additional clueBlood cooling, bleeding control and pregnancy Heat.Drying Heat in the Lung and Stomach with damaged fluids.

Nature and tasteBitter and cold.Acrid, sweet and very cold.

Primary emphasisClears Heat and dries Dampness.Cools, releases and vents Heat in the muscle layer.
Distinctive actionCools Blood to stop bleeding and quiets the fetus when Heat disturbs pregnancy.Eliminates Heat so fluids can regenerate.
Pattern emphasisDamp-Heat with Lung Heat.Excess Qi-level Heat and drying Heat in the Lung and Stomach.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersLung Heat and Qi-level Excess are shared. Dampness, Blood Heat or pregnancy clues favor Huang Qin; intense drying Heat and fluid regeneration favor Shi Gao.
Memory cueHuang Qin dries Dampness and cools Blood; Shi Gao vents blazing Heat and restores fluids.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), p. 133. Source Exam Key CMID 8982, questions 1–2, and CMID 9074, questions 7–8.
