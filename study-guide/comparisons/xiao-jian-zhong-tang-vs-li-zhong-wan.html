Xiao Jian Zhong Tang vs. Li Zhong Wan

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueXiao Jian Zhong TangLi Zhong Wan
Shared clueBoth warm and strengthen the middle burner and can treat deficiency abdominal pain relieved by warmth and pressure.
Fast distinctionSpasmodic pain from deficiency and disharmony of Yin-Yang/Qi-Blood; palpitations or spontaneous sweating may occur.Spleen-Stomach deficiency-Cold with vomiting, watery diarrhea and loss of appetite.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersBoth improve pain with warmth/pressure. Spasm and nourishment point to Xiao Jian Zhong; dominant vomiting-diarrhea points to Li Zhong.
Memory cueXiao Jian Zhong softens the cramp; Li Zhong warms the digestive center.
SafetySevere, rigid or worsening abdominal pain, fever, bleeding or inability to pass stool/gas requires urgent assessment.
