Yin Chen Hao Tang

Liver/Gallbladder Damp-Heat causing Yang-type jaundice.

High-yield summary

CategoryClear Damp-Heat · Treat Yang Jaundice
PatternLiver/Gallbladder Damp-Heat causing Yang-type jaundice.
ActionClears Heat, drains Dampness, promotes urination and relieves jaundice.
Decisive clueBright yellow skin/eyes with fever or irritability, thirst, abdominal fullness and scant dark urine.

Ingredients

Yin Chen Hao, Zhi Zi and Da Huang.

Commercial preparations and lineages may vary; always verify the source being used.

Differentiation and memory

Do not confuseYin Chen Si Ni Tang treats Yin jaundice from Cold; Yin Chen Hao Tang treats bright Yang jaundice from Damp-Heat.
Memory cueYin Chen targets jaundice, Zhi Zi drains Heat and Da Huang pushes Damp-Heat out.

Safety

Any new jaundice requires urgent medical evaluation for liver, biliary, pancreatic, blood or medication-related disease.
