Tao He Cheng Qi Tang vs. Wen Jing Tang vs. Fu Yuan Huo Xue Tang

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueTao He Cheng Qi TangWen Jing TangFu Yuan Huo Xue Tang
Shared clueAll invigorate Blood and reduce stasis, but they treat different causes and clinical settings.
Fast distinctionLower-burner Blood stasis with Heat: severe lower abdominal pain, constipation, agitation and normal urination.Cold and deficiency obstruct Chong-Ren: irregular menses, infertility or chronic uterine bleeding with cold lower abdomen.Traumatic Blood stasis: severe fixed rib-side, chest or flank pain after injury.

Decisive clueLower abdominal stasis with normal urination.Menstrual Cold-deficiency pattern.Clear history of trauma.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersThe presence of stasis is shared. Temperature and context—Heat, uterine Cold-deficiency or trauma—determine the formula.
Memory cueTao He purges hot stasis; Wen Jing warms menstrual stasis; Fu Yuan restores after trauma.
SafetyThese formulas are not for self-treatment during pregnancy or active bleeding. Classical Fu Yuan Huo Xue Tang used Chuan Shan Jia (pangolin), which is prohibited/protected and requires an ethical legal substitute.
SourceSource bank: FWB 10.1.1, Comparison Formulas to Invigorate the Blood, activity 1; corroborated with individual formula profiles.
