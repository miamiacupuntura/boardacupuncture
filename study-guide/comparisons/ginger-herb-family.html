Family 5 of 20

Ginger 姜 — One Root, Four Temperatures

Fresh runs out, dried stays home, blast-fried plugs leaks and peel drains swelling.

One picture, one rule

RuleProcessing changes direction: surface → interior heat → hemostatic warmth; peel goes to water.

The part decides the job

MedicinalPart / identityJob
Shēng Jiāng 生姜Fresh rhizomeReleases Exterior, warms Middle, stops vomiting and reduces toxicity.
Gān Jiāng 干姜Dried rhizomeWarms Middle, rescues Yang and warms Lung thin fluids.
Pào Jiāng 炮姜Blast-fried/charred rhizomeWarms channels and stops Cold-type bleeding or diarrhea.
Shēng Jiāng Pí 生姜皮Fresh peelPromotes urination and reduces edema.

Exam and safety

Exam keyFresh = Exterior/vomit; dried = Interior/Yang; charred = bleeding; peel = edema.
SafetyGān Jiāng/Pào Jiāng require caution in pregnancy and in Yin deficiency Heat, hot bleeding or warm-febrile disease.
