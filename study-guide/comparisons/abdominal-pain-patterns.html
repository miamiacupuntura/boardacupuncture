Pathology or symptom comparison

Abdominal Pain Pattern Differentiation

Diagnostic orientation

Core ideaAsk whether warmth or pressure helps, whether pain moves or stays fixed, and whether defecation relieves it.
Exam focusPressure, temperature, location and bowel response

Comparative differentiation

Pattern or presentationDecisive cluesRepresentative formula/herbStrategy
Cold obstructionSudden pain worse with cold and better with warmth; no thirstLiang Fu WanWarm the Middle, scatter Cold and move Qi.
Damp-Heat obstructionPain worse with pressure, distention, thirst, dark urine and yellow greasy coatDa Cheng Qi TangDrain stool and clear Damp-Heat when excess is confirmed.
Spleen-Yang deficiencyDull periodic pain better with warmth/pressure; loose stools and fatigueXiao Jian Zhong TangWarm and supplement the Middle.
Food StagnationPain/distention after food, foul belching, acid regurgitation and relief after stoolBao He Wan; Zhi Shi Dao Zhi Wan if severeReduce food and guide out stagnation.
Liver-Qi stagnationMoving distending pain triggered by anger and relieved by gas or belchingChai Hu Shu Gan SanSoothe Liver and regulate Qi.
Blood stasisSevere fixed pain with a purple tongueShao Fu Zhu Yu Tang when lower-abdominal Cold stasis fitsInvigorate Blood and dispel stasis.

Study rule

How to distinguishBetter with warmth suggests Cold; better with pressure suggests deficiency; worse with pressure suggests excess; moving pain is Qi, fixed stabbing pain is Blood.
Memory cueWarmth—Cold; pressure helps—deficiency; stool helps—food; moves—Qi; fixed—Blood.
SafetyRigid abdomen, rebound tenderness, persistent vomiting, gastrointestinal bleeding, fainting, pregnancy with severe pain, or no stool/gas requires urgent evaluation.
