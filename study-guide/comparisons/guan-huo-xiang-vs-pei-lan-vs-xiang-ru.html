Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersThe Summerheat-Dampness presentation is shared. The decisive clue is nausea, foul oral signs, or the strongest Exterior and urinary action.
Memory cueHuo Xiang stops nausea; Pei Lan clears foulness; Xiang Ru releases and urinates.
