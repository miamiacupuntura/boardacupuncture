Exam boundaries

Shí Zǎo Tāng illustrates the Gān Cǎo rule: it combines Gān Suì, Dà Jǐ and Yuán Huā but uses Dà Zǎo rather than licorice. For a standard pairing question, avoid every listed combination and verify exact characters when pinyin sounds similar.
