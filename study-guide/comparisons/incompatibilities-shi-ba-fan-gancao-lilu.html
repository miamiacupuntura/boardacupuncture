Chapter 6 of 7

Shí Bā Fǎn: Gān Cǎo & Lí Lú

The remaining two traditional groups: licorice and Veratrum.

Gān Cǎo group

BossTraditionally incompatible withMemory
Gān CǎoGān Suì · Dà Jǐ (大戟) · Yuán Huā · Hǎi ZǎoThe peacemaker will not sit with three harsh water-expellers and seaweed.

Name trap: Dà Jǐ 大戟 (Euphorbia/Knoxia, harsh water-expeller) is not Dà Jì 大蓟 (Cirsium, stops bleeding).

Lí Lú group

BossTraditionally incompatible withMemory
Lí LúRén Shēn · Dǎng Shēn · Shā Shēn · Dān Shēn · Xuán Shēn · Kǔ Shēn · Xī Yáng Shēn · Tài Zǐ Shēn · Xì Xīn · Bái Sháo · Chì Sháo“Lí Lú rejects the Shēns, Xì Xīn and Sháo Yào.” Lists vary in how broadly they expand the Shēn and peony names.

Exam boundaries

Shí Zǎo Tāng illustrates the Gān Cǎo rule: it combines Gān Suì, Dà Jǐ and Yuán Huā but uses Dà Zǎo rather than licorice. For a standard pairing question, avoid every listed combination and verify exact characters when pinyin sounds similar.
