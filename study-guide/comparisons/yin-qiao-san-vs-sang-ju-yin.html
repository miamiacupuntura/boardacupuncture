Formula comparison

Yin Qiao San vs. Sang Ju Yin

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueYin Qiao SanSang Ju Yin
Shared clueBoth treat the early stage of Wind-Heat or warm disease with fever, mild aversion to wind, thirst and a floating-rapid pulse.
Fast distinctionStronger Exterior Heat and toxicity: sore throat is prominent.Milder Wind-Heat attacking the Lung: cough is prominent.

Comparative answer key

CompareYin Qiao SanSang Ju Yin
Primary emphasisReleases Wind-Heat and clears toxic Heat.Disperses Wind-Heat and gently clears Lung Heat to stop cough.
Decisive clueSore throat, stronger fever and thirst.Cough with relatively mild systemic signs.
TargetWei level with throat/upper-burner Heat.Lung-centered Wind-Heat.
Exam shortcutThroat first.Cough first.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersBoth are early Wind-Heat formulas. The symptom that dominates—throat pain or cough—usually decides the answer.
Memory cueYin Qiao clears the throat; Sang Ju quiets the cough.
SafetyHigh fever, breathing difficulty, dehydration or rapidly worsening symptoms require medical assessment.
SourceSource bank: FWB 1.2, Comparison Formulas to Release the Exterior; corroborated with Classic Formulas profiles and formula chapters.
