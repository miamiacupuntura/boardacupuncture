Formula comparison

Ding Chuan Tang vs. Ma Xing Shi Gan Tang

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueDing Chuan TangMa Xing Shi Gan Tang
Shared clueBoth disseminate and descend Lung Qi to relieve cough and wheezing, and both contain Ma Huang, Xing Ren and Gan Cao.
Fast distinctionWind-Cold constrains the Exterior while Phlegm-Heat obstructs the Lungs; copious thick sputum is prominent.Lung Heat is more intense; fever, thirst and heat signs dominate, with cough and wheezing.

Comparative answer key

CompareDing Chuan TangMa Xing Shi Gan Tang
Core patternExternal Wind-Cold with internal Phlegm-Heat.Lung Heat with an unresolved Exterior component.
Phlegm clueCopious, thick and often yellow sputum.Phlegm may occur, but strong Lung Heat is the central clue.
Heat clueHeat is present but embedded in a mixed Cold/Heat pattern.High fever, thirst, sweating and a strong rapid pulse may dominate.
Therapeutic emphasisArrests wheezing and clears Phlegm-Heat while releasing constraint.Strongly clears Lung Heat while facilitating Lung-Qi diffusion.
Exam shortcutThink “wheeze + abundant Phlegm.”Think “wheeze + blazing Lung Heat.”

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersBoth can present with wheezing and Heat, but Ding Chuan Tang is more Phlegm-centered and mixed; Ma Xing Shi Gan Tang is more Heat-centered.
Memory cueDing Chuan = dense Phlegm; Ma Xing Shi Gan = Shi Gao-level Lung Heat.
SafetyBoth contain Ma Huang. Cardiovascular disease, hypertension, stimulant use and relevant medications require professional screening.
SourceSource bank: FWB 6.2.1, Comparison Formulas to Transform Phlegm; corroborated with the Classic Formulas profiles and formula practice tests.
