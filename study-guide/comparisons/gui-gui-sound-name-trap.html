Trap 21 of 22

Guī 归, Guī 龟 and Guì 桂

Near-identical sounds encode “return,” tortoise shell and cinnamon.

Memory rule

Anchor归 returns; 龟 is tortoise; fourth-tone 桂 is cinnamon.

Correct identity

NameIdentityJob
Guī 归“Return” characterAppears in Dāng Guī and formula names such as Guī Pí Tāng or Zuǒ/Yòu Guī Wán.
Guī 龟TortoiseGuī Bǎn nourishes Yin and anchors Yang.
Guì 桂CinnamonGuì Zhī releases the Exterior; Ròu Guì warms the Interior/Mìngmén.

Exam and safety

Exam keyCharacters and tone decide whether the question concerns Blood/formula naming, shell, twig or bark.
Safety alertGuì Zhī and Ròu Guì are not interchangeable forms; Guī Bǎn is animal-derived and regulated differently.
