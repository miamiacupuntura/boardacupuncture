Xiāng Wèi vs. Xiāng Shā

One useful detoxifying relationship, named from opposite ends.

The same pair, two viewpoints

Xiāng Wèi 相畏Named from the toxic herb: Bàn Xià fears Shēng Jiāng because ginger reduces its toxicity.
Xiāng Shā 相杀Named from the protector: Shēng Jiāng suppresses Bàn Xià toxicity.

Do not confuse the two Wèi terms

TermMeaningExam answer
Xiāng Wèi 相畏A harsh or toxic substance is tamed by another.Beneficial/intentional relationship.
Shí Jiǔ Wèi 十九畏The traditional “Nineteen Antagonisms” list.Avoid the listed combinations.

Clinical boundary

A traditional mitigating pair is not permission to use raw, incorrectly identified or improperly processed toxic material. Required processing and current safety standards still control practice.
