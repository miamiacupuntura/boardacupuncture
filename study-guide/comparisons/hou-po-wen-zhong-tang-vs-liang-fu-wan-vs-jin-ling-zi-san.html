Cold-versus-Heat pain · FWB Regulate Qi

Hou Po Wen Zhong Tang vs. Liang Fu Wan vs. Jin Ling Zi San

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueHou Po Wen Zhong TangLiang Fu WanJin Ling Zi San
Shared clueAll move Qi and relieve epigastric or abdominal pain, but temperature and pathogenic combination differ.
Fast distinctionDamp-Cold and Qi stagnation fill the middle burner: distention, heaviness, nausea and a thick greasy white coat.Liver-Qi stagnation invades a Cold Stomach: epigastric pain linked to stress and improved by warmth.Liver-Qi stagnation transforms into Heat: burning or sharp rib-side/epigastric pain with irritability and bitter taste.

Comparative answer key

CompareHou Po Wen Zhong TangLiang Fu WanJin Ling Zi San
Thermal patternDamp-Cold.Cold.Heat.
Primary mechanismCold-Damp obstructs middle-burner Qi.Liver Qi invades Stomach while Cold contracts.Constrained Liver Qi generates Heat and pain.
Key ingredientsHou Po, Cao Dou Kou, Chen Pi, Mu Xiang, Fu Ling and Gan Jiang.Gao Liang Jiang and Xiang Fu.Chuan Lian Zi and Yan Hu Suo.
Decisive clueFullness and heaviness with greasy coat.Pain improves with warmth.Pain has Heat signs and irritability.
Exam shortcutWet-cold center.Cold pain from Liver-Stomach.Hot pain from constrained Liver Qi.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersPain location may overlap. The coat, response to warmth and presence of Heat distinguish Damp-Cold obstruction, Cold contraction and Heat from constraint.
Memory cueHou Po warms damp fullness; Liang Fu warms cold pain; Jin Ling Zi clears hot pain.
SafetySevere persistent abdominal pain, guarding, fever, vomiting, jaundice or gastrointestinal bleeding requires medical evaluation.
