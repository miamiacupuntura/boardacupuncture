Blood Deficiency vs. Blood Stasis

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueBlood DeficiencyBlood Stasis
Shared clueBoth can affect menstruation, cause pain and produce a choppy pulse, and deficiency may eventually contribute to stasis.
Fast distinctionNot enough Blood: pallor, dizziness, blurred vision, numbness, cramps and scanty flow.Blood does not move: fixed stabbing pain, dark clots, masses and purple discoloration.

Comparative answer key

CompareBlood DeficiencyBlood Stasis
Primary problemQuantity and nourishment.Circulation and obstruction.
PainDull, cramping or associated with weakness.Fixed, sharp, stabbing and often worse at night.
Tongue / pulsePale; thin or choppy.Purple/dusky with spots; wiry or choppy.
Formula examplesSi Wu Tang; Ba Zhen Tang.Xue Fu Zhu Yu Tang; Tao He Cheng Qi Tang by location/pattern.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersBoth involve Blood, but deficiency lacks substance while stasis lacks movement.
Memory cueDeficiency is pale and empty; stasis is purple and fixed.
SafetySudden severe pain, chest pain, one-sided swelling, pregnancy bleeding or heavy bleeding requires urgent evaluation.
