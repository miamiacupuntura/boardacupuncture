Kidney–Lung Yin vs. Lung Yin Deficiency

Separate a dry Lung pattern from combined upper and lower Yin depletion.

Fast differentiation clues

ClueLung Yin DeficiencyKidney–Lung Yin Deficiency
SharedBoth cause dry cough, dry throat, scant sputum, night sweating and a red tongue with little or no coat.
Fast distinctionLung-centered: hoarse voice, dry cough and possible blood-streaked sputum predominate.Combined depletion: the Lung signs plus dizziness, tinnitus, weak low back/knees and deeper constitutional deficiency.

Clinical table

CompareLung Yin DeficiencyKidney–Lung Yin Deficiency
Added systemLung dryness and Empty Heat.Kidney root fails to support Lung Yin.
Formula associationBai He Gu Jin Tang by presentation.Ba Xian Chang Shou Wan, Bai He Gu Jin Tang or Mai Wei Di Huang Wan in the supplied table.

Exam and safety

Why it mattersTinnitus and low-back/knee weakness prove the Kidney has joined the dry Lung picture.
Memory cueLung Yin dries the cough; Kidney–Lung Yin also weakens the root.
SafetyCoughing blood, breathlessness at rest, weight loss, fever or low oxygen requires prompt medical assessment.
