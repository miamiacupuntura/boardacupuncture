Xiǎo vs. Dà Jiàn Zhōng Tāng

Both warm and build the Middle, but severity and architecture are very different.

Memory rule

AnchorXiǎo is a sweet Guì Zhī family cramp formula; Dà is fierce Interior Cold with moving lumps.

Decisive separation

FormulaArchitectureDecisive presentation
Xiǎo Jiàn Zhōng Tāng 小建中汤Yí Táng + Guì Zhī Tāng with doubled Bái SháoDeficiency cramping eased by warmth and pressure.
