How the pair works

HerbRole within the pair
Rǔ XiāngMoves Qi and Blood through the channels and relaxes sinew tension.
Mò YàoBreaks Blood stasis more directly and reduces fixed swelling and pain.

Exam focus and safety

CluesTrauma, painful sores, chest or abdominal pain and painful obstruction with swelling.
Signature formulaXiǎo Huó Luò Dān.
TrapRǔ Xiāng leans slightly more to moving Qi; Mò Yào slightly more to breaking Blood stasis.
PreparationUse processed resins when prescribed to reduce stickiness, smoke and gastrointestinal irritation.
SafetyContraindicated in pregnancy; screen anticoagulants and bleeding disorders. Both can cause nausea and should not replace evaluation of chest or severe abdominal pain.
