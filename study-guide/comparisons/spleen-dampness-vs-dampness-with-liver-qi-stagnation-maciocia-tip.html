Spleen Dampness vs. Dampness with Liver Qi Stagnation

Recognize when a heavy Spleen-Damp picture also carries rib-side pain and emotional constraint.

Fast differentiation clues

ClueSpleen DampnessSpleen Dampness with Liver Qi Stagnation
SharedBoth cause epigastric fullness, heaviness, poor appetite and a thick sticky coating.
Fast distinctionDampness alone: digestive heaviness and nausea dominate without clear emotional or rib-side pattern.Added Liver constraint: hypochondriac pain, sighing, irritability and symptoms worsening with stress.

Exam and safety

Why it mattersRib-side pain and emotional variability prove Liver involvement beyond Dampness alone.
Memory cueDampness weighs the middle; Liver constraint pulls the sides.
SafetyJaundice, fever, persistent vomiting, severe pain or weight loss requires medical evaluation.
