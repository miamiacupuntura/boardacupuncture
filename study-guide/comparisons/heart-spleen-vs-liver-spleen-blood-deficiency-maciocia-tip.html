Heart–Spleen vs. Liver–Spleen Blood Deficiency

Use Spirit signs versus eye, tendon and menstrual signs to identify the second Blood-dependent organ.

Fast differentiation clues

ClueHeart–Spleen DeficiencyLiver–Spleen Blood Deficiency
SharedBoth combine Spleen weakness with pallor, fatigue, scanty menstruation and a thin weak pulse.
Fast distinctionHeart signs: palpitations, insomnia, anxiety and poor memory; bruising may occur.Liver signs: blurred vision, dry eyes, cramps, numbness and pale tongue sides.

CompareHeart–Spleen DeficiencyLiver–Spleen Blood Deficiency

Exam and safety

Why it mattersShared Spleen signs cannot decide; Heart affects Shen while Liver affects eyes and sinews.
Memory cueHeart loses sleep; Liver loses sight and suppleness.
SafetyPalpitations, anemia, heavy bleeding or visual change requires biomedical assessment.
