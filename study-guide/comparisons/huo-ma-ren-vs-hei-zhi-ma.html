Herb-name comparison

Huo Ma Ren vs. Hu Ma Ren (Hei Zhi Ma)

Exam trap · One letter changes the herb

QuestionWhich name identifies Cannabis Semen, and which identifies black sesame, Sesami Semen nigrum?
Correct answerHuo Ma Ren = Cannabis Semen. Hu Ma Ren = alternate name for Hei Zhi Ma, Sesami Semen nigrum.
Do not confuseHuo contains an extra “o.” That one letter changes the medicinal substance, category, channels and functions.

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueHuo Ma RenHu Ma Ren (Hei Zhi Ma)
Shared clueBoth are neutral, sweet, oil-rich seeds used to moisten Intestines in constipation.
Fast distinctionHuo Ma Ren: Cannabis seed and Downward-Draining Herb.Hu Ma Ren: alternate name for black sesame and Yin-tonifying herb.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersThe names differ by only one letter, but the herbs, channels and categories are different. Huo Ma Ren is Cannabis seed for downward-draining moistening; Hu Ma Ren is black sesame for Liver-Kidney Yin, Essence and Blood.
Memory cueHuo = hemp/Cannabis; Hu = black sesame.
