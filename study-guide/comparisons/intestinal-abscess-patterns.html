Pathology or symptom comparison

Intestinal Abscess Stage Comparison

Diagnostic orientation

Core ideaThis historical category overlaps appendicitis and intra-abdominal abscess. Stage recognition is educational; suspected cases require urgent surgical assessment.
Exam focusRight-lower abdominal pain, fever, rebound tenderness and systemic toxicity

Comparative differentiation

Pattern or presentationDecisive cluesRepresentative formula/herbStrategy
Initial Qi-Blood stasis with Damp-HeatRight-lower pain worse extending the leg, fever, nausea, constipation, dark urine and yellow sticky coatDa Huang Mu Dan Pi Tang or Hong Teng Jian JiMove Qi-Blood, clear Damp-Heat and free stool.
Blood stasis with Cold-DampMilder abdominal pain without fever, loose stool, clear urine and white sticky coatHuo Xiang Zheng Qi San + Hong Teng Jian JiDispel Cold-Damp and move Blood.
Pyogenic stageSevere localized pain, marked/rebound tenderness, guarding, palpable mass, high fever and vomitingHong Teng Jian JiHistorical strategy: clear toxin and discharge pus.
Rupture with Heat toxinDiffuse severe pain, rigid/tender distended abdomen, persistent high fever, vomiting, thirst and dehydrationDa Huang Mu Dan Pi Tang + Zeng Ye TangHistorical strategy: clear Heat and support fluids.
Rupture with Cold-Damp collapse signsAbdominal pain, listlessness, cold limbs, spontaneous sweat and deep-thready pulseYi Yi Fu Zi Bai Jiang SanHistorical strategy: warm Yang and drain Dampness.

Study rule

How to distinguishLocalized right-lower pain begins the sequence; rebound/guarding and high fever indicate abscess; diffuse rigid pain or cold collapse indicates rupture and emergency.
Memory cueLocalized—suspect; rebound/fever—pus; diffuse rigid or cold collapse—rupture.
SafetyRight-lower abdominal pain with fever, rebound, guarding, vomiting, rigid abdomen or worsening pain requires emergency evaluation. Do not give purgatives or delay imaging/surgery.
