Formula comparison

Gui Pi Tang vs. Ba Zhen Tang

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueGui Pi TangBa Zhen Tang
Shared clueBoth tonify Qi and nourish Blood and may treat fatigue, pale complexion, dizziness, reduced appetite and a weak pulse.
Fast distinctionSpleen Qi fails to produce/contain Blood and Heart Blood fails to nourish the Spirit: insomnia, palpitations, poor memory or bleeding.Generalized Qi-and-Blood deficiency without a defining Spirit or bleeding presentation.

Comparative answer key

CompareGui Pi TangBa Zhen Tang
Primary focusHeart and Spleen.Qi and Blood systemically.
Distinct actionCalms the Spirit and helps Spleen contain Blood.Combines the core four-herb Qi and four-herb Blood tonic strategies.
Decisive clueInsomnia/palpitations or chronic bleeding with Spleen deficiency.Broad fatigue, pallor, dizziness and weakness.
Exam shortcutQi-Blood deficiency with Heart/bleeding consequences.Basic combined Qi-and-Blood deficiency.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersGui Pi Tang is more targeted than a general tonic: it links Spleen failure to Heart-Blood and containment symptoms.
Memory cueGui Pi returns Blood to Heart and vessels; Ba Zhen builds all eight.
SafetyHeavy bleeding, fainting, chest pain, severe palpitations or new neurologic symptoms requires urgent assessment.
SourceSource bank: FWB 8.2.2, Comparison Formulas to Tonify; corroborated with individual formula profiles.
