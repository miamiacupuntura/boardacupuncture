Xue Fu Zhu Yu Tang vs. Shao Fu Zhu Yu Tang vs. Shen Tong Zhu Yu Tang

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueXue Fu Zhu Yu TangShao Fu Zhu Yu TangShen Tong Zhu Yu Tang
Shared clueAll move Blood and Qi to relieve fixed pain from Blood stasis. Location and Cold are the decisive clues.
Fast distinctionBlood stasis in the chest and upper burner.Blood stasis with Cold in the lower abdomen and uterus.Blood stasis obstructing channels and collaterals with persistent body or joint pain.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersThe shared phrase “Qi and Blood stasis” does not choose the answer. The formula names map where the stasis is lodged.
Memory cueXue Fu = chest; Shao Fu = cold lower abdomen; Shen Tong = painful body.
SafetyBlood-moving formulas require caution or avoidance in pregnancy, active bleeding and anticoagulant use. New chest pain, neurologic deficits or severe pelvic pain requires urgent assessment.
