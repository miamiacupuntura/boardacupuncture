Herb comparison

Hai Zao vs. Ge Qiao

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueHai ZaoGe Qiao
Shared clueBoth are salty and cold, soften hardness, reduce Phlegm and move fluids to reduce edema.
Fast distinctionLiver and clumps: goiter, testicular swelling and enlarged liver or spleen.Lungs and descent: cough and chest pain from Phlegm-Fire.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersThe shared salty-cold actions can obscure the channel distinction: Liver clumps and swellings favor Hai Zao, while Lung Phlegm-Fire with cough or chest pain favors Ge Qiao.
Memory cueHai Zao handles Liver clumps; Ge Qiao guides Lung Phlegm down.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), p. 404. Source Exam Key CMID 8968, questions 2–4.

  • Which substance better descends Phlegm-Heat in cough and chest pain?
