Herb comparison

Notoginseng Radix (san qi) vs. Typhae Pollen (pu huang) vs. Rubiae Radix (qian cao)

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueSan QiPu HuangQian Cao
Shared cluesAll three stop bleeding and remove Blood stasis. They are useful when bleeding is caused by stasis or when bleeding must be stopped without leaving residual stasis.
Fast distinctionStops pain and reduces swelling.Promotes urination.Cools the Blood and regulates menstruation.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersAll three stop bleeding without trapping stasis, so the secondary action—pain and swelling, urination, or Blood cooling and menstruation—is the decisive exam clue.
Memory cueSan Qi: pain and swelling; Pu Huang: urination; Qian Cao: cool Blood and regulate menstruation.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), p. 591. Source Exam Key CMID 9051, question 1.
