Theory 6 of 12

Dose Architecture & Formula Size

Read relative dose, potency and formula size as part of the treatment strategy.

Core rule

RuleDose is relational: product concentration, role, formula size, patient and treatment phase all matter.

What changes dose

ConceptWhat it meansApplication
Formula roleChief ingredients usually carry more strategic weight; envoys are commonly smaller.Do not infer role from dose alone.
Formula sizeA small formula can give each ingredient a larger share; a large formula distributes the total across more jobs.Compare proportions, not just raw grams.
Material and preparationCrude decoction, granule, extract, powder and pill are not converted by one universal ratio.Use manufacturer/pharmacopoeial specifications for the exact product.
Potency and toxicityLight aromatics, minerals, concentrated extracts and narrow-therapeutic-index herbs follow different rules.Never extrapolate a “typical herb dose” to a toxic substance.
Treatment phaseAcute excess, recovery and chronic maintenance require different intensity.Reduce, stop or reformulate when the target changes.

Safe interpretation

ConceptWhat it meansApplication
Historical rangesTextbook ranges describe a tradition and dosage form, not a patient-specific prescription.Do not turn study ranges into self-treatment instructions.
RatiosChanging a pair ratio can change temperature, direction and the formula’s clinical identity.Study ratios as therapeutic emphasis.
DocumentationRecord product, concentration, preparation, amount, frequency and duration.Without product identity a dose cannot be meaningfully reviewed.

Responsible use

Educational summary for formula analysis and exam preparation. Doses, toxic preparations, interactions and clinical decisions require current references and professional supervision.
