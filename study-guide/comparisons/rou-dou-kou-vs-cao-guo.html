Herb comparison

Myristicae Semen (rou dou kou) vs. Tsaoko Fructus (cao guo)

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueRou Dou KouCao Guo
Shared cluesBoth have an acrid, warming character and address Cold-Dampness with impaired Qi movement.
Decisive clueGreater astringency, chronic Large Intestine leakage, Spleen-Kidney warming or Si Shen Wan.Middle-burner focus: expels Cold, dries Dampness, resolves clumping and regulates the Spleen and Stomach.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersThe herbs share warming and Qi-moving qualities, but only Rou Dou Kou has the strong astringent, leakage-stabilizing clue.
Common confusionDo not select Cao Guo for Si Shen Wan or chronic intestinal leakage; those clues belong to Rou Dou Kou.
Source-data noteThe exam title places Cao Guo under “Aromatic Herbs that Transform Dampness,” while the keyed answer calls it “Herbs that Relieve Food Stagnation.” This note preserves that discrepancy rather than silently changing the source.
How it may appearAstringency, Large Intestine leakage or Spleen-Kidney warming favors Rou Dou Kou. A specific middle-burner Cold-Damp and clumping presentation favors Cao Guo.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), p. 869. Source Exam Key CMID 8906.
