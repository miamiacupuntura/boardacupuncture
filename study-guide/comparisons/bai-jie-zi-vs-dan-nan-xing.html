Herb comparison

Sinapis Semen (bai jie zi) vs. Arisaema cum Bile (dan nan xing)

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueBai Jie ZiDan Nan Xing
Shared clueBoth mobilize the channels to eliminate Phlegm.
Fast distinctionCold-Phlegm with joint pain, limited movement or Yin flat abscesses.Wind-Phlegm with spasms, cramps or hemiplegia.

Comparative answer key

CompareBai Jie Zi (Sinapis Semen)Dan Nan Xing (Arisaema cum Bile)
CategoryWarm Herbs that Transform Cold-Phlegm.Herbs that Cool and Transform Phlegm-Heat.
Shared actionMobilize the channels and eliminate Phlegm.
Primary emphasisPromotes Qi flow and eliminates pain from clumped Cold-Phlegm.Extinguishes Wind and eradicates Phlegm.
Distinctive indicationsJoint pain, limited movement and Yin flat abscesses.Spasms, cramps and hemiplegia caused by Wind-Phlegm.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersBoth unblock channels through Phlegm treatment, but the Cold-versus-Heat and pain-versus-Wind clues separate them.
Common confusionCold-Phlegm joint pain favors Bai Jie Zi; Wind-Phlegm neurological signs favor Dan Nan Xing.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), p. 425. Source Exam Key CMID 8943.
