Xue Fu Zhu Yu Tang

Blood stasis with Qi stagnation in the chest.

High-yield summary

CategoryInvigorate Blood in the Chest · Move Qi
PatternBlood stasis with Qi stagnation in the chest.
ActionInvigorates Blood, dispels stasis, moves Qi and relieves chest pain.
Decisive clueFixed stabbing chest pain, chronic headache or insomnia with dark lips/tongue and a choppy or wiry pulse.

Ingredients

Tao Ren, Hong Hua, Dang Gui, Sheng Di Huang, Chuan Xiong, Chi Shao, Niu Xi, Jie Geng, Chai Hu, Zhi Ke and Gan Cao.

Commercial preparations and lineages may vary; always verify the source being used.

Differentiation and memory

Do not confuseDan Shen Yin is a focused three-herb treatment for chest/epigastric Blood stasis; Xue Fu Zhu Yu Tang is broader and also redirects Qi and Blood through the chest.
Memory cueDrive stasis from the mansion of Blood: Jie Geng lifts to chest while Niu Xi draws downward.

Safety

Chest pain may be cardiac and requires emergency evaluation. Avoid unsupervised use with pregnancy, active bleeding or anticoagulants.
