Pathology or symptom comparison

Mania and Withdrawal Pattern Differentiation

Diagnostic orientation

Core ideaTempo and behavior lead: gradual dull withdrawal suggests a depressive branch, while abrupt destructive agitation suggests manic Phlegm-Fire.
Exam focusGradual withdrawal versus abrupt agitation, then Heat versus deficiency

Comparative differentiation

Pattern or presentationDecisive cluesRepresentative formula/herbStrategy
Qi stagnation with PhlegmGradual withdrawal, dull senses, incoherent self-talk, sadness and white sticky coatShun Qi Dao Tan TangRectify Qi, transform Phlegm and open orifices.
Heart-Spleen Qi deficiencyLong duration, trance-like withdrawal, palpitations, fright, fatigue, poor appetite and pale tongueYang Xin Tang + Gan Mai Da Zao TangStrengthen Spleen, nourish Heart and quiet Spirit.
Phlegm-Fire harasses the interiorAbrupt severe agitation, aggression, insomnia, red face/eyes and yellow sticky coatSheng Tie Luo YinSettle Heart, transform Phlegm and drain Fire.
Exuberant Fire injures YinProlonged illness with irritability, fright, insomnia, emaciation and red scant-coated tongueEr Yin JianNourish Yin, downbear Fire and stabilize emotions.

Study rule

How to distinguishGradual-sticky withdrawal is Qi-Phlegm; pale-tired withdrawal is deficiency; abrupt red-yellow aggression is Phlegm-Fire; chronic thin-red agitation is injured Yin.
Memory cueWithdraw + sticky—Phlegm; withdraw + pale—deficiency; erupt + red—Fire; chronic + thin—Yin.
SafetyNew psychosis, mania, aggression, inability to sleep/eat, severe disorganization or danger to self/others needs urgent psychiatric and medical assessment.
