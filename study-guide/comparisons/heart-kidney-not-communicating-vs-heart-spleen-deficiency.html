Pattern comparison

Heart–Kidney Not Communicating vs. Heart–Spleen Deficiency

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueHeart–Kidney Not CommunicatingHeart–Spleen Deficiency
Shared clueBoth cause insomnia, palpitations, anxiety, poor memory and fatigue.
Fast distinctionUpper Fire/lower Yin: restlessness, night sweating, five-center Heat, tinnitus, weak low back and red peeled tongue.Qi-Blood deficiency: poor appetite, loose stool, pale complexion, dizziness, easy bruising and pale tongue.

Comparative answer key

CompareHeart–Kidney Not CommunicatingHeart–Spleen Deficiency
Root mechanismKidney Yin fails to restrain Heart Fire.Spleen Qi fails to produce/hold Heart Blood.
Thermal/color clueRed, dry and warm at night.Pale, weak and digestive.
PulseThin-rapid, sometimes floating-empty.Weak and thin.
Formula examplesJiao Tai Wan or Tian Wang Bu Xin Dan by depth.Gui Pi Tang.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersInsomnia and palpitations overlap. Tinnitus/low back plus Empty Heat identify Heart-Kidney; digestive weakness and pallor identify Heart-Spleen.
Memory cueHeart-Kidney is red and restless; Heart-Spleen is pale and depleted.
SafetyChest pain, fainting, sustained arrhythmia, suicidal thoughts, mania or severe prolonged insomnia requires prompt medical assessment.

Possible exam questions · Study prompts

  • Which has tinnitus and night sweats?
  • Which has poor appetite and bruising?
  • How do tongues differ?
