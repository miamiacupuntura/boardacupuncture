Herb comparison

Jin Yin Hua vs. Lian Qiao

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueJin Yin HuaLian Qiao
Shared clueBoth are cooling, resolve toxicity, guide Heat to the surface and cool internal Heat; they are often combined.
Fast distinctionSweeter and more focused on dispersing surface Heat, with less injury to the Stomach.Stronger at cooling Heart Fire; also disperses Qi stagnation at the Blood level and treats painful urinary dribbling.

Comparative answer key

CompareJin Yin HuaLian Qiao
Medicinal nameLonicerae Flos.Forsythiae Fructus.

Shared actionCool and resolve toxicity while addressing both surface and internal Heat.
Surface emphasisTends to disperse surface Heat.Can guide Heat outward but is not given the same surface emphasis here.
Flavor and Stomach clueSweet, so its Cold nature is described as less harmful to the Stomach.No corresponding sweet-protective clue is assigned.
Distinctive actionsGentler Stomach impact while dispersing Heat.Strong Heart-Fire cooling, dispersal of Blood-level Qi stagnation and treatment of painful urinary dribbling.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersThe two are commonly combined and share surface/internal Heat treatment. Sweetness and surface dispersal favor Jin Yin Hua; Heart Fire, Blood-level Qi stagnation or urinary pain favor Lian Qiao.
Memory cueJin Yin Hua gently clears the surface; Lian Qiao clears the Heart and stagnation.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), p. 155. Source Exam Keys CMID 8992, question 1, and CMID 9009, question 1.
