Formula comparison

Bao He Wan vs. Jian Pi Wan

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueBao He WanJian Pi Wan
Shared clueBoth reduce Food Stagnation and may treat epigastric fullness, poor appetite, belching, nausea and an irregular stool pattern.
Fast distinctionAcute excess Food Stagnation: distention, sour belching, foul breath, nausea and a thick greasy coat.Chronic stagnation from Spleen deficiency: poor appetite, fatigue, loose stools and a pale tongue.

Comparative answer key

CompareBao He WanJian Pi Wan
Primary strategyReduce food, guide out stagnation and harmonize the Stomach.Strengthen Spleen Qi while gradually reducing accumulated food.
ConstitutionRelatively robust, excess presentation.Deficient, chronic presentation.
Decisive clueRecent dietary excess with strong stagnation signs.Long-standing weak digestion with recurring stagnation.
Tongue and pulseThick greasy coat; slippery and full.Pale tongue; weak pulse, possibly slippery.
Exam shortcutToo much food.Too little digestive power.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersBoth can show fullness after eating. Acute intensity points to Bao He Wan; fatigue and chronic Spleen weakness point to Jian Pi Wan.
Memory cueBao He clears the traffic jam; Jian Pi repairs the digestive engine.
SafetyPersistent vomiting, severe abdominal pain, weight loss, blood in stool or inability to pass stool/gas requires medical evaluation.
SourceSource bank: FWB 15.1, Comparison Formulas to Reduce Food Stagnation; corroborated with formula chapters and practice materials.
