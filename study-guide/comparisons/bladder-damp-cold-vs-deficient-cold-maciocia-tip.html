Bladder Damp-Cold vs. Deficient Cold

Separate a cold obstructive pathogen from chronic weak urinary containment.

Fast differentiation clues

ClueBladder Damp-ColdBladder Deficient and Cold
SharedBoth have Cold signs, urinary dysfunction and little or no burning.
Fast distinctionDamp-Cold blocks: difficult turbid urination, heaviness and sticky white coat.Deficient Cold leaks: frequent pale abundant urine or incontinence with weak deep pulse.

Clinical table

CompareBladder Damp-ColdBladder Deficient and Cold
Primary failurePathogenic obstruction.Containment and warming weakness.
Formula associationBa Zheng San or Shi Wei San in the supplied table.Suo Quan Wan, Sang Piao Xiao San or Tu Si Zi Wan.

Exam and safety

Why it mattersDifficulty/turbidity indicates blockage; frequency/abundance indicates failed containment.
Memory cueDamp-Cold blocks the outlet; deficient Cold leaves it open.
SafetyInability to urinate, blood, new incontinence, numbness or severe pain requires urgent evaluation.
