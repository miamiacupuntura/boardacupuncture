Xiao Yao San Family

Root formula and architecture

RootXiao Yao San courses constrained Liver Qi while nourishing Blood and protecting a deficient Spleen.
ArchitectureChai Hu moves Liver Qi; Dang Gui and Bai Shao nourish Blood; Bai Zhu, Fu Ling and Zhi Gan Cao support Spleen. Modifications reveal Heat or deeper Blood deficiency.

Study rule

How to chooseUse the base for constraint plus deficiency; add Dan-Zhi when constraint generates Heat; choose Hei Xiao Yao when Blood depletion is the stronger branch.
Memory cueXiao Yao frees; Jia Wei cools; Hei Xiao Yao enriches.
SafetyMenstrual complaints require pregnancy and abnormal-bleeding review. Persistent pelvic pain, heavy bleeding, fever or a new mass requires medical assessment.
