Bao He Wan

Food retention in the Middle Burner with impaired Stomach descent.

High-yield summary

CategoryReduce Food Stagnation
PatternFood retention in the Middle Burner with impaired Stomach descent.
ActionReduces food stagnation, harmonizes the Stomach and transforms Phlegm.
Decisive clueEpigastric fullness after overeating with sour regurgitation, foul breath and a thick greasy coating.

Ingredients

Shan Zha, Shen Qu, Lai Fu Zi, Ban Xia, Chen Pi, Fu Ling and Lian Qiao.

Commercial preparations and lineages may vary; always verify the source being used.

Differentiation and memory

Do not confusePing Wei San treats Dampness obstructing the middle; Bao He Wan requires retained food and sour-foul signs.
Memory cueBao He “preserves harmony” by clearing the meal that blocked it.

Safety

Severe pain, persistent vomiting, fever, inability to pass stool/gas or blood in vomit/stool requires urgent medical evaluation.
