Tao He Cheng Qi Tang vs. Xue Fu Zhu Yu Tang vs. Gui Zhi Fu Ling Wan vs. Wen Jing Tang

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueTao He Cheng Qi TangXue Fu Zhu Yu TangGui Zhi Fu Ling WanWen Jing Tang
Shared clueAll invigorate Blood and treat fixed pain or gynecologic symptoms, but location and associated pathogen determine the choice.
Fast distinctionAcute lower-burner Blood stasis with Heat and normal urination.Qi stagnation and Blood stasis in the chest with chest pain or chronic headache.Relatively chronic uterine Blood-stasis mass, often during pregnancy only under specialist care.Uterine Cold with Blood deficiency and stasis causing irregular menses and cold pain.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it matters“Blood stasis” identifies the family, not the final answer. Map the stasis by location and the factor attached to it.
Memory cueTao He—hot below; Xue Fu—chest; Gui Zhi Fu Ling—mass; Wen Jing—cold womb.
SafetyThese are not interchangeable in pregnancy. Severe chest pain, acute abdominal pain, heavy bleeding or pregnancy bleeding requires urgent evaluation.
SourceSource bank: FWB 10.1, Comparison Formulas to Invigorate the Blood; corroborated with FWB 11.1 and individual profiles.
