Pathology or symptom comparison

Parasitic Worm Clinical Comparison

Diagnostic orientation

Core ideaSymptoms suggest a parasite type, but stool testing, tape testing or imaging establishes the biomedical diagnosis and guides antiparasitic treatment.
Exam focusPain location, nocturnal itching, stool findings and acute biliary pain

Comparative differentiation

Pattern or presentationDecisive cluesRepresentative formula/herbStrategy
Roundworm presentationIntermittent umbilical pain, unusual cravings, emaciation, nose itching, sleep grinding and worms in stoolWu Mei Wan or Shi Jun Zi SanQuiet/expel roundworms and support Spleen-Stomach.
Pinworm presentationIntense nocturnal anal itching, restless sleep and small white worms around the anus, usually in childrenShi Jun Zi Da Huang FenExpel worms and relieve itching.
Tapeworm presentationMild abdominal discomfort, anal itching, diarrhea and visible tapeworm segments in stoolQu Di TangExpel worms and rectify digestion.
Biliary roundworm presentationSudden severe epigastric/right hypochondriac pain radiating to back/shoulder with nausea and vomitingWu Mei Wan or Dan Dao Qu Hui TangExpel worms and relieve pain after urgent evaluation.

Study rule

How to distinguishUmbilical symptoms suggest roundworm; night anal itch pinworm; segments tapeworm; acute right-upper abdominal pain biliary migration.
Memory cueNavel—roundworm; night itch—pinworm; segments—tapeworm; right-upper attack—biliary.
SafetySuspected parasites require diagnostic testing and standard antiparasitic treatment. Severe abdominal/right-upper-quadrant pain, jaundice, fever, persistent vomiting or obstruction is urgent.
