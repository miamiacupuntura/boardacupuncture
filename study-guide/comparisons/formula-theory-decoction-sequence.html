Theory 10 of 12

Decoction Sequence & Special Handling

Know what is pre-decocted, added late, dissolved, wrapped or prepared separately.

Core rule

RulePreparation is part of the prescription because it changes extraction, potency and safety.

Core preparation sequence

ConceptWhat it meansApplication
Authenticate firstConfirm species, medicinal part, processing and special instructions before cooking.Never use preparation to “fix” an uncertain identity.
Soak and extractTraditional decoctions commonly soak material and combine more than one extraction.Exact water, time and method depend on the prescription and product.
Pre-decoct · Xian JianDense minerals/shells and specified processed toxic roots may start earlier.Aconite handling requires professional, product-specific instructions.
Add late · Hou XiaAromatic or heat-sensitive herbs may enter near the end.Long cooking can drive off volatile constituents.
Dissolve · Yang HuaGelatins, syrups or honey-like substances are dissolved into strained hot liquid.Direct boiling may scorch or clump them.
Prepare separately · Ling JianRare or costly substances may be extracted separately and combined.This protects dose control and prevents loss.
Wrap / powder / swallowHairy, fine or mucilaginous substances may be bagged; selected powders are not decocted.Follow the exact route and do not improvise.

Decisive safety points

ConceptWhat it meansApplication
Raw vs processedPao Zhi can change both action and toxicity.The processing word is part of the medicinal identity.
Commercial granulesPre-extracted products are not re-created by applying raw-herb cooking rules.Use the product’s concentration and instructions.
Stop and verifyMissing instructions for a toxic, mineral, animal or gelatin product is a dispensing problem.Do not guess.

Responsible use

Educational summary for formula analysis and exam preparation. Doses, toxic preparations, interactions and clinical decisions require current references and professional supervision.
