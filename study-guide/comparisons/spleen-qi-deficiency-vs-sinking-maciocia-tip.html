Spleen Qi Deficiency vs. Spleen Qi Sinking

Separate weak transformation from the same deficiency losing its lifting and holding function.

Fast differentiation clues

ClueSpleen Qi DeficiencySpleen Qi Sinking
SharedBoth cause fatigue, poor appetite, loose stool, pale tongue and a weak pulse.
Fast distinctionTransformation weakness: digestive symptoms predominate without displacement.Lifting failure: bearing-down sensation, prolapse, chronic diarrhea and symptoms worse standing or exerting.

Clinical table

CompareSpleen Qi DeficiencySpleen Qi Sinking
Failed functionTransform and transport.Raise clear Qi and hold organs in place.
PulseWeak.Weak and possibly submerged.
Formula associationSi Jun Zi Tang.Bu Zhong Yi Qi Tang.

Exam and safety

Why it mattersProlapse or bearing down is the decisive progression from deficiency to sinking.
Memory cueQi deficiency cannot transform; sinking Qi cannot lift.
SafetyNew prolapse, rectal bleeding, severe pelvic pressure or neurologic weakness needs examination.
