Large Intestine Damp-Heat vs. Heat

Use stool moisture, mucus and blood to distinguish damp dysentery from dry bowel Heat.

Fast differentiation clues

ClueLarge Intestine Damp-HeatLarge Intestine Heat
SharedBoth cause abdominal discomfort, anal burning, a yellow tongue coat and a rapid pulse.
Fast distinctionDamp-Heat: urgent diarrhea with mucus and blood, tenesmus and a greasy yellow coat.Heat: dry hard stool, burning anus, thirst and a thick yellow-dry coat.

Clinical table

CompareLarge Intestine Damp-HeatLarge Intestine Heat
Stool directionFrequent, loose, sticky and possibly bloody.Dry, hard and difficult to pass.
Formula associationGe Gen Qin Lian Tang, Bai Tou Weng Tang or Shao Yao Tang.Ma Zi Ren Wan.

Exam and safety

Why it mattersDampness drives sticky diarrhea; Heat without Dampness dries the stool.
Memory cueDamp-Heat leaks sticky; bowel Heat bakes dry.
SafetyBloody diarrhea, fever, severe pain, dehydration or black stool requires prompt medical evaluation.
