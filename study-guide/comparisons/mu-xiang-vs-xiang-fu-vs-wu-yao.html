Three-herb comparison

Mu Xiang vs. Xiang Fu vs. Wu Yao

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueMu XiangXiang FuWu Yao
Shared clueAll regulate and promote the movement of Qi.
Fast distinctionFood accumulation and an obstructed Spleen.Liver-Gallbladder constraint, worry and emotional stagnation.Rebellious Qi with discomfort crossing the chest or abdomen.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersAll three move Qi, so the location and cause decide the answer: digestive accumulation, emotional Liver constraint or broadly rebellious Qi across the chest and abdomen.
Memory cueMu Xiang: food; Xiang Fu: feelings; Wu Yao: rebellious Qi.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), p. 535. Source Exam Keys CMID 9020, questions 11–12; CMID 9089, questions 1–2; and CMID 9096, questions 11–12.
