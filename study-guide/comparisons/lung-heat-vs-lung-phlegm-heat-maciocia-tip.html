Lung Heat vs. Phlegm-Heat in the Lung

Recognize when Lung Heat has condensed fluids into obstructing yellow-green Phlegm.

Fast differentiation clues

ClueLung HeatLung Phlegm-Heat
SharedBoth cause cough, Heat sensation, thirst, red tongue and a rapid pulse.
Fast distinctionHeat dominates: cough, fever/thirst and yellow coat without copious sticky sputum.Heat plus obstruction: thick yellow/green difficult sputum, chest oppression, rattling and slippery-rapid pulse.

Clinical table

CompareLung HeatLung Phlegm-Heat
Added factorHeat disrupts Lung descent.Heat plus condensed Phlegm obstructs descent.
Tongue / pulseRed with yellow coat; rapid.Red with thick sticky yellow coat; slippery-rapid.
Formula associationMa Xing Shi Gan Tang or Xie Bai San.Qing Qi Hua Tan Wan/Tang; Wen Dan Tang only when its broader Phlegm-Heat picture fits.

Exam and safety

Why it mattersSticky sputum, slippery pulse and chest oppression prove Phlegm beyond Heat alone.
Memory cueHeat reddens; Phlegm-Heat reddens and clogs.
SafetyGreen sputum with high fever, breathlessness, chest pain, confusion or low oxygen needs prompt assessment.

Review prompts
