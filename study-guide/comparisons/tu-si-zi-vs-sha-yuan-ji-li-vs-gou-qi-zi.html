Herb comparison

Tu Si Zi vs. Sha Yuan Ji Li vs. Gou Qi Zi

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueTu Si ZiSha Yuan Ji LiGou Qi Zi
Shared clueAll tonify both Kidney Yin and Yang and may be used together.
Fast distinctionTonifies Kidney Yin-Yang and secures Essence; adds Spleen-Kidney Yang diarrhea.Leans toward Yang and has an astringent, securing nature.Leans toward Yin and Blood, especially Liver Blood for vision, with slight Yang support.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersAll support Kidney Yin-Yang, so the exam turns on diarrhea and Essence, astringent securing, or Liver-Blood vision clues.
Memory cueTu Si Zi secures Essence; Sha Yuan Ji Li secures Yang; Gou Qi Zi nourishes vision.
