Exam focus and safety

CluesInsomnia, palpitations and restlessness from Heart Fire above with deficient Kidney warmth below.
Signature formulaJiāo Tài Wán.
TrapThe opposite temperatures are deliberate; this is not a simple all-Heat or all-Cold prescription.

SafetyRòu Guì requires pregnancy and anticoagulant caution. New palpitations, fainting or chest pain requires prompt medical assessment.
