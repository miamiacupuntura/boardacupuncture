Pattern comparison

Phlegm Misting the Heart vs. Phlegm-Fire Harassing the Heart

Study tip · Clues to help answer

CluePhlegm Misting the HeartPhlegm-Fire Harassing the Heart
Shared clueBoth obstruct the Heart orifices and can cause confusion, abnormal speech or behavior, chest oppression and a slippery pulse.
Fast distinctionCold/turbid and withdrawn: mental dullness, lethargy, depression, muttering, stupor, greasy white coat and slippery-slow pulse.Hot and agitated: insomnia, shouting, mania, violent behavior, bitter taste, red tongue with yellow greasy coat and slippery-rapid pulse.

Comparative answer key

ComparePhlegm Misting the HeartPhlegm-Fire Harassing the Heart
DirectionClouds and suppresses consciousness.Agitates and drives behavior upward/outward.
TemperatureUsually Cold or neutral Phlegm-Damp.Phlegm combined with Fire.
Tongue / pulseSwollen with white greasy coat; slippery-slow.Red with yellow greasy coat; slippery-rapid.
Formula examplesDi Tan Tang by presentation.Wen Dan Tang or Huang Lian Wen Dan Tang by Heat severity.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersBoth involve Phlegm and altered behavior. Withdrawal versus agitation, and white-slow versus yellow-rapid, separate misting from Fire harassment.
Memory cueCold Phlegm dims; Phlegm-Fire erupts.
SafetyNew confusion, reduced consciousness, psychosis, suicidal or violent behavior, seizure, fever or suspected intoxication is an emergency requiring immediate biomedical assessment.

Possible exam questions · Study prompts

  • Which pattern is withdrawn and dull?
  • Which is agitated and sleepless?
  • How do tongue coat and pulse separate them?
