Cynanchum — Two Bái Roots, Opposite Jobs

Two white roots in the milkweed family: one clears lingering Heat, one sends cough downward.

One picture, one rule

RuleBái WĒI handles a wee fever; Bái QIÁN pushes the cough forward and down.

The part decides the job

MedicinalPart / identityJob
Bái Wēi 白薇Cynanchum atratum rootClears deficiency Heat, cools Blood, promotes urination and resolves toxicity.
Bái Qián 白前Cynanchum stauntonii root/rhizomeDescends Lung Qi, dispels Phlegm and stops cough.

Exam and safety

Exam keyPost-febrile/postpartum low fever points to Wēi; cough with rebellious Lung Qi points to Qián.
SafetySame genus does not mean same action. Verify the final character and do not substitute the roots.
