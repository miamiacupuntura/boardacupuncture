Batch 5 · Kidney · Tip 8 of 10

Heart–Kidney Not Harmonized vs. Kidney Yin Deficiency

Recognize when Kidney-Yin weakness no longer anchors Heart Fire and prominent Spirit signs appear.

Fast differentiation clues

ClueKidney Yin DeficiencyHeart–Kidney Not Harmonized
SharedBoth may cause night sweating, dry throat, tinnitus, a red peeled tongue and a thin rapid pulse.
Fast distinctionKidney signs dominate: weak back/knees, dizziness and reproductive or urinary deficiency signs.Heart Fire/Spirit signs dominate: palpitations, insomnia, anxiety, restlessness, redder tip and central crack.

Clinical table

CompareKidney Yin DeficiencyHeart–Kidney Not Harmonized
MechanismKidney Yin is depleted.Kidney Water fails to restrain/nourish Heart Fire and Spirit.
Formula associationLiu Wei Di Huang Wan or Zuo Gui Wan.Tian Wang Bu Xin Dan in the supplied table.

Exam and safety

Why it mattersPalpitations and insomnia with tongue-tip/Heart signs establish the Heart–Kidney axis problem.
Memory cueKidney Yin dries the root; disharmony keeps the Heart awake.
SafetyPalpitations with chest pain, fainting, severe breathlessness or sustained rapid/irregular heartbeat requires urgent care.
