Exam focus and safety

CluesCalf cramps, cramping abdominal pain or menstrual spasm with tight, contracted muscles.
Signature formulaSháo Yào Gān Cǎo Tāng.
TrapThis pair directly relaxes cramp; Guì Zhī + Bái Sháo instead emphasizes harmonizing Ying and Wei.
PreparationHoney-fried Gān Cǎo may emphasize supplementation, but processing must match the formula source.
SafetyLarge or prolonged Gān Cǎo doses can raise blood pressure and lower potassium; screen diuretics, corticosteroids, digoxin and cardiovascular disease.

Complete formula, Duì Yào, or formula + pair?

Use the complete formula when the case reproduces a full pattern architecture with root, branches, and protection. Consider the pair for a narrow objective; use it as a modification when a base formula fits but lacks one function. The pair does not automatically replace the formula.
