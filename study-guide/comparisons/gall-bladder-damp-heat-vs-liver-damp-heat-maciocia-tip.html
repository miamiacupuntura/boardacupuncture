Gall Bladder vs. Liver Damp-Heat

Use biliary laterality versus broader Liver-channel signs to refine a closely overlapping diagnosis.

Fast differentiation clues

ClueGall Bladder Damp-HeatLiver Damp-Heat
SharedBoth cause hypochondriac fullness, bitter taste, nausea, yellow sticky coat and slippery-wiry rapid pulse.
Fast distinctionGall Bladder: right-sided coating and biliary/Shaoyang signs dominate.Liver: genital, urinary, menstrual or broader channel signs dominate.

Clinical table

CompareGall Bladder Damp-HeatLiver Damp-Heat
EmphasisBile passage and lateral Shaoyang region.Liver channel and lower burner.
Formula associationYin Chen Hao-based strategy in the supplied table.Long Dan Xie Gan Tang when the full Liver pattern fits.

Exam and safety

Why it mattersThese often coexist; distribution, not one isolated symptom, determines emphasis.
Memory cueGall Bladder marks the side; Liver follows the channel.
SafetyJaundice, fever, severe pain, vomiting or urinary/genital infection symptoms requires medical evaluation.
