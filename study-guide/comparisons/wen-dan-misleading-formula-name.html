Misleading name 1 of 6

Wēn Dǎn Tāng 温胆汤

“Warm the Gallbladder” is functionally a cooling Phlegm-Heat formula.

Name versus reality

What it suggestsThe name sounds like a warm Interior-Cold prescription.
RealityBàn Xià, Zhú Rú, Zhǐ Shí, Chén Pí, Fú Líng and assistants clear/transform Phlegm-Heat and restore the Gallbladder’s calm, decisive nature.

How to answer

Decisive clueTimidity, insomnia, palpitations, dizziness or nausea with Phlegm-Heat signs.
MemoryWēn describes restoring a gentle, harmonious Gallbladder—not necessarily raising temperature.

Safety

AlertUse processed Bàn Xià. New severe palpitations, syncope, neurologic deficit or persistent vomiting requires medical evaluation.
