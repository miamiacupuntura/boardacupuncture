Diarrhea Pattern Differentiation

Diagnostic orientation

Core ideaStool character, burning, relationship to food or emotion, and timing distinguish the branches.
Exam focusStool quality, timing, burning and triggers

Study rule

How to distinguishBurning indicates Heat; watery with chills indicates Cold-Damp; greasy-food sensitivity indicates Spleen; emotions indicate Liver; predawn timing indicates Kidney Yang.
Memory cueCold—watery; Heat—burning; Spleen—food weakness; Liver—emotion; Kidney—dawn.
SafetyBlood or black stool, high fever, severe dehydration, intense pain, recent antibiotics, infant/elderly frailty or persistent diarrhea requires medical evaluation.
