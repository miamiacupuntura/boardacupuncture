Stomach Qi Deficiency vs. Stomach Deficient and Cold

Recognize when weak Stomach function progresses into loss of warming.

Fast differentiation clues

ClueStomach Qi DeficiencyStomach Deficient and Cold
SharedBoth cause fatigue, poor appetite, mild epigastric discomfort, pale tongue and a weak Stomach pulse.
Fast distinctionWeak function without marked Cold: morning tiredness and an empty right-middle pulse.Qi deficiency plus Cold: pain relieved by eating, warmth and pressure, cold limbs and a deeper slower pulse.

Clinical table

CompareStomach Qi DeficiencyStomach Deficient and Cold
TemperatureNeutral or mildly cool.Clear internal Cold.
PainMild discomfort or emptiness.Dull pain relieved by food, warmth and pressure.
Formula associationSi Jun Zi Tang.Huang Qi Jian Zhong Tang or Xiao Jian Zhong Tang.

Exam and safety

Why it mattersCold signs and relief from warmth/food distinguish deficient Cold from simple Qi weakness.
Memory cueQi deficiency is empty; deficient Cold is empty and chilly.
SafetyPersistent pain, vomiting, black stool, fainting, weight loss or anemia signs require medical evaluation.
