Qing Wei San

Stomach Fire blazing upward into the gums and teeth.

High-yield summary

CategoryClear Stomach Heat · Cool Blood
PatternStomach Fire blazing upward into the gums and teeth.
ActionClears Stomach Heat, cools Blood, nourishes Yin and reduces gum swelling.
Decisive clueSevere toothache with swollen bleeding gums, bad breath, thirst and a red tongue.

Ingredients

Sheng Ma, Huang Lian, Dang Gui, Sheng Di Huang and Mu Dan Pi.

Commercial preparations and lineages may vary; always verify the source being used.

Differentiation and memory

Do not confuseYu Nu Jian treats Stomach Fire with underlying Kidney Yin deficiency; Qing Wei San treats stronger excess Stomach Fire and Blood Heat.
Memory cueQing Wei clears the Stomach; Sheng Ma carries the clearing action to gums and teeth.

Safety

Facial swelling, fever, difficulty swallowing/breathing or rapidly spreading dental infection requires urgent dental or medical care.
