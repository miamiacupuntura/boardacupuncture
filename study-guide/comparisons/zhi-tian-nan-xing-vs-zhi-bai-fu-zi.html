Herb comparison

Arisaematis Rhizoma preparatum (zhi tian nan xing) vs. Typhonii Rhizoma preparatum (zhi bai fu zi)

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueZhi Tian Nan XingZhi Bai Fu Zi
Shared clueBoth are Warm Herbs that Transform Cold-Phlegm and are used for Wind-Phlegm.
Fast distinctionWind-Phlegm with spasms, seizures, epilepsy or profuse Phlegm in windstroke.Ascending action that targets excess Wind-Phlegm in the face and head.

Comparative answer key

CompareZhi Tian Nan XingZhi Bai Fu Zi
Medicinal nameArisaematis Rhizoma preparatum.Typhonii Rhizoma preparatum.
CategoryWarm Herbs that Transform Cold-Phlegm.
Shared indicationWind-Phlegm.
Primary emphasisExpels Wind-Phlegm and stops spasms.Ascends and raises to expel excess Wind-Phlegm from the face and head.
Key indicationsWindstroke with profuse Phlegm welling upward, epilepsy and other seizure disorders.Wind-Phlegm disorders concentrated in the upper body, especially the face and head.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersBoth can answer a Wind-Phlegm question, but spasms and seizure disorders favor Zhi Tian Nan Xing, while a face-and-head location favors Zhi Bai Fu Zi.
Memory cueTian Nan Xing stops Wind-Phlegm spasms; Bai Fu Zi rises to the face and head.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), p. 422. Source Exam Key CMID 9080, question 6.
