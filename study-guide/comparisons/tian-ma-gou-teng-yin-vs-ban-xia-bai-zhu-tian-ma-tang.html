Tian Ma Gou Teng Yin vs. Ban Xia Bai Zhu Tian Ma Tang

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueTian Ma Gou Teng YinBan Xia Bai Zhu Tian Ma Tang
Shared clueBoth contain Tian Ma, extinguish internal Wind and treat dizziness, vertigo, headache or unsteadiness.
Fast distinctionLiver-Yang rises with Heat and Wind: headache, tinnitus, irritability, insomnia, red tongue and wiry-rapid pulse.Spleen deficiency produces Damp-Phlegm and Wind: heavy head, nausea, chest fullness, greasy coat and slippery pulse.

Comparative answer key

CompareTian Ma Gou Teng YinBan Xia Bai Zhu Tian Ma Tang
Root patternLiver-Yang rising with internal Wind and Heat.Spleen deficiency with Damp-Phlegm generating Wind.
Thermal clueHeat and agitation.Dampness, heaviness and digestive weakness.
Distinct actionCalms Liver, clears Heat and extinguishes Wind.Strengthens Spleen, dries Dampness and transforms Phlegm.
Exam shortcutHot rising dizziness.Phlegmy heavy dizziness.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersTian Ma and dizziness are shared; tongue coat, pulse and associated Heat versus Damp signs reveal the mechanism.
Memory cueGou Teng cools the rising Liver; Ban Xia dries the spinning Phlegm.
SafetySudden severe vertigo, one-sided weakness, slurred speech, facial droop, inability to walk or severe new headache requires emergency stroke evaluation.
SourceSource bank: FWB 14.1.1, Comparison Formulas to Expel Wind; corroborated with FWB 6.2 and individual profiles.
