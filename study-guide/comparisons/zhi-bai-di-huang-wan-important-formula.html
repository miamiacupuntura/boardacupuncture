Zhi Bai Di Huang Wan

Kidney and Liver Yin deficiency with pronounced Empty Heat.

High-yield summary

CategoryNourish Kidney Yin · Clear Empty Heat
PatternKidney and Liver Yin deficiency with pronounced Empty Heat.
ActionNourishes Yin and drains deficiency Fire.
Decisive clueNight sweats, steaming-bone sensation, heat in palms/soles, dry mouth and seminal or urinary disturbance.

Ingredients

Shu Di Huang, Shan Zhu Yu, Shan Yao, Ze Xie, Mu Dan Pi, Fu Ling, Zhi Mu and Huang Bai.

Commercial preparations and lineages may vary; always verify the source being used.

Differentiation and memory

Do not confuseLiu Wei Di Huang Wan treats Yin deficiency without strong Fire; Zhi Bai adds Zhi Mu and Huang Bai for clear Empty-Heat signs.
Memory cueLiu Wei plus Zhi and Bai: nourish the Yin base and clear the Fire rising from it.

Safety

Persistent fever, drenching night sweats, weight loss, blood or urinary changes requires biomedical evaluation.
