Batch 8 · Large Intestine · Tip 5 of 10

Large Intestine Dryness vs. Heat

Use systemic Heat and coating to separate fluid deficiency from hot dryness.

Fast differentiation clues

ClueLarge Intestine DrynessLarge Intestine Heat
SharedBoth cause dry hard stools that are difficult to discharge.
Fast distinctionDryness deficiency: thin body, dry skin/mouth and scant coating without strong Heat signs.Heat: anal burning, pronounced thirst and a thick yellow-dry coat with rapid pulse.

Clinical table

CompareLarge Intestine DrynessLarge Intestine Heat
RootFluid, Blood or Yin insufficiency.Excess Heat consumes fluids.
Formula associationZeng Ye Tang, Qing Zao Run Chang Tang, Wu Ren Wan or Si Wu Ma Zi Ren Wan.Ma Zi Ren Wan.

Exam and safety

Why it mattersHeat signs and a thick yellow coat distinguish excess Heat from deficient lubrication.
Memory cueDryness lacks oil; Heat burns the oil away.
SafetyNew severe constipation, vomiting, blood/black stool, weight loss or inability to pass gas requires evaluation.
