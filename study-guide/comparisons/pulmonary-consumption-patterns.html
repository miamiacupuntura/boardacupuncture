Pulmonary Consumption (Tuberculosis) Comparison

Diagnostic orientation

Core ideaThis is a historical TCM framework for pulmonary consumption; suspected tuberculosis requires immediate biomedical testing and public-health treatment.
Exam focusDry cough, bleeding, empty Heat and progressive systemic deficiency

Comparative differentiation

Pattern or presentationDecisive cluesRepresentative formula/herbStrategy
Lung Yin deficiencyDry cough, scant/blood-streaked phlegm, afternoon fever, flushed cheeks, night sweat and dry throatBai He Gu Jin TangNourish Yin, moisten Lung and relieve cough.
Yin deficiency with empty HeatChoking cough, yellow scant phlegm, bright-red blood, steaming-bone fever, heavy night sweat and red scant-coated tongueQin Jiao Bie Jia SanNourish Yin and diminish deficient Fire.
Qi and Yin deficiencyWeak cough/voice, dyspnea, pale-red blood, afternoon fever, spontaneous/night sweat, fatigue and poor appetiteBao Zhen TangBenefit Qi and nourish Yin.
Yin and Yang deficiencyAdvanced cough/bleeding with dyspnea, edema, loose stool, spontaneous sweating and reproductive deficiency signsBu Tian Da Zao WanNourish Yin, warm Yang and support severe deficiency.

Study rule

How to distinguishDry cough begins with Lung Yin; intense empty Heat/bright bleeding marks deeper Yin depletion; weak voice/fatigue adds Qi; edema/cold digestive-reproductive failure adds Yang.
Memory cueDry—Lung Yin; burning/bleeding—empty Heat; weak voice—Qi-Yin; edema/cold failure—Yin-Yang.
SafetyCough over 3 weeks, coughing blood, night sweats, fever, weight loss or TB exposure requires prompt testing. Active TB needs prescribed multidrug therapy and public-health precautions.
SourceInternal Medicine 1, Handout 10: Pulmonary Consumption.
