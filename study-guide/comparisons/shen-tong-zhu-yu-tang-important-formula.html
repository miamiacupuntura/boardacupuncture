Shen Tong Zhu Yu Tang

Blood stasis with Qi stagnation obstructing the channels and collaterals.

High-yield summary

CategoryInvigorate Blood in the Channels · Relieve Pain
PatternBlood stasis with Qi stagnation obstructing the channels and collaterals.
ActionInvigorates Blood, moves Qi, dispels Wind-Damp and relieves fixed body pain.
Decisive clueChronic fixed, stabbing body or joint pain with tenderness and possible dark or purple signs.

Ingredients

Qin Jiao, Chuan Xiong, Tao Ren, Hong Hua, Gan Cao, Qiang Huo, Mo Yao, Dang Gui, Wu Ling Zhi, Xiang Fu, Niu Xi and Di Long.

Commercial preparations and lineages may vary; always verify the source being used.

Differentiation and memory

Do not confuseJuan Bi Tang treats acute Wind-Cold-Damp Bi; Shen Tong Zhu Yu Tang treats persistent channel pain in which Blood stasis is central.
Memory cueWhen the whole body hurts from fixed stasis, move Blood and reopen every collateral.

Safety

Avoid unsupervised use in pregnancy or with anticoagulants. New unilateral swelling, chest pain, trauma or neurologic weakness requires urgent evaluation.
