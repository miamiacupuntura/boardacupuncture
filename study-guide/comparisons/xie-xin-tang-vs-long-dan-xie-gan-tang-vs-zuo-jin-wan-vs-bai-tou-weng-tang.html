Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersThe same words—Fire, irritability, red tongue—appear across the group. Organ direction and hallmark discharge or bleeding choose the formula.
Memory cueXie Xin = blood above; Long Dan = Liver/GB; Zuo Jin = sour rebellion; Bai Tou Weng = blood below.
SafetyVomiting blood, significant nosebleed, jaundice, severe abdominal pain, bloody diarrhea or dehydration requires urgent biomedical care.
