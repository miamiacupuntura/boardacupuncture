Deer 鹿 — One Antler, Four Strengths

Velvet, bone, glue and spent residue shift from powerful tonification to gentle binding.

One picture, one rule

RuleVelvet → bone → glue → dust.

The part decides the job

MedicinalPart / identityJob
Lù Róng 鹿茸Velvet growing antlerPowerfully tonifies Kidney Yang, Essence and Blood; strengthens sinews/bones.
Lù Jiǎo 鹿角Hardened antlerMore mildly warms Yang, invigorates Blood and reduces Yin-type swelling.
Lù Jiǎo Jiāo 鹿角胶Antler glueTonifies Yang/Essence/Blood and stops deficiency-Cold bleeding.
Lù Jiǎo Shuāng 鹿角霜Spent antler residueMildly warms Yang and astringes discharge, urine or bleeding.

Exam and safety

Exam keyGrowing velvet is strongest; spent residue is gentlest and easier to digest.
SafetyAvoid Lù Róng in Yin-deficiency Heat, hot bleeding or Exterior patterns. Deer materials have ethical, sourcing and jurisdictional restrictions.
