Ma Xing Shi Gan Tang

Heat lodged in the Lung obstructing the diffusion and descent of Lung Qi.

High-yield summary

CategoryClear Lung Heat · Calm Wheezing
PatternHeat lodged in the Lung obstructing the diffusion and descent of Lung Qi.
ActionClears Lung Heat, disseminates Lung Qi and stops cough and wheezing.
Decisive clueFever, sweating, thirst, cough and wheezing with flaring nostrils and a rapid pulse.

Ingredients

Ma Huang, Xing Ren, Shi Gao and Zhi Gan Cao.

Commercial preparations and lineages may vary; always verify the source being used.

Differentiation and memory

Do not confuseMa Huang Tang treats exterior Wind-Cold without sweating; Ma Xing Shi Gan Tang pairs Ma Huang with much Shi Gao for Lung Heat with sweating.
Memory cueMa opens, Xing descends, Shi Gao cools and Gan Cao harmonizes.

Safety

Breathing difficulty, blue lips, chest pain, confusion or low oxygen is an emergency. Ma Huang requires cardiovascular caution.
