Spleen Blood Deficiency vs. Spleen Not Controlling Blood

Separate insufficient Blood production from active Blood leakage caused by weak containment.

Fast differentiation clues

ClueSpleen Blood DeficiencySpleen Not Controlling Blood
SharedBoth occur on a Spleen-Qi-deficient background with fatigue, pallor, poor appetite and a weak pulse.
Fast distinctionToo little Blood: dizziness, scanty periods and pale-dry signs without active bleeding.Blood leaks: bruising, chronic uterine bleeding, blood in stool or other diffuse bleeding.

Clinical table

CompareSpleen Blood DeficiencySpleen Not Controlling Blood
ProblemProduction/nourishment is insufficient.Containment is insufficient.
Menstrual clueScanty or delayed flow.Prolonged or excessive pale flow.
Formula associationGui Pi Tang when the supplied combined pattern fits.Gui Pi Tang.

Exam and safety

Why it mattersBoth may use the same formula family; scanty flow versus leaking flow defines the clinical problem.
Memory cueDeficiency leaves the tank empty; failed control lets the tank leak.
SafetyHeavy bleeding, black stool, fainting, chest symptoms or pregnancy bleeding requires urgent care.
