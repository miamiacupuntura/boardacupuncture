Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersA deficient constitution does not automatically mean Ren Shen Bai Du San. The type of deficiency and the thermal nature of the exterior pathogen must agree.
Memory cueRen Shen = Qi; Fu Zi = Yang; Wei Rui = Yin.
SafetyMa Huang Xi Xin Fu Zi Tang contains processed Fu Zi, Ma Huang and Xi Xin and requires expert supervision. Frail patients with fever, breathing difficulty or altered mental status require medical assessment.
