Lycium 枸杞 — Berry Feeds, Root Bark Cools

Sweet red berry fills the tank; root bark cools Heat steaming from the bones.

One picture, one rule

RuleBerry banks Yin/Blood; earth-bone bark cools deficiency Heat.

The part decides the job

MedicinalPart / identityJob
Gǒu Qǐ Zǐ 枸杞子Fruit / goji berryNourishes Liver-Kidney Yin/Blood, augments Essence, brightens eyes and moistens Lung.
Dì Gǔ Pí 地骨皮Root barkCools Blood, clears deficiency Heat and drains Lung Heat.

Exam and safety

Exam keyWeak eyes/dryness from deficiency = berry; bone-steaming Heat with sweating = root bark.
SafetyGǒu Qǐ Zǐ can raise INR with warfarin. Differentiate Dì Gǔ Pí from Mǔ Dān Pí and verify medications.
