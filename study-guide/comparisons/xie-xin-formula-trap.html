Formula trap 2 of 22

Xiè Xīn Tāng vs. Bàn Xià Xiè Xīn Tāng

“Drain below the Heart” can mean pure excess Fire or a mixed hot-cold epigastric tangle.

Memory rule

AnchorPlain Xiè Xīn has rhubarb; Bàn Xià version has no rhubarb and harmonizes a tangle.

Decisive separation

FormulaArchitectureDecisive presentation
Xiè Xīn Tāng 泻心汤Dà Huáng + Huáng Lián + Huáng QínExcess Fire with bleeding, irritability or constipation.
Bàn Xià Xiè Xīn Tāng 半夏泻心汤Bàn Xià, Gān Jiāng, Huáng Qín, Huáng Lián, Rén Shēn, Dà Zǎo, Zhì Gān CǎoHot-cold tangle: focal epigastric distention, nausea and gurgling diarrhea.

Exam and safety

Exam keyPure bitter-cold three-herb purge versus a seven-herb harmonizer with warming and tonifying support.
SafetyDo not infer “Heart disease” from the name; Xīn Xià denotes the epigastric region. Processed Bàn Xià is required.
