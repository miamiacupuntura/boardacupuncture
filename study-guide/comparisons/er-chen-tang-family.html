Er Chen Tang Family

Root formula and architecture

RootEr Chen Tang is the foundational Damp-Phlegm formula: dry Dampness, transform Phlegm, regulate Qi and harmonize the Middle.
ArchitectureCore: processed Ban Xia, Chen Pi, Fu Ling and Zhi Gan Cao, traditionally supported by Sheng Jiang and Wu Mei.

Study rule

How to chooseWhite Damp-Phlegm stays with Er Chen; Heat disturbing sleep points to Wen Dan; vertigo from Wind-Phlegm points to Ban Xia Bai Zhu Tian Ma.
Memory cueEr Chen = white Phlegm; Wen Dan = hot restless Phlegm; Tian Ma = dizzy Wind-Phlegm.
SafetyBan Xia must be properly processed. New severe vertigo, neurologic deficits, fainting or a sudden severe headache requires urgent assessment.
