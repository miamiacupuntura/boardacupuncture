Xue Fu Zhu Yu Tang vs. Bu Yang Huan Wu Tang

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueXue Fu Zhu Yu TangBu Yang Huan Wu Tang
Shared clueBoth invigorate Blood and dispel stasis, but the Qi pattern, location and clinical setting differ.
Fast distinctionQi stagnation and Blood stasis above the diaphragm: fixed chest pain, headache, irritability or dark complexion.Severe Qi deficiency cannot move Blood: chronic post-stroke hemiplegia, weakness, numbness or facial paralysis.

Comparative answer key

CompareXue Fu Zhu Yu TangBu Yang Huan Wu Tang
Root mechanismQi stagnation drives Blood stasis.Qi deficiency fails to propel Blood.
LocationChest and upper burner.Channels and collaterals, often one side of the body.
Decisive clueFixed stabbing chest pain with emotional or upper-body stasis signs.Weak, flaccid post-stroke deficits with a deficient pulse.
Therapeutic emphasisMove Qi and Blood and relieve pain.Strongly tonify Qi so Blood can move through the channels.
Exam shortcutStagnant and painful.Deficient and weak.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersBoth move Blood, but one disperses an excess-like obstruction while the other supplies Qi to move stasis in a profoundly deficient patient.
Memory cueXue Fu opens the chest mansion; Bu Yang supplies the power to move a weak side.
SafetyNew chest pain or any new stroke sign is an emergency. These formulas are not substitutes for acute cardiac or stroke care; anticoagulant use requires professional review.
SourceSource bank: FWB 10.1 series, Comparison Formulas to Invigorate the Blood; corroborated with individual formula profiles.
