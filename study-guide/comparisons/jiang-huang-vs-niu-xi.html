Herb comparison

Jiang Huang vs. Niu Xi

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueJiang HuangNiu Xi
Shared clueBoth break up Blood, direct Blood downward, unblock menstruation, stop pain and affect the sinews.
Fast distinctionMore strongly breaks Blood, moves Qi and expels Wind from painful obstruction.Tonifies Liver and Kidneys to strengthen sinews and bones.
Location clueChest, ribs, neck, shoulders and upper limbs.Low back, lower Jiao and downward treatment of upper-body Heat or rising Yang.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersBoth move Blood, menstruation and pain, but Jiang Huang combines stronger Blood-breaking with Qi and Wind-Damp movement, whereas Niu Xi combines Blood movement with Liver-Kidney support and a downward direction.
Memory cueJiang Huang moves across the upper body; Niu Xi strengthens and guides downward.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), p. 639. Source Exam Key CMID 8977, questions 3–7.
