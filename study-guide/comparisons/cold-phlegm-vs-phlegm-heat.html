Pattern comparison

Cold-Phlegm vs. Phlegm-Heat

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueCold-PhlegmPhlegm-Heat
Shared clueBoth obstruct Lung Qi and may cause cough, wheezing, chest fullness, nausea, a greasy coating and slippery pulse.
Fast distinctionCopious, thin, watery white sputum; cold limbs and white greasy coat.Thick, sticky yellow sputum; thirst, irritability and yellow greasy coat.

Comparative answer key

CompareCold-PhlegmPhlegm-Heat
SputumWhite, thin, watery and easy to expectorate.Yellow, thick, sticky and difficult to expectorate.
TemperatureCold signs predominate.Heat and inflammation predominate.
Tongue / pulsePale with white greasy coat; slippery-slow.Red with yellow greasy coat; slippery-rapid.
Formula examplesEr Chen Tang; Xiao Qing Long Tang when Exterior Cold is present.Wen Dan Tang by organ pattern; Ding Chuan Tang for wheezing.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersSputum color and texture are high-yield, but must be confirmed by the tongue, pulse and whole thermal pattern.
Memory cueCold makes white water; Heat cooks it yellow and thick.
SafetyBreathing difficulty, low oxygen, high fever, chest pain or coughing blood requires urgent medical care.

Possible exam questions · Study prompts

  • Which produces watery white sputum?
  • Which produces sticky yellow sputum?
  • What tongue coats confirm each?
