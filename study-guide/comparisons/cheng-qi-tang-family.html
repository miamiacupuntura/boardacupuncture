Cheng Qi Tang Family

Root formula and architecture

RootThe Cheng Qi family clears Heat and moves stool. Zhi Shi/Hou Po move Qi, Da Huang purges Heat and Mang Xiao softens hardness.
ArchitectureRemoving or adding one functional unit changes the severity and branch pattern.

Study rule

How to chooseGrade obstruction first; then look for the branch clue—Blood stasis below or damaged Yin and fluids.
Memory cueDa = all four; Xiao = no salt; Tiao Wei = no Qi movers; Tao He = Blood; Zeng Ye = fluids.
SafetyThese are strong purgative formulas, not self-care laxatives. Pregnancy, frailty, dehydration, severe pain, vomiting or suspected obstruction requires avoidance and urgent assessment.
