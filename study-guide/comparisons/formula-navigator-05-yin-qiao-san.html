Formula 5 of 63

Yin Qiao San

Safety reviewed

Early-stage Wind-Heat attacking the Lung and Wei level.

Diagnostic navigation

CategoryRelease the Exterior
Clinical routeFever & Heat · Cough & breathing
PatternEarly-stage Wind-Heat attacking the Lung and Wei level.
Decisive clueFever greater than chills, sore throat, thirst and headache with little or no sweating.

Essential architecture

King herb(s)Jin Yin Hua, Lian Qiao
ActionDisperses Wind-Heat, clears Heat/Toxin and benefits the throat.
IngredientsJin Yin Hua, Lian Qiao, Bo He, Niu Bang Zi, Jing Jie Sui, Dan Dou Chi, Jie Geng, Zhu Ye and Sheng Gan Cao, with Lu Gen in decoction.

Safety and provenance

Breathing difficulty, drooling/inability to swallow, severe dehydration, rash, confusion or persistent high fever requires urgent care.
