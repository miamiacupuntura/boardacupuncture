Gall Bladder Deficiency vs. Liver Qi Stagnation

Use deficient timidity versus constrained irritability and distention to separate two sighing patterns.

Fast differentiation clues

ClueGall Bladder DeficiencyLiver Qi Stagnation
SharedBoth may cause sighing, mood disturbance and difficulty initiating action.
Fast distinctionGall Bladder: timidity, easy fright and indecision with a deficient quality.Liver Qi: irritability, depression, hypochondriac/breast distention and a wiry pulse.

Clinical table

CompareGall Bladder DeficiencyLiver Qi Stagnation
NatureDeficient courage/decision.Excess or mixed Qi constraint.
Formula associationWen Dan Tang or An Shen Ding Zhi Wan.Xiao Yao Wan or Yue Ju Wan in the supplied table.

Exam and safety

Why it mattersDistention and irritability indicate constrained Liver Qi; easy fright and timidity indicate Gall Bladder deficiency.
Memory cueGall Bladder doubts; Liver Qi constrains.
SafetyPersistent depression, panic, inability to function or self-harm thoughts requires professional mental-health care.
