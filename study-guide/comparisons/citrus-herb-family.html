Citrus 橘 / 枳 — Age Decides the Job

Unripe green citrus breaks strongly; ripe or aged material regulates more gently.

One picture, one rule

RuleGreen is mean; old is gold. Shí sinks strongly; Ké opens the chest gently.

The part decides the job

MedicinalPart / identityJob

Exam and safety

Exam keyQīng Pí targets Liver/sides; Chén Pí Spleen-Lung; Zhǐ Shí lower/strong; Zhǐ Ké chest/mild.
SafetyQīng Pí, Zhǐ Shí and Zhǐ Ké require pregnancy caution. Bitter orange contains stimulant synephrine; screen hypertension and stimulants.
