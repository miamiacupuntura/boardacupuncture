Pathology or symptom comparison

Psoriasis Pattern Differentiation

Diagnostic orientation

Core ideaDuration and moisture distinguish the two source presentations; a biomedical diagnosis is important because fungal disease and eczema can resemble psoriasis.
Exam focusMoist red acute lesions versus thick dry chronic plaques

Comparative differentiation

Pattern or presentationDecisive cluesRepresentative formula/herbStrategy
External Wind-Heat-DampnessShort duration with papules, redness, moistness/ulceration, scabs and yellow or greasy coatXiao Feng SanDispel Wind, clear Heat and drain Dampness.
Blood deficiency with Wind-DrynessLong duration with thickened dry peeling plaques, insomnia, dizziness, pale tongue and weak-thready pulseSi Wu Xiao Feng Yin or Dang Gui Yin ZiNourish Blood, dispel Wind and moisten Dryness.

Study rule

How to distinguishShort, red and moist maps to Wind-Heat-Dampness; chronic, thick and dry with pale deficiency signs maps to Blood-Dryness.
Memory cueNew-red-wet—Wind/Heat/Damp; old-thick-dry—Blood deficiency.
SafetyWidespread redness, fever, pustules, severe pain, dehydration or rapid worsening can be erythrodermic/pustular psoriasis and requires urgent care. Joint pain may indicate psoriatic arthritis.
