Trap 18 of 22

Lú Huì 芦荟 vs. Lú Gēn 芦根

The shared Lú character joins unrelated aloe and reed medicines.

Memory rule

AnchorHuì is aloe that purges; Gēn is reed root that generates fluids.

Correct identity

NameIdentityJob
Lú Huì 芦荟AloeBitter-cold purgative; clears Liver fire and treats parasites.
Lú Gēn 芦根Phragmitis Rhizoma / reed rhizomeClears Lung-Stomach Heat, generates fluids and stops vomiting.

Exam and safety

Exam keyConstipation with Liver fire suggests Lú Huì; thirst or vomiting from Lung/Stomach Heat suggests Lú Gēn.
Safety alertLú Huì is a strong purgative and is inappropriate in pregnancy or weak/cold digestive states without expert direction.
