Batch 1 · Formula 6 of 10

Bai He Gu Jin Tang

Lung dryness/Yin deficiency with chronic dry cough.

High-yield summary

CategoryNourish Lung and Kidney Yin
PatternLung dryness/Yin deficiency with chronic dry cough.
ActionNourishes Yin, moistens the Lung, transforms scant Phlegm and stops cough.
Decisive clueDry cough, dry throat and scant difficult sputum, possibly blood-streaked, in a chronic deficient picture.

Ingredients

Sheng Di Huang, Shu Di Huang, Mai Men Dong, Bai He, Dang Gui, Bai Shao, Bei Mu, Xuan Shen, Jie Geng and Sheng Gan Cao.

Commercial preparations and lineages may vary; always verify the source being used.

Differentiation and memory

Do not confuseBei Mu Gua Lou San addresses Dry-Phlegm more directly; Bai He Gu Jin Tang emphasizes underlying Yin deficiency.
Memory cueBai He moistens; Gu Jin “preserves Metal.”

Safety

Coughing blood, breathlessness, fever, low oxygen or unexplained weight loss requires prompt evaluation.
