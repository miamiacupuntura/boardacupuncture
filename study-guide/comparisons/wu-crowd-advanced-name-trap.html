The Wǔ Crowd: Wèi, Bèi and Líng

Four Wǔ names span fruit, insect gall, animal material and a complete formula.

Memory rule

AnchorWèi = five flavors; Bèi = gall; Líng Zhī moves Blood; Líng Sǎn moves water.

Identities

NameIdentityJob
Wǔ Wèi Zǐ 五味子Schisandra fruitAstringes Lung/Kidney, generates fluids and calms Spirit.
Wǔ Bèi Zǐ 五倍子Chinese gallnut / insect gallStrongly astringes leakage, cough and bleeding.
Wǔ Líng Zhī 五灵脂Flying-squirrel fecesInvigorates Blood and relieves stasis pain.
Wǔ Líng Sǎn 五苓散Five-ingredient formulaPromotes urination and transforms impaired water metabolism.

Exam and safety

Exam keyZhī is a Blood-moving animal substance; Sǎn is the water-regulating formula.
SafetyWǔ Líng Zhī is animal-derived and traditionally incompatible with Rén Shēn; verify identity and sourcing.
