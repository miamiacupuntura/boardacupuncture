Xiao Chai Hu Tang vs. Ban Xia Xie Xin Tang

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueXiao Chai Hu TangBan Xia Xie Xin Tang
Shared clueBoth harmonize complex patterns and may include nausea, poor appetite or vomiting.
Fast distinctionShaoyang disorder: alternating chills and fever, bitter taste, dry throat and hypochondriac fullness.Mixed Cold-Heat in the middle burner: focal epigastric distention with nausea, vomiting, borborygmus or diarrhea.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it matters“Nausea” is shared and therefore weak evidence. The alternating Shaoyang pattern points to Xiao Chai Hu; focal epigastric blockage with above-below disharmony points to Ban Xia Xie Xin.
Memory cueXiao Chai Hu turns the pivot; Ban Xia Xie Xin opens the knot.
SafetyNew fever with significant abdominal pain, persistent vomiting, black stools or dehydration requires medical assessment.
