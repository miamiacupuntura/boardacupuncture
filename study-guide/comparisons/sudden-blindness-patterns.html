Sudden Blindness Pattern Comparison

Diagnostic orientation

Core ideaThe source separates two TCM presentations, but any sudden severe decrease or loss of sight needs immediate retinal, vascular and neurologic evaluation.
Exam focusPurple stasis/trauma clues versus painful red Liver-Fire signs

Comparative differentiation

Pattern or presentationDecisive cluesRepresentative formula/herbStrategy
Qi stagnation and Blood stasisSudden vision loss with headache, distended eyes, irritability, dark-purple tongue and stasis spotsTong Qiao Huo Xue TangHistorical strategy: regulate Qi, move Blood and open orifices.
Exuberant Liver FireSudden loss with eye tenderness/pain on movement, headache, tinnitus, irritability, insomnia, bitter mouth and red-yellow tongueLong Dan Xie Gan TangHistorical strategy: clear Liver Fire.

Study rule

How to distinguishPurple/rough signs suggest stasis; red-yellow bitter-irritable signs suggest Liver Fire—but neither distinction should delay emergency eye care.
Memory cueSudden vision loss = emergency first; pattern differentiation only after urgent assessment.
SafetySudden vision loss, curtain/shadow, field defect, severe eye pain, flashes/floaters or neurologic symptoms is an emergency. Do not delay ophthalmology/stroke evaluation to try herbs or acupuncture.
