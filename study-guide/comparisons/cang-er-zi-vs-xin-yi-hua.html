Herb comparison

Xanthii Fructus (cang er zi) vs. Magnoliae Flos (xin yi hua)

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueCang Er ZiXin Yi Hua
Shared cluesBoth disperse Wind, unblock the nasal orifice and treat profuse nasal discharge with headache.
Decisive directionSpreads broadly: upward to the head, downward to the feet and knees, and outward to the skin.Its light, aromatic quality encourages clear Yang Qi to ascend to the head and brain.

Comparative answer key

CompareCang Er Zi (Xanthii Fructus)Xin Yi Hua (Magnoliae Flos)
CategoryWarm, Acrid Herbs that Release the Exterior.
Shared actionsDisperse Wind and open the nasal passages.
MovementSpreads, disseminates and unblocks in multiple directions.Light and aromatic; especially lifts clear Yang toward the head.
Distinctive extensionIts downward and outward reach supports use for painful obstruction and Damp skin disorders.Its upward emphasis makes the head, brain and nasal orifices the central recognition clues.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersBoth can answer a nasal-obstruction presentation, so the direction and breadth of action separate them.
Common confusionDo not reduce both herbs to “opens the nose.” Cang Er Zi has broader downward and outward reach; Xin Yi Hua has a lighter ascending emphasis.
How it may appearPainful obstruction or Damp skin disease favors Cang Er Zi. A light aromatic action that lifts clear Yang to the head favors Xin Yi Hua.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), p. 40. Source Exam Key CMID 8912.
