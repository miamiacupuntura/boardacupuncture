Herb comparison

Xing Ren vs. Ma Huang

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueXing RenMa Huang
Shared clueThe pair restores Lung-Qi flow disrupted by an external pathogen.
Fast distinctionSoft and inward-downward; unblocks Lung collaterals and presses Qi down.Hard and outward-expanding; opens pores and interstices to release the Exterior.

Comparative answer key

CompareXing RenMa Huang
Medicinal nameArmeniacae Semen.Ephedrae Herba.
CategoryHerbs that Relieve Coughing and Wheezing.Warm, Acrid Herbs that Release the Exterior.
Nature clueSoft.Hard.
DirectionPresses downward internally.Expands outward through the Exterior.
Lung actionUnblocks the Lung collaterals.Opens pores and interstices.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersThe herbs complement each other through opposite directions: Ma Huang releases outward while Xing Ren descends inward.
Memory cueMa Huang opens out; Xing Ren presses down.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), p. 439. Source Exam Key CMID 9016, questions 13–14.
