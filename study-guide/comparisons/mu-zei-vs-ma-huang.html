Herb comparison

Mu Zei vs. Ma Huang

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueMu ZeiMa Huang
Shared clueTheir physical form and lifting, spreading action can appear similar.
Fast distinctionNeutral Wind-Heat herb for Liver-Gallbladder Blood-level eye obstruction.Warm, acrid Wind-Cold herb that opens pores, causes strong sweating and controls cough and wheezing.

Comparative answer key

CompareMu ZeiMa Huang
Medicinal nameEquiseti hiemalis Herba.Ephedrae Herba.
CategoryCool, Acrid Herbs that Release the Exterior.Warm, Acrid Herbs that Release the Exterior.
Properties in sourceSweet, bitter and neutral; not acrid or hot.Warm and acrid.
Channel emphasisGallbladder and Liver.Lung and Exterior defensive Qi.
Distinctive actionDisperses Wind-Heat from the Blood level and dissipates superficial visual obstruction.Opens interstices and pores for copious sweating; controls wheezing and cough.
Decisive indicationVisual obstruction requiring restored vision.Wind-Cold Exterior excess with absence of sweating.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersTheir stems and spreading action may look similar, but the thermal pattern, target channels and clinical purpose are different.
Memory cueMu Zei lifts Blood-level Wind-Heat to the eyes; Ma Huang opens pores for Wind-Cold.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), p. 69. Source Exam Keys CMID 9016, questions 8 and 10, and CMID 9021, questions 1–2.
