Calm the Mind · FWB 13.1.1

Suan Zao Ren Tang vs. Tian Wang Bu Xin Dan vs. Gan Mai Da Zao Tang

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueSuan Zao Ren TangTian Wang Bu Xin DanGan Mai Da Zao Tang
Shared clueAll nourish deficiency and calm the Spirit, but the organ relationship and defining mental-emotional picture differ.
Fast distinctionLiver Blood and Heart Yin deficiency with Empty Heat: restless sleep and irritability.Heart-Kidney Yin-Blood deficiency with Heart Fire: insomnia, dryness, night sweats and palpitations.Zang Zao from Heart deficiency: crying, frequent yawning and emotional disorientation.

Comparative answer key

CompareSuan Zao Ren TangTian Wang Bu Xin DanGan Mai Da Zao Tang
Organ focusLiver and Heart.Heart and Kidney.Heart with Liver involvement.
Key ingredientsSuan Zao Ren, Chuan Xiong, Fu Ling, Zhi Mu and Gan Cao.Sheng Di, Tian Men Dong, Mai Men Dong, Xuan Shen, Dan Shen, Dang Gui, Ren Shen, Fu Ling, Wu Wei Zi, Yuan Zhi, Bai Zi Ren and Suan Zao Ren.Gan Cao, Fu Xiao Mai and Da Zao.
Decisive clueIrritable insomnia with dizziness and deficiency Heat.Frequent waking, palpitations, dry mouth, night sweats and five-center Heat.Uncontrolled sadness or crying, yawning and erratic behavior.
Tongue/pulseRed, dry tongue; wiry, thin or rapid pulse.Red dry tongue with scant coat; thin rapid pulse.May be red with scant coat; thin rapid pulse in the source set.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersInsomnia alone does not distinguish them. Liver-Blood restlessness, Heart-Kidney disconnection and the Zang Zao picture point in different directions.
Memory cueSuan Zao settles restless Liver Blood; Tian Wang reconnects Heart and Kidney; Gan Mai comforts crying.
SafetySevere agitation, suicidal thoughts, mania, new confusion or persistent insomnia requires medical or mental-health assessment. Modern Tian Wang Bu Xin Dan products should omit toxic cinnabar (Zhu Sha).
