Facial Pain Pattern Differentiation

Diagnostic orientation

Core ideaTemperature and associated eye/nasal signs separate the two presentations in the source.
Exam focusCold relief versus burning pain with red Heat signs

Comparative differentiation

Pattern or presentationDecisive cluesRepresentative formula/herbStrategy
External Wind-ColdSudden facial pain/spasm, worse with Cold, better with Heat, clear runny nose and floating-tight pulseChuan Xiong Cha Tiao SanDispel Wind-Cold and relieve pain.
Liver and Stomach FireSudden burning facial pain, irritability, red tearing eyes, thirst, constipation and red-yellow tongueXiong Zhi Shi Gao TangClear Liver-Stomach Fire and relieve pain.

Study rule

How to distinguishCold aggravation, Heat relief and clear discharge indicate Wind-Cold; burning pain, red eyes, thirst and constipation indicate Fire.
Memory cueCold hurts and warmth helps—Wind-Cold; face burns and eyes redden—Fire.
SafetyNew facial weakness, speech/vision change, severe sudden headache, rash near the eye, fever/swelling, dental infection or persistent new facial pain requires prompt medical evaluation.
