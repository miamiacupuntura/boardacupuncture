Trap 8 of 22

Bái Huā Shé Shé Cǎo 白花蛇舌草 vs. Bái Huā Shé 白花蛇

Dropping Shé Cǎo changes a Heat-clearing plant into an animal-derived Wind-expelling medicinal.

Memory rule

AnchorThe long name ends in Cǎo—grass. The short name ends in Shé—snake.

Correct identity

NameIdentityJob
Bái Huā Shé Shé CǎoScleromitrion diffusum / Hedyotis diffusa herbClears Heat, resolves toxicity and promotes urination.
Bái Huā ShéAgkistrodon / pit-viper materialExpels Wind and unblocks channels in traditional use.

Exam and safety

Exam keyCount the final words: Shé Shé Cǎo is the herb; Bái Huā Shé is the snake material.
Safety alertBái Huā Shé is an animal-derived, potentially toxic medicinal requiring expert sourcing and supervision; it is not a substitute for the plant.
