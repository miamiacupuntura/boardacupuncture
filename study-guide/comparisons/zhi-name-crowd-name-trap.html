Trap 14 of 22

Zhī/Zhǐ: Zhī Zǐ, Zhǐ Shí, Zhī Mǔ

Tone and final character distinguish Gardenia, immature bitter orange and Anemarrhena.

Memory rule

AnchorZǐ drains Fire; Shí breaks clumped Qi; Mǔ clears Heat while protecting Yin.

Correct identity

NameIdentityJob
Zhī Zǐ 栀子Gardeniae FructusDrains Fire from all three burners and clears Damp-Heat.
Zhǐ Shí 枳实Aurantii Fructus immaturusBreaks Qi stagnation and reduces focal fullness.
Zhī Mǔ 知母Anemarrhenae RhizomaClears Heat, enriches Yin and moistens dryness.

Exam and safety

Exam keyChoose by mechanism: Fire, forceful Qi stagnation, or Heat with fluid/Yin injury.
Safety alertDo not ignore the third syllable or tone; these herbs belong to different treatment categories.
