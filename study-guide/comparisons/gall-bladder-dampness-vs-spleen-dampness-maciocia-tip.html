Gall Bladder Dampness vs. Spleen Dampness

Use lateral localization versus central digestive heaviness to identify the affected system.

Fast differentiation clues

ClueGall Bladder DampnessSpleen Dampness
SharedBoth cause heaviness, nausea, poor appetite and a sticky tongue coating.
Fast distinctionGall Bladder: hypochondriac fullness and a unilateral—often right-sided—coat.Spleen: central abdominal fullness, loose stool and whole-body heaviness with broader greasy coat.

Clinical table

CompareGall Bladder DampnessSpleen Dampness
LocationLateral ribs/Gall Bladder region.Middle burner and digestion.
Formula associationSan Ren Tang plus Yin Chen Hao in the supplied table.Ping Wei San or Huo Xiang Zheng Qi San by context.

Exam and safety

Why it mattersLocation and coating distribution distinguish two presentations of Dampness.
Memory cueGall Bladder Dampness sits at the side; Spleen Dampness weighs the center.
SafetyPersistent pain, fever, jaundice, vomiting, weight loss or dehydration requires evaluation.
