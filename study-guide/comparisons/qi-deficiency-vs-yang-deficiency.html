Qi Deficiency vs. Yang Deficiency

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueQi DeficiencyYang Deficiency
Shared clueBoth cause fatigue, weakness, low voice, shortness of breath, spontaneous sweating and a weak pulse.
Fast distinctionFunctional weakness without pronounced Cold.Qi deficiency plus internal Cold, edema or failed fluid transformation.

Comparative answer key

CompareQi DeficiencyYang Deficiency
TemperatureUsually neutral; Cold may be mild.Clear aversion to cold and cold limbs.
FluidsTransport may be weak, but edema is not defining.Water retention and edema are common.
Tongue / pulsePale; weak.Pale, swollen and wet; deep-slow-weak.
Formula examplesSi Jun Zi Tang; Bu Zhong Yi Qi Tang by mechanism.Li Zhong Wan; Zhen Wu Tang by organ and fluid pattern.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersYang deficiency includes Qi deficiency plus loss of warmth and transformation.
Memory cueQi is weak; Yang is weak, cold and wet.
SafetySudden profound weakness, hypothermia, fainting, breathlessness or rapid edema requires urgent assessment.
