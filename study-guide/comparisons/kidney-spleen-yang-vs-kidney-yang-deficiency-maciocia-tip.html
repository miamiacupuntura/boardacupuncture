Kidney–Spleen Yang vs. Kidney Yang Deficiency

Recognize when Kidney coldness combines with failed Spleen transformation and chronic diarrhea.

Fast differentiation clues

ClueKidney Yang DeficiencyKidney–Spleen Yang Deficiency
SharedBoth cause cold back/limbs, fatigue, pale wet tongue and a deep weak pulse.
Fast distinctionKidney-centered: urinary, reproductive, low-back and water-metabolism signs predominate.Combined pattern: chronic diarrhea, undigested food, poor appetite and abdominal pain/fullness join the Kidney signs.

Clinical table

CompareKidney Yang DeficiencyKidney–Spleen Yang Deficiency
Added systemKidney warming and water transformation.Spleen transformation/transport also loses warmth.
Formula associationYou Gui Wan or Jin Gui Shen Qi Wan.Li Zhong Wan plus Jin Gui Shen Qi Wan in the supplied table.

Exam and safety

Why it mattersChronic digestive Cold signs—not loose stool alone—establish Spleen involvement.
Memory cueKidney Yang chills the root; Kidney–Spleen Yang also chills digestion.
SafetyPersistent diarrhea, dehydration, blood or black stool, severe pain, fever or weight loss requires medical evaluation.
