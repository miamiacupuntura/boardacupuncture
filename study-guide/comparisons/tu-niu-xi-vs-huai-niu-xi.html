Herb comparison

Tu Niu Xi vs. Huai Niu Xi

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueTu Niu XiHuai Niu Xi
Shared clueBoth invigorate Blood and direct downward.
Fast distinctionClears Heat, resolves toxicity and unblocks painful urinary dribbling, but does not tonify deficiency.Removes Blood stasis, tonifies the Kidneys and strengthens the lumbar region and knees.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersThe similar names and shared downward Blood action conceal opposite secondary emphases: Tu Niu Xi clears pathogenic Heat and toxicity, while Huai Niu Xi tonifies Kidney deficiency.
Memory cueTu clears toxicity; Huai supports the waist and knees.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), p. 214. Source Exam Key CMID 8977, questions 1–2.
