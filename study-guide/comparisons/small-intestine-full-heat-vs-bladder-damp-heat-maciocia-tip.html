Small Intestine Full Heat vs. Bladder Damp-Heat

Separate Heart-linked urinary Heat from a heavier, turbid Damp-Heat urinary pattern.

Fast differentiation clues

ClueSmall Intestine Full HeatBladder Damp-Heat
SharedBoth may cause burning, scant dark difficult urination and a red tongue with yellow coating.
Fast distinctionSmall Intestine Heat: tongue ulcers, thirst and Heart-channel Heat signs stand out.Bladder Damp-Heat: heaviness, turbid or foul urine and a thicker sticky yellow coat stand out.

Clinical table

CompareSmall Intestine Full HeatBladder Damp-Heat
Pathogen qualityPrimarily Fire/Heat.Heat complicated by Dampness.
Formula associationDao Chi San or Dao Chi Qing Xin Tang.Ba Zheng San.

Exam and safety

Why it mattersOral ulcers point upward to the Heart–Small Intestine axis; turbidity/heaviness point to Bladder Damp-Heat.
Memory cueSmall Intestine Heat links mouth to urine; Bladder Damp-Heat makes urine heavy and turbid.
SafetyFever, flank pain, vomiting, pregnancy, blood in urine or inability to urinate needs urgent medical care.
