Herb comparison

Hou Po vs. Zhi Shi

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueHou PoZhi Shi
Shared clueBoth treat obstruction from food, Dampness or Phlegm with chest-abdominal fullness, distention and constipation.
Fast distinctionWarmly dries Dampness while directing Qi downward.Coldly breaks Qi stasis so Phlegm disperses and Intestines move.

Comparative answer key

CompareHou PoZhi Shi
Botanical nameMagnoliae officinalis Cortex.Aurantii Fructus immaturus.
CategoryAromatic Herbs that Transform Dampness.Herbs that Regulate the Qi.
Temperature clueWarm.Cold.
Primary mechanismDirects Qi downward; its warmth dries Dampness and supports Stomach and Spleen.Breaks up Qi stasis so Qi moves, Phlegm disperses and Intestinal obstruction is guided out.
Flavor-action clueIts acrid flavor disperses clumped Qi to eliminate distention and fullness.The source emphasizes forceful regulation and breaking of stagnant Qi.
Decisive patternDamp obstruction with Qi constraint and Middle-Burner weakness.Cold-regulated breaking of Qi stasis with Phlegm and constipation.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersThe presenting symptoms overlap closely. Warm aromatic Dampness drying and Spleen-Stomach support favor Hou Po; cold, forceful breaking of Qi stasis and Intestinal guidance favor Zhi Shi.
Memory cueHou Po dries and descends; Zhi Shi breaks and clears obstruction.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), p. 472. Source Exam Key CMID 8986, questions 1–2.
