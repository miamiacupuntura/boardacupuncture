Trap 16 of 22

Cì Jí Lí 刺蒺藜 vs. Shā Yuàn Jí Lí 沙苑蒺藜

“Spiny” Tribulus treats Liver Wind/Yang; “sandy garden” Astragalus seed tonifies Kidney.

Memory rule

AnchorThe spiny one sharpens the Liver; the sandy one locks the Kidney.

Correct identity

NameIdentityJob
Cì Jí Lí 刺蒺藜Tribuli FructusCalms Liver Yang, courses Liver Qi, dispels Wind and relieves itching.
Shā Yuàn Jí Lí 沙苑蒺藜Astragali complanati SemenTonifies Kidney, secures Essence and brightens the eyes.

Exam and safety

Exam keyExcess-type Liver signs/itching point to Cì Jí Lí; deficiency leakage/weak vision point to Shā Yuàn Jí Lí.
Safety alertThese are botanically unrelated. Never shorten a prescription to Jí Lí when identity matters.
