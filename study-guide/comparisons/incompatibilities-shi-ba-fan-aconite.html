Chapter 5 of 7

Shí Bā Fǎn: Aconite Group

The first “boss and gang” of the traditional Eighteen Incompatibilities.

Master rule

Aconite sideWū Tóu, including Chuān Wū and Cǎo Wū; exam summaries commonly include the daughter root Fù Zǐ.
Opposing side“Four B’s and a gourd”: Bàn Xià, Bèi Mǔ, Bái Liǎn, Bái Jí and the Guā Lóu family.

What the names include

EntryExam scopeIdentity clue
Wū Tóu groupChuān Wū · Cǎo Wū · Fù ZǐAconite roots; processing does not erase the traditional pairing rule.
Bèi MǔChuān Bèi Mǔ · Zhè Bèi MǔFritillaria bulbs.
Guā Lóu familyGuā Lóu · Guā Lóu Pí · Guā Lóu Rén · Tiān Huā FěnFruit, peel, seed and root of Trichosanthes.
Other threeBàn Xià · Bái Liǎn · Bái JíKeep each pinyin identity distinct.

Memory and safety

Four B’s and a gourd bite aconite. Some classical literature contains exceptions; for standard exam questions, select “do not combine.” Aconite also requires exact identity, proper processing, controlled dosing and professional supervision because of cardiac and neurologic toxicity.
