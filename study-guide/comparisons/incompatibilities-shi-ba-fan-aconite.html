Master rule

Aconite sideWū Tóu, including Chuān Wū and Cǎo Wū; exam summaries commonly include the daughter root Fù Zǐ.
Opposing side“Four B’s and a gourd”: Bàn Xià, Bèi Mǔ, Bái Liǎn, Bái Jí and the Guā Lóu family.

Memory and safety

Four B’s and a gourd bite aconite. Some classical literature contains exceptions; for standard exam questions, select “do not combine.” Aconite also requires exact identity, proper processing, controlled dosing and professional supervision because of cardiac and neurologic toxicity.
