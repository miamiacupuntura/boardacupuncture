Herb comparison

Clematidis Radix (wei ling xian) vs. Gentianae macrophyllae Radix (qin jiao)

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueWei Ling XianQin Jiao
Shared cluesBoth dispel Wind-Dampness and treat pain with restricted movement, especially in the lower limbs.
Fast distinctionWarm, piercing and highly mobilizing; favors Cold painful obstruction with marked pain, numbness and severe movement restriction.Neutral to slightly cool and not overly drying; favors Wind-Dampness accompanied by Heat, especially when Dampness predominates.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersBoth treat lower-body Wind-Damp painful obstruction, but temperature and secondary actions separate them: forceful warmth and hardness-softening favor Wei Ling Xian; Heat, predominant Dampness, steaming bones or jaundice favor Qin Jiao.
Memory cueWei Ling Xian mobilizes Cold obstruction; Qin Jiao gently clears Damp-Heat.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), pp. 327 and 354. Source Exam Key CMID 9033, questions 2–7.
