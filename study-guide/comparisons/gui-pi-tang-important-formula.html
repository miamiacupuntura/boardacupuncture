Gui Pi Tang

Spleen Qi and Heart Blood deficiency, sometimes with Spleen failing to control Blood.

High-yield summary

CategoryTonify Spleen Qi · Nourish Heart Blood
PatternSpleen Qi and Heart Blood deficiency, sometimes with Spleen failing to control Blood.
ActionTonifies Qi, nourishes Blood, calms the Spirit and controls bleeding.
Decisive clueInsomnia, palpitations and poor memory together with fatigue, poor appetite and possible chronic bleeding.

Ingredients

Ren Shen, Huang Qi, Bai Zhu, Fu Shen (or Fu Ling), Suan Zao Ren, Long Yan Rou, Mu Xiang, Zhi Gan Cao, Dang Gui and Yuan Zhi, with Sheng Jiang and Da Zao.

Commercial preparations and lineages may vary; always verify the source being used.

Differentiation and memory

Do not confuseBa Zhen Tang broadly tonifies Qi and Blood; Gui Pi Tang specifically links Spleen Qi to Heart Blood, Shen and bleeding.
Memory cueRestore the Spleen so it can make Blood, hold Blood and house a settled Heart Spirit.

Safety

Heavy bleeding, black stool, fainting, chest symptoms or severe anemia signs require urgent medical evaluation.
