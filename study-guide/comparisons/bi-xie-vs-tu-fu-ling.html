Herb comparison

Dioscoreae hypoglaucae Rhizoma (bi xie) vs. Smilacis glabrae Rhizoma (tu fu ling)

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueBi XieTu Fu Ling
Shared clueBoth excel at dispelling Dampness and may be used for turbid painful urinary dribbling, toxic Damp-Heat sores and sores associated with Wind-Dampness.
Fast distinctionSeparates the pure from the turbid and directs turbidity downward.More strongly resolves toxicity while expelling Dampness.

Comparative answer key

CompareBi XieTu Fu Ling
Medicinal nameDioscoreae hypoglaucae Rhizoma.Smilacis glabrae Rhizoma.
CategoryHerbs that Drain Dampness.Herbs that Clear Heat and Resolve Toxicity.
Primary strengthEncourages separation of the pure from the turbid and directs turbidity downward.Expels Dampness and more strongly resolves toxicity.
Best clueCloudy urinary difficulty or vaginal discharge.Toxic sores, particularly the historical indication of syphilitic lesions.
Additional source associationEmphasis remains on turbid urinary and discharge patterns.The source also associates it with eliminating excessive mercury from the body.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersThe shared Dampness indications can obscure the category distinction: urinary turbidity points to Bi Xie; toxicity and severe sores point to Tu Fu Ling.
Memory cueBi Xie divides clear from turbid; Tu Fu Ling clears toxic Dampness.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), p. 302. Source Exam Key CMID 9084, questions 1–2.
