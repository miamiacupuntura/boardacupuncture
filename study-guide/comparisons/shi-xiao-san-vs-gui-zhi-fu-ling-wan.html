Shi Xiao San vs. Gui Zhi Fu Ling Wan

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueShi Xiao SanGui Zhi Fu Ling Wan
Shared clueBoth invigorate Blood and relieve pain from stasis, especially in gynecologic presentations.
Fast distinctionPain is the defining feature: severe fixed chest, epigastric or lower-abdominal pain from Blood stasis.A uterine mass or persistent uterine stasis is central, often with abdominal pain and irregular bleeding.

Comparative answer key

CompareShi Xiao SanGui Zhi Fu Ling Wan
Primary emphasisRapidly dispel stasis and stop pain.Gradually reduce uterine masses while harmonizing Blood.
Clinical tempoMore acute and pain-centered.More chronic and mass/bleeding-centered.
Key ingredientsPu Huang and Wu Ling Zhi.Gui Zhi, Fu Ling, Mu Dan Pi, Tao Ren and Bai Shao.
Decisive clueSevere fixed pain without a required mass.Palpable lower-abdominal mass or persistent uterine bleeding.
Exam shortcutSudden smile after stasis pain moves.Cinnamon-Poria pill for uterine stasis mass.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersBoth may treat dysmenorrhea and fixed pain. A discrete uterine mass or long-standing uterine stasis points toward Gui Zhi Fu Ling Wan; pain itself dominates Shi Xiao San.
Memory cueShi Xiao stops stasis pain; Gui Zhi Fu Ling reduces the uterine mass.
SafetyPregnancy, heavy uterine bleeding, severe pelvic pain or suspected ectopic pregnancy requires urgent professional evaluation; these are not self-treatment formulas.
