Exam focus and safety

CluesEarly graying, dizziness, blurred vision or Yin-deficiency bleeding without severe cloying.
Signature formulaÈr Zhì Wán — the formula is the pair.
TrapThis is a gentle two-herb Yin tonic, not the heavy anchoring shell strategy of Guī Bǎn + Biē Jiǎ.
PreparationCorrect botanical identity matters because both fruits/herbs can be confused with regional substitutes.
SafetyUse cautiously with chronic loose stools or Spleen deficiency. Unexplained bleeding still requires medical diagnosis.
