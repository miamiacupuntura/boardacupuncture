Stop Bleeding · FWB 11.1

Huang Tu Tang vs. Jiao Ai Tang

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueHuang Tu TangJiao Ai Tang
Shared clueBoth warm deficiency-Cold, nourish Blood and stop chronic bleeding, but their organ and channel focus differs.
Fast distinctionSpleen Yang fails to govern Blood: bleeding may arise from the stool, Stomach, nose or uterus.Chong and Ren deficiency-Cold with Blood deficiency: uterine bleeding is central.

Comparative answer key

CompareHuang Tu TangJiao Ai Tang
PatternSpleen-Yang deficiency with Empty Cold and failure to control Blood.Chong-Ren deficiency and Cold with Blood deficiency.
Bleeding cluePale blood with cold limbs, wan complexion, fatigue and no thirst.Pale, thin uterine bleeding without clots, with lower-back weakness and abdominal pain.
Key ingredientsFu Long Gan, Sheng Di Huang, E Jiao, Bai Zhu, Gan Cao, Fu Zi and Huang Qin.E Jiao, Ai Ye, Sheng Di Huang, Dang Gui, Chuan Xiong, Bai Shao and Gan Cao.
Tongue and pulsePale tongue with white coat; deep, thin and forceless pulse.Pale tongue with thin white coat; thin, frail or rough pulse.
Exam shortcutSpleen Yang cannot hold Blood anywhere.Cold-deficient Chong and Ren cannot hold uterine Blood.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersBoth contain E Jiao and Sheng Di Huang, but Huang Tu Tang warms Spleen Yang to govern Blood broadly; Jiao Ai Tang directly nourishes and warms the Chong-Ren and uterus.
Memory cueYellow Earth holds Blood through the Spleen; Gelatin-Mugwort holds Blood in the uterus.
SafetyUnexplained bleeding, black stool, vomiting blood, pregnancy bleeding or heavy uterine bleeding requires urgent medical assessment. Huang Tu Tang contains processed Fu Zi and requires expert supervision.
SourceSource bank: FWB 11.1, Comparison Formulas to Stop Bleeding, activity 10; corroborated with the supplied formula compendium.
