Lung Qi Deficiency vs. Lung Yin Deficiency

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueLung Qi DeficiencyLung Yin Deficiency
Shared clueBoth may cause chronic cough, shortness of breath, fatigue and a weak pulse.
Fast distinctionWeak function: low voice, spontaneous sweating and recurrent colds.Dryness and empty Heat: dry cough, scant sticky sputum, dry throat and night sweating.

Comparative answer key

CompareLung Qi DeficiencyLung Yin Deficiency
Core failureLung cannot govern Qi or secure the Exterior.Lung lacks moistening and cooling Yin.
CoughWeak, low and worse with exertion.Dry, hacking, with scant sputum.
Tongue / pulsePale; weak, especially right cun.Red and peeled; thin-rapid.
Formula examplesYu Ping Feng San for Defensive Qi; Bu Fei Tang.Sha Shen Mai Men Dong Tang; Bai He Gu Jin Tang.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersBoth are deficient coughs. Sweat timing, voice strength and dryness separate functional Qi weakness from tissue-fluid deficiency.
Memory cueQi cannot push or protect; Yin cannot moisten.
SafetyCough over three weeks, coughing blood, chest pain, low oxygen or weight loss requires medical evaluation.
