Trap 20 of 22

Huái 槐 vs. Huái 怀

The same sound means either pagoda tree or a geographic-quality prefix from Huáiqìng.

Memory rule

Anchor槐 is the tree; 怀 is the address.

Correct identity

NameIdentityJob
Huái Mǐ / Huái Huā 槐Sophora japonica bud/flowerCools Blood and stops bleeding, especially lower-body bleeding.
Huái Niú Xī / Huái Shān Yào 怀Huáiqìng regional designationNames the regional origin/quality of Achyranthes or Dioscorea, not a shared plant.

Exam and safety

Exam keyRead the character: tree radical 槐 identifies Sophora; heart radical 怀 marks the place-name prefix.
Safety alertA regional prefix does not create a botanical family. Confirm the entire medicinal name and species.
