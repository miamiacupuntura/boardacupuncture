Supplemental family 6 of 6

Chinaberry — Fruit Moves Qi, Bark Expels Worms

Fruit and bark from chinaberry share toxicity but have different clinical jobs.

Memory rule

AnchorChuān Liàn fruit treats Liver-Qi pain; Kǔ Liàn bark drives parasites.

Identity decides the job

MedicinalIdentityJob
Chuān Liàn Zǐ 川楝子Melia fruitMoves Liver Qi, clears Heat and relieves flank/hernia pain.
Kǔ Liàn Pí 苦楝皮Melia root/stem barkExpels intestinal parasites; generally outside the core list.

Exam and safety

Exam keyQi pain points to fruit; worms point to bark.
SafetyBoth are toxic; Chuān Liàn Zǐ has liver-injury concern. Jaundice, dark urine, right-upper pain or severe fatigue requires stopping exposure and urgent evaluation.
