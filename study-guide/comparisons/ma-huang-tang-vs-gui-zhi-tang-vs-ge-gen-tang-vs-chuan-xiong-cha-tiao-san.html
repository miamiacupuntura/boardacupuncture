Study guide · Exam focus

Why it matters“Releases Wind-Cold” is not enough. Sweat distinguishes excess from deficiency, while neck stiffness and headache location redirect the answer.
Memory cueMa Huang = no sweat; Gui Zhi = sweat; Ge Gen = neck; Cha Tiao = head.
SafetyMa Huang formulas require cardiovascular and medication screening. Sudden severe headache, neurologic deficit, fever with neck stiffness or breathing difficulty requires urgent assessment.
