Li Zhong Wan vs. Si Ni Tang

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueLi Zhong WanSi Ni Tang
Shared clueBoth warm internal Cold and may treat abdominal pain, vomiting, diarrhea, absence of thirst and a deep pulse.
Fast distinctionMiddle-burner deficiency-Cold: digestive signs dominate.Devastated Yang: severe cold limbs, exhaustion and a minute or faint pulse dominate.

Comparative answer key

CompareLi Zhong WanSi Ni Tang
LocationSpleen and Stomach.Heart-Kidney Yang and the whole body.
Primary actionWarms and tonifies the middle burner.Restores Yang and rescues collapse.
Decisive clueChronic digestive Cold with abdominal pain relieved by warmth/pressure.Cold extending up the limbs with profound weakness.
Exam shortcutCold center.Failing fire everywhere.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersThe digestive symptoms may be identical. Distribution and severity distinguish localized middle-burner Cold from systemic Yang failure.
Memory cueLi Zhong warms the center; Si Ni rescues the system.
SafetySi Ni Tang contains Fu Zi and requires processed material and qualified supervision. Suspected shock is an emergency.
SourceSource bank: FWB 7.1 series, Comparison Formulas to Warm the Interior; corroborated with individual profiles.
