Formula name trap

Xie Xin Tang vs. Ban Xia Xie Xin Tang

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueXie Xin TangBan Xia Xie Xin Tang
Shared clueBoth may present with epigastric focal distention and use bitter medicinals to drain pathological Heat downward.
Fast distinctionPure excess Heat/Fire: irritability, red face, possible bleeding and a rapid-forceful pulse.Mixed Cold-Heat with middle deficiency: nausea, vomiting, borborygmus and diarrhea.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersThe shared “Xie Xin” name and fullness clue conceal opposite complexity: one is pure Heat, the other mixed Cold-Heat with deficiency.
Memory cueXie Xin drains Fire; Ban Xia Xie Xin unties mixed Cold-Heat.
SafetyVomiting blood, black stools, severe pain or persistent vomiting requires urgent medical evaluation.
