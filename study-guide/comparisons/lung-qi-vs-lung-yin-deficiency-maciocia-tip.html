Batch 3 · Lung · Tip 1 of 10

Lung Qi Deficiency vs. Lung Yin Deficiency

Separate weak respiratory function and Defensive Qi from dry Lung tissue with empty Heat.

Fast differentiation clues

ClueLung Qi DeficiencyLung Yin Deficiency
SharedBoth may cause chronic cough, shortness of breath, fatigue and a weak pulse.
Fast distinctionWeak function: low voice, spontaneous sweating, recurrent colds and cough worse with exertion.Dry tissue and empty Heat: dry cough, scant sputum, hoarse voice, dry throat and night sweating.

Clinical table

CompareLung Qi DeficiencyLung Yin Deficiency
Core failureLung cannot govern Qi or secure the Exterior.Lung lacks moistening/cooling Yin.
Tongue / pulsePale; weak, especially right cun.Red and peeled; thin-rapid.
Formula associationRen Shen Bu Fei Tang or Bu Fei Tang.Yang Yin Qing Fei Tang by presentation.

Exam and safety

Why it mattersSweat timing, voice strength and dryness separate functional Qi weakness from Yin-fluid deficiency.
Memory cueQi cannot push or protect; Yin cannot moisten.
SafetyBreathlessness at rest, blue lips, chest pain, low oxygen or coughing blood requires urgent evaluation.
