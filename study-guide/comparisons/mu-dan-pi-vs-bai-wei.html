Moutan Cortex (mu dan pi) vs. Cynanchi atrati Radix (bai wei)

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersThe herbs share Blood-cooling and deficiency-Heat treatment, so Blood invigoration versus urination is the decisive distinction.
Memory cueMu Dan Pi moves Blood; Bai Wei moves urine.
