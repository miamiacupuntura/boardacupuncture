Liver Overacting on Spleen vs. Liver Overacting on Stomach

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueLiver Overacting on SpleenLiver Overacting on Stomach
Shared clueBoth arise when constrained Liver Qi disrupts digestion; symptoms fluctuate with stress and the pulse is often wiry.
Fast distinctionTransport fails below: abdominal pain/distention with loose stool or alternating diarrhea and constipation, often relieved after defecation.Stomach Qi rebels upward: epigastric/rib-side distention with belching, nausea, vomiting or acid regurgitation.

Comparative answer key

CompareLiver Overacting on SpleenLiver Overacting on Stomach
Affected functionSpleen transformation and stool formation.Stomach receiving and descending.
DirectionIrregular movement through intestines.Rebellious upward movement.
Decisive clueEmotion-linked bowel change.Emotion-linked belching, nausea or reflux.
Formula examplesTong Xie Yao Fang.Chai Hu Shu Gan San, Ban Xia Hou Po Tang or Zuo Jin Wan by thermal branch.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersStress is shared. Stool disturbance localizes to Spleen; belching, nausea, vomiting and reflux localize to Stomach.
Memory cueLiver-Spleen disrupts stool; Liver-Stomach pushes upward.
SafetyPersistent vomiting, blood, black stool, progressive swallowing difficulty, severe localized pain or unexplained weight loss needs medical evaluation.
