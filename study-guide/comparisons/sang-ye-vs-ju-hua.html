Herb comparison

Mori Folium (sang ye) vs. Chrysanthemi Flos (ju hua)

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueSang YeJu Hua
Shared clueBoth are cool, acrid Exterior-releasing herbs used to disperse Wind-Heat.
Fast distinctionStronger at spreading and dispersing; moistens the Lungs, stops cough and bleeding.Stronger at calming the Liver and brightening the eyes; mildly resolves toxicity.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersBoth disperse Wind-Heat, but Lung dryness, cough or bleeding favors Sang Ye, while Liver and eye symptoms favor Ju Hua.
Memory cueSang Ye moistens Lung and stops bleeding; Ju Hua clears Liver and brightens eyes.
