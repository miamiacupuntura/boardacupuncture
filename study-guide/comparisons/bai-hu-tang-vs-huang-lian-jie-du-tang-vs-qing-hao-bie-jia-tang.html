Bai Hu Tang vs. Huang Lian Jie Du Tang vs. Qing Hao Bie Jia Tang

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueBai Hu TangHuang Lian Jie Du TangQing Hao Bie Jia Tang
Shared clueAll clear Heat, but they target different depth, mechanism and constitutional state.
Fast distinctionBlazing excess Qi-level Heat with the four greats.Severe Toxic Fire affecting all three burners.Deficiency Heat after Yin has been injured by febrile disease.

Comparative answer key

CompareBai Hu TangHuang Lian Jie Du TangQing Hao Bie Jia Tang
Heat typeExcess Qi-level Heat.Excess Toxic Heat/Fire.Deficiency Heat hidden in Yin.
Decisive signsGreat fever, great thirst, great sweating and a great/flooding pulse.High fever, irritability, delirium, bleeding or toxic inflammatory signs.Night fever, emaciation and fever receding without sweat.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersThe word “Heat” does not select the formula. First classify excess versus deficiency, then identify Qi-level blaze versus systemic toxicity.
Memory cueBai Hu = Qi blaze; Huang Lian = toxic Fire; Qing Hao = Yin-hidden Heat.
SafetyHigh fever, altered mental status, bleeding, severe infection or dehydration requires urgent biomedical assessment.
