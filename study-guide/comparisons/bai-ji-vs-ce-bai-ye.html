Herb comparison

Bletillae Rhizoma (bai ji) vs. Platycladi Cacumen (ce bai ye)

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueBai JiCe Bai Ye
Shared cluesBoth restrain and stop bleeding and are grouped as hemostatic herbs in the source.
Decisive clueStronger restraining/astringent action and Aconite incompatibility.Stronger phlegm-expelling and cough-stopping action, plus hair-growth and topical-burn clues.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersThe shared hemostatic action makes the degree of restraint versus the respiratory and hair indications decisive.
Common confusionBai Ji supplies the stronger restraint and Aconite-incompatibility clues; Ce Bai Ye supplies phlegm, cough and hair-growth clues.
Cautions in sourceBai Ji: cautions with Wind invasion and hemoptysis, early Lung abscess, Lung/Stomach Excess or toxic Heat, and ulcerated sores. Ce Bai Ye: cautions involving absence of Heat/Dampness, prolonged or large use, deficiency bleeding with Blood Stasis, Spleen/Stomach Deficiency Cold, pregnancy and breastfeeding.
Evidence noteThe Western-use material is reproduced as exam-source content, not verified here as clinical guidance or a recommendation for cancer treatment.
Source question formatsQuestions 8–11 are Matching.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), p. 587; additional links cited by the source activity. Source Exam Key CMID 8908.
