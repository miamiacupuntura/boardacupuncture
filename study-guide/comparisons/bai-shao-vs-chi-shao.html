Herb comparison

Paeoniae Radix alba (bai shao) vs. Paeoniae Radix rubra (chi shao)

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueBai ShaoChi Shao
Shared cluesWhite and red peony root come from closely related plants. Both are cold in nature, enter the Blood and Liver, and can stop pain.
Core contrastTonifies and restrains: nourishes Blood and Liver Yin, preserves Yin and softens the Liver.Drains and disperses: clears Heat, cools Blood, invigorates Blood and removes stasis.
Pain patternCramping from Liver Yin or Blood deficiency and poor nourishment of muscles and tendons.Pain from Blood stagnation, often with Heat or visible signs of stasis.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersThe two herbs share a botanical relationship, cold nature, Liver-Blood affinity and pain indication, but move in opposite therapeutic directions: Bai Shao nourishes and restrains; Chi Shao clears and disperses.
Common confusionBoth can appear in Blood and pain questions. Deficiency, cramping, Yin preservation or sweating favors Bai Shao; Heat, stasis, masses, trauma or dispersing action favors Chi Shao.
High-yield formula clueGui Zhi Fu Ling Wan appears in both source lists. Si Wu Tang and Shao Yao Gan Cao Tang favor Bai Shao; the Zhu Yu Tang family and Xi Jiao Di Huang Tang favor Chi Shao.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), p. 623.
