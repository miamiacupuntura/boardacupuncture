Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersMouth lesions alone are insufficient. Teeth/gums point to Stomach; dryness and Kidney signs point to Yu Nu; urinary burning points to Dao Chi.
Memory cueQing Wei = gums; Jade Woman = Stomach Fire over dry Kidney; Dao Chi = mouth plus urine.
SafetyDental infection with facial swelling or fever and urinary symptoms with fever, flank pain, pregnancy or retention requires urgent medical evaluation.
