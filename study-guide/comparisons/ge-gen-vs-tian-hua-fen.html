Herb comparison

Puerariae Radix (ge gen) vs. Trichosanthis Radix (tian hua fen)

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueGe GenTian Hua Fen
Shared clueBoth treat fever and wasting-thirsting disorder involving Lung and Stomach fluids.
Fast distinctionAcridly disperses Heat, raises Yin fluids and releases the muscle layer.Cold directly prevails over Heat and moistens Lung-Stomach Dryness.

Comparative answer key

CompareGe GenTian Hua Fen
Botanical namePuerariae Radix.Trichosanthis Radix.
CategoryCool, Acrid Herbs that Release the Exterior.Herbs that Clear Heat.
How it treats feverAcrid dispersal of Heat.Cold prevailing over Heat.
How it relieves thirstRaises Stomach Yang fluids to nourish the Lungs; lifts Yin Qi.Increases what is lacking and moistens Lung and Stomach Dryness.
Additional actionDisperses Exterior pathogens, releases the muscle layer and can lift Yin Qi to stop diarrhea.Its soft, moistening, bitter-cold nature expels Lung and Stomach Heat; it is not described as lifting Yin Qi.
Cautionary clueOveruse can exhaust Stomach fluids according to the source.The comparison does not give the same overuse warning.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersBoth answer fever-and-thirst questions, but ascent, muscle release and diarrhea point to Ge Gen, while bitter-cold moistening of Lung-Stomach Dryness points to Tian Hua Fen.
Memory cueGe Gen raises and releases; Tian Hua Fen cools and moistens.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), p. 72. Source Exam Key CMID 8955, questions 1–4, and CMID 9077, questions 1–4.

Possible exam questions · Study prompts

  • Which herb treats fever through its cold nature?
  • Which herb lifts Yin Qi and can stop diarrhea?
  • Which herb moistens Lung and Stomach Dryness?
