Jīng Jiè + Fáng Fēng

荆芥 + 防风

One picture, one job

PictureThe mild wind-chasing duo.
Combined jobDispel Wind from the surface and stop itching across Wind-Cold, Wind-Heat, rash and early sore presentations.
CategoryDispel Wind from the Exterior

How the pair works

HerbRole within the pair
Jīng JièLightly vents the Exterior and rashes; the charred form changes toward stopping bleeding.
Fáng FēngExpels Wind while relieving pain, spasm and itching.

Exam focus and safety

CluesItching or migrating superficial symptoms without the harsh sweating action of stronger Exterior herbs.
Signature formulaJīng Fáng Bài Dú Sǎn; Xiāo Fēng Sǎn.
TrapThe pair is broader than a simple Wind-Cold duo; both can appear in rash and Wind-Heat contexts when balanced.
PreparationUse charred Jīng Jiè only when the intended job changes to hemostasis.
SafetyA rapidly spreading rash, breathing difficulty, facial swelling, blistering or fever requires urgent assessment.
