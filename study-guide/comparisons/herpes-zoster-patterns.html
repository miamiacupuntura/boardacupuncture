Pathology or symptom comparison

Herpes Zoster Clinical Comparison

Diagnostic orientation

Core ideaThe source gives one main active presentation. The key modern comparison is active vesicular shingles versus persistent pain after the rash resolves.
Exam focusUnilateral band-like vesicles, burning pain and timing of antiviral treatment

Comparative differentiation

Pattern or presentationDecisive cluesRepresentative formula/herbStrategy
Active Liver Fire and Damp-HeatUnilateral band of erythema/papules becoming clear then turbid vesicles, with burning/stabbing pain, bitter mouth, dark urine and constipationLong Dan Xie Gan TangHistorical strategy: drain Liver Fire and Damp-Heat.
Postherpetic painBurning, stabbing or electric pain persists after vesicles crust and the skin healsSource modification: Xiao Yao San with Blood-moving/pain-relieving herbsBiomedical priority: neuropathic-pain assessment and treatment.

Study rule

How to distinguishNew grouped vesicles in a one-sided dermatome indicate active shingles; pain continuing after skin healing indicates postherpetic neuralgia.
Memory cueOne-sided band + blisters—active zoster; healed skin + nerve pain—postherpetic neuralgia.
SafetySeek same-day care because antivirals work best within 72 hours. Eye/forehead/nose-tip or ear involvement, weakness, widespread rash, pregnancy or immunosuppression requires urgent evaluation.
