Dan Shen Yin

Blood stasis with Qi stagnation in the chest or epigastrium.

High-yield summary

CategoryInvigorate Blood · Move Qi
PatternBlood stasis with Qi stagnation in the chest or epigastrium.
ActionInvigorates Blood, dispels stasis, moves Qi and relieves pain.
Decisive clueFixed chest or epigastric pain in which Blood stasis is primary but Qi constraint is also present.

Ingredients

Dan Shen, Tan Xiang and Sha Ren.

Commercial preparations and lineages may vary; always verify the source being used.

Differentiation and memory

Do not confuseXue Fu Zhu Yu Tang treats broader chest Blood stasis with a larger multi-herb strategy; Dan Shen Yin is a focused three-herb combination.
Memory cueDan Shen moves Blood; Tan Xiang and Sha Ren move the Qi around it.

Safety

Possible cardiac chest pain is an emergency. Dan Shen may interact with anticoagulant or antiplatelet medicines.
