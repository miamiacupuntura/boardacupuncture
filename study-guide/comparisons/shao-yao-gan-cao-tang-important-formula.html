Shao Yao Gan Cao Tang

Blood and fluid deficiency failing to nourish the sinews.

High-yield summary

CategoryNourish Blood/Yin · Relax Spasm
PatternBlood and fluid deficiency failing to nourish the sinews.
ActionNourishes Blood and Yin, harmonizes the Liver and relieves spasm and pain.
Decisive cluePainful muscle cramping or spasm, especially in the legs, that improves as the sinews relax.

Ingredients

Bai Shao and Zhi Gan Cao.

Commercial preparations and lineages may vary; always verify the source being used.

Differentiation and memory

Do not confuseXiao Jian Zhong Tang adds warming, Qi-supporting ingredients for deficiency-Cold abdominal pain; Shao Yao Gan Cao Tang is the focused two-herb antispasmodic pair.
Memory cueSour Bai Shao gathers and nourishes; sweet Gan Cao relaxes and harmonizes.

Safety

New severe cramping, weakness, swelling, dark urine or persistent pain requires evaluation. Gan Cao may worsen edema, hypertension or low potassium.
