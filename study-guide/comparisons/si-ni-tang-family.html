Study rule

How to chooseAll members require genuine deep Cold and collapse; apparent Heat with a nearly absent pulse points to Tong Mai, while fluid/Qi depletion points to Ren Shen.
Memory cueSi Ni = rescue; Tong Mai = pulse and false Heat; Jia Ren Shen = depleted fluids/Qi; Hui Yang = broader collapse.
SafetyThese are high-risk emergency-level presentations. Fu Zi must be properly processed and pre-decocted; suspected shock, altered consciousness or unstable vital signs requires emergency care, not self-treatment.
