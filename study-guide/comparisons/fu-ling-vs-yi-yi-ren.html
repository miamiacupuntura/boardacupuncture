Herb comparison

Fu Ling vs. Yi Yi Ren

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueFu LingYi Yi Ren
Shared clueBoth strengthen the Spleen and facilitate removal of Dampness for Spleen deficiency with excessive Dampness or edema.
Fast distinctionNeutral; augments Heart and Spleen and calms the Spirit.Cold; clears Heat, treats abscesses and removes Dampness from bones, muscles and sinews.

Comparative answer key

CompareFu LingYi Yi Ren
Medicinal namePoria.Coicis Semen.
CategoryHerbs that Drain Dampness.Herbs that Drain Dampness.
NatureNeutral.Cold.
Distinctive actionAugments the Heart and Spleen and calms the Spirit.Clears Heat and removes Dampness at the level of bones, muscles and sinews.
Decisive indicationSpleen deficiency with Dampness when tonification or Spirit calming is also needed.Lung or intestinal abscess, Lung atrophy or Dampness affecting bones, muscles and sinews.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersBoth address Spleen-Dampness and edema, but Spirit signs favor Fu Ling while Heat, abscess or musculoskeletal Dampness favors Yi Yi Ren.
Memory cueFu Ling calms; Yi Yi Ren cools and reaches the limbs.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), p. 276. Source Exam Key CMID 8954, question 3.
