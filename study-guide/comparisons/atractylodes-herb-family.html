Family 16 of 20

Atractylodes — Bái Builds, Cāng Dries

White Atractylodes supplements Spleen; dark Atractylodes aggressively dries Dampness.

One picture, one rule

RuleBái builds; Cāng dries.

The part decides the job

MedicinalPart / identityJob
Bái Zhú 白术Atractylodes macrocephala rhizomeTonifies Spleen Qi, dries Dampness, stops spontaneous sweating and calms fetus.
Cāng Zhú 苍术Atractylodes lancea rhizomeStrongly dries Dampness, induces sweating, expels Wind-Damp and benefits eyes.

Exam and safety

Exam keyDeficiency with Dampness = Bái Zhú; excess Damp obstruction with thick greasy coat = Cāng Zhú.
SafetyCāng Zhú is warm and drying and can injure Yin/fluids; Bái Zhú still requires pattern-appropriate use.
