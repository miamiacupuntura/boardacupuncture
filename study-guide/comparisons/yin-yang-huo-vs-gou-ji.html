Herb comparison

Epimedii Herba (yin yang huo) vs. Cibotii Rhizoma (gou ji)

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueYin Yang HuoGou Ji
Shared clueBoth enter Liver and Kidney, strengthen sinews and bones and expel Wind-Damp painful obstruction.
Fast distinctionMore powerfully tonifies the Gate of Vitality and treats impotence.More powerfully expels Wind-Dampness and strengthens sinews and bones.

Comparative answer key

CompareYin Yang HuoGou Ji
Botanical nameEpimedii Herba.Cibotii Rhizoma.
CategoryHerbs that Tonify the Yang.Herbs that Tonify the Yang.
Properties and channelsWarm, acrid and sweet; Liver and Kidney.Warm, bitter and sweet; Liver and Kidney.
Primary emphasisTonifies Kidney Yang and the Gate of Vitality.Strengthens Liver-Kidney sinews, bones and spine while expelling Wind-Dampness.
Decisive indicationsImpotence, spermatorrhea, urinary dribbling, memory lapse, weak back or legs, hemiplegia or numbness.Stiffness or weakness of spine and legs, Wind-Damp painful obstruction, incontinence or vaginal discharge.
Formula or use clueEr Xian Tang; the source notes use as a substitute for Fu Zi or Rou Gui.The source does not assign a formula in these questions.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersBoth tonify Yang and treat Wind-Damp Bi, but reproductive Kidney-Yang signs favor Yin Yang Huo while spine, sinew and bone weakness favors Gou Ji.
Memory cueYin Yang Huo powers Yang; Gou Ji grips bones and expels Dampness.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), p. 794. Source Exam Keys CMID 8956 and CMID 9113, questions 1–3.
