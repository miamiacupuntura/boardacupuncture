Pathology or symptom comparison

Constipation Pattern Differentiation

Diagnostic orientation

Core ideaDetermine whether stool is dry, whether expulsion lacks force, and whether Heat, stagnation, Blood deficiency or Cold accompanies it.
Exam focusDryness, effort, distention and Cold/deficiency signs

Comparative differentiation

Pattern or presentationDecisive cluesRepresentative formula/herbStrategy
Heat constipationDry stool, thirst, halitosis, abdominal fullness and yellow dry coatMa Zi Ren WanClear Heat and moisten Intestines.
Qi stagnationConstipation with frequent belching and chest/abdominal distentionLiu Mo YinMove Qi and stagnation.
Qi deficiencyDifficult expulsion without marked dryness; fatigue, short breath and sweatingHuang Qi TangBoost Qi and free stool.
Blood deficiencyDry hard stool with pallor, dizziness and palpitationsRun Chang WanNourish Blood and moisten Intestines.
Cold / Kidney-Yang deficiencyDifficult stool with clear urine, cold limbs and weak painful low backJi Chuan JianWarm Kidney Yang and moisten bowels.

Study rule

How to distinguishDry yellow Heat differs from weak pushing Qi deficiency; pallor/dizziness identifies Blood deficiency; distention/belching identifies Qi stagnation; Cold limbs and lumbar weakness identify Kidney Yang.
Memory cueHeat—dry/yellow; Qi stagnation—distended; Qi deficiency—cannot push; Blood—pale/dry; Cold—cold back.
SafetyNo stool or gas with severe distention, vomiting, blood, fever, weight loss or new constipation after age 50 requires prompt medical evaluation.
