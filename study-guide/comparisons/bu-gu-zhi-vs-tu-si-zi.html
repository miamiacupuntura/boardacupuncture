Herb comparison

Psoraleae Fructus (bu gu zhi) vs. Cuscutae Semen (tu si zi)

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueBu Gu ZhiTu Si Zi
Shared clueBoth tonify Kidney Yang, support the Spleen and stop diarrhea; both are dried ripe seeds in the source.
Fast distinctionVery warming and stronger for Kidney Yang and diarrhea.Neutral and gentler; tonifies both Kidney Yang and Yin, secures Essence, nourishes Liver vision and calms the fetus.

Comparative answer key

CompareBu Gu ZhiTu Si Zi
Medicinal namePsoraleae Fructus.Cuscutae Semen.
CategoryHerbs that Tonify the Yang.
Nature and tasteVery warm, bitter and acrid.Neutral, acrid and sweet.
ChannelsKidney and Spleen.Liver and Kidney.
Primary strengthStrongly warms Kidney Yang and has the more powerful diarrhea-stopping effect.Gently warms Yang while also tonifying Kidney Yin and securing Essence.
Distinctive indicationsFailure of Lung and Kidney to grasp Qi; also used topically for alopecia in the source.Nourishes Liver vision and calms the fetus in addition to treating Spleen-Kidney Yang deficiency diarrhea.
Formula cluesHei Xi Dan; Si Shen Wan.Shou Tai Wan; Tu Si Zi Wan; You Gui Wan; Zhu Jing Wan.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersBoth may answer Kidney-Yang diarrhea questions, but intense warming and stronger diarrhea control favor Bu Gu Zhi; balanced Yin-Yang support, vision or pregnancy clues favor Tu Si Zi.
Memory cueBu Gu Zhi burns warmer and binds diarrhea; Tu Si Zi tonifies gently and secures Essence.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), p. 801. Source Exam Key CMID 9085, questions 4–7.

Possible exam questions · Study prompts

  • Which Yang-tonifying and diarrhea-stopping actions do both herbs share?
  • Which herb is very warm and more powerful?
  • Which herb also tonifies Kidney Yin and nourishes vision?
  • Which herb calms the fetus?
