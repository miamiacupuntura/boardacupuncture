Herb comparison

Dianthi Herba (qu mai) vs. Polygoni avicularis Herba (bian xu)

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueQu MaiBian Xu
Shared cluesBoth clear Heat, facilitate urination and unblock painful urinary dribbling, especially burning, painful and difficult urination.
Fast distinctionGuides Small-Intestine Heat outward; favors burning pain, blood in the urine and amenorrhea.Clears Damp-Heat from the Bladder; favors scanty, dark, difficult urine and Damp-Heat jaundice.

Comparative answer key

CompareQu MaiBian Xu
Botanical nameDianthi Herba.Polygoni avicularis Herba.
Common categoryHerbs that Drain Dampness.
Shared actionsClear Heat, facilitate urination and unblock painful urinary dribbling.
Primary focusFacilitates the Small Intestine in guiding internal Heat outward.Clears Damp-Heat from the Bladder.
Urinary clueBetter for burning pain during urination or blood in the urine due to Heat.Superior when urine is scanty, dark and difficult to pass because Dampness is intermixed with Heat.
Additional actionBreaks up Blood stasis and unblocks the channels.Also treats Damp-Heat jaundice.
Additional indicationAmenorrhea.Damp-Heat jaundice.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersBoth treat painful urinary disorders, so the decisive clues are the organ focus and the secondary action outside the urinary tract.
Memory cueQu Mai: Small Intestine, burning or bloody urine, Blood stasis and amenorrhea; Bian Xu: Bladder Damp-Heat, scanty dark urine and jaundice.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), p. 290. Source Exam Key CMID 9037, questions 1–2.
