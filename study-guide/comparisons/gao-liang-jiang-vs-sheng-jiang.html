Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersBoth herbs warm the middle, disperse cold, and settle nausea. Gao Liang Jiang belongs to Herbs that Warm the Interior and Expel Cold, while Sheng Jiang belongs to Warm, Acrid Herbs that Release the Exterior. The distinction depends on the clinical context, not the shared action alone.
Common confusionVomiting and loss of appetite appear in both clue sets. Abdominal pain, diarrhea and acid reflux favor Gao Liang Jiang; common cold, nasal congestion and coughing favor Sheng Jiang.
How it may appearA vignette emphasizing abdominal pain, diarrhea and acid reflux could point to Gao Liang Jiang. A vignette emphasizing common cold, nasal congestion and coughing could point to Sheng Jiang. The distinction may also be tested through categories or formula associations.
Why a switched match is wrongReversing the match assigns the Warm the Interior associations to Sheng Jiang and the Release the Exterior associations to Gao Liang Jiang, changing the source classification.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), p. 695. Source Exam Keys CMID 8967, questions 1–7, and CMID 9061, questions 6–12.
