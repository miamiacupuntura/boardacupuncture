Formula comparison

Ping Wei San vs. Li Zhong Wan

Study tip · Clues to help answer

CluePing Wei SanLi Zhong Wan
Shared clueBoth regulate the middle burner and treat poor appetite, epigastric or abdominal fullness, nausea, vomiting and diarrhea.
Fast distinctionExcess Dampness: heaviness, pronounced fullness and a thick greasy coating.Deficiency-Cold: chronic loose stool, abdominal pain improved by warmth/pressure, fatigue and a pale tongue.

Comparative answer key

ComparePing Wei SanLi Zhong Wan
Root patternDampness obstructing Spleen and Stomach.Spleen-Stomach Qi/Yang deficiency with internal Cold.
Primary actionDries Dampness and moves Qi.Warms and tonifies the middle burner.
Tongue clueThick, greasy white coating.Pale tongue with white coating.
Exam shortcutExcess wet blockage.Deficient cold center.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersSimilar digestive symptoms can result from a pathogen blocking the center or from the center lacking warmth and strength.
Memory cuePing Wei dries what is too much; Li Zhong warms what is too weak.
SafetySevere abdominal pain, persistent vomiting, gastrointestinal bleeding or dehydration requires medical evaluation.
SourceSource bank: FWB 5.2, Comparison Formulas to Expel Damp; corroborated with FWB 7.1 and individual profiles.
