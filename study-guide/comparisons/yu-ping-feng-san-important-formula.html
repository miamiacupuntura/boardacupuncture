Yu Ping Feng San

Wei Qi deficiency with an unstable exterior.

High-yield summary

CategoryTonify Wei Qi · Stabilize the Exterior
PatternWei Qi deficiency with an unstable exterior.
ActionTonifies Qi, strengthens the Spleen, stabilizes the exterior and stops spontaneous sweating.
Decisive clueFrequent colds, spontaneous sweating and aversion to Wind in a chronically deficient patient.

Ingredients

Huang Qi, Bai Zhu and Fang Feng.

Commercial preparations and lineages may vary; always verify the source being used.
