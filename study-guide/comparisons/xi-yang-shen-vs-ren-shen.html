Herb comparison

Panacis quinquefolii Radix (xi yang shen) vs. Ginseng Radix (ren shen)

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueXi Yang ShenRen Shen
Shared actionsBoth tonify Qi and generate fluids.
Fast distinctionCool Qi-Yin tonic: choose it when Qi tonification is needed without warming.Warm, powerful Qi tonic: choose it when strong restoration of Yuan Qi or Spleen-Lung Qi is needed.

Comparative answer key

CompareXi Yang ShenRen Shen
Medicinal and botanical namesPanacis quinquefolii Radix; Panax quinquefolius; American ginseng.Ginseng Radix; Panax ginseng; ginseng.
Primary categoryHerbs that Tonify the Qi; it also nourishes Yin.Herbs that Tonify the Qi.
Nature and tasteCool; bitter and sweet.Warm; bitter and sweet.
ChannelsHeart, Lung, Stomach and Kidney.Heart, Lung and Spleen.
Main emphasisBenefits Qi, nourishes Yin—especially Lung Yin—generates fluids and calms restlessness.Strongly tonifies Yuan Qi and Spleen-Lung Qi, generates fluids, supports the Heart and calms the Spirit.
Decisive exam clueQi-Yin deficiency, dryness, thirst or Heat signs when warm tonification is unsuitable.Marked Qi deficiency, weak Spleen-Lung function or a need for stronger Yuan-Qi restoration.
Formula clueQing Shu Yi Qi Tang.Common examples include Si Jun Zi Tang, Shen Ling Bai Zhu San, Bu Zhong Yi Qi Tang and Sheng Mai San.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersBoth can tonify Qi and generate fluids, so temperature and tonic emphasis decide the answer: Xi Yang Shen cools and nourishes Yin; Ren Shen warms and restores Qi more forcefully.
Memory cueXi Yang Shen: cool Qi + Yin. Ren Shen: warm, strong Yuan Qi.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), p. 712. Source Exam Key CMID 9104, questions 1–12.
