Herb comparison

Ju Hong vs. Chen Pi

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueJu HongChen Pi
Shared clueBoth citrus peels regulate Qi, dry Dampness and transform Phlegm.
Decisive clueRed outer peel; warmer, more dispersing and Lung-focused for Wind-Cold cough with copious sputum.Aged whole peel; more moderate and Middle-Jiao focused for distension, poor appetite, nausea and vomiting.

Comparative answer key

CompareJu HongChen Pi
Medicinal nameCitri Exocarpium Rubrum.Citri Reticulatae Pericarpium.
Primary regionLung.Spleen and Stomach, with a secondary Lung action.
Temperature and movementWarmer and more dispersing; expels Cold and dries Damp-Phlegm.Warm, aromatic and more moderate; regulates Middle-Jiao Qi and dries Dampness.
Board stemAcute Wind-Cold cough, abundant white sputum that is easy to expectorate.Epigastric fullness, poor appetite, nausea or vomiting with Damp-Phlegm.
Formula boundaryCo-chief of Er Chen Tang in the current 63-formula roster.Co-chief of Ju Pi Zhu Ru Tang in the current roster.
CautionDry cough, Yin deficiency or Phlegm-Heat.Yin/fluid deficiency, dry cough or Heat.

Board connection

MemoryJu Hong goes upstairs to dry the cold Lung; Chen Pi stays in the kitchen to move Middle Qi.
SourceYang, Chinese Herbal Medicines: Comparisons and Characteristics, 2nd ed., chapter 10, question 3; Bensky et al., Chinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, 3rd ed.
