Spleen Qi Deficiency vs. Spleen Not Controlling Blood

Recognize when weak Spleen Qi loses its ability to contain Blood in the vessels.

Fast differentiation clues

ClueSpleen Qi DeficiencySpleen Not Controlling Blood
SharedBoth show fatigue, poor appetite, pale tongue and a weak pulse.
Fast distinctionQi weakness without active leaking: loose stool, bloating and weak limbs.Containment failure: easy bruising, chronic uterine bleeding, blood in stool or other pale-deficiency bleeding.

Clinical table

CompareSpleen Qi DeficiencySpleen Not Controlling Blood
Added failureTransformation/transport.Qi cannot hold Blood inside vessels.
Blood qualityNo necessary bleeding.Usually chronic, pale or diffuse rather than hot-bright.
Formula associationSi Jun Zi Tang.Gui Pi Tang.

Exam and safety

Why it mattersBleeding plus Spleen-Qi deficiency signs—not bleeding alone—establishes failure to control Blood.
Memory cueWeak Bazo drops food; uncontained Bazo drops Blood.
SafetyHeavy bleeding, black stool, fainting, shortness of breath or pregnancy bleeding requires urgent care.
