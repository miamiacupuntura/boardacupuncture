Herb comparison

Lu Gen vs. Bai Mao Gen

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueLu GenBai Mao Gen
Shared clueBoth clear Heat, generate fluids and promote urination; their fresh roots may substitute for one another in the source comparison.
Fast distinctionClears Qi-level Heat with a Lung-Stomach and exterior-disseminating emphasis.Clears Blood-level Heat, stops bleeding and extends to the Heart and Bladder.

Comparative answer key

CompareLu GenBai Mao Gen
Medicinal namePhragmitis Rhizoma.Imperatae Rhizoma.
CategoryHerbs that Clear Heat and Drain Fire.Herbs that Stop Bleeding.
Heat levelExcels at clearing Qi-level Heat.Better at clearing Blood-level Heat and cooling the Blood.
Organ emphasisLungs and Stomach.Heart, Lungs, Stomach and Bladder.
Distinctive actionsCools the Lungs, drains the Stomach, guides out Phlegm-Heat and vents exterior pathogens.Stops bleeding and guides Heart Fire downward through the urine.
Decisive indicationsLung-Heat cough, externally contracted disorders or failure of measles to express.Nosebleed, coughing or spitting Blood, Blood in the urine, or Damp-Heat jaundice.
Fresh-root substitutionMay substitute for fresh Bai Mao Gen when that root is unavailable.The preferred fresh root in the source's substitution statement.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersThe herbs share three core actions and can substitute in fresh form, but Qi-level respiratory or exterior clues favor Lu Gen while bleeding and Blood-level Heat favor Bai Mao Gen.
Memory cueLu Gen clears Qi-level Lung-Stomach Heat; Bai Mao Gen cools Blood and stops bleeding.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), p. 107. Source Exam Key CMID 9002, questions 1–3.
