Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersThis is a nested formula family. Recognizing the architecture prevents memorizing four unrelated lists.
Memory cueFour Gentlemen make Qi; Four Substances make Blood; Eight Treasures unite them; Ten Complete adds warmth.
SafetyRich tonic formulas can worsen Food Stagnation, Dampness or acute excess conditions. Shi Quan Da Bu Tang contains Rou Gui and requires differentiation when Heat or pregnancy is present.
