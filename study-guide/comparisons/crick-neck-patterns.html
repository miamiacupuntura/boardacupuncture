Crick in the Neck Pattern Differentiation

Diagnostic orientation

Core ideaBoth branches restrict neck movement; exterior symptoms are the decisive difference.
Exam focusLocal palpable tightness versus systemic exterior signs

Comparative differentiation

Pattern or presentationDecisive cluesRepresentative formula/herbStrategy
Qi and Blood stagnationNeck stiffness/pain, restricted motion, tight muscles and sometimes palpable local knotsShu Jin Huo Xue TangMove Qi and Blood, soothe sinews and harmonize channels.
External Wind-ColdStiff painful neck plus aversion to Cold, slight fever, headache and floating-tight pulseGe Gen TangDispel Wind-Cold and relax the muscle layer.

Study rule

How to distinguishPurely local tight painful restriction suggests stagnation; chills, slight fever and headache add an exterior Wind-Cold pattern.
Memory cueLocal knot—stagnation; stiff neck + chills/headache—Wind-Cold.
SafetyNeck stiffness with high fever, severe headache, confusion, rash, neurologic deficit or recent major trauma is an emergency.
