Herb comparison

Lu Hui vs. Fan Xie Ye

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueLu HuiFan Xie Ye
Shared clueBoth are bitter, cold purgatives that drain Heat and relieve constipation caused by accumulated Heat drying the Intestines.
Fast distinctionCools the Liver and kills parasites.Mobilizes water for ascites; a small dose strengthens the Spleen and dissolves food stagnation.

Comparative answer key

CompareLu HuiFan Xie Ye
Medicinal nameAloe.Sennae Folium.
CategoryDownward-Draining Herbs · Purgatives.Downward-Draining Herbs · Purgatives.
Primary emphasisCools the Liver and kills parasites.Drains downward and mobilizes water.
Decisive indicationConstipation associated with Liver Heat or abdominal parasites.Ascites; in a small dose, Spleen weakness with food stagnation.
Shared precautionExcessive doses can cause painful, colicky diarrhea.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersBoth answer a Heat-constipation prompt, so the additional Liver-parasite or water-ascites clue determines the correct herb.
Memory cueLu Hui cools Liver and kills parasites; Fan Xie Ye drains water.
