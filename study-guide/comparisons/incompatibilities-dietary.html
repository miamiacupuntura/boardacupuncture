Nonspecific guidance shown

While taking herbsThe chart advises avoiding raw, cold, greasy or otherwise difficult-to-digest foods.
During feverIt advises avoiding greasy foods and oils.
Exam useIf a question explicitly cites the traditional dietary chart, match the listed medicinal and food. Do not silently generalize the rule to every patient.

Evidence and clinical boundary

These are historical dietetic teachings. They are not all supported as pharmacologic contraindications, allergies or toxic reactions. Current advice must consider the patient’s diagnosis, nutrition, medications, allergies and reliable interaction evidence.
