Exam focus and safety

CluesNausea or vomiting with Phlegm, a stuffy epigastrium and upward-rebellious Stomach Qi.
Signature formulaXiǎo Bàn Xià Tāng; also used within Xiǎo Chái Hú Tāng and Èr Chén Tāng.
TrapGinger moderates Bàn Xià but does not make raw Bàn Xià safe for dispensing.
PreparationUse prepared Bàn Xià only; ginger-processing also creates Jiāng Bàn Xià for vomiting-focused use.
SafetyClassical 18 Fǎn prohibits Bàn Xià with aconite. Persistent vomiting, blood, severe pain, confusion or dehydration requires urgent evaluation.
