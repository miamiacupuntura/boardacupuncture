Herb comparison

Mu Li vs. Lian Xu

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueMu LiLian Xu
Shared clueBoth have astringent or binding relevance, but their categories and additional actions differ.
Fast distinctionSalty, astringent and slightly cold shell that transforms hardened areas and resolves Heat.Astringent lotus stamen that stabilizes and binds Essence leakage.

Comparative answer key

CompareMu LiLian Xu
Medicinal nameOstreae Concha.Nelumbinis Stamen.
CategorySubstances that Anchor, Settle, and Calm the Spirit.Herbs that Stabilize and Bind.
Properties in the sourceSalty, astringent and slightly cold.Characterized here by its stabilizing astringency.
Distinctive actionTransforms hardened areas and resolves Heat.Stabilizes and binds watery semen and spermatorrhea.
Material clueOyster shell.Lotus stamen.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersThe original answer is phrased indirectly. Salty astringency, slight coldness, transformation of hardness and Heat resolution belong to Mu Li, not Lian Xu.
Memory cueMu Li softens hardness and clears Heat; Lian Xu binds Essence leakage.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), p. 882. Source Exam Key CMID 9010, question 3.
