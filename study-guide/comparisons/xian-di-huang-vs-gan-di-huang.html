Herb comparison

Fresh Rehmannia (xian di huang) vs. Dried Rehmannia (gan di huang / sheng di huang)

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueXian Di HuangGan Di Huang
Shared cluesBoth are forms of Sheng Di Huang that clear Heat, cool the Blood, enrich Yin and generate fluids.
Fast distinctionFresh, colder and more bitter; stronger for clearing Heat and cooling Blood.Dried, sweeter and less bitter; stronger for enriching Yin and nourishing Blood.

Comparative answer key

CompareXian Di HuangGan Di Huang
Medicinal nameRehmanniae Radix recens.Rehmanniae Radix.
FormFresh Rehmannia root.Dried Rehmannia root.
Common categoryHerbs that Clear Heat and Cool the Blood.
Flavor and temperatureBitterness predominates over sweetness; colder.Sweetness predominates over bitterness; less cold than the fresh form.
Primary emphasisClears Heat and cools the Blood more strongly.Enriches Yin and nourishes Blood more strongly.
Decisive clueMore intense Blood Heat requiring the fresh, colder form.Heat with greater Yin-fluid or Blood depletion requiring nourishment.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersBoth forms cool and nourish, but freshness strengthens the Heat-clearing direction while drying emphasizes sweetness and nourishment.
Memory cueXian is fresh and colder for Heat; Gan is dried and sweeter for nourishment.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), p. 122. Source Exam Key CMID 9060, question 1.
