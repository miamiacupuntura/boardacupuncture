HIV/AIDS Supportive Pattern Comparison

Diagnostic orientation

Core ideaThese are supportive historical TCM presentations, not HIV treatments. Effective ART, viral-load monitoring and infection prevention remain central.
Exam focusSupportive TCM differentiation alongside viral suppression and interaction monitoring

Study rule

How to distinguishDigestive fatigue is Spleen; cold/urination Yang; night Heat/dryness Yin; red inflammatory excess Toxic Heat; fixed-purple pain stasis; heaviness/greasy coat Phlegm.
Memory cueDigest—Spleen; cold—Yang; night Heat—Yin; red-toxic—Heat; fixed-purple—stasis; heavy-greasy—Phlegm.
SafetyNever replace or interrupt ART. Screen every herb for antiretroviral interactions and liver/kidney effects with the HIV clinician/pharmacist. Fever, dyspnea, neurologic symptoms or severe diarrhea in an immunocompromised patient is urgent.
