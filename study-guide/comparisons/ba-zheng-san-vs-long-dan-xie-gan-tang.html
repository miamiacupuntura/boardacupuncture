Formula comparison

Ba Zheng San vs. Long Dan Xie Gan Tang

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueBa Zheng SanLong Dan Xie Gan Tang
Shared clueBoth clear Damp-Heat from the lower burner, promote urination and may treat painful urination with dark, scanty urine.
Fast distinctionBladder Damp-Heat: severe urinary burning, urgency, frequency or stones dominate.Liver-Gallbladder Damp-Heat/Fire: rib-side pain, bitter taste, headache, red eyes or genital itching/discharge accompanies urinary signs.

Comparative answer key

CompareBa Zheng SanLong Dan Xie Gan Tang
Primary channel-organ focusBladder and urinary tract.Liver and Gallbladder, with downward drainage through the urine.
Decisive clueUrinary pain is the central presentation.Liver-Gallbladder signs occur above or in the genital region.
Therapeutic emphasisDrains Fire and strongly unblocks painful urinary dribbling.Drains Liver Fire and clears Damp-Heat from all three burners.
Exam shortcutHot urinary tract.Liver Fire/Damp-Heat draining downward.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersBoth can produce urinary burning, but Ba Zheng San is urinary-tract centered; Long Dan Xie Gan Tang is selected when the Liver-Gallbladder pattern explains the lower-burner symptoms.
Memory cueBa Zheng burns in the bladder; Long Dan starts in Liver-GB.
SafetyUse only verified Akebia Mu Tong or an appropriate modern substitute—never aristolochic-acid Guan Mu Tong. Fever with flank pain, inability to urinate or blood in urine requires urgent care.
SourceSource bank: FWB 5.2.1, Comparison Formulas to Expel Damp; corroborated with individual profiles and modern safety references.
