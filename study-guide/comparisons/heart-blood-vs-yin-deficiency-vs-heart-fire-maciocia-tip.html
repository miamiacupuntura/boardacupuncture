Heart Blood Deficiency vs. Heart Yin Deficiency vs. Heart Fire

Separate pale undernourishment, deficient cooling with empty Heat and a full-Heat Heart presentation.

Fast differentiation clues

ClueHeart Blood DeficiencyHeart Yin DeficiencyHeart Fire Blazing
SharedAll can disturb the Spirit and cause palpitations, insomnia, anxiety or poor concentration.
Fast distinctionPale and undernourished: poor memory, dizziness and a pale tongue.Dry empty Heat: night sweating, malar flush, five-center heat and a red peeled tongue.Full Heat: tongue or mouth ulcers, strong thirst, agitation, bitter taste and a forceful rapid pulse.

Clinical table

CompareHeart Blood DeficiencyHeart Yin DeficiencyHeart Fire Blazing
TemperatureNo important Heat.Empty Heat, mostly evening/night.Full Heat, often intense.
Tongue / pulsePale; thin or choppy.Red, peeled, possible central crack; thin-rapid.Red tip/body with yellow coat; rapid-forceful.
Formula associationGui Pi Tang when Heart-Spleen deficiency fits.Tian Wang Bu Xin Dan.Xie Xin Tang or Dao Chi San according to the presentation.

Exam and safety

Why it mattersColor, moisture, coating and Heat intensity separate the three even when insomnia and palpitations overlap.
Memory cueBlood is pale; Yin is red and peeled; Fire is red, coated and forceful.
SafetyNew severe palpitations, fainting, chest pain, confusion or sustained high fever requires urgent biomedical evaluation.
