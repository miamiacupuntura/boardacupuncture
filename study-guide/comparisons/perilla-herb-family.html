Perilla 紫苏 — Leaf, Stem, Seed

The leaf leaves, the stem steadies and the seed sinks.

One picture, one rule

RuleLeaf to surface; stem to chest/pregnancy; seed descends Lung Qi.

The part decides the job

MedicinalPart / identityJob
Zǐ Sū Yè 紫苏叶LeafReleases Wind-Cold, moves Middle Qi, calms fetus and resolves seafood toxicity.
Zǐ Sū Gěng 紫苏梗StemLoosens chest, moves Qi and calms fetus with stuffiness/morning sickness.
Zǐ Sū Zǐ 紫苏子SeedDescends Qi, stops cough/wheezing, transforms Phlegm and moistens Intestines.

Exam and safety

Exam keyExterior = leaf; chest with pregnancy nausea = stem; cough/wheeze = seed.
SafetyDo not boil the aromatic leaf long. The oily seed can worsen loose stools.
