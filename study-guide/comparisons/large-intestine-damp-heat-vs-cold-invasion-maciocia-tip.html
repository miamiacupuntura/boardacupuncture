Large Intestine Damp-Heat vs. Cold Invasion

Use stool quality, odor, temperature and pulse speed to separate two acute diarrheas.

Fast differentiation clues

ClueLarge Intestine Damp-HeatCold Invading Large Intestine
SharedBoth can begin acutely with abdominal pain and diarrhea.
Fast distinctionDamp-Heat: foul urgent stool with mucus/blood, anal burning, fever and yellow greasy coat.Cold: watery stool, sudden cramping, cold feeling, no thirst and white coat with deep-tight pulse.

Clinical table

CompareLarge Intestine Damp-HeatCold Invading Large Intestine
Temperature / odorHot, foul and burning.Cold, watery and less odorous.
Formula associationGe Gen Qin Lian Tang, Bai Tou Weng Tang or Shao Yao Tang.Lian Fu Wan plus Zheng Qi Tian Xiang San.

Exam and safety

Why it mattersBurning, odor and yellow stickiness indicate Damp-Heat; cold/watery signs indicate invasion.
Memory cueDamp-Heat burns and smells; Cold chills and pours.
SafetyBlood, high fever, severe pain, fainting or dehydration requires prompt medical care.

Review prompts
