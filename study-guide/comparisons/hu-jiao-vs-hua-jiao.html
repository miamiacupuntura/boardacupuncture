Herb-name comparison

Hu Jiao vs. Hua Jiao

Exam trap · One “a” changes the pepper

Hu JiaoBlack pepper · Piper nigrum · Piperis Fructus.
Hua JiaoSichuan pepper husk · Zanthoxylum schinifolium · Zanthoxyli Pericarpium.
Do not confuseHu is black pepper; Hua is Sichuan pepper. They are different plants and medicinal substances.

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueHu JiaoHua Jiao
Shared clueBoth warm the Interior and treat deficiency Cold of the Middle Burner.
Fast distinctionHotter but narrower: Stomach Cold, vomiting, diarrhea, food or Phlegm stagnation.Broader: dries Dampness, relieves pain and itching, and kills parasites.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersBoth are warming peppers for Middle-Burner Cold, but black pepper is hotter and Stomach-focused, while Sichuan pepper adds Dampness drying, antiparasitic and itching-relieving actions.
Memory cueHu = black pepper for Stomach Cold; Hua = Sichuan pepper for worms and itching.
SafetyThis page records the supplied Exam Key for study and is not a prescribing, dosing or poisoning-treatment guide.
