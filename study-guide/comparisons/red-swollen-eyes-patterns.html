Pathology or symptom comparison

Sore, Red and Swollen Eyes Comparison

Diagnostic orientation

Core ideaBoth presentations can produce redness, pain, photophobia and tearing; the accompanying systemic signs distinguish them.
Exam focusExterior respiratory signs versus bitter-irritable Liver-Fire signs

Comparative differentiation

Pattern or presentationDecisive cluesRepresentative formula/herbStrategy
External Wind-HeatRed painful swollen eyes with photophobia, tearing/itching or gummy discharge plus fever, headache, stuffy nose and thirstXie Fei YinDispel Wind and clear Heat.
Liver-Gallbladder FireSame eye inflammation with bitter dry mouth, irritability, tinnitus, headache, constipation, dark urine and wiry-rapid pulseLong Dan Xie Gan TangDrain Liver-GB Fire.

Study rule

How to distinguishFever, nasal symptoms and exterior onset indicate Wind-Heat; bitter mouth, anger, tinnitus and bowel/urine Heat indicate Liver-GB Fire.
Memory cueRed eye + cold signs—Wind-Heat; red eye + bitter/angry—Liver Fire.
SafetyEye pain with reduced vision, severe photophobia, contact-lens use, trauma/chemical exposure, fixed pupil, severe headache/nausea or corneal clouding requires urgent eye evaluation.
