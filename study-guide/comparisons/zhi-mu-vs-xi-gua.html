Herb comparison

Anemarrhenae Rhizoma (zhi mu) vs. Citrulli Fructus (xi gua)

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueZhi MuXi Gua
Shared cluesBoth clear Summerheat, relieve irritable thirst and facilitate urination when Heat has injured fluids.
Fast distinctionBitter, cold and moist; clears and moistens across the three burners while enriching Yin.Sweet, bland, juicy and cold; emphasizes Summerheat relief, thirst and Dampness drainage through urine.

Comparative answer key

CompareZhi MuXi Gua
Medicinal nameAnemarrhenae Rhizoma.Citrulli Fructus · watermelon fruit.
Shared categoryHerbs that Clear Heat and Drain Fire.
PropertiesBitter, cold and moist.Sweet, bland, juicy and cold.
Heat-clearing scopeClears Lung Heat in the upper burner, cools the Stomach in the middle burner and drains ministerial Fire in the lower burner.Clears Lung and Stomach Heat with a particular emphasis on intense Summerheat and thirst.
Fluid actionIts clearing and moistening quality enriches Lung and Stomach Yin.Its juicy nature alleviates thirst, while its blandness leaches Dampness through urination.
Urination mechanismFacilitates urination as part of its shared Summerheat action.Guides interior Heat downward through urine and reduces Dampness that obstructs normal venting of Heat.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersBoth address Summerheat thirst. A three-burner pattern, ministerial Fire or Yin enrichment points to Zhi Mu; a juicy thirst-relieving and Dampness-leaching clue points to Xi Gua.
Memory cueZhi Mu clears and moistens all three burners; Xi Gua cools Summerheat and drains through urine.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), p. 101. Source Exam Key CMID 9123, question 3.

Possible exam questions · Study prompts

  • Which Summerheat actions do both herbs share?
  • Which herb clears Heat across all three burners?
  • Which herb drains ministerial Fire and enriches Yin?
  • Which herb is sweet, bland and juicy?
  • Which herb more directly leaches Dampness through urination?
