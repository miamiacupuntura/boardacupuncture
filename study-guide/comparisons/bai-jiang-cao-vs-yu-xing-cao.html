Herb comparison

Patriniae Herba (bai jiang cao) vs. Houttuyniae Herba (yu xing cao)

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueBai Jiang CaoYu Xing Cao
Shared cluesBoth clear Heat, resolve toxicity, reduce sores and expel pus.
Fast distinctionEnters both Qi and Blood levels; treats Stomach-Intestine Heat, broader abscesses and painful Blood stasis.Primarily enters the Lung; excels at Lung Heat and Lung abscess and also promotes urination.

Comparative answer key

CompareBai Jiang CaoYu Xing Cao
Medicinal namePatriniae Herba.Houttuyniae Herba.
Shared categoryHerbs that Clear Heat and Resolve Toxicity.
Properties and levelBitter, acrid and slightly cold; enters both Qi and Blood levels.Acrid and cold; primarily enters the Lung channel.
Primary emphasisClears Heat from the Stomach and Intestines, expels pus and dispels Blood stasis to relieve pain.Cools Lung Heat, resolves toxicity expressed as Lung abscess and expels pus.
Abscess clueLung, Liver or intestinal abscess.Especially Lung abscess.
Distinctive indicationPelvic Blood stasis with toxic accumulation, including postpartum lochia patterns.Promotes urination and unblocks painful urinary dribbling.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersBoth clear toxicity and expel pus. Intestinal or hepatic abscess, Blood stasis and postpartum pelvic signs favor Bai Jiang Cao; a Lung-centered abscess or urinary-dribbling clue favors Yu Xing Cao.
Memory cueBai Jiang Cao reaches Blood and Intestines; Yu Xing Cao reaches Lung and urine.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), p. 175. Source Exam Key CMID 9115, questions 1–2.
