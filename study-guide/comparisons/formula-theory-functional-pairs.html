Theory 3 of 12

Balanced Functional Pairs

Use opposing temperature, direction and movement as one controlled therapeutic unit.

Core rule

RuleOpposite properties can cooperate when both resolve different parts of one pathomechanism.

Classic balanced units

ConceptWhat it meansApplication
Ban Xia + Huang QinWarm/acrid dispersion and bitter/cold clearing address mixed Cold and Heat in the middle.Do not choose only from the dominant temperature word.
Ma Huang + Shi GaoMa Huang diffuses Lung Qi while Shi Gao clears constrained Lung Heat and controls excessive warming.The ratio and surrounding formula determine the final thermal emphasis.
Huang Qi + Fang FengHuang Qi secures Defensive Qi; a smaller dispersing element helps prevent trapping an exterior pathogen.Containment and release are coordinated, not contradictory.
Ren Shen + Wu Wei ZiQi generation is paired with sour containment to sustain and stabilize the effect.Movement plus restraint can protect a deficient patient.
Huang Lian + Sheng MaDescending bitter Cold is balanced by a small ascending/dispersing action.A strategic assistant may oppose the chief direction while improving the result.

How to read the pair

ConceptWhat it meansApplication
Primary jobIdentify which herb or action directly treats the dominant mechanism.The primary job usually controls the ratio.
Corrective jobName the excess, side effect or blocked direction being corrected.The corrective herb is not automatically less important.
Net directionRead the entire formula, dose relationship and pattern.An isolated hot/cold label cannot predict the finished prescription.

Responsible use

Educational summary for formula analysis and exam preparation. Doses, toxic preparations, interactions and clinical decisions require current references and professional supervision.
