Family 13 of 20

Fritillaria 贝母 — The Bèi Mǔ Twins

Chuān is cherished for chronic dry cough; Zhè zaps acute hot Phlegm and lumps.

One picture, one rule

RuleChuān = small, sweet, moistening; Zhè = big, bitter, breaking.

The part decides the job

MedicinalPart / identityJob
Chuān Bèi Mǔ 川贝母Fritillaria cirrhosa bulbClears Heat, transforms Phlegm, moistens Lung and treats chronic Yin-deficiency cough.
Zhè Bèi Mǔ 浙贝母Fritillaria thunbergii bulbMore strongly clears hot Phlegm and dissipates scrofula, goiter or abscess nodules.

Exam and safety

Exam keyChronic dry/scant sputum = Chuān; acute thick yellow sputum or lumps = Zhè.
SafetyBoth are traditionally incompatible with aconite. Chuān Bèi is frequently adulterated; require authenticated sourcing.
