Herb comparison

Pinelliae Rhizoma preparatum (zhi ban xia) vs. Zingiberis Rhizoma recens (sheng jiang)

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueZhi Ban XiaSheng Jiang
Shared cluesBoth harmonize the Stomach, settle nausea and relieve Cold-Phlegm cough with thin, profuse mucus.
Fast distinctionDries Dampness and disperses Phlegm and thin mucus.Warms the Stomach, awakens the Spleen and warms the Lungs to transform thin mucus.

Comparative answer key

CompareZhi Ban XiaSheng Jiang
Medicinal namePinelliae Rhizoma preparatum.Zingiberis Rhizoma recens.
CategoryWarm Herbs that Transform Cold-Phlegm.Warm, Acrid Herbs that Release the Exterior.
Nausea actionDries Dampness and disperses Phlegm and thin mucus to stop nausea.Warms the Stomach and awakens the Spleen to harmonize the Stomach and settle nausea.
Cough actionTransforms Cold-Phlegm by drying Dampness.Warms the Lungs and transforms thin mucus to stop cough.
Shared clinical settingCold-Dampness injuring the Stomach with nausea; Cold-Phlegm cough with thin, profuse mucus.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersBoth settle nausea and cough, but direct Phlegm-Damp drying identifies Zhi Ban Xia; Stomach-Spleen and Lung warming identifies Sheng Jiang.
Memory cueZhi Ban Xia dries and disperses; Sheng Jiang warms and harmonizes.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), p. 416. Source Exam Key CMID 9061, questions 2–4.
