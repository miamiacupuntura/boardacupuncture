Supplemental family 3 of 6

Arborvitae 柏 — Leaf Stops Blood, Seed Calms Spirit

One tree gives a cooling hemostatic leaf and a nourishing Spirit-calming seed.

Memory rule

AnchorCè Bǎi Yè seals bleeding; Bǎi Zǐ Rén settles sleep.

Identity decides the job

MedicinalIdentityJob
Cè Bǎi Yè 侧柏叶Platycladus leaf/twig tipCools Blood, stops bleeding, transforms Phlegm and supports hair.
Bǎi Zǐ Rén 柏子仁Platycladus seedNourishes Heart Blood/Yin, calms Spirit and moistens Intestines.
Huáng Bǎi 黄柏Phellodendron bark—not arborvitaeClears Lower-Jiao Damp-Heat and deficiency Fire.

Exam and safety

Exam keyLeaf = bleeding; seed = insomnia/dry constipation; yellow bark = different tree.
SafetyThe shared 柏 character does not establish identical species or action. Oily Bǎi Zǐ Rén may aggravate loose stools.
