Herb comparison

Inulae Flos (xuan fu hua) vs. Haematitum (dai zhe shi)

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueXuan Fu HuaDai Zhe Shi
Shared cluesBoth direct rebellious Lung and Stomach Qi downward to stop nausea, vomiting and wheezing.
Fast distinctionWarm, acrid Phlegm-transformer for clumped turbid Phlegm obstructing the Qi dynamic.Heavy mineral that sinks rebellious Qi, cools Liver Fire, sedates ascending Liver Yang and stops bleeding.

Comparative answer key

CompareXuan Fu Hua (Inulae Flos)Dai Zhe Shi (Haematitum)
Category in sourceHerbs that Transform Phlegm and Stop Coughing · Warm Herbs that Transform Cold-Phlegm.Substances that Extinguish Wind and Stop Tremors.
Properties emphasizedBitter, salty, warm and acrid.Massively heavy, bitter and cold in this comparison.
MechanismSaltiness softens clumped Phlegm, warm acridity disperses it and bitterness directs it downward.Massive weight sinks rebellious Qi and bitter coldness cools Liver Fire.
Best clueClumped turbid Phlegm blocks the ascent and descent of Qi, producing nausea, vomiting or wheezing.Liver Yang ascendancy or Blood Heat accompanies rebellious Qi.
Additional actionsTransforms and disperses Cold-Phlegm.Sedates Liver Yang, cools Blood and stops bleeding.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersBoth descend Lung and Stomach Qi and can address the same symptoms, but only Xuan Fu Hua centers on clumped Phlegm and only Dai Zhe Shi adds Liver- and Blood-level actions.
Common confusionTurbid Phlegm obstructing Qi favors Xuan Fu Hua; Liver Yang, Liver Fire or bleeding favors Dai Zhe Shi.
Memory cueXuan Fu Hua transforms and descends turbid Phlegm; Dai Zhe Shi uses mineral weight to sink Qi, Yang and Fire.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), p. 990. Source Exam Keys CMID 8942 and CMID 9105, questions 3–6.

Possible exam questions · Study prompts

  • Which descending actions do the two substances share?
  • Which herb softens and disperses clumped turbid Phlegm?
  • Which substance relies on massive weight to sink rebellious Qi?
  • Which one also cools Blood and stops bleeding?
