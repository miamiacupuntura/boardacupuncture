Kun Bu vs. Hai Zao

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueKun BuHai Zao
Shared clueBoth cool and transform Phlegm-Heat and treat Phlegm-Fire nodules such as goiter, neck swelling and testicular swelling.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersThe herbs overlap strongly, so choose Kun Bu when hardness and congealed Blood dominate, and Hai Zao when Phlegm nodules such as goiter or scrofula dominate.
Memory cueKun Bu breaks hardness; Hai Zao dissolves Phlegm nodules.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), p. 403. Source Exam Key CMID 8968, question 1.
