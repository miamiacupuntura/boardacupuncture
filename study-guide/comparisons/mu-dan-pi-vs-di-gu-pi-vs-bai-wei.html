Herb comparison

Moutan Cortex (mu dan pi) vs. Lycii Cortex (di gu pi) vs. Cynanchi atrati Radix (bai wei)

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueMu Dan PiDi Gu PiBai Wei
Shared cluesAll cool Blood and reduce deficiency Heat, including Yin-deficiency consumptive disorders with low-grade fever.
Fast distinctionDispels Blood stasis; steaming bones without sweating.Steaming bones with sweating.Vents residual pathogenic Heat late in a febrile disorder.

CompareMu Dan PiDi Gu PiBai Wei

Lung clueNo Lung-specific comparison is given.Di Gu Pi and Bai Wei have similar ability to cool the Lungs.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersAll three treat Yin-deficiency Heat, so stasis, sweating and residual febrile Heat provide the differentiation.
Common confusionStasis or no sweating favors Mu Dan Pi; sweating favors Di Gu Pi; late-stage residual Heat favors Bai Wei.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), p. 225. Source Exam Key CMID 8931.
