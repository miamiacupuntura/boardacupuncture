Substance comparison

Gypsum fibrosum (shi gao) vs. Anemarrhenae Rhizoma (zhi mu)

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueShi GaoZhi Mu
Shared cluesBoth clear Yang-Brightness Qi-level Heat and cool Lung and Stomach Heat, especially with fever, irritability, thirst and a large, forceful pulse.
Fast distinctionClears and disperses blazing Heat; protects fluids by draining Fire.Clears Heat while moistening; directly nourishes Yin and generates fluids.

Comparative answer key

CompareShi GaoZhi Mu
Medicinal nameGypsum fibrosum.Anemarrhenae Rhizoma.
CategoryHerbs that Clear Heat and Drain Fire.
Main actionIts acrid quality clears and disperses Heat toward the Exterior, while its heaviness reaches the Interior.Clears Heat and moistens Dryness.
Effect on fluidsProtects fluids indirectly by eliminating Fire.Benefits Yin by directly generating fluids.
Organ emphasisPrimarily clears intense Heat from the Lung and Stomach.Cools Lung and Stomach Heat and also cools the Kidneys and nourishes Yin.
Shared presentationYang-Brightness Qi-level Heat or Lung/Stomach Heat with high fever, irritability, strong thirst or cough.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersBoth treat intense Heat and thirst, but Shi Gao removes blazing Fire so fluids can recover, whereas Zhi Mu directly moistens and nourishes Yin.
Memory cueShi Gao drains the Fire; Zhi Mu moistens and nourishes.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), p. 94. Source Exam Keys CMID 9074 and CMID 9123, questions 1–2.
