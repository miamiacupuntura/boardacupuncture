Pattern comparison

Cold in the Liver Channel vs. Liver Qi Stagnation

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueCold in the Liver ChannelLiver Qi Stagnation
Shared clueBoth may cause lower-abdominal, genital or rib-side pain and a wiry pulse because the Liver channel is involved.
Fast distinctionCold contraction: severe fixed lower-abdominal, groin or scrotal pain, worse with cold and improved by warmth; pale tongue and deep-wiry-tight pulse.Qi constraint: distending variable pain, sighing, mood changes and menstrual symptoms that worsen with stress; tongue may be normal.

Comparative answer key

CompareCold in the Liver ChannelLiver Qi Stagnation
Pathogen/mechanismCold contracts the Liver channel and blocks Qi/Blood.Liver Qi fails to course freely.
Pain qualityContracting, severe and cold-sensitive.Distending, mobile and emotion-sensitive.
Tongue / pulsePale with white moist coat; deep-wiry-tight.Normal/dusky; wiry.
Formula examplesTian Tai Wu Yao San or Nuan Gan Jian.Chai Hu Shu Gan San or Xiao Yao San when deficiency coexists.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersChannel location overlaps. Cold response and contraction identify Cold; emotional variability and distention identify Qi stagnation.
Memory cueCold contracts the groin; Qi stagnation distends with stress.
SafetySudden severe testicular, pelvic or abdominal pain, swelling, fever, vomiting or pregnancy-related pain requires urgent evaluation.

Possible exam questions · Study prompts

  • Which improves with warmth?
  • Which fluctuates with emotion?
  • How do tongue and pulse distinguish them?
