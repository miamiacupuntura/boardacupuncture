Kidney Yang Deficiency vs. Yang Deficiency with Water Overflow

Recognize when deficient Kidney warming progresses to clinically significant fluid retention.

Fast differentiation clues

ClueKidney Yang DeficiencyYang Deficiency with Water Overflow
SharedBoth cause cold back/legs, fatigue, weak low back, pale wet tongue and a deep weak pulse.
Fast distinctionCold deficiency without major retention: abundant clear urine and general Yang weakness.Water overflows: edema—especially legs/ankles—abdominal fullness and scant clear urination.

Clinical table

CompareKidney Yang DeficiencyYang Deficiency with Water Overflow
Water metabolismWeak but not necessarily visibly retained.Failure produces obvious edema and fluid accumulation.
Tongue / pulsePale, wet; deep-weak.Pale, swollen and wet; deep-slow-weak or submerged.
Formula associationYou Gui Wan or Jin Gui Shen Qi Wan.Jin Gui Shen Qi Wan plus Wu Ling San in the supplied table.

Exam and safety

Why it mattersEdema plus scant urination marks the progression from Yang weakness to Water overflow.
Memory cueYang deficiency feels cold; Water overflow feels cold, full and swollen.
SafetySudden edema, rapid weight gain, reduced urine, breathlessness or chest symptoms needs urgent heart/kidney evaluation.
