Two Western “Cassia” Names

“Cassia bark” and “cassia seed” are unrelated medicinals hidden by an English common name.

Memory rule

AnchorRòu Guì is cinnamon heat; Jué Míng Zǐ is a cooling Senna seed.

Identities

NameIdentityJob
Ròu Guì 肉桂Cinnamomum cassia barkWarms Kidney Yang/Mìngmén and channels.
Jué Míng Zǐ 决明子Senna obtusifolia/tora seed; “cassia seed”Clears Liver, brightens eyes and moistens bowels.

Exam and safety

Exam keyBark warms and enters Kidney; seed cools Liver and bowel.
SafetyCommon English “cassia” is unsafe shorthand. Ròu Guì and Jué Míng Zǐ have opposite thermal directions.
