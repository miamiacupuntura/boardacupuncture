Herb comparison

Artemisiae annuae Herba (qing hao) vs. Lycii Cortex (di gu pi)

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueQing HaoDi Gu Pi
Shared cluesBoth clear Heat, cool Blood and treat steaming-bone disorder from deficiency Heat.
Fast distinctionVents latent Heat, resolves Summerheat and malarial-like disorders; steaming bones without sweating.Sweet, moist Lung-Liver-Kidney cooling; steaming bones with sweating and wasting-thirsting disorder.

Comparative answer key

CompareQing Hao (Artemisiae annuae Herba)Di Gu Pi (Lycii Cortex)
Category in sourceHerbs that Clear Heat from Deficiency; also Herbs that Clear Summerheat.Herbs that Clear Heat and Cool Blood.
Shared actionsClear Heat, cool Blood and treat steaming-bone disorder.
PropertiesBitter and cold.Sweet, bland and slightly cold; also moistening.
Primary emphasisClears Liver and Gallbladder Heat and vents lurking latent Heat from deep within Yin.Acts primarily on the Lungs, Liver and Kidneys to treat Yin-deficiency Heat.
Sweating clueSteaming-bone disorder without sweating.Steaming-bone disorder with sweating.
Distinctive indicationSummerheat and malarial-like disorders.Wasting and thirsting disorder.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersBoth treat deficiency Heat and steaming bones, so sweating and the secondary disease pattern are the decisive clues.
Memory cueQing Hao: no sweat, Summerheat and malaria; Di Gu Pi: sweat and wasting-thirsting.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), p. 223. Source Exam Keys CMID 8931 and CMID 9035, questions 6–9.
