Formula 58 of 63

Er Chen Tang

Safety reviewed

Damp-Phlegm from Spleen dysfunction affecting the Middle Burner or Lung.

Diagnostic navigation

CategoryTransform Phlegm
Clinical routeCough & breathing · Digestion & abdomen
PatternDamp-Phlegm from Spleen dysfunction affecting the Middle Burner or Lung.
Decisive clueCopious white sputum, chest or epigastric fullness, nausea and a greasy white tongue coat.

Essential architecture

King herb(s)Ban Xia, Ju Hong
ActionDries Dampness, transforms Phlegm, regulates Qi and harmonizes the Middle Burner.
IngredientsBan Xia, Ju Hong (or Chen Pi), Fu Ling and Zhi Gan Cao; Sheng Jiang and Wu Mei are used in the traditional preparation.

Safety and provenance

This drying formula is inappropriate for unconfirmed dry cough or Yin deficiency. Persistent cough or breathing difficulty requires evaluation.
