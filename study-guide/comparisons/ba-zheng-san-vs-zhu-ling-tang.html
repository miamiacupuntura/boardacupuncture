Formula comparison · FWB Expel Damp

Ba Zheng San vs. Zhu Ling Tang

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueBa Zheng SanZhu Ling Tang
Shared clueBoth clear lower-burner Damp-Heat, promote urination and may treat painful, scanty or bloody urination.
Fast distinctionAcute excess Heat Lin: severe burning, urgency, frequency and dark scanty urine.More chronic water-Heat with Yin injury: urinary difficulty plus thirst, irritability or insomnia and a thin-rapid pulse.

Comparative answer key

CompareBa Zheng SanZhu Ling Tang
Pattern characterAcute, hot and excessive Bladder Damp-Heat.Water and Heat accumulate after fluids/Yin have been injured.
Treatment directionStrongly clear Heat, drain Fire and unblock painful urination.Promote urination, clear Heat and protect/nourish Yin.
Decisive clueUrinary burning and urgency are intense and central.Thirst and Yin-fluid injury accompany the urinary signs.
Key ingredientsMu Tong, Qu Mai, Bian Xu, Che Qian Zi, Hua Shi, Zhi Zi and Da Huang.Zhu Ling, Fu Ling, Ze Xie, Hua Shi and E Jiao.
Tongue and pulseRed with greasy yellow coat; slippery and rapid, often forceful.Red with reduced coat; thin and rapid.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersBoth can look like Heat Lin. Grade acuity and excess, then ask whether the Heat has already damaged Yin and fluids.
Memory cueBa Zheng clears the acute burn; Zhu Ling drains while preserving Yin.
SafetyUse only verified Akebia Mu Tong or an appropriate modern substitute—never aristolochic-acid Guan Mu Tong. Fever with flank pain, inability to urinate or visible blood requires urgent evaluation.
