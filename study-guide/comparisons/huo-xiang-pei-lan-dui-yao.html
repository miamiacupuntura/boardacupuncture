Appendix B pair · 47 of 51

Huò Xiāng + Pèi Lán

藿香 + 佩兰

One picture, one job

PictureTwo fragrant leaves air out a damp, musty Middle.
Combined jobAromatically transform Dampness, awaken the Spleen and relieve Summerheat-Dampness.
CategoryAromatically Transform Dampness

How the pair works

HerbRole within the pair
Huò XiāngWarmer; better for vomiting and an accompanying Exterior disorder.
Pèi LánMore neutral; better for a sweet, greasy or foul sensation in the mouth.

Exam focus and safety

CluesNausea, stuffy chest, poor appetite and a sweet-sticky mouth in Summerheat or Dampness.
Signature formulaHuò Xiāng Zhèng Qì Sǎn modified with Pèi Lán.
TrapHuò Xiāng emphasizes vomiting/Exterior; Pèi Lán emphasizes oral foulness and mild Damp-Heat.
PreparationAdd both aromatic medicinals late and use a short boil to preserve volatile oils.
SafetyAromatics can consume Qi and Yin with excessive use. Persistent vomiting, high fever or dehydration requires evaluation.
