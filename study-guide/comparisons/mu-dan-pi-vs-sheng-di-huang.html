Herb comparison

Moutan Cortex (mu dan pi) vs. Rehmanniae Radix (sheng di huang)

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueMu Dan PiSheng Di Huang
Shared cluesBoth cool the Blood, treat Heat from Yin deficiency and are often combined.
Fast distinctionCool, aromatic and piercing; disperses Heat so Yin can recover.Sweet and cold; directly nourishes Yin so Heat subsides as Yin is restored.

Comparative answer key

CompareMu Dan Pi (Moutan Cortex)Sheng Di Huang (Rehmanniae Radix)
Shared categoryHerbs that Clear Heat and Cool the Blood.
Shared useHeat from Yin deficiency.
Nature and characterCool, aromatic and piercing.Sweet and cold.
Primary methodDisperses Heat, thereby assisting Yin to recover.Nourishes Yin, thereby alleviating Heat as Yin returns to strength.
Decisive clueDisperse deficiency Heat.Directly nourish Yin.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersBoth address Yin-deficiency Heat, but Mu Dan Pi does so by dispersing Heat while Sheng Di Huang does so by rebuilding Yin.
Memory cueMu Dan Pi disperses; Sheng Di Huang nourishes.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), p. 127. Source Exam Key CMID 9022, question 1.
