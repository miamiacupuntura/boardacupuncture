Ma Zi Ren Wan

Intestinal Dryness and Heat with Spleen Bind constipation.

High-yield summary

CategoryMoisten the Intestines · Drain Heat
PatternIntestinal Dryness and Heat with Spleen Bind constipation.
ActionMoistens the intestines, drains Heat, moves Qi and unblocks the bowels.
Decisive clueHard dry stool with frequent urination, thirst and abdominal fullness in an otherwise relatively mild excess pattern.

Ingredients

Huo Ma Ren, Xing Ren, Bai Shao, Zhi Shi, Hou Po and Da Huang, traditionally made into pills with honey.

Commercial preparations and lineages may vary; always verify the source being used.

Differentiation and memory

Do not confuseJi Chuan Jian treats constipation from Kidney Yang deficiency and Cold; Ma Zi Ren Wan treats intestinal Dryness with Heat.
Memory cueHemp seed and apricot seed moisten; the Xiao Cheng Qi trio moves the dry blockage.

Safety

Severe pain, vomiting, marked distention, blood in stool or inability to pass gas requires urgent evaluation for obstruction.
