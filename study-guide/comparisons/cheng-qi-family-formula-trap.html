Formula trap 4 of 22

The Three Chéng Qì Formulas

Grade Yangming bowel excess by fullness, distention, dryness and hardness.

Memory rule

AnchorDà has all four; Xiǎo lacks salt; Tiáo Wèi lacks Qi movers.

Decisive separation

FormulaArchitectureDecisive presentation
Dà Chéng Qì Tāng 大承气汤Dà Huáng + Máng Xiāo + Zhǐ Shí + Hòu PòSevere fullness, distention, dryness and hardness.
Xiǎo Chéng Qì Tāng 小承气汤Dà Huáng + Zhǐ Shí + Hòu Pò; no Máng XiāoDistention/fullness without the same dry-hard stool emphasis.
Tiáo Wèi Chéng Qì Tāng 调胃承气汤Dà Huáng + Máng Xiāo + Zhì Gān Cǎo; no Qi moversDry hard stool with less focal distention.

Exam and safety

Exam keyIdentify what is missing: softening salt or forceful Qi-moving pair.
SafetyThese are strong purgative strategies, not routine constipation remedies; rule out obstruction, pregnancy, frailty and fluid depletion.
