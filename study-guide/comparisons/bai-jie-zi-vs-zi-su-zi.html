Herb comparison

Sinapis Semen (bai jie zi) vs. Perillae Fructus (zi su zi)

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueBai Jie ZiZi Su Zi
Shared clueBoth treat cough and dyspnea due to Cold-Phlegm.
Fast distinctionIntensely warm and drying; promotes Qi movement, dries Phlegm and mobilizes the channels and collaterals.Directs rebellious Lung Qi downward, settles acute wheezing and disperses Phlegm.

Comparative answer key

CompareBai Jie ZiZi Su Zi
Medicinal nameSinapis Semen.Perillae Fructus.
CategoryWarm Herbs that Transform Cold-Phlegm.Herbs that Relieve Coughing and Wheezing.
Nature and emphasisIntensely warm and drying; emphasizes drying Phlegm and promoting the flow of Qi.Emphasizes redirecting rebellious Lung Qi downward, settling wheezing and dispersing Phlegm.
LocationMobilizes the channels and collaterals.Acts primarily on rebellious Lung Qi.
Best cough clueCold-Phlegm that requires strong warming, drying and Qi movement.Cough with acute wheezing from Cold-Phlegm.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersBoth address Cold-Phlegm cough and dyspnea. Strong drying with channel-collateral involvement favors Bai Jie Zi; rebellious Lung Qi with acute wheezing favors Zi Su Zi.
Memory cueBai Jie Zi dries and mobilizes; Zi Su Zi descends and settles.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), p. 424. Source Exam Key CMID 9131, questions 1–3.
