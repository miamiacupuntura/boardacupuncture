Herb comparison

Qiang Huo vs. Gao Ben

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueQiang HuoGao Ben
Shared clueBoth are warm, acrid Exterior-releasing herbs that enter the Governing Vessel.
Fast distinctionRises more strongly for neck, shoulder and joint Wind pain.Reaches the vertex but is better for lower-body disorders.

Comparative answer key

CompareQiang HuoGao Ben
Botanical nameNotopterygii Rhizoma seu Radix.Ligustici Rhizoma.
CategoryWarm, Acrid Herbs that Release the Exterior.Warm, Acrid Herbs that Release the Exterior.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersBoth travel through the Governing Vessel, but Qiang Huo has the stronger upward, joint-releasing action while Gao Ben combines vertex reach with lower-body use.
Memory cueQiang Huo climbs and clears joints; Gao Ben reaches vertex and below.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), p. 21. Source Exam Keys CMID 8966, question 1, and CMID 9032, question 3.
