Herb comparison

Rhei Radix et Rhizoma (da huang) vs. Crotonis Fructus (ba dou)

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueDa HuangBa Dou
Shared cluesBoth are purgatives. When prepared and used in small doses, both can “drive out the old to bring in the new,” reduce accumulation and harmonize the Middle.
Fast distinctionCold purgative for Heat accumulation in the Yang organs.Hot, harsh cathartic for Cold accumulation in the Yin organs.

Comparative answer key

CompareDa Huang (Rhei Radix et Rhizoma)Ba Dou (Crotonis Fructus)
Shared actionPurge downward and reduce accumulation.
NatureCold.Hot.
PatternSurplus Heat buildup in the Yang organs.Surplus Cold accumulated internally in the Yin organs.
Source classificationDownward-Draining Herb · Purgative.Downward-Draining Herb · Harsh Expellant (Cathartic).

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersBoth purge and reduce accumulation, but their opposite thermal natures determine the appropriate pattern.
Common confusionHeat in the Yang organs points to Da Huang; Cold in the Yin organs points to Ba Dou.
Memory cueDa Huang: cold for Heat. Ba Dou: hot for Cold.
Source question formatsQuestions 1–4 are Matching and repeat the same comparison with small variations.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), p. 259. Source Exam Key CMID 8939.
