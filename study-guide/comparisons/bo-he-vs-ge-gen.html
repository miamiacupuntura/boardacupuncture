Herb comparison

Menthae haplocalycis Herba (bo he) vs. Puerariae Radix (ge gen)

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueBo HeGe Gen
Shared cluesBoth are cool, acrid herbs that can release the exterior.
Decisive clueSore throat, head and eyes, rash or Liver Qi constraint.Stiff neck, muscle-level heat, Yang Ming heat, fluid damage or diarrhea.

Comparative answer key

CompareBo HeGe Gen
Properties/channelsCool, acrid and aromatic; Lung and Liver.Cool, sweet and acrid; Spleen and Stomach.
Exterior emphasisDisperses Wind-Heat and clears the head, eyes and throat.Releases the exterior through the muscles, raises Yang and relieves muscle heat.
Distinctive actionsVents rashes and smooths Liver Qi.Relieves stiff neck, generates fluids and stops diarrhea.
Negative clueDoes not characteristically generate fluids or treat neck pain.Not the benchmark for rash venting plus Liver Qi stagnation.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersThe exterior-releasing overlap can hide two very different secondary profiles.
Common confusionBo He centers on Wind-Heat of the head and throat; Ge Gen centers on the muscles, fluids and diarrhea.
How it may appearStiff neck, thirst or diarrhea favors Ge Gen. Rash with sore throat and Liver Qi stagnation favors Bo He.
Source question formatsQuestion 3 is Matching. Question 12 is Multiple Choice: the herb that releases through the muscles, relieves stiff neck, generates fluids and treats diarrhea is Ge Gen. Questions 13–16 reinforce this in True/False review blocks.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), p. 51. Source Exam Key CMID 9277.
