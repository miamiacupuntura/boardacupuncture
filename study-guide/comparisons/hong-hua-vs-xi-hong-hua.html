Herb and name comparison

Hong Hua vs. Xi Hong Hua (Fan Hong Hua)

Exam trap · Safflower is not saffron

Hong HuaCarthamus tinctorius · safflower · dried flower.
Xi/Fan Hong HuaCrocus sativus · saffron · dried stigma.
Key warningSimilar English and pinyin names do not indicate the same plant or plant part.

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueHong HuaXi/Fan Hong Hua
Shared clueBoth invigorate Blood, dispel Blood stasis and enter Heart and Liver.
Fast distinctionWarm, acrid safflower; more gynecological and rated higher for expelling stagnation in the source scale.Neutral, sweet saffron; more Blood-nourishing, cools Blood, resolves toxicity and is reserved for severe cases.

Comparative answer key

CompareHong HuaXi Hong Hua / Fan Hong Hua
Medicinal and scientific nameCarthami Flos · Carthamus tinctorius.Croci Stigma · Crocus sativus.
Plant partDried safflower flower.Dried red stigma of the saffron crocus.
Botanical family and appearanceAsteraceae; thistle-like plant with yellow or red flowers.Iridaceae; purple flower with three bright red stigmas.
Properties and channelsWarm and pungent/acrid; Heart and Liver.Neutral and sweet; Heart and Liver.
Stagnation versus nourishmentSource scale: expels stagnation +++++++; nourishes Blood +++.Source scale: expels stagnation +++; nourishes Blood ++++++++++.
Distinctive actionsLarge doses dispel Blood stasis; small doses harmonize Blood; often used for gynecological problems.Cools Blood and resolves toxicity; used for severe Blood stasis with chest pain and for menorrhagia, amenorrhea, dystocia, retained lochia or traumatic pain.
Cost and identificationRelatively affordable and widely available.Much more expensive because each flower yields only a few hand-harvested stigmas; therefore vulnerable to adulteration.
How to read “stronger” in this sourceThe source specifically rates Hong Hua higher for expelling stagnation.Other source questions call Croci Stigma “stronger” and reserve it for severe cases; it is also far stronger for Blood nourishment in the supplied scale.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersThe English names safflower and saffron are easily confused. Identify plant and plant part first, then distinguish warm stagnation-expelling Hong Hua from costly, neutral, nourishing and Blood-cooling Croci Stigma.
Memory cueHong Hua = whole safflower; Xi/Fan Hong Hua = three saffron stigmas.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), pp. 630 and 651. Source Exam Key CMID 8985, questions 9–19.
