Trap 4 of 22

Hàn Fáng Jǐ 汉防己 vs. Guǎng Fáng Jǐ 广防己

Regional prefixes separate Stephania from a prohibited Aristolochia source.

Memory rule

AnchorHàn = Stephania; Guǎng = Aristolochia danger.

Correct identity

NameIdentityJob
Hàn Fáng Jǐ 汉防己Stephaniae tetrandrae RadixPromotes urination and treats edema or Wind-Damp painful obstruction.
Guǎng Fáng Jǐ 广防己Aristolochiae fangchi RadixObsolete aristolochic-acid source.

Exam and safety

Exam keyThe modern accepted Fáng Jǐ in standard study material is Hàn Fáng Jǐ / Stephania.
Safety alertGuǎng Fáng Jǐ is nephrotoxic and carcinogenic and must not replace Hàn Fáng Jǐ.
