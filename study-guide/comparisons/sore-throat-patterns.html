Pathology or symptom comparison

Sore and Swollen Throat Pattern Differentiation

Diagnostic orientation

Core ideaOnset, severity and time-of-day distinguish the three presentations; airway and deep-neck infection signs take priority.
Exam focusAcute exterior onset, severe excess Heat or chronic evening dryness

Comparative differentiation

Pattern or presentationDecisive cluesRepresentative formula/herbStrategy
Lung Wind-HeatAcute red swollen painful dry/burning throat worse swallowing, with fever/chills, cough and yellow phlegmShu Feng Qing Re TangDispel Wind-Heat and benefit throat.
Exuberant Lung-Stomach HeatSevere throat pain radiating to ear/jaw, difficult swallowing/obstruction, high fever, thirst, halitosis, constipation and thick yellow phlegmQing Yan Li Ge TangClear Heat-toxin and reduce swelling.
Lung-Kidney Yin depletionSlow/chronic mild red dry itchy throat worse afternoon/night, dry cough, weak back/knees, insomnia and five-center HeatYang Yin Qing Fei Tang + Zhi Bai Di Huang WanNourish Yin and clear deficient Fire.

Study rule

How to distinguishAcute exterior signs indicate Wind-Heat; severe high fever/obstruction/halitosis indicates excess Lung-Stomach Heat; chronic evening dryness with empty Heat indicates Yin deficiency.
Memory cueAcute-cold signs—Wind; severe-hot-blocked—excess Heat; chronic-dry-evening—Yin.
SafetyDrooling, inability to swallow liquids, muffled voice, stridor, breathing difficulty, neck swelling, dehydration or severe one-sided pain requires urgent evaluation. Test suspected strep when appropriate.
