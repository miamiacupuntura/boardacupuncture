Trap 10 of 22

Shān Zhū Yú 山茱萸 vs. Wú Zhū Yú 吴茱萸

One prefix separates a sour Liver-Kidney astringent from a hot, dispersing Evodia fruit.

Memory rule

AnchorShān stores and secures; Wú warms, descends and disperses Cold.

Correct identity

NameIdentityJob
Shān Zhū Yú 山茱萸Corni FructusTonifies Liver-Kidney and secures Essence or excessive leakage.
Wú Zhū Yú 吴茱萸Evodiae FructusWarms the Middle/Lower Jiao, disperses Cold and redirects rebellious Qi downward.

Exam and safety

Exam keyLeakage from deficiency suggests Shān Zhū Yú; Cold pain with vomiting or diarrhea suggests Wú Zhū Yú.
Safety alertWú Zhū Yú is very hot and slightly toxic; avoid confusing it with the gentler tonic-astringent Shān Zhū Yú.
