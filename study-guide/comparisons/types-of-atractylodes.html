Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersThe shared name and Spleen-Dampness actions make these easy to confuse. The decisive issue is whether the question emphasizes Qi tonification, forceful drying and sweating, or gentler drying with a moistening quality.
Common confusionRegular and winter Bai Zhu share the same botanical label, but winter Bai Zhu is less extremely drying and is weaker for diarrhea.
How it may appearFetal calming or exterior stabilization favors regular Bai Zhu; Damp-predominant obstruction or strong sweating favors Cang Zhu; a request for less harsh drying favors winter Bai Zhu.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), p. 728.
