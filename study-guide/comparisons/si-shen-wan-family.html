Si Shen Wan Family

Root formula and architecture

RootSi Shen Wan combines two small units to warm Spleen-Kidney Yang, bind the intestines and stop chronic pre-dawn diarrhea.
ArchitectureEr Shen Wan supplies Bu Gu Zhi and Rou Dou Kou for warming and binding; Wu Wei Zi San supplies Wu Wei Zi and Wu Zhu Yu for astringing and dispersing Cold.

Study rule

How to chooseChronic Cold diarrhea is the base; the highly testable clock clue—before dawn or fifth watch—selects the complete Si Shen Wan combination.
Memory cueTwo warm and bind + two stop leakage = Four Miracles at dawn.
SafetyDo not astringe infectious, Damp-Heat or Food-Stagnation diarrhea. Blood, fever, severe pain, dehydration, weight loss or persistent diarrhea requires medical evaluation.
