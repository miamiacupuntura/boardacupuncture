Pathology or symptom comparison

Depression Pattern Differentiation

Diagnostic orientation

Core ideaMood symptoms are differentiated by the dominant mechanism: constraint, Heat, Phlegm obstruction or deficient nourishment of the Heart-Spirit.
Exam focusEmotional constraint, Heat, plum-pit sensation and deficiency signs

Comparative differentiation

Pattern or presentationDecisive cluesRepresentative formula/herbStrategy
Liver-Qi stagnationDespondence, frequent sighing, chest/hypochondriac distention, belching and wiry pulseChai Hu Shu Gan SanSoothe Liver, regulate Qi and relieve constraint.
Constrained Qi transforms into FireAgitation, irritability, bitter dry mouth, acid regurgitation, constipation and red tongueDan Zhi Xiao Yao San + Zuo Jin WanClear Liver Fire, harmonize Stomach and relieve constraint.
Qi and Phlegm obstructionPlum-pit throat sensation, chest blockage and slippery-wiry pulseBan Xia Hou Po TangRegulate Qi, transform Phlegm and dissipate binds.
Yin and Blood deficiencyTrance-like state, fright, grief/crying, restlessness and pale tongueGan Mai Da Zao TangNourish Heart and quiet the Spirit.

Study rule

How to distinguishSighing/distention marks Qi stagnation; red-bitter-irritable marks Fire; throat obstruction with sticky coat marks Phlegm; pale, fragile emotionality marks deficiency.
Memory cueSigh—Qi; burn—Fire; lump—Phlegm; pale and fragile—deficiency.
SafetyDepression requires biomedical assessment. Suicidal thoughts, self-harm plans, inability to care for oneself, psychosis or immediate danger requires emergency help.
