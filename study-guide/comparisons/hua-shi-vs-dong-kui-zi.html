Herb comparison

Talcum (hua shi) vs. Malvae Fructus (dong kui guo / dong kui zi)

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueHua ShiDong Kui Zi
Shared cluesBoth are slippery, unblock the orifices and treat urinary difficulty with rasping urethral pain.
Fast distinctionCools Summerheat, removes Dampness and is used topically for Damp sores or eczema.Assists bowel movements, promotes lactation and reduces swelling.

Comparative answer key

CompareHua Shi (Talcum)Dong Kui Zi (Malvae Fructus)
Shared categoryHerbs that Drain Dampness.
Shared qualitiesSlippery and able to unblock the orifices.
Shared indicationUrinary difficulty with rasping pain in the urethra.
Primary emphasisCools Summerheat and facilitates the removal of Dampness.Assists bowel movements.
Distinctive additional actionApplied topically for Damp sores and eczema.Promotes lactation and reduces swelling.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersBoth share slippery urinary-opening actions, so Summerheat and skin clues must be separated from bowel and lactation clues.
Common confusionSummerheat, Damp sores or eczema favors Hua Shi; constipation, insufficient lactation or swelling favors Dong Kui Zi.
Name noteMalvae Fructus appears in the source as both Dong Kui Guo and Dong Kui Zi.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), p. 299. Source Exam Keys CMID 8936 and CMID 8989, question 10.
