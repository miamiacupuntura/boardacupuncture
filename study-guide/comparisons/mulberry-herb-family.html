Family 1 of 20

Mulberry 桑 — One Tree, Four Jobs

Climb the mulberry from leaf to fruit: look, reach, breathe and bank.

One picture, one rule

RuleLeaves go up/out, twig reaches the limbs, root bark descends Lung Heat, berry nourishes.

The part decides the job

MedicinalPart / identityJob
Sāng Yè 桑叶LeafDisperses Wind-Heat, clears Lung/Liver Heat, moistens dryness and benefits eyes.
Sāng Zhī 桑枝TwigDispels Wind-Damp and unblocks channels, especially the upper limbs.
Sāng Bái Pí 桑白皮Root barkDrains Lung Heat, calms wheezing, promotes urination and reduces edema.
Sāng Shèn 桑椹FruitNourishes Yin and Blood, generates fluids and moistens Intestines.
Sāng Jì Shēng 桑寄生Taxillus lodger—not mulberryTonifies Liver-Kidney, treats Bi and calms the fetus.
Sāng Piāo Xiāo 桑螵蛸Mantis egg case—not mulberrySecures Essence and reduces urination.

Exam and safety

Exam keyThe first four are tree parts; the last two use Sāng as an address, not a species.
SafetySāng Jì Shēng and Sāng Piāo Xiāo must not be inferred from mulberry pharmacology.
