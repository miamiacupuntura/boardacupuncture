Herb comparison

Curcumae Radix (yu jin) vs. Curcumae longae Rhizoma (jiang huang)

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueYu JinJiang Huang
Shared clueBoth invigorate Blood, move Qi and facilitate the Gallbladder to relieve jaundice.
Fast distinctionCold and Blood-level; cools Blood Heat, relieves constraint and is stronger for jaundice.Warm and moves both Qi and Blood; uniquely treats Wind painful obstruction.

Comparative answer key

CompareYu JinJiang Huang
Medicinal nameCurcumae Radix.Curcumae longae Rhizoma.
Shared categoryHerbs that Regulate the Blood · Herbs that Invigorate the Blood.
Temperature and levelCold; enters the Blood level.Warm; moves both Qi and Blood.
Blood actionCools Blood Heat while breaking Blood stasis and moving Qi at the Blood level.Moves Qi and Blood without the Blood-cooling emphasis.
Jaundice actionStronger at facilitating the Gallbladder and eliminating jaundice.Also facilitates the Gallbladder to address jaundice.
Distinctive actionClears Heart Heat and relieves constraint.Treats Wind painful obstruction, which Yu Jin does not.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersBoth move Qi and Blood and may appear in jaundice questions. Cold Blood-level Heat or constraint favors Yu Jin; warmth and Wind painful obstruction favor Jiang Huang.
Memory cueYu Jin cools, clears and relieves constraint; Jiang Huang warms, moves and reaches Wind-Bi.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), p. 611. Source Exam Keys CMID 8998, question 2, and CMID 9114, question 7.
