Jiao Ai Tang vs. Wen Jing Tang vs. Gu Chong Tang vs. Gu Jing Wan

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueJiao Ai TangWen Jing TangGu Chong TangGu Jing Wan
Shared clueAll treat uterine bleeding, but the color, amount, thermal signs, timing and deficient organ system identify the formula.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it matters“Uterine bleeding” is only the starting clue. Thermal quality, flow and pathogenesis distinguish the four strategies.
Memory cueJiao Ai warms and holds; Wen Jing warms and moves; Gu Chong lifts and secures; Gu Jing cools and secures.
SafetyHeavy bleeding, fainting, pregnancy bleeding or postmenopausal bleeding requires urgent gynecologic evaluation.
