Zingiberis Rhizoma preparatum (pao jiang) vs. Artemisiae argyi Folium (ai ye)

Study tip · Clues to help answer

CluePao JiangAi Ye
Shared cluesBoth warm the channels and stop bleeding.
Fast distinctionWarms Deficiency Cold primarily in the Middle Burner.Acts primarily on the Lower Burner.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersTheir shared warming-hemostatic action can make them easy to confuse; the location separates them: Middle Burner points to Pao Jiang, while Lower Burner points to Ai Ye.
Memory cuePao Jiang warms the Middle; Ai Ye acts below.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), p. 595. Source Exam Key CMID 9031, question 1.
