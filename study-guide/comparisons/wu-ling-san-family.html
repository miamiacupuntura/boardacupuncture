Wu Ling San Family

Root formula and architecture

RootWu Ling San promotes urination and restores Bladder Qi transformation while warming the Yang that moves fluids.
ArchitectureZe Xie, Zhu Ling and Fu Ling drain water; Bai Zhu strengthens Spleen transport; Gui Zhi warms Yang and transforms Qi.

Study rule

How to chooseGui Zhi/Bai Zhu indicate warming transformation; add Ping Wei for heavier Dampness, Yin Chen for jaundice, or choose Zhu Ling when Heat and damaged Yin prohibit warming.
Memory cueWu Ling = transform water; Wei Ling = Damp middle; Yin Chen = yellow; Zhu Ling = hot and dry.
SafetyWater-draining formulas can alter fluid and electrolyte balance and raise lithium levels. Anuria, severe dehydration, shortness of breath or rapidly worsening edema needs urgent assessment.
