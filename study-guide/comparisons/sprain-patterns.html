Diagnostic orientation

Core ideaThe source gives one principal presentation; the exam task is to recognize local traumatic Qi-and-Blood stagnation and distinguish it from injuries requiring imaging.
Exam focusLocal swelling, discoloration, pain and restricted movement

Study rule

How to distinguishAfter trauma, swelling plus red-purple discoloration and limited motion identifies local Qi-and-Blood stagnation.
Memory cueTrauma + swollen + purple + restricted = Qi and Blood stagnation.
SafetyInability to bear weight, deformity, bony tenderness, loss of sensation/circulation, severe swelling or high-energy trauma requires prompt medical assessment and possible imaging.
