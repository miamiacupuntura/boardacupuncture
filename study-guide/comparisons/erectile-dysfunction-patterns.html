Erectile Dysfunction Pattern Differentiation

Diagnostic orientation

Core ideaThe source contrasts a cold Kidney-deficiency presentation with a hot, heavy Lower-Burner excess.
Exam focusCold deficiency versus hot, heavy urinary signs

Comparative differentiation

Pattern or presentationDecisive cluesRepresentative formula/herbStrategy
Life-Gate Fire debilitationWeak erection with cold limbs, fatigue, tinnitus, weak back and frequent urinationWu Zi Yan Zong Wan or Zan Yu DanSupplement Kidney and invigorate Yang.
Lower-Burner Damp-HeatErectile difficulty with scrotal dampness, heavy legs, bitter taste and burning dark urineLong Dan Xie Gan TangClear Heat and drain Dampness.

Study rule

How to distinguishCold, fatigue and lumbar weakness indicate Kidney Yang; damp genital signs, heaviness and burning urine indicate Damp-Heat.
Memory cueCold and weak—Ming Men; hot, heavy and damp—Damp-Heat.
SafetyNew erectile dysfunction can signal cardiovascular, endocrine, neurologic or medication-related disease. Chest symptoms or exertional pain requires prompt evaluation.
