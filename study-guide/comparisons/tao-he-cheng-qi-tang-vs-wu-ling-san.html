Formula comparison

Tao He Cheng Qi Tang vs. Wu Ling San

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueTao He Cheng Qi TangWu Ling San
Shared clueBoth treat lower-burner Taiyang accumulation patterns involving the Bladder region and may present with lower-abdominal fullness.
Fast distinctionBlood accumulation with Heat: urination remains normal; acute lower-abdominal pain, mental agitation and Blood-stasis signs dominate.Water accumulation from failed Qi transformation: urinary difficulty, thirst, edema or vomiting after drinking dominate.

Comparative answer key

CompareTao He Cheng Qi TangWu Ling San
Accumulated substanceBlood with Heat.Water and fluids.
UrinationTypically normal.Scanty or difficult.
Distinct actionPurges Heat and breaks lower-burner Blood stasis.Promotes urination and warms Yang to transform Qi.
Exam shortcutLower abdominal stasis + normal urine.Fluid retention + abnormal urine.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersUrination is the classic separator between Taiyang Blood accumulation and Taiyang water accumulation.
Memory cueTao He: Blood, urine okay; Wu Ling: water, urine blocked.
SafetyTao He Cheng Qi Tang is strongly moving and purgative and is avoided in pregnancy. Acute abdominal pain, pregnancy bleeding, urinary retention or altered mental status requires urgent care.
SourceSource bank: FWB 10.1, Comparison Formulas to Invigorate the Blood; corroborated with FWB 5.2 and formula profiles.
