Supplemental family 5 of 6

Artemisia Trio — Deficiency Heat, Jaundice and Moxa

Three Artemisia medicinals move in different thermal and organ directions.

Memory rule

AnchorQīng clears hidden Heat; Yīn Chén clears yellow; Ài warms and stops bleeding.

Identity decides the job

MedicinalIdentityJob
Qīng Hāo 青蒿Artemisia annua herbClears deficiency/lingering Heat, Summerheat and malaria-type fever.
Yīn Chén Hāo 茵陈蒿Artemisia capillaris herbClears Damp-Heat from Liver/Gallbladder and treats jaundice.
Ài Yè 艾叶Artemisia argyi leafWarms womb/channels, stops Cold-type bleeding and supplies moxa.

Exam and safety

Exam keyBone-steaming fever, jaundice and Cold uterine bleeding are the three anchors.
SafetyQīng Hāo is added late when decocted; Ài Yè/moxa requires pregnancy, bleeding and burn/smoke precautions.
