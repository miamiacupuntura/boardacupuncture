Misleading name 3 of 6

Dāng Guī Bǔ Xuè Tāng 当归补血汤

The Blood-tonifying formula named for Dāng Guī is mostly Huáng Qí.

Name versus reality

What it suggestsThe title suggests Dāng Guī should dominate the prescription.
RealityHuáng Qí and Dāng Guī are classically used in a 5:1 ratio: abundant Qi generates and holds Blood.

How to answer

Decisive clueBlood deficiency after loss with floating deficiency Heat, fatigue or weak recovery.
MemoryFive parts Qi build one part Blood.

Safety

AlertIdentify and control the source of blood loss. Heavy bleeding, faintness, chest pain or pregnancy-related bleeding requires urgent care.
