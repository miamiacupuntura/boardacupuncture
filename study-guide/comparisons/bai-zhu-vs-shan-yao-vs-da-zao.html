Herb comparison

Bai Zhu vs. Shan Yao vs. Da Zao

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueBai ZhuShan YaoDa Zao
Shared cluesAll belong to Herbs that Tonify the Qi and tonify the Spleen while augmenting Qi.
Fast distinctionDrying Spleen Qi tonic; calms the fetus.Neutral and moistening; benefits Spleen, Lung and Kidney and binds Essence.Tonifies Qi and Blood, nourishes the Heart and harmonizes other herbs.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersAll three can answer a general Spleen Qi deficiency question, so the secondary organ system, moisture quality and distinctive action provide the separation.
Common confusionBai Zhu and Da Zao are both warm and enter the Spleen-Stomach. Bai Zhu dries Dampness and calms the fetus; Da Zao nourishes Blood and harmonizes formulas.
How it may appearDrying and fetal calming favors Bai Zhu; Lung-Kidney support, Essence binding or xiao ke favors Shan Yao; Blood nourishment, spirit calming or harmonizing other herbs favors Da Zao.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), p. 731.
