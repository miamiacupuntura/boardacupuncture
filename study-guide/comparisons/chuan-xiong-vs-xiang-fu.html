Herb comparison

Chuanxiong Rhizoma (chuan xiong) vs. Cyperi Rhizoma (xiang fu)

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueChuan XiongXiang Fu
Shared cluesBoth act at the Qi–Blood interface, are described as “Qi within Blood” herbs, and enter the Liver and Triple Burner.
Fast distinctionBlood herb; light, aromatic, upward/outward and channel-guiding.Qi herb; solid/heavy, downward, middle-harmonizing and constraint-relieving.

Comparative answer key

CompareChuan XiongXiang Fu
CategoryHerbs that Regulate and Invigorate the Blood.Herbs that Regulate the Qi.
Substance/directionLight and aromatic; raises and discharges upward toward the head and outward to the exterior.Solid and heavy; directs downward, harmonizes the middle and relieves constraint.
Channel reachClassic guide for Liver, Gallbladder, Triple Burner and Pericardium.Extends its effects to all twelve channels.
Distinctive actionDisperses Wind and Cold at the exterior while moving Blood and Qi.Regulates Qi broadly, with downward and middle-jiao emphasis.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersThe herbs are highly complementary at the Qi–Blood interface but move in opposite directions.
Common confusionUpward/outward and Blood-guiding clues favor Chuan Xiong; downward, all-twelve-channel Qi regulation favors Xiang Fu.
