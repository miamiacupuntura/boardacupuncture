Herb comparison

Nu Zhen Zi vs. Mo Han Lian

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueNu Zhen ZiMo Han Lian
Shared clueBoth enrich Kidney Yin and augment the Liver.
Fast distinctionSlightly less tonifying; cools Yin-deficiency Heat and brightens the eyes.More strongly nourishes Liver-Kidney Yin; blackens hair and cools Blood to stop bleeding.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersBoth nourish the same Liver-Kidney axis, but eye-brightening and deficiency-Heat cooling favor Nu Zhen Zi, while stronger Yin nourishment, blackened hair and hemostasis favor Mo Han Lian.
Memory cueNu Zhen Zi clears and brightens; Mo Han Lian nourishes, blackens and stops bleeding.
