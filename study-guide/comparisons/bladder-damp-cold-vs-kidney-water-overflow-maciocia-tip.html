Bladder Damp-Cold vs. Kidney Water Overflow

Separate localized urinary obstruction from systemic Yang-deficient fluid retention.

Fast differentiation clues

ClueBladder Damp-ColdKidney Water Overflow
SharedBoth cause difficult or scant urination, heaviness, Cold signs and a wet tongue.
Fast distinctionBladder obstruction: turbid urine and lower-abdominal heaviness predominate.Kidney Water overflow: leg/ankle edema, cold weak back and systemic fluid accumulation predominate.

Clinical table

CompareBladder Damp-ColdKidney Water Overflow
ScopeLocalized lower-burner obstruction.Systemic water transformation failure.
Formula associationBa Zheng San or Shi Wei San in the supplied table.Jin Gui Shen Qi Wan plus Wu Ling San.

Exam and safety

Why it mattersEdema and Kidney-Yang signs identify systemic overflow beyond a local Bladder obstruction.
Memory cueBladder Damp-Cold blocks the drain; Kidney overflow floods the body.
SafetySudden edema, rapid weight gain, reduced urine, breathlessness or chest symptoms requires urgent heart/kidney evaluation.
