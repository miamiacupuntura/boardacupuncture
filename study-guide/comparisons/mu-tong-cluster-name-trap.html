Mù Tōng 木通: Safe Names vs. Guān Mù Tōng

Mù Tōng is a label cluster: botanical identity decides whether the product is acceptable or dangerous.

Memory rule

AnchorRead beyond “Mù Tōng”: Akebia/Clematis/Tōng Cǎo are not Guān Mù Tōng.

Exam and safety

Exam keyNever treat all “Mù Tōng” names as synonyms. Match the pinyin, characters and botanical source.
Safety alertGuān Mù Tōng is nephrotoxic/carcinogenic and obsolete. Do not substitute species blindly; regulation varies by jurisdiction.
