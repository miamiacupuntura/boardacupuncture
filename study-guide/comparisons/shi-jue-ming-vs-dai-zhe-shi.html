Shi Jue Ming vs. Dai Zhe Shi

Study tip · Two heavy anchors

ClueShi Jue MingDai Zhe Shi

Formula boundary and safety

Current rosterShi Jue Ming is co-chief in Tian Ma Gou Teng Yin. Dai Zhe Shi is not chief or co-chief in the current 63-formula roster.
PreparationCrush and predecoct both. Require authenticated, contaminant-tested material.
SafetyDai Zhe Shi is especially heavy and may contain trace arsenic; avoid large doses, prolonged use and pregnancy. Both can burden weak digestion.
