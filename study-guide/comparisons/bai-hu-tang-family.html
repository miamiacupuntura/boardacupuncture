Bai Hu Tang Family

Root formula and architecture

RootBai Hu Tang clears blazing Yangming Qi-level Heat and protects fluids.
ArchitectureShi Gao powerfully clears Heat; Zhi Mu clears Heat and enriches Yin; Geng Mi and Zhi Gan Cao protect Stomach Qi and fluids.

Study rule

How to chooseBai Hu requires exuberant Heat. Add Ren Shen for collapse of Qi/fluids, Gui Zhi for painful obstruction, or move to Zhu Ye Shi Gao when Heat is residual and deficiency dominates.
Memory cueBai Hu = four greats; Ren Shen = depleted; Gui Zhi = joints; Zhu Ye = recovery stage.
SafetyAvoid in Cold from deficiency or without genuine blazing Heat. Shi Gao is traditionally pre-decocted; persistent high fever or altered mental status needs medical assessment.
