Dà vs. Xiǎo Qīng Lóng Tāng

Both release Wind-Cold, but the Big Dragon breathes Fire and the Little Dragon spits water.

Memory rule

AnchorDà adds Shí Gāo for Heat; Xiǎo warms and transforms cold thin fluids.
