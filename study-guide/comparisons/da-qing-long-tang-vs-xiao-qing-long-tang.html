Formula name pair

Da Qing Long Tang vs. Xiao Qing Long Tang

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueDa Qing Long TangXiao Qing Long Tang
Shared clueBoth strongly release Exterior Wind-Cold and may present with chills, fever, body aches and absence of sweating.
Fast distinctionInternal Heat: irritability, thirst and stronger fever.Internal Cold fluids: cough, wheezing and profuse thin frothy sputum.

Comparative answer key

CompareDa Qing Long TangXiao Qing Long Tang
Interior componentHeat constrained beneath an Exterior Cold pattern.Cold fluids retained in the Lungs and Stomach.
Decisive ingredient clueShi Gao clears internal Heat.Gan Jiang, Xi Xin and Wu Wei Zi warm and contain fluid metabolism.
Decisive symptomRestlessness or thirst with no sweating.Watery sputum, retching or inability to lie flat.
Exam shortcutBig Dragon = Heat inside.Small Dragon = cold water inside.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersThe Exterior signs overlap almost completely. The internal branch—Heat versus Cold fluids—is the real comparison.
Memory cueDa Qing Long carries fire; Xiao Qing Long carries cold water.
SafetyBoth contain Ma Huang; Xiao Qing Long also requires verified Xi Xin sourcing. Cardiovascular and medication screening is essential.
