Pathology or symptom comparison

Low Back Pain Pattern Differentiation

Diagnostic orientation

Core ideaWeather and temperature identify Damp branches, fixed stabbing pain identifies stasis, and recurrent aching with weakness identifies Kidney deficiency.
Exam focusTemperature, weather, pain quality and deficiency signs

Comparative differentiation

Pattern or presentationDecisive cluesRepresentative formula/herbStrategy
Cold-DampCold heavy pain worse in cold/rain and relieved by warmthGan Cao Gan Jiang Ling Zhu TangWarm channels and drain Dampness.
Damp-HeatHot lumbar pain worse in hot/rainy weather with dark urine and yellow greasy coatSi Miao SanClear Heat and drain Dampness.
Blood stasisFixed stabbing night pain, worse with pressure; purple tongueShen Tong Zhu Yu TangInvigorate Blood and open channels.
Kidney Yang deficiencyRecurrent aching better with rest/pressure plus cold and fatigueYou Gui WanWarm Kidney Yang.
Kidney Yin deficiencyRecurrent aching plus insomnia, dry throat and five-center HeatZuo Gui WanNourish Kidney Yin.

Study rule

How to distinguishCold-heavy-weather pain is Damp; hot-yellow signs are Damp-Heat; fixed stabbing is stasis; chronic weakness with Cold is Yang and with empty Heat is Yin.
Memory cueCold-heavy—Cold-Damp; hot-sticky—Damp-Heat; fixed-purple—stasis; cold-weak—Yang; hot-dry—Yin.
SafetyNew weakness, saddle numbness, bladder/bowel loss, fever, major trauma, cancer history or severe night pain requires urgent evaluation.
