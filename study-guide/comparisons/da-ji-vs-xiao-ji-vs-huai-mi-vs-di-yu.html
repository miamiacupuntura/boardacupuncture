Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersAll four cool Blood and stop bleeding, so the accompanying action identifies the correct herb.
Common confusionHeat toxin = Da Ji; urine = Xiao Ji; Liver Fire = Huai Mi; painful swollen lesions = Di Yu.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), p. 572. Source Exam Keys CMID 8940; CMID 8933 and CMID 8976, question 1; and CMID 9099, question 5.
