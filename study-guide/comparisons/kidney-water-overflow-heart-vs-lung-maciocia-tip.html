Kidney Water Overflow to Heart vs. Lung

Use palpitations and circulation versus cough, dyspnea and frothy sputum to locate the fluid complication.

Fast differentiation clues

ClueWater Overflow to HeartWater Overflow to Lung
SharedBoth arise from Kidney-Yang deficiency with edema, cold back/legs, abdominal fullness and scant clear urine.
Fast distinctionHeart involvement: palpitations, breathlessness, cold hands and possible chest discomfort.Lung involvement: cough, asthma/dyspnea and watery or frothy sputum.

Clinical table

CompareWater Overflow to HeartWater Overflow to Lung
Organ cluePulse/circulation and Heart symptoms.Cough, descent failure and fluid in the chest.
Formula associationShen Fu Tang in the supplied table.Jin Gui Shen Qi Wan plus Wu Ling San in the supplied table.

Exam and safety

Why it mattersThe same fluid root can affect both organs; palpitations versus respiratory fluid signs locate the branch.
Memory cueWater shakes the Heart; Water floods the Lung.
SafetyChest pain, fainting, severe breathlessness, inability to lie flat or pink/frothy sputum is an emergency.
