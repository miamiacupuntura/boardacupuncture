Large Intestine Heat vs. Heat Obstruction

Recognize when dry Heat constipation escalates into an acute excess obstruction.

Fast differentiation clues

ClueLarge Intestine HeatHeat Obstructing Large Intestine
SharedBoth cause constipation, dry stool, anal burning, thirst and a yellow-dry coat.
Fast distinctionHeat dries: constipation and burning without marked systemic excess.Heat obstructs: significant abdominal pain/distention, fever and a big forceful pulse.

Clinical table

CompareLarge Intestine HeatHeat Obstructing Large Intestine
SeverityDry Heat constipation.Acute excess accumulation and blockage.
Formula associationMa Zi Ren Wan.Tiao Wei Cheng Qi Tang in the supplied table.

Exam and safety

Why it mattersPain, fever, distention and pulse force identify obstruction rather than simple dryness.
Memory cueHeat dries the stool; obstruction locks it behind pain and fever.
SafetySevere pain, vomiting, swollen abdomen or inability to pass stool/gas may be bowel obstruction—seek emergency care.
