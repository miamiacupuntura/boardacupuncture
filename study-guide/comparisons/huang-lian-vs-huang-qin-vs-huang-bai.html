Herb comparison

Huang Lian vs. Huang Qin vs. Huang Bai

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueHuang LianHuang QinHuang Bai
Shared clueAll three clear Heat, drain Fire, resolve toxicity and dry Dampness; they may be combined for Damp-Heat or Heat toxin with overwhelming Fire.
Fast distinctionHeart and Stomach; middle burner.Lung; also quiets the fetus.Kidney and lower burner.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersThe category and four principal actions are shared, so the organ, burner and distinctive accompanying symptom determine the keyed answer.
Memory cueLian: Heart-Stomach; Qin: Lung and pregnancy; Bai: Kidney and lower burner.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), p. 140. Source Exam Keys CMID 8978, question 1, and CMID 8980, question 2.
