Gui Zhi Fu Ling Wan vs. Wen Jing Tang

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueGui Zhi Fu Ling WanWen Jing Tang
Shared clueBoth warm and move Blood in gynecologic disorders with lower-abdominal pain and irregular uterine bleeding.
Fast distinctionBlood stasis mass is primary: fixed lower-abdominal mass, pain or persistent bleeding.Cold and deficiency are primary: irregular menses, cold lower abdomen, Blood deficiency and stasis.

Comparative answer key

CompareGui Zhi Fu Ling WanWen Jing Tang
Primary mechanismBlood stasis obstructs the womb.Cold in the channels plus deficiency causes Blood stasis and irregular flow.
Therapeutic emphasisDispel stasis and reduce masses.Warm channels, dispel Cold, nourish Blood and remove stasis.
Decisive clueFixed mass or focal stasis.Cold signs with thin/dry Blood-deficiency signs.
Exam shortcutMass first.Cold-deficiency cycle disorder first.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersBoth move Blood and contain Gui Zhi, but Gui Zhi Fu Ling Wan is mass/stasis-centered while Wen Jing Tang is Cold-deficiency-centered.
Memory cueGui Zhi Fu Ling breaks the mass; Wen Jing warms the menstrual pathway.
SafetyPregnancy, heavy bleeding, severe pelvic pain or a suspected mass requires obstetric/gynecologic evaluation before herbal treatment.
SourceSource bank: FWB 10.1 and FWB 11.1 comparisons; corroborated with individual formula files.
