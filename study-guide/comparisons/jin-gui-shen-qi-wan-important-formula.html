Batch 4 · Formula 3 of 10

Jin Gui Shen Qi Wan

Kidney Yang deficiency with impaired transformation of fluids and weakness of Essence.

High-yield summary

CategoryWarm and Tonify Kidney Yang
PatternKidney Yang deficiency with impaired transformation of fluids and weakness of Essence.
ActionWarms Kidney Yang from within a Yin-nourishing base and restores water metabolism.
Decisive clueCold weak low back, lower-body edema or urinary disturbance with a pale swollen wet tongue and deep weak pulse.

Ingredients

Sheng Di Huang (often Shu Di Huang in later products), Shan Zhu Yu, Shan Yao, Ze Xie, Mu Dan Pi, Fu Ling, Gui Zhi and Zhi Fu Zi.

Commercial preparations and lineages may vary; always verify the source being used.

Differentiation and memory

Do not confuseThe name is often used for the same classical eight-ingredient formula as Ba Wei Di Huang Wan; prepared versions may substitute Shu Di Huang or Rou Gui.
Memory cueSix nourish and drain the Kidney base; Gui Zhi and Fu Zi kindle its Yang.

Safety

Fu Zi requires correct processing and professional prescribing. Sudden edema, low urine or breathlessness requires urgent evaluation.
