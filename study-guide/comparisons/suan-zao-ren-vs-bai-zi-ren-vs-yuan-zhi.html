Herb comparison

Ziziphi spinosae Semen (suan zao ren) vs. Platycladi Semen (bai zi ren) vs. Polygalae Radix (yuan zhi)

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueSuan Zao RenBai Zi RenYuan Zhi
Shared cluesAll enter the Heart, calm the Spirit and treat fright palpitations and insomnia.
Fast distinctionLiver Blood and Heart Yin deficiency; restrains sweating.Heart-focused nourishment; moistens dry constipation.Reconnects Heart and Kidneys; improves concentration and transforms Phlegm.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersAll three can answer an insomnia question. Sweating and Liver-Blood deficiency point to Suan Zao Ren; dry constipation and Heart nourishment point to Bai Zi Ren; cognition, Heart-Kidney communication or Phlegm point to Yuan Zhi.
Memory cueSuan Zao Ren restrains; Bai Zi Ren moistens; Yuan Zhi reconnects and transforms Phlegm.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), p. 927. Source Exam Key CMID 9072, questions 1–2.
