Herb comparison

Arnebiae/Lithospermi Radix (zi cao) vs. Arctii Fructus (niu bang zi)

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueZi CaoNiu Bang Zi
Shared cluesBoth resolve toxicity, vent rashes, moisten the bowels and unblock the Intestines. They may be combined when constrained Heat-toxin prevents a rash from fully expressing and constipation is present.
Fast distinctionCools the Blood and treats unexpressed rashes from Heat-toxin constrained at the Blood level.Expels Wind-Heat and treats rashes from Wind-Heat confined in the Exterior.

Comparative answer key

CompareZi Cao (Arnebiae/Lithospermi Radix)Niu Bang Zi (Arctii Fructus)
CategoryHerbs that Clear Heat and Cool the Blood.Cool, Acrid Herbs that Release the Exterior.
Shared actionsResolve toxicity, vent rashes, moisten the bowels and unblock the Intestines.
Nature and characterPurple, slippery, sweet, salty and cold.Heavy and slippery; acrid, bitter and slightly cold.
Primary levelBlood level: clears and regulates Blood Heat.Qi and Exterior levels: releases the Exterior and disperses Wind-Heat.
Rash clueHeat-toxin constrained in the Blood prevents full rash expression.Wind-Heat confined in the Exterior causes the rash.
Additional actionsModerately eliminates Damp-Heat through stool and urine.Benefits the throat, disperses clumps, directs Qi downward and resolves internal Heat-toxin.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersBoth can appear in rash with constipation, so the decisive clue is Blood-level Heat-toxin for Zi Cao versus Exterior Wind-Heat for Niu Bang Zi.
Memory cueZi Cao cools the Blood; Niu Bang Zi expels Wind-Heat.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), p. 129. Source Exam Key CMID 9025, questions 2–4.
