Trap 6 of 22

Chì Xiǎo Dòu 赤小豆 vs. Xiāng Sī Zǐ 相思子

A food-like red bean medicinal can be confused with the deadly rosary pea seed.

Memory rule

AnchorChì Xiǎo Dòu drains Dampness; Xiāng Sī Zǐ contains abrin.

Correct identity

NameIdentityJob
Chì Xiǎo Dòu 赤小豆Vignae Semen / rice beanPromotes urination, reduces swelling and helps discharge pus.
Xiāng Sī Zǐ 相思子Abrus precatorius seed / rosary peaNot an internal substitute; contains the potent toxin abrin.

Exam and safety

Exam keyUse full characters and botanical identity; color or “red bean” appearance is not enough.
Safety alertXiāng Sī Zǐ can be fatal if chewed or ingested. Suspected exposure is an emergency/poison-control case.
