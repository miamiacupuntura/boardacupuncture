Pathology or symptom comparison

Excessive Tearing Clinical Comparison

Diagnostic orientation

Core ideaThe source gives one deficiency pattern; the clinical comparison is between noninflamed clear tearing and tearing caused by irritation, infection or obstruction.
Exam focusClear cool tears without inflammation versus painful or infected tearing

Comparative differentiation

Pattern or presentationDecisive cluesRepresentative formula/herbStrategy
Liver-Kidney deficiencyFrequent clear cool thin tears worse in Wind, without redness/pain/swelling, plus dizziness, tinnitus, blurred vision and weak back/kneesZuo Gui YinNourish Liver and support Kidney.

Study rule

How to distinguishClear tears without inflammation plus chronic deficiency signs fit the source pattern; redness, pain, pus or a focal inner-corner swelling suggests another diagnosis.
Memory cueClear + cool + no red pain + weak back = Liver-Kidney deficiency presentation.
SafetyPain, photophobia, vision change, trauma, chemical exposure, pus or tender swelling near the nose requires eye assessment. Persistent one-sided tearing may need tear-duct evaluation.
SourceInternal Medicine ophthalmology section: Tearing Patterns (Liu Lei Zheng).
