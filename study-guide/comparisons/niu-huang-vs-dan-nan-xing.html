Herb comparison

Bovis Calculus (niu huang) vs. Arisaema cum Bile (dan nan xing)

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueNiu HuangDan Nan Xing
Shared cluesBoth clear Heat, eliminate Phlegm and settle convulsions in severe Phlegm-Heat patterns with impaired consciousness.
Fast distinctionMore powerfully clears the Heart and opens the orifices; also cools Blood and resolves toxicity.More strongly eliminates Phlegm, expels Wind and arrests convulsions.

Comparative answer key

CompareNiu Huang (Bovis Calculus)Dan Nan Xing (Arisaema cum Bile)
CategoryAromatic Substances that Open the Orifices.Herbs that Cool and Transform Phlegm-Heat.
Shared actionsClear Heat, eliminate Phlegm and settle convulsions.
Primary emphasisClears the Heart and opens the orifices.Eliminates Phlegm, expels Wind and arrests convulsions.
Distinctive indicationsHigh fever, delirium, impaired consciousness or swollen sores from Heat toxin.Overabundant Phlegm-Heat with Wind-Stroke, Phlegm collapse or recurrent convulsions.
Additional actionsCools Blood and resolves toxicity.The source emphasizes stronger Phlegm elimination and Wind expulsion.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersBoth can appear in severe Heat, Phlegm and convulsion presentations, so the Heart-orifice versus Wind-Phlegm emphasis determines the answer.
Common confusionHigh fever, delirium or Heat-toxin sores favor Niu Huang; dominant Phlegm-Heat and convulsions favor Dan Nan Xing.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), p. 960. Source Exam Keys CMID 8943 and CMID 9024, question 1.
