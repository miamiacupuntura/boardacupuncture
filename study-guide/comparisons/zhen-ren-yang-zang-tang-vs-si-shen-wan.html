Zhen Ren Yang Zang Tang vs. Si Shen Wan

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueZhen Ren Yang Zang TangSi Shen Wan
Shared clueBoth warm Spleen-Kidney deficiency-Cold, astringe the Intestines and treat chronic diarrhea with abdominal pain and weakness.
Fast distinctionSevere, long-standing diarrhea or dysenteric disorder with loss of control and broad Qi-Blood deficiency.Characteristic pre-dawn “fifth-watch” diarrhea from Kidney-Yang deficiency.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersThe pre-dawn timing is the high-yield Si Shen Wan clue; Zhen Ren Yang Zang is broader and more strongly astringent for severe chronic leakage.
Memory cueSi Shen owns the dawn; Yang Zang closes severe leakage.
SafetyClassical Zhen Ren Yang Zang Tang contains obsolete opium poppy husk; modern practice requires a legal, safe substitute. Bloody stool, fever, dehydration or weight loss needs medical evaluation.
