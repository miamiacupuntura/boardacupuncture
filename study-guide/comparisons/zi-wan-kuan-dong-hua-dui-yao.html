Exam focus and safety

CluesPersistent cough where both difficult expectoration and cough intensity need treatment.
Signature formulaShè Gān Má Huáng Tāng.
TrapThe pair is thermally flexible; surrounding herbs determine whether the full formula treats Heat or Cold.
PreparationHoney-fried forms are commonly selected to strengthen moistening and cough-relieving action in chronic cases.
SafetyUse professionally and avoid prolonged unsupervised use. Coughing blood, low oxygen, chest pain, high fever or breathing difficulty requires prompt medical care.
