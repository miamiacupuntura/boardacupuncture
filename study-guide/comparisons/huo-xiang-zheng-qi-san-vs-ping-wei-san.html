Huo Xiang Zheng Qi San vs. Ping Wei San

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueHuo Xiang Zheng Qi SanPing Wei San
Shared clueBoth aromatically transform Dampness, regulate Qi and treat nausea, vomiting, diarrhea, poor appetite and epigastric fullness.
Fast distinctionExterior Wind-Cold plus middle-burner Dampness: chills, fever and headache accompany digestive symptoms.Interior Dampness stagnates in Spleen-Stomach: fullness, heavy limbs and greasy coat without a defining Exterior invasion.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersThe shared digestive picture is separated by an Exterior layer. Huo Xiang Zheng Qi reaches outward; Ping Wei stays centered on internal Dampness.
Memory cueHuo Xiang = Exterior + center; Ping Wei = level the damp center.
SafetyBloody diarrhea, high fever, severe dehydration, persistent vomiting or severe abdominal pain requires medical care.
