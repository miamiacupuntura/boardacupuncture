Herb comparison

Arisaematis Rhizoma preparatum (zhi tian nan xing) vs. Arisaema cum Bile (dan nan xing)

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueZhi Tian Nan XingDan Nan Xing
Shared clueBoth are forms of Arisaema used to transform Phlegm, but their thermal nature, toxicity and clinical emphasis differ.
Fast distinctionExtremely warming, acrid and toxic; transforms Cold-Phlegm.Cooling, bitter and moistening; transforms Phlegm-Heat and settles Wind-related convulsions.

Comparative answer key

CompareZhi Tian Nan XingDan Nan Xing
CategoryWarm Herbs that Transform Cold-Phlegm.Herbs that Cool and Transform Phlegm-Heat.
Properties in sourceExtremely warming, acrid and toxic.Cooling, bitter, less acrid and described as no longer toxic.
Moisture effectThe source contrasts it with the drying tendency of the raw form.Moistening; lacks the raw form's strong tendency to dry and damage Yin.
Primary emphasisTransforms Cold-Phlegm.Transforms Phlegm-Heat, extinguishes Wind and settles convulsions and spasms.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersThe two substances share an Arisaema origin but answer opposite Cold-Phlegm and Phlegm-Heat patterns.
Common confusionExtreme warmth, acridity and toxicity favor Zhi Tian Nan Xing; cooling bitterness, moistening and convulsions favor Dan Nan Xing.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), p. 419. Source Exam Key CMID 8943.
