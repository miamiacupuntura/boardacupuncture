Herb comparison

Menthae haplocalycis Herba (bo he) vs. Arctii Fructus (niu bang zi)

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueBo HeNiu Bang Zi
Shared cluesBoth are cool, acrid exterior-releasing herbs that disperse Wind-Heat, benefit the throat and vent rashes.
Decisive clueSweating, heat in the face or eyes, Liver Qi constraint or aromatic clearing of turbid filth.Toxic throat swelling, stronger toxicity-clearing, cough with phlegm or constipation.

Comparative answer key

CompareBo He (Field Mint)Niu Bang Zi (Arctii Fructus)
Shared categoryCool, Acrid Herbs that Release the Exterior.
Exterior actionAcrid and cool; induces sweating and disperses Wind-Heat.Bitter, draining and slippery; vents Wind-Heat through the exterior without inducing sweating.
Throat and toxicityBenefits the throat and clears heat from the head, face and eyes.Stronger for the throat and toxicity, including toxic throat swelling, mumps and erysipelas.
Distinctive actionsVents rashes, regulates Qi, relieves Liver Qi constraint and aromatically clears turbid filth.Resolves toxicity, disseminates Lung Qi, stops cough, transforms phlegm and facilitates bowel movements.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersBoth may appear in Wind-Heat with sore throat or rash, so the extra symptom determines the answer.
Common confusionBo He is the stronger clue for sweating and Liver Qi constraint; Niu Bang Zi is the stronger clue for toxic swelling and intestinal lubrication.
How it may appearChoose Bo He for rash plus constrained Qi. Choose Niu Bang Zi for a severely swollen toxic throat, cough-phlegm or constipation.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), p. 51. Source Exam Keys CMID 9277 and CMID 9025, question 1.
