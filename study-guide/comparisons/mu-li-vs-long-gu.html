Exam trap · Category correction

Mu Li vs. Long Gu

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueMu LiLong Gu
Shared clueBoth are heavy, astringent substances that anchor, settle and calm the Spirit and are often combined.
Fast distinctionSalty and Kidney-directed; softens hardness, transforms Phlegm, cools Heat and supports Yin.Heart-directed and stronger for calming the Spirit; cannot soften hardness or disperse clumping.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersBoth substances can stabilize and bind, but that shared action does not change Mu Li's formal category.
Exam trapCMID 9000 labels Mu Li as “Herbs that Stabilize and Bind.” The correct category is Substances that Anchor, Settle, and Calm the Spirit.
Memory cueMu Li softens and nourishes Yin; Long Gu calms the Heart-Spirit.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), p. 912. Source Exam Key CMID 9000, question 3; category corrected here.
