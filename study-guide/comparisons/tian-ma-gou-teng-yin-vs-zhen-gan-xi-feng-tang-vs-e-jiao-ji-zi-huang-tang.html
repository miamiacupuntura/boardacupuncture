Tian Ma Gou Teng Yin vs. Zhen Gan Xi Feng Tang vs. E Jiao Ji Zi Huang Tang

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersAll may present with tremor and dizziness. Heat-dominant rising Yang, severe root deficiency with ascendance, or pale Blood deficiency determines the prescription.
Memory cueTian Ma calms hot rising Wind; Zhen Gan anchors a dangerous surge; E Jiao and egg yolk nourish deficient Wind.
SafetySudden weakness, facial droop, speech difficulty, severe new headache or loss of balance may be stroke and requires emergency care. Animal-derived ingredients require legal, ethical sourcing and appropriate substitutes.
