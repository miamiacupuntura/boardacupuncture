Pattern comparison

Damp-Cold vs. Damp-Heat

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueDamp-ColdDamp-Heat
Shared clueBoth cause heaviness, fullness, sticky symptoms, a greasy tongue coating and a slippery pulse.
Fast distinctionCold, pale and clear: no thirst, white greasy coat, loose stool and clear discharges.Hot, red and foul: thirst, yellow greasy coat, dark urine; may cause dysentery, painful urination, jaundice, furuncles or eczema.

Comparative answer key

CompareDamp-ColdDamp-Heat
Color / odorPale, white, clear and mild odor.Yellow, dark, red and strong odor.
TemperatureCold limbs or preference for warmth.Fever, burning or aversion to heat.
Tongue / pulsePale with white greasy coat; slippery-slow.Red with yellow greasy coat; slippery-rapid.
Formula examplesPing Wei San; Huo Xiang Zheng Qi San.Ba Zheng San; Long Dan Xie Gan Tang according to location.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersDampness supplies heaviness and stickiness; the attached temperature supplies the decisive color, odor and tongue clues.
Memory cueCold Damp is white and clear; Damp-Heat is yellow and foul.
SafetyBitter-cold Damp-Heat medicinals can injure a deficient Spleen/Stomach and require careful differentiation. Fever with flank pain, jaundice, inability to urinate, bloody diarrhea or severe dehydration requires urgent evaluation.

Possible exam questions · Study prompts

  • Which has dark urine and foul odor?
