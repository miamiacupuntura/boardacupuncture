Formula family

Zuo Gui / You Gui Family

Root formula and architecture

RootThe Zuo/You Gui lineage rebuilds Kidney deficiency: Left directs attention to Yin and Essence; Right to Yang and Mingmen fire.
ArchitectureWithin each side, Wan is the richer, more Essence-focused pill, while Yin is the simpler decoction for a comparatively moderate presentation.

Study rule

How to chooseFirst choose Left/Yin or Right/Yang; then choose Yin/decoction for the simpler presentation or Wan/pill for deeper Essence depletion.
Memory cueZuo = Yin; You = Yang; Yin = lighter; Wan = deeper.
SafetyRich Yin tonics can worsen Dampness and weak digestion; warming Yang formulas can aggravate Heat. You Gui Wan commonly contains processed Fu Zi and requires professional supervision.
