Ji Chuan Jian

Constipation from Kidney Yang deficiency with insufficient intestinal moisture.

High-yield summary

CategoryWarm Kidney Yang · Moisten the Intestines
PatternConstipation from Kidney Yang deficiency with insufficient intestinal moisture.
ActionWarms Kidney Yang, enriches Essence and Blood, moistens the intestines and promotes movement.
Decisive clueConstipation in an older or deficient patient with clear frequent urine, coldness and low-back weakness.

Ingredients

Dang Gui, Niu Xi, Rou Cong Rong, Ze Xie, Sheng Ma and Zhi Ke.

Commercial preparations and lineages may vary; always verify the source being used.

Differentiation and memory

Do not confuseMa Zi Ren Wan treats intestinal Dryness with Heat; Ji Chuan Jian treats deficient Cold and Kidney Yang weakness.
Memory cueWarm and moisten the river, then guide it downward.

Safety

Severe abdominal pain, vomiting, distention, blood in stool or inability to pass gas may indicate obstruction and requires urgent care.
