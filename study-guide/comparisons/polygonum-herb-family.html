Polygonum 蓼 — Root, Vine and Family Streak

The root builds Blood, its vine builds sleep, and relatives reveal a laxative/draining streak.

One picture, one rule

RuleMr. Hé’s root darkens hair; the night vine wraps the pillow.

Exam and safety

Exam keyPrepared root tonifies; raw root drains/moistens; vine calms sleep.
SafetyHé Shǒu Wū has clinically important liver-injury reports, including processed products. Avoid unsupervised use and evaluate liver symptoms/interactions.
