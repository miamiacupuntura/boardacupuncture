Small Intestine Deficient Cold vs. Spleen Yang Deficiency

Separate lower intestinal pain and borborygmi from a broader digestive transformation deficiency.

Fast differentiation clues

ClueSmall Intestine Deficient and ColdSpleen Yang Deficiency
SharedBoth cause chronic diarrhea, cold limbs, fatigue, pale wet tongue and a deep slow weak pulse.
Fast distinctionSmall Intestine focus: lower abdominal pain and borborygmi are prominent.Spleen focus: poor appetite, post-meal bloating, undigested food and edema are prominent.

Clinical table

CompareSmall Intestine Deficient and ColdSpleen Yang Deficiency

Formula associationXiao Jian Zhong Tang or Shen Ling Bai Zhu San.Li Zhong Wan.

Exam and safety

Why it mattersPain location and dominant function distinguish two overlapping Yang-deficient digestive pictures.
Memory cueSmall Intestine rumbles below; Spleen fails to transform the middle.
SafetyPersistent diarrhea, dehydration, blood/black stool, fever, severe pain or weight loss requires evaluation.
