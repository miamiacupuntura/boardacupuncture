Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersThese formulas all “stop” something, but only after treating the root: Heat, Cold, Qi collapse, Damp-Heat or Kidney failure to secure Essence.
Memory cueNight—Dang Gui Liu Huang; dawn—Si Shen; flooding—Gu Chong; yellow—Yi Huang; Essence—Jin Suo.
SafetyNight sweats, abnormal bleeding, malodorous discharge or persistent sexual/urinary symptoms require diagnosis before astringent treatment. Acute heavy bleeding is an emergency.
