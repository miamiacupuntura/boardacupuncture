Herb comparison

Lasiosphaera/Calvatia (ma bo) vs. Sophorae tonkinensis Radix (shan dou gen)

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueMa BoShan Dou Gen
Shared clueBoth clear Heat, benefit the throat, resolve toxicity and disperse clumps, especially for throat swelling and pain.
Fast distinctionAcrid and neutral; focuses on Lung and Blood, stops bleeding and treats epistaxis.Bitter and cold; acts more broadly through Heart, Lung and Intestines, dries Dampness and kills parasites.

Comparative answer key

CompareMa BoShan Dou Gen
Medicinal nameLasiosphaera/Calvatia.Sophorae tonkinensis Radix.
CategoryHerbs that Clear Heat and Resolve Toxicity.Herbs that Clear Heat and Resolve Toxicity.
Properties in sourceAcrid and neutral.Bitter and cold.
Channel and organ emphasisLung channel; focuses on Lungs and Blood.Heart, Lung and Large Intestine channels; focuses on Heart, Lungs, Intestines and Stomach.
Distinctive actionsClears Lung Fire, disperses constrained Fire, stops bleeding and helps sores heal.Directs Large-Intestine Fire downward, dries Dampness and kills parasites.
Decisive indicationNosebleed or epistaxis accompanying Lung or Blood involvement.Swelling and pain of the teeth and gums.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersBoth answer throat-swelling prompts. Bleeding and Lung-Blood focus identify Ma Bo; broader bitter-cold downward drainage, Dampness or parasites identify Shan Dou Gen.
Memory cueMa Bo clears throat and stops bleeding; Shan Dou Gen drains Fire and kills parasites.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), p. 206. Source Exam Keys CMID 9015, question 1, and CMID 9053, question 1.
