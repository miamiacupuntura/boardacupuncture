Two Formulas Called Juān Bì Tāng 蠲痹汤

Two historical prescriptions share exactly the same name but target different Bi presentations.

Name versus reality

What it suggestsThe title “Remove Painful Obstruction Decoction” does not identify which source formula is meant.
RealityChéng Zhōng-Líng’s 1732 version treats general Wind-Cold-Damp Bi; an older Huáng Qí–Jiāng Huáng version emphasizes shoulder/arm pain in Qi-Blood deficiency.

How to answer

Decisive clueIf the ingredient list features Huáng Qí and Jiāng Huáng, choose the upper-limb deficiency version.
MemorySame title: check author, ingredients and body region.

Safety

AlertDo not dispense from title alone. New weakness, hot swollen joint with fever, trauma or suspected infection requires medical evaluation.
