Pattern comparison

Full Heat vs. Empty Heat

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueFull HeatEmpty Heat
Shared clueBoth may cause thirst, irritability, a red tongue, Heat sensations and a rapid pulse.
Fast distinctionStrong excess: high or continuous fever, intense thirst for cold drinks, red tongue with yellow coat and a forceful rapid pulse.Yin deficiency: afternoon fever, night sweating, five-center Heat, malar flush, peeled red tongue and a thin rapid pulse.

Comparative answer key

CompareFull HeatEmpty Heat
MechanismExcess pathogenic Heat blazes internally.Yin and fluids fail to restrain Yang.
Timing / strengthStrong and persistent, often worse in daytime.Low-grade or tidal, classically worse afternoon/night.
Tongue / pulseRed with dry yellow coat; full/forceful-rapid.Red with little or no coat; thin-rapid.
Formula examplesBai Hu Tang by Qi-level presentation.Zhi Bai Di Huang Wan or Qing Hao Bie Jia Tang by context.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersRedness and rapidity are shared. Coat and pulse strength reveal whether Heat is an excess pathogen or appears because Yin cannot cool.
Memory cueFull Heat has force and coat; Empty Heat has timing and no coat.
SafetyHigh persistent fever, confusion, stiff neck, breathing difficulty, severe dehydration or immunocompromise requires urgent medical evaluation.

Possible exam questions · Study prompts
