Historical substance comparison

Bubali Cornu (shui niu jiao) vs. Eretmochelydis Carapax (dai mao)

Conservation notice

Dai MaoThis traditional source material comes from an endangered sea-turtle species. It is included only to understand the historical exam comparison and is not presented as a clinical option.

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueShui Niu JiaoDai Mao
Shared historical actionsBoth enter Heart and Liver, cool Heat, resolve toxicity, sedate convulsions and alleviate spasms.
Fast distinctionBitter; focuses on Heart and Blood-level Heat, cooling Blood and dispersing stagnation.Sweet, salty and cold; historically described as more strongly extinguishing Wind and settling Liver convulsions.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersFor exam recognition, stronger Blood-level and Heart-Heat clearing indicates Shui Niu Jiao; the historical Wind and convulsion clue identifies Dai Mao.
Memory cueShui Niu Jiao cools Blood-level Heat; Dai Mao is only a historical Wind-convulsion comparison.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), pp. 1066–1067. Source Exam Key CMID 9071, questions 1 and 3.
