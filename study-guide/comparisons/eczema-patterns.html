Pathology or symptom comparison

Eczema Pattern Differentiation

Diagnostic orientation

Core ideaAcute eczema divides by whether Heat/redness or Dampness/oozing dominates; long-standing dry thick skin forms the deficiency branch.
Exam focusBright inflammation versus heavy oozing versus chronic dry lichenification

Comparative differentiation

Pattern or presentationDecisive cluesRepresentative formula/herbStrategy
Heat-predominant Damp-HeatAcute bright-red macules/papules/vesicles, severe itch, excoriation, ulceration and Heat signsLong Dan Xie Gan Tang + Er Miao SanClear Heat and drain Dampness.
Dampness-predominant Damp-HeatAcute poorly demarcated red lesions with blisters, heavy oozing, ulceration, chest fullness and greasy coatChu Shi Wei Ling TangStrengthen Spleen, drain Dampness and clear Heat.
Blood deficiency with Wind-DrynessChronic recurrent lichenification, rough scaling skin, intense itch, dizziness, fatigue and pale dry tongueSi Wu Xiao Feng YinNourish Blood, moisten Dryness and dispel Wind.

Study rule

How to distinguishBright red and hot means Heat leads; copious ooze and greasy coat means Damp leads; thick dry scaling after a long course means Blood-Dryness.
Memory cueBright-red—Heat; wet-oozing—Damp; thick-dry-chronic—Blood deficiency.
SafetyRapidly spreading redness, fever, pain, pus, honey crust, eye involvement or widespread blistering may indicate infection or a serious skin reaction and needs prompt care.
