Herb comparison

Taraxaci Herba (pu gong ying) vs. Patriniae Herba (bai jiang cao)

Study tip · Clues to help answer

CluePu Gong YingBai Jiang Cao
Shared cluesBoth cool Heat, resolve toxicity and reduce swollen abscesses; both are used for toxic sores and abscesses.
Fast distinctionQi-level herb focused on Liver and Stomach; stronger for dredging constraint and dispersing clumping.Enters both Qi and Blood levels; stronger for eliminating stasis and expelling pus, especially accumulated toxicity in Stomach and Intestines.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersBoth treat toxic swelling and abscesses, but Qi-level Liver-Stomach constraint points to Pu Gong Ying; Blood-level stasis, pus and intestinal accumulation point to Bai Jiang Cao.
Memory cuePu Gong Ying opens Qi-level clumping; Bai Jiang Cao moves Blood stasis and pus.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), p. 164. Source Exam Key CMID 9029, question 1.
