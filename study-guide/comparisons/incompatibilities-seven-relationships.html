The seven and their exam aliases

RelationshipCommon English labelsAnchor example
Dān Xíng Single effect; one medicinal acts alone.Rén Shēn alone in Dú Shēn Tāng.
Xiāng Xū Mutual necessity / mutual accentuation; similar actions reinforce.Shí Gāo + Zhī Mǔ.
Xiāng Shǐ Mutual employment / mutual enhancement; a different-action helper assists.Huáng Qí + Fú Líng for deficiency edema.
Xiāng Wèi Mutual fear / mutual counteraction; the toxic herb is tamed.Bàn Xià “fears” Shēng Jiāng.
Xiāng Shā Mutual killing / mutual suppression; the protector suppresses toxicity.Shēng Jiāng suppresses Bàn Xià toxicity.
Xiāng Wù Mutual aversion / mutual antagonism; one herb weakens another’s effect.Lái Fú Zǐ weakens the Qi-tonifying emphasis of Rén Shēn.
Xiāng Fǎn Mutual opposition / mutual incompatibility; the combination raises harm.The Shí Bā Fǎn groups.

Safety frame

These are historical classification terms and required exam knowledge. They do not replace exact species identification, processing, dose, medication reconciliation or current toxicology guidance.
