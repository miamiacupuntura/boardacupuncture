Herb comparison

Gu Sui Bu vs. Gou Ji

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueGu Sui BuGou Ji
Shared clueBoth tonify Liver and Kidneys and strengthen sinews and bones for weak or painful lower back and knees.
Fast distinctionMore strongly invigorates Blood, stops pain and directs downward.More strongly tonifies bones and spine and expels Wind-Dampness.

Comparative answer key

CompareGu Sui BuGou Ji
Botanical nameDrynariae Rhizoma.Cibotii Rhizoma.
CategoryHerbs that Tonify the Yang.Herbs that Tonify the Yang.
Properties in sourceBitter and warm.Sweet and warm.
Primary emphasisInvigorates Blood and stops pain more strongly.Tonifies bones and spine more strongly and expels Wind-Dampness.
Decisive extensionDirects downward to treat tinnitus or toothache due to floating Yang.Adds treatment of Wind-Damp painful obstruction.
Shared indicationLower-back or knee soreness and weakness of sinews and bones from Liver-Kidney insufficiency.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersBoth strengthen bones, but Blood movement with tinnitus or toothache selects Gu Sui Bu, while Wind-Dampness and spine tonification select Gou Ji.
Memory cueGu Sui Bu moves Blood downward; Gou Ji fortifies the spine.
