Formula comparison

Wu Zhu Yu Tang vs. Li Zhong Wan

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueWu Zhu Yu TangLi Zhong Wan
Shared clueBoth warm the Interior, support the middle burner and treat vomiting, abdominal discomfort, cold limbs and a slow or weak pulse.
Fast distinctionCold in Liver/Stomach drives Qi upward: severe vomiting, acid regurgitation and vertex headache.Spleen-Stomach deficiency-Cold fails to transform food and fluids: chronic diarrhea, poor appetite and abdominal pain.

Comparative answer key

CompareWu Zhu Yu TangLi Zhong Wan
Primary organsLiver and Stomach; may involve Kidney.Spleen and Stomach.
IngredientsRen Shen is shared; Wu Zhu Yu and Sheng Jiang distinguish this formula.Ren Shen is shared; Gan Jiang and Bai Zhu distinguish this formula.
Qi directionStrongly descends rebellious Qi.Warms and restores middle-burner transformation.
Decisive clueVertex headache with vomiting or acid regurgitation.Chronic loose stool with cold abdominal pain.
Exam shortcutCold rebellion rises.Cold deficiency weakens the center.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersBoth warm vomiting patterns, but Wu Zhu Yu Tang is chosen for upward Cold rebellion and Liver involvement; Li Zhong for deficient middle transport.
Memory cueWu Zhu Yu sends Cold rebellion down; Li Zhong warms the weak center.
SafetySevere headache with neurologic symptoms, blood in vomit/stool, dehydration or severe abdominal pain requires urgent evaluation.
