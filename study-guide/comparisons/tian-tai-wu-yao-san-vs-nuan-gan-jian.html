Formula comparison

Tian Tai Wu Yao San vs. Nuan Gan Jian

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueTian Tai Wu Yao SanNuan Gan Jian
Shared clueBoth warm the Liver channel, move Qi and relieve lower-abdominal, groin or testicular pain from Cold.
Fast distinctionMore excess and obstructive: Cold-Qi stagnation with severe shan-disorder pain.More deficient: Liver-Kidney Cold with weakness requiring warming plus nourishment.

Comparative answer key

CompareTian Tai Wu Yao SanNuan Gan Jian
Primary strategyWarm, disperse Cold and strongly move/downbear Qi.Warm and nourish Liver-Kidney while regulating Qi.
Decisive clueSevere fixed or radiating pain from excess Cold stagnation.Chronic pain with weakness, Cold and deficiency signs.
Formula characterMore dispersing and attacking.More warming and supplementing.
Exam shortcutCold obstruction.Cold deficiency.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersThe pain location overlaps. Constitution and formula direction—attack stagnation versus warm/nourish deficiency—make the distinction.
Memory cueTian Tai drives Cold-Qi out; Nuan Gan warms and supports the Liver.
SafetyClassical Tian Tai Wu Yao San preparation historically involves Ba Dou processing; it is not for home preparation or self-dosing. Acute testicular pain is an emergency.
SourceSource bank: FWB 9.1 series, Comparison Formulas to Regulate the Qi; corroborated with formula references.
