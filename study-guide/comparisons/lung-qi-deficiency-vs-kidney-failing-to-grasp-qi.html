Lung Qi Deficiency vs. Kidney Failing to Grasp Qi

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueLung Qi DeficiencyKidney Failing to Grasp Qi
Shared clueBoth can cause chronic shortness of breath, weak cough, fatigue and a weak pulse.
Fast distinctionUpper/source weakness: weak voice and cough, spontaneous sweating, recurrent colds and breathlessness with speech or mild exertion.Lower/root weakness: difficulty inhaling deeply, chronic wheezing worse with exertion, weak low back/knees and possible urinary or reproductive signs.

Comparative answer key

CompareLung Qi DeficiencyKidney Failing to Grasp Qi
Failed functionDiffuse/descend Lung Qi and secure Defensive Qi.Receive and anchor inhaled Lung Qi.
Breathing clueWeak respiration and voice overall.Inhalation is especially difficult or shallow.
PulseWeak, especially right cun.Deep-weak at chi positions.
Formula examplesBu Fei Tang or Yu Ping Feng San by presentation.Ren Shen Ge Jie San or Su Zi Jiang Qi Tang by Cold-Phlegm context.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersBreathlessness overlaps. Weak voice/sweating/recurrent infections localize to Lung; inspiratory difficulty plus low-back signs localizes to Kidney.
Memory cueLung Qi cannot send; Kidney Qi cannot receive.
SafetySevere or rapidly worsening breathlessness, blue lips, chest pain, confusion, low oxygen or inability to speak full sentences is an emergency.

Possible exam questions · Study prompts

  • Which struggles mainly to inhale?
