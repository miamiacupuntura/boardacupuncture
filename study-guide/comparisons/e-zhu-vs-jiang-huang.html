Herb comparison

E Zhu vs. Jiang Huang

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueE ZhuJiang Huang
Shared clueBoth are warm, acrid and bitter herbs that break Blood stasis and move Qi to stop pain.
Fast distinctionStronger for breaking Blood stasis and reducing internal accumulations.Moves through both interior and exterior, reaching Wind painful obstruction.

Comparative answer key

CompareE ZhuJiang Huang
Botanical nameCurcumae Rhizoma.Curcumae longae Rhizoma.
CategoryHerbs that Regulate the Blood · Herbs that Invigorate the Blood.Herbs that Regulate the Blood · Herbs that Invigorate the Blood.
Primary emphasisBreaks Blood stasis more forcefully and reduces internal accumulations.Moves through the interior and exterior and treats Wind painful obstruction.
Formula cluesTiao Ying Yin; Mu Xiang Liu Qi Yin; San Leng He Shang Tang.Juan Bi Tang.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersBoth move Blood and Qi, but an internal mass points to E Zhu, while painful obstruction involving the exterior points to Jiang Huang.
Memory cueE Zhu breaks accumulations; Jiang Huang reaches obstruction.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), p. 613. Source Exam Key CMID 8945, questions 3–4 and 7.
