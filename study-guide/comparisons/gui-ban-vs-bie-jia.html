Herb comparison

Gui Ban vs. Bie Jia

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueGui BanBie Jia
Shared clueBoth are shells that enrich Yin, subdue Yang and soften areas of hardness.
Fast distinctionKidney emphasis; sweeter and stronger at enriching Yin to anchor Yang.Liver emphasis; stronger at cooling Heat and softening areas of hardness.

Comparative answer key

CompareGui BanBie Jia
Botanical or medicinal nameTestudinis Plastrum.Trionycis Carapax.
CategoryHerbs that Tonify the Yin.Herbs that Tonify the Yin.
Primary channelPrimarily enters the Kidneys.Primarily enters the Liver.
Flavor and nature clueSweet; its sweetness supports a superior ability to enrich Yin and anchor Yang.Salty and cold, but not sweet.
Stronger actionEnriches Yin to anchor Yang and unblocks the Conception Vessel.Cools Heat and softens areas of hardness.
Gynecological clueContinuous uterine bleeding.Abdominal masses and clumping around the flanks.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersBoth nourish Yin and anchor Yang, but Kidney-Conception Vessel deficiency and uterine bleeding favor Gui Ban, while Liver Heat with masses or flank clumping favors Bie Jia.
Memory cueGui Ban: Kidney and Conception; Bie Jia: Liver and clumping.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), p. 842. Source Exam Key CMID 8962, question 2.
