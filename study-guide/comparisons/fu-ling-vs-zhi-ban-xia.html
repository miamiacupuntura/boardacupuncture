Herb comparison

Fu Ling vs. Zhi Ban Xia

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueFu LingZhi Ban Xia
Shared clueBoth expel Phlegm-Dampness and are paired to address its root and branch.
Fast distinctionTreats the root by strengthening the Spleen and draining Dampness.Treats the branch by drying and transforming visible Phlegm.

Comparative answer key

CompareFu LingZhi Ban Xia
Medicinal namePoria.Pinelliae Rhizoma preparatum.
CategoryHerbs that Drain Dampness.Herbs that Transform Phlegm and Stop Coughing · Warm Herbs that Transform Cold-Phlegm.
Primary actionStrengthens the Spleen, expels Dampness and transforms Phlegm.Dries and transforms Phlegm.
Pattern roleCorrects the Spleen-Damp root that generates Phlegm.Drains the branch manifestation—the visible Phlegm.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersThe pair treats the same Phlegm-Damp pattern from two directions: Fu Ling changes the terrain while Zhi Ban Xia removes the manifestation.
Memory cueFu Ling fixes the root; Zhi Ban Xia clears the branch.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), pp. 415–416. Source Exam Key CMID 8954, questions 1 and 4–5.
