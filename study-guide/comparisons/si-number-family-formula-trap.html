Formula trap 9 of 22

The Sì “Four” Formula Family

Four can tonify Qi, nourish Blood, treat cold limbs, bind dawn diarrhea or clear hot swollen legs.

Memory rule

AnchorJūn Zǐ = Qi; Wù = Blood; Shén = dawn diarrhea; Miào = hot legs.

Exam and safety

Exam keyThe noun after Sì—not the number—defines the strategy.
SafetySì Shén is warm/astringent while Sì Miào is clearing/draining; confusing them reverses treatment direction.
