Herb comparison

Ophiopogonis Radix (mai men dong) vs. Asparagi Radix (tian men dong)

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueMai Men DongTian Men Dong
Shared clueBoth nourish Lung Yin, moisten Dryness and stop cough; they also treat injured-Yin thirst and constipation from Intestinal Dryness.
Fast distinctionSlightly cold, less cloying and able to eliminate irritability.Very cold, more strongly nourishes Yin, clears Heat, moistens Dryness and enriches Kidney Yin.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersThe shared Lung-Dryness pattern can obscure relative intensity: Mai Men Dong is gentler and less cloying, while Tian Men Dong is colder, richer and extends to Kidney Yin.
Memory cueMai Men Dong is milder and calms irritability; Tian Men Dong is colder and reaches Kidney Yin.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), p. 827. Source Exam Key CMID 9012, question 2, and CMID 9079, question 2.
