Pathology or symptom comparison

Goiter Pattern Differentiation

Diagnostic orientation

Core ideaThe palpable thyroid enlargement is the pathology; emotional/Phlegm signs versus empty-Heat signs distinguish the historical TCM presentations.
Exam focusNeck mass with constraint and sticky coat versus hypermetabolic empty-Heat signs

Comparative differentiation

Pattern or presentationDecisive cluesRepresentative formula/herbStrategy
Qi stagnation, Blood stasis and congealed PhlegmNeck enlargement with depression/irritability, chest-hypochondriac oppression, insomnia, dark tongue and sticky white coatHai Zao Yu Hu TangRegulate Qi, move Blood, transform Phlegm and soften the mass.
Yin deficiency with empty FireGoiter with weight loss, hunger, dry mouth, palpitations, sweating, tremor, insomnia and red scant-coated tongueZhi Bai Di Huang WanNourish Yin, downbear deficient Fire and address the mass.

Study rule

How to distinguishConstraint plus dark-sticky signs indicates Qi-Blood-Phlegm; weight loss, tremor, palpitations and empty Heat indicates Yin deficiency with Fire.
Memory cueDark and sticky—Qi/Blood/Phlegm; thin, sweaty and trembling—Yin-Fire.
SafetyEvery new thyroid/neck mass needs examination and thyroid testing; rapid growth, hoarseness, trouble swallowing/breathing or hard fixed nodes needs urgent assessment. Classical Hai Zao Yu Hu Tang contains the Hai Zao–Gan Cao incompatibility.
