Herb comparison

Gu Ya vs. Mai Ya

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueGu YaMai Ya
Shared clueBoth are sprouted grains that reduce Food Stagnation, strengthen digestion and are especially useful for grain-based meals.
Decisive clueGentler and slower; supports the Spleen and is preferred for weak, older or pediatric patients.Stronger for wheat, rice and fruit stagnation; also courses constrained Liver Qi and stops lactation.

Comparative answer key

CompareGu YaMai Ya
Medicinal nameOryzae Fructus germinatus.Hordei Fructus germinatus.
Shared categoryHerbs that Relieve Food Stagnation.
Relative food-digestion index40 in Yang figure 9.1.60 in Yang figure 9.1.
Best Board distinctionGentle digestion plus Spleen support; poor appetite with deficiency.Food stagnation plus Liver-Qi constraint or the need to stop lactation.
Preparation clueRaw Gu Ya is better at harmonizing and opening the Stomach.Raw Mai Ya courses Liver Qi; dry-fried Mai Ya is more focused on digestion and stopping lactation.
CautionUse according to pattern; its gentle nature does not replace diagnosis.Avoid when continued lactation is desired; use carefully during breastfeeding.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersIf the stem gives only food stagnation, Mai Ya is stronger. If it emphasizes deficiency, age or gentleness, choose Gu Ya. Liver-Qi constraint or lactation clearly points to Mai Ya.
Memory cueGu Ya is gentle; Mai Ya moves more—and makes milk stop.
SourceY. Yang, Chinese Herbal Medicines: Comparisons and Characteristics, 2nd ed., chapter 9, question 3 and figure 9.1; Bensky et al., Chinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, 3rd ed.
