Pathology or symptom comparison

Wasting-Thirst Pattern Differentiation

Diagnostic orientation

Core ideaThe classic three-burner map distinguishes predominant thirst, hunger or urination; biomedical glucose testing remains essential.
Exam focusThirst, hunger and urination by burner

Comparative differentiation

Pattern or presentationDecisive cluesRepresentative formula/herbStrategy
Upper Burner · Lung Heat injures fluidsExcessive thirst and drinking with dry mouth; urination and hunger may accompanyXiao Ke FangClear Lung Heat and generate fluids.
Middle Burner · Stomach FireExcessive hunger/eating, emaciation, thirst and dry stoolYu Nu JianClear Stomach Fire and nourish Yin.
Lower Burner · Kidney Yin depletionFrequent copious turbid urine, thirst, weak back, dizziness and red cheeksLiu Wei Di Huang WanNourish Kidney Yin and secure Kidney.

Study rule

How to distinguishThirst leads Upper Burner, hunger leads Middle Burner, and urination leads Lower Burner—but overlap is common.
Memory cueUpper drinks; Middle eats; Lower urinates.
SafetyThis framework does not diagnose or replace diabetes care. Confusion, vomiting, deep breathing, severe dehydration or very high/low glucose requires urgent care.
