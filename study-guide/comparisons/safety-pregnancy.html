Safety 6 of 12

Pregnancy & Lactation

Screen uterine stimulation, Blood movement, purgation, toxicity and infant exposure before prescribing.

Core rule

RulePregnancy is not a setting for self-treatment; benefit, trimester, dose, processing and alternatives require qualified review.

Major risk groups

Group / stepWhat to knowSafe decision
Strong Blood breakersSān Léng, É Zhú, Shuǐ Zhì and Tǔ Biē Chóng may disturb pregnancy.Generally avoid unless a specialist determines an exceptional indication.
Drastic purgativesBā Dòu, Gān Suì, Dà Jǐ, Yuán Huā and Qiān Niú Zǐ may cause severe cramping and fluid loss.Contraindicated or avoided during pregnancy.
Penetrating/opening agentsShè Xiāng and similarly forceful movers may stimulate uterine activity.Avoid and verify wildlife/legal status.
Toxic medicinalsAconites, Mǎ Qián Zǐ, Léi Gōng Téng and toxic minerals risk maternal and fetal harm.Do not use outside specialist protocols; several are contraindicated.
Strong downward moversDà Huáng, Máng Xiāo and Zhǐ Shí require case-specific caution.A common herb is not automatically safe at any dose.

Pregnancy and lactation workflow

Group / stepWhat to knowSafe decision
Before treatmentConfirm gestational age, prior loss, bleeding, contractions, placental problems and all medications.Document rationale, alternatives and coordination with obstetric care.
LactationConsider transfer into milk, infant prematurity, toxicity, sedation and effects on milk supply.Avoid high-risk products and observe feeding, alertness, rash and stool.

When to act immediately

EmergencyHeavy bleeding, severe or one-sided pain, fainting, contractions, fluid leakage, reduced fetal movement or breathing difficulty requires urgent obstetric evaluation.

Educational use: this library does not replace medical evaluation, poison control, local regulation or pharmacist consultation.
