Cold Invading the Stomach vs. Stomach Deficient and Cold

Separate sudden excess Cold pain from chronic deficient Cold relieved by food and pressure.

Fast differentiation clues

ClueCold Invading the StomachStomach Deficient and Cold
SharedBoth cause epigastric pain, cold sensation, preference for warmth and a deep slow pulse.
Fast distinctionExcess invasion: sudden severe pain, vomiting and a tight forceful pulse; pressure may aggravate.Deficient Cold: chronic dull pain relieved by eating, warmth and pressure with fatigue and cold limbs.

Clinical table

CompareCold Invading the StomachStomach Deficient and Cold
Onset / forceSudden, acute and excess.Gradual, chronic and deficient.
Formula associationLiang Fu Wan.Huang Qi Jian Zhong Tang or Xiao Jian Zhong Tang.

Exam and safety

Why it mattersTiming, pain force and response to pressure distinguish acute excess from chronic deficiency.
Memory cueInvading Cold strikes; deficient Cold aches and wants support.
SafetySudden severe abdominal pain, rigid abdomen, repeated vomiting, fainting or blood requires emergency evaluation.
