Sheng Mai San vs. Tian Wang Bu Xin Dan

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueSheng Mai SanTian Wang Bu Xin Dan
Shared clueBoth support Heart Yin and fluids and may treat palpitations, thirst, fatigue and a thin or weak pulse.
Fast distinctionQi and Yin collapse from Heat or chronic illness: sweating, shortness of breath, thirst and weak pulse.Chronic Heart-Kidney Yin and Blood deficiency: insomnia, anxiety, poor memory, dry mouth and night sweats.

Comparative answer key

CompareSheng Mai SanTian Wang Bu Xin Dan
Primary targetLung-Heart Qi and Yin with loss of fluids.Heart-Kidney Yin/Blood with deficient Fire disturbing Shen.
Primary actionAugments Qi, generates fluids and restrains leakage.Nourishes Yin and Blood, clears deficient Heat and calms Spirit.
Decisive clueProfuse sweating with short breath and weak pulse.Insomnia and palpitations with red scant-coated tongue.
Exam shortcutPreserve the pulse and fluids.Nourish the Heart to settle Shen.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersBoth address Yin/fluid deficiency, but Sheng Mai San is leakage and functional collapse; Tian Wang is chronic Spirit disturbance from Heart-Kidney deficiency.
Memory cueSheng Mai saves Qi and fluids; Tian Wang feeds Yin and calms Shen.
SafetyClassical Tian Wang Bu Xin Dan may list Zhu Sha; use only cinnabar-free, quality-controlled modern products. Collapse, chest pain or severe dehydration requires urgent care.
