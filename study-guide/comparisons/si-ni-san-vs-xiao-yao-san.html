Formula comparison

Si Ni San vs. Xiao Yao San

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueSi Ni SanXiao Yao San
Shared clueBoth spread Liver Qi, harmonize Liver and Spleen, and share Chai Hu, Bai Shao and Gan Cao.
Fast distinctionConstraint blocks Yang from reaching extremities: cold fingers/toes, abdominal pain and a wiry pulse.Constraint occurs with deficiency: emotional/PMS signs, fatigue, poor appetite and a wiry-weak pulse.

Comparative answer key

CompareSi Ni SanXiao Yao San
Pattern characterMore constrained/excess.More deficient and chronic.
Distinct actionUnbinds constrained Yang and regulates the pivot.Nourishes Blood and strengthens Spleen while moving Liver Qi.
Decisive clueCold extremities with a warm trunk.Mood and menstrual symptoms with digestive weakness.
Exam shortcutQi cannot reach the limbs.Liver constraint drains Spleen and Blood.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersShared herbs and Liver-Spleen language make them easy to confuse. Cold extremities signal Si Ni San; deficiency and menstrual/emotional context signal Xiao Yao San.
Memory cueSi Ni frees trapped Yang; Xiao Yao frees Liver while rebuilding.
SafetyNew severe abdominal pain, fainting or cold extremities with systemic collapse requires urgent medical assessment.
