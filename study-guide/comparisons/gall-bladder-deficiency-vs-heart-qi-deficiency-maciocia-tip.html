Batch 9 · Gall Bladder · Tip 7 of 10

Gall Bladder vs. Heart Qi Deficiency

Separate timidity and indecision from weak Heart function with palpitations and exertional fatigue.

Fast differentiation clues

ClueGall Bladder DeficiencyHeart Qi Deficiency
SharedBoth may cause uneasiness, low confidence, fatigue and a weak pulse.
Fast distinctionGall Bladder: timidity, indecision, easy fright and frequent sighing dominate.Heart Qi: palpitations, exertional fatigue, shortness of breath and spontaneous sweating dominate.

Clinical table

CompareGall Bladder DeficiencyHeart Qi Deficiency
Primary domainDecision, courage and startle response.Heart function, circulation and exertional capacity.
Formula associationWen Dan Tang or An Shen Ding Zhi Wan.Yang Xin Tang in the supplied table.

Exam and safety

Why it mattersPalpitations and exertional signs identify Heart Qi; timidity/indecision identify the Gall Bladder label.
Memory cueGall Bladder hesitates; Heart Qi tires and palpitates.
SafetyPalpitations with chest pain, fainting, severe breathlessness or irregular rhythm requires urgent cardiac assessment.
