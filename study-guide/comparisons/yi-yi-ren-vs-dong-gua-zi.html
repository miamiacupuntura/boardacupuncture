Herb comparison

Coicis Semen (yi yi ren) vs. Benincasae Semen (dong gua zi)

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueYi Yi RenDong Gua Zi
Shared cluesBoth clear Heat and expel pus and may be combined for Lung or Large Intestine abscesses.
Fast distinctionStrengthens the Spleen and drains water and Dampness for diarrhea, painful obstruction or edema.Slippery and moves the Large Intestine for Damp-Heat constipation or incomplete bowel movements.

Comparative answer key

CompareYi Yi Ren (Coicis Semen)Dong Gua Zi (Benincasae Semen)
Shared categoryHerbs that Drain Dampness.
Shared actionsClear Heat and expel pus.
Shared indicationAbscesses in the Lungs or Large Intestine.
Primary emphasisStrengthens the Spleen and facilitates drainage of water and Dampness.Slippery and facilitates movement through the Large Intestine.
Distinctive indicationsDampness with diarrhea, painful obstruction disorder or edema.Large Intestine Damp-Heat with constipation, irregular bowel movements or an unfinished sensation.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersBoth clear Heat, expel pus and treat abscesses, so the Spleen-water versus Large-Intestine-movement distinction selects the answer.
Common confusionDiarrhea, edema or painful obstruction favors Yi Yi Ren; constipation or incomplete stools from Large Intestine Damp-Heat favors Dong Gua Zi.
Source question formatQuestion 1 is Matching.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), p. 296. Source Exam Key CMID 8935.
