Appendix B pair · 21 of 51

Jié Gěng + Gān Cǎo

桔梗 + 甘草

One picture, one job

PictureThe sore-throat duo.
Combined jobBenefit the throat, diffuse Lung Qi and expel phlegm or pus from the upper burner.
CategoryBenefit the Throat · Expel Pus

How the pair works

HerbRole within the pair
Jié GěngCarries the action upward, diffuses Lung Qi and expels phlegm and pus.
Gān CǎoSoothes the throat, clears toxicity and moderates Jié Gěng.

Exam focus and safety

CluesSore throat, hoarseness or cough with thick sputum; classically also Lung abscess.
Signature formulaJié Gěng Tāng.
TrapJié Gěng opens and lifts toward the Lung and throat; it is not primarily a descending cough herb.
PreparationUse the processed form of Gān Cǎo specified by the formula source; do not assume honey-fried in every case.
SafetyLarge or prolonged Gān Cǎo doses can raise blood pressure and lower potassium. Fever, chest pain, foul sputum, blood or breathing difficulty requires medical evaluation.
