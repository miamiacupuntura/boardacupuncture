Herb comparison

Ligustici Rhizoma (gao ben) vs. Chuanxiong Rhizoma (chuan xiong)

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueGao BenChuan Xiong
Shared cluesBoth treat headache and are described as similar in nature, but their channel reach differs.
Fast distinctionBladder and Governing Vessel; does not reach Liver/Gallbladder.Liver and Gallbladder; reaches head/eyes but not the vertex.

Comparative answer key

CompareGao Ben (Ligustici Rhizoma)Chuan Xiong (Chuanxiong Rhizoma)
CategoryWarm, Acrid Herbs that Release the Exterior.Herb that Invigorates the Blood.
Channel focusBladder and Governing Vessel.Liver and Gallbladder.
ReachFollows and unblocks its channels above and below, but does not reach Liver/Gallbladder.Moves to the head and eyes, but the source says it does not reach the vertex.
Shared indicationHeadache.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersThe source emphasizes that headache treatment depends on guiding herbs reaching the proper channels.
Common confusionBladder/Governing Vessel selects Gao Ben; Liver/Gallbladder and head/eyes selects Chuan Xiong.
Source question formatQuestion 1 is Matching.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), p. 23. Source Exam Key CMID 8921.
