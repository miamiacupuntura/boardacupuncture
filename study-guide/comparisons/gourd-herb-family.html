Family 4 of 20

Gourd 瓜 — Guā Lóu Goes Low

Fruit moves hot Phlegm down through chest and bowel; root restores fluids.

One picture, one rule

RuleWhole does both halves; peel opens chest; seed moistens bowel; root makes fluids.

The part decides the job

MedicinalPart / identityJob
Quán Guā Lóu 全瓜蒌Whole fruitClears Heat, transforms Phlegm, unbinds chest and moistens Intestines.
Guā Lóu Pí 瓜蒌皮PeelFocuses on Lung Heat, Phlegm and chest constraint.
Guā Lóu Rén 瓜蒌仁SeedMoistens Lung and Intestines for dry cough/constipation.
Tiān Huā Fěn 天花粉Root starchClears Heat, generates fluids, reduces swelling and expels pus.

Exam and safety

Exam keyChest = peel; bowel dryness = seed; wasting-thirst = root.
SafetyAll Trichosanthes parts are traditionally incompatible with aconite. Tiān Huā Fěn is contraindicated in pregnancy; seeds can worsen loose stools.
