Herb comparison

Ge Gen vs. Ma Huang

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueGe GenMa Huang
Shared clueBoth lightly lift, bring out and disperse Exterior pathogens.
Fast distinctionCool, Yang Brightness and Spleen; acts on muscles and flesh.Warm, Greater Yang and Lung; acts on skin and hair.

Comparative answer key

CompareGe GenMa Huang
Botanical namePuerariae Radix.Ephedrae Herba.
CategoryCool, Acrid Herbs that Release the Exterior.Warm, Acrid Herbs that Release the Exterior.
Channel levelYang Brightness.Greater Yang.
Organ focus in sourceEnters the Spleen rather than the Lung.Enters the Lung rather than the Spleen.
Tissue correspondenceSpleen governs muscles and flesh; the root is light and extended like muscles around the body.Lung governs skin and hair; the herb is hollow and resembles the Lungs.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersThe shared lifting and dispersing action hides opposite thermal categories and different channel-tissue targets.
Memory cueGe Gen—Spleen and muscles; Ma Huang—Lung and skin.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), p. 72. Source Exam Key CMID 8955, questions 5–6.
