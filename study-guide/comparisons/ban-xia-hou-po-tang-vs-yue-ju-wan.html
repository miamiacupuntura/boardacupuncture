Ban Xia Hou Po Tang vs. Yue Ju Wan

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueBan Xia Hou Po TangYue Ju Wan
Shared clueBoth move constrained Qi and can treat emotional constraint with chest or epigastric discomfort.
Fast distinctionQi and Phlegm bind in the throat: “plum-pit Qi,” globus sensation, cough or nausea.Six stagnations: Qi, Blood, Phlegm, Fire, Food and Dampness with broad digestive/emotional constraint.

Comparative answer key

CompareBan Xia Hou Po TangYue Ju Wan
Primary mechanismQi stagnation generates Phlegm that lodges in throat/chest.Qi stagnation drives several interlocking forms of constraint.
Decisive clueSensation of something stuck in the throat that cannot be swallowed or coughed out.Distention, pain, depression and digestive stagnation without a defining throat sensation.
Therapeutic emphasisMove Qi, transform Phlegm and direct Qi downward.Move Qi and relieve the six constraints.
Exam shortcutOne knot in the throat.Many knots throughout digestion and emotion.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersBoth regulate Qi, but Ban Xia Hou Po Tang has a precise throat/Phlegm hallmark; Yue Ju Wan is broader and multi-stagnation.
Memory cueBan Xia Hou Po clears the plum pit; Yue Ju opens six restraints.
SafetyPersistent or progressive difficulty swallowing, weight loss, choking or a neck mass requires medical evaluation.
