Misleading name 4 of 6

Yù Nǚ Jiān 玉女煎

“Jade Woman” is not a gynecology formula—it treats Stomach Heat with Kidney Yin deficiency.

Name versus reality

What it suggestsThe poetic name can misdirect the learner toward women’s health.
RealityShí Gāo clears blazing Stomach Heat while Shú Dì, Zhī Mǔ and Mài Dōng support Yin; Niú Xī guides downward.

How to answer

Decisive clueToothache, bleeding/swollen gums, thirst and hunger with underlying Yin deficiency.
MemoryJade Woman cools the face and teeth while protecting deep Kidney water.

Safety

AlertDental abscess, facial swelling, fever, difficulty swallowing or spreading infection requires urgent dental/medical care.
