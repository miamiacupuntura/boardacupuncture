Herb comparison

Rosae rugosae Flos (mei gui hua) vs. Albiziae Flos (he huan hua)

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueMei Gui HuaHe Huan Hua
Shared cluesBoth regulate Qi, relieve constraint and invigorate the collaterals. They may be combined for Liver constraint and Qi stagnation with oppression in the chest or epigastrium.
Fast distinctionMore aromatic and stronger at regulating Qi and dispersing Blood stagnation.More strongly calms the Spirit and nourishes the Heart and Spleen.

Comparative answer key

CompareMei Gui Hua (Rosae rugosae Flos)He Huan Hua (Albiziae Flos)
CategoryHerbs that Regulate the Qi.Herbs that Nourish the Heart and Calm the Spirit.
NatureSweet, slightly bitter and warm.Sweet and neutral.
Primary emphasisRegulates Qi and moves at the Blood level to disperse Blood stagnation.Moves at the Qi level while calming the Spirit and nourishing the Heart and Spleen.
Relative strengthMore aromatic, with stronger Qi- and Blood-invigorating action.More calming than Mei Gui Hua.
Distinctive indicationsLiver-Qi constraint and stagnation in the collaterals.Insomnia, poor memory and irritability accompanying constraint.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersBoth release constraint, but Mei Gui Hua emphasizes aromatic Qi regulation with Blood movement, whereas He Huan Hua emphasizes calming the Spirit.
Memory cueMei Gui Hua moves; He Huan Hua harmonizes the Spirit.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), pp. 543 and 938. Source Exam Key CMID 9018, questions 1–5 and 12.
