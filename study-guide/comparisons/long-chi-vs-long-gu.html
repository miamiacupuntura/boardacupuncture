Herb comparison

Long Chi vs. Long Gu

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueLong ChiLong Gu
Shared clueThe two fossil substances are very similar and both anchor, settle and calm the Spirit.
Fast distinctionStronger Spirit-calming and heavier suppression of mania, childhood convulsions, palpitations and insomnia.Better at binding lower-burner Essential Qi, restraining Dampness and assisting the healing of sores.

Comparative answer key

CompareLong ChiLong Gu
Medicinal nameFossilia Dentis Mastodi.Fossilia Ossis Mastodi.
Material clueFossil teeth.Fossil bone.
CategorySubstances that Anchor, Settle, and Calm the Spirit.Substances that Anchor, Settle, and Calm the Spirit.
Spirit emphasisSomewhat stronger at calming the Spirit and weighing down severe agitation.Calms the Spirit but is not assigned the same relative strength here.
Binding emphasisLess able to bind the Essential Qi of the lower burner.Stronger at binding lower-burner Essential Qi.
External and Dampness clueDoes not restrain Dampness or assist healing of sores.Restrains Dampness and assists healing of sores.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersThe two names and actions are highly similar. Severe Spirit disturbance favors Long Chi; lower-burner binding or sore-healing favors Long Gu.
Memory cueLong Chi weighs down the agitated Spirit; Long Gu anchors and binds more broadly.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), p. 911. Source Exam Key CMID 9000, question 2.
