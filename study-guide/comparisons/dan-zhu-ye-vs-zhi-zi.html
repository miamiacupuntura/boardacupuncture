Herb comparison

Lophateri Herba (dan zhu ye) vs. Gardeniae Fructus (zhi zi)

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueDan Zhu YeZhi Zi
Shared cluesBoth cool Heat, eliminate irritability, facilitate urination and enter the Qi level.
Fast distinctionMilder, acrid and limited to the Qi level; vents Heat from the Heart and Lungs.More obviously bitter and cold; enters Qi and Blood levels and drains constrained Heat throughout the Triple Burner.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersBoth clear Heat, relieve irritability and promote urination, so strength, level and organ system provide the distinction.
Common confusionMild Qi-level Heart-Lung Heat favors Dan Zhu Ye; constrained Triple-Burner Heat, Blood Heat or toxicity favors Zhi Zi.
Source question formatsQuestions 1–4 are Matching.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), p. 99. Source Exam Key CMID 8926.
