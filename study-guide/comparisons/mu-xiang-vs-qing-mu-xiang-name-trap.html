Trap 2 of 22

Mù Xiāng 木香 vs. Qīng Mù Xiāng 青木香

Adding Qīng changes a standard Qi-regulating herb into an obsolete Aristolochia drug.

Memory rule

AnchorPlain Mù Xiāng moves Qi; Qīng Mù Xiāng carries the Aristolochia red flag.

Correct identity

NameIdentityJob
Mù Xiāng 木香Aucklandiae Radix / costus rootMoves Qi, relieves abdominal distention and supports digestion.
Qīng Mù Xiāng 青木香Aristolochiae RadixObsolete drug associated with aristolochic acid exposure.

Exam and safety

Exam keyFor Spleen-Stomach Qi stagnation, the expected herb is Mù Xiāng—not Qīng Mù Xiāng.
Safety alertQīng Mù Xiāng is nephrotoxic and carcinogenic and should not be used. Verify the full name and botanical identity.
