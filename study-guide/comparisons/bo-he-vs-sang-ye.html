Herb comparison

Menthae haplocalycis Herba (bo he) vs. Mori Folium (sang ye)

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueBo HeSang Ye
Shared cluesBoth are cool exterior-releasing herbs used for Wind-Heat.
Decisive clueSore throat, rash or Liver Qi stagnation.Dry cough from Lung heat, dryness or bleeding.

Comparative answer key

CompareBo He (Field Mint)Sang Ye (Mulberry Leaf)
Shared categoryCool, Acrid Herbs that Release the Exterior.
Relative emphasisStronger for venting rashes and relieving Liver Qi stagnation.Stronger for dry cough due to Lung heat and for dryness.
Distinctive clueSore throat with rash or constrained Qi.Stopping bleeding in an appropriate Wind-Heat or dryness presentation.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersBoth can treat Wind-Heat, but the Lung-dryness and bleeding clues belong to Sang Ye.
Common confusionBo He enters the Lung channel, but the source comparison still identifies Sang Ye as better for dry cough from Lung heat.
How it may appearDry cough or bleeding favors Sang Ye. Rash venting or Liver Qi stagnation favors Bo He.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), p. 51. Source Exam Key CMID 9277.
