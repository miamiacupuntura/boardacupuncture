Herb comparison

Rhei Radix et Rhizoma (da huang) vs. Natrii Sulfas (mang xiao)

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueDa HuangMang Xiao
Shared cluesBoth are cold, downward-draining purgatives that treat constipation from clumped Heat in the Large Intestine, and they are commonly used together.
Fast distinctionBitter; also enters the Blood level to cool Blood, resolve toxicity and break static accumulation.Salty; clears the Large Intestine and softens hardness.

Comparative answer key

CompareDa Huang (Rhei Radix et Rhizoma)Mang Xiao (Natrii Sulfas)
Shared categoryDownward-Draining Herbs · Purgatives.
Nature and flavorBitter and cold.Salty and cold.
Primary distinctionDrains Qi-level Heat and also enters the Blood level.Clears the Large Intestine and softens areas of hardness.
Additional actionsCools Blood, resolves toxicity and breaks up static accumulations.The source comparison emphasizes softening hardness in constipation.
Distinctive indicationsBlood-Heat bleeding, red swollen painful eyes and gynecological abdominal masses.Constipation with hardness from clumped Large Intestine Heat.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersBoth purge Heat and may appear together, but Da Huang has broader Qi- and Blood-level actions while Mang Xiao specializes in softening hardness.
Common confusionBitter, Blood-cooling and stasis-breaking points to Da Huang; salty and hardness-softening points to Mang Xiao.
Memory cueDa Huang drains and breaks; Mang Xiao softens hardness.
Source question formatsQuestions 5 and 6 are Matching.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), p. 241. Source Exam Key CMID 8939.
