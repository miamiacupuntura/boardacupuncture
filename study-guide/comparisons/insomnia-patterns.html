Pathology or symptom comparison

Insomnia Pattern Differentiation

Diagnostic orientation

Core ideaThe accompanying mental, digestive, Heat and deficiency signs are more diagnostic than sleeplessness alone.
Exam focusHeat, Phlegm, deficiency and emotional clues

Comparative differentiation

Pattern or presentationDecisive cluesRepresentative formula/herbStrategy
Liver constraint transforming FireIrritability, headache, red eyes, bitter taste, hypochondriac painLong Dan Xie Gan TangDrain Liver Fire and calm Spirit.
Phlegm-Heat disturbing SpiritHeavy head, nausea, epigastric fullness, bitter taste and yellow greasy coatWen Dan TangClear Heat, transform Phlegm and harmonize Stomach.
Yin deficiency with empty FirePalpitations, tinnitus, weak back, five-center Heat and red peeled tongueHuang Lian E Jiao TangNourish Kidney Yin and descend Heart Fire.
Heart-Spleen deficiencyLight sleep, many dreams, poor memory, fatigue, poor appetite and pale complexionGui Pi TangSupplement Qi and Blood of Heart and Spleen.
Heart-Gallbladder Qi deficiencyTimidity, easy fright, palpitations, short breath and copious clear urineAn Shen Ding Zhi WanSupplement Qi, settle fright and calm Spirit.

Study rule

How to distinguishAnger and bitter taste indicate Liver Fire; greasy coating and nausea indicate Phlegm; five-center Heat indicates Yin deficiency; fatigue/appetite indicate Heart-Spleen; fright indicates Heart-Gallbladder.
Memory cueAngry—Liver; greasy—Phlegm; hot/dry—Yin; tired/pale—Heart-Spleen; timid—Heart-Gallbladder.
SafetyInsomnia with suicidal thoughts, mania, psychosis, dangerous sleepiness, breathing pauses or abrupt neurologic change requires urgent medical care.
