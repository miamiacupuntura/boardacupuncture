Zhen Wu Tang

Spleen and Kidney Yang deficiency with internal water retention.

High-yield summary

CategoryWarm Yang · Promote Water Metabolism
PatternSpleen and Kidney Yang deficiency with internal water retention.
ActionWarms Yang, promotes urination, transforms water and stabilizes the interior.
Decisive clueEdema, scant urine, heavy painful limbs, abdominal pain/diarrhea and dizziness with a pale swollen wet tongue.

Ingredients

Fu Ling, Bai Shao, Sheng Jiang, Bai Zhu and Zhi Fu Zi.

Commercial preparations and lineages may vary; always verify the source being used.

Differentiation and memory

Do not confuseWu Ling San addresses Taiyang water accumulation with less profound deficiency; Zhen Wu Tang treats internal water from Spleen–Kidney Yang deficiency.
Memory cueTrue Warrior Fu Zi restores Yang while Fu Ling and Bai Zhu move retained water.

Safety

Fu Zi requires correct processing. Sudden edema, low urine, breathlessness, chest pain, confusion or fainting requires urgent care.
