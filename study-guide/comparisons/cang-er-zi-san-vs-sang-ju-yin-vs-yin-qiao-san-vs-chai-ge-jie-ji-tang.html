Study guide · Exam focus

Why it matters“Wind-Heat” is too broad. The question usually gives the organ or tissue target: nose, Lung, throat or muscle layer.
Memory cueCang Er = nose; Sang Ju = cough; Yin Qiao = throat; Chai Ge = muscles.
SafetyCang Er Zi is toxic in excessive doses. Facial swelling, visual change, breathing or swallowing difficulty, confusion or persistent high fever requires urgent assessment.
