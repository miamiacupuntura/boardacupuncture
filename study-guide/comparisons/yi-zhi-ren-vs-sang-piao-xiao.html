Herb comparison

Alpiniae oxyphyllae Fructus (yi zhi ren) vs. Mantidis Ootheca (sang piao xiao)

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueYi Zhi RenSang Piao Xiao
Shared cluesBoth warm and tonify the lower burner, contain urine and secure Essence. They treat deficiency Cold with enuresis, urinary frequency or spermatorrhea.
Fast distinctionAlso warms the Spleen, contains saliva and stops diarrhea.Primarily secures deficiency Cold in the lower burner.

Comparative answer key

CompareYi Zhi RenSang Piao Xiao
Botanical nameAlpiniae oxyphyllae Fructus.Mantidis Ootheca.
CategoryHerbs that Tonify the Yang.Herbs that Stabilize and Bind.
Shared actionsWarm and tonify the lower burner, contain urine and secure Essence.
Shared indicationsEnuresis, urinary frequency and spermatorrhea from deficiency Cold in the lower burner.
Distinctive emphasisWarms the Spleen, contains saliva and stops diarrhea.Primarily treats lower-burner deficiency Cold by securing Essence and urine.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersBoth address Kidney-Yang weakness with urinary or Essence leakage. Spleen, saliva or diarrhea clues indicate Yi Zhi Ren; a direct lower-burner containment clue indicates Sang Piao Xiao.
Memory cueYi Zhi Ren adds Spleen, saliva and diarrhea control; Sang Piao Xiao secures the lower burner.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), p. 901. Source Exam Keys CMID 9046, question 2, and CMID 9111, question 4.
