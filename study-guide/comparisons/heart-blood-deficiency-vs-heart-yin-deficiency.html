Pattern comparison

Heart Blood Deficiency vs. Heart Yin Deficiency

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueHeart Blood DeficiencyHeart Yin Deficiency
Shared clueBoth fail to nourish the Heart and Spirit, causing palpitations, insomnia, anxiety, poor memory and a thin pulse.
Fast distinctionPale deficiency without Heat: dizziness, pale lips/tongue and scanty menses may coexist.Blood deficiency plus dryness and empty Heat: night sweat, dry mouth, five-center heat and red peeled tongue.

Comparative answer key

CompareHeart Blood DeficiencyHeart Yin Deficiency
DepthBlood nourishment is insufficient.Yin fluids are deficient, often including Blood deficiency.
Thermal signsNo significant Heat.Empty Heat, especially at night.
Tongue / pulsePale; thin or choppy.Red, little coat; thin-rapid.
Formula examplesGui Pi Tang; Si Wu Tang when appropriate.Tian Wang Bu Xin Dan.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersHeart-Yin deficiency resembles Heart-Blood deficiency with added dryness and Heat.
Memory cueBlood deficiency is pale; Yin deficiency is red and hot.
SafetyChest pain, fainting, severe or irregular palpitations requires biomedical assessment.

Possible exam questions · Study prompts

  • Which has a pale tongue?
  • Which has night sweating and empty Heat?
  • What Spirit symptoms are shared?
