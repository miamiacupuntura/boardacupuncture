Tíng Lì Zǐ + Dà Zǎo

葶苈子 + 大枣

One picture, one job

PictureA harsh drain with a bodyguard.
Combined jobDrain water and Phlegm from the Lung, calm wheezing and promote urination while protecting the Middle.
CategoryDrain Lung Fluid · Calm Wheezing

How the pair works

HerbRole within the pair
Tíng Lì ZǐForcefully drains Lung fluid and directs Lung Qi downward.
Dà ZǎoProtects Spleen/Stomach Qi from the harsh draining action.

Exam focus and safety

CluesWheezing, inability to lie flat and facial or chest fluid accumulation.
Signature formulaTíng Lì Dà Zǎo Xiè Fèi Tāng.
TrapDà Zǎo is protective here; it does not cancel the forceful draining nature of Tíng Lì Zǐ.
PreparationTíng Lì Zǐ is commonly crushed before decoction; dose and duration require professional control.
SafetyBreathlessness when lying flat, swollen legs, chest pain or blue lips may indicate heart failure or another emergency and requires urgent evaluation.
