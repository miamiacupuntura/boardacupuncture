Pattern comparison

Qi Stagnation vs. Blood Stasis

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueQi StagnationBlood Stasis
Shared clueBoth obstruct free flow and can cause pain, menstrual irregularity, masses, irritability and a wiry pulse.
Fast distinctionQi obstruction: distending, moving or fluctuating discomfort strongly linked to emotion; tongue may be normal.Blood obstruction: fixed, stabbing pain worse at night, dark clots, palpable masses and a purple or spotted tongue.

Comparative answer key

CompareQi StagnationBlood Stasis
Nature of obstructionFunctional movement of Qi is impaired.Blood circulation is physically fixed or congealed.
PainDistending, wandering and variable.Sharp, stabbing, fixed and often worse at night.
Tongue / pulseNormal or slightly dusky; wiry.Purple/dusky with spots; choppy or wiry.
Formula examplesChai Hu Shu Gan San or Yue Ju Wan.Xue Fu Zhu Yu Tang or other Zhu Yu formula by location.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersQi stagnation often precedes Blood stasis, so they can coexist. Fixity, clots and purple signs show that obstruction has reached Blood.
Memory cueQi moves and distends; Blood fixes and stabs.
SafetyNew chest pain, unilateral swelling, neurologic deficit, pregnancy pain, heavy bleeding or a new mass requires urgent biomedical evaluation.

Possible exam questions · Study prompts

  • Which pain moves with emotion?
  • Which worsens at night?
  • Which produces a purple spotted tongue?
