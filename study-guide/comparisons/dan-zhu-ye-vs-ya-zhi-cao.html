Herb comparison

Lophateri Herba (dan zhu ye) vs. Commelinae Herba (ya zhi cao)

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueDan Zhu YeYa Zhi Cao
Shared cluesBoth cool Heat and promote urination and belong to Herbs that Clear Heat and Drain Fire.
Fast distinctionMore prolonged cooling and urination-promoting effects.Slightly stronger cooling and urination-promoting action, but less prolonged.

Comparative answer key

CompareDan Zhu Ye (Lophateri Herba)Ya Zhi Cao (Commelinae Herba)

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersThe herbs share the same two exam actions, so the distinction is entirely strength versus duration.
Memory cueDan Zhu Ye lasts longer; Ya Zhi Cao acts slightly stronger.
Source question formatQuestion 5 is Matching.
