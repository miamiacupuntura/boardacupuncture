Herb-identity comparison

Guang Huo Xiang vs. Tu Huo Xiang

Exam trap · Two plants share the Huo Xiang name

QuestionWhich Huo Xiang name identifies Patchouli, and which identifies Korean mint?
Correct answerGuang/Guan Huo Xiang = Pogostemon cablin, Patchouli. Huo Xiang/Tu Huo Xiang = Agastache rugosa, Korean mint.
Do not confuseThe source may use “Huo Xiang” generally when the exact plant is unclear, although it describes Pogostemonis Herba as the standard product at the time of publication.

Study tip · Clues to help answer

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it matters“Huo Xiang” may refer to either plant. The exam distinction depends on Patchouli/Pogostemon versus Korean mint/Agastache and on the source's aroma-quality ranking.
Memory cueGuang = southern Patchouli; Tu = Korean mint.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), p. 473. Source Exam Key CMID 8984, questions 4–5.
