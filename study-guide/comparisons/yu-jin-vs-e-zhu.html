Herb comparison

Curcumae Radix (yu jin) vs. Curcumae Rhizoma (e zhu)

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueYu JinE Zhu
Shared clueBoth are pungent and bitter Curcuma herbs that move Blood, alleviate pain, move Qi and resolve constraint.
Fast distinctionCold: clears Heart fire, cools Blood and benefits the Gallbladder.Warm and stronger: breaks Blood stasis, reduces accumulations and treats food retention.

Comparative answer key

CompareYu JinE Zhu
Botanical nameCurcumae Radix.Curcumae Rhizoma.
Nature and channelsCold; Liver, Heart and Lung.Warm; Liver and Spleen.
Decisive actionActivates Qi, resolves constraint, clears Heart fire, cools Blood and normalizes the Gallbladder.More strongly invigorates Blood, breaks Blood stasis and reduces internal accumulation.
Clinical clueDamp-Heat jaundice or cholelithiasis; the source also identifies antidepressant and cholagogue use.Food retention with Blood accumulation; the source emphasizes antitumor use and avoidance during menstruation and pregnancy.
Current formula boundaryNeither herb is a chief or co-chief ingredient in the current 63-formula Board roster.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersThe shared Curcuma name and Blood-Qi actions make these easy to confuse; temperature and clinical direction separate them.
Memory cueYu Jin cools and clears; E Zhu warms and breaks.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), pp. 608, 613. Source Exam Keys CMID 8945, questions 1–2 and 5–6, and CMID 9114, questions 5–6 and 8–9.

Possible exam questions · Study prompts

  • Which herb is stronger for Blood stasis and internal accumulations?
