Formula comparison

Ma Huang Tang vs. Gui Zhi Tang

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueMa Huang TangGui Zhi Tang
Shared clueBoth release Exterior Wind-Cold and may present with fever, chills or aversion to cold, headache, body aches and a floating pulse.
Fast distinctionExterior excess: closed pores, no sweating, stronger chills and wheezing.Exterior deficiency: spontaneous sweating and aversion to wind from disharmony of the Nutritive and Defensive levels.

Comparative answer key

CompareMa Huang TangGui Zhi Tang
Primary actionStrongly induces sweating, releases the Exterior and disseminates Lung Qi.Releases the Exterior, harmonizes Ying and Wei, and regulates the muscles.
Decisive sweat clueAbsence of sweating.Spontaneous sweating.
Respiratory clueCough or wheezing is prominent.Respiratory signs are not the defining clue.
PulseFloating and tight.Floating and moderate or frail.
Exam choiceChoose when the pathogen is strong and the Exterior is tightly constrained.Choose when Exterior deficiency and disharmony permit leakage of sweat.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersThe central distinction is not simply “strong versus mild.” It is a closed Exterior excess pattern versus a deficient, disharmonized Exterior that cannot secure the pores.
Memory cueMa Huang Tang closes sweat out; Gui Zhi Tang lets sweat leak out.
SafetyThese formulas are not interchangeable. Ma Huang Tang contains Ma Huang and requires cardiovascular and medication screening and qualified professional supervision.
SourceSource bank: FWB 1.2, Comparison Formulas to Release the Exterior; corroborated with the Classic Formulas profiles and Chinese Herbal Medicine: Formulas & Strategies, 2nd ed.
