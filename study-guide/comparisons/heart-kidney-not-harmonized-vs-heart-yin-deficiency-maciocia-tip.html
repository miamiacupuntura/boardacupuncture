Heart–Kidney Not Harmonized vs. Heart Yin Deficiency

Separate a two-organ failure of communication from a predominantly Heart-centered Yin deficiency.

Fast differentiation clues

ClueHeart–Kidney Not HarmonizedHeart Yin Deficiency
SharedBoth cause palpitations, insomnia, restlessness, night sweating and a red peeled tongue with a thin-rapid pulse.
Fast distinctionTwo-organ axis: Heart signs plus tinnitus, weak low back/knees, nocturnal emission or a deep midline tongue crack.Heart-centered: palpitations, anxiety, poor memory and red tip predominate without clear Kidney signs.

Clinical table

CompareHeart–Kidney Not HarmonizedHeart Yin Deficiency
MechanismKidney Yin fails to anchor Heart Fire; Heart and Kidney lose communication.Heart Yin alone is insufficient to nourish and cool the Spirit.
Formula associationTian Wang Bu Xin Dan or Huang Lian E Jiao Tang according to Heat and deficiency.Tian Wang Bu Xin Dan by presentation.

Exam and safety

Why it mattersKidney signs and a Heart-to-Kidney axis—not insomnia alone—justify the combined diagnosis.
Memory cueHeart Yin stays upstairs; disharmony adds the Kidney downstairs.
SafetyPersistent insomnia with severe mood change, chest symptoms, fainting or suicidal thoughts needs prompt professional evaluation.

Review prompts

  • Which adds tinnitus and weak low back?
  • Which centers on the Heart?
  • Why is the tongue crack supportive but not sufficient alone?
