Herb comparison

Chrysanthemi indici Flos (ye ju hua) vs. Chrysanthemi Flos (ju hua)

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueYe Ju HuaJu Hua
Shared clueBoth are chrysanthemum flowers with overlapping Heat-clearing actions.
Fast distinctionWild, yellow flower that more powerfully drains Fire and resolves toxicity.True chrysanthemum, identified as the white variety in this source; disperses Wind-Heat and emphasizes the Liver and eyes.

Comparative answer key

CompareYe Ju HuaJu Hua
Botanical nameChrysanthemi indici Flos.Chrysanthemi Flos.
Identity in the sourceWild chrysanthemum flower; yellow variety.True chrysanthemum flower; white variety.
CategoryHerbs that Clear Heat and Resolve Toxicity.Cool, Acrid Herbs that Release the Exterior.
Primary emphasisMore powerfully drains Fire and resolves toxicity.Disperses Wind, clears Heat, clears the Liver, brightens the eyes and pacifies Liver Wind.
Clinical clueToxic swollen sores and boils, often with other cooling toxicity-resolving herbs.Wind-Heat, Liver Heat, eye symptoms or Liver-Wind patterns.
Prescription clueMust be specified as Ye Ju Hua.According to the source, an unspecified “Ju Hua” prescription is filled with the white variety.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersThe similar names hide different priorities: Ye Ju Hua is the stronger Heat-toxin herb, whereas Ju Hua is the Exterior, Liver and eye herb.
Memory cueYe Ju Hua attacks toxic swelling; Ju Hua clears Wind-Heat and brightens the eyes.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), p. 167. Source Exam Keys CMID 8995, questions 2–3, and CMID 9119, questions 1–2.
