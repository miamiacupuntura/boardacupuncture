Liver Qi Stagnation vs. Liver Blood Stasis

Use pain quality, menstrual blood and tongue color to recognize progression from Qi constraint to fixed Blood stasis.

Fast differentiation clues

ClueLiver Qi StagnationLiver Blood Stasis
SharedBoth cause rib-side, abdominal or menstrual pain and may worsen with emotional stress.
Fast distinctionDistending, moving and variable pain; menstrual timing may be irregular but color is not necessarily dark.Fixed stabbing pain, dark clotted menses, palpable masses and a purple tongue.

Clinical table

CompareLiver Qi StagnationLiver Blood Stasis
ObstructionQi movement is impaired.Blood movement is materially fixed.
Tongue / pulseNormal/dusky; wiry.Purple or purple spots; choppy or wiry-choppy.
Formula associationChai Hu Shu Gan San.Ge Xia Zhu Yu Tang or Shi Xiao San by location and presentation.

Exam and safety

Why it mattersLong-standing Qi stagnation may lead to Blood stasis, but fixed pain, clots and purple signs mark the transition.
Memory cueQi distends; static Blood stabs and clots.
SafetySevere pelvic pain, pregnancy, heavy bleeding, fainting or an abdominal mass requires biomedical assessment.

Review prompts

  • Which pain moves?
  • Which has clots?
