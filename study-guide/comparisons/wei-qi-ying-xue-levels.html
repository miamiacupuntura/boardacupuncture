Wei, Qi, Ying and Xue Levels

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueWei LevelQi LevelYing LevelXue Level
Shared clueAll describe depth and severity in warm-febrile disease; progression inward generally means stronger Heat and greater fluid, Spirit or Blood injury.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersFever is shared. Chills, thirst/sweat, night Spirit change, then bleeding/convulsion mark progressively deeper levels.
Memory cueWei defends; Qi blazes; Ying disturbs Shen; Xue bleeds and stirs Wind.
SafetyThis framework never replaces urgent infectious evaluation. High fever with confusion, stiff neck, breathing difficulty, non-blanching rash, bleeding or seizure is an emergency. Rhinoceros horn is prohibited/obsolete.
