Pathology or symptom comparison

Whooping Cough Stage Comparison

Diagnostic orientation

Core ideaThe stage pattern follows the clinical course, but suspected pertussis needs testing, antibiotics, isolation guidance and public-health reporting.
Exam focusCatarrhal onset, paroxysmal whoop and recovery deficiency

Comparative differentiation

Pattern or presentationDecisive cluesRepresentative formula/herbStrategy
Initial Wind-ColdCough with thin white foamy phlegm, fever, sneezing and clear runny noseXing Su SanDispel Wind-Cold and diffuse Lung.
Initial Wind-HeatCough with difficult thick yellow phlegm, chills/fever and red sore throatSang Ju YinDispel Wind-Heat and diffuse Lung.
Spasmodic coughing stageRepeated prolonged coughing fits with inspiratory whoop, post-tussive vomiting, worse at night, Heat and possible bleedingSang Bai Pi TangClear Lung Heat and transform Phlegm.
Recovery · Lung-Yin deficiencyLess frequent but dry cough, scant phlegm, dry thirsty throat and red peeled tongueSha Shen Mai Dong TangNourish Yin and moisten Lung.
Recovery · Spleen-Stomach Qi deficiencyWeak cough with thin phlegm, dyspnea, fatigue, poor appetite and spontaneous sweatRen Shen Wu Wei Zi TangStrengthen Spleen-Stomach Qi.

Study rule

How to distinguishClear/foamy is early Cold; yellow/sticky early Heat; whoop/vomit is spasmodic stage; dry recovery is Yin; weak-digestive recovery is Qi.
Memory cueClear—Cold; yellow—Heat; whoop—spasm; dry recovery—Yin; weak recovery—Qi.
SafetyPertussis is contagious and reportable in many jurisdictions. Infants, pregnancy and household contacts need prompt medical management. Apnea, blue color, breathing difficulty, dehydration or exhaustion is an emergency.
