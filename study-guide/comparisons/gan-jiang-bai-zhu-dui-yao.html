Gān Jiāng + Bái Zhú

干姜 + 白术

One picture, one job

PictureDry heat for a cold, soggy Middle.
Combined jobWarm the Middle, strengthen the Spleen and dry Cold-Dampness.
CategoryWarm the Middle · Dry Dampness

How the pair works

HerbRole within the pair
Gān JiāngWarms Spleen/Stomach Yang and disperses interior Cold.
Bái ZhúStrengthens Spleen Qi and dries Dampness that produces loose stool.

Exam focus and safety

CluesCold abdominal pain, loose stools, vomiting, poor appetite and cold limbs from Middle deficiency-Cold.
Signature formulaLǐ Zhōng Wán.
TrapThe pair is warm and dry; it is wrong for Yin deficiency, dry stool or Damp-Heat.
PreparationDistinguish dried Gān Jiāng from fresh Shēng Jiāng; they are not interchangeable in this warming role.
SafetyPersistent vomiting, blood in stool, severe abdominal pain or dehydration requires medical assessment.
