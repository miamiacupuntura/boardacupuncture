Pattern comparison

Liver Yin Deficiency vs. Kidney Yin Deficiency

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueLiver Yin DeficiencyKidney Yin Deficiency
Shared clueBoth cause dryness, dizziness, tinnitus, night sweating, five-center Heat, red peeled tongue and a thin rapid pulse; they often coexist.
Fast distinctionLiver emphasis: dry blurred eyes, night blindness, cramps, tremor, brittle nails and scanty menstruation.Kidney emphasis: weak low back/knees, stronger tinnitus or hearing loss, reproductive/Essence weakness and developmental or bone signs.

Comparative answer key

CompareLiver Yin DeficiencyKidney Yin Deficiency
Nourished tissuesEyes, sinews, nails and menstrual Blood.Bones, marrow, brain, ears and reproductive Essence.
Decisive locationVisual and sinew symptoms.Low back/knees, ears and Essence symptoms.
Common progressionMay allow Liver Yang to rise.Can fail to nourish Liver Yin and underlie the combined pattern.
Formula examplesYi Guan Jian by presentation.Liu Wei Di Huang Wan or Zuo Gui Wan by depth.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersEmpty Heat is shared and cannot localize the diagnosis. The affected sensory organ and tissue system identify Liver versus Kidney emphasis.
Memory cueLiver Yin moistens eyes and sinews; Kidney Yin fills ears, bones and Essence.
SafetySudden vision/hearing loss, severe headache, neurologic deficit, unexplained bleeding or significant weight loss requires medical assessment.

Possible exam questions · Study prompts

  • Which pattern emphasizes dry eyes?
  • Which emphasizes weak low back and hearing?
  • Why do the two often coexist?
