Herb comparison

Cynanchi stauntonii Rhizoma (bai qian) vs. Eriobotryae Folium (pi pa ye)

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueBai QianPi Pa Ye
Shared clueBoth effectively direct rebellious Lung Qi downward.
Fast distinctionWarming and parching; favors externally contracted pathogenic Qi binding the Lungs with chest fullness and audible Phlegm.Cooling and moistening; favors dry cough or difficult-to-expectorate Phlegm and also stops nausea.

Comparative answer key

CompareBai QianPi Pa Ye
Botanical nameCynanchi stauntonii Rhizoma.Eriobotryae Folium.
CategoryWarm Herbs that Transform Cold-Phlegm.Herbs that Relieve Coughing and Wheezing.
Temperature and moistureWarming and parching.Cooling and moistening.
Best presentationExternally contracted pathogenic Qi binding the Lungs, with chest fullness and the sound of Phlegm in the throat.Dry cough or Phlegm that is difficult to expectorate.
Additional distinctionThe comparison emphasizes Lung treatment.Also stops nausea, which Bai Qian does not do.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersBoth descend rebellious Lung Qi, but temperature, moisture and accompanying signs decide the answer: a warming-drying approach for chest fullness with audible Phlegm points to Bai Qian; a cooling-moistening approach for dry cough with nausea points to Pi Pa Ye.
Memory cueBai Qian warms and dries bound Phlegm; Pi Pa Ye cools, moistens and settles nausea.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), p. 447. Source Exam Key CMID 9028, questions 1–3.
