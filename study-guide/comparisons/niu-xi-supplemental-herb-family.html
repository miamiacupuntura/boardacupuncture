Supplemental family 1 of 6

Two Niú Xī — Two Plants, One Downward Direction

Both guide downward, but Huái Niú Xī supplements while Chuān Niú Xī moves Blood more strongly.

Memory rule

AnchorHuái supports the knees; Chuān clears the channels.

Identity decides the job

MedicinalIdentityJob
Huái Niú Xī 怀牛膝Achyranthes bidentata rootTonifies Liver-Kidney, strengthens sinews/bones and guides Blood/Fire downward.
Chuān Niú Xī 川牛膝Cyathula officinalis rootMore strongly invigorates Blood, unblocks channels and guides downward.

Exam and safety

Exam keyChronic weak low back/knees favors Huái; stronger stasis/amenorrhea favors Chuān.
SafetyBoth move Blood and guide downward and are contraindicated in pregnancy; use caution with heavy bleeding or anticoagulants.
