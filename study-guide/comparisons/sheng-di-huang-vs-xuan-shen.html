Herb comparison

Rehmanniae Radix (sheng di huang) vs. Scrophulariae Radix (xuan shen)

Study tip · Clues to help answer

ClueSheng Di HuangXuan Shen
Shared cluesBoth clear Heat, cool the Blood, enrich Yin and generate fluids.
Fast distinctionStronger at cooling and enriching the Blood and stopping bleeding.Directs Fire downward, resolves toxicity, disperses clumps, cools the Lungs and benefits the throat.

Comparative answer key

CompareSheng Di HuangXuan Shen
Medicinal nameRehmanniae Radix.Scrophulariae Radix.
Common categoryHerbs that Clear Heat and Cool the Blood.
Shared actionsClear Heat, cool Blood, enrich Yin and generate fluids.
Level and organ focusBlood level; Heart, Liver and Kidneys.Yin level; Lungs and Kidneys.
Primary emphasisStronger Blood cooling, Blood enrichment and bleeding control.Downward Fire drainage, toxicity resolution, clump dispersal, Lung cooling and throat benefit.
Decisive indicationHeat entering the Blood and causing bleeding.Heat entering the Blood and producing toxic rashes or skin lesions.

Study guide · Exam focus

Why it mattersBleeding and a Blood-level Heart-Liver-Kidney focus identify Sheng Di Huang; toxicity, rashes, clumps, Lung or throat signs identify Xuan Shen.
Memory cueSheng Di Huang cools Blood and stops bleeding; Xuan Shen drains Fire and resolves toxicity.
SourceChinese Herbal Medicine: Materia Medica, Third Edition, Dan Bensky, Steven Clavey, Erich Stöger and Andrew Gamble (2004), p. 125. Source Exam Keys CMID 9060, questions 3–4, and CMID 9106, questions 1–2.
