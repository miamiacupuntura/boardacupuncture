## Important findings

1. The existing multiple-choice bank is strongly concentrated in formulas.
2. Individual-herb and Duì Yào material is extensive, but it is currently study content rather than reviewed multiple-choice questions.
3. The Safety Domain has rich source material but almost no independently focused keyed questions.
4. Scope and alias content has reliable rosters but no dedicated keyed questions.
5. A mixed 100-question exam must remain blocked until every Domain has enough approved questions; one Domain must never silently borrow another Domain’s allocation.
