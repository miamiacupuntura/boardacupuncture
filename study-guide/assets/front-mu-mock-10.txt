1. Which point is the Front-Mu point of the Lung in a teaching case of cough and chest discomfort, rather than its Luo-Connecting, Xi-Cleft or He-Sea point?
A) LU1 Zhongfu +++++
B) LU7 Lieque
C) LU6 Kongzui
D) LU5 Chize
Feedback: LU1 Zhongfu is the Front-Mu point of the Lung and has traditional respiratory and chest indications. Why are the other options incorrect? LU7 is Lung Luo-Connecting and the Ren vessel confluent point, LU6 is Lung Xi-Cleft, and LU5 is Lung He-Sea and Water. These points can share respiratory indications but do not have the Front-Mu classification requested.

2. A question asks for the point that is both the Front-Mu point of the Spleen and the Hui-Meeting point of zang. Which point satisfies both classifications?
A) LIV14 Qimen
B) LIV13 Zhangmen +++++
C) SP3 Taibai
D) REN12 Zhongwan
Feedback: LIV13 Zhangmen is the Spleen Front-Mu point and Hui-Meeting point of zang; its carrying channel is Liver, while its Front-Mu correspondence is Spleen. Why are the other options incorrect? LIV14 is Liver Front-Mu, SP3 is Spleen Shu-Stream and Yuan-Source, and REN12 is Stomach Front-Mu and Hui-Meeting of fu. Identify both requested classifications rather than choosing any point associated with Spleen function.

3. Which point is the Front-Mu point of the Gall Bladder, located in the seventh intercostal space on the chest, directly inferior to the nipple region?
A) LIV14 Qimen
B) GB25 Jingmen
C) GB24 Riyue +++++
D) LIV13 Zhangmen
Feedback: GB24 Riyue is Gall Bladder Front-Mu and is located in the seventh intercostal space, four cun lateral to the midline. Why are the other options incorrect? LIV14 is Liver Front-Mu in the sixth intercostal space, GB25 is Kidney Front-Mu at the free end of the twelfth rib, and LIV13 is Spleen Front-Mu at the free end of the eleventh rib. The intercostal and rib landmarks distinguish nearby point regions.

4. A practitioner intends to combine the Kidney Front-Mu and Back-Shu points. Which pair gives these two classifications in that order?
A) KID3 Taixi and UB23 Shenshu
B) GB24 Riyue and UB19 Danshu
C) LIV13 Zhangmen and UB20 Pishu
D) GB25 Jingmen and UB23 Shenshu +++++
Feedback: GB25 Jingmen is Kidney Front-Mu and UB23 Shenshu is Kidney Back-Shu. Why are the other options incorrect? KID3 is Kidney Shu-Stream and Yuan-Source, not Front-Mu; GB24 with UB19 is the Gall Bladder Mu-Shu pair; LIV13 with UB20 is the Spleen Mu-Shu pair. A Front-Mu point may lie on a channel different from the organ it corresponds to.

5. Which point is both the Front-Mu point of the Stomach and the Hui-Meeting point of fu in a teaching case involving epigastric discomfort?
A) REN12 Zhongwan +++++
B) ST25 Tianshu
C) REN14 Juque
D) REN5 Shimen
Feedback: REN12 Zhongwan is Stomach Front-Mu and Hui-Meeting of fu, with traditional actions that harmonize the Stomach and middle jiao. Why are the other options incorrect? ST25 is Large Intestine Front-Mu, REN14 is Heart Front-Mu, and REN5 is San Jiao Front-Mu. Shared abdominal location does not make these correspondences interchangeable.

6. Which point is the Front-Mu point of the Large Intestine, located two cun lateral to the umbilicus, in a question concerning regulation of bowel function?
A) REN12 Zhongwan
B) ST25 Tianshu +++++
C) REN4 Guanyuan
D) REN3 Zhongji
Feedback: ST25 Tianshu is Large Intestine Front-Mu and lies two cun lateral to the umbilicus. Why are the other options incorrect? REN12 is Stomach Front-Mu, REN4 is Small Intestine Front-Mu, and REN3 is Urinary Bladder Front-Mu. The organ and location cues both identify ST25; being on the Stomach channel does not change its Large Intestine correspondence.

7. Which set correctly lists the Front-Mu points of Heart, Pericardium and San Jiao, in that order?
A) REN17, REN14, REN5
B) REN14, REN17, REN4
C) REN14, REN17, REN5 +++++
D) REN12, REN17, REN3
Feedback: REN14 Juque, REN17 Shanzhong and REN5 Shimen are the Front-Mu points of Heart, Pericardium and San Jiao respectively. Why are the other options incorrect? “REN17, REN14, REN5” reverses the Heart and Pericardium correspondences. “REN14, REN17, REN4” substitutes Small Intestine Front-Mu REN4 for San Jiao Front-Mu REN5. “REN12, REN17, REN3” substitutes Stomach Front-Mu REN12 for Heart and Bladder Front-Mu REN3 for San Jiao. Match each organ to its point before evaluating the set.

8. Which pair correctly identifies the Front-Mu points of Small Intestine and Urinary Bladder, in that order?
A) REN3 Zhongji and REN4 Guanyuan
B) REN5 Shimen and ST25 Tianshu
C) REN4 Guanyuan and REN5 Shimen
D) REN4 Guanyuan and REN3 Zhongji +++++
Feedback: REN4 Guanyuan is Small Intestine Front-Mu and REN3 Zhongji is Urinary Bladder Front-Mu. Why are the other options incorrect? “REN3 Zhongji and REN4 Guanyuan” reverses the Small Intestine and Bladder correspondences. “REN5 Shimen and ST25 Tianshu” gives the Front-Mu points of San Jiao and Large Intestine, not Small Intestine and Bladder. “REN4 Guanyuan and REN5 Shimen” correctly identifies Small Intestine but substitutes San Jiao Front-Mu REN5 for Bladder Front-Mu REN3. Nearby lower-abdominal points have different Front-Mu relationships.

9. Which point is the Front-Mu point of the Liver in a question describing the sixth intercostal space and hypochondriac discomfort?
A) LIV14 Qimen +++++
B) LIV13 Zhangmen
C) GB24 Riyue
D) GB25 Jingmen
Feedback: LIV14 Qimen is Liver Front-Mu and lies in the sixth intercostal space, four cun lateral to the midline. Why are the other options incorrect? LIV13 is Spleen Front-Mu despite being on the Liver channel, GB24 is Gall Bladder Front-Mu in the seventh intercostal space, and GB25 is Kidney Front-Mu near the twelfth-rib tip. Use both the organ correspondence and landmark to distinguish the alternatives.

10. Which statement correctly describes GB25 Jingmen in a question combining Front-Mu classification, point location and needling safety?
A) It is Kidney Front-Mu on the Kidney channel, so deep perpendicular needling is always appropriate.
B) It is Kidney Front-Mu on the Gall Bladder channel near the free end of the twelfth rib; deep needling can risk injury to the Kidney. +++++
C) It is Gall Bladder Front-Mu in the seventh intercostal space.
D) It is Spleen Front-Mu at the free end of the eleventh rib.
Feedback: GB25 Jingmen is Kidney Front-Mu on the Gall Bladder channel, anterior and inferior to the free end of the twelfth rib; Deadman cautions against deep needling because of possible Kidney injury, especially in thin patients. Why are the other options incorrect? “It is Kidney Front-Mu on the Kidney channel, so deep perpendicular needling is always appropriate” assigns the wrong carrying channel and makes an unsafe unconditional depth claim. “It is Gall Bladder Front-Mu in the seventh intercostal space” describes GB24 Riyue, not GB25. “It is Spleen Front-Mu at the free end of the eleventh rib” describes LIV13 Zhangmen, not GB25. Nearby anatomy supports safety assessment but does not independently prove the traditional organ correspondence.
