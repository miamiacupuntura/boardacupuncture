1. Which sequence correctly follows the Five Shu categories from the distal extremities toward the elbows or knees?
A) Jing-Well → Ying-Spring → Shu-Stream → Jing-River → He-Sea +++++
B) Jing-Well → Shu-Stream → Ying-Spring → Jing-River → He-Sea
C) He-Sea → Jing-River → Shu-Stream → Ying-Spring → Jing-Well
D) Jing-Well → Ying-Spring → Yuan-Source → Jing-River → He-Sea
Feedback: Jing-Well, Ying-Spring, Shu-Stream, Jing-River and He-Sea form the distal-to-proximal Five Shu sequence. Why are the other options incorrect? Jing-Well → Shu-Stream → Ying-Spring → Jing-River → He-Sea — Ying-Spring precedes Shu-Stream, so this sequence reverses them. He-Sea → Jing-River → Shu-Stream → Ying-Spring → Jing-Well — This is the opposite, proximal-to-distal direction. Jing-Well → Ying-Spring → Yuan-Source → Jing-River → He-Sea — Yuan-Source is a different classification and does not replace Shu-Stream in the Five Shu sequence.

2. Which set gives the Five Elements of the Yin Five Shu points in Jing-Well, Ying-Spring, Shu-Stream, Jing-River and He-Sea order?
A) Metal, Water, Wood, Fire, Earth
B) Wood, Fire, Earth, Metal, Water +++++
C) Wood, Water, Earth, Metal, Fire
D) Fire, Earth, Metal, Water, Wood
Feedback: The Yin sequence is Wood, Fire, Earth, Metal and Water. Why are the other options incorrect? Metal, Water, Wood, Fire, Earth — This is the Yang sequence, not the Yin sequence. Wood, Water, Earth, Metal, Fire — Ying-Spring is Fire and He-Sea is Water on Yin channels; those two elements are misplaced. Fire, Earth, Metal, Water, Wood — This begins with Fire rather than Jing-Well’s Wood and shifts the entire sequence.

3. Which set gives the Five Elements of the Yang Five Shu points in Jing-Well, Ying-Spring, Shu-Stream, Jing-River and He-Sea order?
A) Wood, Fire, Earth, Metal, Water
B) Metal, Fire, Wood, Water, Earth
C) Metal, Water, Wood, Fire, Earth +++++
D) Water, Wood, Fire, Earth, Metal
Feedback: The Yang sequence is Metal, Water, Wood, Fire and Earth. Why are the other options incorrect? Wood, Fire, Earth, Metal, Water — This is the Yin sequence. Metal, Fire, Wood, Water, Earth — Yang Ying-Spring is Water and Jing-River is Fire, so those elements are reversed. Water, Wood, Fire, Earth, Metal — This starts at Ying-Spring’s Water rather than Jing-Well’s Metal.
