8. Which statement correctly describes the Yuan-Source relationship to Shu-Stream?
A) They coincide on all twelve primary channels.
B) They are separate only on Yin channels.
C) Yuan-Source is always the fourth numbered point.
D) They coincide on Yin channels; Yang channels have a separate Yuan-Source point. +++++
Feedback: The six Yin Shu-Stream points are also Yuan-Source; the six Yang channels have distinct Yuan-Source points. Why are the other options incorrect? They coincide on all twelve primary channels. — Yang channels have separate points, so the overlap is not universal. They are separate only on Yin channels. — This reverses the Yin and Yang relationship. Yuan-Source is always the fourth numbered point. — Point numbering is not category order; Stomach Yuan-Source is ST42, for example.

9. Which pair follows the Yuan-Luo rule using the Lung Yuan-Source point and the Luo-Connecting point of its paired Large Intestine channel?
A) LU9 Taiyuan and LI6 Pianli +++++
B) LU7 Lieque and LI4 Hegu
C) LU9 Taiyuan and LI4 Hegu
D) LU6 Kongzui and LI6 Pianli
Feedback: LU9 is Lung Yuan-Source and LI6 is Large Intestine Luo-Connecting, forming the requested paired-channel Yuan-Luo relationship. Why are the other options incorrect? LU7 Lieque and LI4 Hegu — LU7 with LI4 gives Lung Luo with Large Intestine Yuan, the reverse emphasis. LU9 Taiyuan and LI4 Hegu — LU9 with LI4 combines two Yuan-Source points, not Yuan with Luo. LU6 Kongzui and LI6 Pianli — LU6 is Lung Xi-Cleft, not Yuan-Source.

10. Which statement accurately distinguishes LI3 Sanjian and LI4 Hegu?
