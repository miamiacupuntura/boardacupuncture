9. Which statement most accurately describes the general Xi-Cleft category while allowing for individual point differences?
A) All Xi-Cleft points are only for gynaecological disease.
B) They are associated with acute conditions and pain; Yin-channel Xi-Cleft points also have important Blood-related indications. +++++
C) Every Xi-Cleft point treats the same biomedical emergency.
D) Xi-Cleft is a sixth Five Shu category.
Feedback: Xi-Cleft points are traditionally associated with acute conditions and pain, with important Blood-related indications on Yin channels; the individual entry and differentiated presentation still guide selection. Why are the other options incorrect? All Xi-Cleft points are only for gynaecological disease. — Gynaecological indications are not the entire category’s scope. Every Xi-Cleft point treats the same biomedical emergency. — A traditional category does not make all points interchangeable or establish a biomedical emergency treatment. Xi-Cleft is a sixth Five Shu category. — Xi-Cleft is separate from the five transporting categories.
