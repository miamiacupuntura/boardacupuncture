1. Which point is the same-channel Mother point of the Lung channel in the Five Element generating cycle?
A) LU9 Taiyuan +++++
B) LU11 Shaoshang
C) LU10 Yuji
D) LU8 Jingqu
Feedback: LU9 Taiyuan is the Mother point: its Earth element generates the channel’s Metal element. Why are the other options incorrect? LU11 Shaoshang — LU11 Shaoshang is the Jing-Well and Wood point of the Lung channel. Its element does not satisfy the mother relationship requested. LU10 Yuji — LU10 Yuji is the Ying-Spring and Fire point of the Lung channel. Its element does not satisfy the mother relationship requested. LU8 Jingqu — LU8 Jingqu is the Jing-River and Metal point of the Lung channel. Its element does not satisfy the mother relationship requested.

8. Which classical principle underlies the Mother-point approach in the Nanjing, Difficulty 69?
A) In deficiency, reinforce the Mother; in excess, reduce the Child. +++++
B) In deficiency, always reduce the Child.
C) Mother points are always the Yuan-Source points on Yang channels.
D) Every Mother point belongs to the same element as its channel.
Feedback: Difficulty 69 supplies the Mother–Child principle: in deficiency, reinforce the Mother; in excess, reduce the Child. Application still requires pattern differentiation. Why are the other options incorrect? In deficiency, always reduce the Child. — This replaces the deficiency principle with the excess principle. Mother points are always the Yuan-Source points on Yang channels. — For example, LI11 is Mother while LI4 is Yuan-Source. Every Mother point belongs to the same element as its channel. — A Mother element precedes and generates the channel element; it is not the same element.

9. The Lung channel belongs to Metal. Which element identifies its Mother point within the generating cycle?
A) Water
B) Earth +++++
C) Fire
D) Wood
Feedback: Earth generates Metal, so Lung Mother-point selection seeks the Earth point LU9. Why are the other options incorrect? Water — Water is Metal’s Child, not Mother. Fire — Fire controls Metal in the controlling cycle, rather than generating it. Wood — Wood is neither the immediate Mother nor Child of Metal in the generating cycle.

10. Which statement correctly distinguishes a Mother-point classification from a complete clinical prescription?
A) Selecting a Mother point establishes the diagnosis without symptoms.
B) Every patient on that channel must receive the Mother point.
C) The classification identifies a generating-cycle relationship; individual actions and the patient’s pattern still guide selection. +++++
D) Mother and Child are interchangeable terms.
Feedback: Mother identifies the generating-cycle relationship, while point actions and pattern differentiation determine the clinical application. Why are the other options incorrect? Selecting a Mother point establishes the diagnosis without symptoms. — A point classification cannot independently establish diagnosis. Every patient on that channel must receive the Mother point. — Channel identity does not make a Mother point mandatory for every presentation. Mother and Child are interchangeable terms. — Mother generates the channel element; Child is generated by it.
