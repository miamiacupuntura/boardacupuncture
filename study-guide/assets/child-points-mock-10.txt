8. Which classical principle specifically supports using a Child point in a presentation of excess?
A) Always reinforce the Mother regardless of pattern.
B) Every Child point is the channel’s Fire point.
C) Every Child point is a Yuan-Source point.
D) In excess, reduce the Child. +++++
Feedback: The classical Mother–Child rule states that in excess the Child may be reduced; it is a pattern-based principle rather than an automatic prescription. Why are the other options incorrect? Always reinforce the Mother regardless of pattern. — Reinforcing the Mother is the deficiency principle and is not universally applied to excess. Every Child point is the channel’s Fire point. — The Child element depends on the channel element. Every Child point is a Yuan-Source point. — For example, Lung Child LU5 is not Lung Yuan-Source LU9.

9. The Lung channel belongs to Metal. Which element and point identify its same-channel Child relationship?
A) Water — LU5 Chize +++++
B) Earth — LU9 Taiyuan
C) Fire — LU10 Yuji
D) Wood — LU11 Shaoshang
Feedback: Metal generates Water, so LU5 Chize, the Water point, is Lung’s Child. Why are the other options incorrect? Earth — LU9 Taiyuan — Earth generates Metal, making LU9 Mother rather than Child. Fire — LU10 Yuji — Fire controls Metal in the controlling cycle; it is not Metal’s generated Child. Wood — LU11 Shaoshang — Wood is generated by Water, not directly by Metal.

10. Which statement correctly compares the Lung Mother and Child points?
A) LU5 is Mother and LU9 is Child.
B) LU9 is Mother and LU5 is Child. +++++
C) LU10 is both Mother and Child.
D) LU11 and LU8 are the same classification.
Feedback: LU9 is Earth and Lung Mother; LU5 is Water and Lung Child. Why these roles differ is determined by Earth → Metal → Water in the generating cycle. Why are the other options incorrect? LU5 is Mother and LU9 is Child. — This reverses the two generating-cycle relationships. LU10 is both Mother and Child. — LU10 is Fire and is neither the Metal channel’s Mother nor Child. LU11 and LU8 are the same classification. — LU11 is Jing-Well and Wood; LU8 is Jing-River and Metal.
