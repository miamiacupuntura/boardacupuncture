What you will study

  • A · Herbs: distinguish them by properties, actions, indications, dosage and safety.

Recommended route

  • 1. Open the 3 cards to understand rosters, changes and priorities.
  • 2. Study individual herbs before comparing pairs and formulas.
  • 3. Continue with Duì Yào, then formula composition and comparisons.
  • 4. Review safety throughout the route, not only at the end.

3 · Read

Your completion goal

  • Distinguish similar options using the decisive clue in the case.
  • Explain each herb’s role inside a pair or formula.
  • Select a formula from the pattern and justify modifications.
  • Detect when a choice is unsafe, obsolete or requires substitution.
