Study route

Follow steps 1–3 in order. Check safety at every step.

What is this module for? It tells you what to study, which resources to open in what order, and what you must be able to do before the exam. The three cards orient the route; study begins with A · Herbs, continues through B · Duì Yào and C · Formulas, with D · Safety integrated throughout.

1 · Step

What you will study

  • A · Herbs: distinguish them by properties, actions, indications, dosage and safety.
  • B · Duì Yào: understand why all 51 pairs combine and what each herb contributes.
  • C · Formulas: connect ingredients and hierarchy to pattern, use and modification.
  • D · Safety: recognize toxicity, interactions, contraindications and substitutions.

2 · Step

Recommended route

  • 1. Open the 3 cards to understand rosters, changes and priorities.
  • 2. Study individual herbs before comparing pairs and formulas.
  • 3. Continue with Duì Yào, then formula composition and comparisons.
  • 4. Review safety throughout the route, not only at the end.

3 · Step

Your completion goal

  • Distinguish similar options using the decisive clue in the case.
  • Explain each herb’s role inside a pair or formula.
  • Select a formula from the pattern and justify modifications.
  • Detect when a choice is unsafe, obsolete or requires substitution.
