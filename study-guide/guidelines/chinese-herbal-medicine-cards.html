Current formula roster

For each formula: ingredients, hierarchy, actions, indications, modifications, contraindications and contrast with neighboring formulas. Official Appendix C controls inclusion.

How to read the current guideline

BlockResponsibilityStudy application
IMateria medica and formulasRecognition is not enough: connect properties, function and pattern.
IISafety and dispensingProcessing, dosage, toxicity, interactions, pregnancy, quality and obsolete substances.
IIITreatment planningSelect, combine, modify, substitute, monitor and refer from the patient assessment.

Key change: use NCBAHM as the current name and NCCAOM only when identifying historical material. Do not relabel old rosters as current without checking edition and date.
