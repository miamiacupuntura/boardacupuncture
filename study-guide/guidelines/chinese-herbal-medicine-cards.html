Card 3 · Analysis

How to read the current guideline

BlockResponsibilityStudy application
IMateria medica and formulasRecognition is not enough: connect properties, function and pattern.
IISafety and dispensingProcessing, dosage, toxicity, interactions, pregnancy, herbal product quality control and obsolete substances.
IIITreatment planningSelect, combine, modify, substitute, monitor and refer from the patient assessment.

Key change: you do not need to start over. Keep the materia medica and formulas that remain current, reorganize review around Appendices A–D, and stop using obsolete substances as therapeutic answers.

Priority: after recognizing names and categories, devote more practice to comparing close options, selecting by pattern, adjusting the formula, and solving safety, dosage, interaction and contraindication questions.
