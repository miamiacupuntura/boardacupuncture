Study plan adjustment

NCCAOM → NCBAHM

A guide to what knowledge to retain, what to stop studying as active content and where to increase practice for the current Board.

Goal: reorganize your preparation around the current NCBAHM scope without losing foundations that still apply.

Level 1

Study the current scope

Begin with the NCBAHM topics and appendices for your Board; these define the content to master now.

Level 2

Retain applicable foundations

Keep concepts, herbs and formulas that remain in scope; do not restart material you already master.

Level 3

Redirect practice

Devote more questions to comparison, clinical selection, safety and content that is new or reorganized.

Changes that affect study

What to add to the plan

  • The current acronym is NCBAHM; NCCAOM remains a historical reference.
  • Herbal Medicine uses the Appendix A–D structure and Appendix B contains 51 Duì Yào pairs.
  • Appendix D separates nine obsolete substances to recognize for safety and substitution, not as active therapeutic options.
  • The new ACPL outline takes effect September 1, 2026 and publishes four explicit weights.

Using practice resources

Do not confuse quantity with scope

  • 247 comparative notes do not mean 247 official herbs.
  • 110 formula comparisons do not mean 110 official formulas.
  • 85 formula summaries indicate practice volume, not the number of formulas required by the Board.
  • A pinyin variant, synonym or processed form is not automatically an addition or removal.
