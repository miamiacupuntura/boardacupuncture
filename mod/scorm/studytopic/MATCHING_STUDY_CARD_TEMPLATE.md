# Approved Matching Study Card Template

Status: approved baseline for future Herb and Formula comparison cards.

Reference sample:
`samples/Real_Herb_Comparison_Matching_Polished_Preview.docx`

## Two-item comparison

The approved card is one landscape letter-size sheet in this order:

1. Full source names in the title, including botanical or formula name and pinyin.
2. `Study tip · Clues to help answer` comparison table.
   - Shared clues are highlighted.
   - Distinguishing symptoms or characteristics are aligned by item.
3. `Comparative answer key` table.
   - Category appears before formulas or other keyed associations.
4. `Study guide · Exam focus`.
   - Why the distinction matters.
   - Common confusion.
   - How it may appear on the exam.
   - Why a switched match is wrong.
5. `Possible exam questions · Study prompts`.

The card must not include a cover, internal contents page, chapter label, source
CMID, generic `Question` heading, or generic `Matching` interaction label.
Parenthetical pinyin-only names are reserved for the separate library index;
the card title retains the full names.

## Comparisons with three or more items

Do not force a multi-item question into the two-column layout.

- Three or four items use a landscape comparison matrix: one item per column
  with aligned rows for category, shared clues, distinguishing clues, and keyed
  associations.
- Shared information spans every item column and remains visually highlighted.
- The Study Guide explains the main confusion pattern across the full group,
  then identifies the strongest distinguishing clue for each item.
- Possible exam questions remain at the bottom as unscored study prompts.
- Full names remain on the card; concise pinyin names remain in the external
  index.
- Content clarity takes priority over page count. Target one landscape sheet,
  but never shorten a necessary explanation, collapse meaningful distinctions,
  or tighten tables until they are difficult to scan merely to force one page.
- Keep body text at a readable size; 8 pt is the absolute lower limit, not the
  default target.

When several items, unusually long formula lists, or images make one sheet
unclear, use exactly two coordinated sheets labelled `Card 1 of 2` and `Card 2
of 2`. Do not exceed two sheets. Keep the original Matching question ID in the
export metadata so both sheets remain traceable and are counted as one source
question.

The preferred two-sheet split is:

- Card 1: full comparison title, shared clues, distinguishing clues, categories,
  and comparative answer key.
- Card 2: formula or herb associations, Study Guide exam focus, and possible
  exam questions.

## Index and reporting

The separate Matching index groups comparisons by the category of the first
item and lists concise pinyin names. It reports source-question occurrences and
unique comparisons. Study prompts are not surveys and are not scored; survey,
checklist, and student weakness reporting remain a later phase.
