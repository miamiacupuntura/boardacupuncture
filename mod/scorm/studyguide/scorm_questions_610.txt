QUESTION 1
QUESTION TEXT:
Goiter
Goiter denotes an enlargement of the thyroid gland, causing a swelling in the front part of the neck, which is not accompanied by pain, ulceration or skin discoloration. According to the records of ancient literature, it can be classified as "qi goiter," "flesh goiter" and "stone goiter."
Differentiation
Main manifestations: Diffusive swelling in the neck, soft, gradually increasing in size with unclear margins, normal color. absence of pain; in some cases big and drooping, accompanied with dyspnea and hoarseness of voice. The size of the goiter usually changes with emotions.
What is the acupuncture prescription?.

ANSWERS:
1. Naohui (TE 13), Tianding (LI 17), Tianrong (SI 17), Tiantu (CV 22). Hegu (LI 4), Zusanli (ST 36)
2. Naohui (TE 13), Lingtai (GV 10), Shenzhu (GV 12), Ximen (PC 4), Hegu (LI 4)
3. Naohui (TE 13), Jianjing (GB 21), Tanzhong (CV 17), Hegu (LI 4), Zusanli (ST 36)
4. Naohui (TE 13), Tianding (LI 17), Tianrong (SI 17), Tiantu (CV 22), Lingtai (GV 10), Shenzhu (GV 12)

RIGHT ANSWER:
Naohui (TE 13), Tianding (LI 17), Tianrong (SI 17), Tiantu (CV 22). Hegu (LI 4), Zusanli (ST 36)

FEEDBACK:
- Goiter: Qi goiter. Naohui (TE 13), Tianding (LI 17), Tianrong (SI 17), Tiantu (CV 22). Hegu (LI 4), Zusanli (ST 36).
- The correct answers is: Goiter: Qi goiter. Naohui (TE 13), Tianding (LI 17), Tianrong (SI 17), Tiantu (CV 22). Hegu (LI 4), Zusanli (ST 36).
