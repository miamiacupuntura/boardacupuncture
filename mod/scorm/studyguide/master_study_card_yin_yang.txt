📚 MASTER STUDY CARD
☯️ YIN YANG THEORY
================================================================

☯️ 1. CORE IDEA
YIN: Cold · Stillness · Darkness · Inward · Downward · Inhibition · Nutrient substances
YANG: Heat · Movement · Brightness · Outward · Upward · Functional activity · Potency
KEY: Yin and Yang are opposite, interdependent, and relative.

----------------------------------------------------------------

🔄 2. FIVE FUNDAMENTAL RELATIONSHIPS
1. OPPOSITION → Different / opposite
2. INTERDEPENDENCE → Need each other
3. MUTUAL CONSUMING & SUPPORTING → Dynamic influence and support
4. INTER-TRANSFORMATION → Change into each other under appropriate conditions
5. INFINITE DIVISIBILITY → Further subdivision / relative classification

----------------------------------------------------------------

🕐 3. RELATIVE YIN-YANG & TIME
Morning → Yang within Yang
Afternoon → Yin within Yang
First half of night → Yin within Yin
Second half of night → Yang within Yin
KEY: Yin and Yang are relative to time and frame of reference.

----------------------------------------------------------------

🧍 4. BODY STRUCTURE
Upper → Yang · Lower → Yin · Exterior → Yang · Interior → Yin
Lateral → Yang · Medial → Yin · Zang → Yin · Fu → Yang

----------------------------------------------------------------

⚙️ 5. PHYSIOLOGY
YIN → Nutrient substances / Material basis
YANG → Functional activity / Functional processes
KEY: Yin provides the material basis for Yang; Yang provides the activity through which Yin is utilized and transformed.

----------------------------------------------------------------

☯️ 6. YIN WITHIN YANG / YANG WITHIN YIN
Yang contains a Yin aspect · Yin contains a Yang aspect
KEY: Internal unity + relative classification + infinite divisibility.

----------------------------------------------------------------

⚖️ 7. DYNAMIC BALANCE
Oppose · Depend · Consume / Support · Transform · Subdivide
KEY: Balance is dynamic, not fixed 50/50.

----------------------------------------------------------------

🧩 8. THREE YIN & THREE YANG
THREE YIN: Taiyin → Greater Yin · Shaoyin → Lesser Yin · Jueyin → Declining Yin
THREE YANG: Taiyang → Greater Yang · Shaoyang → Scanty Yang · Yangming → Extreme Yang
KEY: Further subdivision according to relative degree.

----------------------------------------------------------------

☯️ 9. TAIJITU
Represents:
Opposition · Interdependence · Yin within Yang · Yang within Yin
Dynamic balance · Inter-transformation · Infinite divisibility
MEMORY: The Taijitu summarizes the dynamic relationship and mutual inclusion of Yin and Yang.
